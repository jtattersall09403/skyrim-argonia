# Mere Neck (place.route.archon-gideon-mere-neck): design

## Site
The Long road (route.road.archon-gideon, chainage 1460-1625 m) crosses a strip of mud 15-25 m wide. A mere lies to the east of the strip and a marsh pond to the west. The strip is centred at 4755, 3542 m. About 55 m of the road (x 4787 to 4736) lies under 0.1-1.28 m of water and the water is deepest at 4739-4745 (measured on the chunk ground, water 7.89 m). Crossings 030 and 033 fall on this wade. No junction pin stands on the stretch, so road-dressing rule 4 rules out a signpost. A marsh track (track.hist-heartland.heretic-stone-restarted) leaves the road at the north-west bend. The west half of the wade lies inside the outer rim of the 175 m circle of the unbuilt place Long-Stride. Only road furniture stands there (ruling R111).

## Intent
The road runs down into the mere and a walker sees the causeway sink below the water. Two lines of poles mark its edges over the wade. On the far side the ground rises again at a cairn. The neck has no residents and the marks were left by travellers.

## Walk-through
1. From Archon the road runs along a dry crest. A cold fire ring lies a few paces off the road on the single flat dry spot.
2. The south-east cairn stands on the last dry verge. The first poles stand on both verges beside it.
3. The wade has two pole lines about 14 m apart, with a pole every 5 m on each verge. The north line stands in 0.2-0.8 m of water at the mere's edge. The south line stands on the dry verge until the deep end, where the water crosses the whole road (0.9 m at the south pole there).
4. The north-west cairn stands where the road climbs out and a wet rock lies at its foot.
5. The track-head cairn stands on the inside of the bend, with loose rocks along the bend. The track goes off south-west into the reeds.

## Palette
Kothringi driven poles (`tamu_woodpole01`, Here There Be Monsters; dug-in, members of the wade-head parcels), vanilla cairns (rockcairn01, 02, 04), wet rocks and a cold fire ring. The place has no planks and no grave. This contrasts with the Greenspring crossings, which are plank runs.

## Brief
The place has four waymark parcels (east-wade-head, west-wade-head, track-head, drying-fire). A short footpath from the road reaches each parcel. 22 poles (dug-in) are bound as ground members of the two wade-head parcels and the place has two road terminals. The west boundary stands at u 0.6375 (x 4700.6), just east of where the marsh track meets the road. The track therefore never crosses the place's edge and needs no terminal.
