The game is set in Black Marsh, the swamp province in the south-east of Tamriel, in the year 4E 201, the
same year as Skyrim. It plays like Morrowind: you walk in, talk to people, and learn the place from them.

**The land.** Hot, wet, mostly swamp and drowned forest, with firmer hills on the western border with
Cyrodiil and a coast of trade towns. Roads are few and old; most travel is by boat. Poisonous plants,
predators and disease are everywhere, and the deep interior has never been tamed by anyone.

**The Argonians.** Lizard-folk who call themselves the Saxhleel, the People of the Root; their own language
is Jel. They do not think of Black Marsh as one nation. Each tribe and village is its own place with its own
ways. They are private about their culture and slow to explain it to outsiders.

**The Hist.** Great trees with minds of their own, older than any people. Argonians hatch from eggs laid
near a Hist's roots, and on the naming day each hatchling drinks its sap. The sap shapes their body and
scales for life; it is their blood and soul, and returns to the Hist at death. A village's Hist is tended by
a tree-minder. Argonians name it casually, as part of daily life; outsiders find it strange.

**The An-Xileel.** The Argonian party that took Black Marsh out of the Empire after the Oblivion Crisis. It
rules loosely from Lilmoth and distrusts anything Imperial. It coined *lukiul*, a slur for Argonians raised
outside Black Marsh who cannot hear the Hist. *Ojel* is the plain, neutral word for an outsider.

**The Empire's remnant.** The Empire held the coasts and borders for thirteen centuries and used the
province as its prison. It withdrew in decay, not by force. What is left: Gideon on the western frontier,
old Imperial road stages and toll posts, prisons, Imperial families who stayed, and Legion men and their
heirs who were never paid when the Legion was recalled.

**Debt and the Chainbreakers.** Slavery has ended, but debt labour has not. An Owing is a debt worked off in
labour, sometimes for generations; in Gideon it is a paper contract a court can contest. The Chainbreakers
fight the Owing and free the people bound by it.

**The tribes and the Naga.** The interior holds tribes outsiders rarely meet; the Naga are raiders who hunt
the roads and rivers and keep their own rites.

**Outsiders.** Dunmer to the north (old slavers, now traders and settlers), Khajiit traders, Imperials,
Bretons, Redguards, Nords and Bosmer who came with the Legion, the prisons or trade. To an Argonian village
the player is ojel: tolerated, watched, told little until trusted.
