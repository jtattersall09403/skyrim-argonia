# Talk brief: Seeks-The-Channel (05D7C8:Black Marsh.esm)

Write each topic's reply in this person's voice. State only the facts listed under the topic; add none. One to three sentences a reply (a route may take four); a second line only where the person would go on. No Skyrim place, person or thing (decision 0006). British English.

**Directions** are routes from where the person stands, as Morrowind gives them: retell the legs in order with their landmarks and headings ("walk west until you see the wrecked boat, then follow the shore north..."), in this person's words; drop a leg's count of paces or round it as a local would, merge two short legs, but never add a landmark, change a heading or skip a turn. Vary the telling with the person; a place in sight is paces and a compass point only.

**Standing.** The player is a stranger unless they become a friend. Every topic reply is said to a stranger: neutral or cool by temperament, no warm address. The friend greeting is the one place a warm word fits; at most two warm address terms ("my friend", "brother", "dear") in all of this person's lines (lint B11).
