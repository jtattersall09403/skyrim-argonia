# Journal brief: TG01 A Dry Hand in a Wet City

Write each entry in the style guide's journal register (story/design/text/style-guide.md § journal):
first person; past tense for events; "I'm to" for the objective; full names every entry; re-summarise the
chain; directions in landmark prose; failure entries flat, with the consequence.

## Stage 20 (`text.journal.tg01.20`)
- Draft: Tilts-The-Jug, inside the house on Soulrest's dock, keeps the Chapter's desk at Soulrest and wants to see how I work. Keeps-What-Floats, a dock thief, took a necklace of polished blue jaspers from another thief, and it must come back with no harm done to him. I'm to follow him on the dock until he goes to his bed for the night, or get out of Sits-By-The-Water, who drinks with him, where he sleeps.
- Directions: He walks the dock west of the house by day, and in the evening he drinks at the bench by the water with Sits-By-The-Water; where he sleeps is learnt by following him or from her.
- The player knows: fact.tg01.desk-door, fact.tg01.test, fact.tg01.necklace

## Stage 30 (`text.journal.tg01.30`)
- Draft: Keeps-What-Floats, who took the necklace of blue jaspers, keeps it on him on the dock by day, at the bench by the water in the evening, and on his bedroll north-west of the house at night. I'm to get the necklace back without hurting him, by lifting it off him by day or while he sleeps, or by talking him out of it, and bring it to Tilts-The-Jug inside Soulrest's house.
- Directions: By day he is on the dock west of the house; in the evening he drinks at the bench by the water, and at night he sleeps on his bedroll north-west of the house. Tilts-The-Jug is inside the house, behind its one open door.

## Stage 40 (`text.journal.tg01.40`)
- Draft: I brought the necklace of blue jaspers back to Tilts-The-Jug at Soulrest, and Keeps-What-Floats came to no harm. She paid me and gave me three lockpicks. The Chapter's way down is a locked trapdoor just north of Pusbottom in Lilmoth. I'm to take the coast ship from Soulrest's dock to Lilmoth, open the trapdoor, go down to the Chapter and speak to whoever waits below.
- Directions: The coast ship's door is on Soulrest's dock (039827); inside the ship, the other door (0AA837) comes out at Lilmoth Dock (0AA664), on Lilmoth's east side. Walk west to the Lilmoth Hist; Pusbottom lies past it by the canals, and the trapdoor (16BF7F) is in the ground just north of Pusbottom. Below, Bent-Spine keeps to the Chapter's inner room, past the foot of the trapdoor's ladder.
