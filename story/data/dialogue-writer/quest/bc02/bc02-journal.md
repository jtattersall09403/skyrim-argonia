# Journal brief: BC02 One Out of the Smoke

Write each entry in the style guide's journal register (story/design/text/style-guide.md § journal):
first person; past tense for events; "I'm to" for the objective; full names every entry; re-summarise the
chain; directions in landmark prose; failure entries flat, with the consequence.

## Stage 20 (`text.journal.bc02.20`)
- Draft: Never-Sold told me the heirs' outer post, the old keep on the hill west of Blackrose, is burning. The heirs, born of the prisoners once held at the Rose, keep it. Three they hold are inside, Rows-For-Another's mother among them, and the heirs' gate-keepers will not go in. I'm to get past Third-Born Tsaxi at the keep's way in, by talking him round or slipping by, and go inside.
- Directions: The keep stands on the hill a short climb west of the 'Black Rose Jail' marker, at Blackrose's edge; Tsaxi keeps its way in.
- The player knows: fact.bc02.fire, fact.bc02.three-held

## Stage 30 (`text.journal.bc02.30`)
- Draft: I'm inside the burning keep above Blackrose. Three the heirs hold are bound inside: Says-It-Plain, held for speaking against the heirs; Hides-In-Reeds, held for a killing; and Keeps-The-Old-Count, Rows-For-Another's mother, held on her Owing. The way out will bear one trip. I'm to choose one and tell them to come with me.
- Directions: The three held are on the keep's floors in the smoke; the way out is the way I came in.
- The player knows: fact.bc02.fire, fact.bc02.three-held, fact.bc02.three-held, fact.bc02.one-out
