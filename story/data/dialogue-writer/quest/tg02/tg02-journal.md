# Journal brief: TG02 The Drowned Lockbox

Write each entry in the style guide's journal register (story/design/text/style-guide.md § journal):
first person; past tense for events; "I'm to" for the objective; full names every entry; re-summarise the
chain; directions in landmark prose; failure entries flat, with the consequence.

## Stage 20 (`text.journal.tg02.20`)
- Draft: I'm in the Night-Reed Chapter now, under Pusbottom in Lilmoth. Bent-Spine, the Chapter's fence, wants the lockbox of a merchant whose stilt house fell into the deep water just north of Pusbottom, by the Chapter's trapdoor; it lies locked on the floor beside the house, and ripper eels hunt the water. Three clients on the bank want it. I'm to talk to each of them, Neeta-Haj, Okan-Tei and Dar-Zeel, and get out of each what they claim the box holds for them.
- Directions: Climb the trapdoor's ladder out of the Chapter; the fallen house lies in the deep water just north of Pusbottom by the trapdoor, and the three clients wait on its south bank.
- The player knows: fact.tg02.job, fact.tg02.box, fact.tg02.eels, fact.tg02.claim-figurine, fact.tg02.claim-charm, fact.tg02.claim-beads

## Stage 40 (`text.journal.tg02.40`)
- Draft: Neeta-Haj says the bone figurine in the lockbox was carved for her clutch, Okan-Tei that the charm was only pledged and never sold, and Dar-Zeel that the beads are old family work. The box lies locked on the floor of the deep water north of Pusbottom beside the fallen stilt house, and ripper eels hunt the water. I'm to swim down to it, slip past the eels, pick its lock and take the figurine, the charm and the beads, then settle it on the bank, by handing all three things to one client or by selling each client the thing they claimed.
- Directions: The fallen house lies on its side on the floor of the deep water below the bank; the lockbox is in the open beside it. The three clients wait on the bank above.
- The player knows: fact.tg02.job, fact.tg02.box, fact.tg02.eels, fact.tg02.claim-figurine, fact.tg02.claim-charm, fact.tg02.claim-beads, fact.tg02.figurine-old, fact.tg02.charm-remounted, fact.tg02.beads-kothringi
