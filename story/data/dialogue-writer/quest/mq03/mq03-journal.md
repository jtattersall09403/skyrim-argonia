# Journal brief: MQ03 A Dead Prisoner's Use

Write each entry in the style guide's journal register (story/design/text/style-guide.md § journal):
first person; past tense for events; "I'm to" for the objective; full names every entry; re-summarise the
chain; directions in landmark prose; failure entries flat, with the consequence.

## Stage 10 (`text.journal.mq03.10`)
- Draft: The camp on Hereguard Plantation's paddy edge is a ruin and I came out of the fire burnt. Two strangers, who say a friend of the courier's sent them, are taking off the hurt. Sap the cutters spilled lies on the shed floor, and the courier Mee-Tuja's brass tag fell through the burnt plank boards at the fire into the deep water. I'm to taste the sap, take a phial of it or dive for the tag in the families' sunk fish-trap and pick the trap's lock, and then tell the stranger on the shore I am ready to go.
- Directions: The shed stands beside the withdrawn cutting at the camp's far end. The burnt plank boards lie at the camp's fire over the deep water; the courier's strap and tag lie in the families' locked fish-trap on the bottom straight below them, one breath down; lockpicks lie loose on the boards beside the fire.

## Stage 20 (`text.journal.mq03.20`)
- Draft: The strangers took the hurt to a house in Lilmoth that a fish-trader, Salts-The-Catch, keeps. In the back room, behind a screen, a woman who calls herself Nesh-Deeka sits by the mats, and Ties-The-Loose-Plank lies on one of them. Nesh-Deeka is of the Veiled Reed. The roll burnt at the camp, so nobody can show what I owe or that I was on it, and almost no one knows my face. I'm to tell her what I saw, and get Ties to tell it too, then answer her offer of the oath.
- Directions: The house has no mark; the back room is past the front room's table, behind the screen, the mats beside it. The street door opens from inside; the camp is the shore path north and west of the dock.

## Stage 40 (`text.journal.mq03.40`)
- Draft: I lay down on the mats in the house in Lilmoth with the camp's sap still on my tongue, and dreamed. I stood in fog among a ring of trees whose roots climbed up and up, and at the top was a gap like a door that nobody has built. A dry voice spoke that I could not place. In the dream I walked to the door.
- Directions: The roots of the ring rise from the fog to the door-gap at the top; the dream walks you there.
