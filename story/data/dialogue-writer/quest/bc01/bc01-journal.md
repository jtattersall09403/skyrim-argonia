# Journal brief: BC01 Four Months for a Boat

Write each entry in the style guide's journal register (story/design/text/style-guide.md § journal):
first person; past tense for events; "I'm to" for the objective; full names every entry; re-summarise the
chain; directions in landmark prose; failure entries flat, with the consequence.

## Stage 10 (`text.journal.bc01.10`)
- Draft: Never-Sold of the Chainbreakers, in their house in Blackrose, wants Rows-For-Another freed from the estate north of the town, where she has worked the same Owing, a debt paid in months of work, for eleven years. The steward, Hulm-Tei, keeps the estate's debt book in a locked chest on the landing. I'm to meet Never-Sold there and bring her the book, or speak to Rows-For-Another after dark, unseen by the estate's men.
- Directions: From the Chainbreakers' house take the swamp road north out of Blackrose; the estate's landing is a short walk on, on the channel side, with boats at the water. The steward's chest stands on the landing.

## Stage 40 (`text.journal.bc01.40`)
- Draft: Never-Sold of the Chainbreakers sent me to free Rows-For-Another from the estate north of Blackrose. She will not wait on the steward's book. She is following me from the landing, past the two estate men, down the channel to where it leaves the estate. She cannot drown in it. I'm to take her there tonight.
- Directions: The channel runs from the landing's boats north-east, away from the road; where it leaves the estate it passes under a fallen trunk.

## Stage 90 (`text.journal.bc01.90`) ending: complete
- Draft: Never-Sold of the Chainbreakers sent me to free Rows-For-Another, who took a boat from an estate north of Blackrose on four months' work eleven years ago. I took Hulm-Tei's debt book from his chest on the landing. Never-Sold tore out Rows-For-Another's page and gave it to her, and left the first price, four months' worth in coin, in the steward's chest. She walks off the swamp road free, without her boat.
