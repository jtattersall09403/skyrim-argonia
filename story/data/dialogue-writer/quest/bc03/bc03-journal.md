# Journal brief: BC03 The Broker's Mercy

Write each entry in the style guide's journal register (story/design/text/style-guide.md § journal):
first person; past tense for events; "I'm to" for the objective; full names every entry; re-summarise the
chain; directions in landmark prose; failure entries flat, with the consequence.

## Stage 10 (`text.journal.bc03.10`)
- Draft: On Soulrest's open dock the broker Ussa-Rekh, who bought out Owings for thirty years, refused to let Heavy-Rope's crew end their last season early. The crew know me as the Chainbreakers'. I'm to hear Ussa-Rekh and Heavy-Rope on the dock and take one side.
- Directions: Soulrest's open dock is the long dock on the town's water, where the crews wait for hire; Ussa-Rekh keeps his chest beside him on the planks.

- Directions: Ussa-Rekh's chest stands on the planks beside him, on a low lock.
