## topic.mq01-the-march (the years from home; stage 10; not essential; said on the march when the player walks beside her)
# facts stated: fact.mq01.washed-out, fact.mq01.gang-hist. fact.mq01.owing-roll is known to her and is not stated.

# [low | any]
Walk if you wish. I do not talk on the march. I do not speak of the Hist to strangers, so keep beside me and I may speak when I know you.

# [words | argonian]
You walk like one of the marsh. Good. I will tell you three things. The Hist is far from us. The years have been long. Two of us in this gang have been kept from the Hist so long that our colour and markings have thinned. My own is going.

# [words | other]
You walk beside me, [player race], so you will hear it. A Hist is a great tree. We hatch near it, drink its sap and listen to it. Two of us in this gang have been kept from the Hist so long that our colour and markings have thinned. My own is going.

# [words | any, what the cutting is to the families]
At the camp we keep a cutting of the Hist, brought from Nine Bends. Our egg clutch lies at its roots. There is a cutting, a clutch and a shed.

# [high | argonian]
I will tell you more, because you walked the whole march at my side. Two of us in this gang have been kept from the Hist so long that our colour and markings have thinned. Mine is going. I want to stand beside the camp's cutting before it is gone. I cannot stand beside my own Hist, so the cutting must serve. It is a piece of the Hist from Nine Bends. The gang families keep it at the camp. Our egg clutch lies at its roots. I fear that I will reach the camp and find the cutting gone.
The camp is where the shore path meets the reeds. You will see the huts, the fire and the hatching shed. The shed stands beside the cutting at the far end.
