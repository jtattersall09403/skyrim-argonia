# [low | any]
I hold this gang's months. I hire them out to Hereguard Plantation, at the far end of the shore path. That is all I tell a stranger on my pier. The line with no name on it is another matter. I do not discuss it with strangers, so speak to me again when I have reason to trust you.

# [words | argonian]
You are Argonian, so I will say it plainly. Every hand in this gang owes months of work. I hold those months. I hire the gang out to Hereguard Plantation, where the shore path meets the reeds. The roll adds months for food as well. Your line on the roll has no name on it. That line is mine to fill or to leave. I cannot hire out a hand who owes nothing, so if you want it filled, give me a reason.
