I enjoyed the atmosphere. Argonian dialogue is spare and distinct, and Dives-Without-A-Rope is a sympathetic character. Choosing to persuade her, then pay for the key, felt like real choices. I understood who everyone was: the Veiled Reed, the broker, and the woman with the knotted cord, who is still unexplained and left me curious. Rough edges: persuading her did not give me the key, and only paying did. The game called opening the box with her key "stealing" and asked whether I was seen. Delivering the jar needed several repeated dialogue attempts before it registered. The three identical "I will taste a drop" options looked like a bug. The quest ended with only a vague hint about the Hist at Low-Reed turning its face, and no marker or journal objective for it, so I stopped because nothing was left to do. I would want to know what happens next, especially the cutters, the Hist and the knotted-cord woman.
