I hold two seal-stones (Blackrose and Gemin), about 350 gold, Reel-Ka armour, rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, sap jar, keystone, eye-setting. The Gemin tablet warns: use the Eye in place at the Root and it strengthens every tree; lift it out and all trees are cut off from each other and the weak die. So never lift it. Still needed: third stone in the deepest hall of Vorannuhl (boat, near Noota Nara); then all three go into the Kings Gate under Teku-Sul Spire (dive from Bell Station). The Unbound and the Collector (Ennodius Vanin, at Blackrose) are rivals. At Gemin I took the stone without a fight; the Warden would not talk. Open questions: why the Unbound seal trees, what moans in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar, how Nesh-Deeka knows about the Unbound.
