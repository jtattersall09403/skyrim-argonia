I now hold two seal-stones, from Blackrose and Gemin, plus about 50 gold, rapier, lockpicks, 3 Grey Seals, 3 Unbound Seals and the Warden Vault Key. The Gemin tablet says the Eye, if lifted, parts every tree from every other, and if used where it sits it strengthens them. The last stone is in the deepest hall of Vorannuhl and is marked; it is the worst of the three. After that, take all three to the Kings Gate at Teku-Sul Spire off Lilmoth. Still unknown: what the Root and Eye truly are, whether Ennodius Vanin is the real master (his Kothringi silver ring aids locks and pockets), and what is moaning in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar, which I have not checked.
