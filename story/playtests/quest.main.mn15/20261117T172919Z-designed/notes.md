I now hold two seal-stones: Blackrose and Gemin (taken from the drowned hall; nobody there would talk, and I did not need to fight). About 350 gold, Reel-Ka armour, rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, sap jar, keystone, eye-setting. The Gemin tablet says: the Eye used in place at the Root strengthens every tree; lifted from its place it cuts every tree off from every other and the weak die alone. The king sealed it so no one hand could do either alone. Still needed: third stone from the deepest hall of Vorannuhl (boat, near Noota Nara). Then the Kings Gate under Teku-Sul Spire (dive from Bell Station). Rivals: the Unbound (who drain trees) and the Collector Ennodius Vanin at Blackrose. Open: why the Unbound drain trees, what moans in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar, how Nesh-Deeka knows about the Unbound. Do not lift the Eye.
