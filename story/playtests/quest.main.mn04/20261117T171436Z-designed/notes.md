She said the Unbound (a tribeless cult led by Cuts-the-Old-Knot) drain Hist sap into jars that go to him, then seal the scar with grey paste called "the seal". The sap goes into a draught tied to "the Eye". I do not know what the seal is made of, what the Eye is, or where Cuts-the-Old-Knot is. Sesk left to find a boat. My journal says to tell Vosh-Leera in Jal-Veesa. I went to Jal-Veesa, but it is empty, and marker 1 and talking to Vosh-Leera both fail. I could not find her, so I am stuck there. I carry 2 lockpicks, the Note to the Sap-Takers and 50 gold.
