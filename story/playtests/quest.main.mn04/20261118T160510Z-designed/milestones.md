## 2. Stage 30 (stage): The Sap Camp

Journal: Sesk talked as soon as I opened her cage. She drew sap for the Unbound, a cult of the tribeless whose leader is Cuts-the-Old-Knot, an Argonian woman. The sap is for a draught she brews, and the draught has something to do with "the Eye". They call the grey paste in the scars "the seal". Why drain a tree and then seal it, if they only want its sap? Vosh-Leera in Jal-Veesa should hear this.

## 3. Stage 40 (stage): The Sap Camp

Journal: Vosh-Leera says this is more than a herald can judge, and paid me a hundred gold. She is sending me to the order's handler, Nesh-Deeka, at her house in Helstrom, far to the south through deadly country. She told me to ride the rootworm: a great blind worm that swims the waters under the roots from one Hist's pool to another's. It stops at a tree's root pool, the open water among the roots at its foot, where a root-tender calls it and takes the fare. Here the tender is Weeltul, at the Hist pool in the middle of the town, and the fare is twenty-five gold. I should ask her to send me.
