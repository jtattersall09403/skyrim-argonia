I am Tarin, a thief working for the Toh-Reel-Ka via Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa. At Karsh-Naal Camp I picked the novice lock on Sesk's Cage and freed Sesk. She said the Unbound, a cult of the tribeless led by Cuts-the-Old-Knot (an Argonian woman who moves about), drain Hist trees. Jars go via the camp to her, and the sap goes into a draught for something to do with "the Eye". The grey paste in the scars is called the seal, and its contents are unknown. Vosh-Leera paid me 100 gold and sent me to the order handler Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom, far south. Weeltul at Jal-Veesa's Hist pool will call the rootworm for 25 gold, and it surfaces in Helstrom's city tree pool. The root-tender there can point to Nesh-Deeka's House. I have about 150 gold, 2 lockpicks, the Note to the Sap-Takers, Cure Poison potions and the Hist blessing. Unknown: what the Eye is, what the seal is, where Cuts-the-Old-Knot is, and whether the Teel-Fen tree lives.
