I am Tarin. I freed Sesk from the cage at Karsh-Naal Camp by picking the lock unseen, and gave her sack back. She said the Unbound, a tribeless cult led by Cuts-the-Old-Knot, drain Hist sap for a draught tied to "the Eye" and seal scars with grey paste called the seal. Jars go to the camp and then to Cuts-the-Old-Knot. Sesk left to find a boat. My objective is to tell Vosh-Leera in Jal-Veesa, but when I arrived Jal-Veesa showed nobody and the marker would not let me talk to her. I am stuck there. I carry 2 lockpicks, the note to the sap-takers and 50 gold. Unknown: what the Eye is, what is in the seal, where Cuts-the-Old-Knot is.
