I am Tarin, a thief serving Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa. I freed Sesk at Karsh-Naal Camp by picking her cage and returned her sack. She said the Unbound, a tribeless cult led by Cuts-the-Old-Knot, drain Hist sap, seal scars with grey paste called the seal, and send the sap to a draught tied to something called the Eye. I have about 150 gold, a lockpick, the Hist blessing and the Unbound note. Vosh-Leera sent me to Nesh-Deeka, the order handler in Helstrom. Next, talk to Weeltul at Jal-Veesa pool, pay 25 gold, ride the rootworm to Helstrom and find Nesh-Deeka House. Still unknown: what the Eye is, what is in the seal, where Cuts-the-Old-Knot is, and what Umbriel did.
