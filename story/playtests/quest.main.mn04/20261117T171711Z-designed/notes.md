I am Tarin, a thief freed by Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa, who serves the Toh-Reel-Ka. I carry about 150 gold, a lockpick, the Hist blessing and the Unbound note. I have not yet paid for the rootworm. At Karsh-Naal Camp I picked Sesk's cage and returned her sack. She said the Unbound, a cult of the tribeless led by Cuts-the-Old-Knot, drain Hist sap into jars, seal each scar with grey paste called the seal, and send the jars on to the leader. The sap goes into a draught for something called the Eye. Vosh-Leera paid me 100 gold and sent me to the order handler Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. Weeltul at the Jal-Veesa Hist pool will call the rootworm for 25 gold; ask her when I am ready, then find Nesh-Deeka's house in Helstrom, where the rootworm caller can point the way. Heem-Jasa in Jal-Veesa teaches alchemy and sells sap, fungus and eggshells. Unknown: what the Eye is, what the seal is made of, why they drain and then seal the trees, and where Cuts-the-Old-Knot is.
