I am Tarin, a freed thief serving Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa. I picked the lock of Sesk cage at Karsh-Naal Camp, took her sack and gave it to her. She said the Unbound, a tribeless cult led by Cuts-the-Old-Knot, drain Hist sap, seal scars with grey paste called the seal, and send jars on to him; the sap goes into a draught tied to something called the Eye. I reported to Vosh-Leera, who paid 100 gold (I should have about 150) and sent me to the handler Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. Weeltul at Jal-Veesa pool will call the rootworm for 25 gold; it rides root-waters to the Helstrom city tree. Nesh-Deeka house is there; the rootworm caller can point the way. I still have my lockpick, the Hist blessing and the Unbound note. Unknown: what the Eye is, what the seal is made of, who Cuts-the-Old-Knot really is, and Nesh-Deeka. Heem-Jasa will teach alchemy and sell goods. Sesk escaped; guards at the camp were not fought.
