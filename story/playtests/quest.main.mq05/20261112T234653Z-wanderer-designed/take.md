Short, tidy quest. I understood the goal: carry the news of the cut Hist to Helstrom. The travel was effortless and Nesh-Deeka laid out the route clearly. Ei-Tuja gave a satisfying payoff with the Mnemic Egg hook, which makes me want to know what the cutters are after. Kaska-Meen was a letdown: she just said she had nothing to add, and the Ei-Tuja disagreement was never really resolved. The wasps never showed up, so the warning about them went nowhere. It ended abruptly, with no new objective, though plenty of threads are open: the cutters, the woman with the knotted cord, and the Reed.
