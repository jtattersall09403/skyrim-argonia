I am Ka-Deelith of Toh-Reel-Ka, with the three seal-stones (Blackrose, Gemin, Kings), the order pike, gauntlets and boots, Reel-Ka armour, rapier, about 350 gold, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, sap jar, keystone, eye-setting, Bronze Key and Cuts-the-Old-Knot Draught. I gave the stones to Never-Writes-Twice, who sent me to the Kings Gate at Sunken-Ruin-23-M112 under Teku-Sul. There Cuts-the-Old-Knot told me the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar to see through the trees, that she was one, and that she wants the Eye lifted, which would kill about three in ten trees. I could not verify her claims. I chose to fight her and killed her. The gate is still shut. I must set the three stones, face the guardian alone, and at the Eye I will use it in place and not lift it. Afterward the stones go back to Never-Writes-Twice. Open questions: the guardian, whether the dreamer claim is true, and what the Xeech-Leel really intend.
