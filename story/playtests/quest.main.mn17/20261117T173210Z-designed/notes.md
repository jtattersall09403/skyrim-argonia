I am Ka-Deelith of the Toh-Reel-Ka with the order pike, gauntlets and boots, rapier, about 350 gold, 3 paralysis poisons, 2 lockpicks, and all three seal-stones. At the Sunken Ruin before the Kings Gate under Teku-Sul I met Cuts-the-Old-Knot. She says the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar to see the marsh through the trees, and that Never-Writes-Twice lied about her. I chose to believe her and she waits beyond the gate to fight the guardian with me. Still to do: set the three stones in the gate. Then decide at the Eye: use it in place, which heals trees but strengthens the order dreamers, or lift it, which ends the dreams but kills about three in ten trees. I made no promise to Cuts about the Eye. I do not know whether her account is fully true, or what the guardian is.
