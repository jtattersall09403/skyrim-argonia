Notes: I took the three seal-stones (Blackrose, Gemin, Vorannuhl) to Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom. She made me Ka-Deelith of Toh-Reel-Ka and gave me the pike (life-drinking), gauntlets and boots. At the sunken ruin under Teku-Sul I met Cuts-the-Old-Knot, who said the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in Fort Chalkmoth cellar and that Never-Writes-Twice lied and kept the key. I chose to fight and killed her; I carry her Draught. I have not verified her claims. Still carry the stones, Eye-Setting, Keystone, gold, poison, sap jar, rapier, Warden Vault Key, Bronze Key. Next: set the stones in the Kings Gate; the Eye choice remains. Using it in place heals trees but lets the order see through them; lifting it ends the dreams but kills about three in ten trees. The order says do not lift it, and return the stones to Never-Writes-Twice. Open: Fort Chalkmoth key and cellar, Ennodius Vanin the Collector.
