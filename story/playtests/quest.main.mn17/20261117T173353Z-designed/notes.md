I gave the three seal-stones to Never-Writes-Twice and became Ka-Deelith of Toh-Reel-Ka. I took the pike and have the gauntlets and boots, plus the Reel-Ka armour, rapier, about 350 gold, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, the sap jar and the Bronze Key. I went to the Kings Gate at Sunken-Ruin-23-M112, where Cuts-the-Old-Knot waited with an Unbound Rearguard. She told me the order keeps dreamers fed on sap in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar, and that the Xeech-Leel use them to watch the marsh. She was a dreamer for nine years. She wants the Eye lifted; that ends the dreams but kills about three in ten trees and their villages. I cannot verify her account. I chose to believe her. She will fight the guardian beside me beyond the gate. I have not promised her the Eye. Still to do: set the stones in the gate, face the guardian, and decide at the Eye. The Gemin tablet says lifting it cuts every tree off, so I do not mean to lift it. Never-Writes-Twice wants the stones returned to her afterwards. Still unknown: what the guardian is.
