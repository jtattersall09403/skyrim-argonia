Notes: I took the three seal-stones (Blackrose, Gemin, Vorannuhl) to Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom. She made me Ka-Deelith of the Toh-Reel-Ka and gave me the pike, gauntlets and boots. At the sunken ruin (Teku-Sul) Cuts-the-Old-Knot met me without stones. She says the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar and sees the marsh through them, and that Never-Writes-Twice kept the key and lied. I chose to believe her and not fight her. She will fight the guardian beside me past the gate. I still carry the stones, Eye-Setting, Keystone, gold, poison and the rest. Next is to set the stones in the Kings' Gate. The final choice is mine at the Eye: use it in place (heals every tree, dreams continue) or lift it (ends the dreams, about three in ten trees die, the order breaks). I promised Cuts nothing about the Eye. I could not verify her claims. Unknown: what the guardian is and what is truly in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar.
