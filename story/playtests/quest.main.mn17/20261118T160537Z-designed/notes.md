Notes: I gave the three seal-stones to Never-Writes-Twice and became Ka-Deelith of Toh-Reel-Ka, with the Ka-Deelith pike and the gauntlets and boots. At the Kings Gate under Teku-Sul, Cuts-the-Old-Knot said the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar to spy through trees, and she was one for nine years. She says healing the Eye helps the dreamers, while lifting it ends the dreams but kills about three in ten trees and villages, and she does not know which. I chose to believe her; she will fight the guardian beside me and has no promise of the Eye. I still hold the stones, gold, rapier, lockpicks, paralysis poisons and sap jar. Next step is to set the stones in the gate and decide at the Eye. I have not verified her claims. The Gemin tablet says do not lift the Eye. Never-Writes-Twice wants the stones returned to her afterwards.
