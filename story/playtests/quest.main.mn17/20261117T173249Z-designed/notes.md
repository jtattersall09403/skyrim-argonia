Notes: I am Ka-Deelith (War-Teacher) of Toh-Reel-Ka, with the Ka-Deelith pike, gauntlets and boots, Reel-Ka armour, rapier, three seal-stones, about 350 gold, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, the sap jar, keystone, eye-setting, Bronze Key and Cuts-the-Old-Knot Draught. I handed over the stones to Never-Writes-Twice, who only looked at them and let me keep them. I went to the Kings Gate at Sunken-Ruin-23-M112 and killed Cuts-the-Old-Knot, as I had chosen. Her claims, which I could not verify: the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar, and Never-Writes-Twice lied about her. She wanted the Eye lifted, which would kill about three in ten trees. Next: set the stones in the gate and use the Eye in place, never lifting it. Then take the stones back to Never-Writes-Twice and check the Fort Chalkmoth cellar. The Unbound and Collector Vanin may remain.
