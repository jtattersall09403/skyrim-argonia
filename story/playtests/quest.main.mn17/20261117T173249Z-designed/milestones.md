## 3. Stage 30 (stage): At the Kings' Gate

She says the Toh-Reel-Ka, the Xeech-Leel's secret warriors, made to stop another Umbriel, keep dreamers in the cellar of Fort Chalkmoth, fed on sap, and see the whole marsh through them. She was one of them for nine years, and Never-Writes-Twice kept the key. That is why she seals the trees she drains: a sealed tree shows the dreamers nothing. If I fight her, I take what she carries and go on alone, and giving her the Eye is closed to me. If I believe her, she fights the guardian beside me. Either way the choice at the Eye stays mine. Used where it sits, the Eye heals every tree, and the dreamers see through them all again; lifted, it parts the trees, about three in ten die, and the dreams end. I'm to choose.
