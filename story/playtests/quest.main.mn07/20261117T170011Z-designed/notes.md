I am a Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka under Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. At the Pale Harvest wreck I killed the Drowned Captain, took his key and opened the strongbox. It held a Kothringi Keystone, which I still carry along with the Pale Harvest Strongbox Key, the Old Silver Setting, The Collector note and the smuggler key. I have about 600 gold after Nesh-Deeka paid me 100. She does not know the keystone, but she says it is Kothringi. The Chime-Keepers of Toklea, on the river fork in the Tulmek Washes, keep Kothringi lore, and their elder should know it. She says the Unbound have not found the wreck, but she will not say how she knows ("we listen"). The Collector (Ennodius Vanin) and the Unbound are still hunting the Eye. I have not yet asked her about the Kothringi or the Chime-Keepers. Next step: go to Toklea and show the keystone to the elder.
