I am Tarin, thief of Nesh-Deeka house in Helstrom. I killed the drowned captain and sailor at the Pale Harvest wreck, took the key and got the Kothringi Keystone, and Nesh-Deeka paid 100 gold (about 550 total). I still have the keystone, Old Silver Setting, lockpick, Voh-Vastei armour. Nesh-Deeka says the carving is Kothringi (the silver-skinned people, dead of the Knahaten Flu) and sends me to the Chime-Keepers elder at Toklea, in the Tulmek Washes on the river fork. The Unbound lost the trail at Gideon and have not found the wreck; Nesh-Deeka knows it because "we listen". The Eye of Argonia heals every tree and lies at the Root under the sea. Unknown: seal paste, Cuts-the-Old-Knot, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel, and the Collector Ennodius Vanin, who was at the wreck first. Next: go to Toklea.
