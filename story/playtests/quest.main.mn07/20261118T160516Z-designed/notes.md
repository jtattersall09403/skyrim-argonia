I am Tarin, Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka. I cleared the Pale Harvest wreck at Hatandros Bight by killing the drowned sailor and captain, took the strongbox key and opened it. I now carry the Kothringi Keystone, on a chain. I also have the old silver setting, the Collectors note about Ennodius Vanin, my lockpick and armour, the Hist blessing, and roughly 630 gold. Nesh-Deeka paid me 100 gold. She does not know the keystone, but it is Kothringi, and she says the Chime-Keepers of Toklea, on the river fork in the Tulmek Washes, keep Kothringi lore and their elder will know. She knew the Unbound had not found the wreck and only says "We listen" if asked how. Next step: go to Toklea with the keystone. Still unknown: Cuts-the-Old-Knot, seal paste, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel, and what the keystone does.
