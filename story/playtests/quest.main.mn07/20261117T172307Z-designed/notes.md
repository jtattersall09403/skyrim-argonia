I am Tarin, thief of Nesh-Deeka house in Helstrom. I finished the Pale Harvest: killed the drowned sailor and captain at the wreck, took the strongbox key and opened the strongbox, and got a Kothringi Keystone. I gave my find to Nesh-Deeka, who was paid me 100 gold (I should have about 550). She did not know the carving but said it is Kothringi; the Kothringi were silver-skinned marsh people who died of the Knahaten Flu. The Chime-Keepers of Toklea, a town on the river fork in the Tulmek Washes, keep Kothringi lore and their elder should know it. The Eye of Argonia is the kings jewel that can heal every tree, at the Root under the sea. The Unbound lost the trail at Gideon and have not been to the wreck; Nesh-Deeka says "we listen" about how she knows. I still hold the Old Silver Setting and my gear. Unknown: seal paste, Cuts-the-Old-Knot, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel, the Collector Ennodius Vanin plans. Next: go to Toklea and ask the Chime-Keepers elder about the keystone.
