I am a Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka, working for Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. The Eye of Argonia on Ovinius Calo stall in Gideon was glass; I paid him 20 gold and learned the smugglers had the old silver setting at Gideon South Watch. I killed their boss, opened the chest with his key and took the setting and the Collector note. The Collector is Ennodius Vanin, an Imperial relic-hunter who has hunted the Eye for twenty years. The setting has the ship name Pale Harvest scratched inside, a Company freighter sunk in Hatandros Bight (east coast south of Thorn) with a Gideon dealer stock aboard. Nesh-Deeka paid me 100 gold and gave the setting back, so I still carry it, plus the Collector note. She says the Unbound asked after the glass Eye in Gideon a month ago, and she says "we listen". Next lead: the wreck in Hatandros Bight; the real Eye may have been aboard. Still unknown: whether the real Eye is there, and what the Unbound know.
