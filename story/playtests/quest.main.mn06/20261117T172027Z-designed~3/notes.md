I am Tarin, Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka. In Gideon the Eye at Ovinius Calo stall was glass (I paid 20 gold for the truth). I killed the smuggler boss and another at Gideon South Watch, used the boss key to open the chest, and took the Old Silver Setting and the Collector note (Ennodius Vanin, Imperial relic-hunter, twenty years chasing the Eye). Nesh-Deeka read the ship name inside the setting: the Pale Harvest, a Company freighter sunk in Hatandros Bight, east coast south of Thorn, with a Gideon dealer stock aboard. She paid 100 gold and returned the setting; I still carry it, the key, and the note. Nesh-Deeka says the Unbound asked about the glass Eye a month ago ("we listen"). Next lead: the wreck of the Pale Harvest. Still unknown: Cuts-the-Old-Knot, seal paste, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel; Nesh-Deeka may tell. About 430 gold.
