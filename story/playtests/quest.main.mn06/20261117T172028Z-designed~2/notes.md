She paid 100 gold, so I have about 430 gold, plus the setting, the note, lockpick, armour and Hist blessing. The Unbound asked after the glass Eye in Gideon a month ago; she says we listen. Next lead: the wreck at Hatandros Bight. Still unknown: Cuts-the-Old-Knot, seal paste, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel.
