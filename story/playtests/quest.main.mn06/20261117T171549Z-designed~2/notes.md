I am a Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka, working for Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. In Gideon the Eye of Argonia on Ovinius Calos stall was cheap glass. I persuaded him to say he bought it from smugglers at Gideon South Watch. I sneaked in, picked the chest and took the old silver setting and the Collectors note. The Collector is Ennodius Vanin, an Imperial relic-hunter who has chased the Eye for twenty years. Two Unbound asked after the glass Eye a month ago. Inside the setting is scratched the ship name Pale Harvest, a Company freighter sunk in Hatandros Bight (east coast south of Thorn) with a Gideon dealers whole stock aboard. Nesh-Deeka read it, paid me 100 gold (plus the 200 earlier) and gave the setting back, so I still carry it. She knew about the Unbound by we listen and would not say more. Next lead: the Pale Harvest wreck as a possible place for the real Eye. Unknown: whether the Eye is really there, and what the Collector will do.
