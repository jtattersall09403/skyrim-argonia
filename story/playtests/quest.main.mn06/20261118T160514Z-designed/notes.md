I am Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka under Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. The Eye on Ovinius Calo stall in Gideon was glass; I paid him 20 gold and he said smugglers sold it. I picked their chest at Gideon South Watch and took the old silver setting and the Collectors note. The Collector, Ennodius Vanin, is an Imperial relic-hunter who has chased the Eye for twenty years. The setting has the name Pale Harvest scratched inside, a Company freighter sunk in Hatandros Bight (east coast south of Thorn) with a Gideon dealers whole stock. Nesh-Deeka paid me 100 gold and gave the setting back, so I have it, the note, my armour, a lockpick and about 430 gold. She knew two Unbound had asked after the glass Eye in Gideon a month ago, and would not say how (I did not ask). Still unknown: the seal, what guards the Root, who Cuts-the-Old-Knot is, the Xeech-Leel, Umbriel. Next: the Pale Harvest wreck at Hatandros Bight.
