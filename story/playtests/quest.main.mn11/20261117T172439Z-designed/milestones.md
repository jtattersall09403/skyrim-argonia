## 1. Stage 10 (start): The Grey Tooth

Journal: Never-Writes-Twice read the parchment map from Vicrium and named the tooth-shaped crag: the Grey Tooth, the seamark of Skeith Isle. The hired men of the Collector, Ennodius Vanin, an Imperial relic-hunter who hunts the Eye too, are camped at the foot of its goat-path and dig in a small cave on its north face. The order hunts the Eye of Argonia, the old kings' jewel that can heal every sick Hist, which lies at the Root, the place under the sea where the roots of every Hist meet. The Unbound, the tribeless cult of Cuts-the-Old-Knot that drains Hist sap, want it first. I'm to find out what they found in the Grey Tooth Cave. I may fight them or slip past them.
