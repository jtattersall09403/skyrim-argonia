I am Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker for Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. At the Grey Tooth cave I found the niche empty with the Collector Vanin rubbing; it sent me to the wreck of the Stormwidow, where I took the kings marker stone from the ballast crate, and delivered it to Never-Writes-Twice for 100 gold (now about 1050). The stone shows seven drowned towers off Lilmoth, one cut deeper; the divers of Bell Station (islet east of Lilmoth, boat from Lilmoth) will know which. That is the order hunt for the Eye of Argonia at the Root. I also carry the Unbound seal recipe (grey caps and dried root paste that numbs and closes a tree scar, signed C.), the rapier, lockpicks, 3 Grey Seal, Pale Harvest key, disc, keystone, eye-setting. Tah-Leesh, a hurt Unbound scout, said Cuts-the-Old-Knot tells the order to ask what moans in the cellar of Fort Chalkmoth (Archwarden fort, Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara). Never-Writes-Twice dismissed it. Next: Bell Station, or Fort Chalkmoth. Still unknown: what the Root and Eye truly are, what moans in the cellar, how the order listens, the Collector Vanin plans.
