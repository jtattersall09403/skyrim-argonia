Notes: I am a Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. I carry 100 gold; the marker stone now belongs to Never-Writes-Twice in Nesh-Deeka's House, Helstrom. The stone shows seven drowned towers off Lilmoth, one cut deeper; the divers of Bell Station (islet east of Lilmoth, boat from Lilmoth) will know which. Two leads: Bell Station for the hunt, or Fort Chalkmoth (Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara) to learn what moans in its cellar; Cuts-the-Old-Knot says to ask that of the order, and Never-Writes-Twice brushed it off. The Unbound seal recipe is grey caps and dried root ground to a numbing paste; Tah-Leesh, a hurt Unbound scout, is left at the Stormwidow wreck. Still unknown: which tower, what moans in the cellar, and how Nesh-Deeka knows the Unbound so well.
