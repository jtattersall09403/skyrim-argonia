I am Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker, working for Nesh-Deeka under Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom. Quest The Grey Tooth done: the Collector Vanins rubbing at the Grey Tooth cave led me to the wreck of the Stormwidow on Skeith Isle. I killed three Unbound scouts there and took the kings marker stone, which shows seven towers with one cut deeper. I gave the stone to Never-Writes-Twice and she paid 100 gold. The towers are the drowned spires off Lilmoth; the divers of Bell Station, an islet east of Lilmoth reached by boat from Lilmoth, will know which one. Next step: go to Bell Station. The order hunts the Eye of Argonia at the Root, where all Hist roots meet. The Unbound (Cuts-the-Old-Knot) seal tree scars with a paste of grey caps and dried root after drawing sap; I carry the Recipe for the Seal and 3 Unbound Seals, plus the rapier, about 1050 gold, lockpicks, 3 Grey Seals, Pale Harvest key, disc, keystone and eye-setting. Tah-Leesh, a wounded Unbound scout left alive at the wreck, said Cuts-the-Old-Knot tells the order to ask what moans in the cellar of Fort Chalkmoth (Archwarden fort, Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara). Never-Writes-Twice calls it nothing, but I mean to check it myself. Still unknown: what the Root and Eye really are, what moans in the cellar, and the Collector, who is hunting the Eye too.
