Notes: The Grey Tooth is done. The Grey Tooth cave niche was empty. The Collector (Vanin) rubbing sent me to the wreck of the Stormwidow on Skeith Isle SW beach, where I took the Kings Marker Stone from a ballast crate ahead of Unbound scouts. I read their seal recipe: grey caps plus dried root ground into a numbing paste, a thumbs weight per tree. Wounded scout Tah-Leesh said Cuts-the-Old-Knot tells servants of the Toh-Reel-Ka to ask what moans in the cellar of Fort Chalkmoth (Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara). I gave the stone to Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom; she said the seven towers are drowned spires off Lilmoth in Benthic Yobesh, and the divers of Bell Station (islet east of Lilmoth, boat from Lilmoth) know which is the marked one. She paid 100 gold and dismissed the Chalkmoth rumour as wind, which I distrust, given Nesh-Deeka always knows too much about the Unbound. I no longer hold the stone. Next: Bell Station for the hunt, or Fort Chalkmoth cellar first. I am Toh-Wo.
