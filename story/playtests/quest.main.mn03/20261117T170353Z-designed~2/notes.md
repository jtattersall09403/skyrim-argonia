I am Tarin, a thief working for Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa, who serves the Toh-Reel-Ka. Hists are sickening. I drank Jal-Veesa sap and saw Teel-Fen Weir, south of Jal-Veesa. There Heem-Jasa minds a young Hist whose scar was sealed with grey paste. Unbound Sap-Takers drain trees and seal scars. I took their note from the reed hide without a fight. It says to bring jars to Karsh-Naal Camp, a Naga camp in the marsh east of the weir. I told Heem-Jasa her tree will live, the comforting choice. She gave me three Cure Poison potions but will not teach or sell to me. I have about 50 gold, a lockpick, the Hist blessing (resist poison), the note and the potions. Next: go to Karsh-Naal Camp. Still unknown: who leads the Sap-Takers, what the paste is, and what Umbriel did. Weeltul at Jal-Veesa has untried topics.
