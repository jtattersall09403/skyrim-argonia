I am Tarin, a thief freed by Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa, who serves the Toh-Reel-Ka. I drank Jal-Veesa sap and saw Teel-Fen Weir. Heem-Jasa, minder of the young Hist there, found its scar sealed with grey paste. I killed the three Unbound Sap-Takers at the reed hide and took their note: their master orders jars brought to Karsh-Naal Camp and every scar sealed. I told Heem-Jasa honestly her tree may die; she thanked me and will teach alchemy and sell sap, fungus and eggshells. I carry the note, 0 gold, a lockpick and the Hist blessing (resist poison). Next: Karsh-Naal Camp, the Naga camp in the marsh east of the weir. Unknown: who the Unbound master is, why Hists are drained, what the grey paste is, and what Umbriel did.
