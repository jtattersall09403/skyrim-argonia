I am Tarin, working for Vosh-Leera of the Toh-Reel-Ka. I drank Hist sap and saw Teel-Fen Weir, south of Jal-Veesa. Heem-Jasa, its tree-minder, had her young tree drained and its scar sealed with grey paste. The Unbound Sap-Takers, three of them, used a reed hide west of the weir. I took their note unseen. It tells the crew to bring jars to Karsh-Naal Camp, a Naga camp east of the weir, and to seal every scar behind them. I told Heem-Jasa honestly her tree may die. She will now teach me alchemy and sell sap, fungus and eggshells. I have about 50 gold. I have not reported back to Vosh-Leera. I do not know who the crew master is, what the paste is, or why they want sap. Next: go to Karsh-Naal Camp, and report to Vosh-Leera.
