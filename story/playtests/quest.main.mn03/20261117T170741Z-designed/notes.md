I am Tarin, a thief freed by Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa, who works for the Toh-Reel-Ka. I drank Jal-Veesa sap and saw Teel-Fen Weir. There I met Heem-Jasa, tree-minder of the young Hist grown from Jal-Veesa roots. Its scar had been sealed with grey paste by the Unbound sap-takers. I killed the three at the reed hide and took their note. It orders jars taken to Karsh-Naal Camp and every scar sealed behind them. I comforted Heem-Jasa, saying her tree will live. She gave me three Cure Poison potions but will not teach or sell to me now. I carry about 50 gold, a lockpick, the Hist blessing (resist poison), the note and the potions. Next I go to Karsh-Naal Camp, a Naga camp in the marsh east of the weir. Still unknown: who leads the Unbound, why they want the sap, what the grey paste is, and what Umbriel did. Weeltul has untried topics.
