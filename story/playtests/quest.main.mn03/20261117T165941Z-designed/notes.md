Notes: I work (unknowingly at first) for Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa, who serves the Toh-Reel-Ka. I drank Hist sap and saw Teel-Fen Weir, south of Jal-Veesa. Heem-Jasa, minder of its young Hist, showed me the scar sealed with grey paste three nights ago. Strangers (Unbound Sap-Takers, three) work from a reed hide west of the weir. I took the Note to the Sap-Takers without a fight: their master orders them to bring jars to Karsh-Naal Camp and seal every scar. So trees are being drained and sealed, which is probably why they sicken. I told Heem-Jasa the truth that her tree may die, so she will teach me alchemy and sell sap, swamp fungus and eggshells. I carry 50 gold, two lockpicks and the note. Unknown: who the master is, where Karsh-Naal Camp is, why they want sap. Next: ask Heem-Jasa about Karsh-Naal Camp and Umbriel, report to Vosh-Leera, then go to the camp.
