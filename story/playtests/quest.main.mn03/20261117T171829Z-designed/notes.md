I am Tarin, a thief freed by Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa, who works for the Toh-Reel-Ka. I drank Jal-Veesa sap in her house and saw Teel-Fen Weir, a Sedge-Wardens camp south of town. Heem-Jasa minds the young Hist there. Its scar was drained and sealed with grey paste three nights ago. Three Unbound Sap-Takers keep sap jars at a reed hide west of the weir. I took their note unseen. It orders jars brought to Karsh-Naal Camp by the next dark moon, stacked by the longhouse, for a Naga to pay for them, and says to seal every scar behind them. It is signed K. I told Heem-Jasa honestly that her tree may die. She thanked me and will teach me alchemy and sell me sap, swamp fungus and eggshells. I have the note, 50 gold, a lockpick and the Hist blessing. Next is Karsh-Naal Camp, the Naga camp in the marsh east of the weir. I do not know who K. or the Naga is, what the grey paste is, why they want the sap, or what Umbriel did beyond the 4E 48 story. Weeltul still has untried topics, and Heem-Jasa has a topic on Umbriel and one on Karsh-Naal Camp.
