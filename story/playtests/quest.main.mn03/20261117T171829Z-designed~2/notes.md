I am Tarin, a thief freed by Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa, who works for the Toh-Reel-Ka. I drank Jal-Veesa sap at her house and saw Teel-Fen Weir, a Sedge-Wardens camp south of town. Heem-Jasa, minder of its young Hist, showed me the scar sealed with grey paste three nights ago. I crept to the reed hide and took the Note to the Sap-Takers: the master of the Unbound crew orders jars brought to Karsh-Naal Camp and every scar sealed behind them. I comforted Heem-Jasa by saying her tree will live, a lie I did not know to be true. She gave me three Cure Poison potions but will not teach or sell to me. I carry about 50 gold, 2 lockpicks, the Hist blessing and the potions. Unknown: who leads the Unbound, what the grey paste is, why sap is wanted, and whether the tree lives. Next lead is Karsh-Naal Camp, a tribeless Naga camp in the marsh east of the weir, which is dangerous. Weeltul and Vosh-Leera have more to tell.
