I am Tarin, freed thief working for Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa (for the Toh-Reel-Ka). I drank Jal-Veesa sap and saw Teel-Fen Weir, south of town. Heem-Jasa minds its young Hist, whose scar was drained and sealed with grey paste three nights ago. Three Unbound Sap-Takers keep a reed hide west of the weir; I took their note, which orders jars brought to Karsh-Naal Camp and every scar sealed. That is likely why the Hists sicken. I told Heem-Jasa honestly her tree may die; she will now teach me alchemy and sell sap, fungus and eggshells. I carry the note, the Hist blessing (poison resist) and a lockpick, with 0 gold. I did not fight the Sap-Takers, who are still at the hide. Next: go to Karsh-Naal Camp, a Naga camp in the marsh east of the weir. Still unknown: who the master is, why they want the sap, what the grey paste is, and what Umbriel did; Heem-Jasa and Weeltul have Umbriel topics I have not tried.
