Short and clear. I killed a crocodile for Okan-Zish, who then admitted his crew had baited it on purpose to scare traders off a rival. I chose to give the bucket to Sells-Dear, got 60 gold, and the crew lost their trade. I understood the setup and the three NPCs, and the moral choice (blackmail payout or rat them out) was a neat twist on a simple kill quest. It was a little harsh that the crew is ruined, and I would have liked to see the consequences, such as Okan-Zish reacting afterwards. The fight was a single command with no tension. The talk topics were fairly generic, and the old-style fast travel made it quick. One oddity: the crocodile was already hostile and listed as present before I accepted the quest. I would play more if there were a larger story, but nothing further started.
