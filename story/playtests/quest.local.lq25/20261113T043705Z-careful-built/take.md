A short, clear quest. I killed a crocodile for a rafter. He then confessed his crew had faked a river monster to scare traders away from a rival, and had accidentally drawn a real one. I had to choose between taking hush money and giving the evidence to the rival captain. I chose the honest route and got 60 gold, but it ruined the crew, which felt a bit harsh. I liked the twist and the real moral choice, and the NPCs had distinct voices. The weak points: the game never told me about my surroundings, so I could only follow people by name, and the world felt thin. Fighting was a single command and had no tension. The "look" command gave very little information. I would play more if the story continued, but nothing else is active in my journal.
