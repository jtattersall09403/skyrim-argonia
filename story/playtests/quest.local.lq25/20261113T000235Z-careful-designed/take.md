Short and easy to follow. I killed a crocodile, reported it, and learned the raft crew had caused the problem with their bait. I then had a bribe-or-betray choice. I liked that choice: both sides were sympathetic, and the journal laid out the stakes clearly. One thing I did not expect: I only meant to hear Wuleen-Taza out, but asking about the offal made me take her 60 gold and ended the quest. I would have wanted a clearer accept/refuse step, and a chance to talk to Sells-Dear first. The world felt thin, with no markers and an empty starting journal, but the people had personality and I would like to know what happens next at the landing. I stopped because nothing active is left in the journal.
