Short, clear quest. A rafter asked me to kill a crocodile, then admitted the crew had caused it by dumping offal to scare traders off a rival boat. I exposed them to Sells-Dear with the bucket and got 60 gold. I understood the story and the people well, and the twist was fun. The choice between selling out and keeping quiet was a nice touch. Weak points: many lines show "[not yet written; the gist]" placeholders, the crocodile fight was a one-word win, and the world is very small. The ending leaves the rafters unpunished, and I would like to see what happens to them.
