Short and clear. I killed a crocodile, and the quest turned into a moral choice: take hush money from the rafters who caused the danger, or hand their bait bucket to the rival boat captain. I chose the captain. I understood who everyone was and why they wanted what they wanted. The outcome felt harsh: two people lost their livelihood and I got 60 gold, and I would have liked a middle option. The text-only play worked well and the journal was easy to follow. I did not hear Wuleen-Taza out, so I never saw her price. The world felt thin, with no other leads after this, but I would like to see what the wider Black Marsh has to offer.
