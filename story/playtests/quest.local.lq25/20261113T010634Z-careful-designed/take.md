Short, clear quest. I killed the crocodile, learned the rafters had faked a monster by dumping offal, and chose to give the bucket to Sells-Dear instead of taking hush money. I got 60 gold, and the crew lost their trade. The story was easy to follow and the choice had real weight. The twist that the crew caused the monster themselves was a nice reveal. It felt a bit thin: the fight was one command, and I never saw the consequences for the crew. I also never heard Wuleen-Taza or Sells-Dear explain their side. A small hiccup: I had to walk to the bucket before I could take it, and the map did not name Sells-Dear until I needed her. I would play more if there were more.
