Kings Gate done. Stone marked Teku-Sul Spire (via Meer-Tasi at Bell Station). Ghost Sul-Xeeth says three seal-stones open the gate: Blackrose Prison warden vault, Gemin flooded vaults, Vorannuhl deepest hall. Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom made me Reel-Ka of the Toh-Reel-Ka and gave a cuirass, helm, 300 gold, free beds in Archwarden forts. Map markers set for all three sites; Blackrose is easiest, Vorannuhl worst. Still open: the Fort Chalkmoth cellar errand from Tah-Leesh, which she dismissed, and Vanin digging ahead of us. I carry the Unbound recipe, lockpicks x2, 3 paralysis poisons, a rapier, and about 1350 gold. I did not buy waterbreathing and did not need it for the gate visit.
