I am Toh-Wo, a Reel-Ka (Warrior) of the order under Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom. Teku-Sul Spire, near Lilmoth, holds the great gate to the Root and the Eye; it opens only with three seal-stones set: Blackrose Prison (warden vault, least dangerous), Gemin flooded vaults, Vorannuhl deepest hall (worst). All three are marked on the map. The Unbound are racing us for them. I carry the rapier, about 1350 gold, lockpicks, 3 Unbound Seals, Recipe for the Seal, 3 Grey Seals, Pale Harvest key, disc, keystone, eye-setting, Reel-Ka cuirass and helm, and have a free bed in every Archwarden fort. Still unknown: what the Root and Eye truly are, what moans in the cellar of Fort Chalkmoth (Tah-Leesh hinted at it), and who the Collector is. Next: Blackrose first, sneaking.
