Journal: Before the great gate in Teku-Sul stands the ghost of a priest of the old kings, Sul-Xeeth. One lies in the warden's vault at Blackrose Prison, one in the flooded vaults of Gemin, one in the deepest hall of Vorannuhl. I'm to tell Never-Writes-Twice in Nesh-Deeka's House in Helstrom.
