## 2. Stage 20 (stage): The Kings' Gate

Journal: The steward, Meer-Tasi, knew the cut tower at once: Teku-Sul Spire, the drowned tower nearest Lilmoth, whose great gate her divers have never forced. The dive is long. I need a Hist's blessing, a spell or potion of waterbreathing (she sells them) or an enchantment, unless I am Argonian; inside the tower there is air. I'm to dive to Teku-Sul and find the great gate. The drowned priests' attendants walk its east halls; I may fight them or slip past.

## 3. Stage 30 (stage): The Kings' Gate

Journal: Before the great gate in Teku-Sul stands the ghost of a priest of the old kings, Sul-Xeeth. The gate has three empty sockets, one for each seal-stone the last king's priests cut, and only all three open it. One lies in the warden's vault at Blackrose Prison, one in the flooded vaults of Gemin, one in the deepest hall of Vorannuhl. I'm to tell Never-Writes-Twice in Nesh-Deeka's House in Helstrom.
