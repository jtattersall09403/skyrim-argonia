Notes: Meer-Tasi at Bell Station said the cut tower is Teku-Sul Spire; its great gate has three sockets. Ghost Sul-Xeeth said the three seal-stones are at Blackrose Prison (warden vault), the flooded vaults of Gemin, and the deepest hall of Vorannuhl; all three needed. I reported to Never-Writes-Twice, who made me Reel-Ka with Reel-Ka cuirass and helm, 300 gold (about 1350 total), free beds in Archwarden forts. Map markers for all three places are set; Blackrose is easiest, Vorannuhl worst. The order wants the stones before the Unbound. I still carry the rapier, lockpicks, 3 Grey Seals, 3 Unbound Seals, Recipe for the Seal, Pale Harvest key, disc, keystone, eye-setting. Unknown: what the Root and Eye really are, what moans in Fort Chalkmoth cellar (my own errand, still open), and who the Collector is.
