Its great gate has three sockets; ghost Sul-Xeeth says all three seal-stones are needed: one in the warden vault of Blackrose Prison, one in the flooded vaults of Gemin, one in the deepest hall of Vorannuhl (all marked on the map). Never-Writes-Twice made me Reel-Ka, gave Reel-Ka cuirass and helm, 300 gold, free beds in Archwarden forts, and ordered me to take all three stones before the Unbound (Blackrose easiest, Vorannuhl worst). I carry the rapier, about 1350 gold, lockpicks, 3 Grey Seals, 3 Unbound Seals, the Recipe for the Seal, Pale Harvest key, disc, keystone, eye-setting.
