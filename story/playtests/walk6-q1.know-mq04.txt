What you know so far, from your journal. From "No Name on the Work Roll": I woke in the hold of a ship, moored at a pier west of Lilmoth in Black Marsh, among a gang of Argonians I do not know. I'm to go up the stairs and out of the door to the pier, and speak to the man who stands there. Buys-The-Months, the broker, holds his gang's Owing, the months of work they owe. I am on no line of his roll, so he will not keep me. He says Nesh-Deeka, a guest at Salts-The-Catch's house south of the second big ship off Lilmoth's dock, pays for work. I'm to find her there, east along the shore. A woman with a knotted cord killed Nesh-Deeka's courier at the gang's camp the night it burned, took his satchel and let me live. Nesh-Deeka serves the Veiled Reed, the state's quiet service, and wants a hand whose name is on no roll. I'm to hear her out and say whether I will work for the Reed. I took work with the Veiled Reed, and Nesh-Deeka put fifty gold in my hand. She has told me the first work the Reed wants of me. Why the woman with the knotted cord let me live, I still do not know. From "The Cutters' Jar": Nesh-Deeka gave me the Veiled Reed's first work. Dives-Without-A-Rope, one of the broker's gang, carried off a jar of sap that the cutters dropped at the burnt camp. Nesh-Deeka wants it to learn what the cutters wanted. I'm to ask Dives-Without-A-Rope for the jar in the ship's hold at the pier west of Lilmoth. I talked Dives-Without-A-Rope round. She gave me the key to her lockbox in the ship's hold. She asked me in return to find who cut her tree. I'm to take the jar from the lockbox and bring it to Nesh-Deeka. I tasted the cutters' sap in Salts-The-Catch's house. Nesh-Deeka wrote down what I said under it: a ship's hold with roots through its deck, a door that was never built, a dry voice. She gave me the page and twenty-five gold. She has heard that the Hist of Low-Reed Village, east of Lilmoth, has turned its face. You are standing in Salts-The-Catch's house with Nesh-Deeka.
