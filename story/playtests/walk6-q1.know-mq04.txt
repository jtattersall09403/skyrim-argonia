What you know so far, from your journal. From "No Name on the Work Roll": I woke in the hold of a ship at a pier west of Lilmoth, in Black Marsh, among a gang of Argonians I do not know. Buys-The-Months, the broker who holds the gang's Owing, the months of work they owe, has no line for me on his roll. I owe him nothing, so he will not keep me. He says a woman called Nesh-Deeka, a guest at Salts-The-Catch's house in Lilmoth, pays for work from anyone off his ship. Nesh-Deeka serves the Veiled Reed, the ruling state's quiet service. The gang carried me off the bank the night their camp burned, she says, when a woman with a knotted cord killed her courier, took his satchel and let me live. She wants a hand nobody can claim. I took work with the Veiled Reed. Nesh-Deeka put fifty gold in my hand and told me what the Reed wants first. Why the woman with the knotted cord let me live, nobody has told me yet. From "The Cutters' Jar": Dives-Without-A-Rope, one of the broker's gang, carried aboard a jar of sap the cutters dropped at the burnt camp. I talked her round, and she gave me the key to her lockbox in the ship's hold. I put a drop of the cutters' sap on my tongue at Nesh-Deeka's table. She wrote down what I said while it held me: the ship's hold, roots grown up through its deck, a door nobody built, a dry voice without words. She cannot say what it means. She has word that the Hist of Low-Reed Village, east of Lilmoth, has turned its face. You are standing in Salts-The-Catch's house with Nesh-Deeka.
