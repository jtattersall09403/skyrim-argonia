Short but sharp. The Owing setup (debt-work gone rotten, Hist-colour fading) is good Black Marsh worldbuilding, and I understood who everyone was. The moral design stood out: every route has a cost, and my "clever" choice sent a season of rowing onto a dozen innocent hands who will never know. Never-Sold said she would have picked the keeper, which stung. I wish I had been warned the leaf swap would shift the debt onto the keeper before I committed. The persuade option failed with no hint of what would have worked. Telling Rows-For-Another she was free had no dialogue, which felt flat. Hulm-Tei was the most interesting character, a keeper who is himself owing. The quest ended with no hook to more work, only "come back later". I would want to see what Never-Sold has next, and whether the hands ever learn.
