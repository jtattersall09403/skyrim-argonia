Short, tight quest. I liked the Owing idea: a Black Marsh answer to prisons that rots into debt-slavery in the south. It gave me a real moral problem. The three routes each carried a cost, and the twist that freeing Rows-For-Another dumps her eleven years onto Hulm-Tei, himself born owing, landed well. Never-Sold admitting she hid it from me fit her. I was annoyed that persuasion failed on both Hulm-Tei and the fetcher with no hint of what would have worked, and that I had 100 gold when the fetcher wanted 150. The "seen" or "unseen" choice on the lock and the line "you pay a fine or fight, the game goes no further" felt like a seam. The bystanders were generic and told me nothing. I understood who everyone was and I want to know what happens to Hulm-Tei, but the ending left me guilty and with no further task in the journal.
