A short, clear quest. I understood it: pay the steward, free Rows-For-Another, return the page. Dialogue was well written and the debt-custom theme was interesting, with the true lines adding up to a wrong sum. I failed the persuasion because my Speechcraft was 15 against 25, so I paid the gold. That meant the cheaper route was closed to me, and nothing told me that beforehand. It ended quickly, and I would like to know more about the Chainbreakers. I did not like that Hulm-Tei and the estate go unchallenged, and that the quest says so itself.
