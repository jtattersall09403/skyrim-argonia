Short, clear quest. I understood who everyone was: Never-Sold keeps the Chainbreakers house, Rows-For-Another is held by a debt, and Hulm-Tei is the steward with his book. Hulm-Tei was the best written. He insists every line is true and never calls it slavery. Never-Sold answers with "true lines can add up to a wrong sum." I liked that the moral weight came from the dialogue and not from a fight. Persuading him let me keep the 100 gold, which felt like a reward for paying attention. Some things were odd. Rows-For-Another said she would just slip away, but then accepted the buy-out with no fuss, and I never felt the choice between the two ways. The "Speechcraft" and "Persuade" duplicate options were confusing. Nothing was said about the estate treating the next person held any better, and I would like to know what happens there. It is a small, polite start, and I would play on.
