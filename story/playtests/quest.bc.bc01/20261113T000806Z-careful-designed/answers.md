1. I'm not sure what comes next. The last thing I did was hand the page back to Never-Sold and check the journal, and I don't remember a clear new objective. If the journal is empty, I'd go back and talk to Never-Sold or Rows-For-Another to see whether anything new opens up. Otherwise the Chainbreakers' wider work, such as helping the next person held by a debt, seems like the natural thread.

2. Informally, I work for Never-Sold and the Chainbreakers house. She gave me the task, and I did it for her: I settled Rows-For-Another's Owing and returned the page. I'm not paid a wage that I know of, though I kept the 100 gold. I went along because I sympathised with Rows-For-Another and liked Never-Sold's stance. I haven't been told it's a formal job.

3. The most confusing moment was Rows-For-Another's change of stance. She first said she'd simply slip away, then accepted the buy-out with no fuss, so I never felt that the choice mattered. The duplicate "Speechcraft" and "Persuade" options were also muddled. I wasn't sure which one actually ran the check, or whether picking dialogue number 2 and using the persuade command were the same thing.

4. I'd tell the writer that the dialogue is the strength. Hulm-Tei's insistence that every line in his book is true, paired with "true lines can add up to a wrong sum," carried real moral weight without a fight. I'd ask them to make the choice between the two ways of settling the debt feel like it has consequences, and to clean up the duplicate menu options. I'd also like the end of the quest to say clearly what happens next, and whether the estate changes at all, so I know where to go afterward.
