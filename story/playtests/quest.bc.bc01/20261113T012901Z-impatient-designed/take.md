Short and easy to follow: one quest, markers took me straight through it. I understood the setup (Owings are debts that hold people, Chainbreakers free them), and Rows-For-Another and Never-Sold were clear enough as characters. Much of the dialogue was unwritten placeholder text ("not yet written; the gist"), which broke immersion, though the gists were readable. I liked that there were several routes (speech, lockpick, bribe, kill). I had too little Speech and no picks, and 150 gold was just beyond my 100, so I killed the fetcher. That was one click, with no weight to it, though Never-Sold did call me out for it, which was a nice touch. Things that made no sense: the false leaf I was given was never used, and the Hulm-Tei key and count-box route went untouched. I did not feel connected to the Black Marsh yet. It was a thin slice, but I would play on to see what the Chainbreakers do next.
