Short but engaging. The Owing setup is original and I understood it fast: Argonian debt-work gone bad, Hist and greying as the stakes, three legs to break. Never-Sold and Rows-For-Another felt like real people. The moral weight was the best part: I took the leaf and learned her eleven years land on Hulm-Tei, who is himself born owing. Never-Sold admitting she hid that from me stung nicely. Complaints: the persuade option with Hulm-Tei failed and gave no clear way to meet his hint about mending the count. The "face Hulm-Tei" path in the journal had no dialogue option I could find. The seen/unseen choice for theft felt odd, and farm bystanders (Okeemu, Heshra, Pip) were generic filler. I did not get to try the fetcher route. I do want more work from Never-Sold, and I want to know what happens to Hulm-Tei.
