1. I don't think I'm meant to do anything right now. My journal is empty and nothing new started after I talked to Never-Sold. The natural next step would be to go back to her, or to the Chainbreakers generally, and ask for more work. I'd also like to find out how she knew what a keeper's leaf looks like. The game hasn't pointed me at that, so it's my own curiosity, not an objective.

2. I work for Never-Sold and the Chainbreakers, at least informally. She gave me the job of freeing Rows-For-Another, and I did it and reported back. I'm not sure I trust her, since she knew the debt would fall on someone else and let me find that out myself. I stay with her because she is the only person offering me work, and I believe in what the Chainbreakers say they are trying to do.

3. The most confusing moment was the "seen/unseen" prompt on the lockpick and the take actions. It seemed to let me choose whether I got caught, so I wasn't sure what I was actually risking. The second most confusing thing was not knowing, after the leaf was returned, who ended up paying the debt. The hands lose a season each and the game never made that visible to me.

4. I'd tell the writer the central dilemma is good. Three routes, none of them clean, and Never-Sold admitting she knew is a strong moment. Here is what to fix:
   - Give the bystanders something real to say, because Okeemu, Heshra and Pip only showed generic menus.
   - Let Rows-For-Another react once she's free, because that reaction is the payoff of the whole quest.
   - Make the seen/unseen choice a real risk, not a decision the player makes.
   - Give the player a lead or an objective after the ending, so the journal isn't empty.
   - Show the consequences for the hands, so the cost I accepted is felt and not just stated.
