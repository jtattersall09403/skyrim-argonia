The setup was strong: a quiet, morally grey quest about debt-bondage with three clear routes. I understood who everyone was. Persuasion failed on a flat check. Pickpocketing the key and swapping leaves worked cleanly, and then the game let me hand Hulm-Tei his own leaf. That choice hurt a dozen nameless hands, which Never-Sold said she would not have chosen, and that stung in a good way. Her admission that she withheld what she knew was an interesting reveal. Some things made no sense: I never told Rows-For-Another she is free, and the bystanders had generic dialogue. Nothing new started after the quest, so I stopped. I would like to know who Never-Sold really is and where the Chainbreakers go next.
