Short and interesting. I understood the setup: Never-Sold wants a boat-hand freed from a debt that never ends, and I could do it by stealth, speech, gold or a fight. My Speech was too low, so I picked the lock. The twist is that the keeper Hulm-Tei, who seemed honest, ends up carrying the eleven years of debt, because I left his leaf behind. Never-Sold knew this and did not tell me. That moral sting was the best part, and I would like to see what happens to him. Weaknesses: nearly every line of dialogue was a visible "[not yet written; the gist:]" placeholder, so the voices were flat. Never-Sold also wanted me to pick between a jailer and a boat-hand without telling me, which felt manipulative but fitting. The quest ended quickly and no new objective appeared, though she says to come back for more work. I would play on.
