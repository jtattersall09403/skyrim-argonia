Short and satisfying. I took the sneaky route: lifted the keeper's key, opened the box, swapped the leaf, and got paid. It played like a small Skyrim thieves guild job, which I liked. The setup talk was a big wall of text I skimmed, but the quest was clear and the journal and markers guided me well. The twist about the keeper's leaf was interesting, but I never went back to Rows-For-Another, so I do not know how she took it. The theft was too easy: unseen worked first try with no stealth challenge, and the Adept lock never came into play. I did not fully get the Owing and Hist lore, and I never asked. I would play more if Never-Sold had another job, but the journal shows nothing active now.
