Short and clear. One quest, Four Months for a Boat, with three ways to solve it. I took the lockpick route, and it worked easily. The quest text was easy to follow, and the markers and "go" travel kept it quick. The moral wrinkle was the best part: freeing Rows-For-Another dumps her eleven years onto Hulm-Tei, who seemed more a bookkeeper under pressure than a villain. I would have liked a clearer sign of that cost before I took the leaf. The option to hand back his leaf with the first price never showed up in dialogue, which was confusing. The "seen/unseen" prompt on theft felt odd, since I was just asked to choose. The world and terms (Owing, leaf, held going grey, Hist) were only partly explained, but I got the gist. I would like to see what more work for the Chainbreakers looks like. The journal showed no active objective afterward, so I stopped.
