## 2. Stage 20 (stage): The Handler

Journal: Nesh-Deeka heard my report. The Eye is the Eye of Argonia, the old priest-kings' jewel of Hist amber. It lies at the Root, where every tree's roots meet under the sea, behind a gate the old kings sealed, and no living diver has found it. I said the draught must be to kill whatever guards the Root, and she agreed. To prove me, she wants a jar of stolen sap from Keel-Raa, an Unbound go-between lodging at the Boatmen's Lodge with two hired men. I can lift it from his pack on the crates behind them while they are not looking, buy it for a hundred gold, or fight the three of them for it, which the levy will not count as a crime. I'm to bring her the jar.
