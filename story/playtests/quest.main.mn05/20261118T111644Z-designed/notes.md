I am Tarin, a freed thief. I am now Voh-Vastei, an apprentice of the Toh-Reel-Ka, under Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. I killed Keel-Raa, an Unbound go-between, at the Boatmens Lodge, took his jar of sap and gave it to Nesh-Deeka. The levy did not count it a crime. I carry the Voh-Vastei armour (cuirass, boots, gauntlets, helm), about 350 gold, my lockpick, the Hist blessing and the Unbound note. The Eye of Argonia is the priest-kings jewel of Hist amber. It lies at the Root under the sea, behind a gate the last king sealed, and no living diver has found it. Cuts-the-Old-Knot wants it and means to kill whatever guards the Root. The order wants to find the Root first and use the Eye to heal the trees. Next step: ride the rootworm from Helstrom city tree to Gideon, with Cheeval-Xu calling it. There I should find the Eye of Argonia for sale in the market and learn where it came from, since most are glass. I have not yet asked Nesh-Deeka about the Xeech-Leel, Umbriel, the order history or ranks. I still do not know what the seal is made of, what guards the Root, or who Cuts-the-Old-Knot really is. Heem-Jasa teaches alchemy and sells goods. The order traders will now deal with me.
