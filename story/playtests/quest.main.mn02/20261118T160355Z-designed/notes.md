I am Wanderer, a hunter, freed from Dralin Uveran by Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa. I fetched Sithis beard from Reed-Sill Weir without a fight, gave it to Weeltul the tree-minder, and got the Hist blessing (resist poison). Jal-Veesa Hist is sick: dry bark, closed scar, cooling egg-pool; cause unknown, and trees along the river are falling sick. Vosh-Leera serves the Toh-Reel-Ka, the Secret Warriors of the Xeech-Leel, who were founded after Umbriel to stop any tree turning on the marsh. They use kinless strangers like me as cheap spies. I have 50 gold and a lockpick. She has a second task for me, so go back to her in her house in Jal-Veesa. Weeltul would not tell me about Umbriel just now. Unknown: why the trees are sick, and what the second task is.
