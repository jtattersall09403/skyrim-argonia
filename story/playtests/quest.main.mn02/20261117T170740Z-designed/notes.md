I am Tarin, a thief freed from the Owing by Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa. I killed two young crocodiles at Reed-Sill Weir, picked Sithis beard and gave it to Weeltul, the tree-minder. The Hist is sick (dry dark bark, closed scar, cooling egg-pool) and nobody knows why. I now work for the Toh-Reel-Ka, the Secret Warriors of the Xeech-Leel, who rule the province and free kinless strangers as cheap spies. I have 50 gold, a lockpick and the Hist blessing (resist poison). Vosh-Leera has a second task; ask her in her house in Jal-Veesa. Weeltul can also tell about the sap, scar, eggs and Umbriel. Unknown: the cause of the sickness, and whether to trust the Toh-Reel-Ka.
