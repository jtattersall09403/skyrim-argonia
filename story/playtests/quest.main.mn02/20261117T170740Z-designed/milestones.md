Journal: Vosh-Leera told me who I work for now: the Toh-Reel-Ka, the "Secret Warriors" of the Xeech-Leel, who rule the province. They free strangers like me because we come cheap and have no kin. She paid me fifty gold. She has a second task for me, and I'm to ask her for it here in her house in Jal-Veesa.
