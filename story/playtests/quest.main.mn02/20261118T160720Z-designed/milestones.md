Vosh-Leera asked me to take it to Weeltul at the Hist pool in the middle of the town, the tall grey tree standing in the water, and come back to her after.

## 4. Stage 30 (stage): The Dry Tree

Journal: I gave the moss to Weeltul, the tree-minder of Jal-Veesa. The town's Hist is sick: its bark is dry and dark, its scar has closed and its egg-pool is cooling, and nobody knows why. If it dies, no egg here will hatch again. The moss only warms the pool. She let me lay a hand on the bark for its blessing, and told me Vosh-Leera is waiting for me in her house. I should go back to her.

## 5. Stage 90 (ending): The Dry Tree

Journal: Vosh-Leera told me who I work for now: the Toh-Reel-Ka, the "Secret Warriors" of the Xeech-Leel, who rule the province. They were made after Umbriel, when the An-Xileel used Lilmoth's mad tree to call a flying city down on the marsh, so that no tree and nobody allied with a tree can do it again. They free strangers like me because we come cheap and have no kin. She paid me fifty gold, and she has a second task for me: she asked me to come back to her here, in her house, for it.
