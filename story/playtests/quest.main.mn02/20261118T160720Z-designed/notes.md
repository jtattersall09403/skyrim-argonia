I am Wanderer, freed from the Owing by Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa. I fetched Sithis beard from Reed-Sill Weir (no crocodile trouble) and gave it to Weeltul, tree-minder, for the sick Hist; I got the tree blessing and 50 gold. Vosh-Leera revealed she serves the Toh-Reel-Ka, the secret order of the Xeech-Leel rulers, who want to know why Hist trees are sickening along the river; they use friendless strangers like me as cheap spies. She has a second task: go back to her house in Jal-Veesa. I carry a lockpick and 50 gold. Unknown: the cause of the sickness, the second task. Weeltul would not discuss Umbriel or the scar further when I tried.
