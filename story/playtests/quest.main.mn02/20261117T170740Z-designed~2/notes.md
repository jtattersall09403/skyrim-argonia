I am Tarin, a thief with a lockpick, freed from the Owing barge by Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa. I killed two young crocodiles at Reed-Sill Weir, picked Sithis beard, and gave it to Weeltul the tree-minder; I got the Hist blessing. The Jal-Veesa Hist is sick (dry bark, closed scar, cooling egg-pool) and trees along the river are sickening for unknown reasons. Vosh-Leera serves the Toh-Reel-Ka, secret warriors of the Xeech-Leel, who want the cause found; they use kinless strangers like me as cheap spies. I have 50 gold and a lockpick. Vosh-Leera has a second task for me; go back to her house in Jal-Veesa and ask. Unknown: why trees sicken, what the task is. Weeltul can also tell about eggs, sap, scar and Umbriel.
