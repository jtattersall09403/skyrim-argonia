I am Tarin, freed from the Owing barge at Ashgate Landing by Vosh-Leera of Jal-Veesa. I fetched Sithis beard from the weir (crocodiles did not trouble me) and gave it to Weeltul, the tree-minder; it only warmed the dying Hist pool. Vosh-Leera revealed that she serves the Toh-Reel-Ka, secret warriors of the Xeech-Leel, who want to know why Hist trees are sickening along the river. She hired me as a spy and paid 50 gold. I carry 50 gold and my lockpick, and have the Hist blessing. She wants me to return to her house in Jal-Veesa for a second task. Unasked: Weeltul topics on eggs, sap, scar, Umbriel. The cause of the sickness is unknown.
