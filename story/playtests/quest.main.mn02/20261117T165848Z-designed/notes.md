I am a hunter freed from the Owing at Ashgate Landing by Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa. I picked Sithis' beard at Reed-Sill Weir without a fight and gave it to Weeltul, the tree-minder. It only warms the pool. Jal-Veesa's Hist is dying: dry dark bark, closed scar, cooling egg-pool. Nobody knows why, and trees are sickening along the river. I received the Hist's blessing and 50 gold. Vosh-Leera told me privately that she serves the Toh-Reel-Ka, the Secret Warriors of the Xeech-Leel, who rule the province. They exist to stop another Umbriel-type disaster. They use friendless strangers like me as cheap spies. She wants me back at her House in Jal-Veesa for a second task, which I have not yet taken. I carry 50 gold and two lockpicks. Unknowns: why the trees are sick, and what the second task is. Weeltul has topics on the sap, the scar and Umbriel that I have not asked about.
