I liked it. The opening was clear: I woke on a ship, was sent to Nesh-Deeka, and learned the Veiled Reed was hiring. Nesh-Deeka said up front where she was holding back, and that made me trust her enough to take the work. The jar errand was short but had a real choice in it. Dives-Without-A-Rope refused persuasion and wanted a fair price, so I paid twenty gold. The sap vision (a ship hold, roots, a door never built, a dry voice) is a good hook, though I cannot tell yet what it means. Things I did not follow: why the knotted-cord woman spared me, and what the Reed is really after. I would like to see where the Low-Reed Village lead goes. The journal ended with no objective and no marker for Low-Reed, so I stopped because nothing was left to do, not because I was stuck. The only rough spot was that Nesh-Deeka refused my Alchemy check without telling me the skill requirement up front.
