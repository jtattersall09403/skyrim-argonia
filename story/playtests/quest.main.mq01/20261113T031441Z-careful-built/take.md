I enjoyed this short opening. I woke on a ship, was sent to Nesh-Deeka, and joined the Veiled Reed, an An-Xileel spy service. Then I got a jar of Hist sap from Dives-Without-A-Rope by paying her 20 gold, and Nesh-Deeka had me taste it and wrote down my vision. The characters had clear voices, and I liked that Nesh-Deeka said openly she was holding two things back. Directions and markers were easy to follow. Persuasion failed on Dives but paying worked and felt fair. Unclear: why the knotted-cord woman spared me, what the vision means (roots through the deck, a door never built, a dry voice), and the two things Nesh-Deeka would not name. My Alchemy was too low for the safer option, and the dialogue only told me that after I chose it. The journal now has no active objective, though Nesh-Deeka hinted that the Hist at Low-Reed Village has turned away. I am stopping only because no marker remains. I would like to see what happens next.
