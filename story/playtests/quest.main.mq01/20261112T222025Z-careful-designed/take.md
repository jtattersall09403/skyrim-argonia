The first quest, No Name on the Work Roll, ran smoothly: wake on a ship, speak to the broker, find Nesh-Deeka, and join the Veiled Reed. The premise is intriguing. I have no name or past, a woman with a knotted cord spared me, and a Hist is being drained. I want to know why I was spared. The big problem is that nearly all dialogue was unwritten design notes such as "[not yet written; the gist:]" with Checks/Does lines showing. I could follow the plot from them, but they were not spoken lines. Names were also wrong: the first man showed as Namorine, not Buys-The-Months. The woman was "Aragonian Vliiager" and the guest was "Argonian Dock Worker", so I only worked out who was who by guessing. The journal was clear and the markers worked. The player-choice moment (join the Reed, or not yet) was thin, and I picked the first option quickly. I would continue if the dialogue were finished.
