I enjoyed the setup: waking on a ship with no name on the work roll, the broker, and the Veiled Reed recruiting a hand nobody can claim. The plot hooks are interesting: a woman with a knotted cord who spared me, a cell draining a Hist tree, and a turned Hist at Low-Reed Village. I understood who people were for the most part. Every line of dialogue is a "[not yet written; the gist:]" design note, which read like a script outline rather than speech. That cost the game a lot of its feel. I got stuck at The Cutters Jar. I paid for the lockbox key and used the lockbox, but no jar ever appeared. I could only take the whole lockbox, and the journal never moved on. Nesh-Deeka treated the jar as if she had it. Her "Here is the jar", read-the-sap and taste options all returned text, but the quest never advanced. I could not open the lockbox I carried either. Using it did nothing, and picking it said there was no lock. I would like to see what happens next, but I am stuck on a likely item or quest-stage bug, not on a puzzle I could solve.
