I enjoyed this short opening. I woke on a ship, was sent to Nesh-Deeka, joined the Veiled Reed, and paid Dives-Without-A-Rope twenty gold for her lockbox key to get the jar. Then I tasted the Hist sap and brought the page back. The writing was clear and the NPCs were easy to tell apart. I liked that Nesh-Deeka said outright where she was holding things back, and that I could pay or persuade instead of fighting. Some things were unclear. I do not know why the woman with the knotted cord spared me. I do not know what the vision means: roots through a ship deck, a door never built, a dry voice. I also do not know what the Reed really wants. The Persuade option failed without explanation, and re-asking a topic simply repeated the menu. The journal now shows no active objective, though a hint about Low-Reed Village was dropped. I would want to follow that lead and find out what happens next.
