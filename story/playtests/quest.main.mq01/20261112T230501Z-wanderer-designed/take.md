I liked the setup: a burned camp, a stolen Hist cutting, a spy service with an honest agent, and an Owing gang. Nesh-Deeka admitting she was holding two things back was a nice touch. Dives-Without-A-Rope turning over the key felt earned. I understood who everyone was and what the quest wanted. I got stuck at the lockbox. I used it and then took the whole box before I had the key. After that I could not open it or take the jar, and the journal kept asking me to bring the jar. Nesh-Deeka accepted a jar I never held, then repeated her old brief. The persuade option also did nothing when I ran it as a separate command. It only worked when I picked the paid option in the dialogue. The game needs clearer feedback when an object is used or taken, and the quest should not break if you take the container. I would still like to know who the woman with the knotted cord is and why she spared me.
