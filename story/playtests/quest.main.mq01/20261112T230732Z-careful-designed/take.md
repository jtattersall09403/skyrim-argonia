I enjoyed the opening: a mysterious burnt camp, a woman with a knotted cord who spared me, and a secretive state service whose agent was upfront about where she was holding back. Dialogue was well written and I understood the people and stakes. The Hist cutting and cut roots made a good hook, and I wanted to know what happens next. I got stuck in The Cutters Jar. Early on I took the whole Lockbox from the hold before I had the key, which probably broke the quest, because the jar never showed up as something I could take. The Dives dialogue gave me a key twice, the first time through persuasion without any journal update, and the second time for 20 gold, which did advance the journal. The handover dialogue with Nesh-Deeka played out twice and paid out in her words, but the journal still says to bring the jar to her and never closes. Other oddities: Dives is called he and then she, and two of the options were the same twenty-gold line. I could not progress further, so I stopped here.
