What you know so far, from your journal. From "No Name on the Work Roll": I woke in the hold of a ship at a pier west of Lilmoth, in Black Marsh, among a gang of Argonians I do not know. The broker on the pier had no line for me on his roll and sent me to Nesh-Deeka at Salts-The-Catch's house in Lilmoth. Nesh-Deeka serves the Veiled Reed, the ruling state's quiet service. The gang carried me off the bank the night their camp burned, she says, when a woman with a knotted cord killed her courier, took his satchel and let me live. I took work with the Veiled Reed. From "The Cutters' Jar": I got a jar of sap the cutters dropped at the burnt camp and put a drop on my tongue at Nesh-Deeka's table. She wrote down what I said while it held me: the ship's hold, roots grown up through its deck, a door nobody built, a dry voice without words. She has word that the Hist of Low-Reed Village, east of Lilmoth, has turned its face. From "The Tree That Turned Its Face": At Low-Reed Village the root-herald said the Hist shut eleven nights ago and the eggs in its pool died; the village blamed Pays-Late, the tree-minder. Pays-Late told me the truth: the Hist's root was cut, its sap drawn off and the cut sealed with his own gum, and he hid it, fearing the blame. I took the jars of the Hist's sap from two men on a dry rise north of the leech water, each with a knotted cord at the wrist, like the woman who burned the gang's camp. I gave the jars to Pays-Late on one condition, that he tells the root-herald himself that he found the cut and hid it. The cell drew this tree's sap into jars, as it drew the cutting's. What does it want with it? You are standing in Salts-The-Catch's house in Lilmoth with Nesh-Deeka.
