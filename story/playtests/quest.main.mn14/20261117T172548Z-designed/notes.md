Notes: I work for the Toh-Reel-Ka (officer Never-Writes-Twice, Reel-Ka rank). Hist trees are sickening; the Unbound under Cuts-the-Old-Knot drain sap and want the Root guardian dead. The Eye of Argonia lies behind the Kings Gate in the drowned Teku-Sul Spire off Lilmoth and needs three seal-stones. At Blackrose Prison I chose the duel with Corvus Ulvius but he died instead of yielding, so the garrison turned hostile; I killed the raj-kaal Okaz-Teel, took the Warden Vault Key and now carry the first Seal-Stone of the Kings. Blackrose is now emptied of its leaders and hostile. Ennodius Vanin the Collector was in the hall (I did not speak to him; he had a buyer lined up for the vault). Still to get: the stones at the flooded vaults of Gemin (walk from Jal-Veesa, rootworm) and the deepest hall of Vorannuhl (near Noota Nara, by boat). Open mysteries: what moans in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar and the key to it, why the Unbound seal trees they drain, and how Nesh-Deeka knows the Unbound.
