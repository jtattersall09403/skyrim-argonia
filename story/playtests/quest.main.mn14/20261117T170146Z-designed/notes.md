I am a Reel-Ka of the Toh-Reel-Ka (officer Never-Writes-Twice, Helstrom). I have the Seal-Stone of the Kings from Blackrose Prison. I got it by walking in past the gate-captain, lifting the vault key off Okaz-Teel, and opening the Warden Vault. Two stones are left: the flooded vaults of Gemin (walk from Jal-Veesa, rootworm) and the deepest hall of Vorannuhl (near Noota Nara, by boat). All three go in the sockets of the gate at Teku-Sul Spire, the drowned tower off Lilmoth. The Eye of Argonia is behind that gate, and the order wants it used to heal the Hist. The Unbound want the stones too. Ennodius Vanin, the Collector, was at Blackrose and is after the same relics. Still open: why the Unbound seal the trees they drain, what moans in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar and where its key is, and how Nesh-Deeka knows so much about the Unbound.
