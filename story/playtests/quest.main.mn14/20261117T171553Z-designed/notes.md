I work for the Toh-Reel-Ka as a Reel-Ka under Never-Writes-Twice. I paid the gate-captain 300 gold at Blackrose, then bought the warden vault key from Okaz-Teel for 1,000, and took the first Seal-Stone of the Kings. I carry it with about 350 gold, the Eye-Setting, Keystone, paralysis poison x3, sap jar, rapier, Reelka armour and the Warden Vault Key. Two stones remain: the flooded vaults of Gemin (walk from Jal-Veesa, rootworm) and the deepest hall of Vorannuhl (boat near Noota Nara). All three go into the gate at the drowned Teku-Sul Spire off Lilmoth, which leads to the Eye at the Root. Open questions: why the Unbound seal trees they drain, what moans in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar and where its key is, and how Nesh-Deeka knows so much about the Unbound. The Collector, Ennodius Vanin, is rival hunting the same relics.
