Notes: I work for Nesh-Deeka (Toh-Reel-Ka, Helstrom). The elder Hums-the-Long-Note of Toklea told me, in return for her family grave-stake (I raised it from the Zul-Etha drowned chest without fighting the crocodiles, and gave it to her), that the Kothringi Keystone opens the knight-captain tomb at Eurydike, a roofless Kothringi hall just north of Gideon. The captain was buried with what marked the kings road to the Root. Nesh-Deeka knows the Unbound have been asking Gideon diggers about Eurydike; she says we listen, and told me to hurry. She paid me 100 gold, so I have about 700. I still carry the keystone, Pale Harvest strongbox key, Old Silver Setting, The Collector note, smuggler key. Next: go to Eurydike near Gideon and open the tomb before the Unbound and the Collector (Ennodius Vanin). Unknown: what the Root and the Eye really are, and how Nesh-Deeka knows what she knows.
