I work for Nesh-Deeka of the Toh-Reel-Ka in Helstrom. The Chime-Keepers elder Hums-the-Long-Note in Toklea named a price: her family grave-stake from a chest in drowned Zul-Etha. I killed the crocodile and old bull there, took the stake and gave it to her. She told me the Kothringi Keystone opens the knight-captain tomb at Eurydike, a roofless Kothringi hall a short walk north of Gideon. The knights of Gideon were the last to guard the old kings road to the Root. I told Nesh-Deeka. She said this is one more step towards the Eye of Argonia, a jewel of Hist amber at the Root under the sea behind a gate the last king sealed. Used in place, it can heal the sick Hist trees. The Unbound drain sap for a draught to kill the beast that guards the Root, and they have been asking Gideon diggers about Eurydike, so we must hurry. I have not yet gone to Eurydike. My journal still shows the objective to tell Nesh-Deeka, though I did. I carry the Keystone, the Eye-Setting, an Unbound-Note, 2 lockpicks, my Voh-Vastei armour and 550 gold. Next step: go to Eurydike near Gideon and open the tomb with the keystone. I did not ask her how she knows what the Unbound ask in Gideon.
