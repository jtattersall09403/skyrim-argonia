Notes: I am a Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka under Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. Hums-the-Long-Note, Chime-Keeper elder at Toklea, told me the Kothringi Keystone opens the knight-captain tomb at Eurydike, a roofless Kothringi hall a short walk north of Gideon. The knights of Gideon were the last to guard the kings road to the Root. I paid her by killing a crocodile and an old bull in drowned Zul-Etha and bringing back her family grave-stake. Nesh-Deeka paid 100 more gold, so I have about 700. She knows the Unbound are asking Gideons diggers about Eurydike and told me to hurry. I still carry the keystone, Pale Harvest Strongbox Key, Old Silver Setting, The Collector note and the smuggler key. Next: go to Eurydike and open the tomb before the Unbound and the Collector (Ennodius Vanin). Unknown: what the Eye and the Root are, and how Nesh-Deeka knows ("we listen").
