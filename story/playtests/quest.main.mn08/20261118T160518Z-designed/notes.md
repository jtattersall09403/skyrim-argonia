I am Tarin, thief of Nesh-Deeka house in Helstrom, with about 650 gold, the Kothringi Keystone, Old Silver Setting, lockpick and Voh-Vastei armour. Hums-the-Long-Note, Chime-Keeper elder at Toklea, told me for the price of her family grave-stake, which I took quietly from the chest in drowned Zul-Etha, that the keystone opens the knight-captain tomb at Eurydike, a roofless Kothringi hall just north of Gideon. The knights of Gideon were the last guards of the kings road to the Root. I told Nesh-Deeka, who paid 100 gold and said to hurry: the Unbound are asking Gideon diggers about Eurydike. She knows this because we listen. Next: go to Eurydike before the Unbound. Still unknown: seal paste, Cuts-the-Old-Knot, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel, Collector Ennodius Vanin.
