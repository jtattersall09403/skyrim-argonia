The Drowned Street is finished. I am a Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka under Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. We hunt the Eye of Argonia, a Hist-amber jewel at the Root, under the sea behind a gate the last Kothringi king sealed. Used in place, it can heal the sick trees. The Unbound drain sap for a draught to kill the Roots guardian beast and want to reach it first. Hums-the-Long-Note of Toklea told me the Kothringi Keystone opens the knight-captains tomb at Eurydike, a roofless hall just north of Gideon. I paid her price by raising her family grave-stake from Zul-Etha without a fight. Nesh-Deeka paid me 100 gold and told me to hurry, because the Unbound are asking Gideons diggers about Eurydike. Next step: go to Eurydike and open the tomb. No marker is set yet. I carry the Keystone, the Eye-Setting, an Unbound-Note, two lockpicks, Voh-Vastei armour and about 650 gold. I still do not know what the captain was buried with.
