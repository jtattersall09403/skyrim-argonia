I am Tarin, thief of Nesh-Deeka house in Helstrom, with about 650 gold, the Kothringi Keystone, Old Silver Setting, a lockpick and Voh-Vastei armour. I took the stake from the drowned chest at Zul-Etha by getting past the crocodiles without a fight, and gave it to Hums-the-Long-Note at Toklea. She told me the keystone opens the knight-captain tomb at Eurydike, a roofless Kothringi hall just north of Gideon. The Kothringi knights guarded the kings road to the Root, where the Eye of Argonia lies. Nesh-Deeka paid me and said to hurry, because the Unbound are asking Gideon diggers about Eurydike. Next step is Eurydike. Still unknown: seal paste, Cuts-the-Old-Knot, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel, and Collector Ennodius Vanin.
