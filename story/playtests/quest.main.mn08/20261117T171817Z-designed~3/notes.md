I am a Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka under Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. The Chime-Keeper elder Hums-the-Long-Note told me the Kothringi Keystone opens the knight-captain tomb at Eurydike, a roofless Kothringi hall north of Gideon. I paid her price by fetching her family grave-stake from Zul-Etha, and the crocodiles did not trouble me. Nesh-Deeka paid 100 gold more, so I have about 700. She says this is a step toward the Eye of Argonia at the Root and that I must hurry, because the Unbound are asking Gideon diggers about Eurydike. She knew this because "we listen". I still carry the keystone, the Pale Harvest Strongbox Key, the Old Silver Setting, The Collector note and the smuggler key. Next step is to go to Eurydike. The Collector (Ennodius Vanin) is also hunting the Eye. I have not asked what the Eye or the Root is.
