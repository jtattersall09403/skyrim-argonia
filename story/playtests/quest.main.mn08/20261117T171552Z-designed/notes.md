I am a Voh-Vastei of the Toh-Reel-Ka under Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. Hums-the-Long-Note, elder of the Chime-Keepers in Toklea, named her price for the keystone lore: her family grave-stake from the drowned chest in Zul-Etha. I took it without trouble from the crocodiles and gave it to her. She told me the Kothringi Keystone opens the tomb of the knight-captain at Eurydike, a roofless Kothringi hall a short walk north of Gideon. The Kothringi knights of Gideon were the last to guard the old kings road to the Root, and the captain was buried with what marked the way. I reported to Nesh-Deeka, who paid another 100 gold (about 700 total). She says it is a step towards the Eye of Argonia at the Root and that I should hurry, as the Unbound are asking Gideon diggers about Eurydike. She learns this by "we listen". I still carry the keystone, the Pale Harvest Strongbox Key, the Old Silver Setting, The Collector note and the smuggler key. Next: go to Eurydike near Gideon. The Collector (Ennodius Vanin) and the Unbound are also hunting the Eye. I still do not know exactly what the Eye or the Root is.
