I am Tarin, thief of Nesh-Deeka house in Helstrom. I have about 850 gold, the Kothringi Keystone, the Old Silver Setting, my gear, 3 Grey Seal paralysis poisons from the Unbound packs, and the Knight-Captain Bronze Disc. I opened the tomb at Eurydike quietly and gave the disc to Nesh-Deeka. She says the pick-and-tower mark is Vicrium, an old ochre and alum mine in the clay country behind Seafalls in Okuwun Rahei, in the east. The knights kept a strongbox there. This is the trail to the Eye of Argonia, which lies at the Root under the sea and can heal every tree. The Unbound are ahead of us. They drain trees and then seal the scar with the grey paralysis paste, and nobody can say why. Nesh-Deeka says it does not matter, but it nags at me. She knows the Unbound moves because "we listen", and she does not say who listens. Still unknown: Cuts-the-Old-Knot, Xeech-Leel, Umbriel and Ennodius Vanin. Next: go to Vicrium.
