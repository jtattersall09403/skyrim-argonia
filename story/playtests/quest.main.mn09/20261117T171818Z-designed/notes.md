I work for Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom. At Eurydike I took Grey Seal, a paralysis poison, x3 from the Unbound packs. The Unbound drain trees then numb the scars shut, and nobody has told me why. I opened the knight-captain tomb with the keystone, took the bronze disc and gave it to Nesh-Deeka. The disc bears Vicrium mark, a pick crossed with a tower. Vicrium is an old ochre and alum mine in the clay country behind Seafalls in Okuwun Rahei in the east, where the knights kept a strongbox. Nesh-Deeka paid 100 gold and I have about 750. She knew the Unbound were at Eurydike and only said "we listen". I carry the Pale Harvest strongbox key, Old Silver Setting, The Collector note, smuggler key, lockpicks and the Grey Seal. Next is Vicrium, before the Unbound and the Collector Ennodius Vanin. Still unknown are what the Root and the Eye really are, how Nesh-Deeka knows what she knows, and why the Unbound seal trees.
