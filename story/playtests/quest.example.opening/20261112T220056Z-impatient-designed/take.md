Short and linear: wake on a barge, get sent to Lilmoth, talk to Sample-Contact, agree to serve the Reed, and the quest ends. It took about five commands. I never learned who the Reed are or why I was on the barge, so I was not invested. Several lines were placeholders ("not yet written; the gist"), which broke immersion. Travel and quest markers were clear and easy to follow. I would want to know what the Reed is, but nothing in this stretch made me care yet.
