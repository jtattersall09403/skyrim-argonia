Short and linear: wake on a barge, talk to the Gang Boss, go to Sample-Contact, join the Reed. Travel was automatic and the quest was easy to follow. But the dialogue was placeholder text ("[not yet written; the gist:]"), so I never learned who the Reed is, why I was on the barge, or who I am. Joining something I know nothing about felt hollow. I would want to know what happens next, but only if the story were actually written.
