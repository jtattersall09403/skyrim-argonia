I liked this. Both quests turned on a hard choice, and the game kept the cost in view. I killed the fetcher to free Rows-For-Another, and Never-Sold did not thank me for it. In the second quest I read the reckoner leaves, learned that Mends-Nets killed for Seven-Floods, and still sent the man held for words, because that was the job. Mends-Nets and Seven-Floods stay behind. That felt wrong and I think it was meant to. I understood who people were, and the Rose, the heirs, the leaves and the reckoners were explained clearly through dialogue. My Restoration, Alchemy and Persuade attempts all failed against Seven-Floods. The skill gates were fair, but I only found that out by failing. The ending was quiet and nothing new started. The only loose end is Never-Sold saying she will do better by the next one, and I would want to know what that next job is.
