Short, clear quest: Never-Sold wants a woman freed from an Owing, I follow the markers, kill the fetcher, and relay messages. I understood the flow and the people were distinct. The best part is the closing line that Never-Sold did not thank me for how I did it. It makes the killing feel heavier than a normal quest reward. The weak spot is that I took the violent route without much weighing of it, and the other two options, the leaf in the count-box and Hulm-Tei, never came up. I would like to see what the Chainbreakers do next, but no new objective began, so I stopped.
