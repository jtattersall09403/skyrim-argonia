Short, readable and clear. The Rows-For-Another buy-out led into the prison-fire quest in a natural way. I understood who everyone was. The three prisoners each gave one tidy reason, and that made the choice a real one. I chose the mother. Her debt was only for shelter, it would pass to her daughter, and she was old. I felt a little uneasy that Says-It-Plain and Hides-In-Reeds stay held. Persuasion kept me the 100 gold, which was a nice touch. Oddities: the Tsaxi menu showed the persuade option twice, and the journal started empty. I would like to see what Never-Sold and the heirs do next.
