Short and easy to follow. Never-Sold gave me a job, I met the three prisoners and picked one, and it ended cleanly. I liked the moral weight of the choice. All three cases were sympathetic, and I picked the old mother because her debt was inherited and she was Rows-For-Anothers mother. It stung that the other two stay held. I failed the Speechcraft check (15 against 25), so I paid the gold. That is fine, but there was no way to improve my skill, so the check felt like a dead end. Two things were clunky: the duplicated persuade/non-persuade dialogue options, and the need to re-open talk before each question. Never-Solds hint to hear all three first was good. I would like to see what the heirs do next and whether Rows-For-Another reunites with her mother. Nothing is left in my journal.
