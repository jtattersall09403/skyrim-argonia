Quest finished cleanly: paid Tsaxi, freed the mother, reported back. Clear and easy to follow, and I liked the idea of Owings and the quiet moral weight of choosing one of three, leaving two held. But many lines were unwritten placeholders ("[not yet written; the gist:]"), which broke immersion and left me without real characterisation. I also did not learn why the other two were held beyond one-line debts, and my choice felt pre-decided by the quest. Persuasion was gated at 25 versus my 15, so paying was the only option. I would want to see what happens to the two left behind.
