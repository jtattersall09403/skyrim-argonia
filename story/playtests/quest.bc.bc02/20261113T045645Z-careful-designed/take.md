I liked this. The writing is tight, and the Chainbreakers job is a real moral knot, not a fetch quest. The investigation worked well. Two prisoners claimed the same words, the old woman refused to name either, and my healing and alchemy were too weak to move her. That sent me to the swamp for the leaves, and I felt why the skill gates exist. The choice felt weighty. I freed the killer, Mends-Nets, over the man Never-Sold wanted. Walks-Far owes three seasons, while Mends-Nets would have been put under. Never-Sold took it coldly, and I liked that the game let her stay unhappy with me. The irony stung: Seven-Floods is Rows-For-Another's mother, and I had killed the fetcher who knew the way to her home tree in the earlier quest. Some things confused me. Giving Tsaxi the leaves removed the evidence without much cost. The Rose and the heirs are explained only in dialogue. Travel is instant, so the world feels thin. One lockpick command was blocked by my own quoting, not the game. I do want to know what the dead levels are and what Never-Sold asks next.
