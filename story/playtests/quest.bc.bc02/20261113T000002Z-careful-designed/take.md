Short, readable and affecting. I understood the setup: the Owing is a debt paid in work, the heirs hold people born in the Rose, and Never-Sold works against that. The choice was a real one. Says-It-Plain and Hides-In-Reeds were held for something they said or did. Keeps-The-Old-Count owed for shelter she never asked to owe, and the debt would pass to her daughter, so I freed her. It tied back to Rows-For-Another well. The persuade check worked and I kept the 100 gold. Rough spots: the persuade topic was listed twice, and the three captives were already standing at the shore, so I never saw the one room or Tsaxi turn his back. The ending is a bit tidy, and the other two go back to the Rose with no way to help them, which stung. Never-Sold promised to protect the daughter, and I would like to see whether that holds, and whether Keeps-The-Old-Count and Rows-For-Another meet again. The journal is now empty of objectives, so I am stopping.
