I enjoyed this. The quests are morally grey and the writing is dense. In the first one I killed the fetcher to free Rows-For-Another, and the game noted that Never-Sold did not thank me for how. In the second, I had to work out which of two prisoners had killed a man. The clue was that Otheel was drowned in a net and Mends-Nets mends nets. She also recited the other prisoner's speech word for word, which looked like cover. I picked Walks-Far, and Never-Sold was pleased but uneasy. I never confirmed it, though. The leaves that would settle it are at the reckoner's camp in the western swamp, and I skipped them. I wanted to know if I had picked right, and I did not like leaving Seven-Floods and Mends-Nets behind. Many lines show as "[not yet written; the gist: ...]" placeholders, which broke the immersion. Several Speech and skill options were gated, and my Speech of 15 was too low. The Restoration and Alchemy options did not fail, but they gave the same non-answer. I understood who the main people were and what they wanted. I do want to know what happens to Rows-For-Another, to Walks-Far's clutch-brother, and to the Rose.
