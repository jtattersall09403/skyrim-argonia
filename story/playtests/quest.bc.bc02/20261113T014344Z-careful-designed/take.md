I enjoyed the moral weight: both jobs had me pick who gets freed or hurt, and the world answered. Never-Sold being uneasy and Walks-Far's clutch-brother being taken instead made the choice feel real. I understood the main people and stakes, though some dialogue was placeholder "not yet written; the gist" text, which broke immersion. My biggest regret: I handed Tsaxi the leaves without reading them, so I picked Walks-Far on a hunch (his account felt personal, Mends-Nets recited like a rehearsed cover) rather than on evidence. Skill gates (Speech 40, Restoration, Alchemy 25) blocked the gentler routes with my low stats. I would want to know what happens to Mends-Nets, Seven-Floods and Rows-For-Another, and whether the Chainbreakers get a reputation.
