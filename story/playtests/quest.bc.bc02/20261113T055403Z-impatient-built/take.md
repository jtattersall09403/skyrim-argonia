I ran out of commands partway through The Broker's Mercy. I had the Broker's key and the chest open, but I had not yet taken the tokens or the book. I enjoyed the moral weight of the quests. Killing the fetcher for the first job and choosing between the two prisoners in the second felt like real choices, and Never-Sold's reactions to them felt earned. The quests gave several routes (persuade, pay, steal), which was good. Persuade checks failed with no hint of how to improve my odds. Paying the 30-gold door guard worked right away. The journal and markers kept me oriented, though there were many markers for one quest. I wanted to see how the Broker took being robbed. I understood who people were mostly through the quest text, not their own words. The dialogue was a little wordy for an impatient player, but good.
