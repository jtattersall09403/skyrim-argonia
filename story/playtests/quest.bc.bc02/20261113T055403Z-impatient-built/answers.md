1. I was in the middle of The Broker's Mercy. The chest was open and I had just gone for the Crew's Tokens. Next I'd finish looting the chest (the tokens and the book), slip out, and take them back to Never-Sold or Heavy-Rope for the quest's outcome. I'd also check the journal for a new objective.

2. I work for Never-Sold and the Chainbreakers. She named me one of them and paid me in full, and she keeps handing me jobs through Tsaxi and Heavy-Rope. I do it for the pay and the progress, not out of any deep loyalty. She didn't thank me for how I got the first job done, so I'm not sure she fully approves of me.

3. The most confusing moment was the failed persuade checks with the Broker and Ra'Jhan. The game gave no sign of what would have helped, so I couldn't tell whether I was missing an item, a rumor, or a skill level. The pile of markers for one quest was also hard to read. I could see what each one pointed at but not how they fit together as a plan. Across the whole game, people's roles and relationships came mostly from journal text rather than from what they said to me.

4. To the writer: the moral choices are your strongest material. The fetcher's death and the prisoner decision both had weight, and Never-Sold reacting to how I did things was great. Do two things next:
   - When a persuasion check fails, hint at what would improve it, such as "he'd listen if you knew X".
   - Trim the dialogue and the marker clutter so an impatient player can follow the thread. Paying the guard and stealing the key both worked cleanly, so keep that variety of routes.
