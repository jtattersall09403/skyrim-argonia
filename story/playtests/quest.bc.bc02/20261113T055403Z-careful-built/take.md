I enjoyed the writing. People speak in a way that suits the setting, and the choices are morally hard, not a good option against a bad one. In "One Out of the Smoke" I read the reckoner's leaves, learned that Mends-Nets drowned a man for the old woman, and still sent Walks-Far out because Never-Sold wanted the one held for words. That left the killer and the old woman behind, and it felt uncomfortable, which I think was the point. The Broker's quest was starting to do the same thing: his freed crew against eleven people held at Thorn, and Heavy-Rope admitting the Broker freed him. I understood who most people were and what they wanted. The journal and the topic lists made it easy to follow. Skill-gated options such as persuasion, alchemy and restoration were a nice touch, though I could not use them yet. Two things confused me. The tool refused a command with an apostrophe in the argument until I changed the quoting, and I ran out of commands just as I reached the Broker with the chest in front of me, so I never found out how it ends. I do want to know what happens next, especially what Ussa-Rekh says and whether the crew gets out cleanly.
