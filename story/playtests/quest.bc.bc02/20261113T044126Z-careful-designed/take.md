I liked this more than I expected. The Chainbreakers quests have a real moral edge. The first one left me feeling used: I killed a man so a woman could leave, and Never-Sold said so herself. The second was a proper whodunit. Two prisoners claim the same words, and I had to go into a swamp camp, kill a lamia, pick a lock and read the leaves to find out which one was lying. The reveal was good. The one who sounded gentle and reasonable, Mends-Nets, was the killer. Walks-Far was loud and prideful but honest. The twist that Seven-Floods is Rows-For-Another mother landed hard, because I had just sent her daughter away. The Restoration and Alchemy dialogue options did nothing for me, which was a bit frustrating. Her refusals were in character but gave me no feedback on what would have worked. Some terms (Owing, the Rose, leaves, put under) took a few exchanges to piece together. Quest text was clear about my next step throughout. I want to know what is down in the dead levels, and whether Mends-Nets pays for what I left behind. Nothing is active now and Never-Sold has no more work, so I am stopping.
