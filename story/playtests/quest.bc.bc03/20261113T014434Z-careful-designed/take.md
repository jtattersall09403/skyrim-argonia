I finished The Broker's Mercy: I paid the door guard, took the book, picked the chest, and gave the tokens to Heavy-Rope. The story is interesting. Never-Sold is morally murky, and Ussa-Rekh is a tragic figure. His line "ask her whose boat" suggests a history I would like to know. The three quests fit together well and the choices felt weighty. The unwritten placeholder dialogue ("the gist") broke immersion a lot. The stealing prompts were odd: I was marked as seen, yet nothing happened, so the crime had no consequence. I also never got a clear sense of what "the eleven at Thorn" will suffer now, and Never-Sold herself said he would ask again. I understood the people and goals well enough. I do want to know what the Broker does next and what he was to Never-Sold. No active objective is left, so I am stopping.
