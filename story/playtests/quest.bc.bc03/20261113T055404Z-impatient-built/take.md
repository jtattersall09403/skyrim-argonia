I liked the tone. Every job has a cost, and Never-Sold says so out loud. The Broker quest was clear and I always knew what to do next. I stole the key and tokens unseen, and that was easy. Persuasion failed with both the Broker and Ra'Jhan, and the game gave no hint how to make it work. The quest also never made clear how much the eleven at Thorn would suffer for what I did. The names are hard to keep apart, and I skimmed most of the Argonian lore. Some dialogue repeated, and Never-Sold ran out of work. I would play on to see whether the Chainbreakers face consequences for the killings and thefts.
