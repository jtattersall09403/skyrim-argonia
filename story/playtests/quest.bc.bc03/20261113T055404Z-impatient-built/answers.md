I was also never clear on how much the eleven at Thorn would suffer for what I did. 4. I'd tell the writer the tone and the honesty about costs work well, and the quests are easy to follow. Make the persuasion checks tell you what they need, such as gold, a favor or a piece of information. Show the consequences of the jobs more clearly, because I didn't see what happened to the people affected. Fewer, more distinct names would help, and so would less repeated dialogue. I'd keep playing to see whether the Chainbreakers face any fallout for the killings and thefts.
