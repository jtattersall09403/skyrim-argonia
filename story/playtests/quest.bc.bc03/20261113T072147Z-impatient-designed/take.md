I enjoyed it more than I expected. Every job was a moral squeeze: you free someone, and someone else pays for it. I understood who Never-Sold was and what the Chainbreakers do. The Broker job was the best. Heavy-Rope, Ra'Jhan and the old man each had a fair side, and I could see exactly what each route cost. I picked the pickpocket route because my persuasion attempts failed and I did not have 1000 gold. It worked without a fuss, though it cost the eleven at Thorn. Things that felt thin or unclear: Persuade checks gave no hint of what would succeed. The pickpocket seen/unseen choice felt like a coin flip I could not read. Never-Sold keeps saying she will not forget how I did things, but nothing in the game seemed to change because of it. The setting is bleak and interesting. I want to know what happens to the eleven at Thorn, and the game seems to be heading there. Right now nothing else is open: Never-Sold has no more work, so I am stopping here.
