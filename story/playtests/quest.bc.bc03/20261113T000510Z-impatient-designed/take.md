I liked the setup: Never-Sold sends me to the Soulrest docks, I hear Heavy-Rope and Ussa-Rekh, and I have to pick a side. The Owing and buy-out ideas were clear, and it tied back to my earlier choice about Rows-For-Another. I sided with Ussa-Rekh because his argument was that unpaid berths mean no more people get bought out. It felt like a real dilemma, though I only skimmed it. The ending was buggy. My Speechcraft was 15 against a 30 check. Paying the 50 gold got a reply from Heavy-Rope but did nothing visible: no gold change I could see and no journal update. The other Speechcraft 30 option, which looked the same as the first, worked at 15 and Heavy-Rope agreed to work the season. Even so, the objective stayed open and Ussa-Rekh had nothing more to say. I could not tell whether the quest was finished, so I stopped. I would want to know what Ussa-Rekh and Never-Sold make of the result, and whether the held people actually benefit.
