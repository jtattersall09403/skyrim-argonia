I liked this more than I expected. The writing is quiet and morally gray. Every job has a cost, and the game says so aloud. Never-Sold keeps telling me what I just did, and "we will be called the people who loose killers" stuck with me. The Broker job was the best. Heavy-Rope, Ra'Jhan, Feldril and the old man each have a real position, and the old man is sympathetic and still wrong about the extra season. I could not find a clean way through. I could not pay 1000 gold, my persuasion failed, and my lockpicking was too low for the chest. I lifted the key unseen, and the eleven at Thorn lost their hire. I felt that loss, which I think was the point. My complaints: the Broker was only a rumour until I walked into his house, so I never got to know him before I robbed him. Never-Sold hints she was once his keeper of the count, and I wanted that explored more. The "unseen" and "seen" choice on stealing felt like the game doing the stealth for me. I also never saw what became of Rows-For-Another or Mends-Nets. I do want to know what happens next, especially at Thorn and with the Rose heirs. Nothing is active in my journal now, so I am stopping.
