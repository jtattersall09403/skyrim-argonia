I liked this a lot. The setting is fresh and the quests have no clean answer. Each one cost somebody something: Rows-For-Another, the three held at the heirs' post, and here the eleven at Thorn. I followed the Broker job fine. The persuasion options failed, I had no 1000 gold, and I ended up lifting the key and the tokens unseen. I felt bad for Ussa-Rekh, who is no villain, but the crew had waited long enough. Never-Sold is a sharp character: she does not thank me, she keeps the count, and her backstory as the keeper of the count was a good reveal. What I did not understand is how to get the gold for the persuasion route. I also could not tell what the persuade checks needed, because the game gave no hint. At the end Never-Sold had no more work, and I would like to see where Thorn leads next.
