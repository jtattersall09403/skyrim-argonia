Three Chainbreaker jobs done, and nothing is left in the journal. It was easy to follow: the markers and objectives were clear, and I always knew where to go next. The writing is interesting. Never-Sold is a hard, quiet client, and she says the group will be called "the people who loose killers" and that it will be true. The game lets you solve things by killing, bribing, stealing or talking. The dock job was neat. I paid 30 gold to a guard, lifted the key off the Broker, and the crew went free with no fight. My Speech of 15 blocked the talking routes, which felt fair. Some lines still show as "[not yet written; the gist:]" placeholders, which broke the mood. I also never learned who Never-Sold is to the Broker, and I did not really get who most people were, because I skipped the optional questions. I would like to see the Broker ask again, and to find out what became of Rows-For-Another.
