I liked this one. The Broker job gave me three routes and each one cost something. I stole the tokens and the book, and the crew went free. The eleven at Thorn lost the hire that would have bought them out, and Never-Sold said she now carries them. I never found the "better case" that Ra'Jhan, Heavy-Rope and Ussa-Rekh each asked for. I only half understood what would have persuaded them. I think it was a traders case or the names, or 1000 gold I did not have. That made the persuade routes feel gated behind things I could not find. Ussa-Rekh, who bought people out for thirty years and then let a season become two, felt like a real person, and so did Never-Sold, who wrote the first leaf of her own Owing. Everything still hurts a little, and that fits the setting. The writing was strong and the choices felt morally heavy. I would like to know what happens to the eleven at Thorn, and whether Never-Sold's Chainbreakers keep their cost. One thing made little sense: Feldril unbarred a door I had already picked my way through, and the 30 gold seemed to do nothing but tick a box.
