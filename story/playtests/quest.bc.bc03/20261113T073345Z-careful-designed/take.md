I liked the Broker job most. Nobody in it was simply wrong. Ussa-Rekh freed hundreds of people and then kept them a season longer. Heavy-Rope hated the extra season but chose it for the eleven at Thorn. Never-Sold turned out to be one of the people the Broker had bought out, and the game only told me that through a book on his shelf. I read that book and the list of names, and that is what drove the ending. I could not pay 1000 gold, and persuading Ra Jhan, the Broker and Heavy-Rope all failed. The list of names finally moved Heavy-Rope. Taking it and the book in front of Ussa-Rekh counted as theft, but nothing came of it beyond a line saying I would pay a fine or fight. That felt unfinished. The lack of a real consequence for being seen was the one thing that broke the spell. The ending is bitter. The crew row another season and I got Never-Sold the book, not the tokens. She called me a full Chainbreaker and said she could not tell me I was wrong. I wanted to know what happens to the eleven and whether Ussa-Rekh asks again. The earlier quests, where I killed my way through a fetcher and a post keeper, made me uneasy in a good way, because the game remembers and Never-Sold keeps pointing it out. The menus were clear and I always knew who people were. I ran out of work at the end because Never-Sold had nothing else to offer.
