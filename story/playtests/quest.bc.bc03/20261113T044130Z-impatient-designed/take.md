The Broker job was the best of the three: pick the door, lift the key, open the chest, walk out. That is what I wanted from this game. The talk was heavy and I skimmed a lot. I understood the setup, though. Owings are debt-servitude, and the Chainbreakers free people, at a cost each time. The moral weight landed: the eleven at Thorn lose their hire, and Brings-Them-Back died for a head start. I could not tell who the Broker was to Never-Sold. I did not feel the stakes for her book, which I took without reading. Never-Sold had no new work for me at the end, and the journal is empty, so I am stopping. I would play on to see the eleven at Thorn.
