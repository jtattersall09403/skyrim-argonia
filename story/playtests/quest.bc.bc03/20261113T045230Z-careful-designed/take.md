I liked this. Never-Sold gives you jobs where every way out costs someone, and she says so to your face. The Broker quest was the best of the three. Ussa-Rekh is not a villain. Heavy-Rope calls him the man who freed him. Ra-Jhan, the berth-master, is just doing his job. I tried persuading Ra-Jhan and the Broker and failed, because I could not pay the 1000 gold. What worked was finding the list of the eleven names on the Broker table and showing it to Heavy-Rope, who then chose to sail the season himself. That felt like a real solution, not a lock to pick. It was a little odd that I had to steal the list in front of the Broker and nothing happened. The game says the fine or fight stage just is not implemented. I also never took Never-Sold book, and the quest finished anyway, so I do not know whether I was meant to. The ending is bitter: the crew lose another season, nobody thanks me, and Never-Sold says it will happen again. I understood who people were, and the Broker line about Never-Sold boat made me want to know their history. Earlier killings, Brings-Them-Back and Tsaxi, sat uneasily with the later story, and the game noticed that. Confusing parts: the door was locked Adept even though I was supposed to be able to talk my way in, and the tokens were never resolved. I do want to see what the Broker asks next, and what Thorn is like.
