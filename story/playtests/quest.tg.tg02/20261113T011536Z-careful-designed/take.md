Short and easy to follow. Bent-Spine gave me a clear job and a clear moral line: no blackmail. I opened the lockbox and found three pledges, each claimed by a different client. I sold each one back at the flat price. I used the no-argument option every time, so I never exposed anyone as a liar myself, and the journal still says I showed them up. That disconnect was the oddest part. The journal also says each claim "is a lie" and that they haggled, but the dialogue just had them paying a flat half price. The rest of the choice felt thin: the only real options were to give everything to one client or to sell each pledge back. I would have liked to hear what the three of them said after paying, and to learn more about the drowned merchant. Even so, I want to see what the Chapter gives a Lookout next.
