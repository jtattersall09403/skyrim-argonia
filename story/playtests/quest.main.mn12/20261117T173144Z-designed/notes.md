Notes: I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed the three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan, got the keep key and 100 gold, and picked the quartermaster store lock. Quartermaster Theyne Indarys keeps jars of Hist sap tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, which come up the mesa road each season and go down the stair. The fort has no Hist of its own. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the locked cellar door; persuasion and illusion failed, and only Never-Writes-Twice has the key. Something moans behind it, but I do not know who or what, or why the order feeds it sap. I carry about 1150 gold, rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, the keep key, eye-setting, keystone, bronze disc, Unbound note and parchment map. Still unknown: the Root, the Eye, the Collector, and the cellar prisoner. Next: go to Bell Station, east of Lilmoth, and ask the divers which drowned tower is cut deeper. I could also later ask Never-Writes-Twice about the cellar.
