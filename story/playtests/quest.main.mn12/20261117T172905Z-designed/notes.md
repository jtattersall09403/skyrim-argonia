Notes: I went into Fort Chalkmoth by the yard trapdoor, unseen, to the cellar guardroom. Captain Sakeeus-Wan would open the keep only if I killed three Sarpa raiders, which I did not do. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku, who is the Toh-Reel-Ka own, would not be persuaded and my illusion failed. The cellar door is locked and cannot be picked; Never-Writes-Twice keeps the key. In the quartermaster store are sap jars tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, in a fort with no Hist of its own. Something moans behind the door. I do not know who or what it is, or why the order hides it. Next: the seal-stones at Blackrose Prison, Gemin and Vorannuhl, and Bell Station for the drowned tower. I carry about 1650 gold, 2 lockpicks, rapier, Reelka cuirass and helm, 3 paralysis poisons, keystone, eye-setting, bronze disc, parchment map, Unbound-Note. Unknown: the Root, the Eye, the Collector, and what is in the cellar.
