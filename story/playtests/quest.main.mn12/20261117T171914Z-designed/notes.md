Notes: I entered Fort Chalkmoth unseen by the yard trapdoor (the captain Sakeeus-Wan only offered to open the keep if I killed three Sarpa raiders on the west approach; I did not). Below is a guardroom with gaoler Tsleel-Vaku; the inner cellar door is locked and he refuses persuasion and illusion, carries no key, and says Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. In the quartermaster store above are jars of Hist sap tied with Jal-Veesa's knot, in a fort with no Hist of its own. Something moans behind the door; I do not know who or why. Suspicious of Never-Writes-Twice, who dismissed the fort. Still to do: Bell Station (boat from Lilmoth) for the divers who know which drowned tower off Lilmoth is marked; Vanin the Collector is digging ahead. I carry 1050 gold, 2 lockpicks, the Unbound recipe note, Keystone, Paralysis Poison x3, Rapier and other items.
