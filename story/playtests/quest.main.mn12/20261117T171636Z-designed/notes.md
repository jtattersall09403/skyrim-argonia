Notes: Fort Chalkmoth (Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara) is an old Legion fort; captain Sakeeus-Wan offered the keep for killing three Sarpa raiders (I did not; I used the yard trapdoor instead). In the cellar guardroom the gaoler Tsleel-Vaku (Toh-Reel-Ka) refuses entry; the inner cellar door needs a key, which Never-Writes-Twice keeps in Helstrom. Something moans behind it; the order feeds it Hist sap, jars marked with Jal-Veesa knot, one of which I took. Persuade and illusion only got: they sleep and dream. I carry 1050 gold, 2 lockpicks, the Unbound recipe/note, Kings Marker Stone given away already, paralysis poison x3, rapier, jar of Hist sap. Next: Bell Station, east of Lilmoth by boat, for the divers who know the marked drowned tower; Vanin the Collector is digging ahead. Open questions: who is in the cellar, who Jal-Veesa is, why Never-Writes-Twice dismissed it.
