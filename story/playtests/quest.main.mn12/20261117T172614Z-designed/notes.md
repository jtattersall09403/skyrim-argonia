Notes: I am Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker for Nesh-Deeka under Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed the three Sarpa raiders and got the keep key and 100 gold from wall captain Sakeeus-Wan, and lifted the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store key by pickpocket. In the store were jars of Hist sap with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, though the fort has no Hist of its own. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku would not open the cellar door, which needs a key only Never-Writes-Twice keeps. Persuasion and illusion failed, and lockpicking does not work on it. Something moans behind it, and I do not know who or why the order feeds it sap. I carry about 1150 gold, rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, the Unbound note, the eye-setting, keystone, bronze disc, parchment map, and the keep and store keys. Next: Bell Station, east of Lilmoth by boat, where the divers know which drowned tower the marker stone shows. Still unknown: the cellar occupant, and what the Root and Eye truly are. The Collector hunts the Eye too.
