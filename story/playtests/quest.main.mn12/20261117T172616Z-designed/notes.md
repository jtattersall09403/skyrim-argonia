I am Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker for Nesh-Deeka under Never-Writes-Twice. At Fort Chalkmoth (Archwarden fort, Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara) I killed the three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the keep key and 100 gold. I picked the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store lock and saw stacked sap jars tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot by the cellar stair; the fort has no Hist, and Theyne says they come up the mesa road each season and go down the stair. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the locked cellar door, says he has no key and that Never-Writes-Twice keeps it, and refused persuasion and illusion; I could not lift a key from him. Something moans in the cellar. I do not know what or who, nor why the order feeds it sap. Open: the Root, the Eye of Argonia, and the Collector hunting it. Next step: Bell Station, an islet east of Lilmoth by boat, where the divers know which drowned spire the kings marker stone shows. I carry about 1150 gold, rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, the Unbound note, keystone, eye-setting, disc, parchment map, grave-stake, and the Fort Chalkmoth keep key.
