Fort Chalkmoth: I got in unseen by the yard trapdoor. The cellar below the keep is the Toh-Reel-Ka prison. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku would not be persuaded or charmed. He has no key, and Never-Writes-Twice holds it, so she misled me about the fort. Sap jars from Jal-Veesa tree-minder are stored in the quartermaster store, though the fort has no Hist. Something moans behind the inner door, and I do not know who. I did not pick the lock or kill the Sarpa raiders; Sakeeus-Wan still offers 100 gold for that. I carry about 1050 gold, 2 lockpicks, the Seal recipe, 3 paralysis poisons and a rapier. Next: Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth for the drowned tower, and later ask Never-Writes-Twice about the cellar key. Vanin the Collector is also digging ahead of us.
