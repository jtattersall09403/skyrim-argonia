Fort Chalkmoth: killed the three Sarpa raiders for captain Sakeeus-Wan, got the keep key and 100 gold (about 1150 gold now). Picked the quartermaster store lock (Theyne Indarys) and took a Jar of Hist Sap with Jal-Veesa knot. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku refused me the cellar door; he said only that they sleep and dream, and that Never-Writes-Twice keeps the key. Door is locked, key-only. Sap is sent from Jal-Veesa up the mesa road each season for something in the cellar; the fort has no Hist. I do not know who is down there. Still carry the Unbounds Recipe, the Seal paste note, and the Unbound-Note. Next: Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth for the drowned tower divers, or ask Never-Writes-Twice and Jal-Veesa about the cellar. Vanin the Collector is digging ahead of us.
