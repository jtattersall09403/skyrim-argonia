Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker for Nesh-Deeka. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed the three Sarpa raiders for captain Sakeeus-Wan (keep key plus 100 gold) and paid quartermaster Theyne Indarys 150 gold to get into his store. Jars of Hist sap tied with the knot of the Jal-Veesa tree-minder are stacked by the cellar stair. The fort has no Hist of its own, so someone ships it in each season and it goes down the stair. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the locked cellar door and refused persuasion and illusion; he says Never-Writes-Twice holds the key. Something moans behind the door. I did not pick it. I have about 1000 gold, rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poison, the Unbound-Note, keystone, eye-setting, disc, map and the keep key. Still unknown: who or what moans below, and why the order feeds it sap. Next: Bell Station (islet east of Lilmoth, boat from Lilmoth), where the divers know which of the seven drowned towers is cut deeper, for the Eye of Argonia hunt. Could also confront Never-Writes-Twice over the cellar.
