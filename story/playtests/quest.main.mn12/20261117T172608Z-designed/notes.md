I am Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker under Never-Writes-Twice. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed the three Sarpa raiders, got the keep key and 100 gold from wall captain Sakeeus-Wan, and lifted the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store key. The store holds sap jars tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, which go down the cellar stair, though the fort has no Hist. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the inner cellar door and refused persuasion and illusion; the door needs a key that Never-Writes-Twice keeps, and cannot be picked. Something moans behind it. I carry about 1150 gold, 2 lockpicks, rapier, 3 paralysis poisons, eye-setting, keystone, disc, map, Unbound note and both fort keys. Unknown: who or what is in the cellar and why the order feeds it sap. Next: go to Bell Station via boat from Lilmoth for the drowned tower, and ask Never-Writes-Twice about the key.
