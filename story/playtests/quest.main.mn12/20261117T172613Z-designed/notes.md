Notes: Fort Chalkmoth is the Archwardens fort on Chixathe Mesa. Wall captain Sakeeus-Wan offered to open the keep and pay 100 gold if I killed three Sarpa raiders on the western approach. I did not; I used the yard trapdoor (reached via the guardroom door) and got into the cellar guardroom unseen. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku, of the Toh-Reel-Ka, refused persuasion and illusion; he has no key, and Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. The cellar door cannot be picked. The quartermaster store holds sap jars tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, though the fort has no Hist. Something moans behind the door; who and why is unknown. I still carry the rapier, 2 lockpicks, about 1050 gold and my order items. Next: Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth, to ask the divers which drowned tower is cut deeper. Still unknown: what moans in the cellar, what the Root and Eye are, and who the Collector is.
