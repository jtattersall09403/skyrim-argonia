Notes: Fort Chalkmoth done as far as I can take it. I killed three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the keep key and 100 gold. I picked the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store lock and saw sealed Hist sap jars tied with Jal-Veesa knot (tree-minder at Noota Nara) by the cellar stair; the fort has no Hist of its own. Theyne says they arrive every season and he asks nothing. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the cellar door, refused persuasion and illusion, and says Never-Writes-Twice keeps the key; I cannot pick the door. Something moans behind it; who or why unknown, and Never-Writes-Twice hid this from me. I carry about 1250 gold, Unbound recipe and note, lockpicks x2, paralysis poison x3, rapier, keep key. Next: Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth to learn which drowned tower in Benthic Yobesh is marked; Vanin the Collector is digging ahead. Option: press Never-Writes-Twice or Jal-Veesa about the cellar.
