Notes: I entered Fort Chalkmoth unseen via the yard trapdoor, which leads to the cellar guardroom. Captain Sakeeus-Wan offered to open the keep and pay 100 gold if I killed three Sarpa raiders, but I did not do it. In the quartermaster store are Hist sap jars with Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, though the fort has no Hist. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku refused me, and persuasion and illusion both failed. The cellar door is locked and needs a key, which Never-Writes-Twice keeps, or it can be picked. Something moans behind it. I do not know who is down there or why the order feeds them sap. I still carry the Unbounds Recipe for the Seal. Next: go to Lilmoth, take a boat to Bell Station and ask the divers which drowned tower is marked. Vanin is digging ahead of us. Later I could press Never-Writes-Twice or Jal-Veesa about the cellar.
