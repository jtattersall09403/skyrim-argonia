Fort Chalkmoth: killed 3 Sarpa raiders for captain Sakeeus-Wan, got keep key and 100 gold (now 200). Lifted quartermaster Theyne Indarys store key and entered the store; took a Jar of Hist Sap (Jal-Veesa knot). Theyne says such jars come up the mesa road each season and go down the cellar stair; the fort has no Hist. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku bars the cellar door: no one passes, he has no key, Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. He said only: they sleep and they dream. The cellar door is locked and opens only with its key; I have no lockpicks. Not yet seen the cellar. Next: get the key from Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom (she dismissed the fort, suspicious), or find another way (yard trapdoor mentioned in journal). Bell Station tower hunt still pending; Unbound recipe still carried; Vanin digging ahead.
