Notes: I am Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker. At Fort Chalkmoth (Archwarden fort, Chixathe Mesa) I entered unseen via the yard trapdoor into the cellar guardroom. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku (of the Toh-Reel-Ka) would not be persuaded or charmed; he says the cellar is closed, he has no key, and Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. Something moans behind the locked inner door. Sap jars tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot are stacked in the quartermaster store though the fort has no Hist, so the order feeds whoever is down there. The wall captain Sakeeus-Wan offered the keep and 100 gold for killing three Sarpa raiders, which I did not do. Next: the three seal-stones at Blackrose Prison, Gemin and Vorannuhl (map markers), and Bell Station for the drowned tower. I carry 1650 gold, 2 lockpicks, rapier, Reelka cuirass and helm, 3 paralysis poisons, keystone, eye-setting, bronze disc, parchment map, grave-stake, Unbound note. Still unknown: who is in the cellar, what the Root and Eye are, and who the Collector is.
