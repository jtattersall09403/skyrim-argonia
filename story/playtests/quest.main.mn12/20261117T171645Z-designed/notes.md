Notes: Fort Chalkmoth (Archwardens, Chixathe Mesa) is done. I got in unseen by the yard trapdoor into the cellar guardroom. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku would not open the inner cellar door; he has no key, and says Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. Something moans behind it. Jars of Hist Sap marked with the Jal-Veesa knot are stacked by the cellar stair, though the fort has no Hist. I took one jar and still carry it. Wall captain Sakeeus-Wan offered 100 gold and the keep for killing three Sarpa raiders on the western approach; I did not do that. I still carry the Unbound recipe for the Seal, 1650 gold, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons and other gear. Next: Bell Station (boat from Lilmoth) to ask the divers which drowned tower is marked, or confront Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom about the key. Unknown: who is in the cellar, who Jal-Veesa is. Vanin the Collector is digging ahead of us.
