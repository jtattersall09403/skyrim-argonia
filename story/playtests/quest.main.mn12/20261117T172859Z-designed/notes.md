Notes: I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth (Chixathe Mesa) I entered unseen via the yard trapdoor into the cellar guardroom. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku refused me (persuade and illusion both failed); the inner cellar door is locked with a key that Never-Writes-Twice keeps; the captain Sakeeus-Wan carries no key and would open the keep only for killing 3 Sarpa raiders (I did not). Jars of Hist sap tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot are stacked in the store though the fort has no Hist. Something moans behind the door; who and why is unknown. Next: go to Bell Station (islet east of Lilmoth, by boat) and ask the divers which drowned tower is cut deeper. I carry about 1050 gold, 2 lockpicks, rapier, paralysis poison x3, eye-setting, keystone, bronze disc, parchment map, Unbound note. Still unknown: the Root, the Eye, the Collector, and what is in the cellar.
