I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the keep key and 100 gold. I lifted the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store key. Jars of Hist sap from Jal-Veesa tree-minder are stacked by the cellar stair, though the fort has no Hist of its own. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the locked inner cellar door and refused me, persuasion and illusion failed; Never-Writes-Twice keeps that key. Something moans behind it; I do not know who or why. I still carry the rapier, lockpicks, seals, recipe, about 1150 gold, and the stolen store key. Next: Bell Station near Lilmoth for the drowned tower, or pick the cellar door lock later, or ask Never-Writes-Twice. Still unknown: the Root, the Eye, the Collector, and what is in the cellar.
