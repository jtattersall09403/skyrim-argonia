Fort Chalkmoth done. I killed three Sarpa raiders for captain Sakeeus-Wan (keep key plus 100 gold), lifted the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store key, and took a Jar of Hist Sap tied with Jal-Veesa knot. The jars go down to the cellar stair; the fort has no Hist. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku refused me the cellar door; Never-Writes-Twice holds its key, and something moans behind it. I still carry the Unbound recipe for the Seal. Unknown: who is in the cellar, why the order feeds them sap, who Jal-Veesa is. Next: Bell Station via boat from Lilmoth for the marked drowned tower, and question Never-Writes-Twice about the cellar. Vanin is digging ahead of us.
