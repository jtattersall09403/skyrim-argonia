Fort Chalkmoth: killed the three Sarpa raiders, got the keep key and 100 gold from wall captain Sakeeus-Wan. Lifted the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store key and opened his store, but it holds nothing of note. The cellar door is in the fort yard and needs its own key, which the gaoler Tsleel-Vaku says Never-Writes-Twice keeps in Helstrom, so it cannot be picked. Tsleel-Vaku refuses entry; his only words were that they sleep and dream down there. My persuade and illusion attempts did not change the journal. Theyne says Hist sap jars tied with Jal-Veesa knot come up the mesa road each season and go down the cellar stair. The fort has no Hist and its people take eggs and dead to the Noota Nara tree. I still carry the Unbound recipe for the Seal. Next: get the cellar key from Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom, who dismissed this fort earlier and so looks suspicious, or go to Bell Station for the tower. I never got a look at the cellar. The Collector Vanin is also digging ahead of us.
