Notes for a friend picking up my save: The Cellar Door is done. I got into Fort Chalkmoth by the yard trapdoor into the cellar guardroom. Sap jars tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot sit in the quartermaster store, though the fort has no Hist. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku would not be persuaded or charmed and says Never-Writes-Twice holds the inner door key; it cannot be picked. Something moans behind it, and I do not know who or why the order feeds it sap. The captain Sakeeus-Wan would open the keep if I killed three Sarpa raiders; I did not. Next step: Bell Station, east of Lilmoth by boat, to ask the divers which drowned tower the marker stone shows. Still unknown: what the Root and Eye really are, what is in the cellar, and who the Collector is. I carry the rapier, about 1050 gold, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, the keystone, eye-setting, bronze disc, parchment map and Unbound note.
