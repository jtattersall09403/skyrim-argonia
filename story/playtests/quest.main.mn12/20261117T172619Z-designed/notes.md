Notes: I killed three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the keep key plus 100 gold (about 1150 gold now). In the quartermaster store (lock picked) I found stacked Hist sap jars tied with Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, in a fort with no Hist. Quartermaster Theyne Indarys says they come up every season and go down the stair, and he asks nothing. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the locked cellar door and says Never-Writes-Twice keeps its key. My persuasion and illusion on him failed. Something moans behind the door. I do not know who or what it is, or why the order feeds it sap. I carry lockpicks, a rapier, 3 paralysis poisons, the Fort Chalkmoth keep key, disc, keystone and eye-setting. Next: go to Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth to ask the divers which drowned spire is marked, and later press Never-Writes-Twice about the cellar.
