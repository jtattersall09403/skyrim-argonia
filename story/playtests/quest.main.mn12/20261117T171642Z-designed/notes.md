1. Fort Chalkmoth (Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara): I killed the three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the keep key plus 100 gold (about 1150 gold now). I picked the quartermaster Theyne Indarys's store lock. Jars of Hist sap tied with Jal-Veesa's knot come up the mesa road each season and go down the cellar stair. The fort has no Hist of its own. 2. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the cellar door in the guardroom. He says only: They sleep, and they dream. The door opens only with its key, which he says Never-Writes-Twice keeps. I cannot pick it. I cannot travel to Helstrom or to her from here, so I am stuck at the door. 3. Next: get the cellar key from Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom, which is suspicious since she dismissed the fort. Or go to Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth for the drowned tower. I still carry the Unbound recipe for the Seal. Vanin the Collector is digging ahead of us.
