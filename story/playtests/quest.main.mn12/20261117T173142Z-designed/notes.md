Notes: I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I got in unseen by the yard trapdoor into the cellar guardroom. The quartermaster store above holds jars of Hist sap tied with the knot of the Jal-Veesa tree-minder, in a fort with no Hist of its own. Something moans behind the locked inner cellar door. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku refused persuasion and illusion, and I could not pickpocket him. He carries no key. Never-Writes-Twice keeps the key. The door cannot be picked. Captain Sakeeus-Wan offered entry and 100 gold for killing 3 Sarpa raiders on the western approach, which I did not do. Still unknown: who or what is down there and why the order feeds it sap. Next: go to Bell Station east of Lilmoth by boat and ask the divers which drowned tower is cut deeper. I carry about 1050 gold, 2 lockpicks, a rapier, paralysis poison x3, an Unbound note, the eye-setting, keystone, bronze disc and a parchment map.
