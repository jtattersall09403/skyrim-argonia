I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan, got the keep key and 100 gold (about 1150 gold now), picked the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store, and found sap jars tied with Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot by the cellar stair, in a fort with no Hist. The order gaoler Tsleel-Vaku refused me at the locked cellar door; persuasion and illusion failed; he has no key, Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. Something moans below. I did not pick the cellar door. Unknown: who or what is in the cellar, why the order feeds it sap, what the Root and Eye truly are, who the Collector is. Next: Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth to learn which drowned tower the marker stone cut deepest. I still carry the rapier, lockpicks, 3 Unbound Seals, 3 Grey Seals, Seal recipe, Pale Harvest key, disc, keystone and eye-setting.
