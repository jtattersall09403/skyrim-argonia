## 1. Stage 10 (start): The Cellar Door

Journal: Never-Writes-Twice says Fort Chalkmoth is a fort like any other, but a wounded Unbound scout told me to ask what moans in its cellar. This is my own errand, not that of my order, the Toh-Reel-Ka. Fort Chalkmoth is the Archwardens' fort on the Chixathe Mesa above Noota Nara. I'm to get into the fort and see its cellar. The keep's door opens only with the wall captain's key and cannot be picked, so he may let me in, or I may find another way in unseen.
