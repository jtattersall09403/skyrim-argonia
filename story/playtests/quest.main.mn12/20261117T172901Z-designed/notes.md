Notes: I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed the three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan (got 100 gold and the keep key), picked the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store lock, and saw sap jars tied with Jal-Veesa tree-minder knots stacked by the cellar stair, in a fort with no Hist. Gaoler Tsleel-Vaku refused me (persuade and illusion both failed); he has no key, Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. The cellar door is locked and something moans behind it; I did not try picking it. Unknown: who or what is down there, why the order feeds it sap, what the Root and Eye are, and who the Collector is. I carry about 1150 gold, a rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, keystone, eye-setting, bronze disc, map, Unbound note and keep key.
