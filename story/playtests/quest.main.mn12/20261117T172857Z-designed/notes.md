Notes: I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed the three Sarpa raiders for wall captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the keep key and 100 gold, so I now have about 1150 gold. I picked the quartermaster store lock. By the cellar stair are jars of Hist sap tied with the knot of the tree-minder of Jal-Veesa, in a fort with no Hist of its own. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the cellar door. Persuasion and Illusion failed on him, and the door opens only with its key, which Never-Writes-Twice keeps. Something moans behind it, and I do not know what. I still carry the rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, the Unbound-Note, Voh-Vastei armour, eye-setting, keystone, grave-stake, bronze disc, parchment map and the keep key. Next step: Bell Station, east of Lilmoth, where the divers know which drowned tower the marker stone shows. Unknown: what is in the cellar, why the order feeds it, what the Root and Eye are, and who the Collector is.
