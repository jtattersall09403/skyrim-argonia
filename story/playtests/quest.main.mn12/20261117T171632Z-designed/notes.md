Fort Chalkmoth: I killed the three Sarpa raiders for captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the Keep Key and 100 gold. I picked the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store lock. He says jars of Hist sap tied with Jal-Veesa knot come up the mesa road each season and go down the stair; the fort has no Hist of its own. In the guardroom the gaoler Tsleel-Vaku says the cellar is closed and he has no key; Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. My persuasion and illusion both got me only: They sleep, and they dream. Lockpicking the cellar door fails, as it opens only with its key. I have not seen the cellar. Next: get the key from Never-Writes-Twice in Helstrom (she dismissed this, which is suspicious), or find another way, such as the trapdoor in the yard, which I did not try. Bell Station tower hunt is still pending. I carry 1150 gold, lockpicks, the Unbound recipe and the other items.
