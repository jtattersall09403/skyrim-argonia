Notes: I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I took the yard trapdoor into the cellar guardroom unseen. The wall captain Sakeeus-Wan would have opened the keep for killing three Sarpa raiders (100 gold), but I skipped that. The quartermaster store holds jars of Hist sap tied with the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, though the fort has no Hist. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the locked inner cellar door and refused persuasion and illusion; he has no key, and Never-Writes-Twice keeps it. Something moans behind the door. I do not know who or what is down there or why the order feeds it sap. I carry 2 lockpicks, Unbound-Note, Voh-Vastei-Armour, Eye-Setting, Keystone, Grave-Stake, 3 Paralysis-Poison, Bronze-Disc, Parchment-Map, Rapier, 1050 gold. Next: Bell Station by boat from Lilmoth, to ask the divers which drowned spire the marker stone cuts deeper. Later: ask Never-Writes-Twice about the cellar key.
