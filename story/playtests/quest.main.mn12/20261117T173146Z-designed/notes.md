Notes: I am Toh-Wo of the Toh-Reel-Ka. At Fort Chalkmoth I killed three Sarpa raiders for the wall captain Sakeeus-Wan and got the keep key and 100 gold. I picked the quartermaster Theyne Indarys store lock. The sap jars there carry the Jal-Veesa tree-minder knot, and the fort has no Hist. The gaoler Tsleel-Vaku guards the cellar door, which is locked. He refused my persuasion and illusion, and says Never-Writes-Twice holds the key. Something moans behind it. I do not know what it is or why the order feeds it sap. I did not try picking the cellar door. I carry about 1150 gold, 2 lockpicks, the rapier, 3 paralysis poisons, the keystone, eye-setting, bronze disc, parchment map and the keep key. Next: go to Bell Station, an islet east of Lilmoth, reached by boat from Lilmoth, to ask the divers which drowned tower the marker stone shows. Later I could ask Never-Writes-Twice or the Jal-Veesa tree-minder about the cellar.
