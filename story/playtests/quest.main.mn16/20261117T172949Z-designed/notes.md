I hold all three seal-stones (Blackrose, Gemin, Seal-Stone of the Kings), plus about 350 gold, Reel-Ka armour, rapier, 2 lockpicks, 3 paralysis poisons, sap jar, keystone, eye-setting, and the Bronze Key. I lifted the key from Hesh-Var at Vorannuhl and took the third stone from the vault without fighting; the wraith Lord of Vorannuhl was there but did not attack. Next: bring the stones to Never-Writes-Twice in Nesh-Deeka House in Helstrom, then the Kings Gate under Teku-Sul Spire (dive from Bell Station). The Gemin tablet says the Eye used in place at the Root strengthens every tree, but lifted from its place it cuts every tree off and the weak die alone. Do not lift the Eye. Rivals: the Unbound (drain trees) and Collector Ennodius Vanin at Blackrose. Unknown: why the Unbound drain trees, what moans in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar, how Nesh-Deeka knows about the Unbound.
