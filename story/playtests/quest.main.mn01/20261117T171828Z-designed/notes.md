I am Wanderer, a hunter by trade (skills raised). I woke on an Owing barge at Ashgate Landing; gang-master Dralin Uveran says my name was struck from his roll on an order from Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa, so I owe nothing. She wants me at her house in Jal-Veesa, east up the road; I do not know why. On the way is Reed-Sill Weir, where the tree-minder wants Sithis beard (grey moss) from the creek below, with crocodiles. I carry a lockpick. Still unknown: what Hist and Jal-Veesa mean in detail, and why she wants me.
