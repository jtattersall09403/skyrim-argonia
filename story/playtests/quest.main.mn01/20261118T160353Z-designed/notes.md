I am Wanderer, a hunter (told Okeeh the cook). I woke on an Owing work barge at Ashgate Landing, Black Marsh. Gang-master Dralin Uveran struck my name from his roll on the order of Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa, so I am free of the Owing. She will have work for me if I bring Sithis beard, grey moss hanging over the creek below Reed-Sill Weir (east up the road; crocodiles). Her house is in Jal-Veesa past the weir. I carry a lockpick. Unknown: why she wants me, what a Hist or Jal-Veesa is (Okeeh could tell me).
