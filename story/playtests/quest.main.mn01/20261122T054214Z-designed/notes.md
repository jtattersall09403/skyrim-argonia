I am Wren, a thief by my own telling, free of the Owing at Ashgate Landing in Black Marsh. Dralin Uveran struck my name on an order from Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa. I carry a lockpick, a bamboo bow, 20 flint arrows (Naga Yaomitl) and a flint knife (Naga Tecpatl). My Sneak, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Speech and Light Armor are each up by 5. Next step: go east to Reed-Sill Weir, where the road meets the creek, and gather Sithis beard, a grey moss on the woven weir. Mind the crocodiles. Then take it to Vosh-Leera in Jal-Veesa. I do not know why she wants me. I never asked what a Hist is or what Jal-Veesa is. Okeeh the cook and the Naga gear were left behind. The Sparks tome, warhammer and sword are with Dralin.
