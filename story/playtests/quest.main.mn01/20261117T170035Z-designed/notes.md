I am Tarin, a self-declared thief, with a lockpick. I woke on an Owing barge at Ashgate Landing in Black Marsh. Okeeh the cook helped; Dralin Uveran the Dunmer gang-master told me my name was struck from his roll on an order from Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa, so I owe no months. She wants me at her house in Jal-Veesa, east up the road; I do not know why. On the way, the town tree-minder wants Sithis beard (grey moss) from the creek below Reed-Sill Weir, where there are crocodiles. I did not ask Okeeh about the Hist, Jal-Veesa or the Owing, so those are still unknown to me.
