Dralin Uveran, the Dunmer gang-master, struck my name on orders from Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa, who speaks for the town elders and tree. I carry a bamboo bow, 20 flint arrows (pick them up after use), a flint knife, 2 lockpicks, 0 gold. Next: go east up the road to Reed-Sill Weir, the weir-wardens camp, pick grey Sithis beard moss from the woven weir (watch for crocodiles under it), then take it to Vosh-Leera house in Jal-Veesa. Unknown: why she wants me, what a Hist is, and what Jal-Veesa tree is. Okeeh the cook is in the barge hold and Dralin is on the landing if you want to ask them more.
