I am Tarin. I woke on an Owing barge at Ashgate Landing, a reed-cutters camp in Black Marsh, west of Loriasel. I named Sneak, Lockpicking, Archery, One-Handed and Light Armor to Okeeh the cook; each rose by five. Gang-master Dralin Uveran says my name was struck from his roll this morning on an order from Vosh-Leera, root-herald of Jal-Veesa, so I owe no months. She wants me at her house in Jal-Veesa, east up the road; no one knows why. On the way, at Reed-Sill Weir where the road meets the creek, the town tree-minder wants Sithis beard, a grey moss hanging over the creek below the weir; watch for crocodiles. I carry a lockpick. Unknown: why Vosh-Leera wants me, who the friends in high places are, what a Hist is.
