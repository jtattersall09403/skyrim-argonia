I work for Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom; her order listens. At Vicrium, Ah-Zish said he would give the key but it never reached my pack, and the lock could not be picked. I went by the old adit and took the Old Parchment Map from the knights strongbox. Nesh-Deeka named me Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker, second rank, paid 200 gold, and I chose the rapier. I now report to Never-Writes-Twice, who read the map: the crag is the Grey Tooth, seamark of Skeith Isle, reached by boat to Archon and Arorha. She warned me not to listen to Cuts-the-Old-Knot, a former order member gone mad on sap. I carry the rapier and the map, plus about 900 gold, and earlier items like the keystone. Unknown: what the Root and Eye are, what the seven towers are, and how the order knows what it knows. Next: Skeith Isle via Archon or Arorha.
