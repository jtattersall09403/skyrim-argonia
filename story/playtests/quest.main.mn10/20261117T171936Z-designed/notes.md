At Vicrium Ah-Zish key did not reach my pack despite persuasion and 100 gold (gold was unchanged), so I went through the old adit and took the knights map from the strongbox. Nesh-Deeka said this is a step towards the Eye of Argonia at the Root and that the Unbound had not reached Vicrium; she said only we listen. I carry the rapier, about 950 gold, lockpicks, Grey Seal x3, Pale Harvest key and other items, the disc, keystone and eye-setting. Never-Writes-Twice warned me of Cuts-the-Old-Knot, once of the order, mad on sap, killed her minders.
