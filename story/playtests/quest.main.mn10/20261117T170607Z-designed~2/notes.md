I work for Nesh-Deeka in Helstrom and am now Toh-Wo, Secret-Speaker, second rank, and I report to her officer Never-Writes-Twice. I paid and persuaded Ah-Zish but the key never reached my pack. I had to pickpocket it, and that worked. I took the old parchment map from the knights strongbox at Vicrium: seven towers in the sea and a tooth crag. Never-Writes-Twice says the crag is the Grey Tooth, seamark of Skeith Isle, reached by boat to Archon and Arorha, and she marked it. I carry the rapier, the map, and about 900 gold by my own count, though the journal listed 0 gold. I also carry the keystone and other items from before. Beware Cuts-the-Old-Knot, a former order member gone mad on sap. Still unknown: the Root, the Eye, the seven towers, how the order listens. The Eurydike tomb near Gideon is still pending, with the Unbound and the Collector competing.
