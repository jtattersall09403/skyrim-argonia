They are a thieves' guild-style group. As far as I could tell, their code is to take from people who can bear the loss and not hurt anyone. I took the work because it was the only lead I had, and the code seemed fair enough to go along with. I'm not sure how far I trust them yet.

3. **The most confusing moment.** It was the lockbox claims. All three clients said an item was theirs, and the journal told me the claims didn't add up. It also hinted at a fairer option, selling each person their own item. When I tried that, my Speech was too low, so the better route was closed. I had no way to know that before I committed, and I ended up giving everything to Neeta-Haj, which felt arbitrary. The ship ride being instant, and the locks already being open, were also odd, but they were less confusing than that.

4. **What I'd tell the writer.** The setup is clear and the lockbox dilemma is a good idea. Here is what I'd change:
   - Show me the Speech requirement before I pick, or give me another way to reach the fair outcome.
   - Make the lockpicking and the dive matter. I never needed the picks, and I never saw any water.
   - Let me see the consequences for Okan-Tei and Dar-Zeel, so the choice feels like it counted.
   - Give the dock NPCs, like Sits-By-The-Water, some reason to exist, or cut them.
