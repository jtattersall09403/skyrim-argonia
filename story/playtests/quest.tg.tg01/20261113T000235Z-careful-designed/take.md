Short and easy to follow. Two quests: get a necklace back for the thieves' guild, then empty a drowned lockbox and decide who gets the contents. The writing was clear and I always knew what to do next and why. Tilts-The-Jug and Bent-Spine had a consistent code: take from those who can bear it, and harm no one. The lockbox quest was the best part, because three people each lied about an item. The journal told me their claims did not hold up. It also offered a fairer option, selling each their own item, but my Speech of 15 was too low for it. That left me handing all three items to Neeta-Haj, which felt like a worse outcome than I wanted, and I would have liked to see that gating before I committed. Some things did not make sense. Both strongboxes and lockboxes were already unlocked, so the lockpicks were never used. The ship ride was instant. I never saw the dive or any water, which was a bit flat. I also never found out who the other dock people were, such as Sits-By-The-Water, because I did not need them. I would like to know what the Chapter asks of me next, and whether Okan-Tei and Dar-Zeel come back angry.
