Short and easy to follow. The quest givers named exactly where to go, and the travel command meant I never had to navigate. I understood the Night-Reed Chapter: thieves who take only from those who can bear the loss and harm no one. The lockbox job was the best part. It was a small moral puzzle: sell the pledges back, give all three to one client, or something worse. I picked the fair-price route, and it ended with all three clients owning their lies. Some things felt off. Both the strongbox and the lockbox were already unlocked, so the lockpicks did nothing, and the three speech options were oddly duplicated. The journal said the clients haggled me down to half price, but the dialogue said they paid full price, and my speech of 15 against the 30 required made the persuade option feel meaningless. The world felt thin, with nine dock people I never met, and I would have liked to meet them. I would play on to find out what the Chapter asks of me next, but the story stops here for now.
