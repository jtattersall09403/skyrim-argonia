I enjoyed the start. The writing was clear, the quest chain was easy to follow, and the Night-Reed Chapter is a nice idea: thieves who take only from those who can bear the loss and who harm no one. I understood who each person was and what they wanted. S'Tabi sent me to Tilts-The-Jug, who sent me to Bent-Spine, so the story moved along. I liked having the choice between lockpicking, persuading and paying for the key. My Speech was only 15, so persuasion was out and I picked the lock. I also liked that the game asked whether anyone saw me commit the crime. Things that bothered me: I picked the strongbox even though Tilts-The-Jug had told me to take the necklace. Choosing "What will you give me for the figurine, as it is?" ended the sale immediately at half price. I did not realise it was a one-way choice, and I would have wanted a warning. The three clients were clearly lying, which felt a bit odd, and my low Speech meant I took the worse deal twice. Walking onto the ship and then waiting did nothing, so I had to jump straight to the marker. That was a little confusing, but it worked. I was left with one open objective, selling the Kothringi Glass Beads to Dar-Zeel, when I ran out of commands. I want to know what the Chapter asks of me next and whether the clients are connected. I would play more.
