Short and easy to follow. Two quests, each with a clear objective, and a thief-guild theme: take from those who can bear it, harm no one, sell no silence. The second quest was the better one. Three liars each claimed a pledge, and I got to expose them and sell each item back. The dialogue was good. Weak spots: the locks were already open, so the lockpicks never mattered. The game also did not say that my speech of 15 was too low for the full price. Because of that I took half price from each client and got 95 gold instead of 185. The persuade options were listed twice, which looked like a bug. The dock had many named NPCs with only one line each, so it felt empty. I would play on to see what the Chapter wants next, but nothing is left to do for now.
