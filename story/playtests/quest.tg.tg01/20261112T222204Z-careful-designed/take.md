Short and smooth. I was sent from S'Tabi to Tilts-The-Jug, fetched a necklace from an unlocked strongbox, was paid 100 gold, and was taken to the Night-Reed Chapter and made a Lookout. The quest flow and markers were clear and the travel commands made it easy. Much of the dialogue shows placeholder text ("not yet written; the gist"), which broke immersion, though the gist was understandable. The strongbox being unlocked made the lockpicks and the speech/gold options pointless, and I never spoke to the thief, so the "no harm" condition had no weight. I know little about the world or the people yet. A new quest (The Drowned Lockbox) started, and I am mildly curious about it, but the people feel thin.
