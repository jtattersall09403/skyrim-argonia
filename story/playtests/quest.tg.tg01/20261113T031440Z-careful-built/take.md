I enjoyed the start. S'Tabi pointed me to Tilts-The-Jug, who sent me to lift a necklace from Keeps-What-Floats without hurting him. Sits-By-The-Water told me he is a harmless poor thief. I had no gold and could not talk him round, so I picked his strongbox. The game made me say whether I was seen, which felt honest but a bit odd. I got paid, joined the Night-Reed Chapter under Lilmoth, and Bent-Spine gave me a job: empty a drowned merchant's lockbox of pledges before three clients can claim items from it. The clients had sympathetic stories (family beads, a mother's scrying charm), and Bent-Spine's warning against blackmail makes me expect a moral choice about who gets what. I understood the tone and the people, and I want to see what happens. Two small things were confusing: the first "go Strong Box" took me to the wrong box far away, and the command budget ran out just as the main decision was coming up. I never dove for the lockbox.
