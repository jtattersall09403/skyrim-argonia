I enjoyed the early going. The two quests were clear and the people were easy to tell apart. S'Tabi sent me to Tilts-The-Jug, who sent me to Bent-Spine. I took a necklace from a strongbox and then looted a drowned merchant's lockbox. The journal and objectives always told me what to do next. The Night-Reed Chapter has a decent rule: take from those who can bear the loss and harm no one. That gave the jobs some moral texture. Two things bothered me. First, I could not afford the 60 gold key at the start, and picking the lock had me choose between "seen" and "unseen". The game said it was not going to follow up on a bounty, which broke the feel a little. Second, my Persuade attempt on Neeta-Haj failed with no hint of how to improve it. I fell back to the half-price sale, and the journal then said I had not pressed her. I did not see that coming. The game also told me the clients were lying before I had found anything out myself, so I never got to work it out. The dive was skipped entirely, because the lockbox was a short walk away. I stopped with the Okan-Tei charm sale still open and I would like to see how it plays out. I did not reach the end, because I ran out of commands.
