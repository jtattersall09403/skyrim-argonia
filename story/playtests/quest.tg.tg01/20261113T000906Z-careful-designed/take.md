Short and clear. I understood the flow: S'Tabi sent me to Tilts-The-Jug, I got a stolen necklace back without hurting the thief, then Bent-Spine had me empty a drowned lockbox. Both quests turned on the same idea: take without harming, and do not blackmail. I liked that the three clients each lied about their pledge and I could sell each item back instead of choosing a favourite. The people were thin, though: lots of nameless "a person" entries and little personality beyond one quest line each. Things that made no sense: the lockbox was already unlocked, and the journal said I could not haggle for full price but the sale amounts were half of what the characters said they would pay, so the dialogue and the gold did not match. The ship trip also happened with no clear sign of sailing, just a wait and a changed location. I would want to know what the Chapter asks next.
