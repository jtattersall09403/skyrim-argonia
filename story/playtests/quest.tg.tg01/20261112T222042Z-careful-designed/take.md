The first quest, A Dry Hand in a Wet City, was easy to follow. S'Tabi sent me to the boat captain, who sent me to fetch a necklace. I took it from an unlocked strongbox, returned it for 100 gold, and travelled to the Night-Reed Chapter. It was clear what to do at each step, and the go and marker commands made travel painless. Problems: nearly all dialogue was placeholder text ("not yet written; the gist"), so the characters had no voice and I learned little about who they were or why the Chapter matters. The boat captain was labelled "Argonian Boat Captain" even though she is Tilts-The-Jug, which confused me. The lockpicks were never needed because the box was unlocked. The theft felt too easy, and nobody reacted to me taking it in front of a crowd. Flaa Heet was also the person at the marker where I expected Bent-Spine. A new quest, The Drowned Lockbox, began right away. I am mildly curious about it, but I stopped at the end of the first quest.
