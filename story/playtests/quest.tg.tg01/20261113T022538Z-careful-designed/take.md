I enjoyed it. The tone is quiet and specific: a dock thief, a fence under a swamp city, and a guild that steals but does not hurt anyone. The quests chain smoothly. I always knew my next objective, and the journal entries were clear. Talking to people felt like Skyrim dialogue. The theme is consistent: take from those who can bear the loss, and do not harm anyone. The game seems to watch how you handled each step. Journal lines like "I did not press her" and "half its fair price" show my choices being recorded. I failed every persuasion check, which I did not expect. The persuade options did not explain what made them hard. I would also have liked to know my gold and Speech skill. Two things made little sense. The game called me "seen" for a crime and then said it would go no further, so the crime had no consequence. And the three clients all being liars felt like a puzzle with a hidden answer. I ran out of commands with Dar-Zeel still open, so the Drowned Lockbox quest was not finished. I do want to see what the Chapter asks of me next, and whether my choices change how Tilts-The-Jug and Bent-Spine treat me.
