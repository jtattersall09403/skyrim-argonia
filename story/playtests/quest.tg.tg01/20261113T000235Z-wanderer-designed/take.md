Short and easy to follow. The quest chain was clear: dock thief job, then the drowned lockbox. The NPC dialogue was readable and the markers were helpful. I liked that both jobs had a moral shape. The necklace job insisted on no harm. The lockbox job had three clients who were all lying, and I had to choose who got the goods. Problems: the strongbox and lockbox were both already unlocked, so the lockpicks I was given never mattered. The fair-price option for Neeta-Haj needed Speech 30 and I had 15. Option 4 let her accept in dialogue but did nothing in the game, which was confusing. Because of that I gave everything to one client, and the other two lose out and resent me. Dive and swim steps were skipped by the text interface. I would like to know what the Chapter does next, but nothing is active in my journal now.
