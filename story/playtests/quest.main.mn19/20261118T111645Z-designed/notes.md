I lifted the Eye of Argonia at the Root. The trees are parted for good. About three in ten died, and Jal-Veesa and Teel-Fen Weir were abandoned. The dreams have ended and the order is broken. The Unbound are friendly and call me Knot-Breaker. The Toh-Reel-Ka are hostile and will hunt me, and no Hist will bless me. The Unbound broke open the Fort Chalkmoth cellar. I had not verified that myself, and I did not go there. I learned the spell The Eyes Gaze. I carry the Eye, 1650 gold, the rapier, the Order-Pike, the Eye-Setting, the Keystone, the Bronze-Key, paralysis poison x3 and two lockpicks. Cuts-the-Old-Knot showed as present at the Root but would not speak to me. Still open: Fort Chalkmoth key and cellar, and Ennodius Vanin the Collector. The three seal-stones stay in the gate. I did not loot the hydras treasure.
