I set the three seal-stones in the Kings Gate (they stay in the gate) and killed the hydra Guardian of the Root in Guardian Hall. I did not loot its treasure. The Door to The Root is at the far end of the hall; I have not entered. The Eye choice remains: use it in place (heals trees, but the order sees through them) or lift it (ends the dreams, kills about three in ten trees); the order says not to lift it. Never-Writes-Twice made me Ka-Deelith; I killed Cuts-the-Old-Knot, who said she lied and keeps dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar. I have not verified that. I carry the pike, gauntlets, boots, her Draught, Eye-Setting, Keystone, gold, poison, sap jar, rapier, Warden Vault Key and Bronze Key. Open: Fort Chalkmoth key and cellar, Ennodius Vanin the Collector.
