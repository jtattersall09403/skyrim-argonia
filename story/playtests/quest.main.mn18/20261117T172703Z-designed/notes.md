I am Ka-Deelith of the Toh-Reel-Ka. I set the three seal-stones in the Kings Gate (they stay there), and with Cuts-the-Old-Knot I killed the hydra Guardian of the Root in Guardian Hall. Its body holds treasure that I have not looted yet. A door at the far end leads to the Root and the Eye. I still carry Eye-Setting, Keystone, gold, poison and the rest. Final choice at the Eye: use it in place (heals every tree, dreams continue) or lift it (ends the dreams, about three in ten trees die, the order breaks). I promised Cuts nothing about it. Unverified: Cuts claims the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar and that Never-Writes-Twice lied and kept the key. I could not check this.
