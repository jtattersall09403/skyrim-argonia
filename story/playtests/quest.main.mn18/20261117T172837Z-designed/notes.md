I set the three seal-stones in the Kings' Gate (they stay there) and it opened. With Cuts-the-Old-Knot I killed the hydra Guardian of the Root in Guardian's Hall. Its body holds treasure, which I have not looted. A door at the hall's far end leads to the Root and the Eye of Argonia. The final choice is still mine: use the Eye in place (heals every tree, dreams continue) or lift it (ends the dreams, about three in ten trees die, the order breaks). I promised Cuts nothing about the Eye. Her claims about the sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar and Never-Writes-Twice lying are unverified. I carry Eye-Setting, Keystone, Grave-Stake, 3 Paralysis Poison, Sap-Jar, Order-Pike, Reelka armour, Rapier, Bronze Key, Bronze Disc, Parchment Map, Unbound-Note, 2 lockpicks, 1650 gold.
