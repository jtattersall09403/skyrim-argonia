I set the three seal-stones in the Kings Gate (they stay in it), and the gate opened. I killed the hydra Guardian of the Root in Guardian Hall. Its body holds treasure which I did not loot, and a door at the far end leads to the Root. Cuts-the-Old-Knot is in the hall but had nothing more to say. I am Ka-Deelith of Toh-Reel-Ka and carry the life-drinking pike, gauntlets and boots, Never-Writes-Twice Draught (I killed her), Eye-Setting, Keystone, gold, poison, sap jar, rapier, Warden Vault Key and Bronze Key. Unverified: Cuts-the-Old-Knot claims the order keeps sap-fed dreamers in the Fort Chalkmoth cellar and that Never-Writes-Twice lied and kept the key. Still open: the Eye choice at the Root (use in place heals trees but lets the order see through them; lift it ends dreams but kills about three in ten trees; the order says do not lift it), the Fort Chalkmoth key and cellar, and Ennodius Vanin the Collector.
