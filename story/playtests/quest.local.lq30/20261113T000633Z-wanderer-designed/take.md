Short, clear quest: find a missing Agaceph man, kill a viper, then choose between hauling him home or letting him finish his walk-out. I understood it throughout and liked that the choice was a real moral one with no obvious right answer. Dialogue was a bit repetitive (the gate warden topics were generic), and leaving Haj-Wan worrying felt slightly unresolved. The world has only hinted at more (Dwemer ruins, boat captain Sells-Dear), and I would like to see where that goes, but nothing further was offered.
