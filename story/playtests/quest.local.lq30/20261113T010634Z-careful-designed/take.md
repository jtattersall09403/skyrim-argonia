Short, tidy quest. I understood who everyone was: Haj-Wan waiting at the gate, the levy warden who will not interfere, and Sei-Wan on his walk-out. The choice at the end was the best part. Letting him walk home alone meant Haj-Wan keeps worrying, and that felt like a real cost. The viper fight was one command with no tension, and travel was instant, so it was more a story than a game. Dialogue was well written. I would want to know more about the Agaceph and Helstrom, but there was nothing left to do and no new objective, so I stopped.
