Short, clear quest. I understood who everyone was: a gate warden, a worried kinswoman, and a proud young man stuck up in the roots. The choice at the end was a good one. It was a real dilemma between respecting his pride and easing Haj-Wan's worry, and the game did not tell me which was right. I liked that Haj-Wan's own words hinted at the answer. Travel and combat were abstract (one command each), so there was little tension, and the viper fight was trivial. The setting is only lightly sketched, with Dwemer ruins and Agaceph customs. I would want to see more of Helstrom. Leaving Haj-Wan unaware felt slightly uncomfortable, which I think was intended. The journal ended empty of objectives, so I stopped.
