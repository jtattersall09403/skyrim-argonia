I never found a quest. The game opened with a hostile viper, which I killed in one command, and then dropped me in Helstrom with an empty journal and no marker. Weesa-Nur and Sells-Dear have distinct, grumpy, mercantile voices that I enjoyed, and the NPCs gave plenty of directions, but I could not use them. "go" only accepts places already on my map, and I only knew two. The dock, bridge, stone tower and Dwarven ruins all failed as destinations, and the map never grew. Directions like "walk north-east until the columns are on your right" were also hard to follow without a way to walk step by step. I stopped because I could find nothing to act on. I would want mentioned places added to the map, or a gentle first objective. I did not get far enough to know what the story is.
