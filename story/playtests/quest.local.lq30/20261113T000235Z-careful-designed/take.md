Short and pleasant. Wandering Helstroms NPCs gave mostly flavour and directions, and I had no lead until I tried Haj-Wan, the silent one at the gate. Her quest about Sei-Wan was well set up: a real dilemma between her wish to have him home and his pride in the walk-out. I had already killed the viper at the start, which made the problem resolve neatly, though it was luck. I chose to let him walk home and stay silent, and it felt like a considered, humane choice. Weak points: the opening gave no direction, so I only found the quest because I talked to everyone; most topics are generic; the duplicated persuade option was odd. I would like to see what comes next in Helstrom and Stormhold, but the quest ended without a follow-up.
