Short and easy to follow. I met two guards at Helstrom gate, got a clear quest from Haj-Wan, found Sei-Wan, killed a viper and let him walk home on his own. The choice at the end felt meaningful: his pride versus his kins worry. The tone was nice. But several lines were placeholder text, like "[not yet written; the gist:]", which broke immersion. The quest was also very small, with no real challenge or exploration. Gih-Xaleel mentioned Dwemer ruins, Sells-Dear and Weesa-Nur, and I would like to see more of Helstrom. Nothing is left in my journal, so I am stopping.
