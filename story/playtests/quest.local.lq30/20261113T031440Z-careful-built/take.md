Short and pleasant. I started with no explanation and a viper beside a silent man, so I killed the viper and wandered Helstrom asking questions. The NPCs gave me directions and flavour, and Haj-Wan eventually handed me the quest. I had already done half of it by accident, which was funny but slightly odd, because Sei-Wan said nothing useful until I knew the story. The choice at the end was a good small dilemma: honour his pride or ease his kin. I picked pride, since Haj-Wan said she would accept that if he could walk. Complaints: travel by name only works for places I have already been told about, so the Ruins were not reachable. Several topics were repetitive, and the directions were confusing. I would like to know what happens when Sei-Wan comes home, and whether the Dwemer ruins hold more quests. The world feels sparse, but the tone is distinct.
