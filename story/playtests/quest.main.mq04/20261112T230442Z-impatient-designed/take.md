Short and easy to follow. The markers took me through everything, and each quest handed me the next one cleanly. I liked the Hist-cut mystery and the Mnemic Egg hook at the end, and I would want to see what the cell does with all that sap. The weak spots: much of the dialogue showed placeholder text like "[not yet written; the gist:]", and the combat was a single command. I also never learned why the woman with the knotted cord spared me, which was the question that interested me most. I leaned on Pays-Late because my Speech was too low, and then chose to return the sap instead of handing it to the Reed. Neither choice seemed to cost me anything, so they felt less weighty than they should have. Overall it was playable and intriguing, but thin.
