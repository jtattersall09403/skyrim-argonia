I enjoyed it. The story held together: the burnt camp, the cutters' jar, the cut Hist at Low-Reed, and the idea of a Mnemic Egg all point at one scheme, and I always knew who people were and what they wanted. Pays-Late was the best part. I leaned on him with a threat and he told me he would remember it. Handing the sap back to his tree rather than to the Reed felt like a real choice. Some things were clumsy. The Pays-Late dialogue did not advance the quest when I showed him the gum pot, and only the threat option finished it. The topic list did not refresh to the "who took the sap" question he offered. My Speech of 15 against a gate of 25 felt harsh at the start. The guards on the rise attacked without any chance to talk. The quest ended with no open objective, so I stopped. I do want to know who the cutters work for, who the woman with the knotted cord is, and why she let me live. Kaska-Meen also seemed to be holding something back.
