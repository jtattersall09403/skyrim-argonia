Enjoyable, compact chain of errands. I understood the arc: the cutters are draining Hist sap and killing eggs, with a hint of a Mnemic Egg plot. Pays-Late was the best bit, a believable man hiding something shameful; the threat option versus the gum-pot route worked well, though my Speech 15 locked out the persuasion path. Travel by command skipped the wasps and the shallows, so Helstrom felt thin and Kaska-Meen was a dead end. The Reed and the woman with the knotted cord are unresolved, and I want to know why she spared me. Some journal entries were duplicated, and I never saw my choice of what to do with the jars matter. I would keep playing.
