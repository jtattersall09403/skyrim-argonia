Make that sort of moment the norm. Show me clearly when a skill check locks me out of an option, and what the stakes are. Fix the duplicate journal entries. Let my choices, like what I did with the sap, visibly change what Nesh-Deeka says afterwards. Fast travel skipped the wasps and shallows, so Helstrom and Kaska-Meen felt thin. Give Kaska-Meen something to do, or a reason to come back. And please pay off the knotted-cord woman soon, because I want to know.
