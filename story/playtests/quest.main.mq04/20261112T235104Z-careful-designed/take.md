Short, clear and readable. I always knew who to see next and why, and the Reed and cutters mystery pulled me along. The Mnemic Egg hook at the end made me want more. Choices had weight: I chose to return the sap to the village and make Pays-Late confess, and to tell the Nisswo rather than the tree-minder. Rough spots: the Pays-Late scene was confusing. The journal did not advance when he gave information through the gentler options, and only the threat option completed the objective, so I ended up threatening him after he had already talked. The gum pot reveal also did not register as progress. The Speech-25 option let me through at Speech 15. Fights were one-line outcomes with no tension. The quest chain ended with no open objective, so I stopped there. I would want to continue: who the cord woman is, what the cutters want, and why I was spared.
