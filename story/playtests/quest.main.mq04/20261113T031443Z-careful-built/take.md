I liked this. The writing is tight and the Argonian names are memorable. Each quest handed off cleanly to the next, so I always knew what to do and why. I understood the story: the cutters are draining Hist sap, which may mean a Mnemic Egg, and the Veiled Reed wants the interior warned without being named. The village scene was the best part. Pays-Late is a sympathetic man who hid a crime out of shame. I threatened him because my Speech failed and I had no lockpicks, and the game made me live with that. Returning the sap with a condition felt right. Some things were rough. I clicked the wrong conversation once because topic numbers carried over. The cutters attacked on sight, so I could not talk to them. The Ei-Tuja versus Kaska-Meen choice was a bit thin, since I never heard from Kaska-Meen. I still want to know who the knotted-cord woman is and why she spared me, and what the cutters are building. The journal is empty now, but I would keep playing.
