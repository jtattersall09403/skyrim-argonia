# Eye of Argonia

**Canon (UESP Lore:Eye of Argonia, fetched 2026-08-23; Arrival at Stros M'Kai
Pt.3):**

- "The priceless king's jewel of ancient Black Marsh." Little is established;
  theories make it "a key to the lost city of Black Marsh" or a gateway to
  undiscovered lands — canon deliberately leaves its nature open.
- Famous seekers: Lark of Rosgard (2E masked thief), Cyrus the Redguard and
  captain Tobias (Redguard-era), archaeologist Rolard Nordssen; the Nerevarine
  once used it as a cover story. None confirmed to have found it.

**Project layer (docs/quests/, PROJECT/AGENT_AUTHORED):** cut gem in an old
setting; reveals prepared route-marks/inscriptions; cipher for selected
pre-Duskfall systems; opens the Lost City; politically symbolic: it grants no
kingship by any power of its own, but it is a king's jewel, and a bearer who
holds it and the Lost City's door can press a claim to the empty throne that
others back for what the jewel means to them (the CLAIM ending,
story/design/quests/main-line-outline.md: the coronation is people's act, not
the jewel's); occasionally shows unbidden visions (wonder budget). Fan
interpretations credited per quests/99.

**Build implications:** the Lost City reservation in the deep basin (plan §86
P12) is the Eye's destination; route-mark sites on the one Eye-route chain
need Eye-reveal material variants (MQ23); the Lost City has no home in BM&V
yet (a recorded need).
