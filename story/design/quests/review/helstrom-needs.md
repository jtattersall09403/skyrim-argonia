# Helstrom: what the stories need, and how each story fits the drafted town

Helstrom is not redrafted for the stories: the stories change to fit the town as drafted (owner, 2026-11-13). This
lists every need the line stories make of Helstrom, judged from the player's side, with the fit the story's writer
should take. The data is `newworld/sources/needs/helstrom.json` (`python -m tools.places needs show helstrom`); the
studio shows the same rows under **Story**. Every need comes from a first draft of its story.

A fresh reader was given only what a player sees at each spot (`python -m tools.places sight helstrom`). They knew
the rootworm keeper, and took the town's sacred tree for the square and the cave for "a temple or guild hall at
best", whose door name "Quick Test Cell" looked like "an unfinished placeholder". They recognised nothing else.

## Met

| Need | Story | How a player knows it |
|---|---|---|
