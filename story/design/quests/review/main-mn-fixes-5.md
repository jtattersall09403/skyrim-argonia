What changed:

- **MN04.** Sesk stood nowhere once the ending switched her off at the camp. The ending now switches her on at
  Helstrom's city landing (a second position of her one reference), where she waits for a raft and says she owes the
  player; her side quest (main-plot §6) is its own record, owed. The rootworm's 25-gold fare is taken by the stop's
  own travel line, so the record only states it. The failed ending's broken journal line was mended.
- **MN05.** Buying gave a second jar. The one jar now lies in Keel-Raa's Pack, a knapsack he owns on the crates
  behind him; buying gives the player the pack, so they take that jar. The pickpocket way became lifting the jar from
  the pack unseen (Sneak), since a carried thing cannot be handed over in the vocabulary.
- **MN09.** The ending's journal said Nesh-Deeka "would not answer" why the Unbound seal the trees; her topic said she
  does not know. Her report line and the journal now say she does not know, and that it does not matter to her.
- **MN19.** The Unbound's highest rank is named Knot-Breaker, in the record's rank, journal, terms (with a topic
  for Cuts-the-Old-Knot) and the plot's Take and Give endings.
