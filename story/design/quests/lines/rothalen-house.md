# The Rothalen house: the plot

This page is the plot of the Dunmer necromancers of Thorn. It was written fresh on 2026-11-16 to the owner's notes
in [the factions overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md). Its rival's plot
is [the Sisei-Xul](sisei-xul.md): the two pages tell one war from its two sides. Every quest is set in a place whose
record and source place hold what it needs (`python3 -m tools.newworld.place_card <place>`). Everything is inside
the quest ceiling ([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the additions listed in
section 9.

**Words.** *The Rothalens*: a Dunmer trading house of Thorn, and the necromancers who run it. *Factor*: a trading
house's agent at a market. *Thrall*: a raised body bound to obey. *Xul-vaat*: an Argonian grave-stake, carved with
the dead one's whole story and driven through the body into the mud (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:123-129`).
*Sisei-Xul*: the Argonian order of healers and grave-singers, "sprout and death" in Jel. *Grave-singer*: the one who
stakes the dead and sings their last song (`topics/material-culture.md:232`). *Hist*: the great thinking trees of
Black Marsh. *Vicecanon*: one of Thorn's governors. *Telvanni*: the Great House of wizards in Morrowind.

## 1. What the faction is

The Rothalen house is a Dunmer trading family of Thorn whose masters are necromancers. By day it sells saltrice and
buys old Velothi pieces from scavengers. Its members rob tombs, raise and bind the dead, and sell the bound
Argonian dead to buyers across the Tear road.

Its front is a stall in Thorn's market square, kept by the factor, Ervyn Rothalen. Its hall is the barred cellar
under the ruin of Ovezza on the bay. Its master, Morvayn Rothalen, sits in the hall under the well at Kaochalit, on
the mesa south of Tsonac. Its people work through Thorn, Tsonac and the clay country round Aroxhu, wherever there
are graves.

Its skills are Conjuration, all of it, with Enchanting for the soul gems, Destruction and Restoration's
Necromantic Healing and its spells against the undead, which a necromancer uses on a rival's dead. Its enemy is the
Sisei-Xul, who stake the dead so that nothing can raise them. Inside the house, the factor and the bone-mistress
want different houses, and both are waiting for the old master to die. Joining gives the player the house's black
arts one spell a quest, its gold and, at the end, the house itself.

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** A respectable house that trades in rice and relics. The factor asks for a
conjuration, then sends the player to a salt mine where the house's diggers are working the far side of a walled
gallery. The player raises the dead, robs tombs and pulls stakes, and is paid well.

**Why the stakes.** Corpses rot fast in the marsh, so foreign necromancers seldom bother with it
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:136`). Life clings to a grave-stake, and a stake can feed necromancy
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:129`). Morvayn found that a body raised with its own stake bound to it does
not rot. So the house needs the body and its stake together, and an Argonian grave-ground is its crop. The salt
mine's dead were never staked, but the rock-salt preserved them. **X**, from the two cited lines.

**Where the dead go.** The house's ship, the *Saltrice Queen*, carries the bound dead north to Telvanni buyers. House
Telvanni refused the Pact that ended Argonian slavery and kept taking Argonians (`regions/thornmarsh-and-east.md:65`).
The dead are property in a Telvanni tower. **X**, from the Telvanni line.

**Who the master is.** Morvayn Rothalen is old even for a Dunmer. As a young man he bought Argonian captives off the
slaver brig *Silt Bride*, which sank off Thorn before the Red Year with her captives shackled in the hold. Her
captain's log still names him (`place_card "Wreck of the Silt Bride"`). He learnt necromancy in a Telvanni tower and
came home to Thorn to sell dead what he could no longer sell alive. He has been raising the dead whole with the
Resurrection spell, which costs the caster nearly all their health (`spell-groups.md`, Necromancy), and it is
killing him.

**Why each side can be argued.** The house says the dead are empty, since an Argonian soul goes back to its Hist
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:130`), and the order binds souls itself: a blasphemer is bound to an urn and
denied the Hist (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:133-134`). The Eldest of the order bound a man to an urn with
her own hands. The order's poisoners end the lives of the sick. Argonian armies sacked Mournhold and nearly
destroyed House Telvanni within living Dunmer memory (`topics/foreign-powers.md:47-48`). The order says that a body
pulled off its stake and made to work for a Dunmer master is the slave-coffles of Thorn over again
(`regions/thornmarsh-and-east.md:56-58`), and the Barsaebic kings did the same to their slaves before the Dunmer did
(`topics/lost-peoples.md:108-109`).

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** In Thorn, Tsonac, Inoja, Arorha and Stormhold, people asked about work, relics or spells speak of
the Rothalens. "They buy Velothi pieces and pay more than the pots are worth." "The factor likes a conjurer. He asks
them to show him." The first such line starts **Find the Rothalens**, and its marker sits on Ervyn at the house's
stall.

**Being let in.** Ervyn is a polite trader to everyone. A player with Conjuration 20 or more is asked to show what
they can do: they cast any conjuration in front of him. He says "very good, I may have work for someone like you",
and sends them to the old shaft of Coldbrine Mine, where the house's diggers work out of sight (quest 1).

**The door shut.** Find the Rothalens fails, with a journal entry, when the player finishes **The Walled Gallery**,
quest 3 of the Sisei-Xul's line, in which they kill the house's work at Coldbrine. Ervyn will not deal with them
after that.

## 5. The acts

The line is thirteen quests in three acts. Act I is the house's daily work: raising, robbing and pulling stakes,
always behind the respectable front. Act II takes the player into the inner house, its hall, its master and its
rivals, and the war with the Sisei-Xul turns open. Act III beats the order, and then the house turns on itself.

Danger climbs with the line. The world holds no place of band D1, so the line opens at D2 in a salt mine and on the
coast road south of Thorn. It goes into the Velothi ruins (D3) and the wrecks and deeper ruins (D4), and ends in the
deepest ruin of the mesa (D5). Conjuration asked climbs from 20 at the door to 75 at the end, and the locks from
easy to master.

Most quests begin with a spell taught by Neeja or Dalvela, and one step of the quest is built for it among other
steps that lead into each other. Every objective has a marker. Where the player may fight or sneak, the journal
says so, and the guards stand few and apart.

### Act I: the front

**1. The Demonstration** (Thorn, the grassland border city; Coldbrine Mine, a rock-salt working south-west of it).
Ervyn watches the player cast and sends them to the old shaft of Coldbrine, past the salt-cutters, to a digger's
camp beyond the walled gallery. Neeja waits there and tells the player what the house is in a few plain words. One of
the house's diggers raised the salt-kept dead of the walled gallery and lost them, and they killed him. Neeja
teaches Command Lesser Undead. The dead are loose in the gallery, with the old giant mudcrab that dug in from the
brine pools. The player commands the dead to fight the crab, or puts both down by fire and conjuring. In the dead
digger's satchel is the house's binding-stone, carved with a spider in a circle. The player takes it to Neeja and is
made a Hireling. *Leads on:* Neeja says the house's carts take the salt dead past Marshgate Watch, and its sergeant
has started asking questions. The journal marks the watch.

**2. The Blind Gate** (Marshgate Watch, a road post south of Thorn; Fort Thornmoth, the toll fort west of the
city). Fort Thornmoth has a guard who lets the house's cart through untouched every few days. At Marshgate Watch the
sergeant has found out a warden of his own taking pay to let boats through, and means to report both to Fort
Thornmoth. Neeja teaches Conjure Flame Atronach. The sergeant patrols the ditch road among the crocodiles and giant
snakes. The player kills him there with a conjured atronach, so that no blade of theirs is seen. Or they take his
report from the chest in the watch-house (an easy lock) and burn it. Or they pay him the house's purse of 300
gold, which Ervyn takes out of the player's reward. *Leads on:* Ervyn pays what is left and gives the player a
buyer's errand, the house's relic trade at its plainest: Kornakim, a Velothi watch-hold down the bight.

**3. The Spell-Fire** (Kornakim, a ruined Velothi watch-hold on Hatandros Bight). Neeja teaches Soul Trap. A brazier
at the top of Kornakim's tower holds a spell-lit fire that has never gone out, because a soul is bound in its
fire-stone. The house wants the stone. The household ghosts keep the inner hall, and the watch-captain's ghost holds
the tower stair. The player fights the hall or slips round it through the sinkhole in the courtyard, into the sea
cave and up. On the stair they kill the captain's ghost with Soul Trap on him, so that his soul goes into their gem.
They take the fire-stone from the brazier. The journal says the player may fight or sneak. *Leads on:* Ervyn sells
the stone for a great price in Thorn and makes the player a Gravewright. Then Neeja gives the player the house's
real work: the Argonian dead of Tazhul.

**4. The Pulled Stakes** (Tazhul, a Root-Folk egg station west of Inoja; Joxhi, a clay-diggers' village north of
Inoja). Tazhul's dead are staked along its trap-line path, and its Root-Folk hunters attack strangers on the line.
Neeja teaches Conjure Ghost (Conjuration 35). The player sends a ghost through the reeds to draw the hunters off,
or fights them. They pull three stakes, each with its body, and the bog blights that rise are fought or left. The
stakes go to the house's store, the buried Ayleid hall at Joxhi that the diggers broke into and will not go back
down. The journal says the player may fight the hunters or go quietly. This quest shuts the Sisei-Xul's door to the
player. *Leads on:* the house's man at Joxhi pays the player and says Ervyn wants them for a tablet.

**5. The Lord's Tablet** (Bala Karag, a ruined Velothi stronghold on an island a day from Thorn). Ervyn has a buyer
for the ancestor tablet carved with the sign of the holding's last lord. He gives the player an amulet of
waterbreathing to break at the arcane enchanter of the Conclave of the One in Thorn, which teaches them the
enchantment (Enchanting 30). The lord's ghost carries the key to the lowest hall, and the lowest hall is flooded,
with slaughterfish trapped in it. The player walks the causeway to the island, fights or turns the ghosts in the
upper halls, and kills the lord's ghost for the key. They enchant a ring or a helm with waterbreathing from their
own gems and dive the lowest hall to the chest that holds the tablet. An Argonian player needs no enchantment. *Leads
on:* Ervyn sells the tablet. Dalvela, the bone-mistress, sends word through him that Tsonac's minder is about to seal
a chamber the house wants.

**6. The Mason's Stone** (Tsonac, the Stone-Sleepers' terraced city; Tselan, a stone hamlet west of it). The
quarry gang at Tselan has broken into a sealed Velothi chamber, and a welkynd stone is set in its wall. Akeeta-Sul of
the Sisei-Xul has come from Tsonac to seal it, and stands at its door with two quarrymen. Dalvela teaches Paralysis
Rune. The player gets in by laying the rune in the minder's path and passing her while she is held, by killing her
and the quarrymen, or by the broken foundation round the back, unseen. Inside, the chamber's skeletons are up. The
player takes the stone. If Akeeta-Sul lives, she knows the player's face: Tsonac's tree-minder trainer and spell
seller will no longer deal with them. *Leads on:* Dalvela makes the player a Soul-Factor. She says the order has
heard of the *Silt Bride*'s log, and Morvayn wants it before them.

### Act II: the inner house

**7. The Drowned Coffle** (Wreck of the Silt Bride, a slaver brig off Thorn). Dalvela teaches Command Undead
(Conjuration 50). The brig lies a short swim off Thorn. Her drowned crew and the shackled dead walk the hold. The
player commands the shackled dead to turn on the crew, or fights them all. The captain carries the key to his
strongbox, which has an adept lock. In it is the log. It names the families who bought from the brig, Morvayn Rothalen
among them. *Leads on:* Dalvela says only Morvayn burns that log. She gives the player a key with the spider in a
circle on its bow, which opens the water door under Ovezza's quay. The journal marks the barred cellar.

**8. Below the Spider** (Ovezza, a ruined Velothi town on the bay). Ovezza's ghosts walk the square and
slaughterfish fill the water over the quay. The key opens the water door into the house's hall, where the bound dead
stand in rows. The wraith that keeps the inner door has slipped its binding and killed two of the house. Dalvela
teaches Sun Fire, a spell against the undead that a necromancer turns on a rival's dead. The player puts the wraith
down with it, or with fire and blade. In the inner room Morvayn waits. He takes the log, reads his own name and
burns it. He tells the player plainly what he did when he was young, and why he thinks the dead owe the living
nothing. *Leads on:* Morvayn gives Neeja's errand to the player: an urn from the Sisei-Xul's hall in Helstrom. Neeja
will meet them at Hiss-Bank Camp with the house's boat.

**9. The Urn** (Helstrom, the interior capital; Hiss-Bank Camp, a Naga camp on the western river bank). Neeja teaches
Necromantic Healing, which heals the player's thralls. The urn stands in a niche of the tree-minders' hall in
Helstrom, in a case with an expert lock. The hall has two grave-singers and the city's guard is the most dangerous
in the province. The player takes the urn unseen by night or picks the case, or walks in with a thrall and fights,
and every guard in Helstrom turns on them. At Hiss-Bank Camp, among the house's hired Naga, Neeja speaks first. The
urn holds her grandfather, Keel-Vaxa, an Archein who sold his village's people to the Rothalens and was bound by the
Eldest of the order herself. She asks the player to let her break it. The player gives it to her, or sends it to
Morvayn by the boat. If they give it to her, she breaks it and leaves the house, and gives the player her own staff.
If they send it to Morvayn, he binds Keel-Vaxa's soul in a black soul gem and gives it to the player, and Neeja leaves
the house hating them. *Leads on:* Ervyn writes to the player, asking for a quiet errand that Dalvela must not hear
of.

**10. The Steward's Ring** (Virtluush, a ruined Velothi river-fort inland of the bight). Ervyn wants the ring of the
lord's house that the steward's ghost still wears. He says it is for a buyer. Dalvela has bound that ghost as a guard
for her own store, and an apprentice of hers keeps it with her raised dead. The Sarpa lookouts shoot at anyone on
the reed-path. The player comes in by the path or by the drowned tunnel from the bight into the cistern. They fight
Dalvela's apprentice and her dead with Sun Fire and Turn Undead, or command her dead from under her (Command Undead),
and take the ring from the steward. In the ring's box is Ervyn's letter to the Vicecanon of Thorn: he means to hand
over Morvayn and Dalvela to save the trade. The player keeps the letter or burns it. *Leads on:* Morvayn sends for
the player. The order has found the hall at Ovezza in its turn, and he means to end the order first.

### Act III: the house turns

**11. The Night at Ixu-Maan** (Ixu-Maan, an egg-tending outpost south of Helstrom). Dalvela teaches Command Greater
Undead (Conjuration 75). The house's Naga from Salt-Tooth Camp and Dalvela's dead go into the order's grave-ground
with the player. Okan-Zeel and two grave-singers stand at the egg pools. The player leads the raid. They raise the
order's own staked dead against it, fight beside the Naga, and kill the Eldest. With her dead the order is beaten: its
grave-singers leave the hall at Helstrom and its teachers stop teaching. *Leads on:* Morvayn makes the player a
Bone-Master. The same night he falls in the hall under the well and cannot rise. Ervyn and Dalvela each send for the
player.

**12. The House Divided** (Kharraluhl, a ruined Velothi settlement north of the bay; Thorn). Dalvela holds
Kharraluhl, where the bound dead wait in the lower halls for the ship. Ervyn holds the *Saltrice Queen* at Thorn's
quay. Each speaks first and asks the player to kill the other. Ervyn says that with Dalvela the house is a war that
will bring the Vicecanon and Morrowind down on it, and with him it is a trade that pays everyone. Dalvela says that
Ervyn would sell them all for a stall, and shows the player his letter if they kept it. The player chooses. At
Kharraluhl they fight through Dalvela's diggers and her dead to her at the household shrine. On the ship they go past
Ervyn's crew to a cabin behind a master lock. The one they choose stands with them. *Leads on:* Morvayn knows. He
sends for the player to come to Kaochalit alone.

**13. The Old Master** (Kaochalit, a ruined Velothi settlement on the mesa south of Tsonac). The herbalist of the
outer tomb lets the player through. Morvayn has raised a priestess of Mephala to keep the hall under the well, and
he waits there. He tells the player he chose them as heir the night they burned his log, and that an heir he cannot
trust is an heir he will raise instead. The player fights the priestess, then Morvayn, who raises the fallen again
and again until he dies. With him dead, the house is the player's. *Leads on:* the ending (section 7).

## 6. The choice inside the house

The house's last choice comes in quest 12, between Ervyn and Dalvela. Each side is argued by its own person, and each gives and
costs something different in play (section 7). In quest 9 the player makes a smaller choice first and gives the urn to
Neeja or to Morvayn. It changes a reward and a few greetings, never a later quest's stages.

## 7. The ending

The player kills Morvayn in the hall under the well and takes his seat. They are Master of the House. In both
endings they gain the hall under the well at Kaochalit as their home, with the herbalist's trade and Morvayn's own
alchemy bench, and Morvayn's staff, enchanted with our custom enchantment Morvayn's Due: a struck foe that dies rises
as the player's thrall for a long time.

| Outcome | It gives | It costs |
|---|---|---|
| **With Ervyn** (Dalvela killed) | the house's trade. The stall in Thorn sells to the player at the house's own prices, and Ervyn pays the master a share in gold every week. Thorn's guards look away from the player's crimes in the city | the bound dead are shipped and sold and the hall at Ovezza is emptied for coin. Dalvela's tomes of command go with her, and the house's dead do not fight for the player |
| **With Dalvela** (Ervyn killed) | the house's dead. Dalvela's bound guard, a wraith of the hall, comes to the player's side with a power and goes with another. The hall at Ovezza becomes a second home with its rows of thralls | the Rothalens' stall in Thorn closes, the Vicecanon names the player, and Thorn's guards attack them on sight |

In both, the Sisei-Xul no longer stakes the dead in the north-east, and the order's people who are left attack the
player on sight. Argonians across the clay country are cooler to the player, and Aroxhu's and Tsonac's tree-minder
trainers refuse them.

## 9. What it needs, and what it changes elsewhere

### What it asks of the world

- **The bound dead:** Skyrim's draugr and skeletons, and the Argonian skeleton and zombie of King of the Murkmire's
  creature set under our names, each wearing its stake (a xul-vaat mesh of Black Marsh & Valenwood as a worn item).
- **The *Saltrice Queen*:** the ship interior of Thorn's first source, moored at the quay.
- **The house's mark:** a spider in a circle on crates, a binding-stone and a key bow.
- **Items:** Skyrim's necromancer robes, soul gems, welkynd stone and urn. Animated Armoury's whip and rapier. The
  staff of Morvayn, each under our names. The custom enchantment Morvayn's Due is the line's last reward.
- **Spells:** all from [Spells by use](../spell-groups.md): Command Lesser Undead, Command Undead and Command Greater
  Undead, Conjure Ghost and Resurrection come from Magic Expanded.
