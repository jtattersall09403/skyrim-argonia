# House Cardruhn: the plot

This page is the plot of the Dunmer necromancers of Thorn. It was written on 2026-11-16 to the owner's notes in
[the factions overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md), under the name the
Rothalen house, and revised on 2026-11-17 to the name the owner chose (#74) and to his story explorer notes on the
other lines. Its rival's plot is [the Sisei-Xul](sisei-xul.md): the two pages tell one war from its two sides. Every
quest is set in a place whose record and source place give what it needs. Each thing a quest asks of a place says in
brackets which source place gives it and how we know: what that source place's buildings, interiors and placed pieces
really are, and which pieces are added to it because the source has none. Everything is inside the quest ceiling
([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the additions listed in section 9.

**Words.** *House Cardruhn*: a Dunmer trading family of Thorn, and the necromancers who run it. *Cardruhn* is
attested Dunmeri for an ancestral tomb, built on *ruhn*, home or hearth-hall, and a Dunmer house takes the name of its
founders or its charge (UESP Lore:Dark Elvish, Lore:Great Houses). *Factor*: a trading house's agent at a market.
*Thrall*: a raised body bound to obey. *Xul-vaat*: an Argonian grave-stake, carved with the dead one's whole story
and driven through the body into the mud (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:123-129`). *Sisei-Xul*: the Argonian
order of healers and grave-singers, "sprout and death" in Jel. *Grave-singer*: the one who stakes the dead and sings
their last song (`topics/material-culture.md:232`). *Hist*: the great thinking trees of Black Marsh. *The Owing*: the
marsh's sentence, a counted number of months of owed work in place of a cell (`topics/labour-and-bondage.md:72`).
*Vicecanon*: one of Thorn's magistrates, chosen by the city. *The Xeech-Leel*: "the People of the New Beginning", the
party that has ruled the province since the An-Xileel fell with Umbriel (lore §1). *The Toh-Reel-Ka*: "the Secret
Warriors", the Xeech-Leel's secret arm (lore §2.5). *Archwardens*: the Xeech-Leel's wardens, whose garrisons sit in
the old Imperial forts and take the road tolls. *Telvanni*: the Great House of wizards in Morrowind.

## 1. What the faction is

House Cardruhn is a Dunmer trading family of Thorn whose masters are necromancers. By day it sells saltrice and buys
old Velothi pieces from scavengers. Its members rob tombs, raise and bind the dead, and sell the bound Argonian dead
to estates in the north as hands that need no food.

Its front is a stall in Thorn's market square, kept by the factor, Ervyn Cardruhn. Its hall is the barred cellar
under the ruin of Ovezza on the bay. Its master, Morvayn Cardruhn, sits in the hall under the well at Kaochalit, on
the mesa south of Tsonac. Its people work through Thorn, Tsonac and the clay country round Aroxhu, wherever there
are graves.

Its skills are Conjuration, all of it, with Enchanting for the soul gems, Destruction and Restoration's Necromantic
Healing and its spells against the undead, which a necromancer uses on a rival's dead. Its enemy is the Sisei-Xul,
who stake the dead so that nothing can raise them. Raising the dead breaks no Argonian law, but a soul kept from its
Hist is the Toh-Reel-Ka's business (**X**, reconciliation item 6), so the Cardruhns keep their black arts behind the
stall and their trade out of the Xeech-Leel's sight. House Cardruhn is kin by an old marriage to House Ulvarys of
Thorn, which buys Owings ([owing-house](owing-house.md)). The two houses do not speak: House Ulvarys says it deals in
the living and want nothing of the dead. Inside the house, the factor and the bone-mistress want different houses,
and both are waiting for the old master to die. Joining gives the player the house's black arts one spell a quest,
its gold and, at the end, the house itself.

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** A respectable house that trades in rice and relics. The factor asks for a
conjuration, then sends the player to a salt mine where a digger of the house has broken into a walled gallery from
the far side. The player raises the dead, robs tombs and pulls stakes, and is paid well.

**Why the stakes.** Corpses rot fast in the marsh, so foreign necromancers seldom bother with it
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:136`). Life clings to a grave-stake, and a stake can feed necromancy
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:129`). Morvayn found that a body raised with its own stake bound to it does
not rot. So the Cardruhns need the body and its stake together, and an Argonian grave-ground is their crop. The salt
mine's dead were never staked, which made them the easiest to raise. **X**, from the two cited lines.

**Where the dead go.** The house's ship, the *Saltrice Queen*, carries the bound dead north up the coast to estates
that want hands that need no food or bed. The house's papers write each body as an Owing that never ends, so the
sale passes the Archwardens' crossings as a sale of Owings, the only trade in people the province has in 4E 201
(**X**, reconciliation item 2; `topics/labour-and-bondage.md:90-93`). The Telvanni's taking of Argonians over the
border is history, ended with the Third Era (`regions/thornmarsh-and-east.md:65`; lore §10.1).

**Who the master is.** Morvayn Cardruhn is old even for a Dunmer. As a young man, late in the Third Era, when the Dres
still raided the Thorn coast (lore §10.1), he bought Argonian captives from the slaver brig *Silt Bride*, which sank
off Thorn with her captives shackled in the hold. Her captain's log still names him. He learnt necromancy in a
Telvanni tower and came home to Thorn to sell dead what he could no longer sell alive. In the 4E 160s he bought from
Keel-Vaxa, an Archein of the Thorn coast, the Owings of forty of Keel-Vaxa's own village's debtors for gold, and sold
the Owings on to House Ulvarys' factor at Thorn (**X**, reconciliation items 2 and 10). He has been raising the dead
whole with the Resurrection spell, which costs the caster nearly all their health (`spell-groups.md`, Necromancy), and
it is killing him.

**Why each side can be argued.** The Cardruhns say the dead are empty, since an Argonian soul goes back to its Hist
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:130`), and the order binds souls itself: a blasphemer is bound to an urn and
denied the Hist (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:133-134`). The First Grave-Singer bound Keel-Vaxa to an urn
with her own hands. The order's poisoners end the lives of the sick. Argonian armies sacked Mournhold and nearly
destroyed House Telvanni within living Dunmer memory (`topics/foreign-powers.md:47-48`). The order says that a body
pulled off its stake and made to work for a Dunmer master is the slave-coffles of Thorn over again
(`regions/thornmarsh-and-east.md:56-58`), and the Barsaebic kings did the same to their slaves before the Dunmer did
(`topics/lost-peoples.md:108-109`).

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** In Thorn, Tsonac, Inoja, Arorha and Stormhold, people asked about work, relics or spells speak of
the Cardruhns. "They buy Velothi pieces and pay more than the pots are worth." "The factor likes a conjurer. He asks
them to show him." The first such line starts **Find House Cardruhn**, and its marker sits on the house's stall in
Thorn's market square. Thorn has a boat stop on the Archon, Seafalls and Tsonac runs.

**Being let in.** Ervyn is a polite trader to everyone. A player with Conjuration 20 or more is asked to show what
they can do: they cast any conjuration in front of him. He says "very good, I may have work for someone like you",
and sends them to Coldbrine Mine, where a digger of the house works out of sight (quest 1). The test is easy by
design: any character reaches Conjuration 20 by casting Conjure Familiar for a short while, and Thorn's apothecary
sells its tome.

**The house's history and its name.** Once the player has joined, Neeja answers the topic *House Cardruhn*: "They
were the Dres's tomb-keepers on this coast. When the Dres went home to Morrowind, this family stayed with the tombs
and kept the trade that came out of them, and named themselves for it: a cardruhn is an ancestral tomb. Morvayn is
the one who taught the tombs to stand up and walk." (**X**, from the Dres coast, lore §10.1, and the house of Dres
overseers that stayed, [owing-house](owing-house.md).)

**Hostile to strangers, not to members.** The house's own places attack a stranger: the bound dead in the hall at
Ovezza, the hired Naga at Hiss-Bank Camp and Salt-Tooth Camp, the diggers at Kharraluhl and the herbalist's guards at
Kaochalit's outer tomb. Each of them is set friendly to the house's faction, so a member walks among them unharmed.

**The door shut.** Find House Cardruhn fails, with a journal entry, when the player finishes **The Walled Gallery**,
quest 3 of the Sisei-Xul's line, in which they stake the dead the house raised at Coldbrine. Ervyn will not deal with
them after that.

## 5. The acts

The line is thirteen quests in three acts. Act I is the house's daily work: raising, robbing and pulling stakes,
always behind the respectable front. Act II takes the player into the inner house, its hall, its master and its
rivals, and the war with the Sisei-Xul turns open. Act III beats the order, and then the house turns on itself. The
line has two choices: a smaller one in quest 9 (the urn) and the house's last choice in quest 12 (Ervyn or Dalvela),
both written out as the player meets them, and summed up in section 6.

Danger climbs with the line. The world has no place of band D1 for this line, so it opens at D2 in a salt mine and
on the coast road south of Thorn. It goes into the Velothi ruins (D3) and the wrecks and deeper ruins (D4), and ends in
the deepest ruin of the mesa (D5). Conjuration asked climbs from 20 at the door to 75 at the end, and the locks from
easy to master.

Most quests begin with a spell taught by Neeja or Dalvela, and one step of the quest is built for it among other
steps that lead into each other. Every objective has a marker on the area to search, never on the exact thing. Where
the player may fight or sneak, the journal says so, and the guards stand few and apart. The Act I quests pay fixed
gold (50, 150, 200, 200, 250 and 300), enough for boat fares. Every place is a short walk from Thorn's or Tsonac's
boat stop, from Arorha's or Inoja's, or from the rootworm at Aroxhu or Helstrom; each quest says how.

|---|---|---|

### Act I: the front

**1. The Demonstration** (Thorn, the grassland border city; Coldbrine Mine, a rock-salt working south-west of it).
Ervyn watches the player cast and sends them to Coldbrine, a short walk south-west of Thorn, to the brine-pool hollow
behind the mine. Neeja waits there by a hole in the rock that the old giant mudcrab dug into the far end of the walled
gallery (Coldbrine's older shaft takes its interior from Whistling Mine in Skyrim, a mine by water whose one interior
is timbered galleries, and the walled gallery is that interior's far end; Whistling Mine has no second way in, so the
hole is one door added in the rock of the hollow, built from the mine's own cave pieces). She speaks first and tells
the player what the house is in a few plain words. A digger of the house went in and raised the salt-kept dead of the
walled gallery and lost them, and they killed him. Neeja teaches Command Lesser Undead (Magic Expanded). The dead are
loose in the gallery (King of the Murkmire's Argonian skeletons, under our name), with the giant mudcrab, unless the
Chainbreakers' line has killed it. The journal says "search the walled gallery for the digger". The player commands
the dead to fight the crab, or puts the dead down by fire and conjuring. The Sisei-Xul's side of this gallery is their
3, The Walled Gallery. In the dead digger's satchel is the house's token, a silver spider in a circle (a Skyrim amulet
mesh under our name). The player takes it to Neeja and is made a Tomb-Hand, and Ervyn pays 50 gold. *Leads on:* Neeja
says the house's carts take the salt dead past Marshgate Watch, and its sergeant has started asking questions. The
journal marks the watch, a short walk south of Thorn.

**2. The Blind Gate** (Marshgate Watch, a road post south of Thorn; Fort Thornmoth, the toll fort west of the city). A
guard at Fort Thornmoth lets the house's cart through untouched every few days (the record's hook). At Marshgate Watch
the sergeant has found out a warden of his own taking pay to let boats through, and means to report both men to Fort
Thornmoth. (Bramman's Brethren pay that warden in their 8, The Hollow Tide Run.) Neeja teaches Conjure Flame Atronach.
The sergeant stands at the toll bar on the ditch road, where crocodiles and giant snakes hunt the ditch (Curse of
Cipactli's). His report lies in a chest in the watch's blockhouse (none of Marshgate Watch's sources has a blockhouse
or any interior, so the blockhouse is a small interior added from Black Marsh & Valenwood's Imperial stone and timber
pieces, as the record lists). The player kills him there with a conjured atronach, or picks the chest (an easy lock)
and takes the report, or pays him 300 gold of their own to tear it up. If the Brethren's 8 has already killed the
watch, the report still lies in the chest, and the player takes it. *Leads on:* Ervyn pays the player 150 gold, and
300 more if they paid the sergeant. He gives them a buyer's errand, the house's relic trade at its plainest: Kornakim,
a Velothi watch-hold a walk south-east down the bight.

**3. The Fire-Stone** (Kornakim, a ruined Velothi watch-hold on Hatandros Bight). Neeja teaches Soul Trap. A brazier
at the top of Kornakim's tower holds a spell-lit fire that has never gone out, because a soul is bound in its
fire-stone (a Skyrim black soul gem mesh under our name). Ervyn wants the stone (Kornakim's main source, Frossel in
Dragonborn, is a Dunmer and Nordic ruin whose one interior is an ice cave, so the inner hall is taken from Fort
Blacklight's bulwark in Journey to Baan Malur, Dunmer brick halls and tower walls with a stair; the shrine niches,
the broken tower and the brazier are added). Household ghosts walk the inner hall, and the
watch-captain's ghost stands on the tower stair (Velothi ghosts, section 9; the captain is the record's boss). The
journal says the player may fight the ghosts or sneak past them. On the stair they kill the captain's ghost with Soul
Trap on him, so that his soul goes into their gem, and take the fire-stone from the brazier. A hole in the courtyard
floor leads down to a sea cave where a smugglers' crew left a cache (Coldcinder Cave in Dragonborn, a basalt sea
cave with standing water and the skeletons of the dead, is the second interior, behind an added door in the hole);
the journal says nothing of it, and a player who goes down finds
300 gold and a sea-chest of goods guarded by mudcrabs. *Leads on:* Ervyn sells the stone to a buyer in Thorn for a
great price, pays the player 200 gold and makes them a Gravewright. Then Neeja gives the player the house's real work:
the Argonian dead of Tazhul.

**4. The Pulled Stakes** (Tazhul, a Root-Folk egg station west of Inoja; Joxhi, a clay-diggers' village north of
Inoja). Inoja's boat stop is a walk east of Tazhul. Tazhul's dead are staked along its trap-line path (the stakes are
a stand-in, Curse of Cipactli's carved Xanmeer post under our name; Tazhul's main source, a small Argonian camp of
huts and posts near Loreisian in King of the Murkmire, has no interior, so the path is open ground and its trap line
is added), and the stakes belong to Tazhul: its Root-Folk hunters attack anyone they see pulling one. Neeja teaches
Conjure Ghost (Conjuration 35, Magic Expanded). The journal says "pull three stakes on the trap-line path" and that
the player may fight the hunters or go quietly. The player sends a ghost through the reeds to draw the hunters off, or
fights them. They pull three stakes, and the bog blights that rise from the graves (Curse of Cipactli's bog blight)
they fight or leave. The house's diggers come for the bodies after. The stakes go to the house's store, the buried
Ayleid hall at Joxhi that the village's diggers broke into and will not go back down (Joxhi's source is Root Whisper
Village in King of the Murkmire, which has Ayleid ruin pieces and an Ayleid hall interior, Vakka Bok, a hall of carved
stone walls, balconies and busts that serves as the buried hall). This quest shuts the Sisei-Xul's door to the player.
*Leads on:* in the buried hall, the house's man pays the player 200 gold and says Ervyn wants them for an ancestor's
urn.

**5. The Lord's Urn** (Bala Karag, a ruined Velothi stronghold on an island off the coast south of Thorn). Ervyn has a
buyer for the ash urn of the holding's last lord (Dragonborn's Dunmer urn, which Curse of Cipactli also carries, under
our name). The lord's ghost and the ghosts of his halls strike with frost, as Skyrim's ghosts do, so Ervyn gives the
player a ring of Resist Frost to break at the arcane enchanter of the Conclave of the One in Thorn, which teaches them
the enchantment (Enchanting; Thorn's own source has only a ship interior, so the Conclave of the One is an interior
added from Imperial and Dunmer pieces, as Thorn's record lists). The player enchants their own armour against frost.
Bala Karag is a walk south of Thorn and across the stone causeway to the rock (Bala Karag's main source, Fort Dulan
in Journey to Baan Malur, is a Velothi fort by the water, so the rock and its stone causeway are added, as the record
lists). The lord's ghost carries the key to the lowest hall, behind a barred door, where knee-deep water has trapped
slaughterfish (Fort Dulan's one interior of Velothi halls, pillared rooms and barred cells serves as the upper halls
and the lowest hall, with the barred door and the knee-deep still water added).
(Runaways hide in the upper halls in the Chainbreakers' line, and the lord's chest is their reward too.) The player
fights the ghosts in the upper halls or turns them, kills the lord's ghost for the key, and wades the lowest hall past
the slaughterfish to the chest that holds the urn. *Leads on:* Ervyn sells the urn to his buyer and pays the player
250 gold. Dalvela, the bone-mistress, sends word through him that Tsonac's minder is about to seal a chamber she
wants. Tsonac is a boat ride up the river from Thorn.

**6. The Mason's Stone** (Tsonac, the Stone-Sleepers' terraced city; Tselan, a stone hamlet west of it). The quarry
gang at Tselan has broken into a sealed Velothi chamber, and a welkynd stone is set in its wall (Tselan's sources have
no interior but the elder's hut, so the chamber is a small interior added from the Velothi tomb pieces of Redeyn
Ancestral Tomb in Journey to Baan Malur; Skyrim's welkynd stone sits in an Ayleid welkynd holder from Black Marsh &
Valenwood, added in the chamber). Akeeta-Sul of the Sisei-Xul has come from Tsonac to seal it, and stands at its door
with two quarrymen. Tselan is a walk west of Tsonac along the river. Dalvela teaches Paralysis Rune (Magic Expanded).
The player gets in by laying the rune in the minder's path and passing her while she is held, or by killing her and
the quarrymen. Inside, the chamber's skeletons are up (Skyrim's skeletons). The player takes the stone.

- *If Akeeta-Sul lives*, she knows the player's face: Tsonac's minder will no longer train them or sell them spells.
- *If she dies*, the quarrymen who saw it carry word to Tsonac, and the player has a bounty there and loses her trade
  as well; her body carries 200 gold and her tome of Ironflesh.

*Leads on:* Dalvela makes the player a Soul-Factor and pays 300 gold. She says the order has heard of the *Silt
Bride*'s log, and Morvayn wants it before them.

### Act II: the inner house

**7. The Drowned Coffle** (Wreck of the Silt Bride, a slaver brig off Thorn). Dalvela teaches Command Undead
(Conjuration 50, Magic Expanded) and gives the player two potions of waterbreathing (Skyrim's own); an Argonian has no
need of them. The brig lies on the sea floor a short swim off Thorn (the broken hull is Pilgrims Trench in Skyrim, a
ship hull of 48 pieces on a rocky shore, and the inside is Icerunner, another Skyrim ship, whose one real interior is
a hold of crates, barrels and hay with a table, benches and shelves at one end, which serves as both the hold and the
stern cabin; the sea-floor shelf, the shackles, the strongbox and the log are added). Her drowned crew (Skyrim's
skeletons) and the shackled dead (King of the Murkmire's Argonian skeletons, with Curse of Cipactli's shackles about
the hold) walk the hold. The player commands the shackled dead to turn on the crew, or fights them all. The captain
carries the key to his strongbox in the stern cabin, or the player picks it (an adept lock). In it is the log (a
Skyrim journal under our name). It names the families who bought from the brig, Morvayn Cardruhn among them. *Leads
on:* Dalvela says only Morvayn burns that log. She gives the player a key with the spider in a circle on its bow (a
Skyrim key under our name), which opens the cellar door behind Ovezza's market. The journal marks Ovezza, a walk west
of Thorn.

**8. Below the Spider** (Ovezza, a ruined Velothi town on the bay). Ovezza's ghosts walk the market square and
slaughterfish swim in the water over the drowned quay. Behind the market, the key opens the cellar door with Mephala's
spider carved on it (Ovezza's cellar is Raven Rock's Ulen Ancestral Tomb in Dragonborn, a small Dunmer tomb of
Redoran halls, ash pits and burial urns that serves as the barred cellar and the house's hall, behind a door whose
spider carving is added). Inside is the house's hall, where the bound dead stand in rows and let a member pass. The wraith that guards
the inner door (the record's boss, a Velothi ghost) has slipped its binding and killed two of the house. Dalvela
teaches Sun Fire (Dawnguard's), a spell against the undead that a necromancer turns on a rival's dead. The player puts
the wraith down with it, or with fire and blade. In the inner room Morvayn waits. He takes the log from the player,
reads his own name in it and says it will go into the fire. He tells the player plainly what he did when he was young,
and why he thinks the dead owe the living nothing. *Leads on:* Morvayn gives the player Neeja's errand: an urn from
the Sisei-Xul's hall in Helstrom. Neeja will meet them at Hiss-Bank Camp with the house's boat.

**9. The Urn** (Helstrom, the interior capital; Hiss-Bank Camp, a Naga camp on the western river bank). Neeja teaches
Necromantic Healing, which heals the player's thralls. Helstrom is a rootworm ride from Aroxhu. This quest brings the
line's first choice, and the player meets it in this order.

- *In Helstrom.* The urn stands in a niche of the tree-minders' hall (a King of the Murkmire Xanmeer urn under our
  name; Helstrom's own source has two interiors, one an Argonian house, so the hall is an interior added from the
  Hist-root pieces King of the Murkmire's Lilmoth Roots is built of, as Helstrom's record lists), in a case with an
  expert lock. Two grave-singers tend the hall, and the city's levy is the most dangerous guard in the province. The
  journal says "take the urn from the tree-minders' hall" and that the player may go unseen or by force. The player
  takes the urn unseen and picks the case (Sneak, Lockpicking), or walks in with a thrall and fights. A fight or a
  seen theft gives the player a bounty in Helstrom, which they can pay at the levy's post, so the city stays open to
  them.
- *At Hiss-Bank Camp*, a walk west along the river bank (Hiss-Bank's main source is Brucas Leap Redoubt in Skyrim, a
  Forsworn camp of cave and mine pieces, and the landing and the house's boat, a Skyrim rowboat, are added), the
  house's hired Naga let a member pass. Neeja waits at the landing by the house's boat, and speaks first. The urn
  holds her grandfather, Keel-Vaxa, who sold Morvayn the Owings of forty of his own people and was bound by the First
  Grave-Singer herself. She asks the player to let her break it.
- *Give it to her.* She breaks it (the urn's broken mesh, switched on in its place), leaves the house and gives the
  player her own staff (a Magic Expanded staff, under our name, enchanted to raise a zombie). Morvayn pays nothing for
  the errand.
- *Send it to Morvayn*, by the boatman at the landing. Morvayn binds Keel-Vaxa's soul in a black soul gem and sends it
  to the player with 400 gold: a filled grand soul for their enchanting. Neeja leaves the house hating them, and
  greets them with a curse if they meet again.

Either way Neeja's teaching ends, and Dalvela teaches from here on. *Leads on:* Ervyn writes to the player, asking
for a quiet errand that Dalvela must not hear of.

**10. The Steward's Ring** (Virtluush, a ruined Velothi river-fort inland of the bight). Ervyn wants the ring of the
lord's house that the steward's ghost still wears. He says it is for a buyer. Virtluush is a walk north of Arorha's
boat stop. Dalvela has bound that ghost as a guard for her own store, and an apprentice of hers tends it with her
raised dead (Virtluush's main source, Thirsk in Dragonborn, carries camp and farm pieces and seven living people that
a haunted ruin does not need, so its halls come from Raven Rock's Morvayn Ancestral Tomb in Dragonborn, a small
Dunmer tomb whose Redoran halls serve as the lower halls and the vault; the steward's ghost is the record's boss). A Sarpa lookout (King of
the Murkmire's raider, as the ecology binds the Sarpa) shoots at anyone on the reed-path. The journal says the player
may shoot the lookout or pass him by stealth. Inside, the cistern is a water-filled hall that the player swims across
to reach the lower halls (the tomb has no flooded hall, so the cistern is a still water plane added in one of its
halls). They fight Dalvela's apprentice and her dead with Sun Fire and Turn Undead, or command her dead from under
her (Command Undead), and take the ring from the steward. In the ring's box is Ervyn's letter to the Vicecanon of
Thorn: he offers to hand Morvayn and Dalvela over to the Vicecanon if she lets the trade go on.

- *Keep the letter.* The player gives Ervyn the ring alone. In quest 12 the letter is Dalvela's proof.
- *Sell it back to Ervyn.* He pays the player 500 gold for it, and in quest 12 Dalvela has only her word.

*Leads on:* Morvayn sends for the player. The order has found the hall at Ovezza in its turn, and he means to end the
order first.

### Act III: the house turns

**11. The Night at Ixu-Maan** (Ixu-Maan, an egg-tending outpost south of Helstrom). Dalvela teaches Command Greater
Undead (Conjuration 75, Magic Expanded). Ixu-Maan is a walk south of Helstrom. The house's Naga from Salt-Tooth Camp
(Curse of Cipactli's Naga) and Dalvela's dead go into the order's grave-ground with the player, placed beside them.
Okan-Zeel and two grave-singers stand at the clay nest-pit (Ixu-Maan's source is a Nordic and Dwemer homestead with
one watch-hut interior and no nest-pit, so the clay nest-pit is added, as the record lists). The player leads the
raid. They raise the grave-singers who fall against the order, fight beside the Naga, and kill the First Grave-Singer.
With her dead the order is beaten: its grave-singers leave the hall at Helstrom and its teachers stop teaching. *Leads
on:* Morvayn makes the player a Bone-Master. The same night he falls in the hall under the well and cannot rise.

**12. The House Divided** (Kharraluhl, a ruined Velothi settlement north of the bay; Thorn). This quest brings the
house's last choice, and the player meets it in this order.

1. Dalvela speaks first, in the hall at Ovezza where the player comes to report. Morvayn cannot rise, she says, and
  Ervyn will sell the house to the Vicecanon for a stall. She asks the player to kill him on the *Saltrice Queen* at
  Thorn's quay. If the player kept Ervyn's letter, she reads it and the journal says so. She goes to Kharraluhl to
  guard the dead that wait there for the ship. The journal marks both the ship and Kharraluhl.
2. At Thorn's quay, Ervyn stands by the ship and asks the player to kill Dalvela instead. With her, he says, the house
  is a war that will bring the Vicecanon and Morrowind down on it; with him it is a trade that pays everyone.
3. The player chooses by whom they kill. Kharraluhl is a walk west of Thorn: its upper walls are Dalvela's diggers'
  camp and bound dead stand in rows in its lower halls (Kharraluhl's main source is Sathil in Solstheim Expand, a
  ruined Dunmer town of 327 buildings whose interiors include Sathil Manor and Sathil Ancestral Tomb, which serve as
  the lower halls and the household shrine). The player fights through her diggers and her dead to her at the
  household shrine. Or they go aboard the *Saltrice Queen* (Thorn's main source, Thorn in Black Marsh North, has one
  interior, a ship with a hold of barrels, a strongbox and an apothecary's satchel, which serves as her, and the
  master lock on its door is added) and fight past Ervyn's crew of three to him.
4. The one they spared is placed in the hall under the well at Kaochalit, waiting to fight beside them.

*Leads on:* Morvayn knows. He sends for the player to come to Kaochalit alone. Kaochalit is a walk from Tsonac's boat
stop.

**13. The Old Master** (Kaochalit, a ruined Velothi settlement on the mesa south of Tsonac). The herbalist of the
outer tomb lets a member through (Kaochalit's source has only one interior, so the outer tomb with the herbalist's
bench is a small interior added from Redoran tomb pieces). Morvayn has raised a priestess of Mephala to guard the hall
under the well, and he waits there (Kaochalit's lower halls are Raven Rock's Temple Ancestral Tomb in Dragonborn, a
Dunmer tomb of Redoran halls, ash pits and burial urns with the hall under the well at its far end; the priestess is
the record's boss, a Velothi ghost). He tells the player he chose them as heir the night they brought him
his log, and that an heir he cannot trust is an heir he will raise instead. The player fights the priestess, then
Morvayn, who raises the fallen again and again until he dies (his combat spells, Skyrim's reanimation). With him dead,
the house is the player's. *Leads on:* the ending (section 7).

## 6. The choices inside the house

The house's last choice comes in quest 12, between Ervyn and Dalvela. Each side is argued by its own person, and each
gives and costs something different in play (section 7). In quest 9 the player makes a smaller choice first and
gives the urn to Neeja or to Morvayn; in quest 10 they keep Ervyn's letter or sell it back. These change a reward and
a few lines, never a later quest's stages.

## 7. The ending

The player kills Morvayn in the hall under the well and takes his seat. They are Master of the House. In both
endings they gain the hall under the well at Kaochalit as their home, with the herbalist's trade and Morvayn's own
alchemy bench, and Morvayn's staff (DX Necromancer Robes' staff), enchanted with our custom enchantment Morvayn's Due:
a struck foe that dies rises as the player's thrall for a long time.

| Outcome | It gives | It costs |
|---|---|---|
| **With Ervyn** (Dalvela killed) | the house's trade. The strongbox at the stall in Thorn becomes the player's, with 2,000 gold in it, and the stall's trader buys and sells for them. Thorn's Vicecanon sets no bounty on them | the hall at Ovezza is emptied for coin, Dalvela's tomes of command go with her, and the house's bound dead there turn on the player |

In both, the Sisei-Xul no longer stake the dead in the north-east, and the order's people who are left attack the

of them (their regard falls).

### What it asks of the ceiling

- **A spell cast in front of a person as a test.** Ervyn asks for a conjuration and answers when one is cast near
  him. The simplest build is one generic script on the speaker that sets a stage when a conjuration effect starts
  within a short reach of him. Until it is proven, the line is gated on Conjuration 20.
- **A person who rises again.** Morvayn raises his fallen dead in the last fight. This is his combat spells (Skyrim's
  own reanimation, given to him as a caster), so it asks nothing of the quest.
- **A named ally called by a power** (the Dalvela ending's wraith).

Every other step is inside the ceiling. A taught spell is a stage's result, and the step it serves is passed by a
named thing taken or a named person dead.

- *Place records:* Coldbrine Mine (Neeja's camp in the brine-pool hollow and the crab's hole), Marshgate Watch and
  Fort Thornmoth (the house's carts), Kornakim (the fire-stone), Joxhi (the house's store), Bala Karag (the lord's ash
  urn, which stands for the ancestor tablet its record names; the buyer is Ervyn), Ovezza (the house's hall), Virtluush
  (Dalvela's apprentice), Kharraluhl and Kaochalit take the rows above as promises. Thorn's record names the house's
  stall and its ship.
- *The cast roster* takes the people of section 3, with the family name Cardruhn in place of Rothalen.
