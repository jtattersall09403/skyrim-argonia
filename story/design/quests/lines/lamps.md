# The Lamps: the plot

This page is the plot of the Lamps, the detectives, written on 2026-11-16 to the owner's notes in
[the factions overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md). It is one of three
plots that share one late event: the Eelskins' ([thieves](thieves.md)) and the Shadowscales'
([shadowscales](shadowscales.md)) are the other two. Every quest is set in a place whose source place holds what the
quest needs (`python3 -m tools.newworld.place_card <place>`). Everything is inside the quest ceiling
([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the additions listed in section 8.

**Words.** *The Lamps*: the investigators of Soulrest, who find out who did a crime. *A case*: one crime the Lamps
are asked to solve. *The arbiter*: the judge whom both sides of a quarrel choose, here the old woman who hears the
Lamps' cases. *The Owing*: the marsh's sentence, months or years of owed work in place of a cell. *Ku-vastei*:
"those who bring needed change", the Argonian name for the Shadowscales when they served as judges and enforcers.
*The Shadowscales*: Argonian assassins, given to the order at birth. *The Eelskins*: the thieves' guild of the south.
*Hist*: the great thinking trees of Black Marsh.

## 1. What the faction is

The Lamps are a small band of investigators in Soulrest who are paid to find out who committed a crime and to prove
it. A Lamp searches the scene, finds the evidence, questions the people, works out who did it and brings that person
before the arbiter.

The Lamps sit in the Lamp-Hall, a stone house in Soulrest's half-empty government quarter, with a branch on the quay
of Truowa. Their skills are Speech, Sneak and Lockpicking for getting at evidence that people hide, and Illusion:
Calm, Fury and Mayhem, with Throw Noise and Clairvoyance from the spells by use
([spell-groups](../spell-groups.md)). Their rivals are the Eelskins and the Shadowscales. Joining gives the player
cases that pay in coin and rank, a bed in Soulrest, and a hand-made reward at each rank.

**Why there are Lamps.** Soulrest has three powers that contest who rules it: the merchant council, the old Imperial
civil office and the river families (`soulrest.md:49-50`; the place record). Black Marsh settles a quarrel before an
arbiter that both sides choose (`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:121-127`). Its other judges were the
Shadowscales, and their order is thought dead (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:61-67`, `:85`). **X:** the three
powers of Soulrest agreed to pay for investigators that none of them controls, with an arbiter whom all three accept.
They hire strangers, because every local belongs to one side. That is why a stranger is useful to them.

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** Cases climb from a stolen pay chest to murder. Late in Act II the player finds killings
done with one clean cut and no struggle, by people who leave nothing behind. The arbiter says the Shadowscales are
gone and the last of them died this year in Skyrim. Then the first voice of Lilmoth's merchant council is murdered
in his bed at Silt-Orrel Estate.

**What is true.** The Shadowscales never ended. The Shadowscale who died in Skyrim this year, Veezara, was real
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:4`, `:85`). The word that he was the last was spread by the order itself, as
cover. The Black-Tongue tribe never stopped finding Shadow-born hatchlings
(`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:214-216`), and since the Archon school closed in 4E 187 (`archon.md:48-52`) an
old Shadowscale, Ukka-Teel, the Eldest, has trained them in secret. The arbiter, Teesh-Vaal, was a Shadowscale
herself before she retired to judge (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:65-67`). She knows the order lives. She
carried the news of Veezara's death to Lilmoth and called him the last. She lies about it to the player until Act III.

### The night at Silt-Orrel (the same in all three plots)

Orrel-Sakka is the first voice of Lilmoth's merchant council and the head of the family that holds Silt-Orrel
Estate, north of Lilmoth. In spring he learnt that the Shadowscales live and that their hatchlings train in the
sealed school at Archon. He agreed to sell that to Lucan Vetrix, an Imperial who comes to the estate as a rice buyer.
Vetrix is an agent of the Dark Brotherhood's Listener at Cheydinhal, who wants the Archon school for the Brotherhood
(`archon.md:48-52`; `topics/guilds-and-orders.md:203-223`).

On the night before Vetrix was to be told, Ukka-Teel came up the reed channel to the drain grate under the
rice-barn. The grate had been cut the night before. He climbed to the bed-hall and killed Orrel-Sakka with one cut.
Orrel-Sakka tore the brass medallion from the Eldest's neck as he died. Every Shadowscale wears one
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:68`). On its back is cut its owner's own sign, a hook and an eye (**X**).

In the cellar below, an Eelskin scout, Drips-From-Eaves, was taking the measure of the vault door for the Eelskins'
great heist. He heard the fall and went up. He found the body and the medallion on the floor beside it. He took the
medallion and left by the cellar hatch. He saw the killer's back at the channel and nothing more. He gave the
medallion to Swims-Without-Ripples, the Eelskins' leader. Then he went to ground at Meris-Keth among the Khajiit
smugglers, sure that a Shadowscale would hunt whoever had it.

The next morning the family sent to Soulrest for the Lamps. Two Lamps and the family's guards hold the house as the
scene of the crime. The medallion lies in Swims-Without-Ripples's strongbox in the Eelskins' hall under Pusbottom, in
Lilmoth.

**How it ends depends on the line the player is in.** Three sides can come of it, and each line offers the player
two of them:

- *The Lamps and the Eelskins against the Shadowscales.* The Eelskins give the medallion to the Lamps. The Lamps take
  the Eldest at Ra'tal Den, where the order holds its trials. The Lamps then look away while the Eelskins rob the
  vault at Silt-Orrel.
- *The Lamps and the Shadowscales against the Eelskins.* The medallion is stolen from Swims-Without-Ripples's
  strongbox. Orrel-Sakka's council ring, which the Eldest took from the body, is shown as found in her hall. She is
  named the murderer and taken in a raid on the Eelskins' hall. The Lamps then let the Shadowscales work.
- *The Eelskins and the Shadowscales against the Lamps.* The Eelskins give the medallion back to the Eldest. A
  Shadowscale kills Marcella Dovi, the Lamps' chief, in the Lamp-Hall. Eelskins kill J'zahri and the Lamps at Truowa.
  The Lamps break up, and the Eelskins rob the vault.

The Lamps' line offers the first two, and a third way that only a great talker can take: Ukka-Teel's confession.

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** People in Lilmoth, Truowa, Blackrose, Archon and Mir-Xal speak of the Lamps when asked about work,
crime or Soulrest: "Soulrest pays strangers to poke into crimes. They call them the Lamps." The first such line starts
the small quest *The Lamps of Soulrest*. Its journal names the Lamp-Hall, and a marker shows its door.

**Being let in.** The player signs on with Marcella Dovi in the Lamp-Hall. She asks only that the player has no kin
in Soulrest, and takes them on because they belong to no side. The small quest completes, and the first case begins
at once.

**What shuts the door.** The Lamps' door shuts when the player reaches the end of quest 4 of the Eelskins' line (they
graduate to the Eelskins' hall) or quest 4 of the Shadowscales' line (they take the brass). *The Lamps of Soulrest*
then fails. In this line, the Eelskins' and the Shadowscales' doors shut at the end of quest 4, when the player takes
the Lamp. Before they take it, Marcella tells them what it costs: every thief and every assassin in the south will
know them, and a player who is already an Eelskin or a Shadowscale of the lowest rank is put out of it.

## 5. The acts

The Lamps' line is fourteen cases in three acts. Act I is petty crime in towns and on farms. Act II is killing, and
the case structure starts to break. Act III is the murder at Silt-Orrel and the Shadowscales behind it.

**The case structure.** Each case in Acts I and II runs the same way, and Marcella explains it in quest 1:

1. The player searches the places of the case and talks to the people. Each clue is a named thing to take or a line
   to hear, and a marker shows each place to search.
2. The player reports to their handler (Marcella in Soulrest, or J'zahri at Truowa) and names a suspect. Then they
   name the suspect's motive, then the means, then the opportunity. Each answer is open only once its evidence has
   been found.
3. Every suspect has a motive and a means. Every suspect but the culprit has an alibi or a clue against them, which
   the player can find.
4. When the player has named all four, the screen fades. The player and the suspect stand before Teesh-Vaal in the
   hearing room. She goes through the case aloud. She either confirms it or shows the key evidence that the player
   missed.
5. *Right:* the culprit is given the Owing, the player is paid and the next case opens. *Wrong:* the player pays a
   fine in gold and must solve the rank's spare case before the next case opens. Three wrong at one rank and Marcella
   puts the player out of the Lamps for good.

The fine climbs with the rank: 25 gold at Wick, 75 at Flame, 150 at Lantern. Each spare case is a short case of the
same shape: *The Smelter's Silver* (Soulrest) at Wick, *The Salt Pan* (Karketa) at Flame and *The Bad Shellfish*
(Amalaja) at Lantern. A spare case can itself be failed, which counts as one more wrong answer.

Danger climbs with the line: towns (D0), farms and wrecks (D2), ruins and ferries (D3), the old forts
and strongholds of the washes (D4) and, last, the dead village of Meris-Keth and Ra'tal Den (D5). Locks climb with
it: Novice locks in Act I, Adept and Expert locks in Act II, Master locks in Act III. Where the player may fight or
sneak, the journal says so. Every objective has a marker.

**Ranks and rewards.** Each is fixed and hand-made. The pay for a case climbs from 50 gold in quest 1 to 600 in
quest 12.

| Rank | Given after | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Wick | quest 1 | the Lamp's coat and hood (King of the Murkmire's fine marsh coat and hood, under our name), ten lockpicks, a bed in the Lamp-Hall |
| Flame | quest 4 | the Hearing Ring (Fortify Speech 15), and Marcella as an Expert Speech trainer |
| Lantern | quest 8 | the Lamp's rapier (Animated Armoury's rapier, under our name), enchanted to paralyse for two seconds on a hit, and the Lamps' house on Truowa's quay as a home |

### Act I: small crimes

**1. The Mate's Pay** (Soulrest, the old capital on the Topal Bay coast). Marcella signs the player on and gives them
a first case. A Khajiit ship's mate's pay chest was opened in his room at the harbour inn and emptied. The suspects
are the innkeeper's son, who owes money (alibi: the harbour smelter's keeper says he worked the night shift), the
mate's shipmate, who quarrelled with him over a share (alibi: none, and a Novice lockpick snapped off in the chest's
lock matches the picks in his sea-chest), and the inn's maid, who has the room key (alibi: the key never left the
innkeeper's belt that night). The player searches the room, picks the shipmate's sea-chest (Lockpicking, a Novice
lock) or slips into his bunk room unseen (Sneak), and talks the smelter's keeper round (Speech) or finds the shift
tally nailed by his door. A new character with a lockpick can do all of it. *Leads on:* after the hearing, Marcella
makes the player a Wick and sends them to J'zahri at Truowa, whose house the journal marks.

**2. The Cloth on the Quay** (Truowa, the lake harbour on Lake Ulaish). Bolts of Lilmoth cloth have gone from a
warehouse on the quay. The council merchant who owned them blames Fort Gysilla's garrison, which has begun stopping
Truowa boats over the toll. The suspects are the merchant (motive: he can claim the loss from the council), a
garrison sergeant drinking in the quay tavern (motive: the quarrel over the toll; alibi: the tavern keeper served him
all night) and the quay's smuggler (motive: profit). The player picks the warehouse's Novice lock or charms the
watchman (Speech), finds the cloth in the smuggler's stash by stealth or by threat, and lifts the merchant's
receipt for the sale from his house at night (Sneak). The merchant sold his own cloth to the smuggler and blamed the
fort. *Leads on:* J'zahri pays and sends the player back to Marcella, who has a case from the farms west of Lilmoth.
The journal marks Seeku Farm.

**3. The Missing Guar** (Seeku Farm, a guar farm west of Lilmoth; Lilmoth, the rebuilt stilt city). A guar has gone
from the farm's stockade, the second this season. Marcella teaches the player Calm before they go: a stranger who
rents the barn keeps a guard dog, and a Lamp calms a dog in place of killing it. The suspects are the stranger in the
barn (motive: he hides something; alibi: what he hides is his sick egg-sister, whom the player finds in the barn
behind the calmed dog), the farm's neighbour from Ixa-Ruu Farm (motive: a boundary quarrel; alibi: his own guar were
taken the same night) and the farm's hired hand (motive: drink debts; means: a guar halter in his bedroll; opportunity:
he keeps the stockade key). In Lilmoth a guar butcher remembers who sold him two guar. The player calms the dog
(Illusion) or kills it, searches the bedroll unseen (Sneak) and gets the butcher's name of the seller by Speech or
coin. *Leads on:* Marcella sends the player to a rice estate where a man has drowned.

**4. The Cut Sluice** (Dunhallow Plantation, a rice estate north-west of Lilmoth). Twice someone has cut the
estate's sluice and flooded the paddies. The second time a tenant drowned in a dyke. It is the player's first death.
The suspects are a son of the rival family that claims the east dykes (motive: the claim; alibi: he was at Lilmoth's
night market, which a fish seller remembers), a tenant whose wife drowned (motive: grief and a grudge against the
family; alibi: he sat up with her body that night) and the family's steward (motive: he wants the ruined paddies
cheap for a Lilmoth buyer; means: the sluice key, which he says he lost; opportunity: he set that night's watch).
The player picks the steward's chest in the plantation house (an Apprentice lock) at night or slips in while the
family is at the paddies, finds the "lost" key, and questions the watchmen. *Leads on:* Marcella offers the player
the Lamp, the rank of Flame. She tells them what it costs (section 4). Taking it shuts the other two doors. The next
case is at Archon, and the journal marks the harbour.

### Act II: blood

From here the cases are killings. In quest 9 the structure begins to break.

**5. The Pilot's Bar** (Archon, the eastern harbour city; Wreck of the Tidewarden, a cog aground north of Archon). An
Archon pilot has been found drowned in the harbour. Marcella teaches the player Throw Noise before they go. The
suspects are the daughter of the Khajiit captain whose cog the pilot ran onto the bar years ago (motive: revenge;
alibi: she was swimming in the harbour games, and twenty children saw her), a rival pilot (motive: the dead man's
berth; alibi: he was out at sea) and a fence on the lower wharves (motive: the dead pilot ran ships aground for him
and wanted out). The fence's store is watched by a wharf watchman. Throw Noise draws him off, or the player waits
for him to turn and slips past (Sneak). In the store is the pilot's knife, bloody. In the Tidewarden's hold, among
mudcrabs, the captain's strongbox (an Apprentice lock) holds the logbook that names the pilot and the fence's
payments. *Leads on:* Marcella sends the player to Chasepoint, on the coast road east of Soulrest.

**6. The Lost Cart** (Chasepoint, a salvage and carting village east of Soulrest; Redgum Camp, a timber camp in the
Percar hills). A carter was found dead at the roadside and his cart is gone. The suspects are a logger at Redgum Camp
who lost money to him at dice (motive; alibi: the camp boss had him on the saw that day), the carter's partner
(motive: the cart; means: an axe), and a salvager of Chasepoint (motive: the cart's load of wreck brass). The player
finds the cart hidden at Redgum Camp's edge, guarded by the salvager's two cousins, who are fought or passed by
stealth. The loggers' foreman will talk only if the player passes an Apprentice Speech check or shows the brass
fittings from the cart. *Leads on:* Marcella sends the player to Lujei, where a ferryman has gone missing.

**7. The Ferryman** (Lujei, the shore village on the Blackrose channel; Lujei Ferry, the cable raft west of it). The
ferryman did not come home. His body lies in the reeds by the landing, near a drowned traveller's purse. The suspects
are the island core's toll-taker (motive: the ferryman kept part of the toll; alibi: none until the player finds the
toll-box full), the ferryman's brother-in-law (motive: a debt; means: a boat-hook; opportunity: he was on the raft)
and a Blackrose boardwalk outlaw (motive: the traveller's purse). Marcella teaches the player Clairvoyance, and it
leads them from the landing to the outlaw's hidden boat in the reeds, where the purse's twin and his alibi lie (he
robbed the traveller a day earlier, far away). The brother-in-law's boat-hook is in his house (an Adept lock). There is
blood under its iron, and crocodiles lie between the house and the landing. *Leads on:* Marcella has a case in the
burnt mines of the cape.

**8. The Burnt Cut** (Naxan, a logging village on the Soulrest coast; Ashfall Cut, a burnt open mine near it). A
log-hauler of Naxan was found at the foot of the old vent shaft in Ashfall Cut. He went there for the grave goods
that lie in the shaft. (The Free Marque's 3, The Naxan Landing, robs the Company's pay chest on the same village's
log landing.) The suspects are two men who went with him and a third who was not meant to be there. The
burnt overseer's corpse, risen, holds the vent shaft. The player fights past it or turns it with Restoration, then
finds the second man's lamp at the top of the shaft and the third man's tracks (a dropped knife) in the cut. The
first man's boots, scorched at the vent, put him there. Speech gets the third man to say what he saw, or his knife
is enough. *Leads on:* Marcella makes the player a Lantern and gives them the Truowa house. Her next case comes from
Mir-Xal.

**9. The Road Watcher** (Mir-Xal, the market town on the Soulrest-Blackrose road; Mudmoth, a ruined road station in
the washes). Travellers on the Soulrest road have been robbed and one killed, and Mir-Xal's toll-man swears it is
outlaws from Mudmoth. The player takes the first two clues: a witness's account in Mir-Xal and a bloody toll token
in the ditch by the road. As the second clue is taken, the toll-man learns that the Lamp is close. When the player
reaches Mudmoth for the third clue, he is already there with the outlaws he pays, and they attack. Marcella teaches
Fury before the case. Fury turns the outlaws on each other. The player can also fight through, or shoot from the
broken upper floor. The toll-man's body holds his share and a key to his Mir-Xal house, where the rest of the
travellers' things lie. There is no hearing: the culprit is dead or he yields, and the journal says what the Lamps
then do. *Leads on:* Marcella has a man who must be brought in alive.

**10. Come Quietly** (Fort Sallowmoth, a ruined fort on the Karketa-Lilmoth road). The case is solved before it
begins. A Lilmoth boat-builder strangled his partner, and the evidence is plain. He has fled to the squatters of
Fort Sallowmoth. Marcella wants him alive. The squatters' lookout on the north wall and the smugglers in the yard
stand between the gate and him. The journal says the player may fight or go quietly. At the armoury he speaks first.
A hard Speech check (60) brings him to come quietly: the screen fades and he is gone to Soulrest. If the check fails,
or the player does not try, he attacks. A dead man pays the Lamps nothing, so the reward is half. *Leads on:* Marcella
has three deaths that look the same.

### Act III: the Eldest

**11. The Clean Kills** (Tarak Shan, a stormed Argonian stronghold east of the washes; Soulrest). Three men who sold
Argonian hatchlings to outsiders died this year, with nothing taken. One died under a fallen roof at Tailfall
Mound. The last died at Tarak Shan,
where the hatchling sellers met. The feather-serpent of the great hall is between the player and the room where he
died. In that room the player finds a hatchling's practice blade and a brass chain link. Back in Soulrest, Teesh-Vaal
tells the player that the Shadowscales are gone. The journal records that she would not look at the link. With the
link and the blade in hand, the player can make her speak: an Expert Speech check (75), or showing her both things
and the sale-notes from Tarak Shan, which name the buyer as "the rice buyer of Lilmoth". She admits that she was a
Shadowscale, that the order lives and that its Eldest is Ukka-Teel. She called Veezara the last to keep the hatchlings
safe. She will not hear any case of theirs. *Leads on:* the next morning a runner from Lilmoth brings word that
Orrel-Sakka is dead. Marcella takes the case herself and sends the player to Silt-Orrel.

**12. The Night at Silt-Orrel** (Silt-Orrel Estate, a rice estate north of Lilmoth). The grand case. The Lamps hold
the house. The suspects are Orrel-Sakka's cousin, back from the Warden fort and wanting a share of the boats (motive;
alibi: he was dicing at the stilt-hall tavern in Lilmoth), Lucan Vetrix (motive: none the player can find, and his
alibi is the same tavern), an Eelskin (means and opportunity: wet footprints from the cellar hatch to the bed-hall
stair; alibi: the footprints come up after the blood had dried, and stop) and the killer. The player finds the cut
drain grate under the rice-barn (Sneak past the family's dogs or Calm them), the single cut to the throat, the torn
neck-chain in the dead man's hand with links that match the one from Tarak Shan, and Vetrix's letter from the
Listener in his room at the tavern (an Expert lock), which says what Orrel-Sakka was selling. The structure breaks
again: Teesh-Vaal will not hear it, and the medallion that the chain held is gone. Marcella makes the player a
Beacon. *Leads on:* the cellar hatch had been opened. The boardwalk fence at Blackrose says an Eelskin went to ground
at Meris-Keth after that night. The journal marks the dead village.

**13. The Witness in the Dead Village** (Meris-Keth, an abandoned stilt village of the Banujha Basin). Khajiit
moon-sugar smugglers hold the dry halls, and the unstaked dead rise from the water under the walkways. Drips-From-Eaves
hides in the headman's hall with the smugglers' captain. The player fights the smugglers or sneaks past them. The
player can also buy their captain off for 500 gold. Drips-From-Eaves tells what he saw and that Swims-Without-Ripples has the medallion.
As the player leaves the hall, three of Ukka-Teel's Shadowscales wait on the walkways. They followed the same trail.
Marcella teaches Mayhem before the case, and it sets the Shadowscales and the smugglers on each other. The player can
also fight, or slip away under the walkways. A dead Shadowscale's satchel holds a brass medallion of their own, and a
note in the Eldest's sign: "The trial at Ra'tal Den, when the moons are dark." *Leads on:* Marcella says the
Eldest will be at Ra'tal Den, and the proof is in the Eelskins' hall. The last case is the player's to finish.

**14. The Eldest** (Lilmoth, the Eelskins' hall under Pusbottom; Ra'tal Den, a Lilmothiit ruin of the Banujha Basin).
The last case has three ways to end it (section 6). In every way the player faces Ukka-Teel in the hearth chamber of
Ra'tal Den, where the order tests its hatchlings past a giant mudcrab. He stands apart from his novices and speaks
first. He tells the player why he killed: Orrel-Sakka was selling children to the Brotherhood. *Leads on:* the line
ends. Marcella names the player Lamp-Master.

## 6. The last case and its endings

The player has three ways to end it. The first needs a very hard Speech check. The other two need a deal, and the
player must make one before they go to Ra'tal Den.

**The confession.** At Ra'tal Den, with the torn chain and Vetrix's letter in hand, the player may ask the Eldest to
confess. It takes Speech 90. He confesses if the player promises that the Lamps will not hunt his hatchlings. The
screen fades, and he is gone to Soulrest. The Eelskins keep their medallion, and the player owes no one.

- *It gives:* the Lamps' highest rank and its reward, the Lamp of Truth (an amulet: Fortify Speech 25 and Illusion
  spells cost 25% less). Teesh-Vaal as a Master Illusion trainer. Neither rival turns hostile, since the Eldest's
  hatchlings are spared and the Eelskins were left alone. The Shadowscales' novices and the Eelskins' fences trade
  with the player.
- *It costs:* nothing in enmity, but only a player who has built Speech to 90 can take it. The Silt-Orrel vault
  stays shut, and no faction owes the player a favour.

**The deal with the Eelskins.** The player goes to the Eelskins' hall under Pusbottom (by the hatch from the
walkways, past the ripper eels in the canal with eel-slime, which the Pusbottom fence sells, or by fighting the
watch at the hatch). Swims-Without-Ripples gives the medallion if the Lamps look away while the Eelskins rob
Silt-Orrel. With the medallion, the player and two Lamps (already waiting at Ra'tal Den's mouth) take the Eldest. He
fights to the death, with his novices.

- *It gives:* Lamp-Master and the Lamp of Truth. The Eldest's own blade, the Hook-and-Eye (a dagger that absorbs
  health), from his body. The Eelskins' fences in Lilmoth, Blackrose and Archon buy from the player at the best price
  and never report a theft. Teesh-Vaal stays as arbiter.
- *It costs:* every Shadowscale attacks the player on sight. The Silt-Orrel vault is emptied, and Orrel-Sakka's
  family blame the Lamps. The family's traders at Silt-Orrel and in Lilmoth no longer deal with the player.

### What it asks of the ceiling

The owner asked for two things that the ceiling lacks. Each is a block owed, to be proven once:

- **The hearing after a fade.** When the player names the fourth answer, the screen fades. The player is moved to the
  hearing room, where the suspect is switched on. Teesh-Vaal speaks first. The simplest build: a fade, the player
  moved to a marker in the hearing room, the suspect's second placement switched on there.
- **The arrest after a fade** (quests 10 and 14, the confession). A line ends in a fade, after which the person is
  switched off. The simplest build: the same fade, with the switch made while the screen is black.

The rest is inside the ceiling. Each answer to the handler is a line gated on a named thing held or a stage reached,
and it stores a number. Teesh-Vaal's verdict is a line gated on those numbers. A fine is gold taken. A wrong answer
starts the rank's spare case, adds one to a stored count, and puts the player out of the Lamps when it reaches three.
