# One province from fourteen stories: the consistency ledger

Fourteen line stories were written at the same time from one [province plan](province-plan.md). This ledger says what
each must change so that all fourteen are true together. It is step 5 of
[the phase plan](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05-story-quest-adapt-1.md), first half. Nothing here
rewrites a story: each line's writer does that in the second round, from section 8, which is the list to act on. The
sections before it hold the evidence and the rulings; section 9 is for the database lane.

**How rulings are made.** The change that costs the story least wins, and the line that makes most of a thing keeps
it. Where two lines both make much of one thing, they share it as a deliberate crossing and the ruling says how.
No ruling lets one line's choices change another line's stages: a crossing shows as a greeting, a rumour, a price,
a person's attitude, or at most one door (province plan, section 4). Helstrom is not redrafted: a story's Helstrom
parts are fitted to the drafted town ([helstrom-needs](../review/helstrom-needs.md)).

## 1. Everyone named

### 1.1 The naming rules the stories are held to

The rules are those `talk-write` cites: [35-cast](../35-cast.md) §54 and
[names we never reuse](../86-never-reuse-names.md).

- Five forms, by who carries them: Jel for the marsh-born; a translated name for city people and those who deal
  with outsiders; a nickname for the notorious; a chosen name; a foreign form for Dunmer, Nibenese and Khajiit.
- At most a third of a cast in the translated verb-and-noun form, and no two people in one quest sharing it.
- An image (reed, root, water, stone, shadow) once in a principal cast.
- No name from the banned list of another mod's inventions. None of the 267 names is on it (checked against the
  list's 261 people and 19 places).
- No canon or lore name on a new living person. Heita-Meen, Saint Roris, Xhon-Mehl and Lark of Rosgard are used
  only as the dead of history, which the rule allows. Nesh-Deeka stands in the cast roster the stories inherit
  ([36-cast-roster](../36-cast-roster.md)) and stays.
- One more rule, which the province needs and fourteen writers working apart could not keep: **one name, one
  person, and no two names a reader could take for each other.** The test used here is the plan's own readers', who
  could not tell Wardens, Reeds and Roots apart: two names clash if they share a first part, or differ by one or
  two letters, and both people can be met.
