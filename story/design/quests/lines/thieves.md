# The Eelskins: the plot

This page is the plot of the Eelskins, the thieves' guild of the south, written on 2026-11-16 to the owner's notes in
[the factions overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md). It is one of three
plots that share one late event: the Lamps' ([lamps](lamps.md)) and the Shadowscales' ([shadowscales](shadowscales.md))
are the other two. Every quest is set in a place whose source place holds what the quest needs
(`python3 -m tools.newworld.place_card <place>`). Everything is inside the quest ceiling
([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the additions listed in section 8.

**Words.** *The Eelskins*: the thieves' guild of the south, named for the eel-slime that keeps Lilmoth's ripper eels
off a swimmer. *Ripper eels*: eels that Lilmoth keeps in its canals to catch Argonians who cross them by night.
*Eel-slime*: what an Argonian rubs on to swim among them. *Pusbottom*: the old, poor heart of Lilmoth, where crime
lives. *A fence*: a trader who buys stolen goods. *The Lamps*: the investigators of Soulrest. *The Shadowscales*:
Argonian assassins. *The Owing*: the marsh's sentence, owed work in place of a cell. *The great heist*: the theft
the Eelskins have planned for years.

## 1. What the faction is

The Eelskins are a guild of thieves in the south of Black Marsh, with their hall hidden under Lilmoth. An Eelskin
steals: they lift purses, pick locks, slip into houses and strongrooms, and sell what they take through the guild's
fences.

Their outpost is a hulk moored at the boardwalks of Blackrose, where new members prove themselves. Their hall is in
the drowned Imperial cellars under Pusbottom, in Lilmoth, which no outsider knows. Their skills are Sneak,
Pickpocket, Lockpicking, Speech and Enchanting, for stealth enchantments on the thief's own gear, with the theft and
stealth spells ([spell-groups](../spell-groups.md)): Telekinesis, Muffle, Throw Noise and Detect Life. Their rivals
are the Lamps, who catch thieves. They are also the Shadowscales, who kill. Joining gives the player fences that buy what they
steal, trainers, a share of every job and, at the end, the great heist.

**Why they are there.** Lilmoth's Pusbottom is canon's criminal quarter (`lilmoth.md:46-47`), and the south's
crime is native: smugglers, the Blackguards, tribeless brigands and Bramman's hidden river from Topal Bay to the
Blackrose lakes (`topics/guilds-and-orders.md:179-195`). Lilmoth keeps ripper eels in its canals, and they leave alone
whoever is rubbed with eel-slime (`topics/guilds-and-orders.md:198-201`). **X:** the Eelskins are the thieves who
learnt that, and took the name. They are no branch of any Imperial guild.

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** The Eelskins rob merchants, smugglers and an Archwarden fort, and they pay well. Their
leader, Swims-Without-Ripples, is planning one great theft: the vault of Silt-Orrel Estate, north of Lilmoth. It
holds the gold that the An-Xileel took from Old Imperial Lilmoth when they looted it (`lilmoth.md:79-80`), which the
estate's family has kept hidden in its cellar since (the place's record). Then the vault's owner is murdered, and
the Eelskins find they hold the proof of who did it.

**What is true.** Swims-Without-Ripples has wanted that vault for twenty years. Her mother was one of the Pusbottom
families whom the An-Xileel robbed and drove out. To her the gold is owed. The murder was done by the Shadowscales,
an order the world thinks dead. The medallion her scout took is worth more than the gold to both of the Eelskins'
rivals, and she means to sell it to whichever of them helps her heist.

### The night at Silt-Orrel (the same in all three plots)

Orrel-Sakka is the first voice of Lilmoth's merchant council and the head of the family that holds Silt-Orrel
Estate, north of Lilmoth. In spring he learnt that the Shadowscales live and that their hatchlings train in the
sealed school at Archon. He agreed to sell that to Lucan Vetrix, an Imperial who comes to the estate as a rice buyer.
Vetrix is an agent of the Dark Brotherhood's Listener at Cheydinhal, who wants the Archon school for the Brotherhood
(`archon.md:48-52`; `topics/guilds-and-orders.md:203-223`).

On the night before Vetrix was to be told, Ukka-Teel came up the reed channel to the drain grate under the
rice-barn. The grate had been cut the night before. He climbed to the bed-hall and killed Orrel-Sakka with one cut.
Orrel-Sakka tore the brass medallion from the Eldest's neck as he died. Every Shadowscale wears one
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:68`). On its back is cut its owner's own sign, a hook and an eye (**X**).

In the cellar below, an Eelskin scout, Drips-From-Eaves, was taking the measure of the vault door for the Eelskins'
great heist. He heard the fall and went up. He found the body and the medallion on the floor beside it. He took the
medallion and left by the cellar hatch. He saw the killer's back at the channel and nothing more. He gave the
medallion to Swims-Without-Ripples, the Eelskins' leader. Then he went to ground at Meris-Keth among the Khajiit
smugglers, sure that a Shadowscale would hunt whoever had it.

The next morning the family sent to Soulrest for the Lamps. Two Lamps and the family's guards hold the house as the
scene of the crime. The medallion lies in Swims-Without-Ripples's strongbox in the Eelskins' hall under Pusbottom, in
Lilmoth.

**How it ends depends on the line the player is in.** Three sides can come of it:

- *The Lamps and the Eelskins against the Shadowscales.* The Eelskins give the medallion to the Lamps. The Lamps take
  the Eldest at Ra'tal Den, where the order holds its trials. The Lamps then look away while the Eelskins rob the
  vault at Silt-Orrel.
- *The Lamps and the Shadowscales against the Eelskins.* The medallion is stolen from Swims-Without-Ripples's
  strongbox. Orrel-Sakka's council ring, which the Eldest took from the body, is shown as found in her hall. She is
  named the murderer and taken in a raid on the Eelskins' hall. The Lamps then let the Shadowscales work.
- *The Eelskins and the Shadowscales against the Lamps.* The Eelskins give the medallion back to the Eldest. A
  Shadowscale kills Marcella Dovi, the Lamps' chief, in the Lamp-Hall. Eelskins kill J'zahri and the Lamps at Truowa.
  The Lamps break up, and the Eelskins rob the vault.

The Eelskins' line offers the player the first and the third.

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** People in Lilmoth, Blackrose, Soulrest, Archon and Truowa speak of the Eelskins when asked about
thieves, the canals or Pusbottom: "Lilmoth's thieves swim among the ripper eels. They call themselves the Eelskins."
"Look for the Eelskins all you like. They watch who steals, and they find you." The first such line starts the small quest
*The Eelskins*, with no marker. Each new rumour the player hears puts a line in its journal.

**Being let in.** The first time the player steals anything, the Eelskins notice. The next time the player sleeps,
the sleep is cut short. Sees-Nothing stands in the room and speaks first, and the talk cannot be walked away from. He
says the player's work was seen, and that there is a place for them in a family if they want one. He gives a note
and a key. The screen fades, and when it clears he is gone. The note reads: "The hulk at the Blackrose boardwalks. The
key fits the deck hatch." The journal marks the hulk, and quest 1 begins.

**What shuts the door.** The Eelskins' door shuts when the player takes the Lamp at the end of quest 4 of the Lamps'
line, or takes the brass at the end of quest 4 of the Shadowscales' line. *The Eelskins* then fails, and the first
theft no longer brings a visitor. In this line, the Lamps' and the Shadowscales' doors shut at the end of quest 4,
when the player is given the key to the hall. Corso warns them first: a player who is a Lamp or a Shadowscale of the
lowest rank is put out of it, and neither will take them again.

## 5. The acts

The Eelskins' line is twelve quests in three acts. Act I is the outpost at Blackrose, where the player proves
themselves. Act II is the hall under Pusbottom and the jobs that build to a first heist. Act III is the great heist:
the scout's night, the preparations, the choice of ally and the heist.

Danger climbs with the line: Blackrose (D0), a farm and a salvagers' village (D2), the villages, ferries and ruins
of the washes (D3), an old keep (D4), and, in the preparations, Meris-Keth (D5). The great heist itself is at
Silt-Orrel (D3 by its band). It is the hardest job of the line in its locks, its guards and its choices of way. Locks
and pockets climb with the line: Novice in quests 1 and 2, Apprentice and Adept in quests 3 to 6, Expert in quests 7
to 10, and Master at the vault. Where the player may fight or sneak, the journal says so. Every objective has a
marker. An Eelskin who kills loses a share: each job pays less for every person killed, and the journal says so.

**Ranks and rewards.** Each is fixed and hand-made. Each job also pays a share in gold, from 75 in quest 1 to 800 in
quest 8.

| Rank | Given after | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Slick | quest 1 | Eelskin boots and hood (Skyrim's thieves' leathers, under our name), fifteen lockpicks, Corso's fence at the outpost |
| Eel | quest 4 | the key to the hall; the Pusbottom fence, who buys stolen goods to 2,000 gold; Sees-Nothing as an Expert Pickpocket trainer |
| Ripper | quest 8 | Eel's Teeth (Animated Armoury's claws, under our name), enchanted to absorb stamina; the hall's arcane enchanter and a bed |
| Deep Eel | quest 10 | the Eelskin hooded cuirass and gauntlets (Skyrim's Linwe-style hooded armour, under our name), enchanted with Fortify Sneak 20 and Fortify Pickpocket 20; a Master Lockpicking trainer in the hall |
| Master Eel | quest 12 | the Still Water ring, the Master Eel's armour (Skyrim's Nightingale-style armour, renamed and recoloured by enchantment only), and the ending's gift (section 6) |

### Act I: the outpost

**1. The Hulk at the Boardwalk** (Blackrose, the lake-island city where three rivers meet). The key opens the deck
hatch of the hulk moored at the boardwalks. Below, Corso Delling keeps the outpost: a fence, a bed and an Eelskin or
two. He sets the first test. The canoe-men's toll-taker at the landing of the island core carries the day's tolls in
a purse. The player lifts the purse (Pickpocket, an easy pocket) or picks the toll-box in his shelter (a Novice lock)
while he is at the landing. A new character with a lockpick can do it. *Leads on:* Corso makes the player a Slick
and sends them to Reedfall Farm, which the journal marks.

**2. The Rice Shed** (Reedfall Farm, a rice farm on the channel east of Blackrose). The family keeps the season's
rice money in a strongbox in the rice shed. They keep watch at night against the marsh beasts that come up the
channel bank, so one of them is always awake. The journal says the player may sneak in or take the key. The player
waits for the watcher to walk the paddies and slips into the shed (Sneak), then picks the strongbox (a Novice lock).
The key is on the eldest son, who sleeps in the stilted house, and can be lifted (Pickpocket). *Leads on:* Corso pays
the player's share. He has an Adept pocket for them at Natai, and the journal marks the salvagers' longhouse.

**3. Light Fingers** (Natai, a salvagers' village at the southern edge of the washes). Corso teaches the player to
enchant. He gives them an old Eelskin ring of Fortify Pickpocket and a petty soul gem. At the outpost's enchanter the
player breaks the ring to learn its enchantment and puts it on a pair of gloves. At Natai, the salvager who strips
the wreck of the Salt Heron first carries a gold pearl in his pocket. The pocket is Adept: the gloves bring a new
thief's Pickpocket to it. The player can instead pick his sea-chest in the longhouse at night (an Apprentice lock) or
dive to the wreck for the other pearls, past the sea-drake in the broken hold. *Leads on:* Corso has heard a Lamp
asking about the hulk. The journal marks Ka-Asei.

**4. The Lamp's Man** (Blackrose; Ka-Asei, a stilt village a day from Blackrose). A man called Bolus Matius lodges
at Ka-Asei and has been asking canoe-men about the hulk. Corso wants the sketch of it in his strongbox (an Apprentice
lock), and the bandits who keep their boat strongbox in the village stand between the landing and his lodging. When
the player has the sketch, Matius speaks first. He says he is a Lamp, and offers 300 gold to learn where the
Eelskins' hall is. The player can refuse him. They can take the gold and give him a false place (Speech). They can
sell him the truth. Matius is Corso's own man, and the offer is the test. A player who sold the truth is put out of
the Eelskins, and the line ends there. A player who refused is made an Eel. A player who took the gold and lied keeps
it, and Corso gives a further 100. *Leads on:* Corso tells them what the rank costs (section 4) and gives them the key
to the hall under Pusbottom. To reach it the player must swim Pusbottom's canal past the ripper eels. They need
eel-slime, and the nearest is in the smugglers' eel pens at Brackwater Waystation. The journal marks it.

### Act II: the hall under Pusbottom

**5. Eel-Slime** (Brackwater Waystation, a ruined customs shed east of Lilmoth; Lilmoth, the rebuilt stilt city).
Smugglers hold the ruin and keep eels in pens below the old signal tower. The player fights them, sneaks past them
to the pens (Sneak, with Muffle from the outpost's spell-seller), or deals with their cache-master for the slime
(Speech and 150 gold). With the slime on, they swim from the hatch on Pusbottom's walkways down the flooded stair
into the canal. The ripper eels leave them alone. Without it, the eels attack. At the far end is the hall. Its
watchers take the player to Swims-Without-Ripples. *Leads on:* she sets the player to work. Her first job is at the
delta ferry.

**6. The Marked Sack** (Delta Ferry, the crossing east of Blackrose). A Lilmoth council merchant ships pearls across
the delta hidden in a marked rice sack. The sack sits in the ferry shed for the night, watched by the ferry-keeper
and two of the merchant's guards. Swims teaches the player Telekinesis. The pearl box sits behind the shed's barred
window, beyond the guards' reach and beyond a hand's. The player lifts it out with Telekinesis from the reeds. They
can also take the guards' key (Pickpocket, an Adept pocket), or walk in and fight. *Leads on:* Swims has a bigger
cellar for them.

**7. The Locked Strongroom** (Fort Swiftmoth, an empty keep inland from Archon). Smugglers of the Free Marque keep
their cache in the keep's locked strongroom, under a watch of four and their captain (the Company's 11, The Scroll
Store, raids the same strongroom). Aroxhu families rest in the dry upper floor and are
to be left alone. The captain carries the strongroom key, and it can be lifted (Pickpocket, an Expert pocket). The
strongroom can be picked (an Expert lock), or the player can fight down to it. Mud-crocodiles lie in the ditch round
the rise. *Leads on:* Swims says the player is ready for a heist of their own. Truowa's council wants the Archwardens'
toll strongroom at Fort Gysilla emptied.

**8. The Toll Strongroom** (Fort Gysilla, an Archwarden toll fort on a lake island; Truowa, the lake harbour). The
first heist. A Truowa council merchant, who will not pay Gysilla's toll, pays the Eelskins to take it. The player
chooses the way in. They can swim from a boat to the fort's water-gate by night and pick it (an Expert lock). They
can ride in a rice crate on the garrison's supply boat, which takes a bribe of 200 gold to its boatman or an Expert
Speech check (75). Or they can go over the gate by force, and the journal warns that the garrison is the
Archwardens'. The same garrison holds runaways for the Ulvarys house (the Chainbreakers' 9, The Island Keep). The
strongroom's key is on the toll-master. The lock is Expert. Two guards stand by the strongroom and
the rest are at the landing, so a quiet player can reach it. *Leads on:* the merchant pays at Truowa. Swims makes the
player a Ripper and calls them to the hall. She is ready to speak of the great heist.

### Act III: the great heist

**9. The Drowned Quarter's Gold** (Lilmoth; Silt-Orrel Estate, a rice estate north of Lilmoth). Swims tells the
player of the vault and why it is owed. Then she tells them of the scout's night (section 2) and shows them the
medallion. The Lamps now hold the house. She sends the player to look it over. They go as a rice buyer by day, which
takes an Adept Speech check, or unseen by night past the dogs. They find the three ways in. One is the drain under
the rice-barn, which the Lamps have barred with a new grate and an Expert lock, and slaughterfish swim the channel
before it. One is the cellar hatch at the boat landing, with a Master lock, its key on the family's steward in
Lilmoth. One is the front door, under the eyes of two Lamps and the family's guards. *Leads on:* Swims says they will
need two of four things before they go in, and marks all four.

**10. Two of Four** (Meris-Keth, an abandoned stilt village of the Banujha Basin; Rushiit Den, a Lilmothiit ruin on
the northern island; Lilmoth; Brackwater Waystation). The preparations. The player does any two of these, and then
Swims calls the plan set. Each one removes a danger from the heist.

- *The scout.* Drips-From-Eaves hides at Meris-Keth among the Khajiit moon-sugar smugglers, over water where the
  unstaked dead rise. The player fights the smugglers, sneaks past them or buys their captain off for 500 gold, and
  brings him back. He knows the vault door. With him, the vault's lock falls from Master to Expert.
- *The hatch key.* The steward drinks at Lilmoth's stilt-hall tavern. The player lifts his key (Pickpocket, an Expert
  pocket) or picks his house by night (an Expert lock). With it, the cellar hatch opens without a sound.
- *Silent feet.* The old bull crocodilian of Rushiit Den wears a golden ring in its nest chamber. The ring carries
  Muffle. The player kills the beast or lifts the ring while it sleeps, breaks the ring at the hall's enchanter and
  puts Muffle on their own boots. With them, the estate's dogs never hear the player.
- *More slime.* The smugglers of Brackwater Waystation have rebuilt their eel pens. A full jar of slime keeps the
  slaughterfish of the reed channel away from a swimmer under the rice-barn.

*Leads on:* Swims makes the player a Deep Eel. Two visitors have come to the hall with offers, and she sends for the
player.

**11. Two Offers** (Lilmoth; Soulrest, the Lamps' city; Archon, the eastern harbour; Ra'tal Den, a Lilmothiit ruin of
the Banujha Basin; Truowa). J'zahri waits in the hall for the Lamps. Hulee-Tah waits there for the Shadowscales. Each wants
the medallion. Swims gives the choice to the player, who will be the one inside the house. The player takes the
medallion to one of them.

- *To the Lamps.* J'zahri's offer: give the Lamps the medallion and help them take the killer, and the Lamps'
  watch leaves Silt-Orrel on the night of the heist. The player gives the medallion to Marcella Dovi in the Lamp-Hall
  at Soulrest. Then they go to Ra'tal Den, where two Lamps already wait at the mouth, and with them take the Eldest in
  the hearth chamber. He and his novices fight to the death.
- *To the Shadowscales.* Hulee-Tah's offer: give the Eldest back his medallion and help put out the Lamps, and the
  two orders share the south. The player gives the medallion to Hulee-Tah at the old ku-vastei's house in Archon.
  While a Shadowscale kills Marcella Dovi in Soulrest, the player's part is the Lamps at Truowa. J'zahri and two
  Lamps hold the Lamps' house on the quay, with the case they built against the Eelskins. The player kills them,
  openly or one at a time unseen.

*Leads on:* either way, the night of the heist is set. The journal marks Silt-Orrel.

**12. The Great Heist** (Silt-Orrel Estate). The player goes in by one of the three ways, and the preparations they
chose make each easier. If they sided with the Lamps, the Lamps' watch has left, and the family's four guards and
their dogs remain. If they sided with the Shadowscales, the Lamps are dead and the frightened family has hired four
more guards, so eight guards and the dogs stand watch. In both, the guards are spread through the yard, the house and
the landing, so a quiet player can reach the cellar. The vault door's lock is Master, or Expert with Drips-From-Eaves.
Inside lie the drowned quarter's gold (3,000 gold), the Imperial governor's seal ring of Old Lilmoth and a chest of
salvaged Imperial silver. The way out is the channel. A guard who sees the player raises the house, and the journal says the player may fight
their way to the landing. *Leads on:* back in the hall, Swims takes her share and gives the player theirs: half the
gold and the seal ring. She names them Master Eel and gives them the Still Water ring. The line ends. The Eelskins'
jobs go on as small thefts that the Pusbottom fence offers again and again.

## 6. The choice and its endings

The choice in quest 11 sets the end. Both endings give the Still Water ring: constant-effect Chameleon 50%, a custom
enchantment made for this line (no Chameleon spell exists).

**With the Lamps.**

- *It gives:* the Hook-and-Eye, the Eldest's dagger that absorbs health, from his body at Ra'tal Den. The Lamps' look
  away: once, Marcella clears the player's bounty in Soulrest and Truowa, and the Lamps never take a case against an
  Eelskin. The Lamps' house at Truowa sells the player lockpicks and potions of invisibility.
- *It costs:* every Shadowscale attacks the player on sight. The heist is still hard: the family's guards remain.

**With the Shadowscales.**

- *It gives:* the Quiet Cloak (Fortify Sneak 25 and Muffle), the Shadowscales' gift. The Shadowscales' traders sell
  the player poisons, and Hulee-Tah trains Archery to Master. The Lamps are gone: no investigator ever comes after
  the player in Soulrest or Truowa.
- *It costs:* the player has killed three Lamps at Truowa. Truowa's harbour watch attacks on sight until the player
  pays a bounty of 1,000 gold. The heist is harder, since eight guards stand watch. Teesh-Vaal, the Lamps' old arbiter, will not
  speak to the player.

## 7. Rivals

- **The Lamps** ([lamps](lamps.md)). The Eelskins' quest 9 tells the night that opens the Lamps' quest 12. The
  Eelskins' quest 11 is the Lamps' quest 14 seen from the other side. The Lamps' deal ending is this line's ending
  with the Lamps.
- **The Shadowscales** ([shadowscales](shadowscales.md)). The Shadowscales' quest 10 is the night of the Eelskins'
  scout. The Shadowscales' quest 11 hunts the same Drips-From-Eaves at Meris-Keth. The Shadowscales' ending with the
  thieves is this line's ending with the Shadowscales.
- **Shared people:** Swims-Without-Ripples, Drips-From-Eaves, Marcella Dovi, J'zahri, Hulee-Tah and Ukka-Teel are
  fixed by the night at Silt-Orrel (section 2).

## 8. What it needs, and what it changes elsewhere

### What it asks of the world

- **Ripper eels:** Skyrim's slaughterfish, under the name "ripper eel", in Pusbottom's canal, set to ignore a player
  who carries the eel-slime effect. **Eel-slime:** a potion of ours (Skyrim's slaughterfish egg, under our name) that
  gives that effect for an hour.
- **Named things from meshes we hold:** the toll purse, the Eelskin ring of Fortify Pickpocket, the gold pearl,
  Matius's sketch of the hulk, the marked rice sack and its pearl box, the cellar hatch key, the golden ring of
  Rushiit Den, the brass medallion, the governor's seal ring (Skyrim's rings and amulets, under our names).
- **The Eelskins' gear:** Skyrim's thieves' leathers, Linwe-style hooded armour and Nightingale-style armour, and
  Animated Armoury's claws, under our names ([reward-gear](../reward-gear.md)).
- **The Still Water ring:** one custom enchantment, constant Chameleon 50%.
