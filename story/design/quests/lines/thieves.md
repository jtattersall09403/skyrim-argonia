# The Eelskins: the plot

This page is the plot of the Eelskins, the thieves' guild of the south, written on 2026-11-16 to the owner's notes in
[the factions overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md) and revised on
2026-11-17 to his story explorer notes. The Eelskins have two rivals, the Lampbearers ([lamps](lamps.md)) and the
Shadowscales ([shadowscales](shadowscales.md)). Each of the three lines tells its own late story; the rivals appear
in it with the same leaders and the same seats. Every quest is set in a place whose source holds what the quest
needs, and a bracket says which source place gives it and how we know: what that source's buildings, interiors and placed pieces are. Everything is
inside the quest ceiling ([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the blocks listed
in section 8.

**Words.** *The Eelskins*: the thieves' guild of the south, named for the eel-slime that keeps Lilmoth's ripper eels
off a swimmer. *Ripper eels*: eels that bite whatever swims among them. *Eel-slime*: what a thief rubs on to swim
among them. *Pusbottom*: the old, poor heart of Lilmoth, where crime lives. *A fence*: a trader who buys stolen goods.
*The Lampbearers*: the investigators of Soulrest. *The Shadowscales*: Argonian assassins. *The Owing*: the marsh's
sentence, owed work in place of a cell. *The Xeech-Leel*: the party that rules the province from Helstrom, which
rose after the An-Xileel fell. *The Archwardens*: the Xeech-Leel's wardens, who man the old forts and tolls. *The
great heist*: the theft the Eelskins have planned for years.

## 1. What the faction is

The Eelskins are a guild of thieves in the south of Black Marsh, with their hall hidden under Lilmoth. An Eelskin
steals: they lift purses, pick locks, slip into houses and strongrooms, and sell what they take through the guild's
fences.

Their outpost is a hulk moored at the boardwalks of Blackrose, where new members prove themselves. Their hall is in
the drowned cellars under Pusbottom, in Lilmoth, which no outsider knows. Their skills are Sneak, Pickpocket,
Lockpicking, Speech and Enchanting, for stealth enchantments on the thief's own gear, with the theft and stealth
spells ([spell-groups](../spell-groups.md)): Telekinesis, Muffle, Throw Noise and Detect Life. Their rivals are the
Lampbearers, who catch thieves. The Shadowscales, who kill, are rivals too. Joining gives the player fences that buy what they
steal, trainers, a share of every job and, at the end, the great heist.

**Why they are there.** Lilmoth's Pusbottom is canon's criminal quarter (`lilmoth.md:46-47`), and the south's
crime is native: smugglers, the Blackguards, tribeless brigands and Bramman's hidden river from Topal Bay to the
Blackrose lakes (`topics/guilds-and-orders.md:179-195`). Lilmoth keeps ripper eels in its canals, and they leave alone
whoever is rubbed with eel-slime (`topics/guilds-and-orders.md:198-201`). **X:** the Eelskins are the thieves who
learnt that, and took the name. They are no branch of any Imperial guild.

**Their history and their name.** After joining, the player can ask Corso Delling at the hulk about both. He
answers: "When Lilmoth was built again on its stilts, the thieves of Pusbottom moved into the drowned cellars, and
the eels came with the water. A woman called Grey-Gill found that eel-slime kept them off, and the cellars became
ours. We named ourselves for her trick. We have never sent a coin to any guild in Cyrodiil."

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** The Eelskins rob merchants, smugglers and an Archwarden fort, and they pay well. Their
leader, Swims-Without-Ripples, is planning one great theft: the treasury of the Xeech-Leel themselves, in the
council house of their seat at Helstrom, the city no invader has ever taken (the place's record). It holds the gold
that the An-Xileel took from Old Imperial Lilmoth when they looted it (`lilmoth.md:79-80`), which passed to the
Xeech-Leel when the An-Xileel fell after Umbriel (`argonia-4e201-state.md` §1). Then, with the heist planned, the
Lampbearers close in on the guild, and two rivals offer the Eelskins a way past them.

**What is true.** Swims-Without-Ripples has wanted that gold for twenty years. Her great-grandmother's family were
among the Pusbottom families whom the An-Xileel robbed and drove out of Old Lilmoth. To her the gold is owed, and the
Xeech-Leel, who say they are everything the An-Xileel were not, have kept it in a strongroom and never given a coin
back. She has not told the player that the Lampbearers' case against the guild began with the player's own work at
Fort Gysilla, or that she will trade with either rival to save her heist.

**Why it does not touch the main quest.** The treasury lies in the council house, not in the Toh-Reel-Ka's quiet
house or any place the main quest uses. A player seen in the theft is wanted only by the council house's wardens, and
the bounty can be paid. The Xeech-Leel blame no one by name, and the journal says the Toh-Reel-Ka ask questions in
Lilmoth and find nobody.

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** People in Lilmoth, Blackrose, Soulrest, Archon and Truowa speak of the Eelskins when asked about
thieves, the canals or Pusbottom: "Lilmoth's thieves swim among the ripper eels. They call themselves the Eelskins."
"Look for the Eelskins all you like. They watch who steals, and they find you." The first such line starts the small
quest *The Eelskins*, with no marker. Each new rumour the player hears puts a line in its journal.

**Being let in.** The first time the player steals anything, the Eelskins notice. The next time the player sleeps,
the sleep is cut short. Sees-Nothing stands in the room and speaks first, and the talk cannot be walked away from. He
says the player's work was seen, and that there is a place for them in a family if they want one. He gives a note
and a key. The screen fades, and when it clears he is gone. The note reads: "The hulk at the Blackrose boardwalks. The
key fits the deck hatch." The journal marks the hulk, and quest 1 begins. Blackrose's canoe-men and the boats of
Lilmoth and Truowa reach it.

**What shuts the door.** The Eelskins' door shuts when the player takes the rank of Flame at the end of the
Lampbearers' 4, The Walled Cellar, or takes the brass at the end of the Shadowscales' 4, The Hatchling Seller. *The Eelskins* then fails,
and the first theft no longer brings a visitor. In this line, the Lampbearers' and the Shadowscales' doors shut at
the end of quest 4, when the player is given the key to the hall. Corso warns them first: a player who is a
Lampbearer or a Shadowscale of the lowest rank is put out of it, and neither will take them again.

## 5. The acts

The Eelskins' line is twelve quests in three acts. Act I is the outpost at Blackrose, where the player proves
themselves. Act II is the hall under Pusbottom and the jobs that build to a first heist. Act III is the great heist:
the plan, the preparations, the choice of ally and the heist.

Danger climbs with the line: Blackrose (D0), a farm and a salvagers' village (D2), the villages, ferries and ruins
of the washes (D3), an old keep and the nest at Rushiit Den (D4). The great heist itself is at Helstrom, a city of
D0 by its band, and it is the hardest job of the line in its locks, its guards and its choices of way. Locks and
pockets climb with the line: Novice in quests 1 and 2, Apprentice and Adept in quests 3 to 6, Expert in quests 7 to
10, and Master at the strongroom. Where the player may fight or sneak, the journal says so. Every objective has a
marker, and each marker leads to the area, the house or the room, never to the pocket. An Eelskin who kills loses a
share: each job pays less for every person killed, and the journal says so.

**Ranks and rewards.** Each is fixed and hand-made. Each job also pays a fixed share in gold, from 75 in quest 1 to
800 in quest 8, so the boats are paid for from the first job.

| Rank | Given after | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Slick | quest 1 | Eelskin boots and hood (Skyrim's thieves' leathers, under our name), fifteen lockpicks, Corso's fence at the outpost |
| Eel | quest 4 | the key to the hall; the Pusbottom fence, who buys stolen goods to 2,000 gold; Sees-Nothing as an Expert Pickpocket trainer |
| Ripper | quest 8 | Eel's Teeth (Animated Armoury's claws, under our name), enchanted to absorb stamina; the hall's arcane enchanter and a bed |
| Deep Eel | quest 10 | the Eelskin hooded cuirass and gauntlets (Skyrim's Linwe-style hooded armour, under our name), enchanted with Fortify Sneak 20 and Fortify Pickpocket 20; a Master Lockpicking trainer in the hall |
| Master Eel | quest 12 | the Still Water ring, the Master Eel's armour (Skyrim's Nightingale-style armour, renamed and recoloured by enchantment only), and the ending's gift (section 6) |

### Act I: the outpost

**1. The Hulk at the Boardwalk** (Blackrose, the lake-island city where three rivers meet). The key opens the deck
hatch of the hulk moored at the boardwalks (checked: Blackrose's source is Black Rose from Black Marsh North, a town of 222 buildings over water with a ship among its interiors, so the ship's pieces and interior serve as the hulk and the outpost below its hatch, and an enchanting table of Skyrim's is set in the outpost). Below,
Corso Delling runs the outpost: a fence, a bed and an Eelskin or two. He sets the first test. The canoe-men's
toll-taker at the landing of the island core carries the day's tolls in a purse. The journal says: "Take the
toll-taker's purse at the landing of the island core." The marker leads to the landing. The player lifts the purse
(Pickpocket, an easy pocket) or picks the toll-box in his shelter (a Novice lock) while he is out at the canoes. A
new character with a lockpick can do it. *Leads on:* Corso pays 75 gold, makes the player a Slick and sends them to
Reedfall Farm, which the journal marks, a short walk east along the channel.

**2. The Rice Shed** (Reedfall Farm, a rice farm on the channel east of Blackrose). The family keeps the season's
rice money in a strongbox in the rice shed (checked: Reedfall's source is Pinewood Farm from Nyhus, a farm of 158 buildings with two interiors, which serve as the stilted house and the rice shed, and the paddies and reed beds are added). Marsh beasts come up the channel bank at night, so one of the family is always awake on watch (the record's
dangers). The journal says the player may sneak in or take the key, and the marker leads to the shed. The player
waits for the watcher to walk to the landing and slips into the shed (Sneak), then picks the strongbox (a Novice
lock). Or the player lifts the key from the eldest son, who sleeps in the stilted house (Pickpocket). *Leads on:*
Corso pays the player's share. He has an Adept pocket for them at Natai, and the journal marks the salvagers'
longhouse.

**3. Light Fingers** (Natai, a salvagers' village at the southern edge of the washes). Corso teaches the player to
enchant. He gives them an old Eelskin ring of Fortify Pickpocket and a petty soul gem. At the outpost's enchanter the
player breaks the ring to learn its enchantment and puts it on a pair of gloves. At Natai, the salvager who strips
the wreck off the beach carries a gold pearl in his pocket (checked: Natai's source is King of the Murkmire's Seekhat Yol, Argonian stilt huts with dock pieces whose fisher's hut and accommodation hut serve as the salvagers' longhouse, and the wreck off the beach is an addition of Skyrim's wrecked ship pieces, as at Pilgrims Trench). The journal says: "Find the
salvager in the longhouse." The pocket is Adept: the gloves bring a new thief's Pickpocket to it. The player can
instead pick his sea-chest in the longhouse at night (an Apprentice lock), or dive to the wreck for the other pearls
past the sea-drake that hunts off the shore (the record's sea-drakes). *Leads on:* Corso has heard a Lampbearer
asking about the hulk. The journal marks Ka-Asei.

**4. The Lampbearer's Man** (Blackrose; Ka-Asei, a stilt village a day from Blackrose). A man called Bolus Matius
lodges in Ka-Asei's longhouse and has been asking canoe-men about the hulk. Corso wants the sketch of it from his
strongbox (an Apprentice lock). Bandits moor their boats under the stilts at night (checked: Ka-Asei's source is Keeba Hollow from King of the Murkmire, an Argonian stilt village whose fisher's hut and crafter's hut serve as the longhouse and whose dock pieces make the moorings under the stilts, where the bandits' boats are placed), so
the walk from the landing to the longhouse passes them. The journal says: "Take Matius's sketch from his lodging in
the longhouse." When the player has the sketch, Matius speaks first. He says he is a Lampbearer, and offers 300 gold
to learn where the Eelskins' hall is. The player can refuse him. They can take the gold and give him a false place
(Speech). They can sell him the truth. Matius is Corso's own man, and the offer is the test. A player who sold Matius
the hall's place for his gold is put out of the Eelskins, and the line ends there. A player who refused is made an
Eel. A player who took the gold and lied keeps it, and Corso gives a further 100. *Leads on:* Corso tells them what
the rank costs (section 4) and gives them the key to the hall under Pusbottom. The hall's way in is guarded by
ripper eels, and they need eel-slime first; the nearest is with the smugglers of Brackwater Waystation. The journal
marks it.

### Act II: the hall under Pusbottom

**5. Eel-Slime** (Brackwater Waystation, a ruined customs shed east of Lilmoth; Lilmoth, the rebuilt stilt city).
Smugglers guard their cache in the sea-cave under the shed, entered by a hatch in the shed floor, and they keep
ripper eels in its deep pool to render slime (checked: Brackwater's source is Old Laboratory from King of the Murkmire, a small Imperial ruin on the water with one interior that serves as the sea-cave, while the shed above it, the hatch in its floor and the deep pool in the cave are added, with Skyrim's slaughterfish placed in the pool). The journal says the player may
fight the smugglers or go quietly. The player fights them, sneaks past them to the pool (Sneak, with Muffle from the
outpost's spell-seller), or buys a jar from their cache-master (Speech and 150 gold). With the slime on, the player
opens the hatch on Pusbottom's walkways and swims the cistern below it, where the hall's own ripper eels leave them
alone (checked: Lilmoth's source is King of the Murkmire's Lilmoth, whose sewers have a hideout room that serves as the hall under Pusbottom, entered through a hatch door that we set on the walkways, and whose first sewer room, the Surface Cisterns, serves as the cistern behind it). Without the slime
the eels attack. At the far end is the hall, and its people, who attack a stranger, let a member pass. Its watchers
take the player to Swims-Without-Ripples. *Leads on:* she sets the player to work. Her first job is at the delta
ferry.

**6. The Marked Sack** (Delta Ferry, the crossing east of Blackrose). A Lilmoth council merchant ships pearls across
the delta, hidden in a marked rice sack (checked: Delta Ferry's source is Alten Meerhleel from King of the Murkmire, a small Argonian dock with a moored ship, whose dock pieces make the plank landing, while the raft, the lamp post and the rice sacks are added). The sack lies overnight on the raft moored at the
landing, with the pearl box tied on top of the load, and the merchant's two guards stand on the plank landing between
the shore and the raft. The journal says: "Take the pearl box from the merchant's raft." The marker leads to the
landing. Swims teaches the player Telekinesis, and from the reeds on the far bank the player lifts the box off the
raft across the water. Crocodilians lie in the channel (the record's dangers), so swimming out is the hard way. The
player can also lift the raft's key from a guard (Pickpocket, an Adept pocket), or walk in and fight. *Leads on:*
Swims has a bigger cellar for them.

**7. The Locked Strongroom** (Fort Swiftmoth, an empty keep inland from Archon). Smugglers of Bramman's Brethren,
the pirates, cache their goods in the keep's locked strongroom off the lower hall, with a watch of four and their
captain (checked: Fort Swiftmoth's source is Mistwatch from Skyrim, a ruined Imperial fort round a tall stair-tower with three interiors that are the tower's halls one above another, so its lowest hall serves as the lower hall, a side room of it as the strongroom behind a door that we lock, and its top hall as the dry upper floor). The Topal Bay Company's 11, The Scroll Store, raids the same strongroom for the
scrolls in it. Each of the two quests switches on its own watch and its own store when it starts, so they can be
played in either order. Aroxhu
families rest in the dry upper floor and are to be left alone. The journal says: "Empty the strongroom off the lower
hall." The captain carries the strongroom key, and the player can lift it (Pickpocket, an Expert pocket), pick the
strongroom (an Expert lock), or fight the watch to reach it. Mud-crocodiles lie in the ditch round the rise. *Leads
on:* Swims says the player is ready for a heist of their own. Truowa's council wants the Archwardens' toll taken
from Fort Gysilla.

**8. The Toll Chest** (Fort Gysilla, an Archwarden toll fort on a lake island; Truowa, the lake harbour). The first
heist. A Truowa council merchant, who will not pay Gysilla's toll, pays the Eelskins to take it (the record's quarrel
between the council and the fort). The tolls lie in an iron chest in the garrison's barracks (checked: Fort Gysilla's walls come from Fort Tulis in Journey to Baan Malur, a ruined Imperial fort with no interior, and its barracks interior comes from Cracked Tusk, an Imperial fort from Skyrim with two keep interiors). The player chooses the way in. They can
swim to the islet by night and climb the curtain wall (FreeClimb, or a scroll). They can ride in a rice crate on the
garrison's supply boat, which takes a bribe of 200 gold to its boatman at Truowa's quay or an Expert Speech check
(75): the screen fades, and the player stands on the fort's landing. Or they can force the gate, and the journal
warns that a fight sets a bounty with Fort Gysilla's garrison. The same garrison buys and sells Owings at its crossing, and some of
those it holds go on to House Ulvarys (the Chainbreakers' 9, The Island Keep). The chest's key is on the toll-master,
and the lock is Expert. Two guards stand in the barracks and the rest are at the landing and the toll chain, so a
quiet player can reach it. A player who freed Naxi-Ru in the Chainbreakers' 9, The Island Keep, has learnt from Naxi-Ru the
hour the guards on the curtain wall change. *Leads on:* the merchant pays 800 gold at Truowa. Swims makes the player a Ripper and calls
them to the hall. She is ready to speak of the great heist.

### Act III: the great heist

**9. The Council's Strongroom** (Lilmoth; Helstrom, the interior capital). Swims tells the player what the great
heist is and why the gold is owed (section 2). She sends them to look the council house over. The Xeech-Leel govern
in the open, so the council house's hall stands open to anyone by day (`argonia-4e201-state.md` §1). The journal
says: "Go to Helstrom and look over the council house" (checked: Helstrom's own source, Black Marsh & Valenwood's Helstrom, has only a one-room Argonian house and a ship among its interiors, so the council house is a building to be added to Helstrom from Dragonthorn Manor of King of the Murkmire's Lilmoth, whose hall with silver plate and writing desks, stair to an upper room and partition doors serve as the hall, the treasurer's room and the doors, with Dragonthorn Manor Barracks as the wardens' quarters and the strongroom added as one locked iron door and a room of Imperial pieces). The marker leads to the house; Lilmoth's lighter-men sail to
Truowa, and Truowa's boat road runs on to Helstrom's raft landing (Truowa's record names the road). Inside by day the
player sees the hall, the wardens on watch, the stair to the treasurer's room and the iron door to the strongroom.
Outside they find the three ways in, and the journal records each as it is found: the front door, where two wardens
stand at night; the back door to the river landing, barred inside and locked (an Expert lock); and the gallery door
above the street, which a warden walks and which a climber reaches up the stilts (FreeClimb). *Leads on:* Swims says
they will need two of four things before they go in, and the journal names all four.

**10. Two of Four** (Lilmoth; Rushiit Den, a Lilmothiit ruin on the northern island; Helstrom). The preparations.
The player does any two of these, and then Swims calls the plan set. Each one removes a danger from the heist.

- *The cook's debt.* Gathe-Ru, the council house's cook, owes a Lilmoth moneylender. His note of her debt lies in the
  strongbox of his manor in Lilmoth (an Expert lock). The journal says: "Take the cook's note of debt from the
  moneylender's manor." The player picks it at night, or lifts his key at the stilt-hall tavern (Pickpocket, an Expert
  pocket), and takes the note to her at Helstrom. With it, she unbars the back door on the night of the heist, and
  it opens without its lock.
- *The treasurer's key.* The council's treasurer carries the strongroom's key on his belt when he walks Helstrom's
  market by day. The journal says: "Lift the treasurer's key in Helstrom's market." The player lifts it (Pickpocket,
  an Expert pocket). With it, the iron door opens without its Master lock.
- *Silent feet.* The bull crocodile of Rushiit Den wears a golden ring in its nest chamber (checked: Rushiit Den's tunnels come from Huluxinoag of King of the Murkmire, an Argonian ruin with one interior of cave pieces that serves as the tunnels and the nest chamber, and the golden ring is an added item). The ring carries Muffle. The player kills the
  beast, or lifts the ring while it sleeps, breaks the ring at the hall's enchanter and puts Muffle on their own
  boots. With them, the wardens never hear the player's feet.
- *The raft out.* A raft-master at Helstrom's city landing (the record's raft-masters) takes 300 gold, or an Expert
  Speech check (75), to wait behind the council house on the night. With it, the player leaves the strongroom by the
  back door and a line with him fades them to Truowa's quay. Without it, they walk out through the city with the
  house roused behind them.

*Leads on:* Swims makes the player a Deep Eel. Then she sends for the player: the Lampbearers are at the guild's door.

**11. Two Offers** (Lilmoth, the hall and Black-Tongue Brews; Soulrest, the Lamp-Hall; Truowa; Ra'tal Den, a
Lilmothiit ruin of the Banujha Basin). This quest holds the line's choice, written as the player meets it.

1. *In the hall*, Swims-Without-Ripples speaks first. J'zahri of the Lampbearers' Truowa branch has squeezed the
   Truowa merchant who hired the Fort Gysilla job, and she is a few days from naming Swims before the arbiter for it.
   A Swims sent to the Owing robs nobody. Two offers have come through the guild's fences. The journal says: "Hear
   the Shadowscale at Black-Tongue Brews and Marcella Dovi at the Lamp-Hall in Soulrest, then give one of them your
   word." Markers lead to both doors.
2. *At Black-Tongue Brews* (checked: Black-Tongue Brews is a named interior of King of the Murkmire's Lilmoth, a city of 46 interiors),
   Hulee-Tah speaks first. The Lampbearers hunt his Eldest for three killings. If the Eelskins help end the
   Lampbearers, a Shadowscale will kill Marcella Dovi, and the player is to kill J'zahri and her two Lampbearers at
   Truowa and take her case chest. Then the two orders share the south.
3. *At the Lamp-Hall*, Marcella Dovi speaks first. She cannot find the Shadowscales' Eldest. If the Eelskins find
   his den and help her Lampbearers take him, she will order J'zahri to drop the Gysilla case and take no case against
   an Eelskin again.
4. *The word.* The player may hear both in either order. Saying "You have a bargain" to one of them is the choice:
   it sets the stage for that ally, and the other's line closes. Until then the journal keeps both markers.

- *With the Lampbearers.* Marcella says the order's people buy from Black-Tongue Brews, and its keeper will know
  where the Eldest meets his novices. The journal says: "Find where the Eldest is to be found. Search Black-Tongue
  Brews." The keeper's strongbox in the back room (an Expert lock), or his key lifted from his belt (Pickpocket),
  holds the order's word: "The trial at Ra'tal Den, when the moons are dark." The journal marks Ra'tal Den, a walk
  west from Blackrose's landing. Two Lampbearers are standing at the den's mouth when the player arrives. With them, the
  player goes in past the mudcrabs (the record's) and takes the Eldest in the hearth chamber. He and his novices
  fight to the death. Marcella sends word to J'zahri, and the case is dropped.
- *With the Shadowscales.* The journal says: "Kill J'zahri and her Lampbearers at Truowa and take her case chest."
  J'zahri and two Lampbearers keep watch in the Lampbearers' house on the quay. The player kills them openly, or one at
  a time unseen, and takes the chest. The same night a Shadowscale kills Marcella Dovi in Soulrest, and Hulee-Tah
  tells the player so at Black-Tongue Brews.

*Leads on:* either way, the night of the heist is set. The journal marks the council house at Helstrom.

**12. The Great Heist** (Helstrom). The player goes in by one of the three ways, and the preparations they chose make
each easier. Six wardens are spread through the house at night: two at the front door, one on the gallery, two in
the hall and one before the iron door, so a quiet player can reach the strongroom by any way. The treasurer sleeps in
his room upstairs, with the key on him for a player who did not lift it before (Pickpocket, an Expert pocket). The
strongroom's lock is Master, or open to the treasurer's key. Inside lie the An-Xileel's gold of Old Lilmoth (4,000
gold), the Imperial governor's seal ring of Old Lilmoth and a chest of Imperial silver. A warden who sees the player
raises the house, and the journal says the player may fight their way out. *Leads on:* back in the hall, Swims takes
her share and gives the player theirs: half the gold and the seal ring. She names them Master Eel and gives them the
Still Water ring. The line ends. The Eelskins' jobs go on as small thefts that the Pusbottom fence offers again and
again.

## 6. The choice and its endings

The choice in quest 11 sets the end. Both endings give the Still Water ring: constant-effect Chameleon 50%, a custom
enchantment made for this line (no Chameleon spell exists).

**With the Lampbearers.**

- *It gives:* the Hook-and-Eye, the Eldest's dagger that absorbs health, from his body at Ra'tal Den. The
  Lampbearers look away: once, Marcella clears the player's bounty in Soulrest and Truowa, and the Lampbearers never

### What it asks of the ceiling

Three blocks that the owner approved, each proven once before its first use:

- **The first theft.** A quest starts when the player first steals. The simplest build: Skyrim's own story event for
  a theft starts the joining quest.
- **The visit that wakes the player from rest.** On the player's next sleep after that, the sleep ends early.
  Sees-Nothing is switched on in the room and speaks first, and the talk cannot be left. The simplest build: Skyrim's
  own story event for the start of sleep; a stage that ends the sleep, switches him on beside the bed and starts his
  talk.
- **The fade after which a person is gone.** When his talk ends, the screen fades and he is switched off while it is
  black. The same block serves the Lampbearers, the Shadowscales and the Chainbreakers. The supply boat of quest 8 and the raft out of
  quest 12 use the same fade, with the player moved while the screen is black.

The rest is inside the ceiling. The pay that falls with each killing is a line gated on named people alive. The four
preparations are four stages, and the heist's locks and the cook's door are switched by them. The word given in
quest 11 is a line that sets one of two stages.
