# The Shadowscales: the plot

This page is the plot of the Shadowscales, the Argonian assassins, written on 2026-11-16 to the owner's notes in
[the factions overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md). It replaces the
earlier page of this name. It is one of three plots that share one late event: the Lamps' ([lamps](lamps.md)) and
the Eelskins' ([thieves](thieves.md)) are the other two. Every quest is set in a place whose source place holds what
the quest needs (`python3 -m tools.newworld.place_card <place>`). Everything is inside the quest ceiling
([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the additions listed in section 8.

**Words.** *The Shadowscales*: Argonian assassins. Argonians born under the sign of the Shadow are given to the order
as hatchlings. *Ku-vastei*: "those who bring needed change", the Argonian name for the Shadowscales when they served
as judges and enforcers. *The brass*: the brass medallion that every Shadowscale wears. *A contract*: one killing the
order takes on. *The Eldest*: the order's head. *Shadow-born*: an Argonian born under the Shadow. *The Black-Tongues*:
the Murkmire tribe that gives the order most of its hatchlings. *The Dark Brotherhood*: the assassins of the Empire,
who once trained the Shadowscales. *The Lamps*: the investigators of Soulrest. *The Eelskins*: the thieves' guild of
the south.

## 1. What the faction is

The Shadowscales are a secret order of Argonian assassins who kill for the Argonian people. A Shadowscale takes a
contract on someone who has wronged Argonians (a slaver, a hatchling seller, a killer the law cannot reach) and kills
them cleanly and unseen, in the way the order sets for that contract.

The order hides in the sealed Shadowscale school at Archon, closed since 4E 187 (`archon.md:48-52`), which it entered
again by the cellar of the old ku-vastei's house beside it. It keeps a branch in Lilmoth behind Black-Tongue Brews, a
brew-house whose keepers send the order its Shadow-born. Its trial ground is Ra'tal Den in the Banujha Basin. Its
skills are Sneak, Lockpicking, Archery, One-Handed and Light Armour, with Illusion: Muffle, Invisibility, Night-Eye and
Throw Noise ([spell-groups](../spell-groups.md); Chameleon exists only as an enchantment). Its rivals are the Lamps and
the Eelskins. Joining gives the player contracts with a bonus for the clever way, a unique enchanted thing nearly
every contract, and the order's trainers.

**Why they are back.** In canon the order served Black Marsh as ku-vastei, judges and enforcers who "act with
impunity" (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:61-67`). Each wears a brass medallion (`:68`), and one Shadowscale
may not kill another (`:69-70`). The Archon school closed in 4E 187 and the Shadowscale Veezara died in Skyrim in
4E 201, called the last of them (`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:85`). The Black-Tongues have had nowhere to
send their Shadow-born since (`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:214-216`). **X:** an old Shadowscale who stayed in
Black Marsh, Ukka-Teel, took them in. Veezara was real, and his death was real. The word that he was the last was
spread by the order as cover, and the old arbiter Teesh-Vaal, once a Shadowscale, carried it.

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** The order takes the player in by a test. It sends them on contracts against slavers
and hatchling sellers, each with a clever way that pays a bonus. Then the Eldest learns that Orrel-Sakka, first voice
of Lilmoth's merchant council, means to sell the order's hiding place to the Dark Brotherhood. The Eldest takes that
contract himself, with the player's help.

**What is true.** The Dark Brotherhood's Listener at Cheydinhal wants the Archon school
(`topics/guilds-and-orders.md:203-223`). Her agent, Lucan Vetrix, has bought Shadow-born hatchlings for a year
through smugglers, to train them for the Brotherhood. A free order of ku-vastei would be the end of that, and an
order shown to the world can be found and destroyed. Ukka-Teel kills to keep the hatchlings his. He tells the player
it is for the hatchlings' sake, and that is half the truth.

### The night at Silt-Orrel (the same in all three plots)

Orrel-Sakka is the first voice of Lilmoth's merchant council and the head of the family that holds Silt-Orrel
Estate, north of Lilmoth. In spring he learnt that the Shadowscales live and that their hatchlings train in the
sealed school at Archon. He agreed to sell that to Lucan Vetrix, an Imperial who comes to the estate as a rice buyer.
Vetrix is an agent of the Dark Brotherhood's Listener at Cheydinhal, who wants the Archon school for the Brotherhood
(`archon.md:48-52`; `topics/guilds-and-orders.md:203-223`).

On the night before Vetrix was to be told, Ukka-Teel came up the reed channel to the drain grate under the
rice-barn. The grate had been cut the night before. He climbed to the bed-hall and killed Orrel-Sakka with one cut.
Orrel-Sakka tore the brass medallion from the Eldest's neck as he died. Every Shadowscale wears one
(`topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:68`). On its back is cut its owner's own sign, a hook and an eye (**X**).

In the cellar below, an Eelskin scout, Drips-From-Eaves, was taking the measure of the vault door for the Eelskins'
great heist. He heard the fall and went up. He found the body and the medallion on the floor beside it. He took the
medallion and left by the cellar hatch. He saw the killer's back at the channel and nothing more. He gave the
medallion to Swims-Without-Ripples, the Eelskins' leader. Then he went to ground at Meris-Keth among the Khajiit
smugglers, sure that a Shadowscale would hunt whoever had it.

The next morning the family sent to Soulrest for the Lamps. Two Lamps and the family's guards hold the house as the
scene of the crime. The medallion lies in Swims-Without-Ripples's strongbox in the Eelskins' hall under Pusbottom, in
Lilmoth.

**How it ends depends on the line the player is in.** Three sides can come of it:

- *The Lamps and the Eelskins against the Shadowscales.* The Eelskins give the medallion to the Lamps. The Lamps take
  the Eldest at Ra'tal Den, where the order holds its trials. The Lamps then look away while the Eelskins rob the
  vault at Silt-Orrel.
- *The Lamps and the Shadowscales against the Eelskins.* The medallion is stolen from Swims-Without-Ripples's
  strongbox. Orrel-Sakka's council ring, which the Eldest took from the body, is shown as found in her hall. She is
  named the murderer and taken in a raid on the Eelskins' hall. The Lamps then let the Shadowscales work.
- *The Eelskins and the Shadowscales against the Lamps.* The Eelskins give the medallion back to the Eldest. A
  Shadowscale kills Marcella Dovi, the Lamps' chief, in the Lamp-Hall. Eelskins kill J'zahri and the Lamps at Truowa.
  The Lamps break up, and the Eelskins rob the vault.

The Shadowscales' line offers the player the second and the third. In this line the player cuts the grate, and in
the third side the player is the Shadowscale who kills Marcella Dovi.

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** People in Lilmoth, Archon, Uluo, Blackrose and Soulrest speak of the Shadowscales when asked about
death, the Black-Tongues or the old school at Archon. "The last of the Shadowscales died in Skyrim, they say." "The
old folk say a brass medallion still turns up on a drowned man now and then." "A drowned Argonian lies under the
landing at Xi-Inou with brass at his neck, and the raft-master's family will not touch him." The first line starts the
small quest *The Shadowscales*, with no marker. Each rumour the player hears goes into its journal. The third names the landing at
Xi-Inou, and a marker then shows it.

**Being let in.** Under the landing at Xi-Inou lie a drowned man's bones and his satchel. In the satchel are a brass
medallion and a sealed writ. The writ is a contract in the order's old form. It names Davos Rethan, a Dunmer smuggler
who puts in at Xi-Inou's night quay, as the man who drowned a raft-boy who saw his cargo. "Needed change. The brass
goes back to the old one by the closed school in Archon when the work is done." The player may do the work (quest 1)
and carry the brass to Archon (quest 2). The dead man is a test: Hulee-Tah left a smuggler's bones there with his own
satchel, and the order watches who finishes the work. This is how the order has always found its outsiders. It is why
the rumour of brass on drowned men exists.

A player who takes the writ to Davos Rethan to warn him, or to the Lamps, ends the test. The small quest fails, and
the order never sends another writ.

**What shuts the door.** The Shadowscales' door shuts when the player takes the Lamp at the end of quest 4 of the
Lamps' line, or is given the key to the Eelskins' hall at the end of quest 4 of the Eelskins' line. *The Shadowscales*
then fails, and the satchel under the landing is gone. In this line, the Lamps' and the Eelskins' doors shut at the
end of quest 4, when the player takes the brass. Hulee-Tah warns them first: a player who is a Lamp or an Eelskin of
the lowest rank is put out of it, and neither will take them again.

## 5. The acts

The Shadowscales' line is thirteen quests in three acts. Act I is the test and the first contracts. Act II is
contracts on the order's enemies, harder each time, each with a clever way. Act III is the Brotherhood's agent, the
night at Silt-Orrel, the lost brass and the choice.

**Contracts.** Hulee-Tah names each contract's target and its bonus way: a clever method the order prefers. The bonus is
paid if the method's thing was used (a rope cut, a lever pulled, a door opened, a dish poisoned) and the target is
dead. A contract done any other way is paid without the bonus. Nearly every contract also earns a unique enchanted
thing, as the Dark Brotherhood's did in Oblivion. Killing anyone but the target costs part of the pay, and the journal
says so before the player goes.

Danger climbs with the line: a hamlet (D1), Archon (D0), a farm (D2), the ruins and forts of the coast (D3), the
abandoned towns and mines (D4) and, last, Meris-Keth and Ra'tal Den (D5). Locks climb with it: Novice in Act I, Adept
and Expert in Act II, Master in Act III. Where the player may fight or sneak, the journal says so. Every objective
has a marker.

**Ranks and rewards.** Each is fixed and hand-made. The order pays from 100 gold in quest 1 to 1,000 in quest 8 for a contract,
and the bonus doubles it.

### Act I: the brass

**1. The Drowned Man's Writ** (Xi-Inou, a raft hamlet on Archon's estuary). The player takes the satchel from under
the landing and reads the writ. Davos Rethan drinks at the night quay with two of his crew. The journal says that the
player may fight or go quietly, and that the order prefers the stack. A stack of logs is roped above the landing, and
its rope can be cut when he stands below it. The player can also shoot him from the bank (Archery), stab him on his
way to his boat (Sneak and One-Handed) or fight all three. His crew are few and stand at the quay's end, so a quiet
player can reach him. A new character can do it. *Leads on:* the writ says to carry the brass to the old one by the
closed school in Archon. The journal marks Beem-Xal's house.

**2. The Old One by the School** (Archon, the eastern harbour city). Beem-Xal takes the brass and says nothing for a
long moment. Then he takes the player down to his cellar, where a door opens into the sealed school. Hulee-Tah waits
inside, alive: the drowned man was his test. He tells the player that the order lives and what it does. He sets a
first trial in the school's flooded training hall. A brass token lies at the bottom, past a sleeping guard dog and
two novices on watch who will report a careless player. The player reaches the token unseen (Sneak) or picks the
grate below the water (a Novice lock) and swims to it. *Leads on:* the Eldest makes the player a Hatchling. Hulee-Tah
gives the first true contract: a toll sergeant who eats each night at Tal-Neesh Farm.

**3. The Supper at Tal-Neesh** (Tal-Neesh Farm, a rice farm on the island road near Fort Reedmoth). The contract is
the toll sergeant of Fort Reedmoth. He drowned a Shadow-born child of the farm's tenants rather than let the order
have her, and he eats each night at the farm. Hulee-Tah teaches the player a poison: he shows them the recipe, and
the player learns the effects of deathbell and nightshade. The player gathers the deathbell at the marsh edge past the
snakes and buys nightshade at Black-Tongue Brews. They make the poison at the school's alchemy table. Then they enter
the farmhouse while the family is out on the paddies and poison the sergeant's own bowl on the table. The farm's dog
is the only watcher at the door. The sergeant comes in from the paddies to eat, away from where the player stands,
and dies at his meal. A player who would rather can kill him on the road to the fort (One-Handed, Archery), without
the bonus. *Leads on:* Hulee-Tah sends the player after the man who buys the hatchlings. He is at Tailfall Mound.

**4. The Hatchling Buyer** (Tailfall Mound, a Lilmothiit ruin near Fort Reedmoth, where smugglers hide). The
smugglers' headman buys Shadow-born hatchlings from desperate families and sells them on. Two hatchlings sit caged in
the lower hall. The smugglers stand in the outer hall and the stair. The player sneaks through or fights through. The
headman sits at his table under a rotten roof prop. Its lever brings the roof down on him. The player can also shoot
him (Archery) or knife him. The cage's lock is Apprentice. Freed, the hatchlings go to Black-Tongue Brews, and the
screen fades as they leave. *Leads on:* the headman's sale-notes name his buyer only as "the rice buyer of Lilmoth".
Hulee-Tah gives the player the brass, the rank of Brass. Taking it shuts the other doors (section 4).

### Act II: the order's enemies

**5. The Spell-Fire** (Mazurath, a ruined Velothi holding on the headland south of Khiar). The smugglers' boss of
Mazurath ships moon sugar into the bayou villages, and two children of Khiar have died of it. A spell-fire burns on
the upper tier, ghosts walk the lower halls and the smugglers hold the middle. Hulee-Tah teaches Muffle before the
player goes. The bonus way is the old door between the lower halls and the smugglers' rooms, which the smugglers have
barred. With it opened (an Adept lock), the ghosts fall on the smugglers and the boss. The player can also climb to
the spell-fire tier and shoot him, or fight. *Leads on:* the next contract is an outlaw captain at Wrackmoth.

**6. The Gatehouse** (Wrackmoth, a ruined Imperial fort on the cape's north coast). The outlaw captain of Wrackmoth
raids Naxan and takes its young men for the Owing work he sells to the cape's mines. He keeps to the gatehouse. Its
portcullis hangs above the gate where he stands to watch the road, and its lever is in the wall-walk. The player gets
to the lever unseen, or shoots him from the cliff path, or fights through the yard. The captain's chest holds the
names of the men he sold. *Leads on:* Hulee-Tah sends the player into the washes, to Khariton.

**7. The Watch Captain** (Khariton, an abandoned Kothringi town in the washes). The Blackguards hold Khariton, and
their watch captain killed a ku-vastei of Lilmoth last winter. Hulee-Tah teaches Night-Eye. The bonus way is to put out
the watch-fire on the old chime-tower, which leaves the town dark, and to kill the captain in the dark. Blackguards
walk the town in pairs, with gaps a quiet player can use. The player can also fight, or shoot from the tower.
*Leads on:* the next contract is in the gold mines of the cape.

**8. The Winch House** (Old Goldseam, a burnt gold mine of the cape). The outlaw chief of Old Goldseam works
captured Argonians in the old galleries. The galleries are dark and full of his men. Hulee-Tah teaches Invisibility.
The chief sits in the winch house above the ore cage. The bonus way is to release the cage from the winch while he
stands below it. The player can slip past the galleries with Invisibility and Muffle, fight down to him, or shoot him
across the shaft. The captives' shackles open with an Expert lock or the chief's key, and when the player frees them
the screen fades and they are gone. *Leads on:* the Eldest makes the player a Ku-Vastei and speaks to them for the
first time. He has heard of a Brotherhood agent.

### Act III: the brass and the Lamps

**9. The Caged Hatchlings** (Sheh-Ara, a half-sunken stilt village south of Lujei; Lilmoth). Ukka-Teel tells the
player that the "rice buyer of Lilmoth" is Lucan Vetrix, and that he keeps hatchlings at Sheh-Ara before they are
shipped. Raiders from the Rose guard them, and a great crocodile lies under the minder's platform. The player frees
the hatchlings (the cage's Expert lock, or its key on the raiders' leader), and the screen fades as they go. The
player then breaks into Vetrix's room at Lilmoth's stilt-hall tavern (an Expert lock) and takes his letter from the
Listener. It says that Orrel-Sakka of Silt-Orrel will tell him where the order hides, on the next new moon. *Leads
on:* the Eldest says he will take Orrel-Sakka himself. The player is to open his way.

**10. The Drain Grate** (Silt-Orrel Estate, a rice estate north of Lilmoth). The drain grate under the rice-barn is
the way in from the reed channel. The family's dogs sleep in the barn, and slaughterfish swim the channel. The player
swims the channel and picks the grate (an Expert lock) by night, unseen by the guard at the barn door. Then they go
back to the boat landing outside the estate, where the Eldest waits. He goes in. While the player waits at the
landing, the killing happens (section 2). When the player next speaks to him he is back, with blood on his hands and
no brass at his neck. "He tore it off me." *Leads on:* the Eldest tells the player to find who has it. The Blackrose
boardwalk fence, who buys what moves through the washes, is the place to ask.

**11. Who Took the Brass** (Blackrose; Meris-Keth, an abandoned stilt village of the Banujha Basin). The boardwalk
fence will say, for 300 gold or a hard Speech check, that an Eelskin went to ground at Meris-Keth after the night at
Silt-Orrel. Khajiit moon-sugar smugglers hold the dry halls there, and the unstaked dead rise from the water under
the walkways. Hulee-Tah teaches Throw Noise, which draws the smugglers off the walkways. The player reaches
Drips-From-Eaves in the headman's hall. He tells them that Swims-Without-Ripples has the brass. The order's rule is
that a witness who saw only a back may live. The player may spare him or kill him, and the journal records which.
*Leads on:* the Eldest says he must choose an ally, and calls the player to the school.

**12. The Eldest's Choice** (Archon; Lilmoth; Soulrest, the Lamps' city). Two offers have come to the Eldest. The
Eelskins will give back the brass if the order puts out the Lamps. Marcella Dovi of the Lamps will let the order work
if it gives her a murderer. Ukka-Teel leaves the choice to the player, who will do the work.

- *With the Eelskins.* The contract is Marcella Dovi, in the Lamp-Hall at Soulrest. Her hearing room has a great
  iron lamp on a chain, and the bonus way is to drop it on her. The Lamp-Hall's two guards keep the door, and its back
  window has an Expert lock. The player can also shoot her from the gallery, or knife her. The Eelskins kill the Lamps
  at Truowa the same night. Swims-Without-Ripples sends the brass to Archon.
- *With the Lamps.* The player goes to the Eelskins' hall under Pusbottom. They swim the canal past the ripper eels
  with eel-slime from Black-Tongue Brews, or fight the watch at the walkway hatch. They take the brass from
  Swims-Without-Ripples's strongbox (a Master lock, or her key by Pickpocket). Then they carry Orrel-Sakka's council
  ring, which the Eldest gives them, to Marcella in Soulrest, and say that it lay in the Eelskins' strongbox. She
  believes it, because the ring is real. The Lamps raid the hall and take Swims-Without-Ripples to the Owing for life.

*Leads on:* the Eldest calls the player to the trial at Ra'tal Den.

**13. The Trial at Ra'tal Den** (Ra'tal Den, a Lilmothiit ruin of the Banujha Basin). The order's last trial for the
player. They must reach the hearth chamber unseen past three novices placed in the dark halls, or fight past them
without killing one (a Shadowscale may not kill a Shadowscale, `topics/sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md:69-70`). The
giant mudcrab of the hearth chamber is killed in the end with any weapon. Ukka-Teel waits beside the hearth. He
makes the player an Elder, gives them his own dagger and tells them that when he dies the order is theirs. *Leads on:*
the line ends. Contracts go on, small and repeating, from Hulee-Tah.

## 6. The choice and its endings

**With the Eelskins: the joint underworld.**

- *It gives:* the Elder's rank and the Hook-and-Eye. The Lamp-Breaker from the contract. A share of the Silt-Orrel
  heist, 1,500 gold, which the Eelskins send to the school. The Eelskins' fences buy from the player at the best price.
  Swims-Without-Ripples trains Pickpocket to Master. No investigator ever comes after the player again.
- *It costs:* the player killed the Lamps' chief. Soulrest's guards attack on sight until a bounty of 1,000 gold is
  paid. Teesh-Vaal will never speak to the player again, and the Lamps' house at Truowa stands empty.

**With the Lamps: the law's blind eye.**

- *It gives:* the Elder's rank and the Hook-and-Eye. The law's blind eye: once, Marcella clears the player's bounty in
  Soulrest, Truowa and Lilmoth. The Lamps never take a case against a Shadowscale. Teesh-Vaal, glad the order
  lives, trains Illusion to Master.
- *It costs:* every Eelskin attacks on sight, and their fences close. The Silt-Orrel vault stays shut, so the player
  gets no gold from it. The player sent an Argonian woman who did not kill to the Owing for life, and the journal says
  so.

## 7. Rivals

- **The Lamps** ([lamps](lamps.md)). The hatchling buyer of quest 4 is one of the three clean kills of the Lamps'
  quest 11. This line's quest 10 is the night of the Lamps' quest 12, and quest 11 is the Lamps' quest 13 from the
  other side. The trial of quest 13 is where the Lamps' quest 14 ends. The Lamps' frame ending is this line's ending
  with the Lamps.
- **The Eelskins** ([thieves](thieves.md)). This line's quest 10 is the night of the Eelskins' scout. Quest 11 hunts
  the same Drips-From-Eaves whom the Eelskins' preparations bring home. The Eelskins' ending with the Shadowscales is
  this line's ending with the Eelskins.
- **Shared people:** Ukka-Teel, Hulee-Tah, Teesh-Vaal, Swims-Without-Ripples, Drips-From-Eaves, Marcella Dovi and
  Lucan Vetrix are fixed by the night at Silt-Orrel (section 2).

## 8. What it needs, and what it changes elsewhere

### What it asks of the world

- **One danger band lowered.** The database has no place at D1. Xi-Inou is asked at strength D1 (Davos Rethan and two
  crewmen at the night quay at level 1, and the channel's slaughterfish), so that a new character's first contract
  starts at D1. Until it is, `plot_check` fails at quest 1.
- **Named things from meshes we hold:** the drowned man's satchel and bones, the writ, the brass medallions (a Skyrim
  amulet, under our name), the brass token, the poison and its two ingredients (Skyrim's deathbell and nightshade),
  the sergeant's bowl, the sale-notes, Vetrix's letter, Orrel-Sakka's council ring.
- **Traps from Skyrim's own:** a roped log stack (Xi-Inou), a falling roof (Tailfall Mound), a portcullis
  (Wrackmoth), an ore cage on a winch (Old Goldseam), an iron lamp on a chain (the Lamp-Hall). Each is a Skyrim trap
  or lift, placed, with its rope or lever as the named thing used.
- **The order's gear:** Sign of Cipactli's light Argonian armour, Skyrim's bow and Animated Armoury's whip, under our
  names.

### What it asks of the ceiling

The owner asked for one thing that the ceiling lacks, and the line uses the fade asked by the other two plots:

- **The poisoned meal.** Using the sergeant's bowl while holding the poison takes the poison and sets a stage. When
  the player next goes outside, the sergeant, who eats inside, dies. The simplest build: the existing block "a stage
  that kills one named person", used here for this one contract, with the kill made away from the player.
- **The fade after which a person is gone** (the freed hatchlings and captives). The same block as the Lamps' and the
  Eelskins'.

The rest is inside the ceiling. A bonus way is a named thing used (a rope, a lever, a door, a bowl) and the target
dead, both of which the game can know. The Eldest's going in and coming back is two placements of him, switched away
from the player at the landing while they wait outside the house.
