# The Free Marque: the plot

This page is the plot of the pirates' line, written fresh on 2026-11-16 from the owner's words in [the factions
overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/new-worldspace-plan/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md). Its rival is the Nibenese
Company of Gideon ([merchants](merchants.md)). The two plots are one war seen from both sides: section 6 fixes the
shared events once. Every quest is set in a place whose source place holds what it needs
(`python3 -m tools.newworld.place_card <place>`). Everything is inside the quest ceiling
([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the additions listed in section 8.

**Words.** *The Free Marque*: the pirates of this line, named for the letters of marque that their grandmothers once
sailed under. *A letter of marque*: a written licence from a power that lets a captain take its enemies' ships.
*The Company*: the Nibenese Company, the Imperial merchants of Gideon. *The Archwardens*: the province's rulers,
whose garrisons hold the old Imperial forts and take its tolls. *Lukiul*: Argonians who live by Imperial ways, as
most of Gideon's do. *A hulk*: an old ship that no longer sails, run aground and used as a house or a store.

## 1. What the faction is

The Free Marque is a brotherhood of pirates who take Imperial merchant ships on the coasts of Black Marsh and
smuggle what they take past the Archwardens' tolls. A member boards ships, raises cargo from wrecks, fights the
Company's hired swords and carries what the Free Marque sells to buyers who ask no questions.

**Where it sits.** Its seat is Rainbreaker Keep on Skeith Isle, an old Imperial sea-watch keep off the east coast,
with the pirates' stilt camp of Marque Landing in the shallows below it. Its western crew works Topal Bay out of
the mangrove landing at Soulrest, at the mouth of the hidden river that runs inland to the Blackrose lakes. The
two halves are the two pirate grounds that canon gives the province: Archon's coast of chartered ex-privateers and
the heirs of Red Bramman in the mangroves by Soulrest (`archon.md:43-47`, `archon.md:76-78`,
`soulrest.md:13-15`, `soulrest.md:66-69`). Canon says the pirates of Topal Bay "have never truly been eliminated"
(`topics/foreign-powers.md:111`), that Argonian captains are famous as pirates, privateers and smugglers
(`topics/material-culture.md:91-92`) and that Black Marsh has no navy, so its coasts are unpoliced
(`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:195-197`).

Topal Bay's one island is Aphon Isle, which is small, watched by the Archwardens from Aphon Belvedere and has no
keep. So the Free Marque's island is Skeith Isle, which already holds a pirates' keep and camp, and its bay crew
gives it the "inland/river branch" the owner asked for.

The Free Marque also carries out by sea people whom the Chainbreakers have freed. The two are allies.

**Its skills.** Pure combat: One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Block, Light Armour and Heavy Armour. The Free Marque
does not teach spells. It hands out scrolls, and a quest that hands out a scroll uses it: Waterbreathing, Water
Walking and Swift Swim to work the water, and Frenzy and Mayhem to sow chaos among a crew.

**Its rival.** The Nibenese Company, which built its trade on the Free Marque's work a generation ago and then sold
the pirates to the Archwardens (section 3). The Company's line ends with Skeith Isle taken. This line ends with
Gideon taken.

**What joining gives.** Work from the first day, a share of every prize, the bay crew's fence at Soulrest, scrolls
at every rank, a bed and a forge at a fort the player takes, and at the end a city.

### Fights at sea

The owner asked whether there could be fights at sea. Skyrim cannot sail a ship with people fighting on it, and a
swimmer cannot strike or shoot. So every fight of this line is fought where a ship stands still and the ground is
solid: on the deck and in the hold of a ship moored at a fort's sea-gate (quest 5), on the hulks of Marque Landing
(quest 6), inside the air-filled hulls of wrecks on the sea floor (quests 2, 10 and 12) and on a landing raided by
night (quest 3). The sea is crossed with scrolls instead: Water Walking to walk to an island, Waterbreathing and
Swift Swim to dive to a wreck.

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** The Company's ships carry Cyrodiilic goods into Topal Bay and gold and timber out.
The Free Marque takes what it can of them. The Company calls the pirates thieves. The pirates call the Company
worse, and at first the player does not know why.

**What is true.** Thirty years ago Gaius Cassena, who founded the Nibenese Company, wrote letters of marque to the
captains of Skeith Isle. Gideon and Soulrest have quarrelled for centuries over who ships the bay's trade
(`gideon.md:106`). Gaius paid the Skeith captains to take Soulrest's ships, and his Company grew rich on the trade
that Soulrest lost. When the Company no longer needed them, he sold the course of their best ship, the
*Stormwidow*, to the Archwardens. She was chased onto the beach of Skeith Isle and her crew were killed. Her captain
hid her letter of marque in a cache under the cairn on the Grey Tooth before she died.

The player finds the letter in quest 7. Its seal is the Company's seal. Gaius is dead. His daughter, Opilia
Cassena, now runs the Company and says the pirates are thieves and always were.

**What the Free Marque hides.** The Free Marque holds captured Company sailors in the undercroft of Rainbreaker
Keep for ransom. Those whose ransom does not come are put in the wet lower cells, beside the pen where the pirates
keep slaughterfish. The player sees the cells in quest 10.

**The extrapolation.** The Company, its founder and his letters are ours (**X**). They follow from the canon
quarrel of Gideon and Soulrest (`gideon.md:106`), the chartered pirates of the Archon coast (`archon.md:43-47`) and
the recorded view that a shipless power charters the pirates it cannot beat
(`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:401-405`).

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** In Soulrest, Naxan, Acra-Tiin, Olvani, Seafalls and Arorha, fishers and boat crews talk of
the pirates: "There is a Khajiit in the ale-house at Olvani who buys drinks for anyone who looks handy. The
ones who take her up do not come back to the nets." The first time the player hears it, the small quest *The Free
Marque* starts. Its journal entry says that a Khajiit recruiter drinks at the ale-house in Olvani, and a marker
points to her. Each time the player hears a further rumour, the journal gains a line (the Free Marque's island, its
bay crew, what it pays) and nothing else changes.

**Being let in.** Quest 1 is the joining. Ma'jirra buys the player a drink and sets them a brawl with an Imperial
in the room. If the Imperial yields, she names the Free Marque's seat and sends the player to her crew. *The Free
Marque* completes.

**What shuts the door.** The Company's quest 5, *Guarding the Leyawiin Pride*, in which a Company player kills the
Free Marque's boarding party. When that quest completes, *The Free Marque* fails with the entry "The Free Marque
know my face from the deck of the *Leyawiin Pride*. They will not take me on now." A player who already belongs to
the Free Marque and takes that quest is put out of the Free Marque at its end, and the pirates attack them on sight.

## 5. The acts

The line is thirteen quests in three acts. Act I is the bay: small jobs out of Soulrest in places a new character
can survive (D2). Act II is the island: the player crosses to Skeith Isle and learns what the war is about
(D3). Act III is the war on Gideon (D4), which ends in the assault on the Company's house.

Every quest is a fight of some kind, and the ways through are kinds of fight: in close with a blade and shield, at
a distance with a bow, in heavy armour through the front, or with a scroll that turns the enemy on each other.
Every objective has a marker, and the journal says where the player may fight from cover or from range.

The Free Marque's ranks come with the line, each with a fixed reward:

| Rank | Given after | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Deckhand | quest 1 | light Argonian boots and bracers (Sign of Cipactli, under our name "bay-runner's") and 50 gold |
| Boarder | quest 5 | a boarding rapier or a boarding pike (Animated Armoury, under our names), as the player chooses. Ma'jirra's crew sell scrolls of Frenzy from now on |
| Bosun | quest 9 | a set of light Argonian armour (Sign of Cipactli) or a set of steel plate (Skyrim's), as the player chooses, and a bed and the Legion forge at Wrackmoth |
| Mate | quest 12 | enchanted claws that drain stamina or an enchanted halberd that strikes with shock (Animated Armoury, under our names). Selvo sells scrolls of Mayhem from now on |
| Captain | quest 13 | the Marque Coat, armour with an enchantment of our own (Fortify One-Handed, Fortify Two-Handed and Waterbreathing together), and the Company house in Gideon as the player's stronghold |

### Act I: the bay

**1. A Drink and a Brawl** (Olvani, a fishing village on the estuary west of Gideon, with an ale-house). Ma'jirra
drinks in Olvani's ale-house, where the Company's sailors drink ashore. She speaks first, buys the player a cup of Surilie wine and asks what they can do with their fists. An Imperial
sailor drinks at the next table: Lucan Ostorius, master of the Company ship *Leyawiin Pride*. Ma'jirra tells the
player to pick a fight with him. The player says something to Lucan that angers him, and he brawls. The fight is
unarmed, as Skyrim's tavern brawls are, so it rewards Heavy Armour's gauntlets and a strong Block. When Lucan is
beaten he yields, and the player accepts. A player who draws a weapon or kills him has failed the test: Ma'jirra
says she has no use for a killer in a tavern. A second Company sailor drinks at the bar, and the player may try
her test again with him.
*Leads on:* Ma'jirra makes the player a Deckhand. She tells them the Free Marque's seat is Rainbreaker Keep on Skeith
Isle, and that nobody goes to the keep unproved. Her crew at the mangrove landing has work. The journal marks the
landing and names the keep.

**2. The Grey Heron** (The Grey Heron, a wreck on the floor of Topal Bay; Olvani, a fishing village on the
estuary west of Gideon). The *Grey Heron* was a Leyawiin merchantman that struck the bar off Gideon's estuary. Her
hold still holds iron ingots and her stern cabin the captain's chest. The Company has bought the salvage from
Gideon's harbour officer and has sent two divers. Ma'jirra gives the player three scrolls of Waterbreathing and
tells them to get there first. The player wades out from Olvani's jetty and dives. Air has held inside the hull.
The Company's two divers are already inside and attack. The player fights them hand to hand in the narrow hold, or
shoots from the stair to the stern cabin while they climb to it. The chest's key is on the older diver. Inside are
the captain's silver and a cask of Cyrodiilic wine. *Leads on:* back at the landing, Ma'jirra keeps the wine and
gives the player half the silver. She has a second job: the Company keeps a pay chest at Naxan. The journal marks
Naxan.

**3. The Naxan Landing** (Naxan, a fishing and timber village on the cape's north coast). The Company pays its
loggers at Naxan from a strongbox on the log landing. Three Company sellswords guard it by night. Ma'jirra gives
the player two scrolls of Frenzy and shows them how a crew turned on itself is half beaten. The journal says the
player may fight the guards or set them on each other, and markers show the strongbox and the guard who carries
its key. The player reads a scroll among the guards and finishes the last one standing, or fights them on the
landing with blade and shield, or shoots from behind the timber stacks. Theft is seen by anyone in the village who
is awake, and a bounty with Soulrest's council follows a theft that is seen. In the strongbox are the loggers' pay
and a sailing order in Lucan's hand: the *Leyawiin Pride* will lie at Fort Shellmoth's sea-gate to load timber.
*Leads on:* Ma'jirra reads the order and says she wants that ship. First she needs her bosun back. Kesh-Ulee is
held in the stockade of Fort Silvermoth, and Gideon has asked for him. The journal marks the fort.

**4. The Stockade** (Fort Silvermoth, an Archwarden toll fort on the coast road). Captain Hareel Tullan holds
Kesh-Ulee in the stockade. Gideon has asked for him, and Hareel has not yet decided who pays better. Hareel stands
at the toll chain and speaks first. The player has two ways:

- *Pay.* Hareel lets Kesh-Ulee go for 400 gold. This is more than the Naxan pay chest held.
- *Break him out.* The stockade's key hangs on the sergeant in the yard. The player fights the garrison of seven,
  from the gate or from the reeds with a bow. The Archwardens put a bounty on the player and the garrisons of Fort
  Silvermoth and Fort Shellmoth attack them on sight until it is paid.

Either way Kesh-Ulee goes free. In the first state Hareel is alive and friendly. In the second he may be dead, and
the fort's gate is closed to the player. Quest 5 is the same in both states, since the *Leyawiin Pride* lies under
Fort Shellmoth's wall and the boarding is by night from the water. *Leads on:* Kesh-Ulee tells the player that the
*Pride*'s crew sleep aboard and keep two men on deck. Ma'jirra gives the order to board. The journal marks the ship.

**5. Boarding the Leyawiin Pride** (Fort Shellmoth, an Archwarden harbour fort near Soulrest, with the ship moored
at its sea-gate). The *Leyawiin Pride* lies moored at the fort's sea-gate. Ma'jirra gives the player two scrolls of
Mayhem and tells them what Mayhem does to a crew woken in the dark. Her crew are already in the water by the hull
when the player arrives. The player climbs aboard. The two sailors on watch are fought or shot. The crew come up from the hold
and are fought on the deck, or the player reads Mayhem among them as they come. The strongbox is in the master's
cabin aft. Lucan Ostorius stands in his cabin and does not fight. He knows the player from the inn. He gives up the
strongbox key if the player asks, or the player kills him for it. In the strongbox is the Company's silver for the
timber. *Leads on:* Ma'jirra makes the player a Boarder. One-Eye has heard who took the *Pride* and wants to see
them on the island. Ma'jirra gives the player two scrolls of Water Walking: no boat goes to Skeith Isle from the
mainland, and the strait is short from Arorha. The journal marks Arorha and Marque Landing.

This quest shuts the Company's door: *The Nibenese Company* fails at its end, and a player who belongs to the
Company is put out of it (section 6).

### Act II: the island

**6. Across the Water** (Arorha, a clay-mound village and sea door on Hatandros Bight; Marque Landing, the
pirates' stilt camp on Skeith Isle). Arorha's boatmen will not row to Skeith Isle, because Skeith crews take boats
that land there. The player reads a scroll of Water Walking at Arorha's quay and walks across the strait to the
island. A player without a scroll may swim, past the slaughterfish under Marque Landing's jetty. Bar-Neeus, the
bosun, stops the player on the jetty and speaks first. The landing's custom is that a newcomer fights the bosun.
The player chooses fists or blades. With blades the fight is to the first good wound, and with fists it is a brawl.
Either way Bar-Neeus yields when beaten. *Leads on:* One-Eye takes the player on as a hand of the island. She has
a job that both camps of the island have failed at: the *Stormwidow*'s strongbox. The journal marks the wreck.

**7. The Widow's Strongbox** (Wreck of the Stormwidow, a privateer galleon run aground on the island's south-west
beach; The Grey Tooth, the island's one crag). The *Stormwidow* lies on the beach with her hold full of sand. Four
scavengers from Hollow Tide Camp are stripping her and attack the player. Mudcrabs have dug into the sand of the
hold, and the strongbox lies under them in the stern. The player clears the scavengers with a bow from the dunes or
in close on the deck, and kills the mudcrabs to reach the box. Inside is a brass key. One-Eye's crews believe it
opens the cache under the cairn on the Grey Tooth. The player climbs the crag. Under the cairn is a small iron box
that the brass key opens. Inside is the *Stormwidow*'s letter of marque, sealed with the seal of the Nibenese
Company. *Leads on:* One-Eye reads the letter and tells the player the story of section 2: Gaius Cassena hired the
Free Marque to take Soulrest's ships, then sold the *Stormwidow* to the Archwardens. She says she will have
Gideon for it. First she needs Hollow Tide on her side, and she sends the player to Rajeel-Kosh. The journal marks
Hollow Tide Camp.

**8. The Hollow Tide Run** (Hollow Tide Camp, the smugglers' beach camp on Skeith Isle; Marshgate Watch, an
Archwarden road post south of Thorn). Rajeel-Kosh will join the war for a price. He wants a cargo of moon sugar
carried to his buyer at Ysan landing on the far side of Marshgate Watch. His lookouts are wary, and the journal
warns that they shoot strangers who come in without a word. The player gives One-Eye's word at the camp's edge and
is let in. Rajeel-Kosh gives the player the cargo and two scrolls of Water Walking. The watch searches goods on the
road. The player has three ways:

- *By the road, paying.* The watch sergeant stops the player and speaks first. A toll of 250 gold buys him blind.
- *By the road, fighting.* The player kills the watch of four. The Archwardens put a bounty on the player.
- *By the sea.* The player reads Water Walking at the shore below Kornakim and walks the coast past the watch.

At Ysan the buyer takes the cargo and pays. *Leads on:* Rajeel-Kosh's crew will fight for One-Eye when the war
begins. One-Eye tells the player the war needs a hold on Topal Bay, near Gideon. Ma'jirra has found one: the
ruined fort of Wrackmoth on the cape's north coast. The journal marks it.

**9. Wrackmoth** (Wrackmoth, a ruined Imperial fort on the cape's north coast). Six outlaws camp in the yard under a
captain in the gatehouse. Mudcrabs and crocodiles lie in the flooded gate. The journal says the player may come in
by the flooded gate or over the broken wall. The player fights in through the gate in heavy armour, or shoots the
yard from the wall top, or reads a scroll of Frenzy in the yard and takes what is left. The outlaw captain is the
last fight. *Leads on:* Ma'jirra's crew take the fort for the bay crew. The player is made Bosun and given a bed in
the gatehouse and the use of the Legion forge in the yard. One-Eye sends word by Ma'jirra that the war on Gideon needs gold, and
the Septim warship off Skeith Isle holds it. The journal marks Rainbreaker Keep.

### Act III: the war on Gideon

**10. The Akaviri Chest** (Rainbreaker Keep, the Free Marque's seat on Skeith Isle; Wreck of the Resolute Crest, a
Septim man-of-war sunk in deep water off the isle). Two of the island's divers have died trying to reach the
*Resolute Crest*'s Akaviri tribute chest. Ixil-Ma, a Seafalls diver, sits in the undercroft of Rainbreaker Keep. He
says he knows a way into the hull by the stern ports, away from the marines on the main deck. Selvo the
quartermaster will open his cell for the ransom price of 300 gold. The player sees the wet cells below and the
slaughterfish pen beside them. The player may pay and free Ixil-Ma. He tells the way and goes home to Seafalls. Or
the player may leave him and go in by the main deck. Selvo gives the player two scrolls of Swift Swim and one of
Waterbreathing for the long dive. Inside the hull there is air. By the main deck, six drowned marines and their
captain stand between the player and the chest. By the stern ports, only the captain does. The player fights them
with blade and shield or shoots them down the length of the gun deck. The chest holds Akaviri gold and an Akaviri
blade, *the Resolute*, enchanted with frost. *Leads on:* One-Eye takes the gold for the war and gives the player
the blade. She says the Company's gold comes from Old Goldseam, which the Company has opened again. The journal
marks the mine.

**11. The Company's Gold** (Old Goldseam, a gold mine of the old Imperial concession on Cape Percar). The tribes
burned the Imperial mines in 3E 427 (`soulrest.md:41-48`). The Company has opened Old Goldseam again with its own
miners and five sellswords. The overseer keeps the gold in the winch house. The journal says the player may fight
in by the mine mouth or swim in by the flooded lower shafts. By the mouth, the player fights the sellswords in the
galleries. By the shafts, the player needs Waterbreathing (a scroll, a potion or an Argonian's lungs) and comes up
behind the sellswords at the winch house. The miners do not fight and run. The player kills the overseer and takes
the gold from his chest. *Leads on:* Ma'jirra sends word that the Hollow Tide crew have quarrelled with One-Eye
over a strongbox on the Kornakim shoals. The journal marks the wreck of the *Brindle Gull*.

**12. The Brindle Gull** (Wreck of the Brindle Gull, a smuggler's sloop sunk on the Kornakim shoals). Rajeel-Kosh
says the *Brindle Gull* was his and her strongbox of Skeith coin is owed to him. One-Eye says her privateers sank
the sloop and the strongbox is a prize. Both want it raised before the war. The water is shallow but the hull is
crowded with weed, and the drowned crew and captain hold the deckhouse. The player fights them in the deckhouse.
The strongbox key is on the drowned captain. The player then chooses whom to give the strongbox to:

- *To One-Eye.* She gives the player a fifth of it, and they are made Mate. Rajeel-Kosh keeps his word on the
  war, and nothing more.
- *To Rajeel-Kosh.* He keeps it all. His crew land at Gideon's quay for the assault, and he buys from the player at
  better prices for the rest of the game. One-Eye makes the player Mate but gives no share.

*Leads on:* One-Eye names the night. Her crews will be at Gideon's quay when the player gets there. The journal
marks Gideon and the Company house.

**13. The Taking of Gideon** (Gideon, the western frontier city and the Company's seat). One-Eye's crews are
already at the quay when the player arrives, and the fight on Blackwood Way has begun. One-Eye gives the player
three scrolls of Mayhem and one of Frenzy. If the player gave the strongbox to Rajeel-Kosh, his crew fight at the
quay as well. The player must kill Gideon's guard-captain at the gate and reach the Company house. Gideon's guards
and the Company's sellswords hold Blackwood Way. The player fights through in heavy armour, holds the narrow
alleys with blade and shield, shoots from the roofs of the quay, or reads Mayhem among the guards in the plaza.
Inside the Company house, Opilia Cassena stands at the door of her strong room with her sellswords. She fights. When
she and the guard-captain are dead, the city is the Free Marque's (section 7).

If One-Eye dies in the fighting, the crews make the player captain in her place at the end, and the ending is the
same.

## 6. The war from both sides

The same events, people and places in both lines. A player can play only one side of each: the door of each line
shuts at its quest 5.

| Event | The Free Marque's side | The Company's side |
|---|---|---|
| Lucan Ostorius drinks at Olvani's ale-house | quest 1: the player brawls him | he is the master of the ship the player guards in quest 5 |
| the race for the *Grey Heron*'s salvage | quest 2: the player kills the Company's divers and takes the captain's chest | quest 2: the player kills the Free Marque's salvors and raises the iron |
| Captain Hareel Tullan and Kesh-Ulee at Fort Silvermoth | quest 4: the player pays for Kesh-Ulee or breaks him out | quest 3: the player gets the toll chain lifted, and may have Kesh-Ulee handed to Gideon |
| the night of the *Leyawiin Pride* at Fort Shellmoth | quest 5: the player boards and takes her strongbox. The Company's door shuts | quest 5: the player kills the boarding party. The Free Marque's door shuts |
| Hollow Tide Camp | quest 8: the player runs its cargo and wins its crew | quest 7: the player takes the camp down and kills or turns Rajeel-Kosh |
| Wrackmoth | quest 9: the bay crew's hold | quest 8: the Company's post on the cape |
| the *Resolute Crest*'s Akaviri chest | quest 10: the Free Marque's war chest | quest 10: the Company's claim |
| Old Goldseam | quest 11: the player raids the Company's gold | quest 9: the player opens the mine for the Company |
| the *Stormwidow*'s letter of marque | quest 7: found under the Grey Tooth | quest 14: found in the strong room of Rainbreaker Keep |
| the end | quest 13: Gideon taken, Opilia dead | quest 13 and 14: Skeith Isle taken, One-Eye dead |

The two endings cannot both happen in one game. The doors shut at quest 5 of each line, so a player reaches only one
line's Act II. Each ending also kills the other line's leader.

## 7. The ending: Gideon held

**What it gives.** The rank of Captain and the Marque Coat. The Company house becomes the player's stronghold: the
master's room as a bed, the strong room's chests to keep things in, and the house's hall. Gideon's guards are gone
from the city and the Free Marque's crews stand in their places, friendly to the player. Selvo keeps a stall on the
quay that sells scrolls of Waterbreathing, Water Walking, Swift Swim, Frenzy and Mayhem. Ma'jirra's fence buys
anything at the quay. Gideon's quay has a boat to Soulrest, Wrackmoth and Marque Landing.

**What it costs.** The Company's people are dead or gone. The Nibenese general store on Blackwood Way closes, and
the council merchant who trained Speech leaves the city. The Archwardens' garrisons at Fort Silvermoth, Fort
Shellmoth and Aphon Belvedere attack the player on sight. Gideon's civic council stays in the old Governor's Mansion
but will not speak to the player. Every Company player and trader in the province is gone, so the Company's line
can never be taken.

## 8. What it needs, and what it changes elsewhere

### What it asks of the world

- **Scrolls** of Waterbreathing, Water Walking, Swift Swim, Frenzy and Mayhem. Skyrim's own scrolls cover Frenzy and
  Mayhem where it has them. The rest are scroll records of ours, each casting the spell of that name from
  [spell-groups](../spell-groups.md) (Water Walking and Swift Swim are Magic Expanded's).
- **A moored ship** at Fort Shellmoth's sea-gate with a deck and a hold that can be entered, from the ships that
  Black Marsh & Valenwood and Skyrim hold. The fort's third source, Forth Isle, is built with dock and ship pieces.
- **The Free Marque's gear:** Sign of Cipactli's Argonian armour, Animated Armoury's rapier, pike, claws and
