# Bramman's Brethren: the plot

This page is the plot of the pirates' line, written on 2026-11-16 from the owner's words in [the factions
overhaul](../../../../docs/phases/nw-05c-factions-overhaul.md) and revised on 2026-11-17 to his
story explorer notes. Its rival is the Topal Bay Company of Gideon ([merchants](merchants.md)). The two plots are one
war seen from both sides: section 6 fixes the shared events once. Every quest is set in a place whose source place
has what it needs, and each sentence that leans on a part of a place, a named thing or a creature says in brackets
which source place gives it and how we know: what that source's buildings, interiors and placed pieces are, or which
named addition supplies it. Everything is inside the quest
ceiling ([quest-system](../../../../.claude/skills/quest-system/SKILL.md)), with the additions listed in section 8.
The line can be played at any point of the main quest: it uses nothing the main quest needs (section 8).

**Words.** *Bramman's Brethren*: the pirates of this line, named for Red Bramman, the pirate-king of Topal Bay whose
bandit kingdom by Blackrose the Imperial Navy broke in 1E 1033 by following his hidden mangrove river up from Soulrest
(`topics/history-timeline.md:31`, `soulrest.md:13-15`). Every pirate of the bay calls himself Bramman's heir
(`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:607`); *brethren* is the old sailors' word for the members of a pirate crew,
who share the prizes as brothers. *The Brethren* for short. *A letter of marque*: a written licence from a power
that lets a captain take ships that power names as its enemies. *The Xeech-Leel*: the party that rules the province,
"the People of the New Beginning". *The Archwardens*: the Xeech-Leel's wardens, whose garrisons man the old Imperial
forts, roads and tolls; the Xeech-Leel issue their letters of marque through them at Archon
(`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:517-522`). *The Company*: the Topal Bay Company, a Gideon house of Imperial
descent. *Lukiul*: Argonians who live by Imperial ways, as most of Gideon's do. *A hulk*: an old ship that no longer
sails, run aground and used as a house or a store. *Alten Corimont*: the free port on the great lake, whose captain
keeps the port neutral inside the town (`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:576-590`).

## 1. What the faction is

Bramman's Brethren are a brotherhood of pirates who take the Company's merchant ships in Topal Bay and smuggle what
they take past the Archwardens' tolls. A member boards ships, raises cargo from wrecks, fights the Company's hired
swords and carries what the Brethren sell to buyers who ask no questions.

**Where it sits.** Its seat is Bramman's Watch on Aphon Isle, an old Imperial view-house and tower on the island's
rise in Topal Bay, which One-Eye's crews took from the Xeech-Leel's customs watch in 4E 196. The watch withdrew to
Fort Shellmoth by Soulrest. From the Watch the Brethren see every ship that comes into the bay to Gideon. Its bay
crew works out of the mangrove landing at Soulrest, at the mouth of Bramman's hidden river, which runs inland to
Blackrose. Its outpost on the Archon coast is Marque Landing on Skeith Isle, a camp of hide tents and cypress
walkways on posts over the shallows, among old Imperial ruins and moored hulks, with the old sea-watch keep of
Rainbreaker Keep above it, which the Brethren took from the Archwardens' chartered crews in 4E 189. Canon
gives the province two pirate grounds: the Archon coast of chartered ex-privateers and the heirs of Red Bramman in
Topal Bay (`archon.md:43-47`, `soulrest.md:13-15`, `soulrest.md:66-69`; lore §5). The Brethren are the second, and
their outpost stands on the first. Canon says the pirates of Topal Bay "have never truly been eliminated"
(`topics/foreign-powers.md:111`) and that Black Marsh has no navy, so its coasts are unpoliced
(`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:264-266`). Their prizes go up Bramman's river to Alten Corimont, the free port
where Red Bramman, as its people tell it, first beached a hull. There they sell what they take and sign on new hands
under the captain of the port's peace.

The Brethren also carry people whom the Chainbreakers have freed out of the province by sea, from the causeway
of Bala Karag near Skeith Isle ([the Chainbreakers](chainbreakers-newworld.md)' 7, Out by Sea). The two are allies.

**Its skills.** Pure combat: One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Block, Light Armour and Heavy Armour. The Brethren
do not teach spells. They hand out scrolls, and a quest that hands out a scroll uses it: Waterbreathing, Water
Walking and Swift Swim to work the water, Tinur's Hoptoad to leap to a ship's rail, and Frenzy and Mayhem to sow
chaos among a crew.

**Its rival.** The Company, which built its trade on the Brethren's work a generation ago and then betrayed them to
the Archwardens (section 2). The Company's line ends with Aphon Isle taken. This line ends with Gideon taken.

**What joining gives.** Work from the first day, a share of every prize, the bay crew's fence at Soulrest, scrolls
at every rank, a bed and a forge at a fort the player takes, and at the end a city.

**History and name.** After the brawl that lets the player in, Ma'jirra answers the topic *Bramman's Brethren*:
"Red Bramman was a pirate-king. He ruled a hidden river from Topal Bay up to the Blackrose lakes, until the Emperor's
navy found its mouth by Soulrest and hanged him. Every crew on this bay calls itself his heir, and brethren is what
sailors call a crew that shares its prizes like brothers." Asked further, she says that the Brethren sailed under
letters of marque once, bought for them by a Gideon merchant, and that she will let the captain tell the rest.

### Fights at sea

Skyrim cannot sail a ship with people fighting on it, and a swimmer cannot strike or shoot. So the player fights every fight
of this line where a ship stands still and the ground is solid. The line uses three kinds:

- **A boarding at sea** (quests 5 and 9). The player speaks to a Brethren boatman at a landing and confirms they are
  ready. The screen fades and a message says that the boatman rows them out by night. When it clears, the player
  sits in a small boat beside a larger ship at anchor; the stage switched both on, far from the landing. The player
  climbs the hull with FreeClimb or leaps to the rail with a scroll of Tinur's Hoptoad, which the boatman gives. Then
  they go unseen to the objective, or fight the crew on the deck and below. To leave, they climb down and use the
  small boat, and the same fade sets them back on the landing.
- **A wreck with air inside** (quests 2, 10 and 11): the player dives with Waterbreathing and fights in the hull.
- **A camp, a beach or a fort on land** (quests 3, 4, 7, 12 and 14).

## 2. The truth under the story

**What the player is shown.** The Company's ships carry Cyrodilic goods across Topal Bay from Leyawiin, and gold and
timber out. The Brethren take what they can of them. The Company calls the pirates thieves. The pirates call the
Company worse, and at first the player does not know why.

**What is true.** In 4E 173, when the Blackwood road opened again, Gaius Cassena of Gideon founded the Topal Bay
Company. Gideon and Soulrest have quarrelled for centuries over who ships the bay's trade (`gideon.md:106`). Gaius
went to Archon and bought letters of marque from the Xeech-Leel's Archwardens for the captains of the Brethren. The
letters let the Brethren take any ship that ran the Xeech-Leel's customs, and Soulrest's ships ran them. Gaius paid the
Brethren to take Soulrest's ships, and his Company grew rich on the trade that Soulrest lost. In 4E 181, when the
Company no longer needed them, Gaius told the Archwardens where the Brethren's best ship, the *Stormwidow*, would
sail. In return the Archwardens let the Company's ships pass their tolls without paying for ten years. The
Archwardens sent their chartered privateers of the Archon coast after her. Captain Dravos Indrelo of the *Black
Kestrel* ran her onto the beach of Skeith Isle, and his crew killed hers. Before he died, the *Stormwidow*'s captain
hid her letter of marque in a cache under the cairn on the Grey Tooth. Gaius had written her course on its back.

The player finds the letter in quest 7. Its seal is the Company's seal. Gaius is dead. His daughter, Opilia Cassena,
now runs the Company and says the pirates are thieves and always were.

**What the Brethren hide.** In 4E 194, while the Brethren and the smugglers of Hollow Tide Camp quarrelled over the
Skeith coves, Brethren crews shut the crew of Hollow Tide's sloop, the *Brindle Gull*, in her deckhouse and sank her on
the Kornakim shoals. Hasha-Jeen, master of Marque Landing, told Hollow Tide that the Archwardens' privateers did it.
The player opens that deckhouse in quest 11.

**The extrapolation.** The Company, its founder and his letters are ours (**X**). They follow from the canon
quarrel of Gideon and Soulrest (`gideon.md:106`), the chartered pirates of the Archon coast (`archon.md:43-47`) and
the recorded view that a shipless power charters the pirates it cannot beat
(`extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md:517-522`).

## 4. Joining

**The rumours.** In Soulrest, Naxan, Acra-Tiin, Olvani, Seafalls and Arorha, fishers and boat crews talk of
the pirates: "There is a Khajiit in the ale-house at Olvani who buys drinks for anyone who looks handy. The
ones who take her up do not come back to the nets." The first time the player hears it, the small quest *Bramman's
Brethren* starts. Its journal says that a Khajiit recruiter drinks at the ale-house in Olvani, and its marker leads
to the ale-house door. Each time the player hears a further rumour, the journal gains a line (the Brethren's island,
its bay crew, what it pays) and nothing else changes.

**Being let in.** Quest 1 is the joining. Ma'jirra buys the player a drink and sets them a brawl with an Imperial
in the room. If the Imperial yields, she makes the player a Deckhand and sends them to her crew. *Bramman's
Brethren* completes. The stage that makes the player a Deckhand also makes peace between the player and the
Brethren's crews, a faction of ours. From then on the crews at Bramman's Watch, Kelp-Mouth Cave, Marque Landing and
Rainbreaker Keep, who attack strangers on sight, let the player walk among them.

The Hollow Tide crew, Rajeel-Kosh's smugglers at Hollow Tide Camp on Skeith Isle, is one crew of Bramman's Brethren.

**What shuts the door.** Joining the Topal Bay Company. The two lines tell the same events from the two sides from
their second quests on, so a player plays only one of them. When Nuxal-Tei signs the player on in Gideon,
*Bramman's Brethren* fails with the entry "I have taken the Company's silver. The Brethren will not take me on now."
Joining the Brethren shuts the Company's door in the same way.

## 5. The acts

The line is fourteen quests in three acts. Act I is the bay: small jobs out of Soulrest in places a new character
can survive (D2). Act II is the islands: the player crosses to Bramman's Watch on Aphon Isle and then to the outpost
on Skeith Isle, and learns what the war is about (D3). Act III is the war on Gideon (D4), which ends in the assault
on the Company's house.

Every quest is a fight of some kind, and the ways through are kinds of fight: in close with a blade and shield, at a
distance with a bow, in heavy armour through the front, or with a scroll that turns the enemy on each other. The
journal names the area to go to and says where the player may fight from cover or from range; the marker leads to
that area, never to the exact chest or person.

The Brethren's ranks come with the line, each with a fixed reward:

| Rank | Given after | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Deckhand | quest 1 | a cutlass (Animated Armoury, under our name "bay-runner's") and 50 gold |
| Boarder | quest 5 | a boarder's coat (Pirate Armor's coat, under our name) and 100 gold. Ma'jirra's crew sell scrolls of Frenzy from now on |
| Bosun | quest 9 | a set of light Argonian armour (Sign of Cipactli) or a set of steel plate (Skyrim's), as the player chooses |
| Mate | quest 11 | an enchanted pike that drains stamina or an enchanted halberd that strikes with shock (Animated Armoury, under our names). Selvo sells scrolls of Mayhem from now on |
| Captain | quest 14 | Bramman's Coat, the captain's set of Pirate Armor under our name, with an enchantment of our own (Fortify One-Handed, Fortify Two-Handed and Waterbreathing together), and the Company house in Gideon as the player's stronghold |

### Act I: the bay

**1. A Drink and a Brawl** (Olvani, a fishing village on the estuary west of Gideon, with an ale-house). Ma'jirra
drinks in Olvani's ale-house, where the Company's sailors drink ashore (checked: Olvani's source town is Morthal, from Skyrim,
which has an inn among its ten interiors, and that inn serves as the ale-house). She speaks first, buys the player a cup of Surilie wine (Skyrim's Surilie
Brothers wine) and asks what they can do with their fists. An Imperial sailor drinks at the next table: Gallus
Ostorius, master of the Company ship *Leyawiin Pride*. Ma'jirra tells the player to pick a fight with him. The player
says something to Gallus that angers him, and he brawls. The fight is unarmed, as Skyrim's tavern brawls are, and it
is easy: Gallus is drunk, unarmoured and placed at a low fixed level, so a new character of any build can beat him
with fists. When Gallus is beaten he yields, and the player accepts. If Gallus beats the player instead, the player
yields; Ma'jirra laughs and points to a second Company sailor at the bar, and the player may try again with him, as
many times as they like. A player who draws a weapon or kills either man has failed the test, and Ma'jirra says she
has no use for a killer in a tavern.
*Leads on:* Ma'jirra makes the player a Deckhand. She tells them the Brethren's seat is Bramman's Watch on Aphon
Isle, and that nobody goes to the Watch unproved. She has a first job here and now (quest 2).

**2. The Grey Heron** (The Grey Heron, a wreck on the floor of Topal Bay; Olvani, a fishing village on the
estuary west of Gideon). The *Grey Heron* was a Leyawiin merchantman that struck the bar off Gideon's estuary. Her
hold still has iron ingots in it and her stern cabin the captain's chest. The Company has bought the salvage from
Gideon's harbour officer and has sent two divers. Still in the ale-house, Ma'jirra gives the player three scrolls of
Waterbreathing and tells them to get there first. The player wades out from Olvani's jetty (checked: the jetty is built of the dock pieces of Seekhat Yol, a village of
King of the Murkmire, as a named addition) and dives. Air has stayed inside the hull (checked: the wreck's second
source is Icerunner, a ship from Skyrim whose one interior of ship timbers, crates and barrels serves as the hold and
the stern cabin, and the Winter Sparrow from Wyrmstooth gives the broken hull outside). The Company's two divers are
already inside and attack. The player fights them hand to hand in the narrow hold, or shoots from among the crates
at the stern end while they come aft. The chest's key is on the older diver. Inside are the captain's silver and a cask of Cyrodilic
wine (Skyrim's wine, under our name). *Leads on:* the journal sends the player to Ma'jirra's crew at the mangrove
landing in Soulrest, and its marker leads there. The player walks the short way to Gideon and takes the boat to
Soulrest from Gideon's quay. At the landing Ma'jirra keeps the wine and gives the player half the silver, 120 gold.
She has a second job: the Company pays its loggers at Naxan from a chest on the log landing. The journal marks
Naxan, a short walk up the coast from Soulrest.

**3. The Naxan Landing** (Naxan, a fishing and timber village on the cape's north coast). The Company pays its
loggers at Naxan from a strongbox on the log landing (checked: Naxan's main source is a small Nordic trading post, so the village is drawn from Seekhat Yol in King of the
Murkmire, which has Argonian stilt houses and dock pieces; the log landing is a named addition built of those dock
pieces, and the strongbox is one placed container). The Lampbearers' 8, The
Burnt Cut, starts from the same village's log-haulers. Three Company sellswords guard the strongbox by night. Ma'jirra
gives the player two scrolls of Frenzy and shows them how a crew turned on itself is half beaten. The journal says
that the player may fight the guards or set them on each other, and that one of them carries the key; its marker
leads to the log landing. The player reads a scroll among the guards and finishes the last one standing, or fights
them on the landing with blade and shield, or shoots from behind the timber stacks. Villagers who are awake see a
theft, and Soulrest's council sets a bounty on a seen theft. In the strongbox are the loggers' pay and a sailing order
in Gallus's hand: the *Leyawiin Pride* will lie at anchor off Fort Shellmoth to load timber. *Leads on:* Ma'jirra
gives the player 100 gold of the pay, reads the order and says she wants that ship. First she needs her bosun back.
Kesh-Ulee is locked in the stockade of Fort Silvermoth, and Gideon has asked for him. The journal marks the fort. It
stands on the coast road a short walk east of Gideon (the boat from Soulrest to Gideon, then the road).

**4. The Stockade** (Fort Silvermoth, an Archwarden toll fort on the coast road). Captain Hareel Tullan has
Kesh-Ulee in the stockade (checked: the fort's source is Cracked Tusk, an Imperial fort from Skyrim with two keep
interiors and wooden stockade walls in its yard; the toll chain is a named addition, and the stockade cell is one
locked door in the yard). Gideon has asked for him, and Hareel has not yet decided
who pays better. This is the line's first choice, and the player meets it so:

1. The journal sends the player to the fort and its marker leads to the toll chain on the road.
2. Hareel stands at the toll chain and speaks first. He says Gideon will pay for the pirate, and asks what the
   Brethren offer.
3. The player answers with one of two lines:
   - *Pay.* "Four hundred gold for Kesh-Ulee." The line is open only to a player who carries 400 gold, which is more
     than the Naxan pay held, so the player has saved for it. Hareel takes the gold and sends a warden to open the
     stockade. Kesh-Ulee walks out and stands at the gate.
   - *Refuse.* "The Brethren do not buy their own." Hareel says then Gideon will have him, and the talk ends. The
     journal says that the stockade's key hangs on the sergeant in the yard. The player fights the garrison of
     seven, from the gate or from the reeds with a bow, takes the key from the sergeant's body and opens the
     stockade. The Archwardens set a bounty on the player, and the garrisons of Fort Silvermoth and Fort Shellmoth
     attack them on sight until it is paid.
4. A player who has not got the gold may leave, gather it and come back; the pay line waits.

Either way Kesh-Ulee goes free. In the first state Hareel is alive and friendly. In the second he may be dead, and
the fort's gate is closed to the player. Quest 5 is the same in both states, since the *Leyawiin Pride* lies at
anchor out in the bay and the boarding is by night from the water. *Leads on:* Kesh-Ulee tells the player that the
*Pride*'s crew sleep aboard and leave two men on deck. Ma'jirra gives the order to board. The journal sends the
player to her boatman at the mangrove landing.

**5. Boarding the Leyawiin Pride** (Fort Shellmoth, an Archwarden harbour fort near Soulrest, with the *Leyawiin
Pride* at anchor off its sea wall). This is the line's first boarding at sea (section 1). Ma'jirra gives the player
two scrolls of Mayhem and two of Tinur's Hoptoad and tells them what Mayhem does to a crew woken in the dark. Her
boatman at the mangrove landing asks if the player is ready, and the player says yes. The screen fades. The player
sits in his small boat under the *Pride*'s side (checked: the ship is the Red Wave, a merchantman from Skyrim's Solitude docks whose one
interior is a cabin and hold; its hull is set at anchor off the fort's sea wall as a named addition, and the small
boat is a Skyrim rowing boat). The player climbs the hull with FreeClimb or leaps to the rail
with Tinur's Hoptoad. The two sailors on watch stand at the bow, away from the deck door. The player kills them, shoots
them, or slips past them and in at the deck door unseen. Below, the crew sleep in the hold. They wake and fight if
they see the player, and the player fights them in the hold or reads Mayhem among them. The strongbox is in the
master's cabin aft. Gallus Ostorius stands in his cabin and does not fight. He knows the player from the inn. He
gives up the strongbox key if the player asks, or the player kills him for it; killing him gives nothing more, and
Ma'jirra pays 50 gold less for a dead master, who would have been worth a ransom note to Gideon. In the strongbox is
the Company's silver for the timber. The player climbs down and uses the small boat, and the fade sets them back on
the landing. *Leads on:* Ma'jirra makes the player a Boarder. One-Eye has heard who took the *Pride* and wants to see
them at Bramman's Watch. The Brethren's boat to Aphon Isle leaves from Olvani's jetty. The journal marks Olvani's
jetty and Bramman's Watch.

### Act II: the islands

**6. Bramman's Watch** (Olvani, a fishing village on the estuary west of Gideon; Bramman's Watch, the Brethren's
seat on Aphon Isle). The Brethren's boatman at Olvani's jetty takes the player across the strait (a stop of the
travel network, fare 20 gold). Bramman's Watch is a tower on the water and a stair of rough stone up to the old
view-house on the rise (checked: the Watch's main source, Waylayer's Watch from Wyrmstooth, is an Imperial tower
on the water with camp pieces and fifteen people and no interior; the view-house and its stair are named additions,
and its one room is a hut interior from Seekhat Yol in King of the Murkmire, which serves as One-Eye's house). Bar-Neeus, the bosun, stops the player on the landing and speaks first. The Watch's custom is
that a newcomer fights the bosun. The player chooses fists or blades. With blades the fight is to the first good
wound, and with fists it is a brawl. Either way Bar-Neeus yields when beaten. If he beats the player, the player
yields and may ask again; the custom allows it. *Leads on:* One-Eye, in the view-house, takes the player on as a hand
of the Watch and tells them of the outpost on Skeith Isle, on the far side of the province. Its master, Hasha-Jeen,
has a job that his crews have failed at: the *Stormwidow*'s strongbox. One-Eye gives the player six bottles of the
*Pride*'s brandy (Skyrim's Cyrodilic brandy) to sell on the way, and two scrolls of Water Walking. The journal marks
Alten Corimont and Marque Landing.

**7. The Widow's Strongbox** (Alten Corimont, the free port on the great lake; Marque Landing, the Brethren's camp
of hide tents and cypress walkways on Skeith Isle; Wreck of the Stormwidow, a privateer galleon on the island's south-west beach; The Grey Tooth,
the island's one crag). The way east is the Brethren's own: up Bramman's river by boat from Soulrest to Blackrose,
on to Truowa and across the great lake to Alten Corimont (three boat stops of the network). At Alten Corimont the
journal sends the player to the beached ship's hold, where the Khajiit fence buys the brandy for 150 gold (checked:
the town's source, Murkmire in Black Marsh & Valenwood, is a town on the water with a ship among its interiors, and
that interior serves as the beached ship's hold and tavern; the fence is placed in it). Inside the port nobody draws a blade, by the captain of the port's rule, and two Company men
drinking in the tavern only watch the player go. From Alten Corimont the player walks east to Arorha on Hatandros
Bight, reads Water Walking at its quay (checked: Arorha's source, Keeba Hollow in King of the Murkmire, is an Argonian
village by water with a few dock pieces, which serve as the quay) and walks across the strait to Skeith Isle. A player
without a scroll swims, past the slaughterfish under Marque Landing's jetty (checked: Marque Landing's main source,
Bonestrewn Grove in King of the Murkmire, is a camp of hide tents and cypress scaffold walkways on posts over the
water among old Imperial ruins, with no stilt house and no interior, so the jetty and the moored hulks are named
additions of Skyrim's ship pieces, and the slaughterfish are creatures the camp is given). Hasha-Jeen meets the player on the jetty and speaks first. He
tells them the story of the coast: the chartered crews who used to live here, and the Brethren who took the landing
and the keep from them in 4E 189. Then he gives the job. The *Stormwidow* lies on the beach. Her captain's strongbox
is in her stern cabin, and the crews believe the brass key inside it opens the cache under the cairn on the Grey
Tooth.

The journal sends the player to the wreck and its marker leads to the stern deck. Four scavengers from Hollow Tide
Camp are stripping her deck and attack. The player clears them with a bow from the dunes or fights them in close on
the deck. The stern cabin has its own door on the stern deck (checked: the wreck's main source, Ink Lagoon from Journey to Baan
Malur, is only a ruined hull, so the galleon's upper hull is added; the sand-filled hold is the one interior of
Icerunner from Skyrim, and the stern cabin is a small added interior of Skyrim's ship pieces behind its own door, which
the quest uses while never sending the player into the hold, which the main quest uses). Mudcrabs, which are Skyrim's,
have dug into the sand on the cabin floor, and the
strongbox lies under them. The player kills the mudcrabs and opens the box. Inside is a brass key (a Skyrim key
under our name). The journal then sends the player up the Grey Tooth by the goat-path on its south face, and its
marker leads to the summit (checked: the crag's main source, Mount Moesring from Dragonborn, is a bare crag with no
buildings, so the goat-path and the cairn with its cache are named additions). A player who meets anyone camped at the path's foot may climb another face with FreeClimb; the

*Leads on:* the player reads the letter. Hasha-Jeen reads it after them and tells the player the story of section 2:

**8. The Hollow Tide Run** (Hollow Tide Camp, the smugglers' beach camp on Skeith Isle; Marshgate Watch, an

One-Eye's word at the camp's edge; that line makes peace with the smugglers' faction, and the player walks in.
Rajeel-Kosh gives the player the cargo and two scrolls of Water Walking. The watch searches goods on the road. The
journal names the three ways, and the player chooses as they come:

- *By the road, paying.* The warden at the gate stops the player and speaks first (checked: no source of the post has a

- *By the road, fighting.* The player kills the watch of four. The Archwardens set a bounty on the player, which
  only Marshgate Watch and Fort Thornmoth enforce.
- *By the sea.* The player reads Water Walking at the shore below Kornakim and walks the coast past the watch.

At Ysan the buyer on the jetty takes the cargo and pays the player 200 gold (checked: Ysan's main source is a small
Nordic hamlet, so the landing is drawn from Ree-An-Wo in King of the Murkmire, an Argonian hamlet by water, and the

**9. The Black Kestrel** (Marque Landing, the Brethren's camp of hide tents and cypress walkways on Skeith Isle; the *Black Kestrel* at anchor off
the island's north shore). This is the line's second boarding at sea, and a harder one. The *Black Kestrel* is the
chartered privateer of Captain Dravos Indrelo, who ran the *Stormwidow* onto the beach. The Archwardens pay him to lie
off Skeith Isle and take any Brethren hull that leaves the landing. In his cabin is the purse they paid him for the
*Stormwidow*, and it is still the Brethren's grudge. Hasha-Jeen gives the player two scrolls of Tinur's Hoptoad and
one of Mayhem. His boatman at the jetty asks if the player is ready, and the player says yes. The screen fades. The player sits in
the small boat under the *Kestrel*'s stern (checked: the ship is a sea-going ship of Black Marsh & Valenwood, whose one interior,
a cabin and hold, is the ship interior of its town of Murkwood; its hull is set at anchor off the north shore as a
named addition, and the small boat is a Skyrim rowing boat). Eight chartered privateers crew her: four on the deck, three in the hold, and Dravos in his

them in the dark to the cabin. The chest's key is on Dravos. The player kills him for it or lifts it from his belt

### Act III: the war on Gideon

**11. The Brindle Gull** (Wreck of the Brindle Gull, a smuggler's sloop sunk on the Kornakim shoals). Rajeel-Kosh says

the crew were shut in before she sank, and only the Brethren knew where she lay. The journal says so. The player then
chooses whom to give the strongbox to, at Marque Landing or at Hollow Tide Camp:

- *To Hasha-Jeen.* He gives the player a fifth of it, 150 gold, and makes them Mate in One-Eye's name. Rajeel-Kosh
  keeps his word on the war, and nothing more.
- *To Rajeel-Kosh.* He keeps it all. His crew land at Gideon's quay for the assault, and he buys from the player at
  better prices for the rest of the game. Hasha-Jeen makes the player Mate but gives no share.

*Leads on:* Hasha-Jeen says One-Eye's word has come: the war needs a base on the cape near Gideon, and Ma'jirra has
found one, the ruined fort of Wrackmoth. The way back west is by boat from Arorha to Archon, Lilmoth and Soulrest.
The journal marks Wrackmoth, a short walk north of Soulrest.

**12. Wrackmoth** (Wrackmoth, a ruined Imperial fort on the cape's north coast). Six outlaws camp in the yard under a
captain in the gatehouse. Mudcrabs and crocodiles (Black Marsh & Valenwood's) lie in the flooded gate (checked: the

**13. The Company's Gold** (Old Goldseam, a gold mine of the old Imperial concession on Cape Percar). The tribes

**14. The Taking of Gideon** (Gideon, the western frontier city and the Company's seat). One-Eye's crews are already

## 7. The ending: Gideon taken

**What it gives.** The rank of Captain and Bramman's Coat. The Company house becomes the player's stronghold: the
master's room as a bed, the strong room's chests to keep things in, and the house's hall. Gideon's guards are gone
from the city and the Brethren's crews stand in their places, friendly to the player. Selvo runs a stall on the
quay that sells scrolls of Waterbreathing, Water Walking, Swift Swim, Tinur's Hoptoad, Frenzy and Mayhem. Ma'jirra's
fence buys anything at the quay. Gideon's quay has a boat to Soulrest and to Olvani for the Watch.

**What it costs.** The Company's people are dead or gone. The Company's general store on Blackwood Way closes, and
the council merchant who trained Speech leaves the city. The Archwardens' garrisons at Fort Silvermoth and Fort
Shellmoth attack the player on sight. Gideon's civic council stays in the old Governor's Mansion but will not speak to
the player. The market's relic dealer, the rootworm's tree-minder and everyone else the main quest needs in Gideon
stay as they were. Every Company player and trader in the province is gone, so the Company's line can never be taken.
