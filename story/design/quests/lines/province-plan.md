# The province plan: the nine lines

How the main quest and the eight faction lines share one province. Each line's plot is its own page; the rules that
keep them true to each other are in [the consistency ledger](consistency.md), and the people are in the
[cast roster](../36-cast-roster.md). House Ulvarys, which cannot be joined, has its own page
([owing-house](owing-house.md)).

## 1. The lines

**The main quest** ([main-plot](main-plot.md)). The Eye and the Root. A stranger freed from an Owing barge at Ashgate
Landing is taken into the Toh-Reel-Ka, the secret warriors of the ruling Xeech-Leel, and hunts the lost Eye of
Argonia before the Unbound, a cult that drains Hist sap, can reach it. The handler sits in a quiet house in Helstrom,
and the order's dreamers are in the cellar of Fort Chalkmoth. It opens in D1.

**The Lampbearers** ([lamps](lamps.md)). Soulrest's investigators, seated in the Lamp-Hall with a branch on Truowa's
quay. A player signs on with Marcella Dovi and solves cases by evidence. Rivals: the Eelskins and the Shadowscales.

**The Eelskins** ([thieves](thieves.md)). The thieves of the south keep their hall in the drowned cellars under Pusbottom in Lilmoth,
with an outpost on a hulk at Blackrose. A first theft brings a visitor in the night. Their great heist is the
Xeech-Leel's strongroom in Helstrom's council house. Rivals: the Lampbearers and the Shadowscales.

**The Shadowscales** ([shadowscales](shadowscales.md)). The secret Argonian assassins, whose door to the world is
Beem-Xal's house beside the sealed school in Archon, with a branch at Black-Tongue Brews in Lilmoth and their trial
ground at Ra'tal Den. A satchel under Xi-Inou's landing is the test. Rivals: the Lampbearers and the Eelskins.

**The Sisei-Xul** ([sisei-xul](sisei-xul.md)). The order of marsh medicine and the rites of the dead, with its mother
hall in Helstrom's tree-minders' hall and branches at Aroxhu and Tsonac. Arorha's tree-minder brings the player in
with a healing potion. Enemy: House Cardruhn.

**House Cardruhn** ([house-cardruhn](house-cardruhn.md)). Dunmer traders of Thorn whose masters are necromancers:
a stall in Thorn's market, a hall under Ovezza and the master's seat at Kaochalit. Any conjuration cast before Ervyn
lets the player in. Enemy: the Sisei-Xul.

**Bramman's Brethren** ([pirates](pirates.md)). Pirates seated at Bramman's Watch on Aphon Isle, with a bay crew at
Soulrest's mangrove landing and an outpost at Marque Landing and Rainbreaker Keep on Skeith Isle. A tavern brawl at
Olvani is the test. Rival: the Topal Bay Company.

**The Topal Bay Company** ([merchants](merchants.md)). A Gideon house of Imperial descent on Blackwood Way, founded in
4E 173, which ships into Topal Bay. Nuxal-Tei signs anyone on. Rival: Bramman's Brethren.

**The Chainbreakers** ([chainbreakers-newworld](chainbreakers-newworld.md)). A band that frees people held under long
Owings, seated in the Well-Room under Stormhold with a second house in Thorn and safe houses across the province; the
Twin Lamps' more militant heirs. Enemy: House Ulvarys, whose counting-house is in Thorn and whose estate is Julo.

Gideon (the Topal Bay Company), Soulrest (the Lampbearers, the Brethren's bay crew), Lilmoth (the Eelskins, the
Shadowscales' branch), Archon (the Shadowscales) and Blackrose (the Eelskins' outpost).

## 3. Where each door shuts

| Line | Joining | Its door shuts when |
|---|---|---|
| The Lampbearers | Marcella signs the player on in the Lamp-Hall | the player takes the Eelskins' hall key (the Eelskins' 4, The Lampbearer's Man) or the brass (the Shadowscales' 4, The Hatchling Seller) |
| The Eelskins | a visitor after the first theft | the player takes the rank of Flame (the Lampbearers' 4, The Walled Cellar) or the brass |
| The Shadowscales | the satchel and writ at Xi-Inou | the player takes the rank of Flame or the Eelskins' hall key |
| The Sisei-Xul | a healing potion for Arorha's tree-minder | the player finishes House Cardruhn's 4, The Pulled Stakes |
| House Cardruhn | any conjuration cast before Ervyn in Thorn | the player finishes the Sisei-Xul's 3, The Walled Gallery |
| Bramman's Brethren | the brawl at Olvani | the player joins the Topal Bay Company |
| The Topal Bay Company | Nuxal-Tei signs the player on in Gideon | the player joins Bramman's Brethren |
| The Chainbreakers | Jeen-Wei's roll at Saltmeadow Farm | the player sells that roll to House Ulvarys' factor |

The main quest's door never shuts, and no faction's door turns on a step of the main quest.

## 4. The order a player is likely to meet them

The main quest's first act runs as one road through the settled north, from Ashgate Landing by Reed-Sill Weir to
Jal-Veesa, Teel-Fen Weir and Karsh-Naal Camp, and then by rootworm to Helstrom. From the first hour the player hears
Owing workers in the fields, and their stories lead to the Chainbreakers. In Helstrom the tree-minders' hall is the
Sisei-Xul's, and the council house is the Xeech-Leel's.

The fifth quest sends the player to Gideon by rootworm. There the Topal Bay Company hires on Blackwood Way, and the
Brethren's recruiter drinks at Olvani, a short walk west. The hunt then goes by boat round the coast: Thorn and
Hatandros Bight, where House Cardruhn's stall and the kilns of Arorha are; Skeith Isle; Toklea in the Tulmek Washes
and the country of Blackrose; and at last the drowned spires off Lilmoth. In these towns the player hears of the
Lampbearers of Soulrest, the thieves who swim among Lilmoth's eels and the brass medallions of the Shadowscales. Every
faction can be joined early and played at any point of the main quest.
