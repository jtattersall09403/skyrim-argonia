# The province plan: the nine lines

This page shows how the nine story lines share one province. Each line's plot is its own page, and the rulings that
keep the plots true to each other are in [the consistency ledger](consistency.md). The people are in the
[cast roster](../36-cast-roster.md).

## 1. The lines

**The main quest** ([main-plot](main-plot.md)). The Eye and the Root: a stranger freed from an Owing barge is taken
into the Reed, a secret order that watches the Hist, and hunts the lost Eye of Argonia across the province before the
Unbound, a cult that drains sap, can reach it. The Reed's handler sits in a quiet house in Helstrom, and its cellar of
dreamers is under Fort Chalkmoth. Its rival is the Unbound of Karsh-Naal Camp. The main quest opens at Sedge-Kal Camp
in the Valley of Storms, and its last choice at the Root sets the player against either the Reed or the Unbound.

**The Lamps** ([lamps](lamps.md)). Investigators paid by Soulrest's three powers to find out who did a crime and
prove it. They sit in the Lamp-Hall in Soulrest, with a branch on the quay of Truowa. Their rivals are the Eelskins and
the Shadowscales. Their door shuts on a player who reaches the end of quest 4 of either rival's line. Taking the rank
of Flame at the end of their own quest 4 shuts the other two.

**The Eelskins** ([thieves](thieves.md)). The thieves' guild of the south. New members prove themselves at a hulk
moored at the Blackrose boardwalks, and the guild's hall is in the drowned cellars under Pusbottom in Lilmoth. Their
rivals are the Lamps and the Shadowscales. A player's first theft brings a visitor while they sleep. Their door shuts
at the end of quest 4 of either rival's line, and the key to the hall at the end of their own quest 4 shuts the other
two.

**The Shadowscales** ([shadowscales](shadowscales.md)). The Argonian assassins, alive in secret though the world
thinks them dead. Their seat is the sealed school at Archon, entered through the cellar of the old ku-vastei's house.
Their branch is Black-Tongue Brews in Lilmoth, and their trial ground is Ra'tal Den. Their rivals are the Lamps and
the Eelskins. A drowned man's writ under the landing at Xi-Inou is their test. Their door shuts at the end of quest 4
of either rival's line, and the brass at the end of their own quest 4 shuts the other two.

**The Sisei-Xul** ([sisei-xul](sisei-xul.md)). The Argonian order of marsh medicine and the rites of the dead. Its
mother hall is the tree-minders' hall in Helstrom, with branches at Aroxhu and Tsonac. Its enemy is the Rothalen
house. A player joins by brewing a healing potion for the burnt potters of Arorha. Its door shuts when the player
finishes the Rothalens' quest 4, The Pulled Stakes.

**The Rothalen house** ([rothalen-house](rothalen-house.md)). A Dunmer trading house of Thorn whose masters are
necromancers who sell the bound Argonian dead to Telvanni buyers. Its front is a stall in Thorn's market, its hall is
the barred cellar under Ovezza, and its master sits under the well at Kaochalit. Its enemy is the Sisei-Xul. A player
joins by casting a conjuration in front of its factor. Its door shuts when the player finishes the Sisei-Xul's quest
3, The Walled Gallery.

**Bramman's Brethren** ([pirates](pirates.md)). Pirates who take Imperial merchant ships and smuggle past the
Archwardens' tolls. Their seat is Rainbreaker Keep on Skeith Isle, with the stilt camp of Marque Landing below it, and
their bay crew works Topal Bay from Soulrest's mangrove landing. Their rival is the Nibenese Company. A player joins
by winning a brawl in the ale-house at Olvani. Their door shuts at the end of the Company's quest 5, and their own
quest 5 shuts the Company's.

**The Nibenese Company** ([merchants](merchants.md)). Imperial merchants of Gideon who ship goods into the province and
gold, timber and iron out of it. Their house stands on Blackwood Way in Gideon, and over the line they take Wrackmoth
and, at the end, Skeith Isle. Their rival is the Brethren. A player signs on at the Company house. Their door shuts
at the end of the Brethren's quest 5, and their own quest 5 shuts the Brethren's.

**The Chainbreakers** ([chainbreakers-newworld](chainbreakers-newworld.md)). An outlawed band that frees people
from Owings that have grown into slavery. Their seat is the Well-Room in the tunnels under Stormhold, and their second
house is in Thorn, with safe houses across the province. Their enemy is the Ulvarys house ([owing-house](owing-house.md)),
which cannot be joined. Their allies are the Brethren. Selling the first freed man's roll to the house's factor is
the only thing that shuts their door.

Every great city holds a seat or a branch: Helstrom (the Sisei-Xul, the Reed's handler), Stormhold (the
Chainbreakers), Thorn (the Rothalen house, the Ulvarys house, the Chainbreakers' second house), Gideon (the Nibenese
Company), Soulrest (the Lamps, the Brethren's bay crew), Lilmoth (the Eelskins, the Shadowscales' branch), Archon
(the Shadowscales) and Blackrose (the Eelskins' outpost).

## 3. What rivals share

**The night at Silt-Orrel** joins the Lamps, the Eelskins and the Shadowscales. The Eldest of the Shadowscales kills
the first voice of Lilmoth's merchant council, an Eelskin scout takes the Eldest's medallion from the floor, and the
Lamps are called to the house. The Lamps' quests 11 to 14, the Eelskins' 9 to 12 and the Shadowscales' 9 to 13 tell
it from three sides, and each line ends with the player siding with one rival against the third.

**The war of the stakes** joins the Sisei-Xul and the Rothalen house. Both lines walk the same events from opposite
sides: the salt dead of Coldbrine Mine, Tazhul's stakes and the store under Joxhi, the welkynd stone under Tselan, the
log of the *Silt Bride*, the urn of Keel-Vaxa, the barred cellar at Ovezza, the raid on Ixu-Maan, the *Saltrice Queen*
and the hall under the well at Kaochalit. Each page holds the table of who does what.

**The war on the bay** joins the Brethren and the Nibenese Company: the brawl at Olvani, the *Grey Heron*'s
salvage, Captain Hareel's toll chain at Fort Silvermoth, the night of the *Leyawiin Pride*, Hollow Tide Camp,
Wrackmoth, the *Resolute Crest*'s Akaviri chest, Old Goldseam and the *Stormwidow*'s letter of marque. The pirates'
page, section 6, holds the table. One line ends with Gideon taken and the other with Skeith Isle taken.

**The Owing** joins the Chainbreakers and the Ulvarys house. The house has no line, so the Chainbreakers' fifteen
quests are the whole of it, struck at the house's farms, mines, forts, barge and estate. The Brethren carry the
freed out by sea from Bala Karag, and the Rothalens are the Ulvarys' kin, though the two houses do not speak.

## 5. The order a player is likely to meet them

The main quest's first act is played in the settled north, from Sedge-Kal Camp to Jal-Veesa, Vel-Sapha Stand and
Helstrom. From the first hour the player hears Owing workers in the fields, and their stories lead to the
Chainbreakers. In Helstrom the tree-minders' hall is the Sisei-Xul's.

The main quest's fifth quest sends the player to Gideon. There the Nibenese Company is hiring on Blackwood Way, and the
Brethren's recruiter drinks at Olvani, a short walk west. Its seventh quest reaches Hatandros Bight, the bay of
Thorn, where the Rothalens' stall and the kilns of Arorha are.

The hunt then crosses the south: Toklea in the Tulmek Washes, the country of Blackrose, and at last the drowned
spires off Lilmoth. In these towns the player hears of the Lamps of Soulrest, the thieves who swim among Lilmoth's eels and
the brass medallions of the Shadowscales. The three rivals are met last, and they are the lines most likely to be
played after the main quest's second act.
