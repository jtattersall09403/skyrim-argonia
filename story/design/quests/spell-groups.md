# Spells by use

The spells a faction's quests can teach and ask for, sorted by what they are used for. The groups are the owner's
(2026-11-16). Each group lists Skyrim's own spells first, then the spells that Magic Expanded adds (our mod list,
row 73, read from its plugin). A faction's plot picks its spells from here.

A spell not on this page is not in the game. Three gaps are listed at the end.

## Combat magic

| School | Skyrim's own | Added by Magic Expanded |
|---|---|---|
| Destruction | every flame, frost and shock spell; the runes; the cloaks | touch spells (Fire Bite, Scorching Blow, Cold Touch, Numbing Blow, Shock, Stormhand); Entropic Bolt and Withering Bolt (plain magic damage); poison (Viperbolt, Poison Spray, Poisonous Touch, Toxic Cloud, Poison Cloak); winds that throw (Twister, Whirlwind, Cyclone; Slight Force, Force, Powerful Force); storms over an area (Stormcell, Marsh Squall, Conflagration); Drain and Absorb of health, magicka and stamina; Corrode Armor, Disintegrate Armor, Disintegrate Weapon; Weakness to fire, frost, shock, poison and magic; Burden; Disarm; Silence; Paralysis Rune |
| Alteration | the flesh spells to Ebonyflesh, Paralyze, Mass Paralysis, Ash Shell | Flame, Frost and Shock Shield (they hurt whoever strikes the caster); Reflect Damage; Telekinetic Grip |
| Conjuration | every atronach, the bound weapons | Bound Mace, War Axe, Warhammer and Greatsword; a full set of Bound Armor |
| Restoration | the healing spells, the wards, Turn Undead, Circle of Protection, Guardian Circle, Sun Fire | Resist fire, frost, shock, poison and magic; Fortify Health, Magicka and Stamina; Spelldrinker (a struck spell becomes magicka); Dispel |

## Gaps

- **Chameleon.** No spell in the game gives it. It exists only as an enchantment, so the thieves' ring of
  chameleon can be made and a chameleon spell cannot be taught.
- **Charm.** No spell raises a person's regard. Calm stops a fight, and a quest's stage can change one named
  person's regard.
- **Crafting.** Our mods add no craft beyond Skyrim's smithing, alchemy and enchanting, and no spell that
  enchants or repairs. A faction's crafting quests use those three.
