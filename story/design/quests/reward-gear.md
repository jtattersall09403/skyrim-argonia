# Reward gear for the factions

This page lists the gear each faction gives its members as they rise. Faction plot writers take their rewards from
it. Every thing here is given under a name of ours, never the name it has in its mod, and none of it is Nordic.
The mods marked "new" are asset-only downloads of the player's: we copy their records into our plugin and ship
none of their files (rows in `mods/dependencies.json`, credits in `mods/sourcing-register.json`). The rest is
gear we already hold: Animated Armoury's weapons, Sign of Cipactli's Argonian armour, King of the Murkmire's
clothing, Black Marsh & Valenwood's Argonian gear, Magic Expanded's staves, and Skyrim's own Dunmer, Imperial and
thieves' gear from the game and its Dawnguard and Dragonborn add-ons. The exact item names in each mod are read
from its plugin when it is fetched.
