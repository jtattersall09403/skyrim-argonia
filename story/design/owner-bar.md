# What the owner has ruled about quests and story

One place for every ruling of the owner's on what the quests, the story and the writing should be like and how they
should feel. Each line gives its source: `OR` is [owner-rulings](../../docs/owner-rulings.md) with the entry's date,
`#n` a GitHub issue, a decision its number. Anyone who writes a story line, a quest record or a line of dialogue
reads this first; a new ruling is added here the day it is made, as well as to owner-rulings.

## The bar

- What Morrowind achieved inside minimal scripting: "beautiful storytelling, deep world building, compelling
  narrative lines, moral ambiguity, genuinely interesting conflict (of various kinds)" (OR 2026-11-12).
- It should "flow like a really nicely written exciting story throughout that any elder scrolls fan would fall in
  love with and have a lot of fun playing through" (OR 2026-11-12).
- The story is not "patched or shoehorned" into its world but "really naturally and deeply integrated with it",
  keeping the factions and the spirit of the original; there is creative licence to rewrite and re-author
  (OR 2026-11-12).
- The old main quest, factions and quest designs are the foundation, improved not replaced (OR 2026-10-28). Keep
  the treasure hunt, the spies and the main quest's spirit; make it "more Black Marsh, more exciting, less
  bureaucratic" (OR 2026-11-06, the quest review loop).
- A new player with no context must be able to tell what is going on as it happens: the walked quests were
  "incredibly confusing" and "super impenetrable" (walk 6, OR 2026-11-12).

## Action and the player's skills

- Quests have "action" (the player's skills used, varied); no talk-to-X-then-Y quests (OR 2026-10-28).
- Every quest uses named Skyrim skills and names which: One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Block, Destruction,
  Alteration, Illusion, Conjuration, Restoration, Alchemy, Sneak, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Speech, Mysticism
  (OR 2026-11-06, #50). Swimming and climbing are not skills and do not count (OR 2026-11-06, #53).
- Every main quest offers at least two ways through, each with different named skills, so different builds can
  finish it (#50). "Different builds being able to complete main" (OR 2026-11-12).
- Faction quests are tied to the faction's own skills, "for consistency and immersion" (#50); "factions have
  their own build leanings" (OR 2026-11-12).
- A quest that does not use the player's in-game skills is not fun, and that is a blocking review finding
  (OR 2026-11-06). Using in-game skills in every quest "is really key to the fun" (OR 2026-11-12).
- Quests lean on action, danger and discovery, not on law and custom (OR 2026-11-06). "Lean towards activity and
  action (of various kinds, I don't just mean combat, use your imagination and anchor on the variety of in game
  skills) over processes and procedures and records" (OR 2026-11-12).
- Combat is always paired with a reward in proportion to its difficulty (OR 2026-11-09, on what places give).

## The vibe of Argonia: not a bureaucratic place

- "Too many ledgers and rolls and tallies. For some reason AI agents are obsessed with these. It's all a bit too
  bureaucratic. I'm not saying never have them I'm saying we should just have a small number" (new-world feedback,
  2026-11-09). Cut to a few, in vocabulary Black Marsh's own lore uses (OR 2026-11-09).
- It is the theme as well as the word: "Black Marsh is not a bureaucratic place, and the player's play should not
  revolve round ledgers and documents." Concepts built on records, registers, counts and papers are reworked into
  Argonian life (the Hist, eggs, trade by water, raiding, ruins, beasts, kin, memory carried in speech and carving),
  keeping a few where a document is natural (an outsider's papers), faithful to canon and the one extrapolation
  spine (OR 2026-11-09).
- Law and custom only as background (OR 2026-11-06).
- Not absolutely none: "at most a very small amount across the board. Might be some particular people or factions
  or contexts where it's appropriate. Use your judgment" (OR 2026-11-13).
- The province's mood is written up, cited to the lore, in [province-vibe](province-vibe.md).

## Shape of a quest and a line of quests

- Nothing more complicated than Morrowind-level scripting, and much less world editing per quest (walk 6,
  OR 2026-11-12; the ceiling: [quest-form](../../docs/design/quest-form.md)). Before that ruling the bar was
  "nothing harder to script than an average Skyrim quest" (OR 2026-11-06); the lower ceiling replaces it.
- A branching budget keeps quests simple, with some branching for interest. Within a questline, earlier choices
  and endings do not change later quests' stages (no multiplying branches); only planned finale choices carry
  forward (OR 2026-11-06).
- Every stage makes plain what to do (#50). The opening needs a quest stage and a journal entry for each thing to
  do next (walk 4, OR 2026-11-08). Quest markers on the map and in the game for objectives and for things to pick
  up or use; directions in dialogue are not relied on alone (walk 6).
- The opening must not be slow (#50), and happens where the player already is, so a new player cannot wander off
  before it completes (OR 2026-11-10). It is now small and Morrowind-like: the man outside the barge's door speaks
  first, the player is not on his list, and is sent to a named person who inducts them into the main quest's
  faction (walk 6).
- No pointless or buggy activity as a step (MQ05's tree climb was cut: #50).
- Anyone can die. A person the main quest or a faction's line cannot go on without gets our version of
  Morrowind's "thread of prophecy" message; a minor side quest just fails with a journal entry (OR 2026-11-12).
- A place or a quest promises only what Skyrim's own systems can deliver (OR 2026-11-09).
- Nothing is levelled against the player; rewards are fixed (decision 0035).
- A detectives' faction, rival to the assassins and the thieves (decision 0025).

## Truth and lore

- Every world, people and quest call cites the lore dossiers or the agreed extrapolation (decision 0032).
  Everything about every quest is consistent with the lore, canon and extrapolated, including people's roles and
  the structure of society (Phase 2 brief). Agents write only what the lore and the data give, so no unsourced
  claim is made in the first place (OR 2026-11-06).
- Nothing invented from nothing: what the lore does not give is derived from it by logical extrapolation, as one
  consistent story and timeline (OR 2026-11-09).
- Every fact a person tells is true of the world the player is standing in (OR 2026-11-07, 2026-11-08).
- No Nordic element reaches the player through what we add (decision 0006).
- A native Argonian place has no jail; the Owing is the sentence; resisting arrest is a fight to the death
  (OR 2026-11-10).
- Quests, places, dialogue, characters and facts are consistent first time, by how they are written.

## People and talk

- Dialogue is unvoiced text, Morrowind-like in structure and style; lines read as if they were Morrowind's,
  adapted to Black Marsh (OR, "The game" and "Writing").
- Everyone the player can walk up to talks, with a light backstory grounded in their place, trade and spot, their
  own personality and their own knowledge, and their own true topics; people who all say the same things are
  "pretty boring" (walks 2 and 3, OR 2026-11-07, 2026-11-08).
- People in one place do not repeat each other; the writer is aware of who else speaks there (walk 5,
  OR 2026-11-10). A lore fact said by two or three people is fine (OR 2026-11-08).
- Directions are Morrowind's: a route by real landmarks from the world, in words, never a count of paces
  (walk 3, OR 2026-11-08, 2026-11-12).
- No personification, no verbless fragments, no dangling modifiers, nothing opaque to the player; British
  punctuation (OR "Writing"; walk 4).
- "My friend" at most once or twice a person; friendly address only where standing warrants it (walk 3).
- Speakers and players may be any of the ten races; address and manner vary by player race, faction and rank.

## How the story is made

- The overall story of the main quest and of each major faction is written by Fable, knowing the quest rules and
  the ceiling so that nothing undeliverable is written (OR 2026-11-12).
- The story is read on paper by fresh readers, quest by quest, before quests are written; quests are played on
  paper by fresh players as they are written and once built. Every round gathers all readers' findings and answers
  them in one coherent set of changes; readers' personas are honest and a little hard to please, not harsh
  (OR 2026-11-12).
- A paper player's finding that a fix clears is folded into the skill that produced the fault (OR 2026-11-12).
- Review findings are weighed by what the player would notice (OR 2026-11-11). Reviews of look are the owner's
  (OR, #43).
