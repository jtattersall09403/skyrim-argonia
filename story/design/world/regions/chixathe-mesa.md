# Chixathe Mesa

**Chixathe Mesa** lies in the north-east of the province: flat-topped mesas with cliff sides, cut by rivers, with quarries and chalk faces. Chixathe Mesa is a high, dry, flat-topped upland south of the Arnesian Jungle, with cliff edges, quarries and chalk pits, Velothi ruins and a Sarpa-held western edge. It is the quarry country of the Stone-Sleepers and the Wardens. It borders Arnesian Jungle to the north, Okuwun Rahei to the south-east, Murkwood to the west, Hatandros Bight to the east and Valley of Storms to the north-west. ## Its history, faith, trade and the present

The Cantemiric Velothi settled the east of Black Marsh before the Knahaten Flu of 2E 560-603 killed them; their ruins at Kaochalit, Balormaak and Vorannuhl stand on the mesa. <!-- region_note:chixathe-mesa/history -->

A mix of Hist customs: the Sarpa keep their own tree beyond the edge, the Stone-Sleepers raise cairns for Tsonac's sleeping Hist and the Agaceph read their mission books at hatchings. <!-- region_note:chixathe-mesa/faith -->

Chixathe Mesa yields quarried stone, chalk and lime, hides and books, sent off the mesa to Thorn and the river towns. <!-- region_note:chixathe-mesa/economy -->

In 4E 201 it is a thin, mixed country: Stone-Sleeper hamlets and quarries to the north and east, Warden fort at Chalkmoth, Root-Folk and Sedge-Warden outposts, an Agaceph hamlet in the south and Sarpa on the western edge. <!-- region_note:chixathe-mesa/present -->
