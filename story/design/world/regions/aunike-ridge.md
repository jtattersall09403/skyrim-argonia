# Aunike Ridge

**Aunike Ridge** lies in the middle of the province: a long low ridge of firm ground, rock outcrops and two summits, among reed marsh. Aunike Ridge is the long rise of drier ground west and south-west of Helstrom, with two peaks and a few Chime-Keeper hamlets. It is the interior's rare high ground and the line between the Hist forest and the Paatru country. It borders Banujha Basin to the south-west, Murkwood to the north-east, Ultherus Swamp to the south-east, Tulmek Washes to the south-east and Nha Enasa to the west. It holds 4 places; the region's row gives it to The Chime-Keepers (Kothringi-named towns). <!-- region:aunike-ridge; cell:region=aunike-ridge -->
