# Aphon Isle

**Aphon Isle** lies in the south-west of the province: island. Aphon Isle is a small island in Topal Bay with a belvedere, a signal tower and a smugglers' cave. It borders Topal Bay to the south-east. It holds 4 places; the region's row gives it to The Warden Marches. <!-- region:aphon-isle; cell:region=aphon-isle -->
