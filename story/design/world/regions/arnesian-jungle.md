# Arnesian Jungle

**Arnesian Jungle** lies in the north-east of the province: low forested uplands cut by a long tidal inlet from thorn's bay, with rises, river terraces and shores. The Arnesian Jungle is the humid, forested upland of the north-east, between Thorn on the coast and the mesa country to the south. It is the Stone-Sleepers' country: stone-built hamlets and a terraced city, a long inlet from the bay, Velothi ruins on the shores and a corner of Warden forts and camps round Thorn. It borders Chixathe Mesa to the south, Hatandros Bight to the south-east, Valley of Storms to the west and Padomaic Ocean to the south-east. It holds 10 places; the region's row gives it to The Stone-Sleepers of Tsonac (Tsona-Kaleel). <!-- region:arnesian-jungle; cell:region=arnesian-jungle -->

## Its nature

The ground is low forested uplands cut by a long tidal inlet from Thorn's bay, with rises, river terraces and shores; dry ground lies about 6 m above the water and the highest about 60 m; the land is wet, with firm rises; about 75% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:arnesian-jungle; cell:region=arnesian-jungle -->

A long inlet from the bay runs west to Tsonac, with rivers branching off to the south and north-west; rises of 30 to 60 m stand between them, and the ground is marshy at the shore. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/landscape --> Hot and wet: rain and fog are the commonest weathers, with clear spells that come and go by chance. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/climate -->

Its water: 4 stretches of shallow coast, 1 estuary, 3 rivers, 1 lake, 109 streams and 38 blackwater swamps. The largest flowing water is a river about 70 m wide running steadily north-east, a river about 60 m wide running steadily north-east and a river about 50 m wide running steadily south-east. <!-- water_body:region=arnesian-jungle -->

Jungle hardwood, tree-ferns, vines and bright fungus; mudcrabs and slaughterfish along the banks and in the inlet, giant snakes and giant spiders inland, grass and saltrice near Thorn. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/ecology --> Terraced stone towns among tall hardwoods, a broad bay inlet with muddy banks, Velothi ruins under vines. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/look -->

Its air is modelled as Lowland swamp rainforest, after borneo's lowland dipterocarp forest (Danum Valley, Sabah): closed canopy, green dim light, steam off the canopy at dawn, afternoon thunderstorms. Humid lowland jungle and riverine forest under tall hardwoods; rain and fog the commonest weathers, sea fog on the coast. <!-- region_atmosphere:arnesian-jungle; atmosphere_class:rainforest -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: undergrowth (17%), jungle tree (12%), boulder (10%), fern (10%), jungle emergent (7%), hanging moss (6%), marsh herb (4%), cliff piece (4%). <!-- region_flora:arnesian-jungle -->

## Its peoples

Mostly Stone-Sleeper Argonians, with Root-Folk hunters, Warden garrisons and field-owners' crews near Thorn and Dunmer traders on the road. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/people -->

The places here belong to The Warden Marches (4 places, about 36 people), The Stone-Sleepers of Tsonac (Tsona-Kaleel) (2 places, about 123 people) and The Root-Folk of Helstrom (2 places, about 14 people); 2 places carry no culture in the data. <!-- place:region=arnesian-jungle -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

Barsaebic Ayleids and Cantemiric Velothi settled the country around Arnesia, Archon and Thorn before their extinction. The Arnesian War of 3E 396 began as a slave revolt in southern Dres lands and ended with Morrowind annexing Arnesia; from 3E 432 the Argonians began to reclaim the ground, and slavers were still active in Arnesia in the late Third Era. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/history -->

The sky-blue people hold their city's Hist to be asleep; hamlets keep cairns for it, while the Root-Folk keep their own trees. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/faith -->

Timber, quarried stone, hides and sap, sold to Thorn; saltrice and tools come back. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/economy -->

In 4E 201 the jungle is the Stone-Sleepers' home country, with Tsonac as capital and the Warden forts, camps and posts stand round Thorn at its coastal edge. <!-- region_note:arnesian-jungle/present -->

Law here, place by place: 6 under native law, 2 under foreign law and 2 under no law. <!-- place_law:region=arnesian-jungle -->
