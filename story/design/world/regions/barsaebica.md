# Barsaebica

**Barsaebica** lies in the north-west of the province: hummocky marsh plain with sunken ruin mounds. The ruin-studded high ground west of Stormhold, the Barsaebic Ayleids' old country, now home to the reed-wardens of Jal-Veesa and the frontier at Slough Point. It borders Valley of Storms to the east, Murkwood to the south-east and Blackwood to the south-west. It holds 14 places; the region's row gives it to The Sedge-Wardens of Jal-Veesa (Veesa-Teeleel). <!-- region:barsaebica; cell:region=barsaebica -->

## Its nature

The ground is hummocky marsh plain with sunken ruin mounds; dry ground lies about 5 m above the water and the highest about 26 m; the land is wet, with firm rises; about 86% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:barsaebica; cell:region=barsaebica -->

A hummocky plain west of Stormhold, with ruins standing on mounds and hills. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/landscape --> The air is humid and the rain frequent. Black tidal water fills the western river mouths, brackish and slow. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/climate -->

Its water: 54 streams and 7 open reed marshes. <!-- water_body:region=barsaebica -->

Lamias live in Loriasel's caverns, and giant snakes, wamasu and hackwings range the plain. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/ecology --> White Ayleid stone in reed beds; stilt huts and weirs; a long road west. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/look -->

Its air is modelled as Green marsh and backwater, after kerala's backwaters and Kuttanad's paddies; Borneo's open freshwater marsh: bright green reed and paddy under a luminous white sky. Fen and sedge with reed beds and frequent rain; 236 pond and 83 green-marsh cells. <!-- region_atmosphere:barsaebica; atmosphere_class:green-marsh -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

Barsaebic Ayleids were driven east after 1E 198 and occupied north-eastern Black Marsh and parts of Blackwood, founding Silyanorn, Twyllbek, Archon, Thorn, Loriasel and Gandranen. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/history -->

The Hist pool at Jal-Veesa is the living shrine of the region, while the Ayleid shrines lie in ruin. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/faith -->

The region lives on reed, fish, tolls and scavenging in the Ayleid ruins. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/economy -->

A country of ruins, weirs and one frontier village; the Veesa-Teeleel guard the ruins as sick ground. <!-- region_note:barsaebica/present -->

Law here, place by place: 7 under no law, 6 under native law and 1 under foreign law. <!-- place_law:region=barsaebica -->
