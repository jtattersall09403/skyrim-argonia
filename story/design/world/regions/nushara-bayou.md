# Nushara Bayou

**Nushara Bayou** lies in the south-east of the province: a bayou of braided channels, reed flats and low hummocks, open at its south edge to the strait. The Nushara Bayou is the wet country of channels, reed beds and low hummocks behind the south-east coast between Archon and the Althii islands. The Three Waters Tribe lives on its rafts and stilts, and its back-water routes carry trade past the customs men. It borders Okuwun Rahei to the north, Tulmek Washes to the south-west, Lake Ulaish to the north-west, Althii Archipelago to the south, Benthic Yobesh to the south-east and Padomaic Ocean to the north-east. It holds 11 places; the region's row gives it to The Three Waters Tribe. <!-- region:nushara-bayou; cell:region=nushara-bayou -->

## Its nature

The ground is a bayou of braided channels, reed flats and low hummocks, open at its south edge to the strait; dry ground lies about 4 m above the water and the highest about 12 m; the land is mostly standing swamp; about 74% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:nushara-bayou; cell:region=nushara-bayou -->

Flat and braided: channels split and rejoin around thin ridges of firm ground, and the walkable ground is a chain of hummocks joined by raft-bridges. The north climbs slightly to clay mounds. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/landscape --> Hot and humid all year; the region's fixed weather is heavy warm rain and low mist over the water. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/climate -->

Its water: 4 stretches of shallow coast, 2 estuaries, 2 rivers, 12 streams and 114 open reed marshes. The largest flowing water is a river about 80 m wide running slowly south, an estuary about 100 m wide running slowly east and an estuary about 80 m wide running slowly south-east. <!-- water_body:region=nushara-bayou -->

Reed, mangrove, saltrice, slaughterfish, wading birds and mud-crabs. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/ecology --> Sheets of brown water between reed banks and hummocks of trees, lines of raft-houses and herons. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/look -->

Its air is modelled as Green marsh and backwater, after kerala's backwaters and Kuttanad's paddies; Borneo's open freshwater marsh: bright green reed and paddy under a luminous white sky. A bayou of reed flats under heavy warm rain and low mist; 408 green-marsh cells. <!-- region_atmosphere:nushara-bayou; atmosphere_class:green-marsh -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: reed (18%), marsh herb (14%), lily (14%), mangrove (7%), fern (6%), undergrowth (6%), swamp log (4%), swamp shrub (4%). <!-- region_flora:nushara-bayou -->

## Its peoples

The people are the Three Waters Argonians. Clay-Mound families live at Badal and Lilmoth-coast families at Oju. Archon's boatmen also work the channels. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/people -->

The places here belong to The Three Waters Tribe (5 places, about 16 people), The Archon Harbour Folk (1 place, about 322 people), The Clay-Mound People of Aroxhu (Oxhu-Teeleel) (1 place, about 10 people) and Lilmoth and the Murkmire Coast (1 place, about 10 people); 3 places carry no culture in the data. <!-- place:region=nushara-bayou -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

The bayou was Lilmothiit and Cantemiric Velothi ground; no Lilmothiit or Velothi lives here after the Knahaten Flu (2E 560-603), while the Three Waters Tribe survived and spread. Its villages lie east of the island path through Murkmire that Umbriel laid waste in 4E 48. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/history -->

Sithis and the Hist, with the town of Uluo keeping the nearest tree for hatching. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/faith -->

Rice, fish, reed, shellfish and back-water freight that avoids customs; Archon is the market. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/economy -->

The Three Waters Tribe holds the channels and its hamlets, Archon and Lilmoth trade through it and Fort Swiftmoth beside the road has stood empty since the secession. <!-- region_note:nushara-bayou/present -->
