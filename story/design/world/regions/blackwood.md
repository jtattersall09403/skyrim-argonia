# Blackwood

**Blackwood** lies in the west of the province: bog and low hills. The western frontier of Black Marsh, the Blackwood end of the Gideon country: hills and bog with a few villages, Hutan-Tzel outposts and the dead Kothringi towns. It borders Nha Enasa to the south, Barsaebica to the north-east, Murkwood to the east and Kotherra Mangroves to the south-west. It holds 7 places; the region's row gives it to The Gideon Frontier. <!-- region:blackwood; cell:region=blackwood -->

## Its nature

The ground is bog and low hills; dry ground lies about 4 m above the water and the highest about 45 m; the land is mostly standing swamp; about 79% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:blackwood; cell:region=blackwood -->

Flooded hillscape with steep, lightly forested hills at the western frontier of Black Marsh. <!-- region_note:blackwood/landscape --> The air is humid. Long rains and fog lie over the bogs; the frontier roads are mud where they are not causeway. <!-- region_note:blackwood/climate -->

Its water: 1 river, 33 streams and 113 blackwater swamps. The largest flowing water is a river about 20 m wide running slowly south into Murkwood. <!-- water_body:region=blackwood -->

Giant spiders, hackwings, hackwings and crocodiles live in the hills and bogs. <!-- region_note:blackwood/ecology --> A flooded hillscape of bog and cairn-fields, with steep hills rising from standing water. <!-- region_note:blackwood/look -->

Its air is modelled as Blackwater peat swamp, after borneo's peat swamp forest (Sebangau, Central Kalimantan; Belait, Brunei): tea-black water, stunted forest, the closest air. Long rains and fog over the bogs of a flooded hillscape; 267 blackwater and 772 pond cells (bog pools). <!-- region_atmosphere:blackwood; atmosphere_class:blackwater-swamp -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: boulder (14%), bald cypress (11%), reed (8%), swamp dead (8%), fern (6%), marsh herb (6%), swamp log (5%), cliff piece (5%). <!-- region_flora:blackwood -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

Kothringi homeland in large numbers; the Knahaten Flu emptied it by 2E 603; the Maran mission died at Umpholo in the 3E. <!-- region_note:blackwood/history -->

Dibella and Z'en remembered; Hist shoots; the Sul-Xan's Dagon heresy. <!-- region_note:blackwood/faith -->

Fish, clay, hunting, herbs and trade along the Blackwood Road support the frontier's people. <!-- region_note:blackwood/economy -->
