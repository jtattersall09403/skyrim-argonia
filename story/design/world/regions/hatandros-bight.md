# Hatandros Bight

**Hatandros Bight** lies in the east of the province: coastal plain and bay shore. Hatandros Bight is the open bay and coastal plain south of Thorn, a strip of grassland, mud-flats and low clay rises with Cantemiric Velothi ruins on its headlands. It borders Padomaic Ocean to the south-east, Okuwun Rahei to the south-west, Chixathe Mesa to the west and Arnesian Jungle to the north-west. It holds 10 places; which culture holds it, the data does not say. <!-- region:hatandros-bight; cell:region=hatandros-bight -->

## Its nature

The ground is coastal plain and bay shore; dry ground lies about 3 m above the water and the highest about 22 m; the land is wet, with firm rises; about 75% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:hatandros-bight; cell:region=hatandros-bight -->

A flat to gently rolling plain behind a wide bay, cut by shallow rivers that end in mud-flats at the coast. Low clay rises carry the villages; headlands of dark stone hold the ruins. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/landscape --> Milder than the interior, with fog and frequent rain. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/climate -->

Its water: 4 stretches of shallow coast, 27 streams and 16 open reed marshes. <!-- water_body:region=hatandros-bight -->

Marsh grasses, reeds and salt-tolerant shrubs; crabs, eels, hackwings and coastal crocodiles; temperate grassland more than swamp, as in the rest of the north. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/ecology --> A pale shore of grey mud-flats and sandy points with grass behind, bright green after rain, low red clay rises, black Velothi ruins on the headlands, sea fog. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/look -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

Cantemiric Velothi held this coast from Thorn to Archon until the Knahaten Flu of 2E 560 killed them. Dres slave-ships sailed from Thorn across it; the Arnesian War of 3E 396 was fought on its northern margin. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/history -->

Mostly Hist-less: coast villages send eggs inland; Velothi ancestor tablets mark the ruins; the Sarpa read the wind. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/faith -->

Salt, stone, timber, clay and pottery, sold up and down the coast to Thorn and Archon. The wardens' posts take a share in tolls, and smugglers take another at the landings. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/economy -->

A thinly peopled coast: Arorha at its south end, small hamlets of Tsonac's stone-folk and Helstrom's Root-Folk, Warden posts toward Thorn and three dead Velothi ruins. The coast road from Thorn to Seafalls and Archon runs through it. <!-- region_note:hatandros-bight/present -->

Law here, place by place: 5 under native law, 4 under no law and 1 under foreign law. <!-- place_law:region=hatandros-bight -->
