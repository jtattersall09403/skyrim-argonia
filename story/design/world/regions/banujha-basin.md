# Banujha Basin

**Banujha Basin** lies in the south-west of the province: flooded basin and river maze. The Banujha Basin is the wet interior behind Soulrest, held by the Mine-Burner tribes who burned the Imperial mines in 3E 427. It borders Cape Percar to the south-west, Aunike Ridge to the north-east, Nha Enasa to the north-west and Tulmek Washes to the south-east. It holds 15 places; the region's row gives it to The Mine-Burners of the Banujha (Banu-Jaleel). <!-- region:banujha-basin; cell:region=banujha-basin -->

## Its nature

The ground is flooded basin and river maze; dry ground lies about 5 m above the water and the highest about 22 m; the land is mostly standing swamp; about 74% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:banujha-basin; cell:region=banujha-basin -->

A shallow basin of channels and islets, with dark water between them. <!-- region_note:banujha-basin/landscape --> The basin is hot and humid, with heavy rain and steam rising off the water after it. <!-- region_note:banujha-basin/climate -->

Its water: 1 lake, 3 streams, 4 open reed marshes and 177 blackwater swamps. <!-- water_body:region=banujha-basin -->

Reeds and mangrove grow in the basin, with gar, mudcrabs and hackwings. <!-- region_note:banujha-basin/ecology --> Flooded channels, scorched timber and tiered stilt halls mark the basin. <!-- region_note:banujha-basin/look -->

Its air is modelled as Blackwater peat swamp, after borneo's peat swamp forest (Sebangau, Central Kalimantan; Belait, Brunei): tea-black water, stunted forest, the closest air. Hot and humid with steam rising off the water after rain; dark water between islets; 561 of its 902 cells are blackwater swamp. <!-- region_atmosphere:banujha-basin; atmosphere_class:blackwater-swamp -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: reed (20%), lily (12%), marsh herb (9%), mangrove (8%), swamp log (8%), swamp dead (8%), fern (6%), bald cypress (5%). <!-- region_flora:banujha-basin -->
