# Cape Percar

**Cape Percar** lies in the south-west of the province: a forested peninsula of low hills and coastal flats, cut by tidal creeks, with mines in the hills and a burn-scarred strip. Cape Percar is the western peninsula wrapping Topal Bay, Soulrest's hinterland of burned mines, quarries, timber camps and coastal villages. Drier and hillier than the washes east of it, it carries the burn scar of the Great Burn. It borders Banujha Basin to the north-east, Sea of Mists to the south-east, Topal Bay to the north-west, Tulmek Washes to the east and Nha Enasa to the north. It holds 17 places; the region's row gives it to Soulrest and the Topal Coast. <!-- region:cape-percar; cell:region=cape-percar -->

## Its nature

The ground is a forested peninsula of low hills and coastal flats, cut by tidal creeks, with mines in the hills and a burn-scarred strip; dry ground lies about 3 m above the water and the highest about 45 m; the land is mostly dry; about 90% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:cape-percar; cell:region=cape-percar -->

Low hills up to about 45 m behind the coast, flat sandy shores, salt creeks and mangrove edges. <!-- region_note:cape-percar/landscape --> Humid and warm; sea mist on the south coast; rain is frequent and the low creeks stand full. <!-- region_note:cape-percar/climate -->

Its water: 2 stretches of shallow coast, 14 streams and 35 blackwater swamps. <!-- water_body:region=cape-percar -->

Red gum, mangrove, wading birds, sea-drakes at the shore; the burn-scar is bare. <!-- region_note:cape-percar/ecology --> Red-gum hills, black burn-scars, grey-stone Imperial ruins and stilt villages on the coast. <!-- region_note:cape-percar/look -->

Its air is modelled as South coast (the Sea of Mists), after borneo's south coast and the Sundarbans' creeks: sea mist most mornings, warm milky haze over mangrove and mud.
