# Kotherra Mangroves

**Kotherra Mangroves** lies in the west of the province: mangrove delta and tidal flat. The Kotherra Mangroves are the tidal delta country around Gideon, where mangrove channels, red-clay banks and the Blackwood Road meet Topal Bay. It borders Nha Enasa to the east, Topal Bay to the south-west and Blackwood to the north-east. It holds 9 places; the region's row gives it to The Gideon Frontier. <!-- region:kotherra-mangroves; cell:region=kotherra-mangroves -->

## Its nature

The ground is mangrove delta and tidal flat; dry ground lies about 3 m above the water and the highest about 14 m; the land is mostly standing swamp; about 70% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:kotherra-mangroves; cell:region=kotherra-mangroves -->

Mudflats and mangrove channels with a few raised banks; the Onkobra delta lays down red clay and the estuary's low ground stands under water. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/landscape --> Hot and humid, with frequent heavy rain and fog off the estuary at dawn. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/climate -->

Its water: 7 stretches of shallow coast, 1 estuary, 1 river, 65 streams and 28 blackwater swamps. The largest flowing water is a river about 60 m wide running slowly south-west and an estuary about 60 m wide. <!-- water_body:region=kotherra-mangroves -->

Mangrove, reed and fern; hackwings, eels and crows over the mud; gar in the channels. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/ecology --> Black water between stilt houses, red mud, tangled mangrove roots and grey Imperial stone along the road. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/look -->

Its air is modelled as West coast (Topal Bay), after a sheltered bay coast facing the sunset (Kerala's lagoons at Alappuzha, Sabah's west coast at Kota Kinabalu): sunny mornings, sea breeze, squalls, fiery sunsets over the water. Mangrove delta on Topal Bay: hot, frequent heavy rain, fog off the estuary at dawn. <!-- region_atmosphere:kotherra-mangroves; atmosphere_class:coast-west -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: mangrove (19%), reed (16%), fern (10%), marsh herb (8%), lily (8%), swamp log (6%), swamp dead (4%), bald cypress (4%). <!-- region_flora:kotherra-mangroves -->

## Its peoples

Majority Lukiul Argonians, Nibenese-descended families, Naka-Desh river-folk and a few Khajiit sailors. Perhaps 130 live in the region. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/people -->

The places here belong to The Gideon Frontier (4 places, about 272 people), The Warden Marches (1 place, about 7 people) and The Naka-Desh (Riverbacks) (1 place, about 11 people); 3 places carry no culture in the data. <!-- place:region=kotherra-mangroves -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

Barsaebic Ayleids, Kothringi and Imperial settlers each built here. The Kothringi died in the Knahaten Flu, and the Imperial garrisons left at the secession; Gideon alone kept the Imperial courthouse. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/history -->

Dibella in Gideon, Sithis in the villages; Kothringi chimes in the ruins; the Hist is a planted cutting. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/faith -->

Hunting, herbs, red-clay pottery, fishing and the Leyawiin trade along the Blackwood Road. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/economy -->

Gideon's council holds the dry land and the Archwardens hold Fort Silvermoth on the south road. The rest is mangrove, and it is crossed only by the Blackwood Way and the delta channels. <!-- region_note:kotherra-mangroves/present -->

Law here, place by place: 4 under native law, 3 under no law and 2 under foreign law. <!-- place_law:region=kotherra-mangroves -->
