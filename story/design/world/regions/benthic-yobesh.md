# Benthic Yobesh

**Benthic Yobesh** lies in the south-east of the province: sea. Benthic Yobesh is the open sea floor east of the Althii islands where seven drowned spires, Echo Deep and the downed Kelp Lantern lie. Only Bell Station's divers work it. It borders Althii Archipelago to the west, Nushara Bayou to the north-west, Sea of Mists to the west and Padomaic Ocean to the north. ## Its history, faith, trade and the present

Myth says the Southern Sea swallowed Murkmire in the Dawn Era, when the Hist's land sank but for one corner; the spires are what is told to remain. <!-- region_note:benthic-yobesh/history -->

Argonians hold the deep as the abode of the drowned and pray before diving. <!-- region_note:benthic-yobesh/faith -->

The spires yield salvage: drowned metal, carved stone and coral-crusted glass. The steward at Bell Station decides who dives. <!-- region_note:benthic-yobesh/economy -->

Bell Station's licensed divers salvage the spires; elsewhere the sea floor is unclaimed. <!-- region_note:benthic-yobesh/present -->
