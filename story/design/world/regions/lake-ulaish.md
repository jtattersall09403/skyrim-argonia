# Lake Ulaish

**Lake Ulaish** lies in the middle of the province: a broad shallow lake with silt islets, reed shores and river deltas at its north-west corner. Lake Ulaish is the great lake south-east of Helstrom where the interior waters pool before running to Lilmoth and Blackrose. Fishing hamlets, a toll fort and a trading city share its shores and boats are its roads. It borders Murkwood to the north-west, Nushara Bayou to the south-east, Okuwun Rahei to the east, Ultherus Swamp to the south-west and Tulmek Washes to the south-west.
