# Althii Archipelago

**Althii Archipelago** lies in the south-east of the province: low islands of firm ground with inner marsh, hills of a few metres and sea-cut coasts. The Althii Archipelago is two main low islands and a scatter of islets off the south-east coast, with Lilmoth at the southern end and the Three Waters town of Uluo on the north. Estates, farms and Imperial and Lilmothiit ruins fill the interior. It borders Benthic Yobesh to the east, Sea of Mists to the west, Tulmek Washes to the west and Nushara Bayou to the north. It holds 22 places; the region's row gives it to Lilmoth and the Murkmire Coast. <!-- region:althii-archipelago; cell:region=althii-archipelago -->

## Its nature

The ground is low islands of firm ground with inner marsh, hills of a few metres and sea-cut coasts; dry ground lies about 3 m above the water and the highest about 16 m; the land is wet, with firm rises; about 60% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:althii-archipelago; cell:region=althii-archipelago -->

Low and flat with a few low hills, cut by inlets and inner channels; roads link the towns across land and ferries. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/landscape --> The islands are hot and humid; the region's fixed weather is a steady sea wind and warm rain. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/climate -->

Its water: 2 reaches of open sea, 21 stretches of shallow coast, 1 lake, 5 streams, 46 open reed marshes and 1 pond. <!-- water_body:region=althii-archipelago -->

Mangrove coasts, paddy fauna, wading birds, guar and crabs fill the islands. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/ecology --> Green rice paddies, stilt villages and brick ruins in vines, with sea on every side. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/look -->

Its air is modelled as Islands, after small tropical islands in clean sea air (the Lakshadweep, Sabah's offshore islands): bright light, turquoise shallows, passing showers, the clearest air. Low islands under a steady sea wind and warm rain, paddies and mangrove coasts, sea on every side. <!-- region_atmosphere:althii-archipelago; atmosphere_class:island -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: reed (13%), boulder (12%), marsh herb (10%), undergrowth (10%), coast palm (8%), mangrove (8%), flowers (6%), dry tree (5%). <!-- region_flora:althii-archipelago -->

## Its peoples

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

The Lilmothiit withdrew inland over generations and the Knahaten Flu (2E 560-603) finished their remnant. The Empire's forts and road came later and were left at the secession (4E 5-10). The planters' estates had emptied in the Third Era. Lilmoth was destroyed in 4E 48, and the islands were refilled in the generations after. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/history -->

Lilmoth's second Hist, grown in 4E 8 and cut off from the others, is the islands' feared tree; the Three Waters keep their own pool. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/faith -->

The people of the islands live on rice, fish, salvage and ferrying; Lilmoth is the market for all of it. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/economy -->

Lilmoth leads the islands; Archwarden forts hold the east and the south-west, some of them against Lilmoth's wishes; Uluo's Three Waters people hold the north. <!-- region_note:althii-archipelago/present -->

Law here, place by place: 12 under native law, 8 under no law and 2 under foreign law. <!-- place_law:region=althii-archipelago -->
