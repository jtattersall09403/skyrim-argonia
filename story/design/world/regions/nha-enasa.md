# Nha Enasa

**Nha Enasa** lies in the west of the province: river plain with low banks. Nha Enasa is the drier river country north-east of Gideon, with Naka-Desh river stations, Kothringi ruins and the market town of Gul-Tej. It borders Kotherra Mangroves to the west, Banujha Basin to the south-east, Blackwood to the north, Murkwood to the north-east, Topal Bay to the south-west, Cape Percar to the south and Aunike Ridge to the east. It holds 11 places; the region's row gives it to The Naka-Desh (Riverbacks). <!-- region:nha-enasa; cell:region=nha-enasa -->

Its water: 2 stretches of shallow coast, 21 streams and 10 open reed marshes. <!-- water_body:region=nha-enasa -->
