# Murkwood

**Murkwood** lies in the middle of the province: low swamp forest of root-hummocks, braided river arms and black heart-mud flats, with a few dry rises. Murkwood is the Hist forest at the heart of Black Marsh, the legendary dark wood that ever moves, around Helstrom. Its people are the Root-Folk, its beasts are medusas and Naga and outsiders who enter without leave do not come out. It borders Aunike Ridge to the south-west, Lake Ulaish to the south-east, Chixathe Mesa to the east, Barsaebica to the north-west, Valley of Storms to the north, Okuwun Rahei to the east, Ultherus Swamp to the south, Nha Enasa to the south-west and Blackwood to the west. It holds 13 places; the region's row gives it to The Root-Folk of Helstrom. <!-- region:murkwood; cell:region=murkwood -->

## Its nature

The ground is low swamp forest of root-hummocks, braided river arms and black Heart-Mud flats, with a few dry rises; dry ground lies about 5 m above the water and the highest about 20 m; the land is mostly standing swamp; about 82% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:murkwood; cell:region=murkwood -->

Low, wet ground cut by braided river arms flowing toward the lake, with dry rises where the roots hold the soil. Heart-Mud flats lie on the south. <!-- region_note:murkwood/landscape --> Hot, wet and still, with heavy rain and morning fog the commonest weathers and clear days rare. <!-- region_note:murkwood/climate -->

Its water: 2 rivers, 1 lake, 54 streams, 83 open reed marshes, 64 blackwater swamps and 3 ponds. The largest flowing water is a river about 50 m wide running slowly east and a river about 50 m wide running slowly east into Lake Ulaish. <!-- water_body:region=murkwood -->

Great Hist trees, mosses and ferns; fellrunners, medusas, rootworms, crocodiles, flesh-flies and torchbugs. Fellrunner pale down is the region's famous good. <!-- region_note:murkwood/ecology --> A dark wood of huge roots, hanging moss and black water; light comes green and dim, the air is thick and loud with insects and the forest never looks the same twice. <!-- region_note:murkwood/look -->

Its air is modelled as Lowland swamp rainforest, after borneo's lowland dipterocarp forest (Danum Valley, Sabah): closed canopy, green dim light, steam off the canopy at dawn, afternoon thunderstorms. A dark wood of huge roots: light comes green and dim, the air thick; morning fog and heavy rain; braided river arms (204 river cells). <!-- region_atmosphere:murkwood; atmosphere_class:rainforest -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: hanging moss (11%), bald cypress (10%), fern (10%), lily (9%), hist root (8%), swamp log (5%), cypress knee (5%), marsh herb (5%). <!-- region_flora:murkwood -->

## Its peoples

The Root-Folk of Helstrom (Saxhleel) hold the heart; the Hutan-Tzel hold the west; tribeless Naga and the toad-like Paatru hold the camps at the margin. <!-- region_note:murkwood/people -->

The places here belong to The Root-Folk of Helstrom (6 places, about 55 people), The Hutan-Tzel (2 places, about 61 people) and The Chime-Keepers (Kothringi-named towns) (1 place, about 3 people); 4 places carry no culture in the data. <!-- place:region=murkwood -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

In the Imperial Simulacrum of 3E 399 Jagar Tharn hid a piece of the Staff of Chaos in Murkwood, and the Eternal Champion fetched it. The forest has moved many times since; Helstrom and the Root-Folk have stood beside it through all of them. <!-- region_note:murkwood/history -->

The Hist: every settlement sends its people to a village tree to hatch and bury. The Root Talk at Helstrom is the occasion on which the tribes hear the Hist through the tree-minders. <!-- region_note:murkwood/faith -->

Fellrunner feathers, sap, eggs, hides and river fish; farmers trade sacks of feathers to hunters, and a single armband of the pale down lets a hunter move through Murkwood undetected. <!-- region_note:murkwood/economy -->

In 4E 201 the forest crowds the river arms around Helstrom, held by the Root-Folk, the Hutan-Tzel at its west edge and several camps of Naga and Paatru. Few outsiders come. <!-- region_note:murkwood/present -->

Law here, place by place: 9 under native law and 4 under no law. <!-- place_law:region=murkwood -->

- The Naga were road-robbers on Imperial plantation routes and retreated to the inner swamps when the roads failed; this camp is where one band settled. <!-- place_note:tribal-camp-m2-m71/history -->
- They live on river raids, hunting and what they take from passing boats. <!-- place_note:tribal-camp-m2-m71/economy -->

**Hiss-Bank Camp.**

- The Hutan-Tzel pay a bounty for the seer; a stolen totem-stone is in the camp. <!-- place_note:tribal-camp-m12-m74/hooks -->
- Eight Naga hold a bend of the western river and raid the Hutan-Tzel's boats. <!-- place_note:tribal-camp-m12-m74/present -->
- The band lives by raids on the Hutan-Tzel's boats, by hunting and by extorting payment from the ferry at Tzel-Haar. <!-- place_note:tribal-camp-m12-m74/economy -->

**Hokal-Ush.**

- A hunter went into Murkwood wearing a feather band and has not come back; a feather-trader will pay for a missing band recovered. <!-- place_note:outpost-6-m77/hooks -->

**Ixu-Maan.**

- A signal went up at night with no visitor in sight; an egg-tender claims the Hist spoke to her in the mud. <!-- place_note:outpost-m2-m79/hooks -->

**Vesh-Aneel.**

- A guide lost a party at the last crossing; the post's fire was lit three nights running without a keeper. <!-- place_note:outpost-m3-m83/hooks -->

**Nemeon.**

- A wrecked boat lies in the reeds with its goods unsold; a Kothringi cairn is hidden behind the houses; the lantern is lit from the water side at night and the family has not seen who does it. <!-- place_note:nemeon/hooks -->

**Gaxu-Reet.**

- A clutch is missing from the nest-pits; boats pass that did not come up the river; the old sap-speaker will name who took it for a price. <!-- place_note:outpost-1-m81/hooks -->
