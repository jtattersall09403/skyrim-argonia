# Ultherus Swamp

**Ultherus Swamp** lies in the south of the province: a low wet maze of channels, hummocks and cattail beds, with rings of firmer ground. The Ultherus Swamp is the great wetland south of Helstrom, in which the Kothringi-named town of Korydon lies. Little is told of it, except that the lovely and deadly dragon's-tongue grows thickest there. It borders Tulmek Washes to the south, Aunike Ridge to the north-west, Murkwood to the north and Lake Ulaish to the north-east. It holds 4 places; the region's row gives it to The Chime-Keepers (Kothringi-named towns). <!-- region:ultherus-swamp; cell:region=ultherus-swamp -->

## Its nature

The ground is a low wet maze of channels, hummocks and cattail beds, with rings of firmer ground; dry ground lies about 5 m above the water and the highest about 14 m; the land is mostly standing swamp; about 72% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:ultherus-swamp; cell:region=ultherus-swamp -->

Flat and low, cut by many channels, with ring-like firm ground such as the one under Korydon; the south opens into the larger waters toward Blackrose. <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/landscape --> Hot and heavy, with rain and a warm fog on the channels the commonest weathers. <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/climate -->

Its water: 8 lakes, 14 open reed marshes and 24 blackwater swamps. <!-- water_body:region=ultherus-swamp -->

Dragon's-tongue, cattails, reed, water-lilies; eels, crocodiles, marsh-lizards and swarms of flesh-flies. <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/ecology --> The swamp is a grey-green maze of cattails, reed and black water, with rings of dry ground and stilt towns hung with metal chimes. <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/look -->

Its air is modelled as Blackwater peat swamp, after borneo's peat swamp forest (Sebangau, Central Kalimantan; Belait, Brunei): tea-black water, stunted forest, the closest air. ## Its history, faith, trade and the present

No song survives of this swamp's past. Its Kothringi-named towns, Korydon, Myroleon and Nemeon, are read as old Kothringi places that Argonians resettled after the Knahaten Flu (2E 560–603). <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/history -->

Sithis and the Hist; Korydon adds Z'en, kept at a chime-hung shrine. <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/faith -->

Dragon's-tongue and other swamp herbs, chimes, rope and boats from Korydon, fish and pelts traded north to Helstrom and south to Blackrose. <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/economy -->

In 4E 201 the swamp is crossed by boat; Korydon's Chime-Keepers harvest the dragon's-tongue, Tumnatu hunters keep to the reeds and a Root-Folk waystation sits at its south. <!-- region_note:ultherus-swamp/present -->
