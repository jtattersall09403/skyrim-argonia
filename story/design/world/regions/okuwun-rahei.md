# Okuwun Rahei

**Okuwun Rahei** lies in the east of the province: mound country, lakes and marsh. Okuwun-Rahei is the mound-and-lake country of the east interior, the home of the Clay-Mound People of Aroxhu, the Agaceph and the Spray-Folk of Seafalls. It borders Chixathe Mesa to the north-west, Padomaic Ocean to the east, Nushara Bayou to the south, Hatandros Bight to the north-east, Murkwood to the west and Lake Ulaish to the west. It holds 22 places; the region's row gives it to The Clay-Mound People of Aroxhu (Oxhu-Teeleel). <!-- region:okuwun-rahei; cell:region=okuwun-rahei -->

## Its nature

The ground is mound country, lakes and marsh; dry ground lies about 4 m above the water and the highest about 26 m; the land is wet, with firm rises; about 77% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:okuwun-rahei; cell:region=okuwun-rahei -->

Low red clay mounds above flat marsh, big lakes in the west and a maze of creeks in the south-east, ending in cliffs and a falls at the coast. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/landscape --> Hot, humid and rainy; rain falls often and the mounds stand dry above the marsh. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/climate -->

Its water: 6 stretches of shallow coast, 1 estuary, 2 rivers, 18 streams, 116 open reed marshes and 6 ponds. The largest flowing water is a river about 60 m wide running steadily south-east, a river about 40 m wide running steadily north-east and an estuary about 50 m wide running slowly south. <!-- water_body:region=okuwun-rahei -->

Reeds, rice, madder and ochre-stained marsh plants; crocodiles, eels, giant snakes and hackwings; guar on the meadows. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/ecology --> Red-brown mounds in green-grey marsh, bright reed thatch, kilns smoking, pale lakes and brown creeks, black Velothi ruins, white falls at Seafalls. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/look -->

Its air is modelled as Green marsh and backwater, after kerala's backwaters and Kuttanad's paddies; Borneo's open freshwater marsh: bright green reed and paddy under a luminous white sky. Red mounds in green-grey marsh, pale lakes and brown creeks; 452 green-marsh cells, the most of any region. <!-- region_atmosphere:okuwun-rahei; atmosphere_class:green-marsh -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: reed (14%), boulder (12%), lily (12%), flowers (9%), marsh herb (9%), undergrowth (8%), kelp (5%), dry tree (5%). <!-- region_flora:okuwun-rahei -->

## Its peoples

Oxhu-Teeleel clay-mound people (the bulk), Agaceph raft-folk at Yulca, Icalca, Xibcan and Yina, Spray-Folk at Seafalls, Sarpa camps, a Root-Folk outpost. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/people -->

The places here belong to The Clay-Mound People of Aroxhu (Oxhu-Teeleel) (7 places, about 128 people), The Spray-Folk of Seafalls (5 places, about 28 people), The Agaceph (4 places, about 51 people) and The Root-Folk of Helstrom (1 place, about 5 people); 5 places carry no culture in the data. <!-- place:region=okuwun-rahei -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

A tribal country long before the Empire; the East Empire Company's boats worked its clay, and Cantemiric Velothi once held its coast. Since the secession the clay-mound people have been the dominant power. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/history -->

The Aroxhu Hist at the centre; the Spray-Folk's sea-memory; the Agaceph's detachment; Velothi ancestor tablets in the ruins; Sarpa wind-omens. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/faith -->

Clay, pottery, dye and ochre; fish and rice; books and illusion-work from the Agaceph; Seafalls' piloting and portage. The river trade to Archon and Thorn runs on clay. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/economy -->

The confederacy at Aroxhu is the largest tribal polity of the east; the Agaceph at Yulca and the Spray-Folk at Seafalls are its neighbours; Skeith's outlaws harass the coast. <!-- region_note:okuwun-rahei/present -->

Law here, place by place: 18 under native law and 4 under no law. <!-- place_law:region=okuwun-rahei -->
