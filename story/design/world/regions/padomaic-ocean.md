# Padomaic Ocean

**Padomaic Ocean** lies in the east of the province: sea. The Padomaic Ocean is the open sea off Black Marsh's eastern coast, where Skeith Isle's pirates and the coast boats work and the wrecks of three eras lie on the shelf. It borders Skeith Isle to the east, Hatandros Bight to the north-west, Okuwun Rahei to the west, Nushara Bayou to the south-west, Arnesian Jungle to the north-west and Benthic Yobesh to the south. ## Its history, faith, trade and the present

In the Dawn Era the Ehlnofey war sank most of the Hist's domain into the Padomaic, 'leaving only a small corner left standing', later Black Marsh. Topal the Pilot charted it first. Uriel Septim V's fleet sailed across it against Akavir in 3E 288. <!-- region_note:padomaic-ocean/history -->

The Spray-Folk hold that memories the Hist let fall are kept in the sea; sailors leave knotted cords and painted shells on shore for the lost. <!-- region_note:padomaic-ocean/faith -->

Fishing, piloting, salvage, piracy and smuggling; the north-east link between Thorn, Seafalls and Archon is run by sea as well as by road. <!-- region_note:padomaic-ocean/economy -->

Argonia has no navy. Chartered pirates do that work. Boats from Archon, Seafalls and Thorn work the shelf; the Archwardens' writ ends at the quay. The wrecks of a Dunmer slaver, a smuggler's sloop, an Imperial man-of-war and a freighter lie here. <!-- region_note:padomaic-ocean/present -->
