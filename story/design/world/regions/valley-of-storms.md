# Valley of Storms

**Valley of Storms** lies in the north of the province: rolling river plain of mounds and ravines. The Shadowfen's northern frontier country around Stormhold: a rolling, ravine-cut plain of mounds, reed beds and river branches beside the Morrowind border. It borders Barsaebica to the west, Murkwood to the south, Chixathe Mesa to the south-east and Arnesian Jungle to the east. It holds 14 places; the region's row gives it to The Shadowfen Border (Stormhold). <!-- region:valley-of-storms; cell:region=valley-of-storms -->

## Its nature

The ground is rolling river plain of mounds and ravines; dry ground lies about 6 m above the water and the highest about 38 m; the land is wet, with firm rises; about 95% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:valley-of-storms; cell:region=valley-of-storms -->

Stormhold stands on a mountainside and ravine-cut mounds; the rest is rolling marsh plain rising to the Morrowind border. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/landscape --> Humid and warm, with heavy rain and thunder in the region's weather; the lower causeway stretches lie under shallow water that carts wade. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/climate -->

Its water: 39 streams and 6 open reed marshes. <!-- water_body:region=valley-of-storms -->

Crocodiles in the ravines, hackwings, giant snakes and wamasu in the deeper water. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/ecology --> Stone-footed farmsteads and mounds linked by stone causeways, the river and its branches and the grey Imperial forts. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/look -->

Its air is modelled as River corridor, after the Kinabatangan and Rajang in Borneo, the Periyar in Kerala: brown rivers in a humid valley, river fog at dawn, storms in the afternoon. A rolling river plain with heavy rain and thunder; the river and its branches carry the causeways; 85 stream cells. <!-- region_atmosphere:valley-of-storms; atmosphere_class:river-corridor -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: boulder (14%), undergrowth (11%), reed (11%), marsh herb (10%), dry tree (8%), flowers (7%), cliff piece (7%), willow (4%). <!-- region_flora:valley-of-storms -->

## Its peoples

Argonians and the oldest Dunmer minority in the province, with Veesa-Teeleel reed-wardens to the west. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/people -->

The places here belong to The Shadowfen Border (Stormhold) (6 places, about 176 people), The Sedge-Wardens of Jal-Veesa (Veesa-Teeleel) (3 places, about 66 people) and The Warden Marches (1 place, about 23 people); 4 places carry no culture in the data. <!-- place:region=valley-of-storms -->

## Its history, faith, trade and the present

Barsaebic Ayleids built Silyanorn here; the Dunmer made the area the foothold of their slave trade; the Pact made Stormhold its capital; Umbriel overran it in 4E 48 with light damage. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/history -->

The town-square Hist, the grave-stake tradition and Dunmer ancestor stones. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/faith -->

River and road trade with Morrowind and Alten Corimont, saltrice, crystals and reed. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/economy -->

A frontier of causeway farms, Archwarden forts and a Vicecanon-run city, still the province's working border with Morrowind. <!-- region_note:valley-of-storms/present -->

Law here, place by place: 10 under native law, 3 under no law and 1 under foreign law. <!-- place_law:region=valley-of-storms -->
