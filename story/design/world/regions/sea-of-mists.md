# Sea of Mists

**Sea of Mists** lies in the south of the province: sea. The Sea of Mists is the Southern Sea off Black Marsh's south coast, between Cape Percar and the Lilmoth coast, held under a low mist most mornings. Wrecks, small islands and a lighthouse mark it. It borders Tulmek Washes to the north-east, Cape Percar to the north-west, Althii Archipelago to the east, Benthic Yobesh to the east and Topal Bay to the north-west.
