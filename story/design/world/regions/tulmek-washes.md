# Tulmek Washes

**Tulmek Washes** lies in the south of the province: flat flooded lowland of river channels, lakes, mud islands and small dry rises, with the blackrose lake at its heart. The Tulmek Washes are the drowned lowland between Soulrest's cape and the Lilmoth delta, centred on Blackrose's lake and its three rivers. A mosaic of channels, islands and ruins, it is the hinge of the south. It borders Sea of Mists to the south-west, Ultherus Swamp to the north, Nushara Bayou to the north-east, Banujha Basin to the north-west, Althii Archipelago to the east, Cape Percar to the west, Aunike Ridge to the north-west and Lake Ulaish to the north-east. It holds 24 places; the region's row gives it to The Island of Blackrose. <!-- region:tulmek-washes; cell:region=tulmek-washes -->

## Its nature

The ground is flat flooded lowland of river channels, lakes, mud islands and small dry rises, with the Blackrose lake at its heart; dry ground lies about 4 m above the water and the highest about 14 m; the land is mostly standing swamp; about 61% of it is dry land on the map. <!-- region:tulmek-washes; cell:region=tulmek-washes -->

Dead-flat lowland of standing water with tiny rises; channels braid round low islands of reed and mud. <!-- region_note:tulmek-washes/landscape --> The washes are hot, humid and still; rain falls most days and the water stands across the whole lowland between the cape and the delta. <!-- region_note:tulmek-washes/climate -->

Its water: 24 stretches of shallow coast, 6 estuaries, 10 rivers, 3 lakes, 82 streams, 4 open reed marshes, 78 blackwater swamps and 1 pond. The largest flowing water is a river about 60 m wide running slowly south-east, a river about 100 m wide running slowly south-east and an estuary about 60 m wide running slowly south into Sea of Mists. <!-- water_body:region=tulmek-washes -->

Reeds, mangroves, cypress, gar, crocodilians, herons; sea-drakes at the south shore. <!-- region_note:tulmek-washes/ecology --> Brown water, reed beds, stilt towns, boardwalks, drowned domes and mist. <!-- region_note:tulmek-washes/look -->

Its air is modelled as Blackwater peat swamp, after borneo's peat swamp forest (Sebangau, Central Kalimantan; Belait, Brunei): tea-black water, stunted forest, the closest air. Hot, humid and still, rain most days, brown water and mist; 331 cells blackwater swamp, the most of any region after Banujha. <!-- region_atmosphere:tulmek-washes; atmosphere_class:blackwater-swamp -->

What grows commonest, by share of its plants: reed (17%), lily (12%), bald cypress (9%), marsh herb (8%), fern (7%), cypress knee (7%), swamp dead (6%), swamp log (6%). <!-- region_flora:tulmek-washes -->
