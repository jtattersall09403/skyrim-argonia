# Skills

- `quest-write`: design a quest record (shapes, principles, skill-use catalogue, brief and action-plan generators) from the story data and the lore (decisions 0024, 0025).
- `quest-review`: independent design critique of a quest record before any dialogue exists.
- `dialogue-write`: write one person's dialogue from a generated brief, with the ban linter, voices and the Morrowind corpus tools.
- `text-review`: one `reader` pass then the linter over changed player-visible prose.
- `cost-review`: periodic Claude usage and cost review from `tooling/session_tokens.py`.
- `grill-me`: stress-test a plan with the owner, one question at a time, after the repo has answered what it can (adapted from mattpocock/skills, MIT).
- `place-system`: the map of the place skills: a place is its source carried whole and adapted by hand; the order of work from record to the owner's studio check, the working method, the hand-over bar and how the lead runs the one agent that owns a place; it says when to open each sub-skill.
- Place drafting sub-skills: `place-plan` (the record and the source choice), `place-site` (the ground, the baseline, fitting to our ground), `place-adapt` (relabel, prune, move, add), `place-rooms` (interiors), `place-streets` (the carried walking mesh and walks), `place-routines` (beds, routines, law), `place-story` (quest things and the sight test), `place-dressing` (sap, lights, lanterns, stations, collision boxes), `place-look` (eyes and hands, the hand-over look), `place-check` (verify once), `place-change` (narrow changes, the owner's notes).
- `place-research`, `place-people`, `place-stock`, `place-lessons`: a place's record, its people's identities, what it holds, and lessons from the owner's finalised edits.
