# HAUNTING, RITE, WONDER, and Daedric or oddity quests

Read with `general.md` (which holds the source tags' key). Black Marsh's dead go to the Hist, are pinned by
grave-stakes (xul-vaat) and can be ancestral spirits; nothing Nordic (no draugr, no barrows) and no vanilla ghost
unreviewed [OT A; OB truth]. The strange is something placed and standing, found by the player: nothing appears,
vanishes or changes where the player can see it [MW §6.8].

## HAUNTING

1. **A haunting is the dead person's biography.** The truth names who died, how, what wrong remains, who knows and
   who is accountable; if nobody is accountable, add someone [OT P1; OT M1].
2. **The death is a physical trace the player reads before anyone explains it** (placed things, a body, a site)
   [OT P1; OT M1].
3. **The manifestation is the same at each meeting with one detail that is the clue**: a spirit standing where it
   is placed, a sound or smell people describe, a thing that is always back in its place [OT M1; OT P5].
4. **Three clues in three holders**: a place, a living person, a thing; each solvable alone, sharper together
   [OT M1].
5. **The real stake is the player's judgement of a living person** they have come to half like; the living have a
   reason to hide it [OT P4; OT M1].
6. **The ghost's wish and need differ**; the player's grasp of the gap is the climax [OT P2].
7. **Resolution is a verb**: lay to rest (the bones brought home), bind, release, avenge (a killing). At least two,
   with different costs; one is the blunt, violent one; one makes the living face the truth [OT P3; OT M1].
8. **Something wearing the dead's shape is wrong in one way** a careful player can learn [OT M2].
9. **A community that knows and will not say** answers its topics evasively [OT P6].

## RITE

1. **Three beats**: separation (what is given up), the trial between (an uncertain status, someone who leads the
   rite), incorporation (the new status, marked by a symbol and spoken by a witness) [OT P7].
2. **The rite as data**: its elements, who leads it, its place, its taboo, its words. Its hour is something people
   say, never a gate [OT M3].
3. **The player performs it by acts**: bringing the elements (hand-ins gated on what is held), saying the words (a
   line), and a witness's line marks the new status [OT M3].
4. **A quest branches on which element is missing, wrong or forced** (a stored number); stopping a rite takes the
   same knowledge as performing it [OT P10].
5. **A rite missing its third beat is the root of a haunting**; anyone stuck between statuses shows it in how they
   stand and what they say [OT P8; OT P9].
6. **Rites are Argonian** (the Hist, the naming, ancestors, Sithis, the Kothringi), never borrowed Nordic or
   Imperial forms [OT A].

## WONDER

1. **One image, one rule, a couple of minutes**: off the critical path, never signposted, not repeated in a region;
   no journal entry unless the player acts [OT P11; OT P15; OT M4].
2. **Unexplained in play, consistent in the data**: the record holds the answer even if nobody says it [OT P12].
3. **Familiar ground first**: place it where the player has already walked, so the strangeness registers [OT P13;
   OT M7].
4. **The image is something built or placed**, standing there to be found; reward none, or small and odd [OT M4].

## Daedric and oddity quests

1. **The patron's character is the errand's tone**, delivered first in a flat, ordinary voice; the strange arrives
   second [OT P16; OT P17].
2. **A real moral squeeze, shown not announced**; a refusal that is remembered, not punished [OT M5].
3. **The reward is single, odd, costly and expresses the patron** [OT M5].
4. **Never a fetch-and-kill with a speech at the end** [OT P18; OT A].

## Found text (all four)

Notes and remnants are written by someone in the middle of the predicament, not by an encyclopaedia [OT P14].
