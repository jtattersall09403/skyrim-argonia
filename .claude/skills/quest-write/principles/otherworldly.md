# HAUNTING, RITE, WONDER, and Daedric or oddity quests

Read with `general.md`. Evidence: `docs/research/2026-10-27/quest-shape-otherworldly.md` (methods M1 to M7).
Black Marsh's dead go to the Hist, are pinned by grave-stakes (xul-vaat) and can be ancestral spirits;
nothing Nordic (no draugr, no barrows) and no vanilla ghost unreviewed (0006, 0012).

## HAUNTING

- **A haunting is the dead person's biography.** The truth names who died, how, what wrong remains, who
  knows and who is accountable; if nobody is accountable, add someone.
- **The death scene is a physical trace** the player reads before anyone explains it.
- **The manifestation is the same each time with one variation, which is a clue.**
- **Three clues in three holders:** a place, a living person, a thing; each solvable alone.
- **The real stake is the player's judgement of a living person** they have come to half like; the living
  have a reason to hide it.
- **The ghost's wish and need differ;** the player's grasp of the gap is the climax.
- **Resolution is a verb:** lay to rest, bind, release or avenge. At least two with different costs; one is the
  blunt, violent one; one makes the living face the truth.
- Something wearing the dead's shape is wrong in one way a careful player can learn.

## RITE

- **Three beats:** separation (what is given up), the liminal trial (ambiguous status, a master of
  ceremonies), incorporation (the new status, marked by a symbol and spoken by a witness).
- The rite as data: elements, officiant, place, hour, taboo, words. Quests branch on which element is
  missing, wrong or forced; stopping a rite takes the same knowledge as performing it.
- **A rite missing its third beat is a haunting template;** anyone stuck in the liminal phase has a clock or
  visible decay.
- Rites are Argonian (the Hist, the naming, ancestors, Sithis, the Kothringi), never borrowed Nordic or
  Imperial forms.

## WONDER

- **One image, one rule, about two minutes,** off the critical path, never signposted, not repeated in a
  region. No journal entry unless the player acts.
- **Unexplained in play, consistent in the data:** the record holds the answer even if nobody says it.
- **Familiarity first:** place it on ground the player already knows, so the strangeness registers.
- Reward none, or small and odd.

## Daedric and oddity quests

- **The patron's character is the errand's tone,** delivered first in a flat, ordinary voice; the strange
  arrives second.
- **A real moral squeeze,** shown not announced; a refusal path that is remembered, not punished.
- **The reward is single, odd, costly and expresses the patron.**
- Never a fetch-and-kill with a speech at the end.

## Found text (all four)

- Notes and remnants are written by someone in the middle of the predicament, not by an encyclopaedia.
