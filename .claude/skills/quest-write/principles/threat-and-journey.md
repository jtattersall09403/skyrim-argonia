# PREDATOR, CONTAMINATION, EXPEDITION

Read with `general.md`. Evidence: `docs/research/2026-10-27/quest-shape-threat-and-journey.md`, including its
ten-step method for directions without markers.

## PREDATOR

- **A puzzle about knowledge before a fight.** Write the creature's habit first (time, tide, bait, territory);
  the fight is the last stage and the clues decide how well the player enters it.
- **Traces, account, lair:** traces in the world, the community's account with one wrong belief, and the lair
  that confirms it. At least one clue is in the world, not in dialogue.
- **Every clue changes a choice** (bait, weapon, hour, route, ally); record "clue, where, the choice it
  changes, who else knows" and drop any clue with no choice.
- **Say why it is unsolved,** and declare a fair twist: the wrong culprit, a provoked creature, a community
  feeding it, a solution that is not killing, a remedy that is a trade.
- **A non-combat resolution** whenever the creature is above the band the player is likely to be ready for.
- **A weak sample** of the threat (a scout of the pack) the player meets unprepared and survives; it teaches
  the habit.

## CONTAMINATION

- **Source, spread, sign, cure,** all in the truth. The place looks wrong before anyone says so.
- **"First ill, last well":** an order of households in the truth, consistent with exactly one spread path.
- **A misdiagnosis beat:** two plausible sources, one confirmed by a test the player can run (upstream and
  downstream water, an animal); each faction favours the wrong cause that suits it.
- **No hidden timer.** A visible clock or a fixed ladder of consequences that fire on the player's acts or
  on visible delay (another house sickens a day after the second clue).
- The cure needs gathering, travel or a trade (often a nested EXPEDITION); the changed world persists.

## EXPEDITION

- **Rumour, route, gate, prize, return,** each a stage.
- **Preparation hints from three sources:** someone who went before, the land at the threshold, and a trader
  (what they will not sell without a warning).
- **Approach by triangles:** a large landmark at the far end, a medium obstruction midway that hides the goal,
  small obstacles for tempo; never the goal on a straight line from the start.
- **The point of no return is flagged twice:** in dialogue before and in the place itself.
- **Failure is not only death:** lost cargo, a drowned boat, a companion left behind, a closed route.
- **The return changes something** in the world or a person; remains of an earlier party carry the warning.
- **Leads chain:** each find names where the next can be found, because players read out of order.

## Record additions (`shapeData`)

- PREDATOR: `habit`, `whyUnsolved`, `twist`, `clueChoices` (clue, where, choice, who else knows),
  `nonCombat`, `weakSample`.
- CONTAMINATION: `source`, `spread`, `sign`, `cure`, `firstIllLastWell`, `misdiagnosis`, `ladder`.
- EXPEDITION: `rumour`, `route` (directions block), `gate`, `prize`, `return`, `prepHints` (3),
  `pointOfNoReturn`, `nonLethalFailure`.
