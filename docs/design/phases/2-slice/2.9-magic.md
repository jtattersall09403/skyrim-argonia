# 2.9 — Magic

**Goal.** The whole magic system in its scaled shape, proved on a
representative set. Every Morrowind effect and spell is authored; timed
effects run on 2.8's stack; a spell sits in a hand like a weapon and is
cast Skyrim's way; one config-driven visual system renders every effect
family; the slice's tree-minders sell spells, blessings and cures by
their own skills; a non-seller teaches; the Teeth of Sithis casts back;
one bite carries a disease the cure answers; Soultrap fills a gem. One
spell per school and one per visual family are built with clips,
visuals and screens, and the recipe that built them makes every later
effect a data row
([0146](../../old-decisions/0146-magic-ships-whole-skyrim-hands-one-effect-family-system-folk-sellers.md)).

## Deliverables

### a. The catalogue and the stack

- **`packages/magic`.** The effect catalogue on the normalised set
  (standard 18, world/98 updated): every Morrowind effect, mined from
  UESP into a dossier first and tuned to §123, typed with op, field,
  school, ranges (self, touch, target), base cost, visual family, colour
  and flags (`rareFortify`, `power`, `traversal`). Every Morrowind spell
  authored against it, tier and cost by the §123 formula. Fire, frost and
  shock resist as separate fields. Soultrap and the soul-gem items
  (petty to grand) are rows here.
- **Timed effects.** 2.8's stack takes timed entries: duration,
  magnitude, source (spell, scroll, potion, blessing, disease, bound
  item), the Morrowind stacking rule, expiry, dispel. Potions and scrolls
  apply through it (2.9b makes them).
- **One disease.** `diseased` from the ladder as a staged timed entry,
  carried by one slice bite (giant leech or bog-dog), Argonian resistance
  applied, cured by a tree-minder's cure or a Cure Common Disease cast.
- **Known spells.** One record on the player, and on any caster, of the
  spells known: written by purchase, informal teaching and the starting
  spells of the compiled sheet, by nothing else. A save family beside the
  stack's timed entries.
- **Powers** once a day on the same stack, the day from `world-time`.

### b. Casting and the hands

- **Hands.** Each hand holds a weapon, a spell, a torch or a shield; the
  equipment record gains a spell hand-content kind beside the weapon
  classes; the same spell in both hands dual-casts (magnitude and cost
  up by Skyrim's ratios tuned to §123). A spell is equipped in a hand the
  way a weapon is, through one hand API, from the inventory and the magic
  window alike. The quick belt holds hand-equippable things (a weapon, a
  shield, a torch, a spell) and hand swaps; a press rotates the thing into
  a hand, displacing what was there. Nothing casts from the belt: casting
  is the hand's use button.
- **The verb.** Hold to charge, release to fire; stream spells run while
  held and drain per second. Self, aimed, ward, stream and dual map to
  the five sourced cast styles, played as upper-body layers over the
  stance on `PlayerMovementController`, never ecctrl. A hit that breaks
  poise interrupts the cast; a hit that does not, does not. Cast time
  from the tier table, castability by the §123 gate, magicka through the
  pool.
- **Levitation** as a movement mode beneath the controller, with
  duration, speed and the interruption rule from the record. Water
  walking is authored, not built.
- **Bound dagger.** A timed effect that equips a conjured dagger through
  2.3's equipment and 2.7's dagger moveset and unequips on expiry.
- **Soultrap.** A timed effect on a creature; its death with an empty gem
  of sufficient size in the inventory fills the gem (2.9b spends it).

### c. Visuals and clips

- **The effect-family system.** One config-driven set of families in
  `packages/magic` rendering (projectile, hit flash, self glow, area
  ring, stream, light orb, summon flash), each parameterised by colour,
  sprite, size and a sound hook, on the fire system's pattern; quality
  bands and the phone budget by design (standard 16). Sprites and shapes
  are mined from the vault's magic and effects pools, with source, hash
  and credit in the same change; no particle mesh is converted wholesale.
- **Clips.** The five Skyrim cast styles (aimed, stream, self, ward,
  dual), each hand, charge, hold and release, sourced from vanilla through
  the shared-rig pipeline with credits, each with a `visual:check`
  scenario.
- **The recipe.** A written, tested path from "a new effect row" to a
  rendered effect and from "a new family" to its config, proved by adding
  the slice set through it and by adding one effect outside the set as
  its test.

### d. Sellers, teachers, casters

- **Sellers.** The two tree-minders take the `spells`, `blessing` and
  `cure` service words. Stock is a pick of 10 to 24 of the catalogue
  spells castable from the seller's own compiled skills, by role and
  school (0148), plus a `signatureSpells` list on their record; `rareFortify` rows never reach a stock (the integrity gate reds
  it). Buying writes known spells and flips the purchase row's `applied`;
  blessing and cure apply their effects the same way.
- **Scrolls** in general traders' stock through 2.6's restock and in loot
  tables; a scroll casts its spell once, unbounded by skill.
- **Informal teaching.** A `teach` service kind on one non-seller slice
  person: a topic that writes one spell to known spells, priced through
  the price contract or a favour
  ([0141](../../old-decisions/0141-one-price-contract-coin-goods-or-a-favour.md)).
- **Enemy casters.** The caster seam scored for any actor whose compiled
  sheet and known spells allow it; the Teeth's Nisswo and priest cast
  with stock by the seller rule; the brain weighs cast against weapon
  intents, keeps distance for aimed spells and is interrupted on poise
  break like the player.

### e. Screens

On 2.2's system, each with ui-lab fixtures on the three viewports: a
spell equipped in a hand and waiting on a belt slot (the school symbol); the cast charge
meter on the reticle; the spell-seller screen (a buy-only list on the
barter shape with the blessing and cure rows beside it); the disease
line on the stats sheet and the "disease caught" notice; known spells
on the sheet; the three dialogue service strings and the `teach` topic;
the active-effects strip fed by the real stack.

## Wide loads

Every Morrowind effect and spell authored; every cast style sourced;
every visual family built. Only the slice set gets per-spell tuning.

## The slice set

One spell per school and one per family, chosen at kickoff from the
authored catalogue and listed in `2.9-loops/README.md`: Destruction (a
projectile with a hit flash), Restoration (a self glow and Cure Common
Disease), Alteration (Levitate), Illusion (a light orb), Mysticism (an
area ring: Detect or Soultrap), Conjuration (Bound Dagger with the summon
flash), plus one stream spell and one ward so every cast style is cast.

## Loops

Many small loops, each on one thing, each fast, each with its own eyes,
run in parallel from the first turn ([loops](../loops.md) § Eyes ride
every iteration; [0144](../../old-decisions/0144-progression-ships-whole-one-stat-schema-compiled-for-every-actor.md)
item 6). Every loop has a survey in `2.9-loops/`, written before its
first measure, and closes when its number is green and its reader finds
nothing. No end-to-end walk runs inside a loop; the owner plays what
every loop has already closed.

**Function loops**, one each:

| Loop | Measure | Eyes |
|---|---|---|
| the catalogue | unit tests on every effect and spell row against `magic.json` and the §123 worked examples; the integrity gate on ids, references and `rareFortify` sources | none |
| each slice spell | a headless cast changes the right field by the right magnitude for the right duration, stacks and expires by the rule, round-trips through save | a sandbox still at the moment of effect |
| the verb | charge, release, stream drain, dual-cast cost and magnitude, cast time by tier, castability gate, interruption on poise break, all headless | a strip of the cast |
| the hands and belt | equip a spell in each hand, swap, dual, belt select, on keyboard, pad and touch, headless | a still of the HUD hands and belt |
| levitation | the controller mode: rise, travel, expiry, landing on the real mesh, the interruption rule | `visual:check` and a strip |
| the enemy caster | the headless combat drive against the Teeth: casts chosen, distance kept, interrupted on poise break | a strip of the fight |
| sellers and teacher | the stock rule from each tree-minder's compiled sheet, `rareFortify` excluded, the purchase flips `applied`, the teach topic writes one spell | none |
| the disease | bite, resist roll, stages, cure by purchase and by cast, headless | a still of the strip and the sheet line |
| Soultrap and the bound dagger | trap, death, gem filled by size; dagger equipped, used, expired | a still of each |

**Visual loops**, one each, every iteration shot from the working tree:

| Loop | Measure | Eyes |
|---|---|---|
| each cast style | `visual:check` scenario green (timing, contact with the stance, both hands) | a strip judged by a `reader` |
| each visual family | the family renders at every quality band within the phone budget | a `look` sheet or sandbox still per family, judged by a `reader` against a checklist written with the family |
| each screen | ui-lab on the three viewports, light and dark, pad focus order | the ui-lab screenshots judged by a `reader` |

**Seam loops**, one per hand-off, each a headless drive of the whole
chain plus one still at its end: buy → known spells → equipped in a
hand → cast; taught → known spells; bite → disease → cure; Soultrap → death →
gem; bound dagger → equip → expiry; levitate → mode → land; cast → poise
break → interrupt; save → load of timed effects and known spells.

The planner reads each loop's timeline at every check-in and speeds the
slow step before the next turn.

## Acceptance

- Every loop above closed: numbers green, readers clear, surveys on disk.
- The recipe adds one effect outside the slice set end to end in one
  step, as its test.
- The owner buys a spell from a tree-minder, is taught one, casts at a
  creature, at themselves and at the Teeth's casters, levitates, catches
  and cures the bite.

## Polish routing

Crafting with the stack is 2.9b; spellmaking, summoning creatures and
Hist communion are 4.5. Effect beauty is `P3`; a missing effect family,
a spell that cannot be learned or a cast that plays no clip is a blocker.
