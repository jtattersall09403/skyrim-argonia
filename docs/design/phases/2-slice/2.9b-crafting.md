# 2.9b — Crafting: alchemy, smithing, enchanting at their stations

**Goal.** The player can make things: brew potions and poisons, forge
and temper weapons and armour, enchant items, at stations in the slice
places, from ingredients and materials the slice places provide, by the
formulas in [world/76](../../world/76-stats-progression.md) §124
([0144](../../old-decisions/0144-progression-ships-whole-one-stat-schema-compiled-for-every-actor.md)).

## Starting state

- The design is complete: alchemy on Morrowind's formulas with a brew
  that always succeeds and skill deciding how good it is, ingredient
  effects visible at skill 15/30/45/60; smithing as repair plus tempering
  to three grades (effective skill 25/55/80, each about +8 %) plus
  crafting up to material tier `1 + floor(effSkill/14)`; enchanting by
  learning from disenchanting; crafting reads effective skills and
  attributes, and the fortify loop is priced by rarity (§123,
  [0144](../../old-decisions/0144-progression-ships-whole-one-stat-schema-compiled-for-every-actor.md) item 7).
  The brew magnitude formula is coded in
  `packages/game-core/src/stats/crafting.ts` and still reads base
  attributes; this chunk switches it to effective values.
- 2.3 gave items `condition` and `materialFrom`, and 39 ingredient and 9
  potion rows in `world/sources/registries/items.json` with no effects and
  no use; 2.6 built the repair and recharge services and the smith; 2.8
  built the skill-use store, the effect stack and the station hook
  (`onSkillUse`, `packages/scene/src/interact/session.ts`).
- 2.9 built `packages/magic` ([0146](../../old-decisions/0146-magic-ships-whole-skyrim-hands-one-effect-family-system-folk-sellers.md)):
  300 effects and 876 spells with `rareFortify` flags and the integrity
  gate; `castSpell` (`src/apply.ts`) onto the timed stack
  (`game-core/src/stats/timed.ts`, whose sources name `potion`, though no
  code writes one yet); 13 scroll items that cast once (`src/scroll.ts`);
  ten soul gems (empty and filled, petty to grand, the `soulGem` field)
  filled by Soultrap from creature souls (`src/soultrap.ts`,
  `data/creatures.json`); bound items (`item.bound-dagger`,
  `src/boundItem.ts`); and the folk sellers' stock rule
  (`packages/economy`).
- No station, recipe, enchantment or resource-node record exists; no
  slice place has a crafting socket filled; no potion can be drunk.

## Read

world/76 §124 and §127; 2.3, 2.8 and 2.9 § Deliverables; the
[place skill](../../../.claude/skills/place/SKILL.md) for sockets;
[20-world-provisions](../../quests/20-world-provisions.md) when placing.

## Deliverables

- Records on the normalised set (standard 18, world/98 updated):
  stations (alchemy apparatus by quality, forge, workbench, grindstone,
  enchanting table), recipes and material tiers, ingredients with their
  four effects by record id ([0070](../../old-decisions/0070-vegetation-and-dressing-read-the-record.md)),
  resource nodes where the slice's ecology grows them. Every ingredient
  or soul source that fortifies a crafting skill or a crafting input
  attribute is flagged `rareFortify`: few placed sources, high price, no
  restock of the rare ones; an integrity check reds a flagged effect
  with a common source.
- Alchemy: brew from ingredients at an apparatus; poisons and oils
  applied to a weapon or arrow coat the next hit (the §124 delivery
  rule).
- Smithing: forge from materials up to the skill's tier; temper to a
  grade; the station mend already reports use.
- Enchanting: disenchant to learn, enchant with a learned effect; the
  charge comes from a filled soul gem (2.9 built the gem items and
  Soultrap's capture), spent by size against the §123 tiers; recharge
  from a gem at the table or through 2.6's recharge service.
- Every slice place with a works or an inn carries its stations placed on
  real sockets; the slice's ingredient and material supply is real
  (gathered, bought or looted).
- The crafting screens on every viewport and input; each craft reports
  skill use into 2.8's store.

## Wide loads

Every recipe, every ingredient's effects, every material tier.

Daedric gear (11 recipes: ebony ingot, daedra heart, leather strips; Skyrim COBJ shape) and falmer gear (5 recipes: chaurus chitin, leather strips; Skyrim has no falmer forge recipe, only chitin to improve it) are forged from ingredients and a misc piece the item table now holds: the recipe gate takes misc or ingredient inputs and tiers 1..8. Where hearts and chitin drop is 2.9b-c6's.

## Loops

One per station kind (alchemy, smithing, enchanting), each a formula test
beside the screen on the three viewports, plus one seam loop per
hand-off (gather → inventory → station; craft → skill rise; poison →
next hit). Surveys in `2.9b-loops/`, written before the first measure.

## Acceptance

- Formula tests green against §124 worked examples; every station in
  every slice place reachable and working in a headless drive; every
  craft round-trips through save; the reader's sweep of the screens
  clear.

## Owner check

"Gather, brew, forge, temper and enchant one thing each; what was unclear
or dull?"

## Rulings

[0150](../../old-decisions/0150-crafting-ships-whole-the-slice-climbs-its-first-rungs.md):
the whole engine ships, the slice climbs its first rungs (common
materials from the village smith, high-tier metals loot and purchase only (import points), one rare
fortify ingredient with one source); a soul caps a constant effect's
size as a share of its range; brewing is Morrowind's picking with a
by-effect filter; three player journeys with a pod strip each close the
phase's loops.

## Polish routing

Cooking, spellmaking and summoning creatures are 4.5. A
station that cannot be used is a blocker.

## Outcome

contract (`types.ts`), 7 stations, 154 generated recipes over 8 material
tiers, 6 resource-node kinds plus the nirnroot, and the alchemy, smithing
