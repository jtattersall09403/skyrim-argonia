One row per hand-off. The number is the headless magic session
(`packages/scene/src/interact/magic-session.test.ts`), which drives the
host binding itself (`packages/scene/src/ui/playerMagic.ts`, composed onto
`CombatRuntime` by `useMagicBinding.tsx` in the sandbox `CombatScene` and
the studio `studioFight`) over the real catalogue rows, the real item rows,
the real creature records and 2.9d's economy calls. The eyes are two
sandbox stills shot from the working tree and judged by a `reader` child:
the gem filled and the moment of a Fireball hit (`lanes/2.9/b3-shots/`).
