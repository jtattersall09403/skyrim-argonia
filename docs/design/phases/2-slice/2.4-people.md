# 2.4 — People and creatures

**Goal.** Every slice place, and the wild between them, has its life: the
people and creatures its record says are there, each one named and unlike
any other in the province, each doing what Elder Scrolls townsfolk and
beasts do over a day, and the same rules carrying a town of three hundred
later without being rebuilt. One shape for every living thing that every
later chunk extends and nobody re-invents.

## What the owner decided

The why is in the record each line links.

- **It depends on the place.** Who is there, how many, why, and whether
  they greet you or attack you comes from the place record: its occupants,
  its why-here, its promise to the player, its stance and its danger band
  ([catalogue README](../../../../world/sources/catalogue/README.md),
  [20-world-provisions](../../quests/20-world-provisions.md) §danger).
  The place record is the source of truth; the roster is regenerated from
  it and gated, never edited by hand.
- **Skyrim-style days, kept coarse.** People walk their routes on the
  path network when a player can see them and are swapped between spots
  when nobody can, as Skyrim itself does. Two to four blocks a day, one or
  two chores each. Nobody runs a timetable. [world/72](../../world/72-navigation-ai.md)
  states the rule: walk when seen, teleport only when unseen.
- **One village clock, pinned to the lamps.** The lamp hours and the
  sleeping hours already exist
  ([lighting](../../../packages/game-core/src/settlement/lighting.ts),
  [0123](../../old-decisions/0123-interiors-are-lit-by-their-sources-windows-and-beams.md) item 8).
  The common day: rise at half past five when the sleeping hours end; at
  work by half past six when the lamps go out; a midday break where the
  place has somewhere to take one; home when the lamps come on at half
  past five in the evening; the evening at home (sit, eat, tend the fire);
  candles out and bed at ten. Shops shut by the keeper going home and the
  door locking. Someone who loiters in a shop is never thrown out: the
  keeper leaves, trading is refused until morning.
- **Exceptions by role are the only per-person authoring.** The inn and
  its keeper up late with the door open; the watch in shifts so somebody
  is always out; fishers and divers on the light; a camp with a tended
  fire. The list is open: every place's review pass may add a role.
- **Every work spot names an activity.** A matching animation plays
  where the rig has one; where none exists the activity is composed from
  moves the game already has (a pearl diver dives, swims about, surfaces,
  repeats). We make no art; a gap is sourced or composed, never left.
- **Everyone is named, at the depth the cast ladder allows**
  ([35-cast](../../quests/35-cast.md) §52): principals get a full sheet,
  texture people get exactly one line and no history. Names by the cast
  rules, never reused ([86](../../quests/86-never-reuse-names.md)).
- **Every person in the province is visually unique.** Skyrim's premade
  characters supply the looks now; the character editor of Part 3 supplies
  unlimited looks for Part 4. The check runs over the whole roster.
- **Hostile places attack.** A hostile person who notices you raises the
  alarm, draws and comes, leashed to their ground, using the enemy brain
  the combat sandbox already has. 2.7 replaces the brain and adds loot,
  movesets and death; it never replaces the person, the camp's day or the
  alarm.
- **Creatures and ecology live here**, inside the places and in the wild
  between them, on the same shape, clock and loops. A creature's den and
  territory are a person's home and work. Thoroughly grounded in the
  ecology research; variety and difficulty follow the fixed danger band
  ([0004](../../old-decisions/0004-fixed-difficulty-constraint.md)). The
  ecology table is province-wide; placement is the slice's places, routes
  and crossings only.
- **Helstrom is out** (paused to 4.1b). The sixteen published Part 1 places
  are in. What is built must carry Helstrom's three hundred people unchanged.
- **Packs are packs.** An authored pack spawns whole and behaves as a
  pack (varied, sensible pack AI: formation, cohesion, lag, alarm), never
  one animal a day.
- **Meals sitting down, never in step.** People sit to eat on the chairs
  and benches already in the places; a seat is added only as a layer
  ([0140](../../old-decisions/0140-layers-on-a-built-place-never-rebuild-it.md)),
  never by republishing or rebuilding anything else. Routine times carry
  per-person jitter, so nobody stops for lunch at the same instant;
  shops, inns and watches keep their hours.
- **Houses and shops lock as Skyrim does**, by the common day and the
  role exceptions, in the studio and the game alike.
- **Factions cut to Morrowind's size.** The registry holds the 32
  real factions. A local group is merged into the real faction it answers
  to, with a written reason, or is a label on its place; none is a faction
  of its own. A villager's
  default faction is none.
- **"Essential" is the doom-warning tier** of
  [40-factions](../../quests/40-factions.md) §99–115: none, warned, doom.
  Nobody is unkillable; the warning is the protection.
- **No followers.** [world/76](../../world/76-stats-progression.md) §115
  stands.
- **Noticing and standing stay here.** The one "who notices you" service
  and faction standing as live data are built in this chunk.

## a. The review pass and the record

The first job, about five minutes of an agent's reading per place, and the
only authoring of who people are.

- For each of the sixteen places, read the record and settle: the
  headcount; every person's name, depth tier, race and sex, trade, faction
  (the real one behind any local group, with the reason), work spot and
  activity, home and bed, role exceptions to the common day, doom-warning
  tier, hostility, what they wear and look like; everything a quest, a
  service or the story needs the place to provide
  ([0104](../../old-decisions/0104-the-world-is-one-normalised-record-set-promises-are-fulfilled-by-placed-interactables.md)).
  The same pass reads the place's creatures from its record.
- The sixteen proposals go to the owner as one plain-English check before
  anyone is placed (headcount and roles per place, one bullet each).
- The place record is updated; the roster is regenerated from it; the
  gate fails whenever a record and its roster drift apart again.
- The record shape in `packages/contracts`: stable id, race and sex, look,
  statblock and archetype on the D-ladder, faction ids and ranks,
  hostility, `Fight/Flee/Alarm`, home place and spots, day blocks and role
  exceptions, activity per spot, services offered (record words from the
  kind table, `packages/economy` `service-kinds.json`), dialogue set id, crime
  standing slot, doom-warning tier, `schemaVersion`. Empty-but-typed slots
  for what 2.5–2.11 fill. Creatures on the same shape with a den, a
  territory and the statblock class.
- The faction registry merged down to the real factions, every merge with
  its reason, the local-group label on the place.

## b. The day

- Spawn from the record at the spot; walk between spots on the path
  network when seen, swap when unseen; the wait, sleep, ferry and door
  screens are catch-up moments. A person stays late only if a player never
  takes their eyes off them.
- The path network: a thin `NavService` (recast tiled bake from kit and
  terrain collision through the world compiler, version pinned); humanoid
  and large-creature classes; the surface-swim network for divers and
  water creatures ([world/72](../../world/72-navigation-ai.md)).
- Activities at spots: matched loops, composed routines where none
  matches; the fallback (standing, a small wander) reported, never silent.
- Doors lock and unlock by the common day and the role exceptions; window
  glow follows the bedtime rule.
- The notice service as the one API every living thing consults (view
  cone, light, noise, line of sight, scent for beasts; cross-check §4
  formulas), consumed by 2.7 enemies and 2.10 sneak. Hostile people and
  creatures react through the sandbox brain: alarm, draw, come, leash.
- `FactionStanding` runtime on the registry ids; the reaction matrix as
  data.

## c. Ecology

Not a side table: a researched system with its own doc, written as
`docs/world/74-ecology.md` and updated with every row.

- **The province ecology table**, one row per creature: what it is, the
  ground or water it lives on, its regions and danger bands, how many per
  stretch and how they group, its day and night, what it eats and what
  eats it, how it notices and reacts (fight, flee, alarm, scent), why it is
  here in the lore. Every row grounded in the lore dossiers or a UESP
  extract recorded as a dossier first under the era policy; the Murkmire
  keep list bounds what we can show.
- **Variety and difficulty follow the danger band.** D1 managed marsh is
  mostly harmless and skittish; D3 and D4 carry what hunts; what a stretch
  holds never scales to the player. Variety per stretch so that two
  crossings of the same band in different regions feel different.
- **Placement rules**: dens and territories laid on the path and swim
  networks from the table and the region's band; a den answers "why
  here?" like a place does (water, cover, prey, ruin); nothing placed
  inside a D0 settlement; creature contents never exceed the place's band.
- **Models and animations by creature type.** Every creature in the
  table gets its own properly sourced model and clip set (idle, move,
  swim where it swims, notice, attack, flee, die, and its activities),
  never made here: the vault first, then mods with the owner's key, the
  Murkmire keep list with bone-name parity as the first check; source,
  hash, credit and a sourcing-register row in the same change
  ([world/90](../../world/90-asset-strategy.md)). A creature the table
  needs and nothing can supply is redesigned around what exists, not
  left unanimated. This chunk owns creature clips: every creature placed in the slice is complete when it is placed.
- **Scope**: the slice's sixteen places, its two routes and the regions a
  slice player crosses between them. Every other wilderness is a Part 4
  packet reading the same table.

## Acceptance

- Every `npc`, `idle`, `encounter` and `fauna` socket in the sixteen places
  bound and spawning; the gate fails on an unbound socket, a person with
  no bed, activity or real faction, a roster that disagrees with its
  record, or two people who look alike anywhere in the province.
- A headless day: every slice person and creature lives its day on the
  path and swim networks without a stuck frame, and the wait-screen,
  shop-loiter, shift-change and hostile-camp scenarios behave as decided.
- The notice-service unit tests reproduce the cross-check's worked
  examples.
- The faction registry at about Morrowind's size with every merge
  reasoned; `world/74-ecology.md` and world/72 corrected and current.
- A frame-cost number for forty walking people on the phone budget.

The measured frame cost and the eyes-loop results are in `lanes/2.4/`;
what the capture tool still misses (wide roof branch, lattice occluders,

## Loops

Three loops, cheapest first, as [loops](../loops.md) and 2.2 run them:
measure everything, diagnose, one fix batch, re-measure; the planner judges
good enough; a surviving finding gets one more pass with a corrected
diagnosis and never a third without a written reason. No loop boots the
studio or a GPU here; the eyes loop runs on the pod.

1. **Paper loop** (seconds, in the gate): the record-and-roster checks
   above, uniqueness of looks, tiers written to the right depth, factions
   real.
2. **Clock loop** (under a minute, no browser): one script runs the
   slice's people and creatures through a full day at speed on the real
   networks, no renderer; reports anyone stuck, late, routeless, or in the
   open at a block change; runs the scenarios (wait-screen catch-up,
   loiter until night, watch handover, walk into each hostile camp at noon
   and at night, a day of wild creatures along each route). Runs after
   every fix.
3. **Eyes loop** (minutes, on the pod its lane boots under
   [0119](../../old-decisions/0119-runpod-is-the-gpu-lane.md)): a small people sandbox in the style
   of the combat sandbox, one place loaded, clock set to four moments
   (just after waking, midday, just after the lamps come on, deep night),
   stills by the headless browser the UI lab uses; plus captured
   sequences as frame strips for the named moments (keeper shutting up and
   walking home, the diver's routine, sitting to eat, lying down at ten, a
   watch handover, a composed activity, a creature hunting, a hostile
   noticing you). A `reader` judges stills against the five owner
   questions and strips for whether the motion reads right, then one free
   pass per place for anything that feels off. Riverwalk and the Opening
   Work Camp first, then every slice place once.

One priced RunPod boot at the end, under the thirty-minute cap, only for
the frame-cost number ([0119](../../old-decisions/0119-runpod-is-the-gpu-lane.md)).

## Owner check

Only after the planner has every bar green. Two places, Riverwalk and the
Opening Work Camp; four moments each by studio link with the clock set.
Five questions: is everyone the record says is here, here? Is each one
doing their trade? Did I see somebody walk somewhere? Did the shop shut
and the keeper go home? Does anyone look like anyone else? One deliberate
test: wait in the shop until night without the wait screen and say what
happened. One route walked at night: what did I meet, and did it fit the
band?

## Polish routing

An empty socket, a stuck person or creature, a hostile who waves, a twin
look, or a dark house with lit windows is a blocker. Idle variety beyond
the named activities and clothing breadth beyond uniqueness are `P2`; a
record field later consumers would need is `P1`.

## Gotchas

- Nothing downstream defines a second living-thing shape; dialogue (2.5),
  combat (2.7), crime (2.10) and quests (2.11) extend this record.
- This chunk owns creature clips and habitat and territory data; 2.7
  makes them fight ([0142](../../old-decisions/0142-combat-is-met-everywhere-hostile-places-roaming-bands-and-answered-violence.md)).
- Lore for names and roles: the cast rules, never invented freely; lore
  for creatures: the dossiers, never a Skyrim bestiary by default.
- Helstrom's people are 4.1b; nothing here may need a different approach
  for three hundred.
