# 2.10b — Indoor combat and people's movement

**Goal.** Combat works in every interior as it does outside: a guard
resisted indoors fights; a hostile placed in a tower, crypt or cellar
finds the player (when they have detected them per the detection/sneak/stealth service);
closes, strikes; searches and gives up per stealth etc; the player's
blows, bow, magic, sneak attacks, death and wake run the same code in a
cell as on the surface. Fighting indoors is the Elder Scrolls dungeon;
the combat phase left it out and the owner ruled it required (2026-10-22,
found by 2.10-fE boot 11 pass 2, burglary frames 0202-0210). The same
navigation fixes people's movement everywhere: people use doors (a
routine or a pursuit crosses a load door), and in settlements their
*routines* (note this is different from other types of movement like crime reaction and combat)
follow the paths and roads where one exists rather than a
straight line, and they no longer walk into furniture and stick
(owner, 2026-10-22).

## Starting state

- Interior navmeshes already exist: `bake_nav.py --interiors` bakes 24 slice
  cells into `apps/world-studio/public/nav/interiors/` (about 518 KB gzip,
  60 s), `PeopleLayer.tsx` loads them and routines use them for the inside
  leg of a walk (`people/service.ts`). Combat, search, crime reaction and
  the guard's custody walk do not: hostile spawns in a cell are dropped
  (`hostileSpawns.ts` `combatHostile`, the filter in `hostileEncounter.ts`),
  `walkCrime` hands a person to combat only outdoors, and a person's crime
  reaction walks `straight` inside a cell (`crimeDrive.ts`). `nav_gate.mjs`
  checks only exterior places, so a stale interior bake passes. Measured
  numbers, file:line and the lane split: lanes/2.10b-research/report.md.
- The outdoor path: `PeopleNav` (`service.ts`) is the superset interface;
  `SteerNav` and `reactionNavOf` derive from it; cell navs are in cell frame
  (shift by `originM`). The 2.7 enemy step and movesets, `stealthStep.ts`.
- Interiors are linked furnished cells from the plugin data
  ([0114](../../old-decisions/0114-the-plugin-data-is-the-interior-manifest.md));
  no interior bundle carries a hostile socket: a cell hostile comes from the
  place roster's `interiorCell` spot (`cast.ts`).

## Deliverables

- **Research first** (done: lanes/2.10b-research/report.md): the baked
  interior navmesh is the navigation; no grid; the interior publish owns
  the bake; a gate on stale interior bakes and arrival-to-hostile
  reachability joins `nav_gate.mjs`.
- One navigation interface for both worlds: the enemy step, the search,
  the crime reaction and the guard's custody walk take a `nav` whatever
  the cell; interiors supply theirs from the baked record; the province
  keeps its own.
- Hostile spawns and answered violence in cells: the drops removed; the
  resisted watch fights indoors; a hostile placed in an interior socket
  is met there (records first, standard 18, world/98 updated).
- People use doors: a routine whose next socket is through a load door
  walks to the door, crosses, and continues; in a fight a hostile follows
  through a load door only where the record allows (a watch pursues; a
  crypt dweller holds its cell).
- Settlement routines follow the place's paths and roads (the published
  boardwalks, runs and road layer) where one joins the two sockets, a
  straight line only off-path; a person blocked by furniture or another
  body steps round it or re-plans, never stands pushing (measured: no
  person stuck more than 2 s in a day's routine on any slice place,
  headless).
- Camera indoors in combat - should already be fine as it works for general player
  movement indoors.
- Checks: headless seam scenes (resist indoors → the watch fights; a
  cellar hostile closes from the far room; search and give-up indoors;
  death and wake in a cell), each filmed as a seam strip and read; the
  2.7 combat `visual:check` groups unchanged; a journey the owner plays:
  rob the store at night, resist indoors, fight, and clear later.

  combat tests green.

## Polish routing

blocker.
