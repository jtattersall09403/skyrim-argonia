## Outcome

Every ruling in the table above is built and recorded; lanes a–x, reports in
`lanes/2.9x/`. What the round settled beyond the table:

- **No dice.** No roll decides whether a blow lands or how hard. The only
  chance draws are the critical-hit chance, enemy variety (heavy choice,
  strafe side, backstep, decision jitter), archer aim spread, creature
  wander and Morrowind's Spell Absorption percentage, all on one seeded
  source per walk (lane n). A new chance draw is an owner call, never a
  mechanic. Recorded in world/76 §121.1.
- **One driver.** The studio and the game share `walkDriver.tsx`; the
  Explorer locomotion is deleted; `fight=`, the preset picker and every
  dev-panel fight option are gone (0149, lane y); draw and sheathe are
  one step saved on the inventory (lanes b, k, u). Proven headless and
  on a pod; the touch device × action matrix is `2.9x-loops/touch.md`.
- **The player journey** (y, y2): one loop (equip, draw, fight with every
  control, off-hand spell, cast, belt switch, sheathe, rest) scripted as
  data in `2.9x-loops/journeys/`, one interpreter (`journey.ts`), proven
  headless in the scene and the game and on a pod by the `journey=` token
  with strips read step by step (`2.9x-loops/journey.md`). Journey probes
  run only when a lane asks, never in check, CI or a regular gate. Lock-on
  now picks the nearest foe within 20 m (owner call on the number).
- **Phone terrain** (tb): the ground shader needed 283 fragment uniform
  vectors against Android's 256, so the program never linked and no
  terrain drew; packed into one vec4 array, the gate's mobile cap fixed
  1024 → 256 and its fixture grown to 48 materials. An iPhone is not
  explained by this; the owner tests on a Honor Magic V5, Android, so this is the whole cause. The AO footprint atlas over
  8192 is a P1 row.
- **Touch combat.** Built to the owner's spec (`lanes/2.9x/owner-touch-spec.md`):
  Draw in the drawer, hand buttons whenever something is in hand, the
  drawer animates both ways with reduced motion honoured, the roll works
  on key, pad and touch. LOCK (tap, swipe to switch foe) and GRIP
  (two-hand) are planner calls where the spec was silent.
- **Studio walk blockers.** Three causes found and fixed at source: a
  null body on `setAngvel` (q), coincident band spawns giving Rapier NaN
  capsules (t), and every hostile mounting as a full fighter at any
  distance (v: wake at 150 m, sleep past 175 m, dormant means no body,
  mesh or step). Walk median fell from 118–143 ms to 21–35 ms; the Naga
  road stays GPU-bound (~1000 draws), a perf row, not this round's.
- **Level pace** (d, 0147): level 2 at 20–30 min, ranks per level 1..8,
  banked at the first rest; birthsign chosen in the Stats lab now.
- **Spell sellers** (i, 0148): stock by rule, 10–24 by skill, 15 at 40.
- **Bound weapons** (h): daedric dagger mesh by `bound_records`, shimmer
  in `bound.json`.
- **Save and reload.** The 2.9 claim was an in-memory snapshot round trip
  only; no storage backend exists and the game loads nothing on boot.
  Real persistence is 2.13.
- **Cost.** Pods: c2 $0.13, p $0.03, s $0.15, t $0.13, v $0.17, w $0.04,
  tb $0, y $0.16, y2 (see its report); every other check ran on the CPU. CSS gzip budget raised
  13700 → 14500 for the new screens (e).

Carried: documents teach no dialogue topic (2.11 design call); arrow
break on metal shows no fragment or flash (owner call); polish rows in
the backlog for the metal plate, side camera and the draw-1 stand-in.
