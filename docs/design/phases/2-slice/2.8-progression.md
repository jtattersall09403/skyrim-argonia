# 2.8 — Progression: one stat system for every actor, skills by use, the rest sitting

**Goal.** The whole system of attributes, skills, levels and derived
numbers is in place and running on every actor in the slice: the player
and every placed person and creature compile from the same schema and
tables, so a retune regenerates everyone. Skills rise by use, vastei
accrues and buys attributes, levels consolidate at a rest sitting that
also saves, heals and respawns, health follows the formula, and the
character sheet and level-up screens show it all on every device and
input. Rest, save and levelling are **one mechanic**
([world/76](../../world/76-stats-progression.md) §126, §120.4;
[0144](../../old-decisions/0144-progression-ships-whole-one-stat-schema-compiled-for-every-actor.md)).

## Outcome

2.8 shipped the whole progression system. One schema compiles every actor:
`compileCharacter` (`packages/game-core/src/stats/compile.ts`) over the
effect stack (`stats/effects.ts`: race packages, the thirteen birthsigns in
`stats/data/birthsigns.json`), every person in `living.json` naming a preset
class and every row carrying `compiled` (`worldgen/compiled_view.py`;
hostiles bit-exact with 2.7), the player on `combat/playerNumbers.ts`, a
person's level its band's `characterLevel` in `ladder.json`, and the
authors' table `stats/ladder-table.md`. The Marsh Hand anchor is the
compiled 24.06 % (world/76 §116). One progression store
(`stats/store.ts`, save family `progression`) takes every use report by the
§120.1 values and §120.2 worthiness, books and trainer ranks with no vastei,
the rested entry on `skillExperience`; vastei drops as one pile where you die
(the whole balance, fraction included) and comes back within 2 m. The rest
sitting (`packages/scene/src/interact/rest.ts`, save family `respawn`)
refuses by the rule, saves, consolidates one level, sells attributes at list
price capped at five per attribute per sitting (§120.4), recomputes health,
fills the pools, records `restAt`, respawns and advances the clock; the
difficulty knob and the repair hammer's wear are data. The sheet, level-up,
rest and wait screens show it on every viewport (`packages/ui/src/screens`).
Proof: the headless session
`packages/scene/src/interact/progression-session.test.ts`, `stats/balance.test.ts`
(level 2 is earned at 20–30 minutes for the warrior, scout and mage, and
level 10 at 4.1–5.9 h; the late gap is 2.1–2.4 h a level; the curve and its
table are world/76 §120.5 and [0147](../../old-decisions/0147-early-levels-come-fast.md)), the [loop surveys](2.8-loops/README.md),
and `lanes/2.8/*-report.md`. Owed, and to whom: the birthsign is null in
both apps until 3.3's character creation sets it; climb and fall-damage use
reports wait on 2.12 (the runtime emits no climb event and deals no fall
damage); the studio has no save registry, so its rest snapshots nothing
until 2.13's slot writer; pick and probe wear is 2.10's; three screen
points are `P3` in the [backlog](../P-polish/backlog.md).

## Deliverables

- **One schema compiled for every actor.** Every person record names a
  class (from the 18; the tree-minders the `tree-minder` preset, the
  Teeth's Nisswo and priest a magic preset, since 2.9 derives spell stock
  and enemy casting from the compiled school skills,
  [0146](../../old-decisions/0146-magic-ships-whole-skyrim-hands-one-effect-family-system-folk-sellers.md)),
  a race and sex, a band and a position; every
  creature its species statblock on the same ladder. One compiler emits
  attributes, skills, derived pools, encumbrance, armour and damage for
  all of them, person and creature alike, through the actor-stats
  interface 2.7 built; enemies keep their band numbers, now derived from
  the same compile. Nothing is hand-written per actor; a table change
  regenerates `living.json`'s compiled view (standard 18, world/98
  updated). Baseline-equivalence tests: at neutral stats the player's
  numbers match the sandbox's calibrated values; every hostile's numbers
  match what 2.7 shipped.
- **The player joins the interface**, fed by the same compile from the
  character's class, race, birthsign and current skills; combat, movement
  and the HUD read the compiled values; the sandbox's preset loadouts
  become compiled characters.
- **Skill experience by use** with the §120.2 worthiness rules, one
  shared store every system reports into: weapon connect, block, hit
  taken, armour on kill and on hit, run, swim, climb, jump, survived fall,
  repair (0.4 a stroke), persuade; skill books (one rank each, five per
  skill) and trainer ranks count toward a level and never yield vastei.
  2.10 reports sneak and lockpicking into the same store; 2.9 spells;
  2.9b brewing, forging and enchanting.
- **Vastei**: accrues by the §120.3 formula, shows on the sheet and the
  level screen, drops as one pile where you die (replacing the last),
  comes back by pickup within a stated radius (set here, recorded in
  world/76), is never granted by trainers, books, quests or loot. The
  level screen carries its one-line explanation; the Nisswo teaching
  scene stays owed by the opening's packet (3.4).
- **Pending levels** at the 10-rank rule (misc and maxed skills 1:3); the
  no-deferral invariant holds.
- **The rest sitting** completes 2.6's sleep interaction: rest in the
  calm wild or in an owned or rented bed; refused in a dungeon, in
  combat, while hunted, or inside a settlement without a bed (the refusal
  says why); saves through the 2.1 contract; consolidates one pending
  level; opens the attribute purchase at the §120.4 prices, at most five
  points per attribute per sitting, list price; recomputes health by the formula; restores pools;
  records `restAt`; fires `onRest` respawn; advances the clock through
  2.6's primitive. Wait passes time only. Waking from death counts as a
  rest. A rented or owned bed adds the timed rested entry (§126: skill
  experience ×1.05 for eight game hours, owned ×1.08); a camp adds
  nothing beyond the sitting.
- **The effect stack** and its field list, with permanent entries:
  all thirteen birthsigns authored as data and chosen directly, race
  packages applied, the sheet listing them. 2.9 adds timed effects to the
  same stack.
- **Health, difficulty, tool wear.** Health by the §117 formula,
  retroactive; the difficulty knob as the pure incoming-damage multiplier
  (§121.4); the repair hammer's `wearPerUse` and `maxCondition` on 2.3's
  condition shape and the Smithing repair ceiling, numbers against
  Morrowind recorded as a dossier, the one-point placeholder replaced in
  the same change; the test reads the field, never a constant.
- **The screens filled.** Character sheet, level-up, rest and wait on
  every viewport, light and dark, keyboard and mouse, touch and pad.
- **The ladder documented in numbers** for 4.2 authors: what a class at a
  band and position compiles to, so content is authored semantically and
  checked once compiled.

## Owner call

Every number is in world/76 or set by this phase against a dossier. The
owner is asked to accept the sweep's outliers
([compile.md](2.8-loops/compile.md)), which are the ladder's design:

- Two danger-1 vermin, the mudcrab and the marsh-rat swarm, die to one
  arrow from a starting marksman (the bottom of the lowest band).
- Nine losses to the two bosses: the swamp leviathan against the master
  build (four) and Xal-Krona against the legend build (five).

The owner is told:

- The reference character's armour anchor is the compiled 24.06 %
  mitigation, not the earlier 25.0 % (world/76 §116).
- Every character reaches level 2 within the first half hour of play, and
  later levels come further apart, at most two and a half hours each (§120.5).
- The studio walk now runs with skill curves on: the player's numbers come
  from the compiled character, not the sandbox's fixed values.
- A danger-2 creature kills an unarmoured level-2 character in two blows.
  That is the design: the danger-2 bar is four blows for that band's
  level-10 character in armour.

## Loops

One small loop per subset and one per seam, each a number beside a
capture, each with a survey file in [2.8-loops/](2.8-loops/README.md)
written before its first measure, closed by an independent reader
([loops](../loops.md)). Lanes run in parallel where files are disjoint.

| Loop | Measure | Eyes |
|---|---|---|
| compile | every living record and the player compile; baseline equivalence; the sim sweep (every archetype × five compiled builds) | sandbox strips of one reference fight before and after the swap, side by side |
| skill use | every use hook fires with the table's value and worthiness; books and trainers count toward a level and yield no vastei | the "rose to" toast on the three viewports |
| vastei | accrual by formula, pile on death, recovery by pickup, never from a grant; save round trip | the pile in the sandbox; the sheet balance |
| rest | allowed and refused cases, save, one level, purchase at list price with the cap, health recompute, pools, `restAt`, respawn, clock | the rest and level-up screens on the three viewports |
| screens | sheet, level-up, rest and wait on every viewport, light and dark, every input, until the reader finds nothing | ui-lab screenshots |
| effects | birthsigns and race packages on the stack; the sheet lists them | sheet capture |
| balance | short targeted sims, seconds each, tied to a moment of play: the first two hours reaching level 2; ten lowest-band fights and their ranks; one sitting's cost at an early and a mid level; a skill at 100 feeding the counter; the no-deferral invariant | none (numbers only) |
| seams | one loop per hand-off: bed or calm ground → rest prompt; rest screen → level-up; death → wake at the rest with the pile left; skill rise → toast → sheet; trainer or book rank → level counter | one still per viewport at the hand-off; a strip where the transition matters |

No long simulated playthrough; no end-to-end journey run inside a loop.
Every iteration is fast: the planner reads each lane's elapsed time at
every check-in and fixes the slow tool, pattern or method before the next
turn. RunPod only where a pod is cheaper and faster than this CPU for the
capture in hand ([0119](../../old-decisions/0119-runpod-is-the-gpu-lane.md)).

## Acceptance

- Every living record in the slice and the player compile from the one
  schema; baseline-equivalence tests and the 19 invariants green; a table
  retune regenerates every actor with no hand edit.
- A headless session: fight, rank skills, rest, level, buy attributes,
  die, wake at the rest with the pile where you fell, recover it; every
  step round-trips through save.
- Every loop's survey green and its reader sweep clear.
- The sweep reports no outlier the owner has not accepted: see
  [compile.md](2.8-loops/compile.md).

## Owner check

One studio play of finished work, never QA: "play until you level, rest
and spend; did the pace and the sitting feel right?" with the URL and
coordinates.

## Carried from 2.7

records arrival, the wallet feeds `die` and the pile returns by pickup
(`lanes/2.8/b-report.md`, `c-report.md`); the game passes `onLoot` and a
kill reaches the respawn ledger (`f-report.md`).
