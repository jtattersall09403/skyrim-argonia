# 2.15 — Boats: the player's craft as Skyrim's horse, sailed like The Wind Waker

(In parallel with this phase, deliver everything in [the owner fixes](../../../lanes/2.15/owner-fixes.md).)

**Goal.** Boats fill the role horses fill in Skyrim: bought, taken or
found, owned, left and recovered, used across many journeys with other
play in between. They sail the way The Wind Waker's boat does: simple to
pick up, steered at a cruise speed, quicker with the wind astern, and
the player can turn the wind with one quick action. The slice builds two
classes on one sailing system, the dugout (seated, paddled, creeks and
rivers) and the sea hull (standing at the helm, under sail, the coast);
every other class is a config row 4.5 adds
([0159](../../old-decisions/0159-boats-are-wind-waker-sailing-dugout-and-sea-hull-first.md)).
The phase runs after 2.12 and 2.9c and before 2.14 and 2.13
([0158](../../old-decisions/0158-boats-and-sound-before-the-save-loop.md)):
tried in full, and dropped by a revert only if the owner calls it.

## a2. Sailing, the wind and the hulls (`research` agent)

Added to the research doc, before any code:

1. **How The Wind Waker sails**: steering, speed against the wind's
   angle, how the sail reads, what the baton changes and why it was slow,
   the camera, getting on and off. Our version is the same feel in our
   world: name each rule and the config value it becomes.
2. **Prior art to take**: three.js and GitHub implementations of Wind
   Waker-style sailing, boat physics on a height-field sea, and boat
   wakes; for each, licence, what it does well, and which parts we lift
   (credited like an asset in the root README § Credits).
3. **Turning the wind, from the lore**: a power, an item or a cheap
   Alteration spell, whichever the lore grounds best (UESP recorded as a
   dossier first; the era policy holds); who teaches or sells it in the
   slice and why there.
4. **The hull crawl**: every hull in the vault, the mods (Nexus API) and
   vanilla that could serve a class, proved from data, never filenames;
   confirm the dugout's and the sea hull's, and the reed canoe's absence.
5. **The sea route**: measure the open water the sea hull can sail
   between the slice's coastal places (Lighter Flotilla, Teeth of Sithis,
   Pearl Lots, Riverwalk, Voriplasm Vault) inside the boundary. If there
   is no real run, add sea-hull moorings at the coastal places whose lore
   allows them, so the hull has somewhere to go.

## b. Data model and the mooring layer

- Records in the normalised set (standard 18; [world/98](../../world/98-data-model.md)
  updated in the same change): `BoatClass` (a config: speed, turn rate,
  wind response, draught, wave limit, reach rules, crew pose, seats,
  price), `Boat` (class, owner, condition, `lastMooring`, current mooring
  or position, state, `schemaVersion`), the `mooring` socket kind (kind
  landing, mooring, beach, boathouse; accepts, capacity, owner, service
  link, dry-season depth, pose) and a derived `navigableReach` per class.
  All five researched classes except the reed canoe are typed and priced;
  hulls and crew only for the dugout and the sea hull.
- The `boat` person-service kind (buy, recover) in 2.6's table; the
  sellers from research M13 that serve the two slice classes.
- Ownership through 2.3's model; taking a boat is theft on the Owing
  ledger (0151), no stolen flag. Recovery: a boat left adrift or beached
  is moved after a rest to its `lastMooring` (where it was bought, found
  or taken, until the player ties it somewhere), or fetched by a
  boat-keeper for ceil(10 percent of price), minimum 5 gold. Every owned
  boat is marked on the 2.11 map.
- The mooring sockets placed as a layer on the places lore and water
  justify ([0140](../../old-decisions/0140-layers-on-a-built-place-never-rebuild-it.md)),
  plotted on the 2D map and in the studio with the navigable reaches.
  No boathouse in the slice.
- The mooring check, a fast focused loop: every socket floats each
  accepted class at the dry-season depth, sits on water that reaches
  another socket or the sea, stands at a real jetty, post or bank on the
  place's geometry, and has a lore reason; a `reader` judges a map still.
- Save family registered with the 2.1 contract (0158).
- Authoring rules from [0042](../../old-decisions/0042-buildout-steers-and-engineering-standards.md) §2:
  bridge clearance and obstacles bound `navigableReach`; placed craft
  float on their tether with cargo riding the hull
  ([world/60](../../world/60-water-traversal.md) §45, edited in the same
  change); the horse and boat data mined by `worldgen/mine_horses.py`;
  the hydrology depth defect fixed at source with a test.

## c. The sailing system

- One controller for every class: `EcctrlVehicle` driving the
  `BuoyantBody` under the water query, exposed only through
  `PlayerMovementController`'s boat mode (combat, input and animation
  see the controller, never ecctrl). The class config sets its handling;
  the dugout is paddled at its own speed, the sea hull sails.
- Sailing per a2: steered at cruise, the weather's wind adding speed
  astern, the sail reading the wind.
- Board from a landing, from the water (2.12's climb-out) and from a
  bank; cast off, steer, beach, moor; fall out and re-board; the boundary
  stop; reach rules from `navigableReach` (falls stop every class; the
  dugout runs rapids down only; the sea hull keeps to deep water).
- Deck combat: research §6 D1–D8 (the platform frame, arrows inheriting
  deck speed, aim and melee in the frame, arrows stopping at the
  waterline); D9 boarding is a hook only.

## d. Crew animation

- One focused loop authors a seated paddle (dugout) and a standing helm
  (sea hull), each judged by `reader` agents on `visual:check` strips.
  If after two iterations the readers find it very bad, the loop stops
  and changes approach: a sourced seated pose on the dugout's bench and a
  standing pose at the sea hull's helm, the paddle or oars still, the
  hull steered as an `EcctrlVehicle`.

## e. Turning the wind

- The form a2 chose (power, item or Alteration spell), learned or bought
  where a2 placed it; one quick action opens a small direction pick and
  sets the weather's wind, so trees, waves, smoke, ambience and sails
  swing together. It costs little and is used often; no long ritual.
- While sailing, a small HUD mark shows the wind's direction relative to
  the boat; it is hidden on land. Both screens go through 2.2's UI
  system and the text catalogue; `text-review` reads their strings.
- Wind state saves with the weather.

## f. Wake and water response

- The boat's movement stamped into the ripple simulation and foam field:
  ripples spread and fade, foam and spray at bow and stern, in
  `world-render`'s water pipeline, on every quality band, phones
  included (owner, 2026-10-24).

## Loop

Each block at its cheapest level ([0156](../../old-decisions/0156-test-each-building-block-at-its-cheapest-level-compose-once.md);
[loops.md § Rules](../loops.md)): reach rules, prices, ownership,
recovery and the mooring check at level 1; the wake sheet and the wind
HUD at level 2; crew poses, boarding and sailing response at level 3
(`visual:check`); the scripted journeys at level 4. The animation loop
(d) stops at two iterations as ruled. Nothing is filmed without a
level-5 reason named in the lane's brief; the owner plays the whole. If
the controller fights the water model for more than one chunk, the
planner tells the owner; only the owner parks it.

river, fall out, climb back in, leave it, come back later and find it."

wind behind you and feel it pick up."
