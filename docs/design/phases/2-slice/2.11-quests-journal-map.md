# 2.11 — Quests, journal and map

**Goal.** The narrative runtime (Q1 of [quests/90](../../quests/90-production-sequence.md))
and the quest-writing recipe proven at scale: every quest that touches a
slice place is authored as data over the one world record and driven
headlessly; the eight that lie wholly inside the slice are played end to
end, the rest play cleanly to the stage that leaves the slice. The rulings
are [0153](../../old-decisions/0153-quests-ship-as-data-over-one-world-record.md).

## Deliverables

### a. One world record, audited

- The place facts record is the one source every consumer of a world
  fact reads: position and extent, what stands where (from the bundle's
  placements and sockets), landmarks and what is in sight of them, the
  people posted there and the topics they carry, the counts of nodes,
  plants and items by area, the services, the hostility block and its
  flips. Directions, journal text, dialogue grounding (`groundedIn`) and
  the quest validator read it; nothing reads the catalogue prose directly.
  `blueprint_promises` and the facts generator grow to emit what is
  missing; a fact the bundle cannot carry is a `claim` with no carrier and
  a red.
- Every slice place's catalogue record, prose and facts audited against
  its built bundle: each claim checked by script where the bundle can
  answer (positions, counts, sockets, nav reach) and by a `reader` over a
  `look` sheet where only eyes can (a crane at the bow, two cairns on the
  bluff). Mismatches fixed in the record or, where the build is wrong and
  the fix is a layer, by the place's own delta (0140). Zero unresolved
  claims is the bar.

### b. The runtime, `packages/narrative`

- The quest blueprint as data on the quest record: `stages` (id, journal
  text key, objectives with their conditions, `onEnter` actions, successors
  and fail-forward successors), `essentialTopics`, `touches`; the
  `schemaVersion` bump and the `worldgen.quests --check` validators.
- The quest store (stage, state, flags, counters, objectives, deed
  counts), the journal store (entries by quest with date, text key, topic
  links, `pinId`, `directionsId`), the action applier for every emitted
  world action, faction joining and rank grants on `standing.ts`
  (`join`, `expel`, `grantRank`), the stance-flip evaluator over the
  catalogue hostility block (`placeStance` and `placeCleared` derived from
  quest state, standing, gold and deeds, read by greetings, the answered
  violence runtime and the travel predicates), grantable persistent map
  markers, the local-state overlay runtime (enable and disable refs,
  schedule and service overrides, ambience) applied from
  `settlement.variants` by stage, the favour paid on `completeQuest`
  through `questCompleted`. Each a save family of its own; `restoreAfter`
  orders them after `knowledge`, `discovery` and `faction-standing`.
- The headless driver (standard 5): runs any quest from a new game and
  from every save point along it, choosing the scripted path; the graph
  validator (every stage reachable, every successor exists, no orphan
  objective, every `touches` id real) and the world-reference validator
  over all 715 records.
- The marsh's quest data ([0157](../../old-decisions/0157-the-marsh-does-what-the-lore-says.md)):
  a calendar condition (month, day, season on `world-time`) and a
  `setWeather` action; the player's Hist-hearing step (`touched`,
  `heard`, `sap-speaker`), saved, granted only by a quest action, read
  by the Hist interactions: resting beneath a Hist's boughs soothes (a
  rest bonus) from `touched`; from `heard` a hand on bark registers the
  hidden places within the tree's reach as known to the discovery
  tracker and the map, and says how dangerous the land around is; a
  grave-stake home record (place, person, carved story) read as a
  document at the stake.
- The studio quest panel is observe-only (0149): the stage, flags,
  objectives and journal of the running quest; a seam's start state
  (stage, flags, kit) lives in test tooling only, and every seam
  reaches at least one of its states through the player's flow.

### c. The consistency validator

The promise process carried down to stage level, run by `worldgen.quests
--check` over every record with stages, gating authoring:

- a direction (journal or dialogue) walks the route graph from where it
  is given, ends inside the named place's extent, and every landmark it
  names is a facts-record entry in sight of the route;
- a cardinal or relative claim ("the cave east of town") agrees with the
  facts record's positions;
- a collect or count objective names a thing the facts record carries in
  at least that count in that area;
- a person to ask or a thing to find stands at the place in the facts
  record, and that person carries the topic the stage needs;
- the first stage, and every stage that enters a place, is reachable at
  the stance the place has at that moment (the baseline or a flip whose
  condition an earlier stage can meet inside the slice), or the stage is
  marked `entry: clear` on a clearable place, or `entry: outside` on a
  socket outside the hostile scope;
- a quest record with `standInFor` names a real quest; once that
  quest's anchor place is built (`gatesOk` in the build ledger) while
  the stand-in still grants, the validator reds, so the Part 4 packet
  that builds the place cannot go green until the stand-in retires
  (0157);
- a partial quest's first stage past the slice names its outside place
  and the Part 4 packet that owns it, and stops cleanly.

### d. UI: the sweep and the gaps

- A thorough crawl first (`find` agents reading `packages/ui/src` and
  `apps/ui-lab/src` in full, never a grep): what quests, journal, map,
  markers, factions, favours, notices and discovery need against what
  exists, as a table with file evidence for every "exists" and every
  "gap". The known gap is factions (standing, rank, membership, the
  join offer); likely gaps are the journal-updated and
  objective-complete notices beyond the fixture, a granted marker's cue
  and the map layer that draws it, a quest's objectives in the journal,
  and the favour debt where the player sees it. Nothing is built until
  the crawl says it is missing.
- The discovery banner names the region beneath the place (the eight
  `regionName`s in `owing.json`; 0157).
- The gaps built in the UI system (tokens, components, the book, the
  deck), each in `apps/ui-lab` on the three viewports, light and dark,
  pad focus order, read by a `reader` against the screen's checklist
  before it is lifted into studio and game.

### e. The slice quests

- The exemplar first: a fully written quest inside the slice, chosen by
  the lane from the eight for the systems it crosses (the Greenspring
  murder LH38 or the Claywater well LF83 are the candidates), its stages,
  journal and lines the reference every other quest is written against.
  No pause for an owner read: the validator, a critic panel (consistency,
  voice, "enough and no more") and the owner's play at the walk judge it.
- Then every quest touching a slice place (37 today; the list is derived
  from the ledger and the anchors, never hand-kept), by region packet:
  stages as data; journal entries and quest lines as catalogue text written
  through the dialogue pipeline (the `dialogue-write` skill, pool and
  override, `groundedIn` on the facts record) so a later rewrite swaps text
  and re-runs the validator without touching logic; directions generated
  from the route graph and only restated in prose; `essentialTopics`
  listed on every record (0145) and asked at disposition 0 by the walk.
- The Hist Trial stand-in (0157): a Root Steward of Hissmir gives the
  Trial of the Burnished Scales at Greenspring and grants `heard`; its
  record carries `standInFor` the canon Trials quest, authored now as a
  record anchored on `place.dunmer-north.hissmir` (its stages written by
  the packet that builds Hissmir).
- At least one favour paid by labour, in an interior where coin is worth
  nothing (0141); at least one quest whose consequence is a local-state
  variant (the silted cove, the sour spring or a cleared camp); at least
  one that enters a hostile place by each entry kind (flip, clear,
  outside).

## Loops

Every block is checked once, at the cheapest level that proves it, and
the composition only for what composition can break
([0156](../../old-decisions/0156-test-each-building-block-at-its-cheapest-level-compose-once.md)
ladder: 1 data, 2 still, 3 motion, 4 headless play, 5 filmed seam, 6 the
owner's play). Each loop is small, owns its survey file in `2.11-loops/`
and runs beside the others on disjoint files. Nothing in 2.11 is filmed:
no join here needs a level-5 reason (a lane that finds one names it in
its brief and the planner rules).

| Loop | Level and measure | Eyes |
|---|---|---|
| place facts, per slice place | 1: the facts audit script, every claim carried or red | 2: a `look` sheet per place, read against the claim list |
| the quest store and driver | 1 and 4: unit tests; the driver over every authored quest from a new game and every save point; the save round trip of every family | none |
| the validator | 1: counts over 715 records, reds by rule, zero on the slice set | none |
| the journal screen | 1: entries from the store; 2: ui-lab on three viewports, light and dark, with the exemplar's real entries | 2: a reader: a book, current apart from done, enough and no more |
| the map screen and markers | 1: discovered-only pins, a granted marker drawn and saved, an undiscovered entry shows directions and no link | 2: ui-lab stills on three treatments and viewports |
| quest-stage transitions and notices | 4: the driver, every stage change raises its notice and journal line once | 2: the notice still over a play frame |
| stance flips and joining | 4: a people drive, hostile at baseline, the flip met, greeting and watch stand down; the join offer and rank grant | 2: the join offer and factions screen stills |
| the overlay | 4: a headless apply per variant: refs enabled and disabled, overrides, ambience | 2: `look` sheets before and after, read as a pair |
| factions and notices screens | 2: ui-lab on three viewports, light and dark, pad order | 2: a reader against the screen checklist |
| directions | 1 and 4: the walk test over every authored direction (arrives inside the place; every landmark named is in sight on the way) | none |

**Seams** (level 4: `2.11-loops/seams.md`, scripted headless sessions in
`seams-session.test.ts` on the scene and game runners, each staging the
blocks below it and playing only its join, each ending in a save round
trip): talk → stage → journal entry → notice; stage → flag → place stance
→ greeting; complete → favour paid → price; complete → rank → sanctuary;
stage → overlay → refs; discover → journal link → map pin; grant marker →
map; clear a place → `placeCleared` → stage; steal → ledger → the crime
quest's stage; read a document → knowledge → topic → stage; opening the
journal, map and factions screens from play and closing back to play;
every stage of every authored quest → save → load → equal. `seams.md`
holds the verb × seam table: every verb kind the slice quests use (talk,
collect, deliver, clear, steal, enter a hostile place, pay a favour, walk
a direction, discover, read) has a seam row, none empty.

**Composition** is the headless driver over every authored quest on both
runners (level 4). The whole, as a player, is the owner's play of the
deployed build (level 6); no agent films or reads a journey.

## Acceptance

- The validators green over all 715 records and every rule of § c green
  over the slice set; the driver completes every authored quest from a new
  game and from every save point; the eight complete; every partial stops
  cleanly at its boundary with its owner named.
- The facts audit: zero unresolved claims across the eighteen slice places;
  every consumer of a world fact reads the facts record.
- Every subset loop's survey green and its reader sweep clear; every seam
  row green on both runners.
- The UI crawl table complete; every gap it names built on three viewports
  with its reader sweep clear.
- The 2.13 coverage rows for quests, journal, map, factions and local
  state each carry their package path, scripted play, save round trip and
  slice evidence; 2.13's and 3.4's Starting states written.

## Owner play

The owner plays the finished game on the deployed build; every check
before this is ours. Find and finish the quests that begin in Greenspring
and Riverwalk without the debug panel. The question afterwards: did the
journal tell you enough and no more, did the directions take you there,
and did any place or person contradict what you had been told?

## Polish routing

The LLM critic, the ending simulation, dream and vision scenes and the
evidence custody runtime are 4.3 and 4.5. A quest that cannot complete, a
direction that does not arrive, or a fact the world contradicts is a
blocker.
