# 2.14 — Sound and music

**Goal.** The world heard in the studio and the game: footsteps, combat,
region ambience by clock and weather, water, creature calls for the
slice's creatures, interior and underwater states, and a music bus with
a sourced score. After 2.15, so the boats are heard, and before 2.13,
so the owner's playthrough hears the slice
([0158](../../old-decisions/0158-boats-and-sound-before-the-save-loop.md)).

## Starting state

- `packages/audio` and `tooling/audio-pipeline` ship 391 files (5 MB),
  an injected `AudioManager`, the `SoundEvent` bus, `footstepSurface`
  and `selectAmbience` ([0094](../../old-decisions/0094-audio-ships-as-opus-webm-sets-read-from-the-plugin-loops-crossfade-at-runtime.md),
  [0095](../../old-decisions/0095-audio-runtime-is-an-injected-manager-over-an-engine-interface-fed-by-typed-sound-events.md)).
  Wired only in the combat sandbox (swings, hits, blocks, footsteps,
  bow, torch emitter, stealth hearing); not in the studio or the game;
  no authored region tables; no reverb; no creature SFX; no music.
  Pipeline commands from `tooling/audio-pipeline`:
  `python3 -m audio_pipeline.build` (and `--check`).
- **Studio wiring, ready to do.** `vite.config.ts` takes
  `audioFiles({ sharedBase? })` (one audio copy on Pages);
  `createThreeAudio(camera)`, an `AudioManager` on `BASE_URL/audio/`,
  `unlock()` on the first gesture, `update()` on the frame scheduler.
  Ambience inputs the studio already computes: region class at the
  camera, `dayPhaseAt`, `seasonState`, `weatherSampleAt` (weather, rain,
  wind), `LocalClimate.canopy`, and the acoustic state (underwater,
  interior, canopy over 0.6, else exterior); call `selectAmbience` and
  `setAmbience` only when an input changes band. One virtual emitter per
  river, rapid, shore or waterfall moved to the nearest polyline point,
  live within 60 m (the round-1 `ambient.water.*` and
  `ambient.waterfall.*` sets). `&audio=0` mutes.
- The first region table can be drafted from the shipped vanilla Morthal
  marsh sets (`ambient.marsh.*`, each with its vanilla chance and weather
  flags in `source.region`); the shape fixture is
  `packages/audio/src/ambience.test.ts`. Marsh, rain, thunder, river and
  underwater sets cost 1.5 MB when all are fetched.
- **The mud-footstep call** (open): Skyrim maps `MaterialMud` to the dirt
  sounds and no mod ships booted mud steps; Barefoot Footstep Extended
  (Nexus SE 40308) has an unverified barefoot `Mud` folder; booted steps
  exist only as CC0/CC-BY field recordings (Freesound 376809, BigSoundBank
  s0495, omnisounddesign 18925). The owner rules whether cutting single
  steps from a sourced recording is sourcing or making.

## Deliverables

- App wiring through the 2.1 adapter: combat and footstep events,
  ambience beds by region, clock and weather, water emitters from the
  hydrology graph, the acoustic stack (exterior, canopy, interior,
  underwater), the weather layer.
- Region tables in `world/sources/audio/` for every region class (a wide
  load: the table shape is the same for all); creature calls sourced for
  the slice creatures with credits; the mud-footstep call.
- Sound sets of three to eight variants with pitch and gain variance
  ([world/57](../../world/57-audio-soundscape.md) §57); settlement ambience profiles, the Hist
  low-frequency signature and interior sound per cell (§119–124).
- The Hist's voices ([0157](../../old-decisions/0157-the-marsh-does-what-the-lore-says.md)):
  sourced whispers and songs when a player who has `heard` rests a hand
  on bark, each tree's way of speaking from the lore (wind and chimes,
  dreams); the soothing tone beneath the boughs from `touched`.
- Music: the bus, ducking, a sourced score (licence per track, credits)
  with explore, tension and combat states.
- The studio audio layer in the reproducible URL; the voice and memory
  probe.

### Voice barks

Feasibility, line counts and the owner steps are in the
[dossier](../../research/text-and-voice/skyrim-voice-barks.md). The
derivation is in `lanes/2.5/x5-bark-design.md`.

- **Source.** Vanilla walk-by lines (DIAL HELO, IDLE and GBYE) from
  `Skyrim - Voices_en0.bsa`, chosen by INFO record and conditions, never
  by filename. The archive is an owner step (copy from an install, or
  DepotDownloader app 489830 with the owner's login).
- **Pipeline.** INFO → `.fuz` in the BSA → strip the FUZE header → xWMA
  → `audio_pipeline.codec` → Opus mono 24 kbps WebM. One sound set per
  row (`voice.<voicetype>.<bark-id>`) with source hash and INFO id in the
  provenance. Credit and sourcing-register row in the same change.
- **Table.** `world/sources/audio/barks/`: `barks.schema.json`, one row
  table per people (shape of `barks.sample.json`), `resolver.json` (the
  friendliness cuts, voice-type variants and repeat windows),
  `plugin-counts.json` from `tooling/audio-pipeline/audio_pipeline/voice_barks.py`. Rows are text-first.
  `clip` is null where no vanilla line fits, and 2.5's bark surface
  shows the text either way.
- **Budget.** At most 1.5 MB per voice type (about 80–150 voice lines), streamed
  per place: about 12 MB for the slice's eight voice types, male and
  female.
- **Acceptance.** One voice type extracted end to end with its measured
  size and credit. Every row validates (`test_barks.py`). No shipped row
  names a Skyrim place or faction (a reader pass). Barks play on the
  voice bus within the voice cap. The owner's walk hears greetings that
  match disposition and race.

## Acceptance

- The probe within budget; every slice creature has calls; the owner's
  dawn-to-night walk with ears on finds region identity and the
  underwater change.
