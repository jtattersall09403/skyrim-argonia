# 2.6 — Trade and services

**Goal.** Buying, selling, training, resting, repairing and travelling are
real, through the dialogue services entry, on one price contract
([0141](../../old-decisions/0141-one-price-contract-coin-goods-or-a-favour.md)),
closing the explore → earn → spend loop, and money thins inland as the
lore says it does.

## Starting state

- `packages/game-core/src/stats/data/economy.json` holds the trainer,
  repair, potion, purse and income numbers; the barter band, the trainer
  cap and the rest rules are prose in
  [world/76](../../world/76-stats-progression.md) §124 and §126. Training
  costs 10 × the skill level per rank, capped by the governing attribute
  (the `trainingCostRange(30, 50) == 7900` test pins it).
- The dialogue services entry exists (`packages/dialogue/src/services`):
  kinds barter, train, rest, travel, spells, repair, stable, each closed
  by default; a person advertises services as record words on
  `npcs.json`; the slice carries trade 5, beds 3, lodging 1, ferry 3,
  repair 1, train 3; no slice person sells spells until 2.9 gives the
  two tree-minders the `spells`, `blessing` and `cure` words
  ([0146](../../old-decisions/0146-magic-ships-whole-skyrim-hands-one-effect-family-system-folk-sellers.md)).
- Travel: `travel-services.json` is the one service graph
  ([0068](../../old-decisions/0068-routes-below-the-gate-records-here-realised-in-16h.md));
  `resolveTrip` returns refused, unavailable, cannot-pay or arrive; the
  fare is taken and the body moved by the studio's `TravelMenu` with no
  fade and no clock change; the game app passes no `teleportTo`, so
  travel is closed there. Fares are flat gold on the record; the
  `services.ferry` key in economy.json duplicates them and is removed.
- Gold is a counter on the inventory, never a row; every item record has
  a `value`. The UI kit has a `BarterScreen` (its haggle slider and
  Offer go) and a `RestScreen`. Disposition is computed per dialogue
  session and clamped 0–100 while `factions.json` ranges it −100..100.
- The world clock (`packages/world-time`) runs; nothing advances it by a
  block; `vendorPurseRefreshHours` has no consumer.
- Rulings: no haggle; finite purses with restock; no camping in
  settlements (in town rest needs an owned or rented bed; the rest
  sitting is 2.8's; wait is free); shrine blessings priced by standing,
  free at rank ([0039](../../old-decisions/0039-source-game-crosscheck-triage.md));
  no stolen flag, no fence. Lore: coin in cities, ports and roads, goods
  and obligation in villages, nothing in the deep interior
  ([settlement-register](../../../../world/sources/lore/extrapolation/settlement-register.md) § 7).

## Deliverables

- **The price contract** (`packages/economy`): a price from the §124 band
  (Mercantile, disposition, the merchant's mirror; no roll, no offer),
  settled by coin, by goods at item value through the same band, or by a
  favour (a quest hook id on the service record; the debt stands open in
  save state until that quest completes; 2.11 runs the quest and marks it
  paid). One pure function the UI, the services and the headless drive
  all call.
- **Service kinds as data**: one table of kind → record word(s), payload,
  pricing rule, which phase owns the effect. `stable` deleted everywhere
  (code, text keys, the Claywater record word becomes `lodging`). 2.15
  adds `boat` to the table; 2.9 fills the effects of `spells`, `blessing`
  and `cure`.
- **Purses and restock**: a purse per merchant from the place tier (city,
  town, village, interior; the interior tier is zero), refilled and
  stock re-rolled on `vendorPurseRefreshHours` of world time; stock
  generated from the merchant's trade class and the place culture with
  per-person overrides, so adding a shop in Part 4 is a class and a
  culture, never a list.
- **Trade classes** on the person record: what they stock and what they
  buy; general traders broad; village traders in kind take what the
  village uses. Every slice merchant classed.
- **Trainers**: a trainer field (skills, the governing-attribute cap) on
  the person record; one rank per session at 10 × level through the
  band; two or three slice people become trainers by the layer path
  (Claywater's well-keeper, Riverwalk's stage-mistress, the upriver
  smith), each with their few lines through the dialogue-write skill.
- **Beds and rental**: lodging as a service priced by place tier, a
  rented bed owned until the next rest; quality tiers camp < rented <
  owned recorded on the bed; the rest sitting itself (save, level,
  recovery, respawn) is 2.8's.
- **Repair and recharge** as services at `services.repairPerConditionPoint`
  (the paid alternative to the station mend, 2.4 people layer) and a recharge price
  rule; both through the contract.
- **Spells, blessings and cure disease**: priced (blessing by standing,
  free at rank) and purchasable, the purchase recorded; the effect is
  2.9's.
- **The clock primitive**: advance the world clock by N hours with the
  people catch-up, in `packages/world-time`; wait uses it (free, in or
  out of town); travel uses it by route length; restock reads it; 2.8's
  rest sitting calls it.
- **Travel complete**: the fade, the clock advance by route length, the
  arrival body set in both apps (the game app passes `teleportTo`); the
  Owing gate evaluates false until 2.10 lands the ledger.
- **UI**: the barter screen on the contract (price band shown, no slider,
  settle in coin or goods, a favour offered as a line), a training row, a
  lodging row, travel as it is; ui-lab shots on the three viewports.
- **Records**: purse, stock, service price, trainer, lodging, open favour
  in the normalised set (home table, stable ids, `schemaVersion`,
  references by id, save families registered), world/98 updated
  (standard 18). Disposition persisted per person in a save family and
  ranged as `factions.json` ranges it.
- The 2.5 brief's service list, the people brief's record words and the
  text catalogue's closed lines follow the kind table.

## Wide loads

Every service kind in the table, with its pricing rule and its closed
line, even where the slice has one or no instance; every trade class the
stock generator can fill; every place tier's purse.

## Acceptance

- A headless drive on every slice service: sell, buy back, pay in goods
  at a village trader, open a favour and settle it by a stubbed quest
  completion, train to the cap, rent a bed, wait past a restock, travel
  both ways with the clock moved; purses, stock and prices match the
  band; every state round-trips through save.
- The owner can earn enough from a slice place's loot to buy something
  useful in it, and leaves a village with goods rather than drakes.
- `npm run visual:check` untouched (no movement code); ui-lab bars green
  for the barter, training and lodging screens.

## Loop

None; the drive is the measure.

## Owner check

"Sell what you found in Claywater, trade in kind at Greenspring, buy a
rank, rent a bed, take the ferry and arrive after dark; did any price,
stock or hour feel wrong?"

## Polish routing

A merchant with no stock, a ferry that strands, or a price that ignores
the band is a blocker. Stock breadth per shop and the number of trade
classes filled per culture are `P2`.

## Dialogue

Dialogue iteration runs in this chunk. Every ruling and finding is in the
[dialogue rulings index](../../research/text-and-voice/dialogue-rulings-index.md);
its open rows are the Open list below. The state the work starts from
(gate results, per-place reds, the owner review's applied and open items)
is in [dialogue-report](../../../lanes/2.5x/dialogue-report.md) §
State at hand-over to 2.6.

**Who runs it.** A `dialogue-lane` agent runs each round by the
[dialogue-write](../../../.claude/skills/dialogue-write/SKILL.md)
skill's sequence, with one `dialogue-writer` per person; `voice-reader`
and `corpus-reader` read it
([0079](../../old-decisions/0079-cheap-hands-expensive-head-agent-cost-controls.md)).
Not `deliver`, and the planner writes no lines.

**Gate policy.** Every gate is hard; `WARN_GATES` (`dialogue_gates.py`)
is empty. Hard: the style gates (`style.contraction`, `style.wordCap`,
`style.closingQuestion` among them), `handOff`, `pronoun`, `road`,
`petWords`, `corpus.restatement` (near-verbatim wording only),
`corpus.grounded`, `newcomer.gaps` and `newcomer.ambient`, the walker and
`lint_prose` with `prose.review` (three lint rules are hard in dialogue
and soft elsewhere). A writer's `freeze` refuses while the last check is
red and checks remain. Deleted by
[0143](../../old-decisions/0143-anyone-may-explain-restatement-is-near-verbatim-only.md):
the explainer cap, `corpus.explainers` and `corpus.paraphrase`.

**Open (each an index row).**
1. Grounding gate (item 9): every rumour, place, road, distance, item,
   person, price, service and payment in kind resolves to a record
   (`groundedIn`); a lie only where the stance marks it with the true
   fact. Plant it, measure the ungrounded count, then fix.
2. Item 8, delete the explain-once rule and explainer cap:
   `corpus.explainers`, `EXPLAINERS_PER_REGION`, `RESTATE_SPEAKERS`, the
   plan's `regionExplainers`, style-core §4 "One person explains" and
   SKILL.md "Explain a term once". It conflicts with `corpus.restatement`:
   the planner decides, before any rewrite, whether restatement stays
   capped only where a line copies the pool.
3. A helpful speaker explains for a newcomer (the Hist clutch, death and
   the spirit); lore said in full; the critic prompt line and the
   `explains` reader's "would a newcomer understand this" are not yet in
   the reader prompts.
4. Promote the three soft lint rules to hard, and add a cap for
   never/keep/count/hold and a non-sequitur question check; built
   before the writer round, with item 1.
5. The closing read gains a sixth check, "grounded".
6. Turn `style.contraction` and `corpus.restatement` hard (empty
   `WARN_GATES`) after the writer round.
7. Per-speaker hooks and the two missing dissent voices (closing read 2).
8. The Cut Circle explaining line; boat-name address in a crew line.
9. 12 stance-deviation pronoun clashes (dialogue-skill-report Part 2).
10. Trainers' lines (Deliverables above) by the same skill.
11. Every load-bearing proper noun has a newcomer topic ([0026](../../old-decisions/0026-main-quest-sharpened.md));
    ambient people do not explain the world ([quests/35](../../quests/35-cast.md)).
12. ui-lab dialogue at three viewports and three input modes (2.5 Loop 2).
13. Owner calls: the studio sample read; dialogue cssGzip 12950; British
    or American spelling ([0043](../../old-decisions/0043-text-quality-workstream.md)).

**The skill-training loop first.** Before the slice corpus, a loop of very fast iterations on throwaway people
and places trains the `dialogue-write` skill: a `dialogue-writer`
writes a handful of answers from a dummy packet; a sharp critic
(`reader`) judges them open-endedly as a Morrowind player and
editor would, and against the style core, the rulings index and the
nearest Morrowind exemplars; every criticism becomes an edit to the
skill (its tells, worked example, packet section or check) so the next
writer gets it right first time; repeat with a fresh dummy packet. The
loop exits when the planner judges the critic's remaining notes minor
(taste, not rule). Only then does the skill write the slice: the planner
rules on item 2; build open items 1 and 4 (the one order), each red on
its plant and measured on the corpus as it stands; a fresh blind draft from a new
dummy packet runs against them. Then one writer
round, worst first:
Riverwalk, Greenspring, Claywater, Upriver, Opening Work Camp; then the
other eight. Per place, the skill's sequence: plan, packets, writers,
apply, gates, walk, lint, critic both seeds (ours wins at least 40
percent), readers (text-review, `voice-reader` lenses `explains` and
`flow`); a `corpus-reader` per three places and one closing read over
all 13 peopled places. The closing read's six checks: curt minority, dominant pattern,
restatement, leaks, dissent, grounded.

**Dialogue acceptance.** `dialogue_gates --all` GREEN with `WARN_GATES`
empty and the grounding gate hard at 0 ungrounded; walker and
`lint_prose` clean on all 13 peopled places; both critic seeds at least 40 percent on
every place; the closing read READY on all six checks; a fresh blind batch
of 6 drafts GREEN after one check; every open item done or an owner call
in the packet.
