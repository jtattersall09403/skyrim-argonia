# 2.7 — Combat and encounters

**Goal.** The owner walks between slice places in the studio and the game
and gets fought: by the creatures in the bound sockets, by the people of
the hostile places on sight, and by roaming bands met in the wild.
Striking anyone is answered by them and theirs. Loot drops, death drops
the vastei, and you wake at your last rest
([0142](../../old-decisions/0142-combat-is-met-everywhere-hostile-places-roaming-bands-and-answered-violence.md)).

## Starting state

- **The combat core is package-shaped and already composed.** Hit
  resolution, poise, block, parry, backstab, the bow with flight,
  planting and recovery, lock-on, landing, the enemy brain and perception
  live in `packages/game-core/src/{combat,ai,perception,anim}` with tests
  behind an injected host ([0089](../../old-decisions/0089-the-combat-runtime-is-a-package-with-an-injected-host.md));
  `packages/character/src/combat/CombatRuntime.tsx` hosts it; the studio's
  `CharacterMode.tsx` and `apps/game/src/GameWorld.tsx` already import it.
  Enemies spawn only from the sandbox host's spawn points
  (`host.ts`), never from sockets or records.
- **Twelve humanoid archetypes** exist in
  `packages/game-core/src/actors/enemyArchetypes.ts` (the warden, nine
  armed wardens, an archer), each a loadout on the shared rig; movesets
  come from the weapon class (`equipment/weaponClasses.ts`, `movesets/`,
  dual wield included).
- **The slice is already populated with enemies.** `npcs.json` holds 217
  hostile living things, 79 of them in twelve slice places: hostile
  creatures in 56 bound `encounter` and `fauna` sockets, and hostile people
  in three places (the Broke Column's descendants D2–D3, the Teeth of
  Sithis cult D1–D2, the Naga highway camp's band D4). Every hostile
  record carries a danger band and `fightFleeAlarm`; `statblock.loadout`
  is `null` on all of them and `archetype` on most (`contracts/src/living.ts`
  marks the loadout as 2.7's). Two route places hold hostile creatures;
  no humanoid lives off a place anywhere in the province.
- **The hostile brain walks but cannot hit.** `ai/hostileReaction.ts`
  runs calm, drawing, coming, fighting, returning and fleeing; its one
  caller is the sandbox's people scene; `fighting` keeps coming and emits
  no attack; `fightFleeAlarm` is validated and read by nothing. The
  2.4 hand-off leaves its nav class names adapted by `people/reactionNav.ts`
  and the Fight/Flee/Alarm thresholds in code, not on the record.
- **Creatures have bodies and clips.** 27 species sets in
  `packages/character-assets/files/creatures/` (attack, hit, die, flee,
  notice, swim where the species swims), played by
  `packages/scene/src/creatures/CreatureActor.tsx`. Four slice socket
  species have no set: giant wasp, hackwing, wisp, swamp jelly.
- **Nav** is baked per place in humanoid, large and swim classes
  (`public/nav/`, `game-core/src/nav/NavService.ts`).
- **Stats.** The model and the sweep harness exist
  (`game-core/src/stats`, `stats/sim/sweeps.ts`); every hostile record's
  band maps to health, hit and armour by the world/76 §128 table. Nothing
  reads it in combat yet.
- **Weapons owed here.** A `shortspear` one-handed thrust class (Spear
  Mechanic's clips on Heavy Armory's 620 meshes); vanilla `h2h` and
  `beasth2h_*` as the unarmed set, clawed by race, with the
  stamina-damage rule as data on the `effects` slot (the finisher is
  4.5's); the landing clip at x2 through `anim/landing.ts`
  `landingAnimationSpeed`. Sources are in the vault
  ([inventory](../../research/combat-and-systems/polearm-and-blade-mod-inventory.md)).
  Weapon speed stays per class (dagger, one-handed, two-handed heavy,
  polearm); nothing is retuned.
- **Torch** (2.3 decision 10): `equipment/lights.ts` `torchClub()` is the
  mace-class moveset and guard; 2.7 sources its swing clips and decides
  whether the off-hand torch swaps guard for an attack.
- **Pad** (2.3 decision 4): sheathe and draw sit on the left face button
  (`io/input.ts` `SWITCH_GAMEPAD.Y_LEFT_SHEATHE`); 2.7 places two-hand (a
  hold on the same button is the nearest fit) and signs the pad table.
- **Not yet anywhere:** arrows and physical materials as records; the
  roaming-band table; loot drops; death, wake and respawn in the save.

## a. Port and numbers

- Enemies spawn from records, not the sandbox host: every hostile living
  thing in a slice place, through the 2.1 scene adapter, in the studio and
  the game; the combat-space probes against the baked nav.
- **Loadouts.** Every hostile person's `statblock.loadout` filled from the
  2.3 weapon and armour table by culture and band (the lore's rule for
  what a tribeless Naga, a cultist or a deserter carries); the gate fails
  on `null`. Creatures get their moveset from the species set's clips.
- **Numbers from the band** through the stats API: health, hit, armour
  and poise from the world/76 §128 table by `statblock.band`, read by
  enemies only; the player stays on the sandbox's calibrated values. The
  API is shaped so 2.8 swaps the player's side in with no change to the
  enemy path ([0142](../../old-decisions/0142-combat-is-met-everywhere-hostile-places-roaming-bands-and-answered-violence.md) § seam);
  world/76 §121 rules (soft requirements, one stamina bar, mitigation,
  block, the crit table, `staggerable:false`) are read, never re-derived.
- **Movesets, wide:** every weapon class sourced and wired in one pass,
  the shortspear and unarmed sets included; dual wield's
  `dual-wield-attack` scenario green.
- **Arrows:** response by surface as a data table (stick, ricochet,
  break, pass through; recoverable where it sticks: world/75 §110–120),
  joined to the record set as the physical-material record; the
  shoulder-aim bow camera ([research](../../research/combat-and-systems/third-person-bow-aim-camera.md)).
- **Hooks owed:** 2.10's damage-multiplier hook in `resolveHit`, the
  alerted window flag, bow draw and release crouched, and the typed
  witnessed-attack and witnessed-kill events; 2.9's caster action seam in
  the enemy brain; 2.15's attack and shoot from a moving hull and targets
  on water; 2.14's combat sound events.

## b. Creatures, places and answered violence

- **Sockets fight.** Every creature in an `encounter` socket hunts by its
  species row (notice, draw, come, leash, flee by `fightFleeAlarm` read
  from the record, pack alarm from world/74). The four species without a
  set are sourced or recast before this chunk closes.
- **Hostile places attack on sight.** The fighting mode of
  `hostileReaction.ts` emits attack intents through the enemy brain; the
  three hostile slice places are fights; Fight/Flee/Alarm thresholds and
  notice profiles move onto the record.
- **Answered violence.** A struck person fights or flees by their row;
  their alarm brings their band or household; a place with no watch
  judges by the crime's weight: a blow is answered by the victim and
  kin, a killing turns the place hostile until 2.10's amends. The
  self-defence rule: a target already hostile makes it no crime.
- **Loot drops** from the 2.3 records (lootTable, condition, ownership);
  **respawn class** (`onRest`, `never`, `untilCleared`) on every hostile
  record, executed at 2.8's rest sitting.
- **Death and wake:** the screen on the 2.2 system; the carried vastei
  stays where you died as one pile, dying again replaces it; wake at the
  last rest, or at the arrival point when you have never rested; no
  invincibility; death, the pile and the wake point in the save contract.

## c. Roaming bands

- **The table**, one row per region: which territory-holders roam, their
  frequency, band, and the band compositions they draw from; the slice
  fills the regions its sixteen places sit in. A new record in world/98,
  the layer beside the region ([0140](../../old-decisions/0140-layers-on-a-built-place-never-rebuild-it.md)).
- **Eligible spots** by geometry and record: on the baked nav, off any
  place and den by a clearance, a sightline and a retreat route by the
  road-approach rule; never a fixed marker; deterministic from the save
  ([0004](../../old-decisions/0004-fixed-difficulty-constraint.md)).
- **Variety**: compositions authored per territory-holder (headcount,
  leader or none, gear by culture and band, a few words of why they are
  here) so no two draws read as clones; a variety measure in the gate.
- **Spawner** in the walk between places, reading the table; the
  2.4 clock loop's route scenario extended to count bands met.

## Loops

Many small loops, each on one subset, each pairing a number with a
picture on every fix turn, each closed by a `reader`
([loops](../loops.md) § Eyes ride every iteration). Each loop's survey is
a checklist file under `docs/phases/2-slice/2.7-loops/<subset>.md`, one
row per measurement with the capture beside it, so the re-measure is the
same survey.

| Subset | The number | The picture |
|---|---|---|
| a weapon class | contact windows (`measure-contact-windows.mjs`), reach, stamina per chain; the sweep row | the class's `visual:check` group and a strip of its chain, judged for feet, reach and contact |
| a fighting species | its band numbers in the sweep; a headless drive: notice, come, attack, flee, die | a strip of attack, hit, flee and die from the creature set |
| a hostile loadout | the sweep against five builds; the loadout gate | a strip of draw, come, attack and flee on the rig |
| a hostile place | a headless drive into the place: attacked on sight, alarm spread, leash home, loot | a still at the moment the first guard draws, from the working tree |
| answered violence | a headless drive in a slice village: strike, kill; who fights, who flees, when the place turns | a strip of the household's response |
| the roaming table | counts per region over many walks against the table; the variety measure; eligibility of every drawn spot | stills of three drawn bands at their spots |
| arrows | the surface table's unit tests; `probe-arrow-flight.mjs` | a strip per material: stick, ricochet, break |
| death and wake | a headless fight to the death, the pile, the wake point, through save | the death screen on the ui-lab viewports |

Captures read the working tree, one at a time; a pod when a sweep is
cheaper than this CPU, booted inside the lane
([0119](../../old-decisions/0119-runpod-is-the-gpu-lane.md)). No publish inside
a loop: loadouts, respawn classes and the table are layers; a place
republishes only its layer after its loop closes. The planner monitors
the loops as they run and makes slow ones faster
([loops](../loops.md) § Rules).

## Acceptance

- Every hostile record in the slice has a loadout, band numbers and a
  respawn class; every encounter socket species has a clip set; the
  integrity gate is green.
- The sweep (every hostile loadout and species × five builds) reports no
  unhittable or trivial case the owner did not accept.
- Headless: a walk between two slice places meets its sockets and at
  least one roaming band; each hostile place attacks on sight; a village
  answers a blow and a killing by the rule; a fight to the death and wake
  round-trips through save.
- Every loop's checklist is green with its reader's close; `visual:check`
  groups for movement, animation and camera green.

## Polish routing

A creature with no clips, an enemy that cannot path, a hostile record
with no loadout or a band that cannot spawn in its region is a blocker.
Moveset breadth beyond the classes is `P2`; boss AI, group tactics,
summons and the finisher are 4.5.
