# 2.9c — Marsh specials: hazards, effect arrows, materials, harvests

**Goal.** What the loading screen tells the player about Black Marsh is
true in play: the wamasu spits lightning and its shock spreads through the water it stands in, peat burns
where fire lands on it, a shock bolt into water hurts everyone standing
in it, arrows carry fire or an enchantment, Hist-sap rubber insulates
against the wamasu, every creature yields what its lore says, and traps
spring from more than a lid. Rulings: [0157](../../old-decisions/0157-the-marsh-does-what-the-lore-says.md).

## Outcome

Every deliverable shipped, live in both hosts (studio and `apps/game`);
reports in `lanes/2.9c-*/report.md`, packet `lanes/2.9c/packet-draft.md`.
(a) `world/sources/registries/hazard-volumes.json` (five rows, each naming
its visual) and `packages/game-core/src/hazards/` (`strikeHazards`, one
host per walk in `useMagicBinding`, drawn by `packages/scene/src/ui/hazardVisuals.ts`);
burning peat 7 s from Skyrim's FireSpellHazard, a strike inside a live
burn refreshes it; the wamasu casts shockball on its roar clip (no howl
in its pool). (b) Fire arrows by workbench imbue for every arrow
material; ammo enchanting by OpenMW's count rule; the stack's
`enchantment` field rides the crafting save family. (c, d) Hist-sap
rubber gear (`resistShock` 15), Stormhold crystals as soul gems, sap
bloodlust and staged sap-poisoning, all 42 harvest rows filled (46
items). (e) `tooling/world-generation/worldgen/mine_traps.py` into `world/sources/placement/traps-mined.json`
(8 cells, 635 bases, 91 gaps in `lanes/2.9c-traps/crawl-report.md`),
`packages/game-core/src/traps/` and `packages/scene/src/traps/`; the Teeth traps
and the Int01 puzzle fire as wired, meshes play their own keyframes, gas
and flame drawn from their NIFs; damage stays as mined (owner call in the
packet). (f) Player scent rows and heart-mud; the `jel-daedra` prose
lint. (g) The new game's pack holds the owner-check set. Owed: the
Dawnguard and Dragonborn trap bases and the fire-arrow blast radius
(Dawnguard.esm is not in the vault); polish rows in the backlog.

## Deliverables

### a. Hazard volumes, peat and shock water

- One home record for hazard volumes (standard 18, world/98 updated):
  shape, effect id and magnitude from `packages/magic`, condition (in
  water, on ground, on touch), lifetime, source (creature id, place,
  spawned by a hit), and what ignites or charges it. The runtime applies
  the effect through 2.9's `castSpell` to every actor inside, player and
  enemies alike; AI weighs a live volume as costly ground but a pursuit
  may cross it.
- **Peat burns**: a fire spell or fire arrow striking ground whose land
  cover is peat lights a standing fire volume there; nothing is painted
  or recorded per patch; no spread; the fire's size and lifetime derived
  and the derivation written beside the numbers.
- **Shock into water**: water is ordinary water. A shock spell or shock
  arrow striking it, or a shock attack from a creature standing in it,
  shocks every actor in that water within the attack's area (an arrow's
  area derived from Morrowind's enchant area rule).
- **The wamasu**: a `creatureCasters` row with a shock attack on its
  roar (the `notice` clip; its set has no howl); standing in water, its
  shock spreads by the rule above. The unread `shock-water` variant is
  deleted.
- Slice instances: the wamasu at the Wamasu Shrine; whichever slice
  places stand on peat ground (the lane lists them from the land cover).

### b. Effect arrows

- **Crafted fire arrows** at the workbench: arrows, `item.linen-wrap` and
  a lore-grounded incendiary the lane sources and records; recipes for
  every arrow material.
- **Enchanted arrows**: ammo joins the enchantable kinds; a stack is
  enchanted whole by Morrowind's ammo rule (cast on strike, the budget
  by count, OpenMW's formula cited).
- The element rides the stack instance (one field beside the temper and
  coating), never a row per material and element; the HUD and inventory
  show it.

### c. Marsh materials

- `item.hist-sap-rubber` and its recipe from `item.hist-sap`; insulated
  gloves and boots and the telescoping wamasu prod carrying `resistShock`,
  each with a sourced mesh.
- Stormhold crystals as soul gems: filled kinds enchant, hollow ones hold
  corruption (the dossier's rule), the `soulGem` field and variants.
- Hist sap rewritten to the lore: on an outsider, bloodlust (strength up,
  personality down, dialogue refused while it lasts); repeated use
  stages sap-poisoning (gold tongue, bark scale) on 2.8's staged-effect
  path. The Hist-hearing step that changes nothing here is 2.11's.

### d. Harvests

- Every `creature-harvest.json` row filled with item rows and four
  alchemy effects each in the dossier (moon-adder venom; daril as its
  prepared drug), every item mesh sourced or the row redesigned.

### e. The Teeth of Sithis traps and puzzle

- **Mine before building.** Everything the mod and game files can tell
  about how each trap and puzzle behaves is mined first, by the
  `kit-mining` protocol (expectations written, a golden fixture on one
  Teeth cell, a fresh batch, then the one full run), into a record the
  runtime reads; nothing about a trap's behaviour is guessed where a
  file holds it. For the three Teeth cells and every other sourced
  xanmeer and ruin cell, and for the vanilla Skyrim.esm and
  Dawnguard/Dragonborn trap base records the mod reuses:
  - the wiring: linked references and their keywords, activate parents,
    enable parents, which trigger fires which trap, which lever opens
    which gate, and the trigger volumes' bounds (primitive sizes);
  - the numbers: script property values on each reference and base
    (damage, push force, reset and cooldown, trigger delay, one-shot or
    repeating, a puzzle's solution and starting state), the hazard,
    explosion, projectile and spell records a gas or flame trap points
    at (radius, duration, magnitude), read as data, never Papyrus code;
  - the motion: each mesh's named animation sequences and their timing
    (fire, reset, open, the pillar's turn steps) and its collision;
  - the sound records each trap and lever plays;
  - the text: the puzzle notes and books that hint a solution, and the
    script names that say which behaviour family a base belongs to.
  A field no file holds is listed with the nearest vanilla equivalent's
  value and the reason, and counted in the report.
- `traps.json` gains triggers `step`, `tripwire` and `lever` and a
  physical damage kind beside the spell kind, shaped by what the mining
  found; disarming by probe and by avoidance.
- The Teeth's traps and its pillar-and-lever puzzle go live as the mod
  wired them, each animated by its own mesh's sequence; the other
  xanmeers' puzzles are 4.5's by the same reading.

### f. Scent and heart-mud

- A player scent multiplier on 2.8's effect stack, read by the
  creatures' scent noticing; being submerged, a mud wallow, eel slime and
  a carried pahnjee (against bog blights) lower it, each a data row.
- Heart-mud: a slow, sticky ground row in `physical-materials.json` the
  feet read, on the land cover the lore names.

### g. Items the player needs for testing

- What reasonably small set of items does the user need to have in their default
  inventory in order to test the things introduced in this phase? Update the default
  inventory so they have those things.
