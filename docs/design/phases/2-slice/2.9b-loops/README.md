## The slice set

Alchemy: Restore Health potion (`restore-health`), a Resist Poison brew, a
poison (`poison` effect) coated on a blade and on an arrow, a fire oil
(`fire-damage`). Smithing: forge a tier-1 weapon from the slice's common
material, temper it to grade 1. Enchanting: disenchant a found magic item
to learn `fire-damage`, enchant a dagger with it from a petty soul, recharge
from a lesser soul. The worked examples live in world/76 §124 and §123.
