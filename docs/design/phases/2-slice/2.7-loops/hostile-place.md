# Loop survey: the hostile places (2.7 § b, lane 2.7b2)

Rule (decision 0138, 0142): a hostile place's people and creatures notice by
their records, draw, come and fight, call their group, hold their ground and
go home when the player leaves it by radius + the leash band (10 m). Drive:
`packages/game-core/src/ai/hostilePlaces.test.ts` on the baked humanoid nav
(`reactionBaked.test.ts`'s approach route; the drive then walks on down the
mesh to the nearest hostile person so the camp's own people meet the player).
dt 0.1 s, the stand-in walks 1.4 m/s. "Routed" are the hostiles the route
reaches (swimmers are left to their day plan). "Alarm" is the number of the
caller's group in `seen` the tick the caller raised it, over the group's size
(a person calls its place's people within the 20 m earshot; a creature calls
its den by pack rule). "Leash home" is the seconds from the player passing the
leash of every ground until every actor stood home and calm. "Loot" is the
stacks and the item count `lootDrop` returns for every routed hostile, once
each, unowned, at the body. Capture: `lanes/2.7/b-shots/drives/hostile-places.txt`.
