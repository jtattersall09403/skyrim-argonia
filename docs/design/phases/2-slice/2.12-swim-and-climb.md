# 2.12 — Swim and climb

**Goal.** A **Breath of the Wild climbing system** as a generalised rule
over the province's geometry: the character climbs the real collision
mesh of anything the rules call climbable (cliffs, rocks, roots, trees,
walls, stilts, xanmeer), hangs, shimmies, leaps, drops, mantles and
climbs down, limited by stamina alone, with every motion covered by a
sourced clip; **ladders**, placed and climbable; **climbing out of the
water** onto a bank, a plank landing or a hull; and **water stealth** on
the per-observer detection. Behind `PlayerMovementController`, in the
studio and the game, on desktop, pad and the phone. Swim itself is
closed ([0152](../../old-decisions/0152-climbing-is-a-province-wide-system-swim-is-closed-ladders-and-water-stealth-join.md) §4).
Runs after 2.11, as its own phase, never as a side lane (0152 §7); then
2.9c, 2.15, 2.14 and 2.13 ([0158](../../old-decisions/0158-boats-and-sound-before-the-save-loop.md)).
§ d's water exit is a hard dependency of 2.9c.

## a. Climbing research (done) and a2. what is climbable, province-wide

a is the BotW rule set and motion list. **a2** (a `research` agent, first,
before any code) extends §11 into a rule that answers "is this surface
climbable?" from geometry alone, as BotW does: the solid-mesh size
below which a thing is not a climb (a post, a sapling, a rail), the slope
bands (walk below the pivot, scramble to the climb threshold, climb
beyond, overhang limit by normal), surface classes from the collider's
material (stone, bark, root, timber, plaster, metal, thatch, water-lily)
with grip and wet grip, the exclusions (boundary colliders, foliage
cards, animated things, the water surface), what a "top" is (standable
area, slope, clutter) and the walk↔climb transitions in both directions.
Each rule is a number or a predicate with its BotW source or `[OURS]`.
The doc ends with the survey's classification table (§ e) and the
tunables that land in `curves.json` `climbing`.

## b. Clips and ladders sourced

- FreeClimb, EVG Animated Traversal, SkyParkour v3 and EVG CLAMBER

  hash, `SOURCES.json`, register row, root README credit, all in the
  same change). Audition every candidate per motion row with
  `pipeline.audition`; one clip per row of §12, chosen on the contact
  sheet, never the first found. Rig pack `climb` with `requires`
  closure ([0040](../../old-decisions/0040-animation-packs-and-combat-parallel-pass.md)),
  compressed, counted against the download budget (standard 16).
- Ladder loop: FreeClimb's climb-up and climb-down loops retimed to a
  rung pitch, mount from EVGAT's ladder start, dismount from its top;
  if the audition says the hands miss the rungs, SkyParkour's steps as
  mount and dismount. Never a one-shot cut into a loop.
- Ladder meshes: vanilla Skyrim statics and the kit pool, read from
  plugin data (rung pitch, rail width, top rest), one or two models;
  placed by the place skill's layer path where lore puts them (stilt
  house decks, xanmeer terraces, dock walls, lookout posts), every
  placement answering "why here?"; `--place <id>`, layer patch only.
- Wade: vanilla's wading set ingested if the audition finds the knee-deep
  walk reads wrong without it; otherwise `P2`.

## c. The climb system

- **Surface classification at runtime**, from the collision meshes of
  terrain, kits, rocks, roots, trees and buildings by a2's rule; the
  classifier is one function over a hit (triangle normal, material,
  solid size by a local probe) with no per-place data; the boundary
  colliders excluded.
- **Contact** following the mesh normals with hand and foot placement
  (two-bone IK on the sourced poses), inside and outside corners, kit
  seams as corners, kinematic body, local-space contact on moving
  mounts (hulls).
- **Motions**: the §12 list, BotW's set only (0152 §1): attach by moving
  into a climbable wall (no chord, no prompt-gated key), climb in any
  direction, hang idle, ledge hang and shimmy, leap up and sideways,
  drop and release, re-grab while falling, climb-down over a top edge,
  mantle tiers onto flat, sloped and cluttered tops, the scramble band,
  slip when wet, the fall at zero stamina. Water exit is § d.
- **Stamina** from the stats API as the sole limiter ([0004](../../old-decisions/0004-fixed-difficulty-constraint.md):
  nothing scales to the player): the §122.1 costs plus `climbLeap`,
  `shimmy` and `wetDrain` tunables with tests; rain and wetness from the
  weather state lower grip and raise drain ([world/60](../../world/60-water-traversal.md) §46);
  the 10/25/40 m wall contracts as tests on the stats API.
- **Progression and damage**: `climb` metres and `fall` survived reports
  emitted to 2.8's store; fall damage from `safeFallMetres`, drowning
  damage from `drowningDamagePerSecond`, both charged by the runtime.
- **Camera**: the climb band of the follow camera (wall-yaw target,
  recentre, never inside geometry, walls facing away, corners), joined to
  the swim band.
- **Controls and HUD** on keyboard, pad and touch (the phone layout:
  attach by movement, leap on the jump slot, drop on the crouch slot,
  dash on the sprint slot); stamina bar states; no new prompt strings
  unless a reader says the attach is not discoverable, then one line
  through the text catalogue and `text-review`.
- Capability profiles carry real climb speeds; `visual:check` groups
  `climb-*` on the micro-lab's rock, tree, hut wall, stilt and cliff.

## d. Water exit, water stealth, the swim pieces

- **Water exit**: climbing out of the water at the waterline onto a
  bank, a ledge, a plank landing and a moored hull (the lighter
  flotilla's), the mantle tier by the top's height above the surface,
  the shared law 2.15 boards with.
- **Water stealth** as data rows on 2.10's per-observer detection: no
  sneak stance in water; submerged breaks line of sight from an observer
  above the surface and from the bank at distance; surface swimming at
  walking pace at night is low and quiet, sprint-swim is loud, the
  stroke's splash is the noise row; the eye reports as on land. The
  crouch → swim → crouch seam from 2.10 closes here.
- The swim stays as built; only these three pieces and their tests.

## e. Reach, the province survey, the journeys

- **Reach**: climb surface detection and dive depth against breath feed
  a socket's `reach` (`settlement/types.ts` SocketExploration; the rule's
  home [world/20 §12.3b](../../world/20-province-design.md)); `worldgen.exploration_rewards`
  proves a `climb`, `swim` or `dive` reach from measured surfaces and
  water depth, not from its present proxy (a `reachVia` placement
  within 12 m, a wet host).
- **The province survey** (`2.12-loops/survey.py`): a headless probe
  over every built place and the terrain, sampling surfaces on a grid
  and classifying each by a2's rule, with counts per class and per
  place, a list of every surface the rules call climbable that the
  runtime refuses and every one it accepts that the rules exclude, and a
  spot-render list for a reader. It is the measure of "climbable
  everywhere" and reruns in Part 4 per packet.
- Three **journeys as staged scenes** ([loops.md § Rules](../loops.md);
  `2.12-loops/journeys/<id>.json`, the
  [2.9x format](2.9x-loops/journey.md), new verbs `swim`, `dive`,
  `surface`, `climb`, `leap`, `drop`, `mantle`, `ladder`), each at
  level 4 on the headless scene and game runners, staging every block
  proved below it; the owner plays them deployed (level 6): **the dive** (Pearl Lots at night: wade
  out, swim, dive towards the deep lot with the breath bar, up for air,
  take the deep-lot cache, save and reload mid-swim); **the climb** (a
  cliff: attach, corner, shimmy, leap,
  drop and re-grab, mantle onto a cluttered top, climb down over the
  edge, a fall that costs health and pays Acrobatics); **the ladder and
  the roof** (the work camp's barge-pier ladder, rest at the camp's rest
  sign, save and reload, the state intact). The slice quests need no
  more of these places: the one that dives (SX06 stage 10) takes
  `socket.pearl-lots.cache-deep-lot`, which the place has.

## Wide loads

The whole motion set and the whole surface rule at once: a climb judged
with half its clips or on four hand-picked surfaces is judged wrong.

## Acceptance

- a2 exists and every rule in it maps to a unit test, a survey column or
  a `visual:check` scenario.
- The survey runs over all 24 places and the terrain with zero rule
  violations in either direction, and its spot renders pass the reader.
- Every §12 motion has a sourced clip in the `climb` pack with a credit
  row; the `climb-*` and `ladder-*` groups are green; a reader judging
  the contact sheets finds hands and feet on the mesh in every sampled
  frame, on the rock, the tree, the hut wall, the stilt and the cliff.
- The water-exit scenarios' frames pass the reader on a bank, a landing
  and a hull.
- The three journeys pass on both headless runners.
- Stamina, wetness, fall and drowning tests green; the climb and fall
  reports land in the store; capability profiles carry real speeds;
  traversal probes green; a `traversal` save family registered here
  holds swim and climb state through a save round trip, with its test.
- Desktop, pad and the phone (ui-lab stills on the fold-inner and phone
  viewports; touch input driving a climb headless) each pass a reader on
  the controls' discoverability.

## Loop

Every block is checked once, at the cheapest level that proves it, and
the composition only for what composition can break
([0156](../../old-decisions/0156-test-each-building-block-at-its-cheapest-level-compose-once.md)
ladder: 1 data, 2 still, 3 motion, 4 headless play, 5 filmed seam, 6 the
owner's play; [loops.md § Rules](../loops.md)). Each loop is small, owns
its survey file in `2.12-loops/` and runs beside the others on disjoint
files; the planner watches each lane's clock and fixes the slow tool
first. Every motion transition (a grip, a catch, a mantle, a surfacing)
is a level-3 `visual:check` scenario in isolation; nothing in 2.12 is
filmed: no join here needs a level-5 reason (a lane that finds one names
it in its brief and the planner rules).

## Owner play

The owner plays the finished game on the deployed build at the opening
work camp, where a cliff, stilts, decks and a xanmeer (a stepped Argonian
temple) terrace stand together; every check before this is ours. Walk
into the cliff and climb it, shimmy round the corner, drop off halfway and
catch the wall, then climb down from the top. Climb the stilts and the
ladder. At Pearl Lots at night, swim out and dive to the deep lot's cache.

## Polish routing

Wading clips and aquatic combat are `P2`; the open swim rows stay where
they are. A surface the rules call climbable that refuses, a character
floating off the mesh, a missing motion or a ladder with no clip is a
blocker.

## Gotchas

- Never tailor a surface: a place-specific climb fix is a rule defect.
- A one-shot cut into a loop is not a legal edit; retime only.
- No studio mode or flag for a climb ([0149](../../old-decisions/0149-the-studio-plays-the-game-no-modes-flags-or-switches-for-game-actions.md)); the journey runner's
  verbs are real input.
- Ladder placements are layer patches, never a republish.
- The survey reads the published colliders, never filenames or labels.
