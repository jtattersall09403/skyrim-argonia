# 2.13 — Persistence and the loop (the Part 2 exit)

**Goal.** Every system saves and loads; the whole slice is played as one
loop, scripted and then by the owner; the coverage gate proves nothing is
missing.

## Starting state

Written by the agent that closes 2.14, the last phase before this one
([0158](../../old-decisions/0158-boats-and-sound-before-the-save-loop.md)):
which systems have registered serialisers (2.9c, 2.12 and 2.15
included; boats only if the owner kept them), which drives exist, what
the ledger says is red.

## Deliverables

- Save and load across every registered system from every slice state;
  migration of one older `schemaVersion` as a test; export and import of
  a save file; the hidden suspend-save restores mid-fight.
- The **coverage gate** (`tooling/repo-standards/slice_coverage.py`):
  reads `docs/research/combat-and-systems/slice-systems-checklist.md`, and for every
  `yes` row asserts a package path, a scripted play, a save round-trip
  test and a slice-place evidence line; fails on any blank.
- The scripted loop: one headless session that explores, talks, trades,
  steals, fights, loots, levels, rests, casts, sneaks, picks, quests,
  swims, climbs, sails (a boat left at a mooring is found there after a
  reload), saves, reloads, and asserts the end state.
- The discovery tracker: discovered places and spots with an
  unvisited-spot count, saved, read by the headless loop and by the
  4.2 exploration rule. Opening a `hidden-cache` container socket
  registers that spot as found and saves it. This is the
  exploration-reward rule of [2.3 § c](2.3-items-and-containers.md);
  its one home is [world/20 §12.3b](../../world/20-province-design.md).
  The scripted loop opens one of the slice caches
  (`socket.pearl-lots.cache-deep-lot` or
  `socket.the-wamasu-shrine.cache-sunk-offerings`) and asserts the spot
  is counted after a reload.
- The owner's playthrough packet: a start URL, the loop in plain English,
  how to reply.

## Acceptance

- The coverage gate green; the scripted loop green; the owner's
  playthrough returns no blocker.

## Loop

The one loop of the part: the owner's findings and the gate's reds are the
measure; one diagnosis across systems; one fix batch; the owner plays once
more.

## Owner check

The playthrough. "Play the slice start to finish on desktop and on the
phone; list what broke, what was missing, what felt wrong."

## Polish routing

Every finding tagged by the polish rule and justified in the row; the
planner reviews the tags before the part closes.

## Close

The Part 3 README's Starting state written; PROGRESS row done; the
`routing-audit` skill over this folder; one decision record (what the
slice proved, what it changed in the data model).
