# 2.10 — Stealth, crime and locks

**Goal.** The player sneaks, steals, picks pockets and locks, probes
traps, and meets the law: a per-observer detection model with the eye
on the HUD, witnessed crime as an Owing entry on a regional ledger, a
guard stop you pay, serve or resist, every lock, key, zone and owner in
the slice places set per record
([0151](../../old-decisions/0151-stealth-crime-and-locks-ship-whole-pickpocket-is-the-one-gamble.md)).

## Deliverables

Records first (standard 18, [world/98](../../world/98-data-model.md)
updated in the same change), then the runtime in `packages/`, then the
screens, then the slice content, then the gate.

### Records

- `detection` rows: the notice profile per actor archetype and
  creature (spot, cone, sight, hearing, scent, alarm radius, search
  timeout), the gait, armour-class and surface noise tables, the light
  factor table; the sneak-attack band table and the one-shot
  exclusions (aware, health band, boss or ward flag).
- `locks.json` grows: every lock in the slice places (doors and lids),
  the class `none | simple | hard | unpickable-key | quest-sealed`, the
  trap slot; pick and probe quality values as rows; a key item record
  per key.
- `traps.json`: trap kinds as data, each an effect from the 2.9
  catalogue with its visual family, a trigger (pick, open), a probe
  threshold and whether it resets.
- `zones` on the place record: private volumes with owner (person,
  household or faction), hours and the standing that admits; the
  `watch` row per place (who answers, radius, custody post) or none.
- `owing.json` per region: the assessment rates per crime kind, who
  assesses, the custody rate (hours per nushmeeko), the amnesty; the
  ledger state lives in the save under its own system id.
- Pickpocket rows: the base chance by Sneak against the target's spot,
  the item band by weight class and value, the worn-armour exclusion.

### Runtime

- One detection API: the notice service, with `stealthStep.ts` moved
  onto it and the awareness API deleted; state names from 0092; the
  eye's four words as the player's reading of the worst state across
  observers; light, gait, armour class and surface feed the noise and
  light terms; hearing range read; per observer, never shared.
- Search: a suspicious or engaged observer walks to the last known
  position, looks, widens, gives up on the timeout and returns to its
  post or route; the alarm call puts hearers into suspicious; a found
  body alerts its finder; the searching window allows a reduced sneak
  attack from outside the cone.
- Sneak attacks on the band table with the one-shot rule and its
  exclusions; melee and bow; an arrow's impact is a noise at its point;
  draw time crouched equals standing; the aim camera stays while
  crouched.
- Locks: the verb on doors and lids reaches `LockScreen`; the Security
  check with pick wear by level over score; keys and Open always work;
  the unpickable class opens to its key or its spell; probes find and
  disarm the trap slot and wear; an unprobed trapped pick fires the
  trap's effect at the player with its visual family and a hit reaction.
- Pickpocket: a verb on a person while sneaking and unseen by them; the
  sheet shows the percent per item; a seeded roll per attempt; success
  takes silently, failure turns the target and files a witnessed
  pickpocket.
- Crime: trespass (zone), theft, pickpocket, assault and murder typed
  from the witnessed events and the verbs; the Owing entry on the
  region's ledger; the self-defence rule; standing effects through
  2.4's runtime; greetings read the ledger; the 72-hour amnesty.
- The guard stop where a watch exists: pay in gold, serve (custody
  advances the clock by the sentence, costs pending skill progress,
  wakes at the custody post), or resist (the watch turns hostile
  through answered violence, assault filed; clearing the Owing makes
  the place peaceful again via 2.11's hooks). A place with no watch
  keeps community justice (0151 item 2): the nearest person who
  witnessed a crime there walks up and challenges as a guard would;
  pay (the place's Owing in gold, and a caught theft's thing handed
  back to its owner) or resist (answered violence, assault filed); no
  custody.
- Custody, picking and sneaking report skill use.

### Screens

- The eye and the crosshair on the HUD grid, shown while sneaking or
  drawing; the lock screen wired, with the probe action; the pickpocket
  sheet (the target's pockets with a percent per row); the guard-stop
  dialogue entry with the three choices; the ledger on the character
  sheet. Every screen on three viewports and every input.
- The studio dev panel keeps the sneak-eye control as a debug override:
  "live" plus the four states; live is the default and the only thing
  the game reads.

### Slice content

Every door and lid in the slice places with its lock, key and owner;
every home, store and counting house with its zone and hours; every
place's watch row from lore ([20-world-provisions](../../quests/20-world-provisions.md)
guard and patrol rows); one or two trapped locks; one unpickable lock
with its spell key; keys placed or carried by the person the lore
gives them to; pick and probe stock on the sellers the lore allows.

## Owner play

The owner plays the finished game on the deployed build; every check
before this is ours. Rob a house by night and by day, get caught, and
pay once, serve once and fight once. Pick a pocket in the Greenspring
crowd, pick a lock and spring a trap. The question afterwards: did the
eye tell you what was happening, and did the response feel fair?

## Polish routing

Reprisals, courier letters, hired thugs, contraband, notoriety and
pursuit by inference are 4.5; distraction verbs beyond the arrow are
4.5. A guard who ignores a seen theft, an eye that lies, or a trap with
no visible effect is a blocker.
