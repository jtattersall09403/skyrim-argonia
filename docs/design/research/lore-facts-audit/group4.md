2. **Grave-stake system** (xul-vaat, grave-singer, bog blight, Ux-Teeba, BC05, calendar Xulomaht): one record shape (carved life-story text, owner person id, status in/pulled) and one rite service; item `grave-stake` already priced.

4. **Food and ingredients** (raw fish cuisine, daril, sap, pressed leaf): item rows in price index plus 4.5 cooking; one ingredient table.
5. **Social-standing rules** (slights forgiven, Dunmer hatred, ojel, lukiul, tree-minder arbitration): disposition modifier table by race/tribe/upbringing and guard-stop "arbitrator" alternative.
6. **Rites and sports as staged quests** (Naming Day, Chukka-Sei, Teeba games, instrument): quests with skill checks; no new animations (no art).
7. **Flavour only**: jel grammar, egg-tooth, centipede pillows, Dominion 2E 582.
