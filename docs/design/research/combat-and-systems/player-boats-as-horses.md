## 1. Skyrim's horse system, end to end

Method. Plugin data is read with the repo's own reader: `tooling/world-generation/worldgen/esp_index.py` (`Plugin`, `walk`),
the route [world/sources/placement/README.md](../../../../world/sources/placement/README.md) § Vanilla Skyrim.esm documents, on
`<vault>/skyrim-source/Data/Skyrim.esm`. No tracked miner reads FACT, ACHR or marker refs; a throwaway script did (see Recommendations).
Plugin strings are localised (FULL is an index), so names below are editor ids. UESP pages are quoted by name.

### 1.1 What the plugin says (Skyrim.esm)

- P1. `HorseCost` (GLOB 000f49bd) = 1000.0; `CarriageCost` (00050765) = 20.0; `CarriageCostSmall` (00107702) = 50.0. UESP *Skyrim:Horses* agrees: 1000 gold, all stables.
- P2. Five stable ownership factions exist, one per city: `WhiterunStablesFaction` 00093b1f, `MarkarthStablesFaction` 0004c446, `WindhelmStablesFaction` 00047cb3, `SolitudeStablesFaction` 000c13f7, `RiftenStablesFaction` 000a2524; each FACT DATA flags = 0x8000 (the "can be owner" bit), crime tracking off. `CarriageSystemFaction` 00017f04 = 0x8001.
- P3. `PlayerHorseFaction` (00068d78): flags 0, one relation (XNAM) to `PlayerFaction` (00000db1) with combat reaction 2 (ally); not an owner faction. It is the faction a bought horse joins, not who owns it.
- P4. Each stable has TWO horse bases: a for-sale `<City>StablesHorseUnsaddled` (Windhelm 00109823, Solitude 00109827, Riften 0010982a, Markarth 0010982b) and a `<City>PlayerHorse` (Whiterun 00109e3d, Solitude 00109e3e, Riften 00109e40, Markarth 00109e41, Windhelm 00109ab1). The player horse is the saddled one that appears after the sale; both ride template `HorseRace` (000131fd) with keyword `ActorTypeHorse` (00026110).
- P5. The five player-horse refs are persistent (ACHR flag 0x400) and owned: Whiterun `PlayersHorseWhiterunRef` (00068d74), Solitude (000ce66f), Riften (000984ab), Windhelm (0009848c) by their `*StablesFaction`; Markarth (0003f351) by the NPC Cedran (form 0001338f), not the faction. UESP *Skyrim:Horses* Bugs records the Unofficial Patch moving all of them to the stablehand because a faction owner let a befriended stable hand lend a 1000-gold horse for free.
- P6. The for-sale refs are owned by the same factions and stand in their stable cells: Windhelm `WindhelmBridge01` (00098491), Riften `RiftenCityNorthGateExterior` (000984a1), Markarth `MarkarthExterior01` (00098493), Solitude `KatlasFarmExterior` (0009b7a2). Solitude's stable IS Katla's Farm (UESP *Skyrim:Stables*: six stables, five cities plus Katla's Farm).
- P7. The owned horse's "home" is a linked marker: each player-horse ref carries one XLKR (null keyword) to an XMarkerHeading: Solitude 00109e3f `SolitudePlayerHorsePaddock`, Whiterun 00109e3c `WhiterunPlayerHorsePaddock`, Riften 000984ac `RiftenPlayersHorseStart`, Windhelm 00098492 `WindhelmPlayersHorseStart`, Markarth 0009846b `MarkarthPlayersHorseStart`. 5 refs, 5 links, one marker each.
- P8. Five more markers pin where the horse FOR SALE stands: `<City>StablesHorseMarker` (Markarth 0010982c, Riften 00109829, Solitude 00109828, Whiterun 00109826, Windhelm 00109825). The paddock and the sale marker sit within ~3 m of each other (Whiterun: 267,-159 vs 266,-162 m).
- P9. Fast-travel arrival has its own markers: 8 `*HorseMarker` refs (Riften 00000e85 and Fallowstone Cave 0010e0e9 on base `HorseMarker` STAT 00000012; Windhelm 0010fd7e XMarkerHeading; Markarth 00035f2d, Rorikstead 0010fd7d, Dragon Bridge 0010fd7c, Falkreath 0010fd7b, Riverwood 0010fd7a XMarker). Small towns without a stable (Rorikstead, Dragon Bridge, Falkreath, Riverwood) have one, so the horse can follow you anywhere a marker exists. UESP *Skyrim:Horses* Bugs: "locations with horse markers".
- P10. Shadowmere (NPC 0009ccd7, ref 0009ccd8 flags 0xc00 = persistent + initially disabled) has NO owner on the ref, an ability `AbShadowmere` (0010de56) and a DB faction; the "returns after ten days" rule is in script, not in the record (UESP *Skyrim:Shadowmere*).
- P11. Scene and purchase live in quests, not records: QUST `stables` 00068d73, `DialogueWhiterunStablesScene1-4`, `DialogueMarkarthStablesBanningCedranScene01-02`. Script bodies are design reference only; their logic below comes from UESP.
- P12. The carriage seat is FURN `CartFurnitureHorse` 00105d4d with keywords `LinkCarriageHorse` 00107700 / `LinkCarriageSeat` 00107701 (the player is seated on furniture while the cart travels).
- P13. A horse witnesses crimes (UESP *Skyrim:Crime* l.12: "Most living creatures can witness crimes, including horses"); player-owned horses reporting was patched out (USSEP 2.1.3, same page).

### 1.2 What UESP says (rules the plugin leaves to script)

- U1. Buy: 1000 gold at a stable; the horse appears ready to ride (UESP *Skyrim:Horses* "Purchasable Horses"). All horses share speed, stamina, health; only Shadowmere and Frost differ.
- U2. Own: an owned horse stays where you dismount; stolen and unowned horses "run home" (same page, Finding a Horse).
- U3. Only the horse you last rode travels with you; a bought horse "returns to where it was purchased as soon as you mount a different horse"; quest-reward horses stay where left (same page, Notes).
- U4. Fast travel: an owned horse you ride moves to the stables outside the destination city; an unowned horse returns to its start; "will fast travel with you to places if lost" (Horse Abilities); leaving a dungeon by another exit and fast travelling reunites you (Notes). Fast travel works while overencumbered only on a horse (UESP *Skyrim:Fast Travel* l.79).
- U5. Steal: mounting an owned horse is a crime; a 50-gold bounty in that hold, repeating each time a guard notices you mounting a stolen horse (even the same one); sneaking while mounting avoids the bounty until you mount in plain sight (UESP *Skyrim:Horses*). The horse stays "stolen" (it runs home when you dismount).
- U6. Free horses: borrowable after a favour (Riften brawl or Shadr's debt; Katla's harvest); dead hunters' horses become free; military camp horses are free only if you side with the camp; unowned wild horses "follow a walking route" and reset to spawn when you fast travel (same page).
- U7. Absence: no decay is recorded; an owned horse waits where left. Dead horses owned by the player respawned at the selling stable in a USSEP-fixed bug (same page, Bugs), so even Bethesda's data model had no clean "replace" rule.
- U8. Death and replacement: killed under you, you are ragdolled and cannot attack or defend until you stand; buying again costs 1000 again; Shadowmere returns ten days later at the death place (or his black pool if drowned) (UESP *Skyrim:Shadowmere*); Frost and Arvak are the other uniques.
- U9. Followers: almost all followers will not ride stolen or owned horses (UESP *Skyrim:Crime* l.19, l.337); a follower's stray hit can turn the horse hostile (Horses, Notes).
- U10. Combat from horseback: enabled since patch 1.6; melee and ranged but NOT magic; dual-wield always uses the right weapon; shields and staves cannot be used; torches can (Horses, Horse Abilities and Notes). A horse "can distract and attack enemies", "has little courage" and bolts if you dismount to fight.
- U11. Terrain: horses climb "extremely steep slopes", swim "very slowly", die from falls that would hurt you (Horses, Horse Abilities and Notes); "Dismount Horse before going through door" blocks cave and building doors (Notes).
- U12. Geography: six stables, one per city plus Katla's Farm, placed OUTSIDE the walls at the main road gate ("just outside the gates of Whiterun", "outside the northern entrance to Riften", "opposite the entrance" at Markarth) (UESP *Skyrim:Stables*, *Skyrim:Riften Stables* l.1-3). Hearthfire adds three homestead stables, each needing a Steward payment of 1000 (Horses, Purchasable).
- U13. Carriages are the horse's twin: 20 gold between the five major hold capitals, 50 to the four small ones; drivers never sleep (UESP *Skyrim:Transport* Carriage). Ours: ferries (2.6).

## 2. Our equivalent, walked step by step from the player's seat

Names: class ids `boatclass.<slug>`, boat ids `boat.<slug>`, sockets `socket.<place-slug>.<slug>` (standard 2). Steps are rules; sources are the repo or the Skyrim line they answer. A number with no source is an EXTRAPOLATED default (distance, speed, time, fee): it lives in the `BoatClass` catalogue or a tuning file and the owner retunes it without code.

### 2.1 One journey

- J1. Find: a boat is found (owner null, beached or adrift; free to take, 0039 "unowned is the norm"), bought (a boatwright or ferry operator NPC, price in § 4, needs a free berth at the seller's mooring: capacity is the limit), or taken (owned by an NPC or faction, J9). Skyrim sells at 1000 (P1); our price ladder is § 4.
- J2. Purchase moves ownership only: `Boat.owner = player`, `lastMooring` = the seller's socket the boat sits in, boat stays at that socket (Skyrim: horse appears at the stable, U1). It does not teleport; the player walks to the berth.
- J3. Board from a landing or a beach: walk up, activate (the 2.3 interaction arbiter prompt "Board"); the player snaps to the helm slot; 0.6 s; `PlayerMovementController` enters boat mode (swim-climb research §2.4, Valheim/SoT snap-to-seat).
- J4. Board from the water: within 2.5 m of the gunwale and ≤ 1.1 m below the rail, activate "Climb aboard" and play 2.12's climb-out (2.12 § b "getting out onto a bank, a deck or a boat (2.15 reuses this)"). A boat moving faster than 1.5 m/s relative to the swimmer cannot be boarded (nobody climbs onto a moving hull).
- J5. Board from a bank: a boat grounded at a bank (J12) boards like a beach; a boat adrift 3 m off the bank requires swimming (J4). No leap-aboard.
- J6. Cast off: leaving the socket frees its capacity slot; the rope is data (`BuoyantMotion.tether`, `packages/game-core/src/water/buoyantBody.ts` l.37), dropped on cast-off and re-tied on moor. Fare, key or permission checks happen here for owned boats (J9).
- J7. Steer: the hull is a `BuoyantBody` (hull probes from its footprint, draught as authored, upright restoring torque; `buoyantBody.ts` l.7-11) driven by `EcctrlVehicle` under the controller (brief § c). Drag acts on velocity relative to the water (swim-climb research §2.2); turn authority scales with way on (§2.1).
- J8. Moor: arriving within 4 m of a socket of an accepting kind (§ 3 M2) shows "Moor"; accepting snaps the hull to the socket pose, sets `Boat.currentMooring`, ties the rope, and the boat sleeps (M9). A socket holds `capacity` hulls; a full one refuses.
- J9. Take a boat that is not yours (the stolen case): casting off an owned boat is a theft on 2.10's Owing ledger by its witness rules ([0151](../../old-decisions/0151-stealth-crime-and-locks-ship-whole-pickpocket-is-the-one-gamble.md), [0159](../../old-decisions/0159-boats-are-wind-waker-sailing-dugout-and-sea-hull-first.md)), never a gold bounty (Skyrim's flat 50, U5, is not ours). After the cast-off the boat is YOURS in data (`owner = player`, `lastMooring` = the socket it was taken from until the player ties it elsewhere, so an untied stolen boat drifts back to its former owner's berth by M3); no stolen flag (0039: "No stolen flag on possessed items, ever"). Only a witness-tied record makes it recoverable by force: if the Owing is resisted and the player taken, the boat returns to the socket it was taken from as evidence (0039: "confiscation survives only for goods a witness record ties to a crime").
- J10. Fall out or get out mid-river: leaving the helm with the boat in water puts the player in swim mode at the gunwale (2.12 swim); the boat does not stop dead (Valheim's convenience is rejected: it teleports state). It carries on at 70 % of speed decaying with drag to the flow speed; classes with an anchor (skiff and up; `canoe:weapons/sfss/anchor` exists) drop it on "Anchor"; a canoe drifts. Drift ends at the first grounding or the next socket. This is the boat 'adrift' state (M3).
- J11. Swim to a wreck and back: the boat keeps its drift (J10); the player may re-board by J4 as long as it is loaded (within 150 m). If the player is > 150 m away the boat is parked by M3's rule, never lost mid-stream.
- J12. Beach on a bank: when `WaterSample.depth` under the keel falls below the class `draughtM` the hull grounds: speed 0, state `grounded`, the keel rests on the bed at the draught datum (§ 4 H2). Re-floating is a controller action "Shove off" (hold 0.5 s) that works only if the water behind the stern is deeper than draught + 0.05 m (the same clearance M2 uses).
- J13. Portage or not at a fall: NOT in the slice. A `vertical-fall` or `sloped-chute` reach is a hard stop for every class; the hull stops 12 m short of the lip (reach `kind` from the water query); the player leaves the boat at a socket or beach upstream and walks. Evidence: 18 falls (drop 5.0 to 119.6 m), 14 chutes (1.2 km), province-wide in `hydrology-graph.json`; none sits between any two slice places that share a component (§ 3.3). Carrying and dragging are Part 4 (world/60 §45: "portage hops resolve to ... real portage features").
- J14. Rapids: `sloped-rapid` reaches flow 1.36-2.3 m/s (`hydrology-graph.json`, 91 reaches, 11.6 km). A class may enter if it may go DOWN; it may go UP only if cruise speed minus flow > 0.4 m/s (§ 4 N2). So the dugout and reed canoe run rapids downstream and cannot return.
- J15. The boundary: the province boundary stops hulls with the same colliders the climb system ignores (2.12 l.28: "The province boundary colliders are excluded from climb-surface detection"; 16d C4 colliders, `16d-border-apron-and-boundary.md` l.490). The hull stops, speed goes to 0 in the blocked axis, a one-line message; it never kills the boat. No water exists beyond; the sea body is inside the boundary.
- J16. Fast travel by ferry: the service dialogue (2.6) is only offered on foot; if the player talks to a ferryman while still aboard, the boat is moored first if a socket accepts it within 8 m, else it is adrift-parked (M3). A ferry fast travel NEVER carries the player's boat (horse analogue U4 is the carriage, not the mount: ours is instant and the boat is not a follower).

### 2.2 The big picture over many journeys

- B1. Leave it and walk inland for hours: a moored boat stays exactly where moored for any length of play, without decay (U7). Save state is `{mooring id | position, yaw, reach id}` plus `condition`; the integrity gate resolves the mooring id.
- B2. The world clock and the seasons act on the SOCKET, not the boat (M7): a boat on a seasonal reach is never left to ground; the dry-season draw-down 0.28 m (`hydrology README` l.125-126: `drySeasonLevelM = level - 0.2 x 1.4 x response`) must still float the class at its mooring.
- B3. Two boats: allowed (a lake boat and a creek canoe). Each occupies one capacity slot where it is moored; the player rides one at a time; nothing "returns to the stable" when you board another (that Skyrim rule, U3, existed to cap the horse that follows you; boats do not follow).
- B4. Lose it: three ways, all recoverable. (1) Adrift beyond 150 m: parked by M3. (2) A boat left in the open (no socket) when the player rests or fast travels: moved by M3 to its last mooring. (3) Wrecked (a quest sets `state = wrecked`): a wreck prop stays, a new boat is a new purchase; there is no auto-respawn (Skyrim's USSEP bug U7 is the anti-pattern). Hull damage in play is 4.5 (world/60 l.326).
- B5. Have it stolen: only by script, never by random NPC thieves: a quest may set a boat's `owner` to an NPC (the boat then moves to a socket that NPC owns). Rationale: 0039 deleted fencing and laundering; a random thief would be an unrecoverable loss, and "a boat that cannot be recovered after a fast travel is a blocker" (brief, Polish routing). The player recovers it by taking it back (J9 applies to the new owner) or by a quest.
- B6. Return from another region: the player arrives by ferry on foot at the destination station; the boat is where it was left; to bring it, the player pays the recovery fee (M4) at the destination's harbourmaster, or walks back.
- B7. Followers and passengers: Skyrim followers won't ride owned horses (U9). Ours: no passengers or cargo on the player's boat in the slice (world/60 l.326, 4.5); `BoatClass.seats` carries the slot count so 4.5 does not change the record.
- B8. Time passes while away: the boat is NOT simulated while the player is > 150 m away; it is a record. On return it is placed from the record (M9) so weather, tide and flow cannot have moved it.
- B9. Taxes: none. Mooring is free at any unowned socket and at any socket whose owner is the player's faction; a third-party socket (owner NPC) charges nothing in the slice but mooring someone else's berth in view of its owner uses the J9 trespass-style witness rule if the berth is marked private (`owner` set and `capacity` taken by their boat).

## 3. The mooring network

### 3.1 Socket kinds and what they are

Home: sockets live in each place's layout (`socket.<place-slug>.<slug>`; world/98 "Socket" row; standard 18). The vocabulary [socket-vocabulary.json](../../../../world/sources/vocab/socket-vocabulary.json) l.8 lists ten kinds (npc, idle, item, container, station, sign, encounter, fauna, ambience, marker); `mooring` is NOT among them, and `worldgen/sockets.py` refuses an unlisted kind (vocabulary `about`). So `mooring` is a new socket kind (2.15b adds it to the vocabulary, `sockets.py` and `game-core/src/settlement/sockets.ts`). One kind, four `mooringKind` values:

- K1. `landing`: a walkable jetty, stair or stage a hull lies beside (Skyrim analogue: the stable yard). Walk-on, service link allowed. Pieces: `docks-v1` `landing-argonian` (tamu_wooddock01, 3.65 x 1.82 x 1.96 m) and `landing-steps` (`travel-services.json` `craft`).
- K2. `mooring`: a post, stake or house-post in water, boat tied alongside; no walkway (marsh doctrine, lore doc §4 rung 2: "House-post mooring"). Piece: `tamu_woodpole01`.
- K3. `beach`: a mud ramp or sand, hull dragged or grounded on it (lore doc §4 rung 1: "Mud landing / drag ashore"). No piece; the ground is the socket, the pose is a footprint on the bank.
- K4. `boathouse`: a roofed berth with secure storage; the only kind that protects from weather and theft sight lines (a boat inside is not visible from outside, so a J9 witness must be inside). No Part 1 place has one; one is placed by the first packet that needs it (hence the kind exists, count in the slice is 0 and says so).
- K5. Fields (standard 2/3): `mooringKind`, `accepts[]` (class ids), `capacity` (hulls), `owner` (npc, faction or null), `valueTier`, `serviceLink` (a `travel-services` service or station id, or null), `minDepthDryM` (the shallowest dry-season depth at the berth), `poseM` (x,y,z,yaw per slot).
- K6. A `berth` on a travel-service station (`travel-services.json` l.1426, l.1448; 2 stations, `hullClass: canoe`, depths 0.96 and 0.72 m) becomes a `landing` socket with `serviceLink` = that station; the berth field is derived from the socket afterwards (standard 18: one home).

### 3.2 The recall rule (decided)

- M1. A boat at a socket stays there, forever, until the player casts off (U2/U7: an owned horse waits where you leave it).
- M2. A socket accepts a hull when: class in `accepts`, a free slot, `minDepthDryM >= draughtM + 0.05`, and the hull is within 4 m of the slot pose.
- M3. A boat NOT at a socket when the player leaves it (dismount in open water, on a bank, or at a socket that refuses it) is `adrift`. It holds its drift while the player is within 150 m and loaded. When the player rests, fast travels, enters an interior, or goes > 150 m away, the boat is RESOLVED by this order: (a) the nearest accepting socket within 120 m along the water; (b) the home mooring if the class's `navigableReach` joins the boat's reach to the home mooring's reach and the straight water path is <= 4 km; (c) else it stays put as a grounded boat at the nearest bank (state `adrift`, `recoverable = true`). A boat is never deleted.
- M4. Recovery for a fee: at any place that holds a `landing` or `boathouse` socket with a service link, the harbourmaster or ferryman sells "bring my boat": the boat is placed at that place's nearest accepting free slot. Fee = 10 % of the class price rounded up, minimum 5 gold (the ferry fare floor `travel-services.json` services[].fare = 5, 8 and 15-20; `economy.json` `services.ferry` = 12). Available whenever `recoverable = true` or the boat is more than 300 m from every socket. A place with no accepting free slot refuses and names the nearest that has one.
- M5. Why not Skyrim's "owned stays, unowned runs home" split: ownership of the boat in the 0039 model is by possession (no stolen flag), so the split has nothing to key on. Why not a pure auto-return: it would make the boat a follower and would hide the mooring network the owner wants to see.
- M6. Only one home mooring per boat. The player may rehome (M4 fee 0) by mooring at a socket whose owner is the player's faction or null and choosing "Make this home" while moored there.
- M7. Seasonal and tidal safety: a socket is placed only on a perennial reach or a body whose `drySeasonLevelM` still floats the largest accepted class at `minDepthDryM`. Tidal water (`altitudeBand: tidal`, `tidal: true`, 3 of 615 reaches) moves with the tide: the boat is tethered (`BuoyantMotion.tether`) and floats; the socket pose is relative to the tether.
- M8. The ferry service and the boat mooring are one pair of records, not one: a ferry hop follows the lane; the boat does not (J16).
- M9. Performance: a moored boat at rest sleeps (`BuoyantBody` "rest when the player is out of range", `buoyantBody.ts` l.11) and beyond 150 m is a record only. This DECIDES swim-climb Open Question 7: moored boats go kinematic/asleep, not deadbanded, because a village of 20 canoes at 6 probes each is 120 probe samples a step the player never sees.
- M10. Visibility: the mooring layer is plotted on the 2D map and the 3D studio (brief § b) as points coloured by `mooringKind`, sized by `capacity`, with a ring for `serviceLink`; navigable reaches per class as a toggle drawn from `navigableReach`.

### 4.2 Which reaches each class may enter (`navigableReach`)

Rules (derived data, regenerated from the graph, never hand-edited; the projection records the graph's `contentSha256`):

- N1. Clearance. A reach is enterable if its depth minus the 0.28 m worst-case draw-down is at least `draughtM + 0.05`; a SEASONAL reach is enterable in the wet season at `depthM >= draughtM + 0.05` only and the boat grounds when it dries. Band 1 passes reed and dugout all year, skiff in the wet season only; band 2 passes skiff and river boat; band 3 passes sea hull (1.72 m vs 1.25 m).
- N2. Current. Moving against the flow needs `cruise - flowSpeed > 0.4 m/s`. Reed canoe: up to 1.0 m/s flow; dugout 1.4; skiff 1.6; river boat 2.1; sea hull 2.6. Rapids run 1.36-2.3 m/s, so they are down-only for the dugout (up only at their gentlest) and closed to the reed canoe and everything bigger.
- N3. Kinds. `horizontal-channel/-tidal/-backwater` by N1. `sloped-riffle` (slope 0.035-0.065, flow 0.71-2.3 m/s) by N1 and N2 for reed and dugout at any band, skiff at band 2+, river boat at band 3. `sloped-rapid`: dugout only. `sloped-chute` and `vertical-fall`: no class (J13).
- N4. Standing water (the marsh family, lakes, tarns): enterable when mean depth minus 0.28 x `seasonResponse` is at least `draughtM + 0.05`. Ocean and lagoon: by the wave limit (N5).
- N5. Surf and swell. The class `maxWaveHeightM` is compared with the local wave height from `WorldWaterQuery` (shore swell, swash and storm gain live in `game-core/src/water/waves.ts`, exported from `water/index.ts` l.1); above the limit the hull cannot leave the surf zone and grounds on the next trough ("a canoe grounds in surf", brief § c). The limits are tuned in 2.15c with the controller.
- N6. A boat at rest can be in a reach it may not ENTER by N1-N5 (a seasonal reach that dried): it is not deleted; M3 resolves it on the next rest.

## 6. Combat from the deck: what 2.7 must leave

Today: bow flight is a drag-integrated body (`game-core/src/combat/arrowFlight.ts`: `advanceArrowVelocity`, `ARROW_LIFETIME_SECONDS` 8, gravity scale 2) with launch speed from arrow energy (`ballistics.ts`); aim comes from `aimConvergence.ts` (convergence 3-120 m); arrows have NO water interaction (no `water` token in `arrowFlight.ts`, `arrowSurface.ts` or `arrowPlant.ts`). 2.7 § a already promises "attacking and shooting from a moving platform (the hull's velocity in the aim and the hit), targets on water" (`2.7-combat-and-encounters.md` l.38). Skyrim's rule for the same job is U10 (no magic, right-hand weapon only, no shield).

- D1. A frame provider. `PlayerMovementController` boat mode exposes a `PlatformFrame { id, velocityAt(worldPoint), angularVelocity, up }`; combat, aim and animation read it and never see ecctrl. 2.7 defines the type; 2.15c implements it. Test: a hull at 3 m/s reports 3 m/s at every point on deck.
- D2. Projectiles inherit: the arrow's launch velocity = the aim-derived velocity + `velocityAt(nockPoint)`. Without it a 3 m/s hull moves a shot about 2 m at 30 m (launch speeds are 36.8-67.0 m/s, `archery-ballistics.md` l.78-83, so ~0.67 s of flight at 45 m/s before drag). Test: a stationary target beside a moving hull is hit, and a shot fired at rest and underway land the same place relative to the water.
- D3. Aim in the platform frame: the convergence point (`aimConvergencePoint`) and lock-on are expressed in platform-local space so a rolling deck does not steer the reticle. The camera follows heading, not roll (swim-climb research §2.4: heavier smoothing than on foot).
- D4. Melee sweeps and hitboxes are evaluated in the platform frame (the swing arc is local), and the hit's relative velocity uses `velocityAt` on both bodies; poise and stagger take the hull's acceleration as an input, capped, so a sharp turn can stagger a standing player (the sandbox's poise model, `poise-and-stagger.md`) but a steady heading cannot.
- D5. Mode gating (our rule, not Skyrim's): seated pose (paddle, row, pole): the weapon is sheathed and drawing it stands the player up in 0.6 s, the hull coasts (Realistic Functional Boats: "hide your hands" to row, equipping exits the mode). Standing at the helm: bow and melee allowed with the tiller locked on its heading ("autopilot"), the hull decays to flow speed over 8 s; magic follows 2.9's own rules, no extra restriction (Skyrim's mounted no-magic is a horse-AI limit, U10, that has no counterpart here). Shields: allowed standing (the unlike-horse case).
- D6. Footing on a moving, pitching deck: the player's grounded query must accept a moving collider as ground (the hull's deck mesh) and carry its velocity; no sliding when stationary relative to the deck. Test: a player standing at rest on a 1 m/s hull has relative velocity 0.
- D7. Falling in: a stagger beyond the rail, a hit while at the gunwale, or the hull capsizing (never: upright torque, `buoyantBody.ts` l.11) puts the player in swim mode; the 2.7 hit reaction must not assume solid ground (no root-motion that keeps going over the side). Re-board by J4.
- D8. Targets on water: swimmers, floaters and creatures partly submerged expose a hurtbox clipped to the waterline (`WaterSample.immersion`); a hit below the waterline is a miss for bows: arrows have no water interaction today, so 2.7 adds one rule, an arrow whose path crosses `WaterSample` surface height stops there (`arrowFlight.ts`, `ARROW_LIFETIME_SECONDS` 8 otherwise); melee reaches 0.5 m below (EXTRAPOLATED). Skyrim has no underwater combat (swim research §3.2); we keep that floor.
- D9. Boarding actions (hook only, 4.5): an `entity.onDeck(hullId)` relation and a seat/slot table on `BoatClass.seats` so an enemy can board; the boat's stand-in is immobile. 4.5 builds crewed enemy boats and boarding fights; 2.15 leaves the relation and no more.
- D10. Witnesses. A boat is a witness-free place only inside a `boathouse` (K4); on open water NPCs on shore and other boats see the player's theft through the 2.4 detection service from the hull's position. Skyrim's horse witnesses crimes (P13, U10); ours does not, because a boat is not a creature.
- D11. Torch and light: a carried torch works while standing or seated (Skyrim allows it mounted, U10); the lantern hook is a hull socket for later.

Done test for 2.7: a scripted bow volley from a moving hull (the `visual:check` group, brief § c) in the sandbox's combat-space probe plus the ballistics test with `PlatformFrame` set; and a unit test that D2's added velocity enters `advanceArrowVelocity`'s initial condition once.
