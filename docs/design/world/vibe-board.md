# Vibe board: the owner's eight reference settings

## vibe-01: Boardwalk under mangrove canopy

- Place: settlement on a flooded forest; a plank boardwalk on log posts with rope rails leads to stilted red-roofed houses. An Argonian stands in the foreground.
- Time and weather: daytime, broken cloud, dry; dappled sun through leaves.
- Light and fog: soft green-grey filtered light, cool shade with warm sun flecks on the planks. Light haze, no heavy fog. Sight runs about 50-80 m through trunks.
- Ground and water: weathered grey wood. Water is blue-teal, fairly clear and calm, lapping straight at posts and trunks (no beach).
- Vegetation: huge broad-crowned trees with arching limbs, hanging moss, ferns and low plants on the platforms. Canopy 15-25 m and closed. Trunks stand in the water.
- Relief: flat water level. Vibe: lived-in, shaded, arboreal swamp town; the canopy roofs the space, dappled light, wood on water. Take the closed canopy, moss and trunk-in-water edges.

## vibe-02: Green misty swamp pool

- Place: deep marsh; a still dark pool bordered by reeds, palms and grass hummocks (wide panorama).
- Time and weather: overcast daylight, humid, no rain.
- Light and fog: strong green tint over everything, grey-green sky. Low ground mist lies on the water and far bank. Sight is about 100 m, fading to a green wall; distant hills show a faint green haze.
- Ground and water: water is opaque olive-brown, matte, no reflection. Muddy flat banks, tall grass, cattails, mushrooms at the front, with sparse twigs and snags in the water.
- Vegetation: tall leaning palms (10-15 m), willow-like drooping trees, dense shrubs and tall grass. Reeds and snags stand in the water.
- Relief: flat to gently hummocky, low hills far off. Vibe: brooding, sickly, quiet. Take the green-tinted fog, opaque olive water and low ground mist.

## vibe-03: Sunlit jungle ruin gorge

- Place: upland jungle gorge, a ruin and a dock or camp at its foot. Carved green-stone pillars and gates, rock walls, an NPC at a fire.
- Time and weather: bright midday, clear blue patch of sky, dry.
- Light and fog: saturated, high contrast, hard sun shafts and deep shade. Hardly any fog. Distant mountain visible through the gap, so sight is long upward but short along the ground.
- Ground and water: dark wet rock, orange-red earth and clay, mossy carved stone, planks. Water not clear; a pale waterfall behind the gate.
- Vegetation: dense palms and broadleaf jungle, hanging ropes and vines, canopy overhead on steep slopes. Brilliant greens.
- Relief: steep: cliffs, rock outcrops, terraces. Vibe: ancient, adventurous, lush. Take the carved-stone ruins grown through, rust-orange earth and sunny saturated greens.

## vibe-04: Lantern-lit treetop village

- Place: settlement; egg-shaped houses of woven roots hang on giant trunks, linked by arched root bridges. Dark forest floor below.
- Time and weather: dusk or deep shade; dry.
- Light and fog: strong warm amber glow from windows against cool dark green-teal. Slight haze in the distance; sight ~60 m.
- Ground and water: dark mossy ground, buttress roots, a black water or soft ground at the base; woven-twig rails and honey-wood platforms.
- Vegetation: huge buttressed trees, purple-leaf crown, drooping willow far off; the canopy is the roof. Understory dark and open.
- Relief: flat floor, vertical architecture. Vibe: fairy-tale, grown rather than built, warm refuge in a dark wood. Take warm lights against cool shade and organic wood-root architecture. (Skyrim logo overlay is a video thumbnail, ignore it.)

## vibe-05: Rainy lantern swamp night

- Place: flooded forest and deep marsh at night; lanterns hang from trees, an Argonian in the foreground.
- Time and weather: night, steady rain.
- Light and fog: blue-teal moonlit cast, small warm lantern points, dim pool of light on the ground. Fog is dense and blue, sight ~30-40 m, silhouettes fade.
- Ground and water: wet dark ground with sparse grass tufts and shallow standing water; shiny wet surfaces, soft ripples.
- Vegetation: big drooping conifer-like or cypress trees, ferns and big-leaf plants, a root arch in the distance.
- Relief: flat, wet. Vibe: eerie, intimate, safe only near a light. Take rain, blue night fog and point lantern light as the only warmth.

## vibe-06: Golden forest shafts

- Place: flooded-forest edge at a village; a huge cypress-like trunk, banana plants, glowing root-houses, a rock wall behind.
- Time and weather: morning or late afternoon, clear after mist.
- Light and fog: strong god rays, gold and green haze, soft bloom, sky pale blue. Mist medium; sight ~60-100 m with a soft fade. Warm doorways.
- Ground and water: grassy and leaf-littered ground, big-leaf ground cover, twisting roots over the path; no water shown.
- Vegetation: very diverse: cypress with hanging moss, banana, monstera, giant flowers (pink, blue), twisted trunks. Canopy 25 m or more, layered.
- Relief: gentle, with large rock faces behind. Vibe: magical, rich, humid, overgrown. Take layered vegetation, light shafts in haze and colourful flowers.

## vibe-07: Fog-drowned gothic arch

- Place: coast or marsh ruin; a ruined vine-covered gothic arch and a hall rising out of heavy fog beside black water.
- Time and weather: overcast, foggy, light drizzle or ash specks; desaturated, almost sepia.
- Light and fog: flat grey-olive, very thick fog; sight about 15-30 m with ghostly shapes behind. No colour but olive and grey.
- Ground and water: dark flat water at the edge, mossy stone causeway, still surface.
- Vegetation: ivy and vines on stone, a few bent dead trunks. Sparse.
- Relief: flat, with the architecture the only vertical. Vibe: dead, melancholy, haunted. Take the whiteout fog density and desaturation as a weather state; it contradicts the lush set.

## vibe-08: Great tree temple plaza

- Place: settlement and ruin; a huge spreading tree grown over a stepped stone temple with a glowing door, thatched domed huts, stone plaza.
- Time and weather: bright day, partly cloudy, dry.
- Light and fog: warm clear sun, deep green shade, blue sky through gaps, almost no fog; sight is long.
- Ground and water: paved stone with dappled shade, carved stone steps, huge roots over the pavement. No water.
- Vegetation: giant broadleaf canopy 30 m or more, hanging vines and red hanging blooms, palms and ground ferns in the foreground.
- Relief: flat plaza, stepped platforms. Vibe: sacred, ancient, Meso-American jungle civilisation reclaimed by nature. Take the scale of one great tree and stone and thatch architecture.

## Summary

1. The owner likes a closed, layered canopy over water or roots, huge old trees with moss and vines, and dappled light.
2. Settlements are organic: wood, thatch, carved green stone, woven roots, fused with trees and warm-lit.
3. Water is dark or teal, calm, and meets the bank straight at trunks, posts and reeds, with no beach.
4. Atmosphere is moist: low ground mist, god rays, a green or blue tint; sight is mostly 30-100 m.
5. Warm light (lanterns, doorways) against cool shade appears in 04, 05, 06 and 08.
6. Contradictions: 07 is desaturated grey fog while the rest are saturated; 03 is hard sun with a steep gorge while the rest are flat; 02 is sickly green and open while 01, 04 and 06 are cooler and closed; 05 is night while the rest are day; the water is opaque olive in 02 and clear teal in 01.
