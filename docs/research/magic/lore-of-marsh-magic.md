## 1. What is distinctive about magic in Black Marsh

Firm canon first, our reading after each.

1. **Argonians are "naturally adept spellcasters"** and Black Marsh day-labourers are "accomplished Illusionists"; the
   female life-phase is "highly intelligent, and gifted in the magical arts", the male phase the hunter (stealth, speed)
   - CANON: Lore:Argonian; Morrowind:Argonian. Racial skill bonuses by game: Daggerfall "train in magery and thievery"
   (Daggerfall:Argonian); Morrowind Alchemy, Illusion, Mysticism +5, Water Breathing, Resist Common Disease 75, Immune to
   Poison (Morrowind:Argonian); Oblivion Alchemy, Illusion, Mysticism +5, Athletics, Security (Oblivion:Argonian); Skyrim
   Alteration +5 and Restoration +5, Waterbreathing, 50 per cent disease resistance, Histskin (a heal power) and, noted on
   the page, no poison resistance any more (Skyrim:Argonian); ESO passives Amphibian, Life Mender, Argonian Resistance,
   Resourceful (healing done, restoration staves, disease resistance, potion yield, swim speed; Online:Argonian).
   EXTRAPOLATED: the pattern across five games is constant: **Illusion, Mysticism, Alchemy, Restoration/healing, water.**
   Destruction and Conjuration are never racial. A Black Marsh system built on that pattern is the lore's own.
2. **Hist-magic "doesn't operate on the same rules as conventional spellcraft"**; a marsh-born Argonian can raise a
   cloud of spores to incapacitate a target - CANON: Lore:Argonian (Hist section). It is innate to the marsh-born; the
   **lukiul** (hatched elsewhere) cannot hear the Hist at all - CANON: Lore:Jel ("lukiul"), `hist-and-sap.md`.
   EXTRAPOLATED: a marsh-born spell tradition is *received and bodily*, not studied; a player-facing split between
   "learned" (foreign, tome-based) and "given" (Hist, sap, kin) magic is natural.
3. **The Hist's sap is the soul, and an Argonian's blood, essence and soul** - CANON: Lore:Hist Sap, Lore:Argonian
   ("Argonian Souls"). Licking sap gives visions, combat prowess, can alter living things; overdose gives gold tongue,
   bark scale, hallucination (Miredancer sap-speakers); on non-Argonians it is a bloodlust hallucinogen - CANON:
   `hist-and-sap.md`. EXTRAPOLATED: the magicka analogue is not "a pool the mind fills" but **sap/blood held in the
   body**, spent at a cost to the body (so Restoration and costs-health spells sit naturally).
4. **Souls return to the Hist and are incarnated again**; those buried by Hist rites carry no Arkay's Blessing "which at
   the very least prevents the souls of other races from being used without their consent"; drained-Hist souls linger
   mad; blasphemers are bound to an urn - CANON: Lore:Argonian ("Argonian Souls"). Soul Trap on an Argonian is therefore
   a **blasphemy**, not a combat tool (EXTRAPOLATED, from the urn punishment and the Remnant of Argon below).
5. **Memory is the Hist's medium.** Mnemic Egg: a dead Hist is "born backwards" into a seed holding the memories of the tree
   and its people, a gateway into the collective mind - CANON: Lore:Mnemic Egg. Grave-stakes carry the whole story of
   a life and keep the body from rising as a bog blight (CANON: Lore:Argonian "Death"; Jel *xul-vaat*). Tide-Born
   of Solstice hold that the sea carries memories to eggs (CANON: Lore:Tide-Born). The Hist "speak through the wind" to
   Bright-Throats (chimes), through dreams to others, rarely through speech (*kuhupwo*, CANON: Lore:Jel).
   EXTRAPOLATED: **memory-magic (Mysticism/Illusion): recall, witness, dream-sight, reading a stake, naming.**
6. **Sithis is Change (*Raj-Sithis*, *vastei*); the pain of holding on is *shunatei*.** The Nisswo teach letting go, make and
   destroy art - CANON: Lore:Nisswo; `sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md`. EXTRAPOLATED: magic that **transforms, sheds, moults,
   rots to renew** (Alteration, Restoration) fits; magic that **fixes, wards forever or preserves** is the pre-Duskfall,
   *xanmeer*, Xit-Xaht error and reads as old, dangerous or mad (see 8).
7. **Rot, poison, disease and water are home**: poison/disease immunity, drugs (daril from moon adder venom, fatal to
   others), Black-Tongue alchemy, Hee-Tepsleel farm crops for Black-Tongue brews (CANON: Lore:Jel "Hee-Tepsleel";
   `material-culture.md` drugs). Alchemists hold "the moon phases dictate the precise positioning of calcinators"
   (CANON: `guilds-and-orders.md` §1). EXTRAPOLATED: poison, cure, disease and potion craft is the **most native
   discipline**, not a sideline.
8. **Old stone sun-magic is real and dead-ish**: xanmeers are stepped pyramids, "powered by Vakka Stones ... superficially
   similar to Welkynd Stones", with defences intact after millennia; Zaht stones (carved from Hist amber) defend
   Hissmir; Stormhold Crystals hold corruption, healing, buffs or teleport; Xit-Xaht "form of stone magic, believing
   stone is theirs to command"; Mazzatun's amber plasm is "chaotic creatia from Oblivion ... associated with Sithis"
   that gave speed and strength at the price of madness - CANON: Lore:Xanmeer, Lore:Hissmir, Lore:Stormhold Crystal,
   Lore:Xit-Xaht, Lore:Amber Plasm. The knowledge of building them was lost at Duskfall (Lore:Xanmeer). Modern
   Argonians tend the xanmeers as cautionary monuments (`sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md`).
   EXTRAPOLATED: **a lost, stone, sun-and-light magic (Vakka, "sun")** belongs to ruins and artefacts, never to the living
   tribes. It is the right home for Restoration "sun" spells that Skyrim already has (Sun Fire, Turn Undead, Circle of Protection)
   *without* them being Divine (Aedric) magic.
9. **Souls can be kept in things**: the Remnant of Argon (a Hist made an amber vessel holding a whole tribe's souls) -
   CANON: Lore:Remnant of Argon; keystones made "through the torment and fear of Argonian captives" by the Barsaebic Ayleids
   - CANON: Lore:Barsaebic Ayleids; Sul-Xan "wide soul snares" - CANON: Lore:Sul-Xan. Soul-keeping is both the Hist's
   last-resort mercy and the Ayleids' crime.
10. **Fire is the province's scar.** The Great Burn of 1E 2828 set the peat afire and "the entire swamp was on fire"
   as far as Soulrest and Gideon, and Argonians remember the Mine-Burners of 3E 427 (CANON: Lore:Argonian; `province.md`
   Banujha). EXTRAPOLATED: a flame spell in a peat bog is an act people have memories of; fire magic is available
   but feared, regulated, and best re-flavoured toward *lightning* (wamasu: "lightning-powered"; "eggs emit lightning"
   - CANON: Lore:Wamasu), *poison*, *spores* and *acid/rot*.
11. **No Daedra words**: "There are no Jel words to describe any Daedra ... the Argonians do not 'taint their language
   with words for abominations from Oblivion'" - CANON: Lore:Jel. Daedric conjuration is not an Argonian tradition at
   all; it is foreign (College of Whispers, Sul-Xan heresy). The Hist-as-conjurer analogues in our records are
   **miregaunts and marsh giants** ("summoned by a Hist to guard, defend or avenge" - CANON: `fauna-hazards.md`) and the
   **xal-krona behemoth** made by the Hist through sap (CANON: Lore:Argonian Behemoth). EXTRAPOLATED: Argonian
   "conjuration" is **calling what the marsh already holds** (miregaunt, bog blight under strict taboo, swarm, wisp,
   wamasu), not opening Oblivion.

What is only our reading: the school mapping in every cell below, the "magic is bodily/sap" frame, any named spell.

## 3. Non-Argonian magic in the province, 4E 201

- **Imperial remnants**: Mages Guild halls were attested at Archon, Thorn, Soulrest, Hissmir and Alten Corimont in the 2E and dissolved at 4E start
  into Synod and College of Whispers; the Synod had "no conclave in Lilmoth, nor any presence at all for roughly 400 miles" (CANON: Lore:Mages Guild, Lore:Lilmoth;
  `guilds-and-orders.md` §2). Foreign mages are only at Gideon, Stormhold, Soulrest, Archon docks. EXTRAPOLATED: they sell tomes openly and are the home of
  textbook Skyrim-style casting; **the contrast is institutional, learned, paid-for, written** versus **bodily, given, inherited, unwritten**.
- **Dunmer**: Stormhold's Dunmer minority; House Dres and Telvanni slave raids for thousands of years, "entire tribes were dragged off in chains" (CANON: Lore:Argonian); Arnesian
  War of 3E (Lore:Black Marsh). The Dunmer brought Daedra-and-spirit-craft; Argonians remember them as slavers and are courteous but wary (province.md Sedge-Wardens). EXTRAPOLATED: Dunmer
  ward-priests and Telvanni-style conjurers are present only near Stormhold and Thorn, where Morrowind spells enter *as the neighbour's craft*.
- **Khajiit and Bosmer on the fringes**: mixed Soulrest; Bosmer seafarers (province.md). Khajiit lunar/Alteration-tradition magic is EXTRAPOLATED only; Bosmer Green Pact/Y'ffre is
  attested only as the failed **Y'ffre heresy** of Argonian refugees (CANON: Lore:Argonian via `sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md`).
- **Kothringi and Ayleid ruins**: Kothringi spellcraft near-invisible, chimes and glass beads (CANON: Lore:Kothringi); Barsaebic Ayleid ruins at Stormhold (Silyanorn), Gideon (Twyllbek), Archon, Thorn; keystones made by
  torment; Fenlord necromancer-mummies (CANON: Lore:Barsaebic Ayleids; `thornmarsh-and-east.md`). EXTRAPOLATED: the Ayleid layer is *the* source of welkynd-style
  light, soul-gem and keystone magic, always sinister; the Sedge-Wardens guard against it.

## 4. What should be absent, rare, feared or changed here

- **Necromancy**: CANON that Argonian burial removes Arkay's Blessing, foreign necromancers "rarely bother with Black Marsh because corpses rot too fast", pulling a grave-stake makes a bog blight, life
  energy clings to stakes (Lore:Argonian). EXTRAPOLATED: **Raise Dead on a marsh grave is a crime against the tribe, not the state**; Skyrim's Reanimate/Thrall spells are foreign, rare and hated; the
  local equivalent is *grave-singing* and pahnjee-warding. Soul Trap on Argonians is blasphemy (urn punishment).
- **Daedric conjuration**: CANON no Jel words for Daedra (Lore:Jel); Sul-Xan is a *Naga heresy*, cursed by the rest; College of Whispers summons Daedra (`guilds-and-orders.md`). Atronach summons and Daedric
  bound weapons should be foreign or heresy. EXTRAPOLATED: re-flavour Conjure Atronach as *miregaunt, marsh giant, wisp, swarm, wamasu* summons.
- **Fire/Destruction**: Great Burn memory (Lore:Argonian); wet land. EXTRAPOLATED: remove or reduce flame spells; keep as foreign/feared; replace with shock (wamasu), poison, spores, acid.
- **Frost**: not native to a humid swamp (only Solstheim/Skyrim; Lore:Argonian says Argonians regulate temperature). EXTRAPOLATED: Frost spells are foreign, northern and rare (the Skyrim collection supplies them anyway).
- **Divine (Aedra) Restoration**: CANON Argonians "worship neither Aedra nor Daedra" (Varieties of Faith: The Argonians in `sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md`). Dibella, Kynareth, Mara
  attested only among Kothringi/Gideon/sailors. EXTRAPOLATED: Skyrim's Turn Undead, Sun Fire, Healing "of the Divines" should be re-skinned to sap, sun (vakka) and Hist; Restoration "of the Hist" is the default.
- **Nordic shouts**: Thu'um is a Nord art (`wind-turning-kynareth.md`). Wholly absent; a Kynareth sailor's call is the nearest allowed precedent (CANON: Online:Winds of Kyne).
- **Fixing and preserving magic**: stone that does not decay, forever-wards, stone-sun defences: the *flawed* old way (Lore:Xanmeer, Lore:Nisswo, Lore:Xit-Xaht). Rare, found in ruins and artefacts.
- **Amber plasm / chaos creatia**: feared and mad (Lore:Amber Plasm). Cult-only or boss-only.
- **Hist-severing and Mnemic Egg**: the worst crime (Lore:Mnemic Egg; Hatching Pools).
- **Mages Guild-style schools and robed mage NPCs**: CANON none within 400 miles; foreign remnant only (`guilds-and-orders.md`).

## 5. Naming: Jel words and patterns usable, and limits

Attested Jel (Lore:Jel dictionary, fetched 2026-11-26) usable as building blocks:
*vastei* change; *ku-vastei* one who brings needed change; *voh-vastei* apprentice; *shunatei* pain of holding on; *xal* sacred; *xal-toh* sacred secret; *toh* secret; *xul* death; *xul-vaat* grave-stake;
*vakka* sun (and first month); *vos* stone; *voskrona* stone-secret; *kur* still-water; *tsona* swim; *hej / hejsetha* vaporous, floating; *wasseek* bright; *haj* hidden; *mota* hunt; *niss* nothing;
*wo* speak or swallow; *kuhupwo* mouth-talk (a Hist speaking); *kaju* dream-beast; *kat* dance; *jum* weave; *tum* house; *uxith* nest; *thtithil* egg; *thtithik* to hatch; *deek / dooka / tsoko* young / mature /
elder; *sei* rite; *chukka-sei* maturation; *gloor* Hist's will; *norg* forbidden; *vahat* unclean/taboo; *ka* apprentice; *kaal* war captain; *raj* elder; *xajhuthi* dangerous; *ixtaxh* precise; *taeed* darkness (potential);
*krona* big; *pahnjee* fumer; *kajthux* giant snake; *masu* big-mouthed reptile (*wamasu*, lightning); *meht* cracker; *uvas* becoming; *toteik* great.
Patterns (CANON: Lore:Jel Grammar): compounds of adjective plus noun or two nouns; fluid word order; head not marked (*bok-xul* head first, *vakka-bok* head last); no tense, no plural; hyphens optional; the names of professions
are descriptive phrases (Daggerfall:Argonian: "Hauls-Ropes-Faster", "City-Swimmer"; Lore:Argonian: personal names often "descriptive phrases"). Tribal names end *-leel / -hleel* (people).
Our repo already ruled the pattern for the Toh-Reel-Ka (`argonia-4e201-state.md` §5): attested words may be compounded on the attested pattern, flagged as our coinage.

**Must not be invented**: Jel words for Daedra or atronachs (the lore forbids them: Lore:Jel); Jel words for *time* or tense; a Jel name for a god other than Sithis (Itz-Teyeesh is the Tide-Born name); any Jel name
for a real tribe or Hist already in canon; a **Jel Hist-spell name that claims to be attested** (name it as coinage). Names that are not Jel should be plain Tamrielic descriptions of a thing ("Spore Cloud",
"Histskin"), which is how Skyrim and ESO name racial powers. The in-game name of a power or spell goes through `text-review`.

**Morrowind naming**: Morrowind's named spells carry Dunmer and Imperial proper names (Almsivi, Alvusia, etc.; `magic-effects.md`) and are Tribunal-faith or Dunmer-house craft, not Argonian; ESO names
Argonian abilities in plain English (Amphibian, Life Mender, Argonian Resistance, Resourceful; Online:Argonian). Oblivion/Skyrim name the race power "Histskin", "Water Breathing"
(Skyrim:Argonian). **Precedent**: plain English plus a place or thing; Jel for people, tribes, offices and places, not for spell names, unless a loan is attested.

## 6. Open lore questions a proposal must rule on

1. **Five schools or six?** The build carries Skyrim's five; the lore pattern is Illusion/Mysticism/Alchemy/Restoration/water. Does Mysticism live as a named category, or as Illusion/Alteration with a flavour?
2. **Is Hist-magic a separate system** (spores, sap-speak, root-bind, sap-body costs) or re-skinned Skyrim spells? It "doesn't operate on the same rules" (Lore:Argonian); a rule must say how it differs for a lukiul, a foreigner or a non-Argonian player.
3. **Who can use it?** Marsh-born versus lukiul; non-Argonians "taste" sap as bloodlust (Lore:Hist Sap). Does the player character, whatever race, hear the Hist? (Hissmir's Trials let non-Argonians speak with it: Lore:Hissmir.)
4. **Where does the soul sit?** Soul Trap and Soul Gems exist (Skyrim, Magic Expanded) but are blasphemous on Argonians; do creature souls differ in the marsh (see `creature-souls.md`)?
5. **Necromancy**: is it removed, foreign-only, or a crime with a tribal consequence (bog blights)? Does Magic Expanded's Resurrection stay?
6. **Fire and frost**: remove, relabel, or keep as foreign? Magic Expanded ships fire/frost/shock touch spells, Flame/Frost Shields, Cyclone; decide which survive.
7. **Daedric summons**: relabel as miregaunt, marsh giant, wisp, wamasu, swarm; or confine to foreign NPCs and the Sul-Xan?
8. **Restoration**: Divine-based spells (Turn Undead, Sun Fire, Circle of Protection, Guardian Circle) re-skinned as vakka-sun and Hist magic?
9. **Morrowind spells**: the owner floats keeping them. Magic Expanded's spells are named for Tribunal-era people and themes; which keep their names and which are re-flavoured?
10. **Mark/Recall, Teleportation, Divine Intervention** analogues: Hist roots carry the *rootworm* tunnels (`hist-and-sap.md`) - is travel a root-network power rather than a Mage's spell? Tied to the Underground Express.
11. **Chaos creatia** (amber plasm) as a power source: allow as cursed, boss-only?
12. **Who sells and teaches?** Nisswo, tree-minders, Gideon chapters, foreign fences (`magic-practice.md` §4): does a spell tome exist at all among Argonians, or is learning by apprenticeship or ritual only?
13. **Thu'um and Skyrim races' shouts**: kept to the Nord body or removed? (Soulslike collection and our opening skip Helgen.)
14. **Sapped or poisoned costs**: does casting cost Health (sap), Stamina, or only Magicka?
15. **Umbriel taboo**: do Hist-summoning spells exist at all given the Toh-Reel-Ka's charge?

## Recommendations

- **Keep the five-school frame; add one skill-tree-neutral layer.** Evidence: the build is Skyrim records (five schools) plus Magic Expanded (`spell-groups.md`); lore's racial pattern (five games) is Illusion, Mysticism, Alchemy, Restoration, water
  (UESP *:Argonian pages). Re-skin within the five (Mysticism content filed under Alteration/Illusion), per the already-accepted filing in `spell-groups.md`.
- **Do not keep Morrowind spells wholesale by name.** Evidence: they are named for Tribunal and Dunmer people (`magic-effects.md`), and the faith-based ones (Divine Intervention, Almsivi) are Dunmer religion;
  Argonians "worship neither Aedra nor Daedra" (Varieties of Faith, quoted in `sithis-nisswo-shadowscales.md`). Keep the *effects*, rename and re-flavour.
- **Lean on Illusion, Alteration (water), Restoration (sap) and poison/Alchemy** as the Argonian core; **demote flame and Daedric conjuration** (Great Burn; no Jel words for Daedra).
- **Mark "foreign" magic visibly** (Gideon, Stormhold, Soulrest, Ayleid ruins) so the contrast of §3 is playable.
- Before any named spell, run the name list in §5 and the *never-reuse-names* check in `story/design/quests/86-never-reuse-names.md`.
