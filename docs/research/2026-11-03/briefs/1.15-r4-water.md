## How
- First measure, on the round-3 build's own data (the DB and `out/terrain`), why pieces hover and face uphill and why surfaces hover: the step rule's output against the ground the generator wrote (the constraint and the piece placement disagree somewhere: find the file:line). Do not guess from the words.
- Then simplify to the owner's rule: water only in cells the ground can hold flat at one level (the whole cell's wet area under the level; banks above it); a step only between two adjacent flat water cells with a level difference, one piece chain per step, the chain's base on the bed and facing down the drop; any reach that cannot be made of flat cells plus such steps becomes a dry gully (reach_kind dry, bed pieces, no surface) or is re-routed in the graph (fewer, flatter reaches: the owner allows the plan to change; record each change with the lore it keeps, 0032). Fewer steps and simpler chains beat clever ones.
- Still water: measure Skyrim.esm's WATR records for lakes versus rivers (flow speed, noise, opacity) and write ours the same; one still type per water class (sea, brackish, blackwater, fresh).
- The whole province water solve plus the terrain constraints must stay under the build-speed lane's incremental scheme (tools/ArgoniaGen/pipeline.py stamps): change only what you must.
- Owed in game (the planner's batch): a `river-steps` journey teleporting to six steps (the three steepest kept, three dry gullies) at the play camera, one still each, plus two still bodies; ledger rows in docs/phases/1-core-loop/checks.md under 1.15.
