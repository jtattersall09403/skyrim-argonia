## Why (owner, 2026-11-03 09:23, #37)
"Still and running water types - we authored several different types of each. Figure out what we can easily implement from the 'do it like Skyrim' system (e.g. can we do different types of swampy water like black water vs green, different types of running water like river vs stream, mountain lakes vs lowland ponds, etc) and do what we can. Look at what's available from Tropical Skyrim too." Ruling in docs/owner-brief.md (2026-11-03). The study found our seven records are four identical stills and three identical flows, templated from a DefaultWater the game never shows (Update.esm and Tropical Skyrim override it), which is the likeliest cause of the round-4 shores reading black-and-green.

## How
- Winner first: for each vanilla editor id, read every plugin in load order and template the last. Write that resolution as code (a helper in WaterRecords.cs or the Python that feeds it), never a pasted colour.
- Types from the DB: no new schema; layer.cell_type joins body kind and strahler. Flow direction from the reach heading, quantised to the four legacy forms.
- Record the lineage: the register row for Tropical Skyrim's and KotM's water records at first use.
