"Shiny reflective land textures generally, e.g. on hillsides: have lots of these, look unnatural."

## How
- Find the pebble texture by its share: the LTEX with the largest area in the round-3 build (the material weights in out/terrain), then its files. Shine on land comes from the landscape normal map's alpha (specular) or a missing normal (the engine's flat default is glossy): measure the laid files' alpha statistics against vanilla's landscape textures (0034) and fix the sets: BM&V's own normal where it has one, Tropical Skyrim's vanilla-named one where not, never our own art.
- Where the gradient falls back to one texture over wide areas (a class with too few LTEX or a wetness/slope band that always wins), widen the gradient from the mods' packs so no texture dominates; wide soft blends as 0033 §3 says.
- Owed in game: two stills (a pebble field's cell and a shiny hillside, picked from the data) in the planner's batch; ledger rows.
