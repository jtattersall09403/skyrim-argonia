## Why (owner, 2026-11-03 08:55, #37)
"'Creatures: lists of 12–21 kinds per region' - is that a similar number of kinds per region as skyrim/the Black Marsh & Valenwood mod? We must have at least as many kinds per equivalent-sized region as those." Ruling in docs/owner-brief.md (2026-11-03). Window 0's measure says four of nine classes fall short of Skyrim and all but flooded-forest fall short of the mod's main worldspace; the dry land lists are the gap (4–5 kinds on the coast, delta and river against Skyrim's 12–20 land species per biome).

- Kinds are species (variants collapsed), the same rule for ours and theirs. Danger bands fixed per band; never a levelled list.
