## Why (owner, play test 3, findings 4, 5 and 32)
"Tree seating: many have bases hovering in mid air (trailing roots touch ground)." "Some weird tree colours: some like a neon blue green, some weirdly washed out and pale (definitely not intentional)." "Not sure the between region plant and tree variation has been done well, different regions felt pretty similar."

## How
- Seating: the placer sinks by bounding box (0033 §e); mangrove-type trees have root tips far below the trunk base, so the bounding box minimum is wrong for them. Measure each tree model's trunk base (the mesh's main trunk cross-section at its lowest ring, or BM&V's own placement offset in its worldspaces: measure how BM&V seats the same model, 0034) and seat by that.
- Colours: list the placed tree bases, their texture sets and MODS alternates against the vault index; a neon or washed look is a missing texture (the engine's fallback), a wrong set, or vertex colour; prove the cause on one model with the mod's own placement as reference.
- Variety: build the per-class lists from the dossiers (0032) and the mods' own worldspace usage, so the interior swamp, the flooded forest, the rootland, the uplands and the coast read differently at the play camera; keep the densest cell's frame time (the round-1 figure) as the bar.
- Owed in game: a `tree-seat` journey (six trees of different kinds at the play camera, one still each, plus one still per class for variety) in the planner's batch; ledger rows.
