# Lane 1.15-r6-claywater-pads-look (round 6)

Evidence: r6-chain23 claywater-look (out/batch/1.15-round-6/20261105T005149/14-claywater-look-1-20261105T013206). No game, no generate.

## Causes and fixes
- **The white slab (02-well-junction-s0; the ford stills 08-*).** The ford stills show the road itself: LDirtPath01 is the road texture, and row 149 already accepts it as "a dry pale lane". The station's slab is the well pad painted the same pale LDirtPath01. `Patches.paint` set weight 1.0 inside the disc and 0 outside. On the 1.83 m vertex grid, a 2 m disc is a square of whole vertices with a step one vertex wide. In the cell -57,11 LAND, the composited dirt weight goes 0 to 0.9 inside one vertex along straight runs of five vertices. Skyrim.esm's wells stand on LDirt02, LFallForestDirt01 or LRiverMud01, and Skyrim's yard paints blend over 1 to 5 vertices (audit §4). Fix: the paint now fades by smoothstep over max(blend_m, 2 vertices) into the ground's own material (test_pad_paint_fades_over_its_blend). The well and stable pads now paint LFallForestDirt01, the rooty floor of our own palette.
- **The guar's post (02-well-junction-s0).** Both pen markers stood at (323.8/325.2, 3059.2), on the well. Each guar's 6 m sandbox held the well, its silt and water, the mortar tub, Livia's spot and the quest board, so the guars walked through props. Fix: the pens and guars, plus socket.claywater-station.pen-kept-guar, move to (342.5, 3076) beside the byre. No prop stands within 7.6 m, and the ground there is 69.00 m, 0.98 m over the water. New rule in `places check`: no prop inside a kept creature's sandbox radius plus 1.5 m of body (test_no_prop_stands_in_a_kept_creatures_pen; on the old pen it flags the well, the well water and the mortar tub).
- **The striped post (02-well-junction-s3).** This is the stable, HTBM's orcawningfull01. Shape 10 (OrcAwningFull01:1) is the collar at 2.68-3.10 m. It shares texture set 9 with the roof and samples that set's strip v 0.70-0.87. In Skyrim.esm's own orcawningfull01, that set is PlatedRoof. HTBM put wicker03 in it, so the collar draws magnified wicker strands as zebra stripes. Its stretch ratio, 1.2, is not the cause. Fix: a new patcher op `shader_from` (nif.Shape.set_shader) has the collar draw with the posts' woodrough08 property (shape 13). Own_Emit (black emissive) is cleared on all three shapes. `meshes patch --check`: 23 meshes, 0 failing.
- **The white cleats and the floating planks (10-signs-full-body-s0; 04/05 s0 are in-wall frames).** BM&V's `sheogorad/dagon fel/cleat.nif` points at `textures\sheogorad\dagon fel\metalironrusty.dds`. BM&V ships only dockswoodplank01 there, so the metal draws white. Its own plank base was deck-seated (0.85 m) beside the jetty, not on it, so it hung over the water. Skyrim's docks moor with their rope posts. Fix: cleat1 and cleat2 are removed (build_ref and layout ops 23 and 24), and the cleat line in WATER_SEATED is dropped. New rule: anything with no z of its own sits within 0.1 m of its ground or at its water seat ("afloat" is red).
- **The raft at the waterline (10-signs-full-body-s1).** This is by design: the mesh's planks run from -0.08 to +0.13 m about the origin. Ferries.esp seats its own plank rafts at 0.00, -0.05, -0.07, 0.00, -0.10 and -0.69 m from their cell's water (6 refs in cells with a water level). Our raft is at 0.00, so it is kept.
