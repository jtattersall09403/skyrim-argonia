## Why, and the fix proposed (not regenerated)
1. Deep fords. The census counts any wet run touching a reach as a ford. Only reach samples that are wet in the corridor get the 0.3 m bed. Three groups are left untouched and keep the water field's carved bed:
   - the 2 samples beside the reach (FORD_GAP);
   - step-channel vertices (`keep=chan`);
   - reach samples that are not wet.

   Fix: in `dry_corridor`, give the FORD_GAP ring and the reach samples the ford bed: lo = hi = level - 0.3 where the cone leaves them wet. For a step channel under a road, cap the channel bed at level - 0.3 in the corridor only; the step chain stays, since the step's edge bed is already 0.3 under the upper level, so the cap only stops a deeper end. Test: a synthetic step under a road.
2. The 19 wet metres sit at causeway ends beside fords (inside the FORD_GAP ring): the same fix covers them.
3. Bank grade. The census measure takes the steepest fall within 30 m of any low causeway point, so natural shores and the slope limit's land are in it. It does not isolate the cone, which the L2 test proves is ≤ GRADE_MAX. Fix: measure only where the ground was raised by `lo` (a diag mask from generate).

## Fix round 2 (built; the planner's regenerate r6-land2 measures it)
1. The ford takes the 2-sample ring beside a reach as well as the reach: lo = hi = level - 0.3 on every wet corridor sample there.
2. A step channel's bed in a road corridor is capped at the vertex's lowest level - 0.3 m (lo = hi = cap where it was deeper). Off the corridor the channel is unchanged. The L2 test `test_step_channel_under_a_road_is_capped_at_the_ford_depth` covers it.
3. Generate writes `road-raised-u8.npy`, the samples dry_corridor raised. The census measures the bank grade only on that mask and fails over GRADE_MAX. Without the mask (the current ground) it measures all low ground and says so.
4. The census prints each road's deepest ford with its reach (id, kind, width).
