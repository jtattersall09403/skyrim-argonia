# Research

Facts about Skyrim, the mods, the machines and the writing method that would cost time to rediscover. A file is
evidence, not instructions; where a file and a decision disagree, the decision wins. Prices and versions go
stale; re-check before relying on one. Many files were written as lane hand-offs for earlier plans: read them for
their facts and ignore their process. Black Marsh & Valenwood (BM&V) is a mod we depend on, so the files about it
are about a source of places and pieces for ours.

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `machines/` | streaming and hosting costs, the play machine's setup and the stack journal (install history, g4dn frame time is CPU-bound), the owner's three early play tests, S3 request cost, the gamepad and touch mapping (`lane-mobile-17.md`), and the per-chunk notes under `play-machine-chunks/` |
| `mods/` | the souls-like, climbing and Morrowind-magic mod landscape; the dependency and reuse audits; Dark Souls Combat SE and parry/riposte notes; Magic Expanded's tomes and schools; how FreeClimb tests a surface; the creature-mod search (`creature-mod-search.md`) |
| `bmv/` | Black Marsh & Valenwood facts: texture-set gaps, the mods it bundles, creature kinds per region, worldspace-building guides |
| `skyrim/` | how Skyrim's own records work: tooling (Creation Kit, xEdit, Spriggit, Mutagen), NPC_ and PACK shapes and navmesh, how a quest becomes a running quest, actor values, respawn, water types (`water-types.md`, `sap-water-bethesda.md`), the native map of where each goal lives in Skyrim's systems, and the level-up sting and music |
| `writing/` | the quest design method (Elder Scrolls and open-world quest design, questline design, the quest shapes, skill-based actions), Morrowind's level scripting (`morrowind-level-scripting.md`) and the post-mortem of the walk 6 opening (`walk-6-post-mortem.md`) |
| `magic/` | the magic proposal and the audits behind it |
| `new-art/` | the new-art proposal and the audits behind it |
| `world/` | joining worldspaces, extending land, interiors and places; what a place gives the player (`new-world-place-gives.md`); the walk 5 measurements of the new world's relief, ground, water, atmosphere, far view and navmesh (`nw-02-walk5-*.md`) |
