# A. Joining BM&V's worldspaces so the player walks between them

Written 2026-11-06 for the owner's question A. Read-only research; nothing built. Owner ruling the same day: the
default is BM&V as it is for the first story slice; this record sets out the choice for later.

## Evidence (measured from the four plugins at `~/.argonia/stack/MO2/mods/bmv/`)

| worldspace | LAND cells | grid x | grid y | NAVM | LOD files (btr/bto/btt) | .lod settings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BlackMarsh (south: Lilmoth, Blackrose) | 1,127 | -35..35 | -39..18 | 3,778 | 1,360 / 54 / 32 | none |
| BlackMarsh2 (interior: Helstrom, Murkmire) | 3,230 | -32..31 | -30..31 | 3,326 | 340 / 0 / 66 | none |
| BlackMarshNorth "Thorn" | 2,733 | -32..31 | -32..32 | 9,685 | 482 / 124 / 85 | none |
| Valenwood | 3,440 | -32..33 | -33..36 | 6,540 | 765 / 253 / 155 | Valenwood.lod |

- **Every world is an island in its own sea.** Every outer edge of all four is flat sea floor at -2,048 units
  (-29 m), the WRLD default land height; the highest edge vertex is -1,032 (-15 m); water is 0 everywhere (no
  per-cell XCLW in any of 11,461 exterior cells). The coasts never meet: there is 15 to 29 m of open sea at each edge.
- **The grids all sit on (0,0) and overlap.** On one grid BlackMarsh2 and BlackMarshNorth share 2,471 cells,
  BlackMarsh2 and Valenwood 2,774. A merge must offset every cell of at least two worlds.
- **No map:** no world-map texture (ICON) and zero MNAM bounds in all four. **No scripts:** no .pex or SCPT; travel
  is purely load doors (ships `01Ship`, `01dock3to4`, the house `01CabinInt2`, book portals; census.md "How each
  worldspace is entered"), so nothing moves the player by coordinates.
- **Navmesh covers 27%** of LAND cells (about 2,820 of 10,530); 23,329 NAVM in all.
- **Missing LOD settings:** only Valenwood ships a `.lod`; BlackMarsh, BlackMarsh2 and BlackMarshNorth have none.
  Whether their distant land draws is unverified (an owner look, below).

**How Bethesda did it (0034):** Skyrim.esm has 37 worldspaces, 34 with a parent; Dawnguard 16 (15 with a parent),
Dragonborn 5. Not one pair is walkable without a load door: cities are child worlds behind gate doors, Blackreach
behind Dwemer doors, Solstheim a separate world reached by ship only. Bethesda never seamlessly joined two worlds,
and Solstheim is the exact case of a separate land over the sea.

## What the old project teaches (git before d269dbf5; decisions 0028, 0031, 0033; rounds 1 to 6)

- Generated terrain, water and LOD passed every off-game check and still looked wrong in game: 20,165 water planes
  hovering or black, 674 cells overflowing VHGT's slope limit, swathes of one texture. Six rounds over eight days
  (340 commits in the world tools alone) ended in "that experiment failed" (0038).
- LOD has no headless mode (xLODGen, TexGen, DynDOLOD are GUI only; the CK's own LOD crashes on large worlds); the
  owner-in-game loop is the only real test, and it is slow.
- Moving cells means rewriting every NVNM and the NAVI index; a NAVM missing from NAVI is silently ignored.

## Options

| | what | work | risk | for quests and factions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** keep as is | ships, the house between worlds, book portals (BM&V's own) | none | none | the allocation stands: the ship line already runs the lore's coast Lilmoth, Soulrest, Tear |
| **A2** walkable border doors | at 2 or 3 plausible places (a cave under the Lilmoth to Helstrom hills, a causeway gatehouse north to Thorn) add a short passage interior with a load door at each end, Skyrim's own pattern | a few days: two interiors re-used from vanilla or BM&V, four doors on existing navmesh; one block | low: doors are a proven kind of change; the walk is a load screen, as every Skyrim gate | adds overland routes (smuggling, a pursuit, a border) without moving any seat |
| **A3** causeway land bridge | sculpt new land across the 15 to 29 m of sea at one edge of each pair, with a door where the worlds meet | weeks: CK sculpting, texturing, navmesh, LOD regenerated by GUI per world | high: the old project's failure mode exactly (new land that looks wrong) | as A2, plus the look of a walked coast |
| **A4** merge into one world | offset every cell of two or three worlds onto one grid, rewrite 10,530 LAND, all refs, 23,329 navmeshes, NAVI, doors, map markers, LOD, then re-sculpt the seas between | months; no tool exists; every BM&V update redone | very high: breaks BM&V wholesale, makes our patch unmaintainable, plus A3's look risk at every join | one seamless province; but the allocation is redone at every seam |

## Recommendation

**A1 now (the owner's ruling), A2 later if a quest wants an overland route.** A2 is Bethesda's answer and a small
proven kind of change; A3 and A4 repeat the old project's failure at greater scale and break BM&V's updates.

One owner look is worth doing at any point: from a high place in Lilmoth and in Helstrom, does the far land draw,
or does it pop in? If it pops, the missing `.lod` files are the cause and are a small fix of their own.
