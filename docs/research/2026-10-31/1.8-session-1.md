  instead of after the cut-over.
- **Duplicate machines from restarted jobs:** killing a job's top pid left its script running, and a rename let a
  second copy start; two g6.2xlarge launched at 13:30. Shipped `job_guard.sh stop <name>` (kills the job's process
  group). The spare became argonia-play-2, so nothing was wasted.
- **The L4 driver fixed by hand on every move:** shipped `gpu-bind.ps1` (a startup task, called by play_move.sh and
  clone.sh).
- **A fresh-from-image disk stalls the first game launch** (EBS lazy loading; the deploy's bridge ping timed out):
  shipped `prewarm.ps1`, run at first boot by play_move.sh and clone.sh.
- **Builders waiting with sleep loops** on another job (watch-new.sh polled every 60 s): use `job_guard.sh run --gpu`,
  which queues on the machine's lock, so a chained job starts the moment the machine is free.
- **The disk:** worktree.sh's tidy ran at the first new lane: 89 GB → 66 GB on the build VM.
