Paris g6 was 9 at 10:00 (lane-g6-move.md, lane-paris.md). Score is a point-in-time proxy for "can I launch now", not for interruption.
C. **Advisor bands are low everywhere; today's behaviour is far worse than they predict.** Bands at the time of fetch (below). A band of 15-20% per month converts to ~0.26% per 10 h running (-ln(1-p)*10/730); <5% to <=0.07%. So the pain is capacity-for-restart plus bursty reclaims, which the monthly average hides. Build VM (m7i-flex, London spot) `~/.argonia/state/boots.log`: 8 "resume" markers 2026-10-29 10:23 .. 10-31 09:15 (some may be manual stops) ~ 1 per 6 h: London spot pool was hot all week.
D. **Capacity Blocks do not exist for this.** AWS docs (ec2-capacity-blocks.html) list only p4d/p4de/p5*/p6*/trn* types, Linux/RHEL/SUSE/Ubuntu Pro platforms only. `aws ec2 describe-capacity-block-offerings --instance-type g6e.xlarge` in eu-west-2/eu-west-3: "not supported for Capacity Blocks". No g-family, no Windows, ruled out.

## 6. Options
- **Fleet.** EC2 Fleet / spot fleet is single-region only; "capacity-optimized(-prioritized)" across several types and AZs needs a launch template, one AMI per region (150 GB copy ~25 min, lane-paris.md; snapshot ~$7/month each, upper bound), and the NVIDIA driver working on every type. Persistent "stop" behaviour restarts only the same type in the same AZ, so a cross-type fallback means a fresh launch from the image with saves restored from the saves.sh backup, not the same disk. Per-new-hardware PnP rebind was needed for L4 (lane-g6-move.md; `pnputil /add-driver C:\Windows\INF\oem81.inf` + reboot) and is UNVERIFIED for the A10G: check the nvgridsw_aws.inf device IDs and the baked image (1.8 Work 4) before relying on g5.
- **1.17 bar** (1.17 file): 60 fps median, 50 acceptable, 95% of frames >= 45, 1% lows 25-30; owner will play at 45-53. g4dn measured at Riverwood: 45 fps typical, 32-35 1% low (shaders on), 53/40 shaders off, i.e. below the bar on the median. g6 numbers are owed (1.17).
- **On-demand fallback for a stretch:** $1.2056 (g6 Paris), $1.251 (g5.xlarge Stockholm), $1.0376 (g6 Stockholm); 10 h/month = ~$10-12.5.

## Method
Advisor JSON parsed with python -I (key spot_advisor[region]["Windows"][type], r -> ranges label, s = savings). SPS: `aws ec2 get-spot-placement-scores --region eu-west-2 --instance-types T --target-capacity 1 [--single-availability-zone] --region-names <5 regions>`. Prices: `describe-spot-price-history --product-descriptions Windows --start-time now-30d` per region; `aws pricing get-products --region us-east-1` with operatingSystem=Windows, licenseModel="No License required", tenancy Shared, preInstalledSw NA, capacitystatus Used. Latency: 15 TCP connects per endpoint; cloudping.co via WebFetch (its page said the direction of the matrix is unstated). Raw: scratchpad sps_*.json, ph_*.json, od.txt, adv/a.json.
