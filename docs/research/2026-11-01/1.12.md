## How each device reaches them

- **Pad** (Wind UI has no pad navigation; ours, padcursor.cpp): Start opens Wind UI's windows; the left stick or
  d-pad moves the game's cursor; A clicks, X right-clicks; the triggers and right stick scroll and zoom; the
  bumpers cycle the panes (stats, inventory, magic, our journal, our map, System last); B closes; Select is wait.
  The layout is laid by `make deploy` on every machine (it had been missing on argonia-play).
- **Keyboard and mouse:** Tab (Skyrim's tween menu is swapped for Wind UI's windows), I, M, J, Esc (the System tab).
- **Touch** (proven through the real stream on the folded V5, `infra/stream-client/e2e/steps/ui-1.12-touch.json`):
  the phone's pad overlay: Start opens the windows, the bumpers cycle to our journal, B closes, Back is wait. The
  HUD's Menu button was removed: a tap on the stream reaches no unfocused web view.

## The owner's two additions

- **FreeClimb.** It was laid and working all along (its 31 clips commit, its hooks install, a chord reaches it);
  the owner could not climb because its default grab is a four-key chord (W+A+D+Space, pad LB+Y). Now: **jump at a
  wall** — W+Space on the keyboard, Y on the pad (FreeClimb's own ini, through the stack). Only surfaces steeper
  than about 45° take a grip (walls, rocks, cliffs; not slopes).
- **Magic Expanded.** Laid from 1.12. Its 600-odd names read for the Nordic review: 12 renamed or reworded at data
  load (Unrelenting Force → Force, Whiteout → Marsh Squall, Arctic Blow → Numbing Blow, …); its 8 poison effects
  move to Destruction (Morrowind's school). Wind UI's own magic window can't show a Mysticism heading (fixed in its DLL), so **our magic pane** stands in for it
  with all six schools; Mysticism lists Mark, Recall, Dispel, Absorb and the rest. **13 Magic Expanded tomes are
  sold by Livia at Claywater Station**, two or three per school, Mark and Recall included (f4ba470).

## Found and fixed on the way (each would have reached the owner)

- `vanilla.txt` "off" switches Wind UI off entirely (it does not switch off Shift); the settings keys are
  `morrowindMap`/`mapPinned`, not the page's row ids. Both misread first, both caught by stills.
- MSVC: `near` is a Windows macro (chart.h), `std::min` in life.cpp.
- The bridge's quit helper called the Journal's quit with no response id (a crash); a close press reached our
  dialogue as it returned from barter; Livia's vendor faction read null on first call (CommonLib caches it late).
- Wind UI drew its windows over the System page and over our map; Skyrim's tween menu opened instead of Wind UI's.
- 567 hidden map markers now exist (one per placed place), so discovery fires and the chart marks a place once found.

## Known limits (for the owner's look)

- Holding Shift while opening a menu still gives Skyrim's (paper) menu: Wind UI has no switch for it.
- Wind UI's taskbar "Journal" still toggles Wind UI's own journal window (ours is J, the pad's cycle).
- Wind UI's stats pane lists Skyrim's One-handed, Two-handed, Archery and Pickpocket at 0 (we pin them); the "Voice"
  slot shows in the magic pane.
- Polish: Wind UI's panes are flat black (fixed in its DLL) beside our textured dark panes; the chart letters the
  seven cities from its art before discovery, and a half-revealed patch shows part of a name. (SkyUI's capitals and
  the System page's leftover Quests hints are fixed: a3cea76, b785dce.)
