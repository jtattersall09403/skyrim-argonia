say "not in the repo" only after reading the index and `world/IMPORTED_FROM.md`. `tools/doc_index.py --write`

Keep the index's lines short (path, then the purpose, ≤ 120 characters); a purpose that the extraction gets wrong is
fixed at the document's first line, not in the index.
