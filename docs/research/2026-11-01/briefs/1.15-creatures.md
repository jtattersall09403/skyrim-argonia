## The task (1.15 §g)
1. **Measure Skyrim.esm first (0034):** how vanilla places wilderness encounters: its spawn markers and levelled
   actor lists (LVLN: which lists, how many entries, level spread and the chance-none), their density per cell by
   terrain type (forest, marsh, tundra, mountain, water), distance from roads, water and settlements, encounter
   zones (ECZN: min and max level, the never-resets flag and how clearable places use it), predators beside dens,
   hostile versus passive animals per cell, day and night differences. Script in `tools/argonia_world/vanilla/`,
   record section in your report, and a line in `skyrim-does-it-this-way.md` pointing at it.
2. **The inventory:** every creature the pinned mods ship (BM&V, King of the Murkmire, HTBM and its cipactli, Mud
   Mother Grove, Depths of Skyrim, Tropical Skyrim's fauna if any; the stack's MO2 mod folders and plugins under
   `~/.argonia/stack/MO2/mods/`, the plugins readable with `tools/argonia_world/terrain/esm.py`): NPC_, RACE,
   their size, aggression, confidence, factions, water and flight flags, the sounds and behaviours they bring; and
   **how each mod places its own creatures in its own worldspace** (REFR positions against its LAND and water:
   in water or on land, slope, distance to trees or bushes, grouped or alone, day or night by package) so our
   placement rules are the modders' own habits, measured. Join every form to a `creature` row (the DB already has
   the design: habitat, hostility, den, territory, diet, day activity, sourcing) or add the row with a why from the
   ecology research; everything from insects and small life (dense, harmless) to the big predators (rare, where
   the lore puts them). Register each form in the sourcing register at first use.
3. **The records, the Skyrim way:** our own levelled-actor lists per region class and danger band with fixed
   levels (never scaled to the player; the band says how tough, and which diseases and poisons a bite or sting
   carries: data rows joining band → creature → effect, realised as the creatures' own spells where the mods have
   them, ours where not); encounter zones per danger band (fixed level, reset rules as data: a clearable place's
   zone resets until cleared, then never: the flags are data, 1.14's runtime applies them on death); spawn-point
   refs placed by the planner on `Sources.load()`'s ground and `water/solve.py`'s cell water: water creatures in
   water cells, marsh, river, coast, upland and mountain by region class, bush or open by the vegetation density,
   a no-spawn radius around every place (by its kind), dens for territorial creatures (`npc_den` sockets or placed
   den markers) with their predator beside them, ambush points by hostility; densities from the vanilla measurement
   scaled by the band. Written by ArgoniaGen into the plugin (LVLN, ECZN, REFR with the zone), checked by
   `creatures check` (the Acceptance list).
4. **The journey:** `tests/journeys/creature-walk.yaml` and its scene (the scene-author skill): three stretches
   (marsh, river, upland) walked at day and at night from fixed starts, reading creatures seen and what attacked
   (state and events, a still per step), and one still per big predator at its den; `--dry` green; the planner
   runs it in the round's batch.
