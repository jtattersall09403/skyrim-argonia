## The task (1.15 §c)
1. **The texture set.** Find what BM&V ships for every vanilla-named landscape texture (its dark pack) and what
   Tropical Skyrim ships: the installed file lists are in `~/.argonia/stack/installed.json` and the mod folders
   under `~/.argonia/stack/MO2/mods/{bmv,tropical-skyrim-sse,king-of-the-murkmire,depths-of-skyrim,htbm-cipactli}`
   (the build VM holds trimmed copies; the full archives are in the S3 mod cache `infra/gpu-box/stack.py` names:
   list an archive's entries without extracting it into the repo). Black Marsh.esm's own LTEX and GRAS records
   are readable here with `tools/argonia_world/terrain/esm.py`. Write the chosen set as `landcover.py`'s table:
   per region class 4–6 LTEX (BM&V first, then the other Black Marsh mods, then Tropical Skyrim, never a vanilla
   Nordic file), each with its grass list and a `why` line citing the dossier or research page. Register each in
   the sourcing register at first use.
2. **The gradient.** `material.py`: per region class a gradient keyed to height above local water
   (`nearest_level`), slope, wetness (a low-pass of the water distance and the climatology's rainfall field) and
   position-seeded noise; blended over 10–20 m (6–11 vertices), never one vertex; riverbed → water's edge → bank
   → land as a gradient everywhere water is; mountainsides in bands and patches by slope, aspect and noise (§8);
   written as layer weights per quadrant (base plus up to 5 by weight, the drop-out at quadrant seams fixed);
   VCLR for shade (slope and aspect) and wet (darker near water). Rows in `material_gradient` (class, stop, LTEX,
   key ranges, why).
3. **The render layer.** `material.py:render_layer(window) -> RGBA` for the ground lane's `terrain/render.py`
   (a false-colour weight map, one colour per LTEX, plus the VCLR); render the four sample windows and send them
   to a `reader` with the checklist (no swathe, no hard edge, riverbed-to-land gradient visible) every iteration.
4. **Unit tests** of the gradient on synthetic cells: layer count, blend width, the seam fix, the water gradient.
5. `build.py` calls `material` where it called `paint`; `paint.py` and the land-cover raster go.

## Report
The texture set per region class with sources; the gradient keys; the layer histogram on the sample; the reader
verdicts; the render time per window; a Starting state hand-off.
