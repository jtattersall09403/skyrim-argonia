## The task (1.15 §b)
`terrain/generate.py` writes the province ground (`out/terrain/ground-f32.npy`, the (4033, 4033) float32 metres
grid of `grid.py`, row 0 north) from:
1. **The prior**: the All-Tamriel heightmap's province window, raw −86..122 m, already on disk as
   `$ARGONIA_DATA/terrain/apron-source-near.npy` rows and columns `[PAD, PAD + 4033)` (see `grid.py`; the old
   sculpt `refined-height-f32.npy` is not the prior). Low-pass it (about 100 m wavelength) and de-terrace it.
2. **Relief per region from the dossiers**, as a `region_relief` table (land_region → target relief: summit
   band, floor band, roughness, erosion strength, cliff share; a `why` column citing the dossier line): the
   Velothi/Valus border ranges north and north-west and the Blackwood uplands west are canon and high; Shadowfen's
   mountain flank and ravines; a low interior; a drowned coast (options §8). Scale the prior's relief to those
   bands per region, blend across region borders, hold every slope to the 82.9° int8 limit by design (no limiter
   moves in the province), add light erosion and noise by region roughness. Cliffs steeper than the limit are
   placed cliff meshes (a later pass): leave a `cliff` mask in `out/terrain/cliff-u8.npy`.
3. **The water constraints**: inside every `cell_water` footprint the bed lies below the level by the body's depth,
   banks above it; at each `water_step` the vanilla join of options §11 (bed through the edge 100–300 units
   under the upper surface, sloping inside the lower cell to below the lower water over 1–5 vertices, banks closed
   above the upper water everywhere but the channel); shores sloped at a designed grade per water type (mangrove
   coast flat, upland tarn steep: the dossiers). Dry gullies (`cell_kind = 'dry'`) carved as a bed with no water.
4. **Road corridors** kept (`tools/argonia_world/routes`, the traced roads' grade rule).
5. **One entrance site per dungeon record** (`place` rows with an interior kind, `place_terrain_requests` and the
   entrance type: options §9 lists the shapes) reserved and shaped: mound, bank, depression, flat landmark, deep
   water; rows in `entrance_site`.
6. **The border apron** as before (`grid.py`, old 0067 §1).
Then `build.py` consumes the generated ground in place of the old sculpt (the regrade and place edits are gone:
re-seating is a later patch), and `check` adds the fit, entrance and overflow checks.

**The L3 renderer** `terrain/render.py`: one image per window (hillshade, water levels and steps, and a layer
registry other lanes fill: `material` from `terrain/material.py:render_layer`, `placements` from the vegetation
lane; import lazily, draw what exists). Commit a working hillshade+water render in your first hour: the
materials lane adds its layer to it. Render the four sample windows (`terrain/world.py` SAMPLE, plus one window
on a river from source to sea and one on a sea-level marsh system: write these as `SAMPLE_1_15`) and send them to
a `reader` with the 1.15 §0 checklist (relief reads as the dossier says; no hard edge; water meets its bank) on
every iteration.
