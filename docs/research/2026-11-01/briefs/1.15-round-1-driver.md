## The round
One machine (the planner's deploy names it in `ARGONIA_PLAY`), one process per batch item unless memory says
otherwise (relaunch when the game passes 6 GB: the bmv-look batches did). Order: world-panel (36 stills), rock-booth
(6), creature-spots (7), then claywater-walk and claywater-look last (crash-prone; expected red until re-seating:
record the gap, do not fix). Run each through `tools/run_batch.py` for the item, or step by step through the bridge
when the runner's timing fails; after every still, confirm it (still_ok; the read-back cell and worldspace; the
load finished: a `cow` holds the main thread, so extend the settle rather than fail), retake with a longer wait or a
turned camera when it fails, and write what you changed in the journey as a timing fix. Three failures on one
step: a harness finding in the report, never a verdict on the world.

## Evidence wanted
- **world-panel:** per vantage, the noon and dawn stills at the play camera, first person, HUD hidden. A reader reads
  each pair with the §0 checklist (relief reads as the dossier says; the look is BM&V's; no texture swathe or hard
  edge; water meets its bank; no hovering, gap or black water; no hollow rock; the weather's authored light and fog;
  god rays and volumetric fog where the weather has them; the feel question) and the vibe board. Where a dawn still
  shows no mist in a region whose regime says mist, note it as a finding, not a failure of the capture.
- **rock-booth:** six stills; the reader checks sunk, no gap, no hollow back, conformed or upright as placed.
- **creature-spots:** at each spot the actors read within 40 m (names and kinds) and one still; the reader checks the
  creature kind fits the habitat (water creature in water, marsh in marsh, upland in upland) and that a den has its
  predator. No fight, no chase.
- **claywater-walk and -look:** the gap between the old pads and the new ground, measured from the state reads and
  one still each; nothing fixed.
- **L5:** one temporal strip only, at one radiation-mist vantage: stills at 06:00, 06:30, 07:00, 07:30, 08:00 side
  by side, to show the mist burning off (the weather lane's regime); the reason is motion in time, which no still
  shows.
- A frame-time read (the bridge's probe) at the densest panel vantage.
