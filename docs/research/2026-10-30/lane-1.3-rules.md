## 2. `topics`

New files: `skse/core/include/argonia/core/topics.h` and `src/topics.cpp`, tested by ctest `core.topics`.

**Store.** `Store::build(topics, lines)` refuses bad ids, links, conditions, actions and line types. `lint()` names each line on an essential topic that reads disposition (old 0145).

**Answering.** First match runs in this order: the speaker's own lines, then lines anyone may speak, and within each, conditioned lines before the plain fallback. The rows have no authored order, so the store sets this one. Bands come from `disposition::band`.

- `offered`, `answer`, `greeting` and `goodbye` work over `TalkFacts`. TalkFacts answers knowsTopic, topicHeardFrom and speakerDispositionAtLeast from the learned set and the speaker, and passes every other fact to the host.
- `keywords(text, known)` matches whole words, case-insensitive, longest first. A possessive still links.
- `deliver` learns the line's links and keywords, recording who told them.

**Co-save.** The learned set is the `TOPC` family, version 1: keyed lists of FNV-1a keys of topics and of the people who told them. It is in families.json and the round trip.

**Fixture.** `fixture_topics.h` holds five Claywater speakers. It is generated by `tools/argonia_world/topic_tables.py` through `core_tables.py`, so the gate's `--check` covers it.

**Tests.**
- A topic learned from A is offered by and answered by B.
- Low, mid and high words change at 29/30 and 69/70.
- An essential topic answers at 0 with its 50 line.
- Conditioned lines come before the plain one.
- Keyword spans are found.
- The TOPC round trip holds.

## 3. Evaluator coverage for LF83

**Conditions.** `test_conditions` gains `lf83_terms`. Each of seasonIs, topicHeardFrom, objectiveComplete, flag, npcAlive, hasItem, goldAtLeast and speakerDispositionAtLeast is tested true, false and unanswered. The new `conditions::from_flat`, which rebuilds a tree from preorder rows, has its own test.

**Actions.** A new `actions` module (`actions.h/.cpp`, `core.actions`). Argument kinds come from a new table, `action_shape_arg`, with rows for all 43 verbs; the table is generated into `tables_actions.h`.

- `run(list, facts, effects)` checks every action first. It also checks the summed takeGold and takeItem against what the player carries. If anything fails, nothing is applied.
- It then applies the list in order and stops at the first refusal.
- changeDisposition is clamped to 0–100 through `disposition`.
- `Effects` has one entry per verb, named as the verb, so it maps 1:1 onto 1.4's natives (TakeGold, GrantTopic, ...). Every other verb goes to `other`. Flags live behind Effects, in 1.4's quest_state.
- Each of the nine required verbs is tested, plus giveGold and the `other` path.

**Stable API for 1.4's quest_runtime.** These are unchanged from 1.1: `conditions::make`, `check`, `evaluate`, `evaluate_valid`, `eval`, `Facts`, `mirrors`. The only addition is `from_flat`.
