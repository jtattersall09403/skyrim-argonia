# 4.4: Assembled interiors

**Goal.** Interiors for the dungeon-kind and building records that no source plugin's interior can serve, built
by the pattern 2.W5 §d proved: room bounds and portals, a lighting template, placed lights, interior navmesh and
load doors. Pulled only when a packet first needs one, never ahead.

**Starting state.** Written by the packet that first needs one. The catalogue counts 327 dungeon-kind interiors;
the plugin-interior manifest (old 0114) covers some, and the rest need assembly or a linked furnished cell.

**Read first.** Old 0114 (inherited-rulings); the 2.W5 Outcome (§d).

## Deliverables
- **The go/no-go measurement:** for each interior record the packets need, can a source plugin's furnished cell
  serve it, linked by a load door? Assemble only when the answer is no.
- **Assembly** by kit pieces that are designed to combine. Placement is decided on geometry (footprints, volumes,
  snap rules), never labels.

## Acceptance
Each assembled interior is navigable (`nav`), lit (no dark interior), within the frame-time bar, and passes a
reader's still panel.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** one per interior: L1 checks, plus an L4 sweep and stills.
- **Joins owned:** door ↔ exterior; navmesh ↔ the exterior.
