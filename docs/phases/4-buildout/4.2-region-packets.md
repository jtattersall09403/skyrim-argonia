# 4.2: Region packets

**Goal.** Build the whole province to Morrowind density, region by region, with the slice's proven pipeline:
- every catalogue place built, or deliberately deferred with the owner;
- every NPC living; every creature placed; every book written;
- each region passing the checks the slice passed.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 4.1 (the packet template, measured costs, O-2 answered),
and rewritten by each packet for the next.

**Read first.** The packet template; imported `docs/design/world/00-core.md`, `20-province-design.md`,
`97-placement-principles.md`, `docs/design/quests/` in full; old 0026–0030, 0041, 0098, 0099, 0102, 0103, 0128,
0132, 0133, 0138–0143, 0153; the catalogue `world/sources/catalogue/places-*.json`.

## The packet (what one region packet contains and the steps it runs)

Each region is split into **packets** of about 20–40 places (a district for cities). One packet is one lane's
brief at a time; on 20x, up to 4 packets run in parallel in **different regions**, sharing the one GPU box
through the `job_guard.sh --gpu` lock (never two builders on one
place or district: the Helstrom lesson, old 0133).

1. **Plan** (L1): from the catalogue, list the packet's places, their classes (settlement, ruin, lair, works,
   lone, transit, sacred, camp, civic, martial), danger bands, cultures, siting, relations, loot tables; the NPCs
   who live there; the quests anchored there; the creatures; the books. Check against density targets.
2. **Kit and asset check** (L1): every mesh each place needs exists in a cleared dependency (sourcing register);
   substitutions recorded; selection order per decision 0006 §8.
3. **Build places** (`world-builder` skill, L1→L4; 4.4 when a building needs an assembled interior): layouts from the placement principles; a place carries its
   own ground; every building enterable via the plugin's interior or an assembled interior; sockets fulfilled
   (old 0104: promises are fulfilled by placed interactables); cities hand-built by district in 3D (old 0133).
4. **People** (L1→L4): NPC records, faces (the G4 method), outfits, schedules on the lamp clock, services, factions.
5. **Dialogue round** (`dialogue-lane` on Sonnet): greetings, topics, per-person opinions over shared facts,
   barks; voice-reader panels; corpus read every third batch (old process).
6. **Quests** (L1→L4): stage graphs for the packet's local quests and any faction/main stages anchored here;
   directions validated against the route graph; journal text; fail-forward for dead NPCs.
7. **Encounters and loot** (L1): creatures and hostiles by band; roaming band table; fixed loot.
8. **Books and signs** (L1): written to the style guide, text-review.
9. **Immersion** (gate): every new element enters the inventory compliant, by the 4.1 method.
10. **Navmesh and LOD:** regenerate navmesh for every cell the packet touched (and its neighbours' edge links)
    in the packet; rebuild LOD once at **region close** on the temporary 64 GiB instance (budget in decision 0008).
11. **Checks:** the packet's journeys on both runners (every quest; a day of its people by time skip); an L4
    sweep entering every building and every socket; frame time per place.
12. **Owner walk** (L6): one per region (not per packet), in the studio from the region's scene: a
    route through the region's new places and its quests; blocker/polish triage; `P2` rows for the region closed
    in its packets.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Per packet:** the packet steps above are its loops.
  - Plan and kit check: L1.
  - Each place: L1, plus an L4 sweep and stills.
  - People: L1, plus an L4 schedule skip.
  - Dialogue: reader panels. Quests: L2 walk, L4 run. Encounters and loot: L1, L2. Books: L1.
  - Immersion: the gate.
- **Joins owned** by the packet:
  - new places ↔ the route graph and directions (L1 validator);
  - new NPCs ↔ schedules ↔ navmesh (L4);
  - the packet's cells ↔ neighbouring packets' cells (L4 edge stills; the later packet owns the edge);
  - new quests ↔ the main quest and faction lines (L2 walk).
- **Journeys:** the packet's quests, and a day of its people (both runners).

## Risks and fallbacks
- **Volume vs quality**: packets are small and judged at the cheapest level; the owner sees regions, not
  packets; reader panels catch sameness (old 0098: variety is measured per settlement).
- **Asset gaps per region** (e.g. no Kothringi remains asset): redesign around what exists (old rule) before
  any Fal or retexture work.
- **Cost**: 20x usage is measured weekly against the plan's cap; if lanes queue on the GPU box, add a second
  GPU box only after costing it against the budget.

## Deliberately out of scope
New systems (a system gap found here goes back to its Part 2 brief through a decision, or to 4.5); new meshes;
regions outside the province (the apron stays unreachable).
