# 4.5: Systems depth

**Goal.** The depth that Part 2 left thin because the slice did not touch it. Each piece is built on its Part 2
system's final interface and pulled when a packet or line first needs it. The candidates are known:
- more boat classes, as config rows (2.15);
- seasonal foliage and creature specials;
- cooking;
- couriers and reprisals;
- the allegiance reward tracks and the stronghold (old 0028);
- levitation, if 2.9 recorded it as feasible later.

Any other gap found in Part 4 is first checked against its Part 2 brief. A missing rule there is a Part 2 defect,
fixed there.

**Starting state.** Written by the packet or line that pulls the depth.

**Read first.** The Part 2 brief of the system being deepened (its Outcome); the decision that pulls it.

## Rules
- One chunk per system (`4.5-<system>`), with its own brief from `brief_template.py`.
- A depth chunk never changes a Part 2 interface without a decision record.
- It keeps that system's journeys green on both runners.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
As the Part 2 brief of the system it deepens.
