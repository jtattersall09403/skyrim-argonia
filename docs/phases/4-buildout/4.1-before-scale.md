# 4.1: Before scale

**Goal.** Everything every packet will replicate is right before the first packet:
- the before-scale polish queue;
- a world-builder that runs unattended on minor place types;
- the cities and Helstrom, built by hand with the owner;
- an immersion method that scales to tens of thousands of rows;
- a packet template;
- a measured plan.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 3.5.

**Read first.** Decisions 0006, 0013, 0023, 0024, 0025; the backlog's `P1` rows; the 2.17 measurements; the old 16k and 4.1
briefs (imported, for the place-skill exit bar); old 0099, 0133 (inherited-rulings).

## Deliverables

### a. The P1 queue
Every `P1 before-scale` backlog row is fixed in the tool, check or default that would have prevented it, with its
test.

### b. The world-builder proved per type; Helstrom and the cities
- **The exit bar (old 16k):** two unattended passes per place type that pass their gates and the readers, with no
  owner fix.
- **Cities are hand-built by district in 3D (old 0133), with the owner.** That covers Helstrom and the majors:
  Gideon, Stormhold, Thorn, Blackrose, Lilmoth, Soulrest, Archon and Alten Corimont. One builder per district,
  never two on one place (the Helstrom lesson). Their packets schedule them; this chunk proves the district
  recipe on Helstrom, including its quests and lines: the writing skills run on Helstrom's first district with
  the owner reading the records and lines before the build (0024 §10).

### c. Immersion at scale
### c1. Inventory completeness
- Re-run the inventory over the full load order and the whole world plan (all regions, including not-yet-built
  places' planned contents from the catalogue). Reachability now includes everything any 4.2 packet can place.
- Group rows into **work queues** by kind (UI, item meshes/textures, creatures, NPC appearance, flora,
  architecture, sounds, music, text, loading screens, menus, effects).
- **Acceptance:** counts per queue and per verdict published in PROGRESS; zero rows without a queue.

### c2. Verdicts at scale (the agentic coverage method)
- For each queue: a cheap first pass decides `keep` / `restyle` / `replace` / `hide` / `remove` from data
  (names, keywords, source mod, Nordic-term list) and a contact-sheet capture (L4 batch renders of the
  element: icon, mesh in a lit booth, texture swatch) judged by `reader` agents with the Nordic-tells checklist;
  uncertain rows go to a second reader; disagreements and samples (5% of each queue, minimum 10) go to the owner
  as a packet.
- Replacement order: Black Marsh mods → Tropical Skyrim (SSE 78044) → other permitted mods → a retexture or
  recolour of the existing asset (a "minor tweak", allowed) → a Fal-made 2D element (UI, icons, paintings,
  map, decals) → `hide`/`remove`. No new meshes.
- Every replacement is a record override or a file reference in our plugin; no third-party files are copied
  into our archives.
- **Acceptance:** zero reachable rows without a passing check; the owner's sample packets agree with ≥ 95% of
  sampled verdicts (disagreements fixed and the rule that caused them updated).

### d. The packet template and the re-plan
- Write `docs/phases/4-buildout/packet-template.md` from what the slice and wave 2 taught: the 4.2 steps with
  measured costs, including time per quest record, per review round and per person's lines. Its quest and
  dialogue steps name the skills, agents, inputs and acceptance of 4.2 steps 5 and 6.
- **The writing skills at scale:** every `P1` row against the skills (a ban, a voice card, a profile, a brief
  builder gap) fixed in the skill, not in a line; `profiles.yaml` holds a profile for every faction line 4.3
  builds.
- **The detectives' line record** (decision 0025): ranks, cases per rank, the recurring cast and rival, the finale
  and the clues seeded for it, written through `quest-write` (`questlines.md`, `investigation.md`) on 2.4's
  lore entry and reviewed by `quest-reviewer`, so every packet plans the cases it hosts.
- Re-estimate Part 4 from the ledgers.
- Set the packet size and lane count, and confirm O-2 (20x) with the measured numbers.
- Confirm O-18 (the quest target).

- The `P1` queue is empty.
- Two unattended passes per type are green.

- The inventory has queues, and the owner's sample agreement is ≥ 95%.
- The template exists, and PLAN's estimates are updated.
- The detectives' line record is `reviewed` with no open finding.
