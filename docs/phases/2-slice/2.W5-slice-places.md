# 2.W5: The slice places, in two waves

**Goal.** Rebuild, natively in Skyrim, the places the old project built and walked (decision 0026). Each one is
complete and built by a `world-builder` recipe that is proved on wave 1 and then run on wave 2. The list and the
reason for each wave are in the [Part 2 README](README.md).

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 2.W4 (its navmesh route is needed by both waves). Expected:
- Claywater Station is built natively (0.4, Part 1);
- the piece resolver is production quality (0.4 §d);
- terrain, water, regions and roads exist around each place.

**Re-check before starting:** the asset mods each place uses (manifest versions and O-5 replies).

**Read first.** Decisions 0006 §8, 0009, 0022, 0026; imported `docs/design/world/97-placement-principles.md`, and
each place's `world/sources/blueprints/<id>.design.md` (the specification) and `.layout.json` (a guide to pieces
and positions only); old 0114, 0128, 0140 (via [inherited-rulings.md](../../inherited-rulings.md)); the old `place`
skill (read in place) for its type sheets and lessons about what makes a place feel right, not its machinery.

## Deliverables

### a. The `world-builder` skill and agent
- The recipe for building a place natively in Skyrim records, the way a modder builds one in the Creation Kit
  (decision 0026 §2). It covers ground edits, placed references, doors, working objects, scatter, navmesh, the
  look loop and the owner check. One place per agent.
- A place is edited in place by its own layer (old 0128, 0140), never rebuilt from scratch once accepted. It
  carries its own ground (old 0102) as direct landscape edits in its cells, owned by that layer.

### b. Building a place
- **From the design note:** what the place is, its story, its "why here?", its parts and their relations. The old
  layout says which pieces it used and roughly where; the agent follows it where it serves the design and departs
  from it where Skyrim does better.
- **Ground:** landscape edits in the place's cells so buildings sit on the ground and paths read.
- **Pieces:** REFRs of source-plugin base records, through the piece resolver (L1: every piece resolves).
- **Interiors** come through the plugin load-door manifest (old 0114, `exterior-interior-links.json`, 38 kit
  interiors). No closed buildings: a building is fully open, or a shell whose load door links to its interior.
- **Working objects** with owners (XOWN) and locks (XLOC) from the frozen loot and lock records:
  - containers, beds and seats (CONT, FURN);
  - stations, shrines and plants (FURN, ACTI, FLOR);
  - moorings, and idle markers (IDLM).

  Everything the design promises is placed and usable; the people who use it are 2.4's.
- **Light and mist:** placed lights and the interior sun method from 2.W3; mist effects where the design calls for
  them.
- **Navmesh** for the place's cells and interiors through 2.W4's route.
- **The look loop:** vantage stills judged by a reader against the design note, with the Nordic-tells checklist
  and the feel question; fix and re-shoot until they match.

### c. Wave 1, then wave 2
- **Wave 1:** Greenspring, The Broke Column, Riverwalk, Lighter Flotilla and the two routes. The owner checks
  after wave 1. Every lesson goes into the skill before wave 2 starts.
- **Wave 2:** Jungle Root Hollow, Rose-Bone Waystation, Bog-Iron Workings, Upriver Hist Village, Naga Highway Camp
  (north), Pearl Lots, The Wamasu Shrine, Chasepoint, Teeth of Sithis, Voriplasm Vault and the Opening Work Camp.
  Wave 2 runs with the proved recipe. The planner measures build cost per place (agent time, GPU time, rounds) for
  the 4.1 template.

### d. One assembled interior (no source-plugin interior to copy)
- Prove one interior assembled from kit pieces, not copied from a source plugin: room bounds and portals for
  occlusion, a lighting template and placed lights, interior navmesh through 2.W4's converter, and load doors.
  This is the pattern 4.4 scales for dungeon-kind interiors without a plugin interior.

## Acceptance
- Each place passes its L4 sweep: every building entered (teleport to each door, activate), every promised socket
  usable (activate each), interiors lit (no dark interior).
- A reader judges each place's vantage stills against its old design notes, with the Nordic-tells checklist and
  the feel question.
- The assembled interior is navigable (`nav`), within the 0.2 §d frame-time bar, and its lighting is judged against
  two vanilla interiors.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** one loop per place (L1 every op resolves, L4 sweep and stills).
- **Joins owned:** place ground edits ↔ terrain at their edges (L4 stills); road ↔ entrance (L4 stills); navmesh ↔ doors and
  interiors (L4 `nav`); place ↔ water (moored hulls, stilts, L4 stills).
- **Journeys:** none (no system state). Places are played in 2.17.

## Owner check
After wave 1, and again after wave 2 in 2.W6.
- In the studio, **Places → Greenspring**, **The Broke Column**, **Riverwalk**, **Lighter Flotilla**: walk each,
  enter every building, use **Report here** on anything wrong.
- Rule each finding blocker or polish (one quick fix, then the backlog with its tag).

## Risks and fallbacks
- **A key asset mod refused (O-5):** re-kit the affected places from cleared sources; record the substitution.
- **Water places** (flotilla hulls, stilts) misbehave on Skyrim water: hulls stay static REFRs (no buoyancy), with
  the bob as an idle animation if a sourced one exists, else still.

## Deliberately out of scope
Helstrom and the cities (4.1); people and creatures (2.4); new places (4.2).
