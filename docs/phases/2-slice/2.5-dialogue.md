# 2.5: Dialogue: the whole runtime and every slice person's lines

**Goal.** Morrowind's dialogue whole: topics and hyperlinks, the full condition vocabulary, disposition and persuasion,
services from the window, barks, and the text catalogue, with every slice person's lines written afresh through
the writing skills and reachable (soul contract C1, decision 0024).

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 2.4. Expected: the first rung (1.3), with three Claywater people, the view and
five predicates; the writing skills fitted to the import (0.1 §i) and the persona fields on every slice person
(2.4).

**Re-check before starting:** PrismaUI version; Fuz Ro D-oh version.

**Read first.** Decision 0005 (C1); imported `docs/design/quests/85-condition-vocabulary.md`, `docs/design/standards/text/`
(the style guide), the dialogue README and culture sheets; old 0139, 0143, 0145 (inherited-rulings);
universal-modder's Skyrim dialogue field note (decision 0020 §4); decisions 0024 and 0025; the `dialogue-write`
skill and 0.1 §i's engine mapping table.

## Deliverables

### a. Dialogue runtime (C1), whole
- From the 1.3 first rung: intercept vanilla dialogue for every Argonia NPC; the dialogue view with greeting,
  hyperlinked keywords, a growing topic list, services (barter, training, travel, spells, repair, spellmaking,
  enchanting) opened from the window as Morrowind does, persuasion (admire, intimidate, taunt, bribe 10/100/1000)
  with the old no-dice thresholds, Goodbye.
- **Condition evaluator** covering the whole old condition vocabulary (`85-condition-vocabulary.md`: ~50
  predicates, ~40 actions), including the ones Skyrim lacks: knowsTopic, topicHeardFrom, witnessed,
  evidenceState, custody, rumourActive, firstTime, daysSince, owingAtLeast, placeStanceIs, histStepAtLeast,
  speakerCulture, settlementStanding, seenBy, successorActive, localVariantActive, and the investigation
  support 0.1 §i found missing (evidence-gated topics, presenting evidence, accusation, rival factions'
  reactions; decision 0025). Native Skyrim conditions are
  used where they exist (GetStage, GetItemCount, GetFactionRank, GetInCurrentLocation, GetCurrentTime, GetDead,
  GetDetected, GetIsCurrentWeather…).
- **Disposition** 0–100 per NPC (old 0145, 76 ~219): base from race, faction (half of reputation) and Personality
  (+0.5 × (Per − 50)); Speechcraft enters only the persuasion score; three bands (< 30 low, 30–69 neutral, ≥ 70
  high) select response variants; daily drift of 5 toward 50; gifts move 1–20 points, at most 30 per day, through a
  "Give a gift" button; disposition changes the talk but never gates greetings or essential topics; floor 0.
- Barks and idle chatter as subtitles, from the old bark lines; no vanilla Nordic barks (decision 0012).
- **Skyrim dialogue-engine facts** (universal-modder's Skyrim field note) for any line on the vanilla path: DLBR
  flags (blocking 0x2, exclusive 0x4, 0x6 to take over an NPC); a shared INFO needs its own conditions to pass;
  voice paths `<plugin>/<voicetype>/<quest>_<topic>_<formid low 12 bits>_<n>.fuz` with quest + topic EDIDs ≤ 25
  characters; delocalise copied strings; force-greet packages re-evaluate only on `EvaluatePackage()`; player
  prompts ~80 characters max; begin fragments are reliable, **end fragments on Goodbye lines are not**: never put
  quest logic there.
- **Acceptance:** L2 tests per predicate and action; L3 view tests; L4 checks: topic learned from A asked of
  B; disposition variants; persuasion moves disposition; services open; an essential topic at disposition 0.

### b. Dialogue and text content
- Flatten the imported dialogue's facts and structure (317 topics; 551 facts; 96 glossary entries; the
  culture-sheet hierarchy province > region > people > faction > office > settlement > group > household) into
  ArgoniaCore's runtime data, keeping "facts once, opinions per person" (old 0139), "anyone may explain;
  restatement near-verbatim only" (0143) and no two identical sentences. The imported written lines (1,471 place
  lines for ~89 people, 569 pool lines) are reference only, never shipped or used as models (0024 §7).
- **Every slice person's lines, written afresh** (0024 §2): `place_brief.py` builds each person's brief for
  their place topics (greeting, my trade, the place, rumours, danger, work, directions); quest topics come from
  2.12's briefs (`quest-write/brief.py`) when their quest is rebuilt. The `dialogue-writer` agent runs
  `dialogue-write` per brief, with `corpus-find` agents over the private corpus once O-21 is answered; each
  brief's disposition bands and listener variants (player race, faction and rank, crime) are written. A fact
  request goes back to the record and the brief is rebuilt; nothing is fixed in the line by hand. Banned
  constructions the owner finds are added to `bans.yaml`, not patched once.
- Text catalogue: every player-visible string from one keyed catalogue (journal, books, signs, place names,
  item names, spell names, loading facts, hydrology names), checked by the prose linter and the `text-review`
  skill against the style guide; the Morrowind exemplar corpus guides new writing.
- **Acceptance:** every slice person has their whole brief's topics written; `lint.py` clean on every line (or
  each kept hit answered in the line file); every fact a line states cites a fact id in its brief; every line
  reachable in game for its speaker (L1 data check plus 20 sampled L4 talks).

**Tests.** The dialogue tests are built from 0.1's test review
(`docs/research/old-tests/dialogue-quests.md`): every keep test ported, every rewrite row rewritten to assert its
design rule, and a new test for every design rule the review found uncovered. Each new or rewritten test is proved
to fail once on a deliberately broken rule. No snapshot of whole dialogue output stands in for a rule.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** the evaluator per predicate and action (L2); the view (L3, L4); disposition and persuasion (L2, L4);
  barks (L4 events: voice lines and subtitles); content per place (L1: every brief builds, every line lints and
  is reachable; `voice-reader` panels per writing batch).
- **Joins owned:** talk → topic learned → journal topics index (with 2.12's index when it lands; L2); faction
  standing → greeting (L2); disposition → service prices (L2); talk → services open (L4); every dialogue family's
  save round trip (L2, L4).
- **Journey:** across three places: learn a topic in one, ask it in another, persuade, give a gift, open a service,
  save and load (both runners).

## Owner check
**Places → Greenspring** and **Riverwalk**: talk to everyone. Does it read like Morrowind; does each person sound
like someone? Reply with any line that jars; each becomes a `bans.yaml` row or a voice-card change, then a
rewrite of the place.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **Intercepting vanilla dialogue** for every NPC is fragile: keep a vanilla-menu path that still works (Skywind
  style) behind the view, so a broken view never strands a quest.

## Deliberately out of scope
Voice acting (unvoiced, O-11); lines for people outside the slice (Part 4 packets).
