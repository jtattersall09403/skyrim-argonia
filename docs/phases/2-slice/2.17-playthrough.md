# 2.17: Persistence, the immersion gate and the playthrough

**Goal.** Every system saves and loads from every slice state; everything the slice can reach is free of any
Nordic element; and the owner plays the whole slice in the studio. This is Part 2's exit, last, after every
system it saves has landed (each registered its own save family as it landed).

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes the last of 2.1–2.16 and 2.W6.

**Read first.** The Part 2 README; decisions 0006, 0012, 0021, 0022; every Part 2 brief's Outcome.

## Deliverables

### a. Save and load across everything, and the coverage gate
- Save and load across every registered family from every slice state: an L2 round trip of every family's
  generated cases, and an L4 save → quit → load → compare from ten slice states.
- **The coverage gate** (`tooling/slice_coverage.py`): reads `docs/design/slice-systems-checklist.md`, which is
  rewritten here from the old checklist. For every system it requires:
  - its plugin, DLL or view path;
  - a journey on both runners;
  - a save round trip;
  - a slice place where the owner met it.

  It fails on any blank.
- The journey set from every chunk, run together twice in a row on both runners:
  - combat, magic, crafting, stealth;
  - quests, climb and swim, boats;
  - persistence (rest, die, recover vastei, save and load);
  - a travel service.

### b. The immersion gate (decision 0006)
- From here on, the gate fails on any reachable `unreviewed` inventory row. Close every row the slice can reach:
  menus, the HUD, items, NPC looks, sounds, music types, text, loading screens.
- Reader panels on a still sweep of the slice (every place, every screen) with the Nordic-tells checklist.
- **The vanilla quarantine, whole** (decision 0012). Extend 0.5's journey to every vanilla system:
  - dragons, shouts, word walls, Dragonborn wording, Sovngarde;
  - the civil war and the standing stones;
  - couriers (the Dark Brotherhood letter, inheritance), radiant quests, guard chatter;
  - Helgen, the Tamriel worldspace, Skyrim's world map;
  - the constellation perk menu, and the Skyrim races' Nordic text.
- A row whose surface Part 3 owns (the main menu, loading screens, the system menu, credits) may carry the verdict
  `scheduled: 3.1`, which the gate accepts until 3.1 closes and refuses after.
- **Acceptance:** zero reachable unreviewed rows; the readers' "obvious" findings fixed; the 50-day quarantine
  journey passes; a text search of every reachable string finds none of the Nordic term list.

### c. The UI map, once more
- The 2.2 transition loop re-run over every transition now built (boss bars, the wind call, climbing stamina,
  death and wake, every station): L5 clips judged by readers, then a transitions owner packet.

### d. The owner's playthrough
- A packet: play the slice in the studio from **Scenes → 2.17 slice start** (a fresh studio character at
  Claywater Station), as long as it holds you. It carries:
  - a checklist of every system to try, each with the place to try it;
  - the measured stream latency;
  - how to report: **Report here** in the studio, plus a reply in the issue (or a voice note, summarised by the
    planner).
- Findings are triaged by the blocker rule: blockers are fixed before Part 2 closes; polish gets one quick fix,
  then a backlog row with its tag.
- **Acceptance:** the owner calls it the game they want, or lists what must change.

### e. Measure and re-plan
- Measure, from the ledgers:
  - time per place, per quest, per dialogue round, per GPU check;
  - costs per week;
  - the owner's turnaround.
- Re-estimate Parts 3–4. Set 4.2's packet size and lane count, and when to move to the 20x plan (O-2, with the
  measured numbers).
- Write Part 3's Starting state.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Journeys:** the whole set, twice, on both runners; the coverage gate.
- **Subsets:** the immersion sweep (L1 counts, L4 stills by readers); the quarantine (L4, 50 days); transitions (L5,
  reason: motion).
- **Joins owned:** any join between Part 2 chunks that no single chunk owned, found by the journeys. Each is fixed in
  the chunk's code that should have owned it, with a test beside it.

## Deliberately out of scope
The new-game flow (Part 3); anything beyond the slice (Part 4).
