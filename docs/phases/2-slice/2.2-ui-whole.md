# 2.2: The UI whole: the OpenMW study, the UI map and every screen

**Goal.** One holistic UI. The windows are studied against OpenMW and Morrowind, every surface and transition is mapped,
every remaining screen is wired as its system lands, and **in normal play the player never sees a vanilla Skyrim
screen** (soul contract C10, C11; decision 0017).

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes Part 1. Expected: the design system, the HUD and the thread's screens are
wired (1.2); every other old screen is in the harness with mock data.

**Re-check before starting:** PrismaUI version; OpenMW's current `files/data/mygui/` layouts.

**Read first.** Decision 0017 in full; 0014 §7–8; the 1.2 Outcome; the old repo's 2.2 brief (imported).

## Deliverables

### a. Study OpenMW and Morrowind's windows; the UI map (decision 0017 §7–8)
- **OpenMW study** (`research` agent): read OpenMW's MyGUI window layouts (`files/data/mygui/*.layout` in its
  GPL source) and its Lua UI, plus Morrowind's own screens (the owner's copy, through tes3conv facts and in-game
  captures if needed): for each window (stats, inventory/paperdoll, magic, map, journal, dialogue, barter,
  container, spellmaking, enchanting, alchemy, training, travel, rest/wait, level-up, chargen, persuasion, pause
  menu, HUD) record what it contains, its controls, its keyboard/gamepad behaviour and how it hands off to the
  next window. Output: `docs/research/ui/openmw-morrowind-windows.md`.
- **Gap analysis** of the ported old UI against that study and the soul contract: what is missing, confusing or
  un-Morrowind-like ("ours still needs some work", owner); a prioritised improvement list feeding §b.
- **The UI map** (`docs/design/ui-map.md`): every screen, overlay, popup, notification, prompt and **every
  transition** (gameplay ↔ dialogue, ↔ inventory and the other windows, ↔ the sitting after rest, death → wake,
  sleep/wait, loading screens, menu ↔ menu, chargen → first control), with the rule that **in normal play the
  player never sees a vanilla Skyrim screen**: each vanilla surface is listed with how it is replaced, suppressed
  or restyled (message boxes, "you cannot sleep now", quest-updated and location-discovered notifications,
  tutorial popups, activation prompts, the loading menu, the perk constellations, the level-up notice, the
  favourites and tween menus, the console only in dev builds).
- **Acceptance:** the study covers every window listed; the UI map has no surface without an owner (our view,
  suppressed, or restyled) and no transition without a planned check; the owner reviews the map and the gap list
  in a packet before the bulk of §b.

### b. Every screen, wired as its system lands
- Every remaining old screen is wired to ArgoniaCore by the chunk that builds its system, on this chunk's
  components and the gap list's improvements:
  - Magic and Spells (by school, powers, active effects) with 2.9;
  - Services (training, travel, repair, spellmaking, enchanting) with 2.6 and 2.9;
  - Alchemy, Smithing and the other stations with 2.10;
  - Crime (the Owing), Lock and Pickpocket with 2.11;
  - Factions with 2.4;
  - Gift with 2.5;
  - the maps with 2.12;
  - Settings, PauseDeck, Load (rest-only saving rules) and Loading with 3.1.
- This chunk owns the components, the map and the review of each wiring, so screens stay one language.
- **Vanilla menus are hidden or replaced** wherever our view covers their function, through the 1.1 router:
  - inventory, magic, map, journal, stats, the skills and perk constellation;
  - barter, container, training, sleep and wait, lockpicking, crafting;
  - dialogue, the race menu, the tween menu, the HUD and the compass.

  Any vanilla screen that must remain is handled by 0014 §7 and listed in the immersion inventory.
- OpenMW's MyGUI layouts and Morrowind screenshots are the structural reference for what each window contains
  (0017 §6). No OpenMW or Bethesda art.

### c. Eyes on transitions, in focused loops
- One UI lane owns the whole UI (no swarm). It works through the UI map transition by transition. For each one
  it records a short in-game clip (**L5, reason: motion and hand-off cannot be judged from stills**), judged by
  readers against the map's intent: no vanilla flash, no jump in style, no focus lost, no input eaten,
  consistent timing and sound. It fixes, then re-records, until clean.
- Transitions belonging to systems not yet built are run by those systems' lanes on these rules. 2.17 re-runs the
  whole map once.

## Acceptance
- The study covers every window it lists.
- The UI map has no surface without an owner (our view, suppressed or restyled) and no transition without a
  planned check.
- Each wired screen passes its old tests and L3 stills at four viewports, and an L4 open/close.
- Every transition built so far has a passing L5 judgement.
- No vanilla menu that our views replace can be opened.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** each screen (L3, L4); the map (L1: every router entry has a map row).
- **Joins owned:** every UI-map transition (L5, reason above).
- **Journeys:** none; screens are steps in every system's journey.

## Owner check
The UI map and the gap list (a read, before the bulk of §b); later a transitions packet in 2.17.

## Deliberately out of scope
The main menu, loading screens and settings (3.1); character creation screens (3.2).
