# 2.4: People: the slice roster, their lives, factions and standing

**Goal.** Every person the slice places promise exists: a record, a whole body that matches its face, an outfit, a home, a day on the lamp clock, a
faction and standing; and every creature socket has its creature. Wide load: the 33 factions' rules.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 2.3 and the 2.W5 wave 1. Expected: G4's three NPCs and Part 1's Claywater
people exist.

**Re-check before starting:** the whole-body template method proved in G4; idle animation mods in the manifest.

**Read first.** Decisions 0014 §3, 0022, 0026 §6, 0024 (the persona fields the brief builders read), 0025; old 0028, 0054, 0096, 0138 (inherited-rulings); imported `72-navigation-ai.md`; the
imported people and creature records for the slice places.

## Deliverables

### a. The record and the roster
- **The old people carry over** (decision 0026 §6). The old repo placed, authored and scheduled them and gave them
  services. Their records, homes, workplaces, routines, services and factions are imported as data and reviewed
  against the design docs, not rebuilt. Their old lines are not used; 2.5 writes them afresh (0024).
- One NPC record for every actor, compiled through one stat schema (old 0144): residents, occupants and cast of
  every slice place, with race, class, sheet, outfit, home, workplace, faction and services, and the persona
  fields the writing skills read (0.1 §i's mapping table: sex, upbringing, role, what the player can see,
  chattiness, friendliness, formality, backstory, wants, fears, household, faction rank).
- **Whole bodies, never faces alone:** each NPC's appearance comes from one template NPC that supplies race, head
  parts, skin, weight and height together (TPLT with the traits flag), by the G4 method. A unique face is
  generated by the installer (decision 0014 §3) on the same template's body. The owner hit face and body
  mismatches before.
- Creatures for every `fauna` and `encounter` socket, from the imported species list: mod-sourced first, vanilla
  only where reviewed.
- **Acceptance:** L1: every slice socket has its actor; `place_brief.py` builds a brief for every slice person
  with no missing field (2.5 writes from them); a full-body L4 still of each slice person, judged by a reader: no dark face, no neck seam, and no
  skin-tone or body mismatch.

### b. People's lives: schedules and the lamp clock
- Day blocks → AI packages with hour windows (rise 05:30, work 06:30, meal 12–13, home 17:30 when lamps light,
  bed 22:00, per-person jitter, role exceptions, predator hunt blocks); shops lock (day-locked doors by script); NPCs
  walk the path network; socket activities (stand, sit, sleep, lean, work-at, **fish, tend, cook, pole**) map to
  idle markers with existing idle animations (Skyrim's or permitted mods'; missing ones recorded as substitutions);
  "walk when seen, swap when unseen" (old 0138) becomes AI packages plus off-screen package jumps.
- **Acceptance:** a 24-hour time-skip check in Greenspring: each NPC is where its schedule says at 6 sampled times.

### c. Factions and reputation
- 33 factions: the imported 32 and the detectives' faction (decision 0025). Its lore pass comes first: a
  `research` agent reads the lore dossiers, the extrapolation and the Shadowscales and Night-Reed records and
  writes its name, founding, patrons, headquarters and why it is trusted as an extrapolation entry with sources
  and confidence; its record follows from that entry. Its line is 4.1's and 4.3's.
- Every faction has ranks as reputation ceilings (associate 0, member 10, senior 40…), reputation −100 to 100,
  crime against a member lowers standing with all; allegiance rites as permanent packages (old 0028); no
  followers.
- **Acceptance:** a journey joins, rises and is expelled (both runners); the detectives' extrapolation entry cites a
  source for each claim.
