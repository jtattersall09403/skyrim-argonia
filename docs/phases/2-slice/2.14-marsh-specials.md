# 2.14: Marsh specials: hazards, effect arrows, materials, harvests

**Goal.** What the loading facts promise the marsh does (old 0157): hazard volumes, burning peat, shock through water,
grave stakes, every creature harvest, effect arrows and coatings, and arrows that stick, ricochet or break by
surface.

**Starting state.** Written by the agents that close 2.9, 2.10 and 2.13.

**Re-check before starting:** none beyond the stack.

**Read first.** Old 0157 (inherited-rulings); the old 2.9c brief (imported); `world/` hazard, grave-stake, harvest and
physical-material registries.

## Deliverables

### a. The world registries this chunk owns
- `hazard-volumes.json` (burning peat and the rest, a 1 s tick) → hazard trigger activators with damage spells;
  shock through water from the 2.9 effects.
- `grave-stakes.json` (xul-vaat, old 0157 item 10) → placed activators with their lore effect.
- `creature-harvest.json` (drops per species) → death items and harvest activators.
- `physical-materials.json` (arrow stick, ricochet, break) → Skyrim impact data where possible.
- **Acceptance:** each registry has a consumer, and an L1 check that every row is used.

### b. Effect arrows and coatings
- Arrows and bolts carrying effects from the 2.9 catalogue; poisons and oils from 2.10 coating the next hit.
- **Acceptance:** L4 checks per effect family; recoverable arrows by surface.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** each hazard kind (L4 events plus a still); each harvest (L1, L4); grave stakes (L4); arrow surface
  response (L4 stills per material).
- **Joins owned:** hazard → effect stack (L2); harvest → crafting ingredient (L2); effect arrow → combat hit (L4);
  shock → water body (L4).
- **Journey:** cross burning peat, fight in water with shock, harvest the kill, brew from it, shoot a coated arrow
  (both runners).
