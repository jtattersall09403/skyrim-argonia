# 2.W6: Performance and the world walk

**Goal.** The composed world holds its frame-time bar where players will be, and the owner walks the province
and the slice places in the studio and can say "the world will get built; it is leg-work now".

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 2.W5 wave 2.

**Re-check before starting:** GPU instance prices and the instance chosen in G2.

**Read first.** Decisions 0007, 0008, 0022; the 2.W4 and 2.W5 Outcomes.

## Deliverables

### a. Performance pass
- Measure frame time across a grid of 100 sampled cells plus every slice place on the chosen instance. Fix the
  worst 10: vegetation density, light counts, LOD settings. Quality defaults are never lowered to win frames
  without an owner ruling.
- **Acceptance:** 95% of sampled cells and every slice place hold ≥ 60 fps median at 1080p on the chosen instance.

### b. The world walk
- An owner packet: a route in the studio across the province by teleport hops and short walks:
  - the coast and its apron;
  - a river reach, a waterfall and a lake;
  - one road leg on foot;
  - each region's light at day and night, in rain;
  - every wave-2 place.
- Each stop is named as a studio place or scene. Findings come by **Report here**.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** frame time per sampled cell (L4).
- **Joins owned:** any cross-chunk world join the walk finds, returned to the chunk that owns it.

## Owner check
The world walk above. Reply in the check-in issue: "the world is leg-work" or the blockers.

## Deliberately out of scope
The final look (4.6); performance of systems not yet built (each system's chunk keeps its own frame-time row).
