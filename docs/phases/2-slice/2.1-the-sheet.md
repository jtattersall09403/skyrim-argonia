## Deliverables

### a. Races, birthsigns, classes (C12)
- Races: Skyrim's ten with Morrowind-style packages (old 0096: one race record keyed by the roster with
  Morrowind's packages); Argonians breathe water; sex is an axis (old 0054).
- Birthsigns: the 13 replace the standing stones (stones removed from the world).
- Class start bonuses (76 ~398–413): +25 major, +10 minor, +5 specialisation skills, +10 favoured attributes,
  ×0.8 skill XP cost in the class's skills; the 18 presets.
- Classes: the old presets plus custom and the question-generated class (Morrowind's questions rewritten for
  Black Marsh); 5 major, 5 minor, specialisation, 2 favoured attributes.
- The records and rules only; the character-creation flow that picks them is 3.2. The studio character (0.4) is
  built from these records, and scenes may name any race, class and sign.
- **Acceptance:** L2 tests: each class's start bonuses and each sign's effects on the derived values; an L4 check
  reads the sheet of one character per race and sign (scenes).

### b. The StatEffect stack, whole
- One stack (field, op, magnitude, source) for races, signs, disease, potions, spells and enchants. Each source is
  mapped onto a Skyrim magic effect where one exists, and an ArgoniaCore modifier where not. Sources arrive with
  their systems (2.9 magic, 2.10 crafting); the stack and its tests are complete here.
- **Acceptance:** L2 tests per source kind; stacking and expiry order match 76.

### c. The perk trees (C4; owner, 2026-10-31)
Perks are visible and taken in Wind UI's perk list, one point per level taken at the sitting; 1.12 reworded Skyrim's
perk text for the Nordic review and gave the skills without a tree a placeholder.
- Enumerate every PERK and skill tree in the load order and classify each: (a) a perk that fits one of our 27 skills
  as it is; (b) one to move, retune or rewrite for a Morrowind skill; (c) a mechanic we need hidden (sneak
  multipliers by weapon band, armour bonuses: our rules through perk entry points where that is the cheapest
  hook); (d) one to remove.
- Give each of the 27 skills a tree (Spear, Athletics and the other skills without one get theirs), replacing
  1.12's placeholders; the constellation menu never shows (Wind UI's list is the perk screen).
- **Acceptance:** every skill has a tree in Wind UI's list; every vanilla perk has a verdict in the immersion
  inventory.
