# The walk of the new world's creatures, people and plants

This is a list of things for you to look at in the game. Each bullet is one check. You do not need to have read anything else.

## What this walk covers

The new world is our copy of Black Marsh and Valenwood. In this round we filled it with living things: animals, insects, swimming creatures, roaming bands of hostile people, and plants that you can pick. We also fixed two faults that you reported earlier: grey grass, and plants and logs that floated above the ground.

## How to get in

1. Start the "Argonia: New World" card.
2. Choose New Game. Go through character creation. You land at the start spot of the new world.
3. To jump to a check spot, use the lesser power "Argonia Travel", which is on the shout button. Press it to open its menus, open the page "Walk-check spots", and pick the spot named in the bullet.

No shader compile (the long "compiling shaders" wait) is expected. If one starts, tell us.

## Before the spots: the two fixes

- Grey grass: three grasses that looked violet or grey have been replaced with grasses that include their flowers. The change covers four regions: the coast, Valley of Storms, Okuwun Rahei and Hatandros Bight. It includes the start cell. At the start spot, look at the ground. Reply ok if the grass looks green and normal, fault if you see grey or violet patches.
- Floating things: 85 kinds of plant and log have been moved down onto the ground. That is 59,547 things in all. Hanging moss with nothing to hang from has also been removed. That is 6,003 things. Look around at any spot for plants, logs or moss hanging in the air. Reply ok if none, fault with the spot if you see one.
- Some far-off flat tree pictures (billboards) show trees that were moved or removed. These pictures stay in their old place until the next far-view (LOD) build. This is a known limit and not a fault. Do not report a distant tree that looks out of place.

## The spots

Each spot is on the "Walk-check spots" page.

- Benign against lethal: go to "D1 Cape Percar", find an animal and let it hit you. An animal here is meant to hit for a few points. Then go to "D5 Murkwood", find an animal and let it hit you. Here an animal is meant to be able to kill an early character in a few blows. The numbers D1 and D5 are the danger bands: D1 is the gentlest, D5 is the deadliest. Reply ok if the difference is clear, fault if the two feel alike. Also say whether each spot has a fitting mix of creatures.
- Fresh water: go to "Banujha Basin" and look in the water. Reply ok if freshwater creatures swim there and none are on land, fault if the water is empty or a creature is in the wrong place.
- Salt water: go to "Aphon Isle" and look in the sea. Reply ok if sea creatures swim there, fault if the water is empty or a creature is in the wrong place.
- Canopy: go to "Arnesian Jungle (canopy)", under the thick tree cover. Then go to "Arnesian Jungle (open)", in the clearing nearby. Reply ok if the two feel different in what lives there, fault if they feel the same.
- Insects at dusk: go to "Nushara Bayou" and wait until dusk (use the wait menu). Torchbugs (glowing insects) are meant to appear at night. Flies are meant to appear by day. Reply ok if you see the right ones for the time, fault if not.
- Hostile people: go to "Chixathe Mesa". A band of hostile people roams there. Reply ok if they differ in race, looks and gear, and none wears Nord armor, fault otherwise.
- Harvestables in a swamp: go to "Ultherus Swamp". Look for plants you can pick. Reply ok if there are plenty and they look at home in a swamp, fault otherwise.
- Harvestables on dry ground: go to "Aunike Ridge" and do the same.
- Harvestables on a sea shore: go to "Hatandros Bight" and do the same.

## Creatures from added mods

More creature mods may be added later, so these bullets name the kinds of creature and where they live. They give no exact numbers.

- Dreugh (crab-like swamp and sea people): on a sea shore or in shallow sea at "Aphon Isle", or the nearest shore spot. Reply ok if you see one.
- River otters: they live by Lake Ulaish. That lake is not on the Walk-check spots page, so go to the nearest river or lake spot and look at the water edge.
- Kagouti (large horned beasts of open ground): on open ground at "Hatandros Bight", "Aunike Ridge" or "Chixathe Mesa".
- Lamia (snake-bodied hostile creatures): at "Aunike Ridge" or "Chixathe Mesa".
- Giant leeches: in swamp water at "Banujha Basin" or "Ultherus Swamp".
- Cave bats, in swarms: at ruins, caves and under dense canopy at dusk. Try "Aunike Ridge" or "Cape Percar" at dusk. If no bat creature has been added to this build yet, none will appear. Say so in your reply.

For each of these, reply ok if you meet the kind where it should be and it looks and moves right, fault if it is missing, floats, sinks or looks wrong.

## How to reply

Comment on the GitHub issue we post for this walk. One line per bullet, with the spot name, then ok or fault, and a few words for a fault, for example: "Banujha Basin: fault, a land animal is swimming". You do not need to cover every bullet in one go.
