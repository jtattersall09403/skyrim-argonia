# The walk of the new world: creatures, plants, regions and music

This is a list of things for you to look at in the game. Each bullet is one check. You do not need to have read
anything else.

## What this walk covers

The new world is our own copy of Black Marsh, built on our own ground. In this round we:

- filled it with living things: animals, insects, swimming creatures, roaming bands of hostile people, and plants you
  can pick;
- made each of its 24 regions feel like its own place: its own light, how far you can see, weather, ambient sound,
  colour of the water and of the flowers in the grass, and a few small things found only there;
- gave it its own music;
- fixed the faults you reported: grey grass, logs and plants floating above the ground, moss hanging from nothing, and
  plants with missing textures.

## How to get in (game pad)

1. Start the "Argonia: New World" card.
2. Choose New Game and go through character creation. You land at the start spot of the new world.
3. To jump anywhere, use the lesser power "Argonia Travel". It is on the shout button. It opens a menu: "Walk-check
   spots" holds the spots named below; "Places by region" lists every place of every region.

No shader compile (the long "compiling shaders" wait before the menu) is expected. If one starts, tell us.

## The fixes

- Grey grass: stand at the start spot and look at the ground. Reply ok if the grass looks green and normal, fault if you
  see grey or violet patches.
- Floating things: 59,547 logs and plants of 85 kinds were lowered onto the ground, and 6,003 strands of hanging moss
  with nothing to hang from were removed. At any spot, look for anything hanging in the air. Reply ok if none, fault
  with the spot's name if you see one.
- Missing textures: plants that showed as bright purple or grey now use their full textures. Reply fault with the spot
  if you see any bright purple or chequered thing.

## Creatures: danger, water and cover

The world has 5,979 creatures and people placed by danger band (D0 is safe, D5 the deadliest): D1 353, D2 1,745,
D3 2,108, D4 1,189, D5 558. Nothing is levelled to you: a creature's strength is set by where it lives.

- Safe against lethal: go to "D1, the lowest danger: Cape Percar open coast", find an animal and let it hit you. Then
  go to "D5, the highest danger: Murkwood cypress swamp" and do the same. In D1 a hit should cost a few points; in D5 an
  early character should die in a few blows. How hard a creature hits is set by an ability we add per band, which has
  not been seen in game yet: if a D5 creature hits as softly as a D1 one, that ability is not working. Reply ok if the
  difference is clear, fault if not.
- Fresh water: go to "Fresh black water creatures: Banujha Basin" and look in the water. Reply ok if creatures swim
  there and none stand in the water that should not, fault otherwise.
- Salt water: go to "Salt water creatures: Aphon Isle coast" and look in the sea. Reply ok if sea creatures swim there.
- Canopy against open: go to "Under the canopy: Arnesian Jungle", then "In the open: Arnesian Jungle grassland". Reply
  ok if what lives in each feels different.
- Insects and dusk: go to "Night insects at dusk: Nushara Bayou reeds" and wait until dusk (wait menu). Glowing
  torchbugs should come out at night, flies by day. Reply ok if you see the right ones for the hour.

## The new creatures

Each was added from a creature mod. Reply ok if you meet it where named and it looks and moves right, fault if it is
missing, floats, sinks or looks wrong.

- Dreugh (crab-like sea people): on the shore at "Salt water creatures: Aphon Isle coast".
- River otters: at "A lake shore: Lake Ulaish", along the water's edge.
- Lamia (snake-bodied hunters): at "A cool fen: Barsaebica", or "A hostile people band: Chixathe Mesa".
- Kagouti (big tusked beasts of open ground): at "A hostile people band: Chixathe Mesa" or "A sea shore: Hatandros
  Bight beach".
- Giant leeches: in the water at "Fresh black water creatures: Banujha Basin" or "Harvestable plants: Ultherus Swamp".
- Megalania (giant lizards): at "Red mud mangroves: Kotherra Mangroves" or "A lake shore: Lake Ulaish".
- Medusa jellies: in the water at "A lake shore: Lake Ulaish" or "A cool fen: Barsaebica".
- Hummingbirds: at "Under the canopy: Arnesian Jungle".
- Sea fish (groupers, tuna, anglerfish, a whale): in the open sea, best seen from "Places by region", then Padomaic Ocean.

## Hostile people

- Go to "A hostile people band: Chixathe Mesa". Reply ok if the band's members differ in race, face and gear, none
  wears Nord armour, and their gear looks fitting for a dangerous place; fault otherwise. There are 113 such bands over
  the world.

## Plants you can pick

7,212 picking plants were added, so each region has as many as a like place in Skyrim, among them four Black Marsh
plants new to the game.

- Swamp: "Harvestable plants: Ultherus Swamp".
- Dry ground: "A dry region: Aunike Ridge scrub".
- Sea shore: "A sea shore: Hatandros Bight beach".

For each, reply ok if there are plenty and they look at home there, fault otherwise.

## How the regions differ

Each pair below are neighbours. Go to the first, then the second, and reply ok if the two feel clearly different in
light, how far you can see, weather, sound and the look of the water and ground; say which pair if not.

- "A dry region: Aunike Ridge scrub" (clear, cool, wide views, upland wind) against "D5, the highest danger: Murkwood
  cypress swamp" (black-green gloom, about a third of Skyrim's view).
- "A hostile people band: Chixathe Mesa" (bright, the clearest air in the world, chalk-pale flowers) against "Under the
  canopy: Arnesian Jungle" (humid green, rain, jungle birds).
- "Storm light: Valley of Storms" (violet storm light, rain dripping, owls at night, will-o'-the-wisps on the flooded
  causeways at night) against "In the open: Arnesian Jungle grassland".
- "Fresh black water creatures: Banujha Basin" (tea-amber steam, tea-brown water, buzzing flies) against "A dry region:
  Aunike Ridge scrub".
- "A lake shore: Lake Ulaish" (silver-blue lake light, storms, blue flowers) against "Harvestable plants: Ultherus
  Swamp" (warm grey-green fog, black water, flesh-fly swarms, near silence at night).
- "A sea shore: Hatandros Bight beach" (pearly sea fog, grey mud flats, surf and gulls, crab shells) against "A hostile
  people band: Chixathe Mesa".
- Small things found in one or two regions only: look for egg clutches in the reeds at Banujha Basin, bone piles by
  the old stones at Barsaebica, glowing fungi on fallen wood in the Arnesian Jungle, crab burrow mounds in the red mud
  at Kotherra. Reply ok if you find them and they sit on the ground.
- Hist lanterns: lanterns hang in the limbs of Hist trees near places in Murkwood only, since people hung them there.
  Go to Mud-Croak Camp, Tzel-Haar and Icalca, and Hiss-Bank Camp (all under "Places by region", Murkwood) and look up
  into the Hist trees' limbs. A lantern may hang through low leaves. Reply ok if you see them hanging from the limbs.

## Music

The new world has its own soundtrack. Your Black Marsh Overture is the main theme; every other piece shares its
instruments. You can hear the whole soundtrack outside the game at https://claude.ai/artifact/TSNTV8nfnLwojg5Xv2g1va
(a private page).

- Exploring: each region plays the pieces of its kind of land: swamps (Banujha Basin, Ultherus Swamp) the mist, Hist
  and rain pieces; jungles (Murkwood, Arnesian Jungle) the canopy pieces; the uplands (Chixathe Mesa, Aunike Ridge) the
  highland piece; the coasts the coast piece; at night the night pieces. Reply ok if the music fits where you are.
- Combat: fight anything; our battle pieces play, and Skyrim's do not. Reply ok if it fits.
- Death: our death sting plays when you die (you approved it).
- Level up: when you level up you hear an 8 second cut of your Overture. If you would rather keep Skyrim's sting, or
  have music follow it, say so.
- Discovering a place plays Skyrim's own discovery music, as you chose.
- To put in a file of your own for any piece: upload it to the bucket folder nw/music/owner/ named after the piece's
  slot (for example theme.wav); we rebuild the pack from it.

## Known limits (not faults)

- Far-off flat tree pictures (billboards) of trees that were lowered or removed stay where the trees were until the
  next far-view (LOD) build. Nothing else in the far view changed.
- Small critters (insects, fish, birds) may feel heavy in places; tell us where.
- The flowers in the grass may feel too dense or too sparse; tell us where.
- The water's colour per region is a deliberately small shift; it is easy to miss.
- Velothi undead have no creature yet and are not in.
- Helstrom's place is not in this deploy: it waits until you have finished editing its draft in the studio.
- Creatures we had no model for were changed to the nearest kind that has one. Overrule any you dislike: the lore's
  bog dog is the nix-hound; regions' "sea skater" and "flying fish" have no creature and are left out; reed-borers and
  flesh-flies have no creature and are left out (Nha-Enasa shows its restless dead instead).

## How to reply

Comment on the GitHub issue we post for this walk: one line per bullet, the spot's name, then ok or fault and a few
words for a fault, for example "Banujha Basin: fault, a land animal is swimming". You do not need to cover every
bullet in one go.
