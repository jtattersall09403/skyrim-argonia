## Walk checks

- **New game on the New World card.** Open the "Argonia: New World" card and start a New Game. Make your character. You should be standing at the start of the new world (the same place the travel power's "The start" button takes you), not in the barge's hold by Lilmoth. Reply "L3 ok" or "L3 fault: where you woke up".
- **The main card's start is unchanged.** Open the "Argonia" card and start a New Game. You should wake in the barge's hold by Lilmoth, as before. Reply "L3 main ok" or "L3 main fault".
- **Travel menu fits the screen.** On the New World card, use the "Argonia Travel" power (the shout button). Every page should fit on the screen, with every button visible. The root page shows The start, Places, Waters and Walk spots, and the text above the buttons explains each one. Reply "L4 ok" or "L4 fault: the page's title and what was cut off".
- **Walk spots, every page.** In the power, choose "Walk spots". On each page, press right on the pad until "More..." is selected and press A. You should reach page "4 of 4", and "Back" should take you back one page each time. The page text names each short label's full spot, for example "D1 Cape Percar: D1, the lowest danger: Cape Percar open coast". Reply "L27 ok" or "L27 fault: which page".
- **Camera looking up.** In third person on open ground anywhere in the new world, zoom the camera out a little and push the stick fully up to look at the sky. The camera should come in towards your character's shoulders, as in plain Skyrim, and never go under the ground. Reply "L19 ok" or "L19 fault".
- **Camera in plants.** In the same way, stand in ferns or reeds (the walk spot "Harvest plants") and look up. Plants near the lens may still show for a moment, as in plain Skyrim, but the camera should not stay stuck behind them. Reply "L19 plants ok" or "L19 plants fault".
- **FreeClimb, two places.** On the "Argonia" card (the main card), walk up to a tall steep rock or a building wall and press Y (jump) facing it. Then do the same on the New World card at a steep rock (try the walk spot "Dry scrub"). Tell me whether it climbs on each card: "L18 main: climbs / does not", "L18 new world: climbs / does not". If it does not climb on either card, also open the SKSE menu (FreeClimb's page) and read out the line that says Runtime: Ready or Unavailable.
- **Atlas relief.** Open the Atlas and the "home" panel. The terrain line should name the relief's terrain build and its date. Move the pointer over the land: the bar at the foot gives the ground's height in metres above or below the water. Reply "L2 ok" or "L2 fault".
