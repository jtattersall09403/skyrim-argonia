# The rulers: rename map

For writers of the plot pages, the cast roster and the place records. The owner ruled on 2026-11-17 that the
An-Xileel fell after Umbriel and that Argonia in 4E 201 is ruled by the **Xeech-Leel**, whose elite intelligence and
security organisation is the **Toh-Reel-Ka**. The truth is in `story/lore/extrapolation/argonia-4e201-state.md`
§1 (the Xeech-Leel), §2.5 (the Toh-Reel-Ka), §10.1 (the dated rows) and §11.2 (the dreamers). Every Jel word below
is on UESP's page Lore:Jel with the meaning given; a name put together from attested words says so.

## The state

| Old term | New term or rule |
|---|---|
