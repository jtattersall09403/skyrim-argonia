# nw-02 flora rules: measured catalogue and placement rules

Researched 2026-11-08, read-only. Machine-readable catalogue: `out/newworld/research/flora_catalogue.json` (untracked, 1.9 MB; 967 catalogue rows carry per-world `seating`). Scripts that produced every number: `out/newworld/research/flora_scripts/` (esmlib.py reads plugins; ana.py/prep.py build per-ref rows; partA.py seating; partC.py density/water/co-occurrence; hollow*.py/orient*.py meshes; depths.py; regn.py; gras.py; catalogue.py). Units: game units / 70 = metres. "Ground" = bilinear VHGT of the winning LAND at the ref's XY. Sink = ground Z minus ref Z, divided by ref scale (positive = pivot below ground).

## 2. Seating (all numbers = per-base medians of measured refs; script partA.py)

### 2.1 Pivot rule: trees and plants sit at the ground; do not use the lowest vertex
- Vanilla Tamriel trees: base-level median sink 0.08 m (IQR 0.00 to 0.14 m, 91 bases n>=40, 99,748 refs); 90 % of bases have |sink| < 0.5 m. The Spearman correlation of sink with mesh root depth (-OBND zmin) is -0.11, i.e. authors did NOT sink by root depth. Example: TreePineForest05 zmin -88 u (1.26 m of roots below the pivot), sink median 3 u (0.04 m); TreeAspen05 zmin -15 u, sink 1 u.
- Verification that OBND zmin = lowest vertex: parsed rockcliff03.nif: vertex min z -107, OBND -106; RockL01 -60 vs -59 (nif parser `flora_scripts/nif.py`).
- BM&V: trees 0.00 m (BM1, IQR -0.06..0.00), 0.32 m (BM2, IQR 0.18..0.76), 0.36 m (Valenwood), 0.53 m (North, root depth 0.94 m); ferns/shrubs 0.0 to 0.3 m. KoTM trees -0.13 m (IQR -0.46..0.00, i.e. very slightly proud), ferns/shrubs -0.54 m (proud; many stand in shallow water). Rule: **pivot Z = ground Z minus per-base median** (catalogue `seating[world].sink_m_p25_med_p75`), jitter inside its IQR; fallback class value 0.1 m. Trees whose OBND zmin is a tail (e.g. TreePineForestSnow04 zmin -1187 u) still seat the pivot, not the tail.
- Scale practice (p5/median/p95): vanilla trees 0.71/1.0/1.2; KoTM trees 1.0/1.0/1.84, cliffs up to 2.41; BM&V BM2 trees 1.0/1.8/5.0, ferns 2.1/5.0/9.0, mangroves 3/8/10 (the mangrove and fern meshes are authored small and scaled up); Valenwood ferns 1/3/3, palms 2/2/4.

### 2.2 Rocks and cliffs sink by size
- Boulders: vanilla base-level sink median 0.38 m (IQR 0.21..1.09), tracks height (rho 0.71): sink/height ref-weighted median 0.26 (KoTM 0.17). Examples (sink p25/med/p75 in u): RockL01 69/113/158, RockL02 27/63/97, RockL03 38/78/112, RockM01 24/45/66, RockM02 24/49/72, RockS02 11/23/32, RockL04 48/97/159, RockPileL01 105/139/167, MountainTrimSlab 180/381/553.
- Cliff pieces: vanilla median 8.3 m (IQR 3.2..10.9), sink/height 0.32 (per-base IQR 0.20..0.72). RockCliff03 537/614/688 u (8.8 m of a 11.9 m piece), RockCliff04 685/807/916, RockCliff06 318/410/502, MountainCliff02 642/890/1115, MountainCliffSm01 1053/1171/1303. KoTM cliffs 1.07 m median (sink/height 0.18); Valenwood cliffs 2.4 m (0.58 for the sunk ones).
- Seabed rocks in Depths: TBPRockL01Moss 0.98 m, M-size ~0.0 to 0.5 m, TBPMountainTrimSlabMoss 3.8 m, TBPRockCliff06Moss 9.5 m (same rule as land).

### 2.3 Tilt and slope
- Vanilla (Tamriel): tilt median / p95 and tilt-to-slope on slopes >8 deg: boulders 15.6/61 deg, 0.85; cliffs 6.8/45, 0.21; trees 2.1/27, 0.10 (pines 1.4 to 1.8 deg, p95 3 to 5; aspens 0 to 1); ferns 8.0/33, 0.58; shrubs 12.8/62, 0.96; reeds 4.9/36, 0.55; kelp/water plants 10.2/39, 0.97; logs 7.1/97, 0.76. Median ground slope under refs: trees 15.6 deg (p95 43.6), boulders 20.9 (57), cliffs 30.4 (64), ferns 15.1 (46.5), reeds 15.6 (39.8).
- **BM&V does not tilt**: BM2 tilt p95 = 0.0 for every class; BM1 trees 5 % > 2 deg (p95 11.5, thickets/pine shrubs); Valenwood trees 9 % > 2 deg the tail is outliers (TreeAspen04 scale 5 sunk 40 m, tilt 58 deg: not ground flora); North 0 %. Their terrain is flat (median slope 0 deg; p95 slope: BM2 trees 33.8, Valenwood cliffs 64). KoTM tilts a minority: boulders 32 % > 2 deg (p95 11.5), cliffs 32 % (p95 22.9), logs 40 %, trees 6 % (p95 4.0), ferns 9 %.
- Rule: yaw uniform; boulders/rocks tilt to 0.85 x ground slope (cap by the class p95 above); shrubs/plants/water plants follow slope fully (0.6 to 1.0); trees stay upright (0.1 x slope, max 5 deg for pines/aspens, 30 deg for thicket/shrub meshes); cliff shells 0.2 x slope, sunk deep instead. Place trees up to ~35 deg ground slope (KoTM p95 20 deg, vanilla p95 44), cliffs only >= 28 deg (vanilla cliff slope p25..p75 = 16 to 47 deg, median 30).

### 2.4 Hollow backs: measured, and the rotation that hides them
Method (`hollow.py`): triangles from the nif (vanilla BSA + BM&V loose), for 8 horizontal directions plus up a 16x16 ray grid is cast through the mesh; a ray whose first hit is a back face means you see into a hollow. 62 vanilla rock/mountain/cliff meshes done (`hollow_vanilla.json`).
- **Free-standing boulders are solid outward**: RockL01-05, RockM01-04, RockS01-03, RockPile S/M, wet variants: back-face fraction 0.00 to 0.06 in all 8 directions. They are open underneath (bottom_open 0.3 to 1.0; RockL04/RockL05/RockM03/RockS01-03 0.31 to 0.49, RockM01/M04/PileM 0.98 to 1.0), so the burial in 2.2 is what hides the underside; sink at least the p50 sink of their class.
- **Cliff shells have hollow backs**: RockCliff01/02/03/04/05/06/07, RockCaveEntrance01/02, RockAnimalDen01, RockPileL01/02/03 (+Wet/Moss/Tundra copies), MountainCliff01-04, MountainCliffSm01, MountainCliffSlope, MountainTrimSlab, DirtCliffs01-03, DirtCliffsCornerIn/Out, MountainPeak01: 0.4 to 1.0 of rays see back faces over a 3-direction arc (e.g. RockCliff03: 0.83/0.57/0.51 at ray directions 0/45/315 deg, i.e. open towards mesh -X). RockCliff08, RockShelf01, MountainPeak02 and MountainRidge02/03 show no open back. All cliff pieces are open underneath (bottom_open 0.71 to 1.0).
- **Rotation rule (VERIFIED on 8,762 vanilla refs on slopes >= 20 deg, 23 open-back meshes)**: with open-side azimuth `a` of the mesh (table in `orient_vanilla.json`) and ref yaw `rz`, Skyrim yaw is **clockwise**: world open side = a - rz. Bethesda sets it within 45 deg of **uphill** for 54 to 97 % of refs per mesh on 21 of 23 meshes (R 0.56 to 0.95; the exceptions are MountainTrim03 R 0.13 and one at 25 %; the counter-clockwise convention gives R 0.04 to 0.19) - the open back goes into the hill. Same practice in Valenwood (558 refs, R 0.79, 81 % within 45 deg, 3 % face downhill), KoTM (388 refs, R 0.87, 90 %), Cipactli (32 refs, R 0.95). Placement rule: yaw_cliff = a_open_side - uphill_azimuth, then +/- 45 deg jitter; never free-standing, never on a convex hilltop, bury the lower edge at the sink in 2.2.
- Not measurable here: KoTM's own rock walls (lsrockwall01-04), TS rocks (rock.nif, rockl01.nif), KoTM wave rocks - nifs not staged. BM&V's staged cliff-class loose meshes are two gkbreachclifftree* (trees, double-sided leaves: back fraction 0.34 to 0.62 in every direction, so leaf cards, not hollow) plus the RM_mountaincliff* set (13 files in landscape\mountains, not analysed because no BM&V ref uses them).

### 2.5 Cliff overlap and spacing
Same-class nearest-neighbour distance (dressed cells): KoTM cliffs median 13.6 m, p5 5.2 m (Clark-Evans R 0.82, near-regular); KoTM boulders median 5.3 m, p5 2.0 m (R 0.52, clumped); Valenwood/BM&V cliffs sparse. Vanilla cliff pieces are large (RockCliff03 18 x 19 m footprint), so overlapping them by 20 to 40 % of the footprint is the practice (median nn 13.6 m < footprint).

## 4. Region object generation (REGN)

## 7. Recommendations (each with its evidence)

1. Stage KoTM, Tropical Skyrim and Depths nifs before the nw-02 mesh checks: only path lists exist (section 1), so tree/hollow checks cannot run on them. Evidence: `flora_catalogue.json` `mesh` field counts (KoTM 322 of 586 rows listed-only, TS 70 of 71, Depths 25 of 60).
