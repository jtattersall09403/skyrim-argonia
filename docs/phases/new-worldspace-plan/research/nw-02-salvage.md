## Lessons as rules (with evidence)
### Texture edges and swathes
- Class map per cell gives rectangles: domain-warp the class lookup by noise (+/-14 then +/-30 vertices over 160) and blend where the class changes. (`lane-1.15-materials` mat0)
- Slope-keyed stops switch on cell-lattice lines when source heights are piecewise linear every 16 vertices: read slope over a 16-vertex tent (`SLOPE_R`), test `no_texture_boundary_runs_along_a_cell_edge`. (b14a0dd6)
- Skyrim.esm blends: 0.02-0.98 alpha run is 1 vertex p50, 5 p90; so paint soft strokes, 1-5 vertices, not 6-17; max base + 6 ATXT per quadrant; p50 4, p90 8 distinct LTEX per cell. (`skyrim-does-it-this-way` section 4)
- Seam fix: restrict each vertex's weights to the textures kept by every quadrant sharing it, renormalise; otherwise a layer drops out at quadrant/cell seams. (87cf546a)
- VCLR: white by default; darken only within ~2-6 vertices of water; no slope/aspect shade (vanilla p10 185, p50 255). A waterline cut in VCLR drew rings. Apron beyond border: base texture only, no blend.
- Rock is a patch texture on noise, not a slope threshold (9 % under 10 deg, 20-25 % over 20 deg in Skyrim.esm).
### The "flat mossy pebble" swathes (cause and fix)
- Cause: `LRiverbedEdge01` (BM&V riverbededge.dds) keyed on height-above-water alone; on flat swamp every flat 1 m above water got it: 16.9 % of the province, 28.3 % of dry ground, 46 % more than 29 m from water.
  The sheen came from landscape normals missing/DXT1 (TS normals alpha 255; Skyrim's mean alpha ~22/255). BM&V diffuses are 512 DXT3 with no mips and no normals.
- Fix commits: `e396d20f` (edge texture only within 2-4 vertices of water; dry ground lifted to class land stops; new patch stops for litter/moss/beach/marsh grass; TXSTs name Skyrim.esm's own `_n`),
  `72fbb962` (flooded-forest leaf-litter gate 0.54->0.48), `267bae8d` (derive mips + normals at Skyrim.esm tilt/alpha at install). Result: edge 28.3 -> 2.6 %, largest single texture in any 1 km window 0.75 -> 0.38; owner #41 closed it.
- Rule: no texture over 40 % of any 1 km window; check against the vanilla riverbed-edge share (2.51 % of dry ground within 16 v of water). `check-materials` does this.
- Grass: every painted LTEX needs a GRAS list or the ground is bare; roads paint with no grass (paint then grass follows), but paint edge must fade by 7 m or ferns grow on the lane.
### Water edge sequence (Skyrim.esm measured)
- River bed (below -384 u) -> mud/edge from -384 to line -> `LRiverbedEdge01` to +128 u -> land. Sea: ocean floor/beach below, `LCoastBeach01` 0 to +256 u. No mud above water.
- Shore profile: marsh rises 0.06 at first vertex, 0.25 by vertex 5, bed flat at 64-80 u (1.1 m); lake/sea 0.19 then 0.375-0.47. Banks keep rising past 10 vertices (330 u marsh, 650-730 u lake).
  The "shelf" mistake: a flat +0.3-0.5 m plate 5-15 m from water gave a straight edge and notches (`lane-1.15-r6-shores-slope`, c3330ce8). With global level 0, do the profile in the heightmap once, not by freeboard snap.
- Water: carry the level into every cell the water touches plus a padding ring (vanilla pads 23 cells with no wet vertex); one XCWT/XCLW per cell, one LOD water type (NAM3) per worldspace.
  Adjacent cells of different WATR at the same level show a hard shallow-colour band (cyan strip, c3330ce8 note): give types a shared shallow colour or use Transition types at rims.
- WATR: read templates from the winner (Tropical Skyrim DefaultWater 81,174,165 / 28,104,140); black swamp reads blue at grazing angles unless reflectivity 0.28/fresnel 0 (Bethesda's dark still water).
### Tree seating
- Cause: pivot set on the ground (sink 0); the mesh's lowest point is root tips, so trunks floated 3-5 m. Fix (`828d16d6`, `seat.py`, `data/tree-seat.json`): per form (fallback per mesh, >=5 refs) the median depth of the mod's own REFR z against that cell's VHGT, divided by ref scale.
  Measured units: KotM Erut02 340, Erut06 390, Palmoke palms 415, thick palm 71.5, fan palm 34.5, swamp cypress ~0; hanging-moss "gallery" forms are OBND z0 +456..543 and must hang from a tree, never stand.
- Placer bounds: trunk base within 10 u over / 40 u under both triangulations of the quad (mean of the two diagonals); refuse quads twisted > 0.45 m. A trunk stand/camera 6-10 m off the trunk footprint (roots flare 5-17 m); 61 non-flora (stockades, walkways) had entered lists by role words (`NOT_FLORA`).
- No tree in the water: 14.3 % stood in water vs Skyrim.esm 5.8 % (all of those mostly <256 u); dry trees >= ~3 m (200 u) from waterline for 90 % of stems; only cypress/mangrove in blackwater. (6cdcfa4c)
- Drop flora with any missing texture slot (`mesh_textures.incomplete`); red/neon trees came from vertex colours and Own_Emit baked in meshes (patched in `data/mesh-patches.yaml`, 33 meshes).
### Rocks and 'hollow backs'
- Skyrim does not conceal hollows: boulders sink 0.5 of OBND height (p10 0.1, p90 0.8), rock piles 0.5, cliff/mountain shells 0.7 (0.4-0.9, ~677 u). Old rule: pivot = scale x (OBND bottom + sink_frac x height) below ground so the open underside is buried;
  `lists.SINK_BOULDER 0.5 / SINK_CLIFF 0.7 / UPRIGHT_BOULDER 0.25` (a quarter of vanilla boulders stand upright; shells always conform ~11 deg less than slope).
  Earlier 0.38/0.25 sinks left rocks hovering.
- Open-backed shell pieces face their back into the hill (yaw = downhill + 180 - openBackYaw); never freestanding; a piece whose lower half hangs > 0.75 m above ground at any point is cut, not floated. (three.js-era 0073/0075 decision 11, `rock-placement-rules.md`; mined bottom-profile idea.) In the Skyrim pipeline only the sink-fraction half was implemented; no mesh-level underside check.
- Rock density: 3-7/ha (Clark-Evans R 0.32-0.58); cliff shells ~0.4/ha, only on steep ground (>=25-28 deg), about half of steep vanilla cells carry 1-2 shells, the rest are bare textured LAND; rock cells are the upland regions; BM&V rocks have no records, so we wrote 20 STATs (`rocks.py`, FormIDs 0x0C0000+, OBND from the NIF vertex box).
### Forest / open transitions (what was actually done)
- Region-class object lists (RDOT-like rows) with clumping + radius, ground-class stops from material.py, per-cell budget thinned to vanilla's forested range (marsh ~18 trees/cell, forest ~31), road/place/water exclusion, river banks opened (tall plants in clumps within 12 m; Skyrim.esm's 0.664 open share is the bar),
  and min-spacing. There was NO shared openness field. If the owner wants glades, add it: three.js-era finding is variation CV 2-3, correlation gone by ~60-200 m, median gap ~10 m, max ~30 m; edge wall within 10-20 m of any canopy break (shrubs x4-8). Implement as noise multiplier on density keyed to distance from water/road/clearing.
- Region variety: pools drafted per class (min share differing 0.00-0.14 before -> 0.77-0.89 after, `lists.FAMILY`/`draft`): each class draws least-shared forms. Cost: fewer kinds per class (about 35-48).
### Terrain relief
- All-Tamriel heights low-passed (sigma 24 m) to kill 1-unit terraces (flat pair share 0.115 -> 0.001). Slope limit 75 deg (vanilla has 0.35 % above; max 86.5). Banks at water: step of 69 m only where bank and bed meet in one vertex.
- Vanilla slope histogram (12.18 M vertices): 0-10 deg 17 %, 10-20 23 %, 20-30 20 %, 30-40 15 %, 40-45 6.5 %, 45-60 14 %, 60-75 4.7 %. Dungeon mouths are cut into a rise (>=550 u higher within 1,024 u behind) not raised on a mound.
### Roads
- Skyrim road = shallow trough (rises 0.02/0.09/0.19/0.29/0.40/0.55 m at 1,2,3,4,5,6 m... measured 0.10 at 2 m, 0.42 at 4 m, 0.75 at 6 m), flat width p50 3.0 m p95 7.5 m, grade p50 0.053, p95 0.164, max 0.219, paint full to 5 m, gone by 7 m; Road* surface meshes (~18 m apart) and RoadChunk stones on the road (lateral p50 2.8 m, ~15 per 100 m where present). No crown, no raised causeway, no edge posts at spacing. Signposts at junctions only. Roads yield to water (split into dry runs, never cut > 1 m).
