# The second walk of the new world: what changed and what to look at

This is the list of things for you to look at in the game after your second walk of the new world. Each bullet is one
check. You do not need to have read anything else. Reply to each bullet with "ok", or with "fault" and a few words
saying where you were and what you saw. A picture helps for anything you see.

## What this round changed

- A new game on the "Argonia: New World" card now starts in the new world, not in the barge's hold.
- The travel menu fits on the screen and every page can be reached.
- Logs, stumps, trees and rocks are now seated by their own shape: a log by the bulk of its body, a tree by the foot
  of its trunk, a rock so that its hollow side is buried. Pieces that could not be seated well were left out.
- Ground textures now blend as the game draws them, which removes the hard-edged squares near and far, and keeps
  grass off the roads.
- The very tall swamp grass was replaced by shorter grass of the same kind.
- The turquoise bamboo leaves were recoloured green. No other tree was moved.
- There are many more creatures, at Skyrim's own rate per cell you can walk on, and every creature a spot promises is
  placed within about a minute's walk of where you land. Birds now fly as Skyrim's birds do.
- Creatures in the safest places hit about as hard as Skyrim's first wolves and mudcrabs.
- Combat music plays in the new world, the water splash is white again, and the map and save list name the region you
  are in.
- The camera comes in towards your character when you look up, as in plain Skyrim.
- The far land and the distant trees are made again at the end of this round (the long "level of detail" build, which
  draws what is far away), so the faults you saw there are fixed by that build.

Two creatures were dropped because their files do not work in the game: the swamp wyvern and Black Marsh's centipede.

## How to get in (game pad)

1. Start the "Argonia: New World" card and choose New Game. Make your character. You should land at the start of the
   new world.
2. To jump anywhere, use the lesser power "Argonia Travel". It is on the shout button. Its first page has four buttons:
   "The start" (where a new game begins), "Places" (every place, by region), "Waters" (the water checks) and "Walk
   spots" (the spots named below).
3. Long lists are split into pages. Press right on the pad to step along the buttons, and A to press one. "More..."
   opens the next page of the same list, and "Back" goes back one page. B closes the menu.
4. The buttons have short names. The text above them gives each short name's full description. Below, each check gives
   the short name in quotes, then the full description.

## Climbing and the camera

- **FreeClimb (line 18).** FreeClimb is the mod that lets you climb rocks and trees. It is on, and it now writes a line
  to its log on every attempt with the reason for any refusal, so one try is enough. Steps:
  1. Put your weapon and spells away (it refuses while either is drawn).
  2. Stand on foot, out of the water and not crouched, and do not touch the keyboard: use only the game pad.
  3. Stand facing a rock face steeper than about 45 degrees, for example at "Dry scrub".
  4. Hold the left stick forward and press Y into the face. Do not hold B or pull the stick back.
  5. Then try the same against a big tree trunk.
  Reply ok if you climb, or fault with "rock", "tree" or both, and say whether your weapon was away.
- **Camera looking up (line 19).** In third person on open ground, zoom out a little and push the stick fully up to
  look at the sky. The camera should come in towards your shoulders and never go under the ground. Reply ok or fault.
- **Camera in plants (line 19).** Do the same standing in ferns or reeds (for example at "Harvest plants"). A plant
  near the lens may show for a moment, as in plain Skyrim, but the camera should not stay stuck behind plants. Reply ok

## Your questions

- **Is the new relief read into the Atlas correctly? (line 2)** Yes. The Atlas shades the same height file the world
  was built from (the two match to the byte), and across 600 sampled cells its heights match the game's own ground to
  within half a unit. Before, you could not see which build it showed; now you can, and it refuses to build from any other.
- **How are creature animations checked? (line 17)** A new check follows every creature we place to its body, its
  skeleton and its animations, and fails one whose skeleton the animations do not drive. That is what your flying
  serpent was: its animations were made for a different skeleton, so it slid about rigid. It found four such
  creatures. The serpent now uses another mod's serpent, which moves properly, the wisp uses Skyrim's, and the swamp
  wyvern and the centipede were dropped. Every creature now moves as it should.
- **How does Skyrim tell you which region you are in? (line 29)** Each place in Skyrim belongs to a "location" (for
  example Whiterun Hold), and the cells of the land carry it. The map, the save list and the journal show its name, and
  "Discovered" names a map marker. Our world had no locations at all, so nothing ever named a region. Now each region
  is a location named for the region, inside one called "Black Marsh", and every cell of the land carries its region's.
- **Was FreeClimb turned off? (line 18)** No. Its log from your walk shows it loaded and ready. It writes no reason
  when it refuses a climb, which is why we could not tell offline what stopped it. The likeliest cause is a drawn
  weapon, which the soulslike mods keep out in combat. Its log now names the reason on every attempt, so the check
  above settles it in one try.
