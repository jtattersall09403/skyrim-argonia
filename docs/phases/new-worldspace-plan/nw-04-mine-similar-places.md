# Mine similar places

Mine BM&Valenwood mod (for Blackrose, Thorn, and all of its minor places and POIs), King of the Murkmire (for all of its minor places and dungeons, plus any major places it has that we also have in our map), and lastly Skyrim (for any places not covered by the first two, or not covered enough - e.g. we need to have enough 'mines' and caves and ruins and so on to match up to all of the ones in our map) for places that are semantically similar to those in our game. Record a selection of similar places for each, ranked by similarity.

After all collected, use an optimisation/matching algorithm to match up places in our new game/map to mined places, such that no two of our places share the same mined place.
