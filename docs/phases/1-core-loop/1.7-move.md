# 1.7: Move: climb, swim, sail

**Goal.** The three traversal promises each run once on the host, each on the mod it will be built on:
- free climbing with our stamina rule;
- swimming and diving with our breath rule;
- one boat sailed with the player aboard, and moored.

These are first rungs of [2.13](../2-slice/2.13-swim-and-climb.md) and [2.15](../2-slice/2.15-boats.md). The boat
is in the thread because it is the traversal system with the least prior art on this host.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 1.1. Expected: G3 confirmed FreeClimb, EVG Animated
Traversal, Underwater NG and Sailable Ship on the pinned runtime (or named their replacements); G7 proved the
water model on one river reach.

**Re-check before starting:** FreeClimb, SkyParkour, EVG Animated Traversal, Underwater NG and Sailable Ship
versions on the pinned runtime.

**Read first.** 2.13 and 2.15 in full; universal-modder's `port-a-mod` skill and its field notes on climbing and
`porting-a-vehicle-semantic-traps` (decision 0020 §4).

## Deliverables
- **Climb** (2.13 §a rules): FreeClimb configured to the old rules:
  - the slope bands and the scramble band;
  - climb speed and drain by band, and the burden drain multiplier;
  - mantle and ledge costs, and fall at zero stamina;
  - no wall running.

  One real cliff in the block and the proving ground's walls of each class, wet and dry.
- **Swim and dive** (2.13 §c rules): native swimming with Underwater NG; the stroke speed by Athletics; breath =
  25 + 0.35 × Athletics + 0.25 × Endurance seconds; Argonians unlimited; drowning damage.
- **A ladder** (EVG Animated Traversal) at one proving-ground and one Claywater ladder.
- **Sail** (2.15 §a, §b rules, first rung):
  - one hull (the dugout on the block's river, or Sailable Ship's ship on the sea, whichever the block's water
    suits);
  - kinematic Wind Waker-style motion toward the steered heading, with frame-time-independent integration;
  - the player carried as a passenger on the deck, never simulated by Havok buoyancy;
  - board, steer 200 m, and tie at a mooring;
  - the owned boat's co-save family;
  - rest and save forbidden at the helm.

## Acceptance
- The proving-ground climbing course passes, with stamina drain within 5% of the formula.
- Breath per race and stats matches the formula.
- The boat holds the player on deck at full speed for 200 m with no fall-through. It reaches the mooring and is
  still there after a rest.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** climb per wall class, wet and dry (L4 plus stills); climb stamina (L2, L4); swim and breath (L2, L4);
  the ladder (L4); sailing motion (L2 sim of the model, L4).
- **Joins owned:** climb → fall damage (L4); climbing out of water **L5, reason: the hand-off shows only in motion**;
  boat → mooring → rest (L4); boat → save rule (L4).
- **Journey:** climb the cliff, drop into the river, dive under the boat, climb aboard, sail to the mooring, tie,
  rest (both runners).

## Deliberately out of scope

recovering boats (2.13, 2.15).
