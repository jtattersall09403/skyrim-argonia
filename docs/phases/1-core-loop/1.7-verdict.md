# 1.7: The first-place journey and the owner's verdict

**Goal.** The first place is played as one loop: by the journey runner until green, by a soak, and then by the owner,
who decides whether this is the game they want (decision [0027](../../decisions/0027-build-in-the-order-of-the-usps.md)).

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes the last of 1.1–1.6.

**Read first.** The Part 1 README; the 0.2 §d latency record; every Part 1 brief's Outcome.

## Deliverables
- **The first-place journey** (`tests/journeys/first-place.yaml`), built from the chunk journeys. It runs: arrive
  at Claywater Station by the road, talk and learn a topic, ask it of a second person, take the quest, follow its
  directions, do its action, loot and equip, fight the ambush, die, wake, retrieve the vastei, finish the quest,
  rest, spend at the sitting, then load the rest save and read every family.
  - It must be green twice in a row.
  - It is also looped for 60 minutes as a **soak**: no crash, Papyrus queue length flat, co-save size flat after
    the first loop, frame time within the 0.2 §d bar.
- **Immersion counts:** the inventory's reachable rows for the block, with verdicts so far. Any Nordic element
  the owner will meet is fixed before the check (a blocker, never polish).
- **The owner packet** (via `owner_inbox.py`), plain English, one check per bullet:
  1. From the console, **`bat first-place`**. Play it through: walk in, talk, take the quest and follow
     the journal, fight, die, recover, rest, level. About 30 minutes, on the phone and once on the Mac.
  2. **`bat duel-ring`:** duel at light, medium and heavy load.
  3. The measured stream latency for each device and network is printed on the packet.
  4. Reply with five answers (yes / no / not yet, and why):
     - does the place feel like Black Marsh;
     - does the talk feel like Morrowind;
     - could you follow the quest from the journal alone;
     - is this the game you want;
     - is combat on the tuned stack good enough to carry on with (its DS1 map is 2.7)?

     Add anything that jarred. Use **Report here** in the studio to mark places.
  - Also in the packet: the wiring-spec draft (1.6) for review, the quest record and its lines (1.4), the costs,
    the re-estimate of Parts 2–4, and the O-2 (20x for Part 2's two tracks, with measured numbers), O-4, O-9,
    O-11 and O-16 asks.
- **Triage:** every finding is a blocker (fixed before Part 2's systems track opens) or polish with its tag and
  the Part 2 chunk that owns it.

## The decision this chunk makes
- **Yes on the first four:** Part 2's systems track opens (the world track is already running).
- **Not yet:** the planner names what would change the answer, fixes it inside Part 1, and asks again (at most
  twice before a re-plan).
- **No:** a re-plan on Skyrim (the owner ruled out OpenMW on 2026-10-28): the planner writes a costed comparison
  of the ways to fix it, and the owner chooses.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Journey:** the first place; the soak.
- **Joins owned:** any join between two Part 1 chunks that no single chunk owned, found by the journey. Each is
  fixed in the earlier chunk's code with a test beside it.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **The stream feels poor over a long session** (fatigue, drops, bitrate): revisit 0007's options (bitrate, codec,
  AV1 on L4) before judging the game.
- **Integration surprises between Part 1 chunks** (dialogue interception × the quest stages, death × the save
  rule, the stack × the sheet): the journey and soak exist to catch them; each is fixed at source, once, with a
  test.

## Deliberately out of scope
New features. Thin content is expected and is not part of the verdict.
