# 1.7: The first-place journey and the owner's verdict

**Goal.** The first place is proven in one batch of in-game checks and then played by the owner, who decides
whether this is the game they want (decision [0027](../../decisions/0027-build-in-the-order-of-the-usps.md)).

**Status.** in progress; waits on 1.2's and 1.5's reds and the distant terrain (2.W4). The row in
[PROGRESS](../../PROGRESS.md) and this part's `checks.md` ledger are the record.

**Starting state (2026-10-31).** 1.1–1.6 and 2.W1–2.W4 are built and green at L1–L3. In game, batches game-14 to
game-21 have run their journeys; the reds left are die-and-recover, menus-quit, ui-sweep and ui-review, and the
distant terrain is being regenerated (PROGRESS). `tests/journeys/batches/all.yaml` holds every runnable journey,
ordered by crash risk with the save-changing ones last; the exit is one pass of it. The owner packet is drafted
in [verdict-packet-draft.md](../../research/2026-10-30/verdict-packet-draft.md); its in-game lines wait on that pass.

**Read first.** The Part 1 README; the 0.2 §d latency record; every Part 1 brief's Outcome; the owner brief's
2026-10-30 rulings.

## The owner's rulings this brief follows (2026-10-30)
- **No soak.** The 60-minute loop is dropped unless the owner asks for it.
- **No duel ring and no test arena.** Checks run in real places: Claywater and the province.
- **We test our integration, not other modders' work.** A check proves what we made (a creature placed, alive and
  configured; a mod wired and loaded; a stage set by the real path) and can fail for a reason about our work. It
  never judges behaviour a modder built and tested (the crocodile's attack, a dodge's feel).
- **Checks are simple, in real places, in one batch**, with the checks most likely to crash run last so they
  cannot block the others.
- **Part 1 checks are headless component checks.** No agent plays the quest start to finish; state and events
  prove the blocks and their joins.
- **The look of the place is the owner's call**; agents check Nordic tells, not feel.
- **DSC (combat groups B and B2) is not viable on this stack** (f74e2f5): Pandora parses it but none of its
  events reach the behaviours, and Nemesis hangs. Combat stays on group A; each DS1 behaviour DSC offered goes to
  group A or our own build (0019, 2.7).
- **Distant terrain (LOD) is made the native way**, by the Creation Kit, and runs after the batch.
- **Frame rate (#16):** the owner plays the shaders-on and shaders-off Moonlight cards, then decides the machine.

## Deliverables
- **The batch** (`tests/journeys/batches/all.yaml`), run once by the game lane. The verdict needs every Part 1
  journey green in it; a red is diagnosed with the others, fixed once at source, and re-proved by its one journey.
- **The first-place journey** (`tests/journeys/first-place.yaml`) is a 16-step walk-through of the loop's talks,
  quest and rest in Claywater: arrive by the road, make camp, talk and learn a topic, ask it of a second person,
  take LF83, hear both sides, draw the well water, take the clay, settle the terms, and finish the quest. It reads
  state and events and keeps a still per step. The fights, death, the sitting, loading and the co-save families
  are owned by the other journeys (croc-den, fight-*, die-and-recover, grow-at-rest, rest-is-the-only-save,
  cosave, suspend-save), so first-place does not repeat them.
- **Immersion counts:** the block's reachable rows with their verdicts. A Nordic element the owner will meet
  outdoors is fixed before the packet (a blocker); interiors may look Nordic (owner, 2026-10-30).
- **The owner packet** (via `owner_inbox.py`, posted by the planner after the batch), plain English, one check
  per bullet:
  1. **`bat first-place`**: walk in, talk, take the quest and follow the journal to its end.
  2. The shaders-on and shaders-off Moonlight cards, then the machine call on #16.
  3. The frame times (0.2 §d) and the stream latency, measured, printed on the packet.
  4. Reply with five answers (yes / no / not yet, and why):
     - does the place feel like Black Marsh;
     - does the talk feel like Morrowind;
     - could you follow the quest from the journal alone;
     - is this the game you want;
     - is combat on the tuned stack good enough to carry on with (its DS1 map is 2.7)?

     Add anything that jarred. Use **Report here** in the studio to mark places.
  - Also in the packet: the 1.6 wiring spec for review, the quest record and its lines (1.4), the costs, the
    re-estimate of Parts 2–4, and the open calls O-2 (20x for Part 2's two tracks, with measured numbers), O-4,
    O-9, O-11 and O-16.
- **Triage:** every finding is a blocker (fixed before Part 2's systems track opens) or polish with its tag and
  the Part 2 chunk that owns it.

## The decision this chunk makes
- **Yes on the first four:** Part 2's systems track opens (the world track is already running).
- **Not yet:** the planner names what would change the answer, fixes it inside Part 1, and asks again (at most
  twice before a re-plan).
- **No:** a re-plan on Skyrim (the owner ruled out OpenMW on 2026-10-28): the planner writes a costed comparison
  of the ways to fix it, and the owner chooses.

## After the batch
- **LOD:** the Creation Kit's own LOD generation for ARG_Province ([ck-lod-plan](../../research/2026-10-30/ck-lod-plan.md)), then
  the world-sample's distant-view stop.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Journey:** the batch, with first-place in it.
- **Joins owned:** any join between two Part 1 chunks that no single chunk owned, found by the batch. Each is fixed
  in the earlier chunk's code with a test beside it.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **The stream feels poor over a long session** (fatigue, drops, bitrate): revisit 0007's options (bitrate, codec,
  AV1 on L4) before judging the game.
- **A crash-prone check blocks the batch:** it runs last by design; a crash is a failure, diagnosed with the rest.

## Deliberately out of scope
New features; the soak; arenas and duel rings; judging other modders' behaviour. Thin content is expected and
is not part of the verdict.
