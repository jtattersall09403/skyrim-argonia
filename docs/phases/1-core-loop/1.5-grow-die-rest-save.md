# 1.5: Grow, die, rest, save

**Goal.** The Morrowind-and-Souls heart of progression runs once, end to end:
- skill use in anger earns vastei, and vastei banks a pending level;
- rest opens the sitting, and the sitting spends;
- rest is the only save;
- death wakes you at the last rest and leaves your carried vastei in one pile you can take back.

This chunk builds the final rules of [2.8](../2-slice/2.8-progression.md) (its §a, §c and §d, in full) on the
thread's content.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 1.4. Expected: the sheet, items and one archetype exist.

**Re-check before starting:** Respawn – Soulslike's current technique and licence; Sleep to Gain Experience's
source on GitHub.

**Read first.** Decision 0005 (C3); 2.8 in full; imported `76-stats-progression.md` §120, §126 and the lines
2.8 cites; old 0031, 0037, 0039, 0144, 0147 (inherited-rulings).

## Deliverables
- **Skill use and vastei** (2.8 §a): the worthiness anti-grind rules; vastei = 4 × worthiness × (1 + effSkill/50)
  per qualifying use; ranks per pending level on old 0147's curve; vanilla level-up and XP suppressed through
  the 1.1 router.
- **Rest and the sitting** (2.8 §a, §c):
  - camp anywhere calm in the wild, and wait only in a settlement;
  - the **sitting view** (1.2) spends vastei at the formula cost, at most 5 points per attribute per sitting;
  - one pending level per sitting by default (O-16 open);
  - health recomputes.
- **Rest is the only save** (2.8 §c): manual saves, quicksave and autosaves are hidden; rest writes the save; the
  1.1 suspend save covers quits and stream drops.
- **Death and the pile** (2.8 §d):
  - death is intercepted (the Respawn – Soulslike pattern), and you wake at the last rest with the world
    persisting;
  - skills, levels, items and gold are kept; carried vastei drops as **one** pile (a second death replaces it);
  - within 2 m, "Take Vastei (N)" takes it all back;
  - the pile is a gold ring with a halo and rising wisps, from vanilla effect shaders;
  - waking counts as a rest for respawn.
- **Respawn on rest** for the thread's archetype (`onRest`). Placed treasure is `never`.

## Acceptance
- L2 tests for every formula.
- An L4 check: scripted skill uses → pending level → rest → the sitting opens → spend → values change.
- Die with vastei → wake at the camp → walk to the pile → take it → the amount is equal.
- A second death replaces the pile. The archetype respawns after rest.
- A save round trip keeps every family.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** vastei accrual and worthiness (L2); the sitting (L3, L4); rest rules (L2, L4); death and the pile (L2,
  L4 plus a still of the pile); respawn (L4).
- **Joins owned:** combat use → skill → vastei (L2); death in combat → pile (L4); rest → sitting → save (L4); wake →
  respawn (L4); the sitting's open and close **L5, reason: the rest → sitting hand-off shows only in motion**.
- **Journey:** fight to a pending level, die, wake, retrieve, rest, spend, load the rest save (both runners).
