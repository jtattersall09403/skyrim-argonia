# 1.6: Fight on the tuned stack, wired to the sheet

**Goal.** Combat in Part 1 is the souls-like stack the owner already played in 0.0 ("definitely playable",
"felt reasonably good"), made Argonia's: wired to the sheet so damage, stamina, carry and protection come from
the Morrowind-based numbers; the gamepad in DS1's layout; lock-on; one hostile archetype at fixed danger in the
block. No combat map, no DSR measurement and nothing built from DS1 here: that is [2.7](../2-slice/2.7-combat.md)
(decisions [0019](../../decisions/0019-combat-maps-each-ds1-behaviour-to-a-tuned-mod-or-our-own-ds1-build.md),
[0027](../../decisions/0027-build-in-the-order-of-the-usps.md) §3). The owner judges only "good enough to carry on
with".

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 1.3. Expected: the sheet (1.3 §a) and the straight-sword
class (1.3 §c); the 0.0 stack pinned by 0.2 on the pinned runtime (`infra/gpu-box/combat-test-mods.json` is the
seed of the manifest); the owner's feel notes in 0.0's hand-off; the HUD's bars (1.2 stage one: the vanilla or
TrueHUD bars restyled, not our own HUD).

**Re-check before starting:** versions of every stack mod on the pinned runtime.

**Read first.** Decisions 0004, 0010, 0019; 2.7 §a (the wiring specification: this chunk drafts it); the 0.0 §d
stack and feel notes; [stream-and-combat-stack](../../research/2026-10-28/stream-and-combat-stack.md) and
[parry-riposte-backstab](../../research/2026-10-29/parry-riposte-backstab.md); the 0.2 §d latency record.

## Deliverables
- **The wiring spec, drafted** (2.7 §a): how the sheet reaches the mods' damage, stamina, burden-tier rolls,
  armour and guard, by game settings, perk entry points and mod settings first; a thin hook only where no setting
  reaches. The archetype's HP and damage → a D2 band. The owner reviews the draft in 1.7.
- **The stack tuned for the thread,** settings only: the straight sword, rolls at the three burden tiers (TK
  Dodge RE's tiers mapped to ours), a medium shield's guard, parry, riposte and backstab as the stack does them
  today (the single-press backstab and every other tweak wait for the map), stamina costs and damage from the
  spec. Settings pinned in the dependency manifest; every mod in the sourcing register.
- **Traversal as installed:** FreeClimb, Swimming Extensions and Sailable Ship are in the stack (0.2), so the
  first place can be climbed, swum and sailed as those mods ship; no rule of ours yet (2.13, 2.15).
- **The pad and lock-on** as 0.0 left them (DS1 layout by position; TDM lock-on on the right stick), carried
  into the pinned stack's install.
- **One archetype:** a sword-and-shield human hostile on a Claywater record, in a fixed encounter zone (min = max,
  soul contract C14), with the stack's AI as installed. No AI tuning here.
- **Combat → skill use:** a landed hit trains Long Blade, not One-handed (1.3 §c's redirect), so 1.5's vastei has
  a source.
- **Journal entry:** `docs/research/journals/combat.md` opened: what the stack does well and badly in play, with
  the owner's 0.0 notes as its first rows. 2.7's map starts from it.

## Acceptance
- L2: the wiring draft's formulas computed for five reference builds, ratios as the spec states.
- L4, from **`bat duel-ring`** and **`bat claywater-ambush`**: draw, lock on, roll through a swing,
  guard, parry and riposte, kill, loot; a hit trains Long Blade; the archetype respawns after rest (1.5).
- A five-minute duel loop on the journey runner shows no crash and no growing script lag.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** the wiring draft (L2); each tuned action (L4 timestamps and state reads); the archetype (L4).
- **Joins owned:** the sheet → damage and stamina (L2, L4); hit → skill → vastei (L2 with 1.5); combat → the
  restyled bars (L4 still).
- **Journey:** draw, lock on, trade blows, roll, parry and riposte, kill, loot.

## Owner check
In 1.7: "good enough to carry on with on the tuned stack?" with the stream latency shown. The feel verdict against
DS1 is 2.7's.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **A stack mod fails on the pinned runtime:** 0.2's conflict list names the replacement; the loss goes to the
  combat journal.
- **A setting cannot reach a sheet quantity:** a thin perk-entry-point or hook row in the draft, flagged for 2.7.

## Deliberately out of scope
The combat map, DSR measurement (2.7 §b), runtime timing (2.7 §e), tweaks and built rows, other weapon classes, ranged,
more archetypes, bosses, encounter bands across the slice (all 2.7); climbing, swimming and boats (0.2 proves
the dependencies; 2.13 and 2.15 build them).
