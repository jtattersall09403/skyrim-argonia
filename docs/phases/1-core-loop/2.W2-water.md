# 2.W2: Water

**Goal.** Sea, lagoons, lakes, rivers, rapids and falls from the hydrology graph, by the method its first step (the stepped-river proof that was 2.W2; decision 0027) proves, swimmable
by the player and NPCs. A wide load: every body.

**Outcome (2026-10-30, lanes water-design and water-design-2).** L1 done; L4 owed. The water is designed (the
owner's ruling of 2026-10-29): nothing falls back, and a puddle may be dropped by design. The water plan
(`tools/argonia_world/water`, `python -m tools.argonia_world.water plan`) gives each of the 2,280 bodies one
designed outcome (`water_body_design`):
- **sea** (37): the worldspace default water at 0;
- **own level** (37): lakes, lagoons, tarns and plunge pools;
- **basin** (783): touching marsh, swamp and pond patches within 1.5 m join into one of 616 kept basins
  (`water_basin`), each at one level (median, lower quartile, lowest, or the spill level its shore holds);
- **dropped by design** (1,423): a basin under `pondMinM2` (500 m2) is a puddle (1,934 basins), under 0.15 m deep
  a film (2), under 2.5 samples wide on average a ribbon (5); 12 bodies have ground above their level everywhere.
  A basin a river runs into is never dropped. A body on another body's samples takes that body's outcome.

Rivers are step-and-pool: steps at least 16 m long, laid as turned square tiles, with rapids, cascade and falls
pieces (203, 382 and 486, and 18 mist). The regrade (`water_regrade`, 90,694 vertices) carves beds to the pool depth,
raises banks, sills and berms, and never carves below adjoining lower water. Its checks are green on the LAND's own
ground: 0 cells over the plane budget, 0 unrealised wet samples, 0 same-height overlaps, 0 floating plane samples,
0 unrealised bodies, 0 step violations (1,130 steps on 281 reaches).
- **The plane budget** is 128 per cell, a hard error naming each cell over it: the in-game run held 127, 65 and 32
  planes on the 60 fps cap with no measurable cost (journal, 2026-10-30). The plan uses 20,165 plane refs (max
  120, p95 26 per cell). If the owed uncapped run shows a cost, the budget tightens with its reason recorded.
- **Counted for the stills, not failed:** 1,530 wall samples (water beside lower dry ground, not under a piece)
  and 2,301 open sides (a water beside lower water over a bed below it), with banks flagged on 105 reaches.
- **ArgoniaWorld.esm** carries the water (115 MB). It is deterministic: `make plugin` twice gives sha256 0190ed40..,
  peak 1.13 GB.
- **Owed at L4**, the Part 1 world sample's stops 4 to 7 and 12 (`world-sample`): one stepped reach with rapids
  (step lines hidden, no z-fighting), one waterfall (the falls piece seated), one marsh basin (the designed level,
  no floating or missing water), one lake shore (banks), and one NPC swim. The uncapped frame time is owed by the
  game lane. Flowmaps are not generated: the rivers are still water by design.

**Starting state** (2.W1, 2026-10-29). The ground exists: `make plugin` builds ArgoniaWorld.esm (decision 0031),
with the WRLD `ArgoniaWorld` (000800:ArgoniaWorld.esm), default water height 0 and default land height -2048
units. Every province cell and every near-apron cell above -2 m has a LAND, flagged HasWater with no XCLW. This
lane's hydrology tables are in the database. The heights API, for any window of the grid (rows from the north
edge of the near apron, columns from its west edge; the province is rows and columns 2176..6208, y south as the
hydrology frame), is in `tools/argonia_world/terrain`: `grid.Sources.load()` memory-maps the inputs;
`src.heights(r0, r1, c0, c1)` gives the heightfield in metres (the frozen post-patch array, the apron joined
beyond it); `build.game_heights_m(src, r0, r1, c0, c1)` gives the ground as the LAND stores it (quantised to 8
units and cliff-limited; it differs from the heightfield by more than 0.06 m at only 0.33 % of vertices, all of
them on cliff faces). A cell's own vertices are `build.read_cells(out/terrain)` with `quantize.decode`. Cell
water heights and water planes are records of ArgoniaWorld.esm by kind (0031 §1): add a field to the cell line or
to the WRLD intermediate, and LandWorld.cs writes it. Its first step is the stepped-river proof
that was 2.W2: one reach dropping 4 m over 200 m as stepped planes with vanilla rapids pieces, the player and an
NPC swimming each plane, diving and breath (Underwater NG), a generated flowmap or still water by design.

## Deliverables

### a. Water (decision 0006; old 0047–0065; the stepped-river method)
- Sea: the worldspace default water at 0. Coast and lagoon water classes as WATR types (salt vs fresh visuals).
- Lakes and flat river segments: cell water height where the whole cell's water is one level; else placed water
  planes.
- Rivers: 101 rivers, 615 reaches, 202 junctions from `hydrology-graph.json`: each reach becomes placed water
  plane segments stepped at the graph's grade, with a vanilla waterfall/rapids piece at each step (old 0064:
  waterfalls are the vanilla kit) and flow from the method the first step proved (generated flowmap) or still water by design. 2,280 bodies are
  classified (sea, lagoon, lake, pond, river, rapids, falls, spring) into placement rules.
- The old project's "the water line is high water" (old 0063) holds: one static level per body. **Seasonal
  water levels (old 0049) are not modelled**: Skyrim water planes are static; flood seasons survive only as
  weather and text (decided here; noted in ACCOUNTING).
- **The look is Skyrim's, not the old project's** (decision 0026 §4): WATR types tuned with Community Shaders'
  water features until a reader judges them good against vanilla and modded reference stills. Nothing copies the
  old three.js water.
- Marsh mist, waterfall mist and cap clouds are Community Shaders volumetric fog and lighting, each weather's fog,
  and placed mist effects from vanilla or mod particle effects in hollows and over water. The old project's
  raymarched mist (old 0124) gives only where mist belongs.
- **Acceptance:** every body of the hydrology graph has its designed outcome realised or dropped with its reason,
  and no cell is over the plane budget (L1, the plan); the world sample's stops 4 to 7 and 12 pass in game (L4).

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** water by class: sea, lagoon, lake, river reach, rapids, falls (L1 every body has its outcome; L4 the world
  sample's stops); mist (L4 stills).
- **Joins owned:** water ↔ banks and fords (L4 stills); water ↔ NPC swim paths (L4 `nav`).
- **Journey:** `world-sample`, stops 4 to 7 and 12.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **Submersion misbehaves on placed planes:** the allowed Ghidra question (decision 0010 §4), as in G7.
- **Flowmaps cannot be generated:** the rivers are still water by design.

## Deliberately out of scope
Seasonal water levels (dropped, below); swimming rules (2.13); boats (2.15).
