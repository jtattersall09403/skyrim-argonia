**Status.** dropped (decision 0033: the world is rebuilt by 1.15; the water planner is retired). The row in
[PROGRESS](../../PROGRESS.md) and this part's `checks.md` ledger are the record.

**What it was.** Every body of the hydrology graph given a designed outcome (sea, own level, basin, dropped), rivers
as step-and-pool runs of placed `Water1024` planes with vanilla rapids and falls pieces, and a regrade carving beds
and raising banks: 20,165 plane refs, 1,531 pieces, 90,694 regraded vertices, a 128-plane budget per cell
([journal](../../research/journals/water.md)). World-sample stops 4–7 passed once in game.

**Why it goes.** Placed planes have no distant water, no mutual awareness and one reflection height per frame, so
they hover, gap and black out; 259 cells could not be planned at all; the owner saw it
([world-rebuild-options.md](../../research/2026-11-01/world-rebuild-options.md) §1, §3 B, §11).

**What stands.** The hydrology tables (`hydrology_body`, `hydrology_reach`, `hydrology_river`, `water_body_design`
as the classification input), the four WATR records, the piece inventory and the field sampler. 1.15 §a solves one
water level per cell and joins steps as Skyrim.esm does (§11 of the options record).
