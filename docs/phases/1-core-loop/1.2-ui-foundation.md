# 1.2: The UI, stage one: Pelagiad, the paper theme, the dialogue window, the journal, our HUD and the chart

**Goal.** The owner never sees a Skyrim-looking screen in the first place, cheaply: the Morrowind font
everywhere, a paper theme over the menus the engine already has, the compass and markers gone, and **our own
screens**: the dialogue window and the journal (content is written for them), **our HUD and the parchment chart**
(owner, 2026-10-29: both early, since a new world needs a new map and the HUD is seen every second), in the
approach 1.2 §0 chose. The whole design system, the Morrowind pause-menu windows and every other screen are
**stage two** (2.2, 3.1, 3.2;
decision [0027](../../decisions/0027-build-in-the-order-of-the-usps.md) §5). The seam (1.1) makes stage two a
replacement of views, never of rules.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 1.1 (dated then). Expected: the message schema and the menu
router exist; the Argonia profile carries Dear Diary, Pelagiad's font files, PrismaUI and SkyUI (0.2); the reuse
audit recorded the paper-theme family's permissions (the owner confirmed them). **The approach for our own screens
is this chunk's first step** (§0; it was a Part 0 spike until decision 0029).

**Re-check before starting:** the versions of what 1.2 §0 chose (PrismaUI 1.5.x, React and Vite if web views;
SkyUI 6 and the SWF tools if Scaleform); the paper-theme mods' permissions and SkyUI version support.

**Read first.** Decision 0017 in full (its four goals stand; stage one meets goal 1 and the font, stage two the
rest); 0014 §7–8; the 1.2 §0 study and the owner's choice; the reuse audit's UI rows; the old repo's `packages/ui`
README (imported) for the dialogue and journal screens' intent.

## Deliverables

### 0. The technology for our own screens, chosen with the owner
- The question is narrow now that the theme is Dear Diary's: **what draws our four screens** (the dialogue window,
  the journal, the HUD, the chart) and, later, stage two. Candidates: **PrismaUI** (HTML over DX11; agent-friendly,
  testable headless, single-author with a custom licence, depended on unmodified), **Scaleform SWF** (the
  engine's own, what Dear Diary is; hard for agents to author), a hybrid. One probe each, in game, on the hardest
  capability: a dialogue window holding 300 topics scrolling and filtering without a hitch, and a window that
  remembers its position after a restart (stage two's need); frame-time cost measured.
- Mock-ups for the owner: the four screens in two directions, as stills at desktop and phone sizes, with the
  recommendation. **The planner chooses on the probes and posts the choice with the mock-ups** (an owner call the
  run does not wait on; stage one's screens are small enough to redo if the owner overrules). Decision 0017
  records the choice; the briefs that name a technology are rewritten to match (a method change).
- **Gate:** the chosen approach's probe passed; the choice posted.

### a. Pelagiad everywhere, and the theme
- **The font library:** Pelagiad (OFL) as the web font for our views and, for every vanilla or SkyUI menu that
  remains, our font library SWF and `fontconfig.txt` (0014 §7), built on the player's machine by the installer.
- **The paper theme** over the vanilla and SkyUI menus (inventory, magic, map, container, barter, book, loading,
  message boxes): by route order (0010), first an existing skin whose permissions allow a public dependency
  (the owner named Dear Diary – Paper SkyUI Menus 23010, its Wood and Paper skin 44174 and the Oblivion-like
  Loading Menu 38708, all by uranreactor; their permissions are the audit's finding), else our own restyle of
  the same SWFs built on the player's machine (0014 §7). Nordic ornament is removed either way (0006).
- **A light Black Marsh retheme of the theme** (owner, 2026-10-29): Dear Diary's SWFs draw from DDS textures, so
  its borders, frames, buttons and ornaments are recoloured and retouched to our palette as **our own texture
  set** (UI art is ours to make, 0006; derived from Dear Diary's under its permissive terms, credited in the
  sourcing register), and the main menu's "Skyrim" logo texture is replaced by an **"Argonia" title** of our own
  (Fal-made or drawn; the wordmark is the one piece of new art here). A light touch: colour, trim and the title;
  layout and behaviour stay Dear Diary's until stage two.
- **Gone:** the compass and quest markers (No Google Maps, `bShowQuestMarkers`, the HUD's compass element
  hidden through the 1.1 router); the perk constellation and level-up notice (suppressed in 1.5); the vanilla
  dialogue menu for Argonia NPCs (intercepted, §b).
- **Magic as a dependency check:** Magic Expanded (133435, Morrowind and Oblivion spells) tried in the stack
  for 2.9's benefit; its verdict (dependency, pattern or no) to the journal and the audit.
- **Acceptance:** an L4 sweep of every menu the player can open from the first place shows Pelagiad, the theme in
  our palette and the Argonia title, no compass, no marker; a reader's Nordic-tells pass is clean; the owner
  sees a before-and-after still of the main menu and one menu in a check-in.

### b. The dialogue window
- The production version of 1.3 §b's window in the chosen approach: NPC text with hyperlinked keywords, the growing
  topic list, persuasion button, Goodbye, disposition shown as Morrowind shows it; Pelagiad; keyboard, pad and
  touch; the four viewports (1920×1080, 1280×720, the Magic V5 folded and unfolded at the 0.2 §d scale).
- Wired to ArgoniaCore through the schema with 1.3 §b.
- **Acceptance:** L3 stills at the four viewports read by a reader; in game it opens from talking, shows live
  topics, closes back to play with no vanilla flash (the open and close hand-off is **L5, reason: a hand-off shows
  only in motion**).

### c. The journal
- A Morrowind-style book journal in the chosen approach: dated entries, the quest index, directions text; the
  topics index is 2.12's. Pelagiad; the four viewports; opened from the pad's Back button and the keyboard's J.
- Wired to ArgoniaCore's log with 1.4.
- **Acceptance:** L3 stills; in game it opens, shows the 1.4 quest's entries, closes back to play (L5 as above).

### d. Our HUD (owner, 2026-10-29: early)
- Our own HUD in the chosen approach: souls-style bars that grow with stats (health, stamina, magicka, breath,
  climb stamina), the sneak eye, the effect stack, the vastei plaque, the place banner, the prompt with input
  glyphs; no compass; Skyrim's own symbols extracted at install (0017 §5). The vanilla HUD is hidden through the
  1.1 router. Boss bars wait for 2.7.
- **Acceptance:** each HUD state in an L3 still and an L4 still; readable at the Magic V5 folded.

### e. The parchment chart (owner, 2026-10-29: early)
- The world map as a paper chart in the paper theme, from a first rendering of the 2.W4 image's **sketch layer**
  (the province outline, coast, major rivers, major roads, and Gideon, Soulrest, Lilmoth, Blackrose, Archon,
  Stormhold and Thorn roughly drawn) with the **revealed layer** filled only where the player has been: "a rough
  sketch map that you fill in by exploring", so the player can see where to aim and discover on the way. No
  player-position marker (C9). The image comes from the heightfield now in `$ARGONIA_DATA` (an early render;
  2.W4 replaces it). Flat World Map Framework if it carries this; else our own view over the 1.1 schema.
- **Acceptance:** opens from the pad and keyboard; the sketch shows everywhere, Claywater's surroundings reveal
  after a walk; L3 at the four viewports, L4 in game.

### f. What is prepared for stage two

- The design language rounds with the owner (decision 0017 §2: mood boards, tokens, the three hardest screens)

  rounds do not gate Part 1.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **A paper skin's permissions do not allow a public dependency:** our own restyle of the same SWFs on the
  player's machine (0014 §7); the look is judged in 2.2's rounds.
- **The chosen approach cannot hold a 300-topic list smoothly:** 1.2 §0's probe already measured it; if it regresses
  in production, the window pages its topics.

## Deliberately out of scope
The design system, the pause-menu windows (draggable, resizable, remembered), the inventory with paperdoll, the
stats sheet view, the sitting view's final look (1.5 uses a plain themed view), the local map, chargen, the main
menu, settings (2.2, 3.1, 3.2); the OpenMW window study and the UI map (2.2).
