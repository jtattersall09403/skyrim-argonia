# 1.4: Fight: the DS1 core, first rung

**Goal.** One weapon class against one archetype, measured from Dark Souls Remastered and wired to our sheet,
built on the final combat engine and adapter (decisions 0004, 0019). The goal is that the owner can say
whether it feels like Dark Souls before anything else is built on it. This is the single largest quality risk
in the plan, so it comes first.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 1.3. Expected:
- G6 gave the first golden-table rows: rolls per tier, two parry windows, a straight-sword R1, one poise pool;
- G9 proved the runtime timing-control method on one roll, one R1 and one parry;
- the sheet exists (1.3).

**Re-check before starting:** BFCO, Precision (and its 1.7.x port), TK Dodge, TDM, OAR and SCAR versions on the
pinned runtime; new DS-style moveset releases on Nexus.

**Read first.** Decisions 0004, 0014 §2, 0019, 0020; [2.7](../2-slice/2.7-combat.md) in full (the whole design:
fixed rules, wiring, the engine, the adapter, measurement, suites); the 0.2 G2 latency record.

## Deliverables (each the first rung of the 2.7 deliverable it names)
- **The wiring spec, drafted** (2.7a): every quantity the thread touches, with its Argonia source and formula:
  - damage, stamina max and per-action costs;
  - burden tiers → roll i-frames and recovery;
  - armour → mitigation and poise;
  - shield → guard;
  - the archetype's HP, poise and damage → a D2 band.

  The owner reviews the draft in 1.8.
- **The golden table for the thread** (2.7b), with every row's provenance and zero `GUESS:`:
  - straight sword: R1, R2, running and rolling attacks;
  - roll and backstep at the three burden tiers;
  - medium shield guard (GuardRate, stability) and its parry window, with the parried state;
  - riposte and backstab multipliers and paired lengths;
  - stamina regen, delay and exhaustion;
  - lock-on range;
  - the archetype's attack set and poise.
- **The combat engine** (2.7c): the full state machine with every state, built once and final. The thread
  fills only its rows. Exhaustive L2 tests are generated from the table.
- **The adapter** (2.7d): BFCO plays actions, Precision supplies contact (the pre-hit callback asks the core),
  TK Dodge gives roll input with its own i-frames off, TDM gives lock-on, OAR exposes core state to clip choice,
  and the G9 timing control runs.
- **Moveset pick for the class, the roll and the shield** (2.7e): the closest permitted moveset to the DSR
  reference capture, with its correction stated. No FromSoftware-derived pack.
- **The ±1-frame suite** (2.7f) for every window above, at capped 30 and 60 fps.
- **One archetype** (2.7g): a DS1 Hollow Soldier-shaped human with sword and shield, mapped onto a Claywater
  hostile record, its AI configured from the soulstruct AI-shape summary (approach and spacing, combo odds,
  punish rules).
- **Feel, side by side** (2.7h): the same scripted inputs in DSR and in Argonia, shown side by side and read by
  readers. This is **L5, reason: feel**.
- The HUD's health, stamina and lock-on states join here (1.2 components).

## Acceptance
- Every thread window is within one DS1 frame (33 ms, aim 16 ms) at 30 and 60 fps.
- State-machine transitions are 100% covered.
- The archetype's duel reproduces the DSR capture's approach distance, combo length and punish reaction within
  the stated tolerances.
- The reader panel reports no "obvious" feel difference.
- A five-minute duel loop on the game runner shows no crash and no growing script lag.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** the windows (L4 timestamps); the state machine (L2); the wiring (L2 over five builds); the AI duel (L2
  sim, L4); feel (L5, reason above).
- **Joins owned:** the sheet → damage, stamina and poise (L2); core windows ↔ clip timing (L4); combat → HUD bars
  (L3, L4); combat state → lock-on camera (L4).
- **Journey:** draw, lock on, trade blows, roll through a swing, parry and riposte, kill, loot (both runners;
  inputs, not `activate`, for every action).

## Owner check
In 1.7, the heart of the verdict: duel the archetype at the three burden tiers in **Scenes → 1.4 duel ring**
(proving ground) and **Scenes → 1.4 Claywater ambush**. The stream latency is shown on the check.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **No permitted moveset is close enough** for the straight sword: try the next class with a close moveset (2.7e
  ranks them) and tell the owner; never ship a poor one.
- **Framework conflicts** (BFCO against others): the 0.3 manifest's conflict list; re-test after each addition.
- **Precision tunnelling** on fast swings: tune its collision step or capsules.

## Deliberately out of scope
Other weapon classes, ranged and thrown weapons, more archetypes, bosses (2.7); death and respawn (1.5).
