## Work
1. **The machine choice, decided on evidence: the owner's call, posted as an issue once the evidence is in.**
   - The g6.xlarge spot in Paris was reclaimed twice on 2026-10-31, the second time at once on a retry. The owner
     will pay a bit more for a machine that isn't interrupted often, but not on-demand's price ($1.02/h).
   - **Evidence to gather (read-only, no machine started):**
     - AWS's Spot interruption frequency band (the Spot Instance Advisor's rate bands) and spot placement scores
       for every GPU type that can run the game (g4dn, g5, g6, g6e and their sizes), in London, Paris, Ireland,
       Frankfurt and Stockholm;
     - the spot price history over 30 days;
     - our own interruption history from CloudTrail and the spot request statuses;
     - the stream latency each region adds from the owner's UK location.
   - **Options to weigh:**
     - a less-contended type or size (e.g. a larger g4dn or g5);
     - a capacity-optimised spot fleet across several types, AZs and regions behind the same image;
     - a Capacity Block or Savings Plan price;
     - on-demand only as a fallback for a stretch.
   - **The call:** two or three options, each with its expected interruptions per 10 hours, cost per hour and per
     month at the owner's hours, latency and frame rate (a fleet mixing types must still meet 1.17's bar).
     Recommend one. The owner rules; nothing is switched before then.
2. **The interruption notice** (any option with spot): a watcher on the machine reads the instance metadata's
   spot `instance-action`, writes the suspend save through ArgoniaCore, and tells the stream page why it stopped.
3. **The start retry (the owner said yes on #29):** the play page retries `IncorrectSpotRequestState` for
   2 minutes and shows "starting…".
4. **Bake the image** from the deployed Paris machine, with the driver rebind, the Networks pop-up off, the LOD
   and today's stack. Point machines.conf and run.sh at it. Delete ami-0b28d03211e7d92d2 one day after Paris has
   run from the new image.
5. **Nothing only on a machine:**
   - archive London's six folders, or name the repo tool that rebuilds each;
   - run `machine_inventory.sh` live on London and on Paris; both show nothing unarchived.
   - Only then may London be retired; that is the owner's call, and 1.17 may still want it.
6. ~~Prove wake.yml's Paris start~~: dropped, the "play" comment switch is removed (owner-brief 2026-10-31).

7. **The agents' machine, the same type** (owner, 2026-10-31): after the cut-over, `clone.sh --image <the London
   image> argonia-play-2 g6.2xlarge eu-west-2b` (tunnel off, SSH from the build VM only). The six folders are
   copied off snap-0a572c76892b61309 onto it and archived (`archive_state.sh`); then argonia-play-london, its
   volume and its spot request go. machines.conf and the game-lane briefs name argonia-play-2.
8. **The stream-feel tool** (owner's bar 3): an ability, not a regular test (owner, 2026-10-31). Agents run
   `infra/stream-client/e2e/feel.sh` for only what their change reaches (one device, one input kind, one screen);
   the full sweep only when the owner asks. It runs drive.mjs on the agents' machine once per device: desktop (mouse and keyboard), v5-folded
   and v5-unfolded (touch), the GameSir pad (ARGONIA_FAKEPAD, raw mapping), from a scene: open the HUD, move,
   attack, open a menu; stills of each and press-to-screen medians (the marker). The owner's estimate = that median
   + the owner's own network round trip and jitter from their logged sessions (collect.sh/summarize.py). Output:
   out/feel/<stamp>/summary.json. One proving run closes the row; it is never part of a regular batch.
9. **The price guard:** the cost report prints the play type's spot price in its zone; past $0.50/h, play_move.sh
   moves both machines to the cheapest L4 slot with a capacity score of 7 or more.
