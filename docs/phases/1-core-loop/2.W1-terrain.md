# 2.W1: Terrain, apron and edges (superseded by 1.15)

**Status.** dropped (decision 0033: the world is rebuilt by 1.15; this chunk's method is retired). The row in
[PROGRESS](../../PROGRESS.md) and this part's `checks.md` ledger are the record.

**What it was.** The whole worldspace's ground from the old project's frozen sculpt, painted one land texture per
land-cover class, with the Transbot9 apron beyond the border and closed playable edges. Built 2026-10-29 as
ArgoniaWorld.esm (decision 0031): 15,876 province cells and 17,899 near-apron cells, an int8 slope limiter on 674
cells, 0 seams, the border push and the first three world-sample stops proven in game
([lane report](../../research/2026-10-29/lane-2.W1-terrain.md), [journal](../../research/journals/terrain.md)).

**Why it goes.** The sculpt is alpine where the dossiers put marsh and uplands, the paint is one texture per
class with a one-vertex blend, and the ground was carved for placed water that cannot join
([world-rebuild-options.md](../../research/2026-11-01/world-rebuild-options.md) §1, §8).

**What stands.** The ESM writer and the cell, LAND and REGN shell pattern (0031); the apron method and the border
gates; the terrain checks (overflow, seams, probe heights) and `terrain/esm.py`; the units module's facts (126×126
cells, 1.828 m per sample, sea at 0). 1.15 §b generates the ground; 1.15 §c paints it.
