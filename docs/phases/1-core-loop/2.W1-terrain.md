# 2.W1: Terrain, apron and edges

**Goal.** The whole worldspace's ground from the frozen heightfield, with the apron beyond the border and closed
playable edges. A wide load: every cell, generated once by the pipeline, places layered on top by id (old 0140).

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 0.4 (this chunk starts beside Part 1; decision 0027 §2).
Expected: the 0.4 block's terrain is generated by the 0.4 §a pipeline. **First step here:** the frozen heightfield and
hydrology arrays are pulled from the old project's R2 vault into `$ARGONIA_DATA` with `python3 tools/fetch_vault.py`
(0.1 §e built and proved it; it needs the owner's three `ES_SNAPSHOT_` keys in `~/.argonia/secrets.env`, asked under
O-1 on 2026-10-29) and every hash matches `world/sources/terrain/freeze.json`. 0.4 uses only the block's corner of the
heightfield and needs the same pull, so the keys are the first thing 0.2 asks the owner for if still missing.

**Re-check before starting:** Tropical Skyrim SSE file version and which vanilla LTEX and GRAS forms it replaces.

**Read first.** Decisions 0009, 0010, 0014, 0022, 0026; [audit-world](../../research/2026-10-27/audit-world.md) §1, §3, §4;
[skyrim-tooling](../../research/2026-10-27/skyrim-tooling.md) §1, §5; imported `docs/design/world/00-core.md` (in
full), `20-province-design.md`, `60-water-traversal.md`, `72-navigation-ai.md`, `97-placement-principles.md`,
`98-data-model.md`; old decisions 0015, 0047, 0049, 0058–0069, 0102, 0114, 0128, 0137, 0140 (via
[inherited-rulings.md](../../inherited-rulings.md)).

**Fixed facts** (from the import; the units module owns them): province 7.37 km square, ~34 km² land, 4033²
samples at 1.828 m = 128 Skyrim units, so **126×126 exterior cells** of 4096 units (58.5 m) with 33×33
vertices; summit target 650 m; sea at 0; era 4E 201. The apron is the Transbot9 All-Tamriel heightmap at 1:1
beyond the border (old 0067; CC BY-NC 4.0, credited): the map ends 16.4 km N, 6.2 km S, 21.5 km W, 8.5 km E of
the border; the haze is opaque by about 10 km.

## Deliverables

### a. Terrain
- Worldspace `ArgoniaWorld` (WRLD: no parent, own map data, default water height 0, land data, music type and
  climate set to ours, "no fast travel" handled by our system).
- All 126×126 cells' LAND from the frozen heightfield plus the old terrain and route-grade patches; VHGT
  quantisation (float offset + int8 deltas per row) re-based where a row's delta exceeds the int8 range; VNML
  computed; VCLR from wetness/soil tint.
- Land textures (decision 0028): map the old land-cover classes and ground-material system (old 0011) to the
  **vanilla LTEX forms that Tropical Skyrim replaces** (its textures and its attached grass come with the player's
  install; we reference the forms, ship nothing), painted into LAND by class. No BM&V or Aendemika textures. Max
  6 layers per quadrant: a per-quadrant layer reducer keeps the strongest; grass then appears by itself.
- **Acceptance (L1+L4):** sampled ground heights within 0.5 m of the heightfield at 1,000 random points (bridge
  `read` after placing a probe); no cell seams (height and normal continuity across every cell edge
  checked in data); a reader agent's look at 20 vantage screenshots finds no texture tiling artefacts flagged
  "obvious".

### b. Apron and edges
- Apron cells beyond the border from the Transbot9 map: coarse terrain (every Nth vertex meaningful, flat
  texture), mostly reached only as LOD; the sea covers it at 0.
- Closed playable edges: the border is closed except the canon exit roads (Blackwood Road, Tear road) which end
  at a gate or ferry with a "the road beyond is closed" message; physical blockers sourced from kit pieces
  (no invisible walls where avoidable).
- **Acceptance:** the player cannot leave the province (an L4 check teleports to each border segment and pushes outward with short inputs); views from the
  coast show the apron through haze, with no hard edge.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** terrain heights and seams (L1 data, L4 probe heights, L4 vantage stills); textures by land-cover class
  (L4 stills); the apron and edges (L4).
- **Joins owned:** terrain ↔ a place's own ground edits at their edges (L4 stills, with 2.W5; decision 0026); terrain ↔ the 0.4 block (L1:
  the block regenerates unchanged).
- **Journeys:** none (no system state); the owner walks it in 2.W6.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **int8 delta overflow on cliffs:** re-base per row (already the method); survey every overflow first.
- **Six-layer limit per quadrant** shows banding: the layer reducer keeps the strongest; a reader judges.

## Deliberately out of scope
Water (2.W2), roads (2.W3), vegetation (2.W4); seasonal ground.
