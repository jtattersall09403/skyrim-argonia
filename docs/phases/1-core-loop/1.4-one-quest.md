# 1.4: One quest through the journal

**Goal.** The game's second priority (quests, storyline, people; decision
[0027](../../decisions/0027-build-in-the-order-of-the-usps.md)) runs once, end to end, at its final interface: one
Claywater Station quest written through the writing skills, generated into records, started in dialogue, followed
from the journal by written directions with no markers, and completed with its consequences. This is the first
rung of [2.12](../2-slice/2.12-quests-journal-map.md) (quests as data, the journal, directions) built on the
imported quest system, so that every later quest is written to a shape that has been played.

**Starting state.** Written by the agent that closes 1.3. Expected: the dialogue runtime's first rung and the
three Claywater people (1.3 §b); the journal view (1.2 stage one); the writing skills fitted to the import
(0.1 §i: `quest-write` → `quest-review` → `brief.py` → `dialogue-write`); the condition evaluator's first
predicates (1.3 §b); the route graph does not exist yet (2.W3), so directions here are written against the
block's own road and landmarks and validated by hand once.

**Re-check before starting:** nothing external.

**Read first.** Decisions 0024, 0025; 2.12 in full (its rules are the rules here); imported
`docs/design/quests/00-overview.md`, `85-condition-vocabulary.md`, the journal design; the quest record format
(`.claude/skills/quest-write/quest-record.md`) and its engine-mapping table (0.1 §i); old 0137, 0153.

## Deliverables
- **The quest, written:** one Claywater Station local quest with skill-based action (0024 §9), from the imported
  premise if one exists for the place, else a new record; its record passes `quest-review`; `brief.py` builds its
  people's briefs; its topics and journal text are written with `dialogue-write` and lint clean; its `new` things
  are placed in the block by hand (the world-builder skill does not exist yet; 2.W5 replaces the hand placement).
- **The quest generator's first rung** (2.12 §a): the quest record → one QUST with stages, log entries, objectives
  (with no target markers) and aliases, plus generated Papyrus begin-fragments; conditions and actions outside
  Skyrim's native set call ArgoniaCore through the 1.3 evaluator; tier protection and the dead-NPC fail-forward
  (old 0138) on this one quest.
- **The journal's first rung** (2.12 §b): dated entries and the quest index in the 1.2 journal view, from
  ArgoniaCore's log; directions text written into the entry; the topics index waits for 2.12.
- **No markers:** no map or compass marker for the quest (No Google Maps, objective target data omitted, the
  compass hidden in 1.2); the place's map marker appears only when reached on foot.
- **The co-save family** for quest-side state the evaluator reads (deeds counters, first rows).

## Acceptance
- The record is `reviewed` with no open finding; `brief.py` refuses once a cited fact is deleted; the lines lint
  clean.
- L2: every branch of the quest walked against the record (a unit test over the stage graph), including the fail-forward.
- L4, from **`bat quest-board`**: start the quest in dialogue, read the entry, follow the directions on
  foot to the target, do the action, return, see the consequence; the journal shows the entry and the stage; a
  save round trip keeps it.
- A reader confirms no marker appears at any point (stills at three steps).

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
- **Subsets:** the record (`quest-review`, `brief.py` as L1); the generator (L1 record checks, L2 walk); the
  journal (L3, L4); markers absent (L4 stills).
- **Joins owned:** talk → stage → journal entry (L2, L4); stage → greeting change (L2); complete → reward, no
  vastei (L2 with 1.5); every stage → save round trip.
- **Journey:** the quest start to end (the main path and the fail-forward).

## Owner check
In 1.7: take the quest and follow it from the journal alone (**`bat quest-board`**). The quest record and its
lines are posted in a check-in as soon as they exist, for the owner to read in their own time; nothing waits on
that read (the hands-on rule of 0024 §10 is for cities and the opening).

## Risks and fallbacks
- **Skyrim's objective system insists on a target:** aliases with no target and the objective text carrying the
  directions; if the HUD still shows one, the 1.1 router suppresses the objective display and the journal is the
  only surface.
- **Begin-fragments cannot reach a needed action:** a named ArgoniaCore call registered through the 1.1 router,
  listed with why it is not Papyrus.

## Deliberately out of scope
The topics index, the world and local maps, the directions generator and validator against the route graph, the
investigation support, the narrative registries (2.12); every other quest (2.12, 3.3, 4.2, 4.3).
