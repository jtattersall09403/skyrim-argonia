# 1.15: The world, rebuilt

**Goal.** The province's ground, water, materials, light and vegetation generated again from the prior, the
hydrology graph and the lore, by rule, to the vanilla water model, judged against the owner's mood board at a fixed
panel of in-game vantages, then frozen. After it, Skyrim's own water, grass, LOD and navmesh "just work" on it,
Claywater and the slice places are re-seated on it as patches, and every later world change is a patch.
Decision [0033](../../decisions/0033-the-world-is-rebuilt-water-first.md) holds the why; the evidence and the
options weighed are [world-rebuild-options.md](../../research/2026-11-01/world-rebuild-options.md).

**Status.** in progress (2026-11-03: round 4 built on the owner's play test 3 (docs/research/2026-11-02/owner-playtest-3.md, 34 findings) in twenty lanes and two in-game batches on Paris (docs/research/2026-11-03/round-4.md): the rivers on the vanilla step rule with dry gullies where a step would be too tall, the roads shaped into the ground as Skyrim's are, the pebble field and the shine gone from the materials, the fog records and the height-fog bank re-authored, the trees seated by their mods' own offsets and the red-leaf forms dropped, the Claywater block re-seated with the station house, pads and docks, creature lists and density at vanilla's measured figures, the chart re-rendered with its sketch layer, our inventory and character sheet on a custom-UI card, the Claywater people talking; still red in game at the second batch: distant water on the round-3 LOD tiles (the LOD rebuilt as the round's last step), the roads' 240 edge walls and the crossings (owed to bridges and the places lane), the menu sweep's pad presses on the Wind UI panes; the owner walks round 4 next)

### a. The water solve (L1)
- `tools/argonia_world/water` is replaced by `water/solve.py`: every body classed to one level per cell
  (`cell_water` table: height, water type among sea, brackish, blackwater, fresh); sea-level inland marsh at 0 as
  brackish or blackwater by salinity; lakes at their level; each river reach quantised to steps at cell edges, one
  step per edge at most, the drop bounded by the tallest falls piece; a reach too steep for that is a dry gully
  (`reach_kind` dry) with bed pieces; `water_step` rows (edge, upper, lower, piece chain) by the vanilla rule.
- **Acceptance:** every body has a `cell_water` outcome or a recorded drop; 0 cells with two levels; every step
  has a chain; `python3 -m tools.argonia_world.water check` green in under a minute.

### b. The ground (L1, L3)
- `tools/argonia_world/terrain/generate.py`: the prior low-passed (the Tamriel esp, raw −86..122 m, de-terraced),
  relief scaled per region from the dossiers (the border ranges and Blackwood uplands high, the interior low, the
  coast drowned), held to the 82.9° ground limit, light erosion and noise; then the water constraints (beds below
  their level, banks above, steps per `water_step`, shores sloped); road corridors kept; one reserved and shaped
  **entrance site per dungeon record** from its landform request and entrance type (`entrance_site` table: mound,
  bank, depression, flat landmark, deep water); the border apron as before.
- **Acceptance:** 0 overflow cells; 0 seams; every `cell_water` level fits its ground (no wet vertex above its
  water, no bank below the upper water at a step); every entrance site shaped; 1,000 random points within 0.5 m
  of the generator's own field; the whole province generates in under 10 minutes on the build VM.

### c. Materials (L1, L3)
- `terrain/material.py`: per region class a gradient of 4–6 LTEX (BM&V's dark pack over vanilla names, Tropical
  Skyrim where BM&V has none) keyed to height above local water, slope, wetness and position-seeded noise, blended
  over 10–20 m, written as layer weights per quadrant (base plus up to 5), VCLR for shade and wet, grass lists on
  the LTEX records; riverbed → water's edge → bank → land as a gradient everywhere water is.
- **Acceptance:** no quadrant over 6 layers; no class border with a one-vertex blend; a reader on the L3 material
  renders of four windows finds no swathe or hard edge.

### d. Light, weather and mist (L1, L4)
- `regions/api.py` rewritten: the LADDER and the one-tint weathers go; every slot of each of our weathers authored
  per time of day from the look board; image spaces without the yellow tint; fog near and far per weather and
  hour; one `ARG_Season_*` global per season on each region weather entry, set from the game month by
  ArgoniaCore's weather rule; a mist regime per region (`region_mist` table: radiation mist over inland water at
  dawn, sea fog on the open coast, a montane haze belt in the ranges) realised as weather lists plus Community
  Shaders' height fog, placed mist meshes only where the panel shows the weather fog is not enough.
- **Acceptance:** no weather colour derived from another; a 7-day skip cycles each region's list; the panel's
  dawn stills show mist where `region_mist` says and nowhere else.

### e. Vegetation, rocks, navmesh, LOD (L1, L4)

### f. The sample, then the whole, then the freeze

- **Acceptance:** `world-panel` proven in game; the freeze manifest committed; `claywater-walk` and

### g. Creature encounters between places (owner, 2026-11-01)
- The world feels alive from the first walk: a very wide variety of Black Marsh creatures, from insects to the big
  predators, spawned between places by rule, the Skyrim way. Inputs already in the DB: the `creature` table (habitat
  ground, water and network, hostility, den type, territory radius and group sizes, day activity, diet, sourcing),
  `creature_region`, `creature_band` and the `danger_band` rows (how tough; the diseases and poisons a bite or sting
  carries), the places' coordinates and kinds (where nothing spawns, where clearable places' creatures respawn
  on death until the place is cleared and then never: the flags are data here, 1.14 owns the runtime rule), the
  ecology and climate research (`docs/design/world/74-ecology.md`, `docs/design/research/`), and the mods'
  creatures (BM&V, King of the Murkmire, HTBM and the rest: their NPC_, RACE and behaviour records; each mod's own
  worldspace placements measured for where its creatures stand: in water or on land, swamp or mountain, bush or
  open, day or night).
- **The Skyrim way (0034), measured first:** how Skyrim.esm places wilderness encounters (its spawn markers and
  levelled-actor lists, their density per cell by terrain, distance from roads, water and settlements, encounter
  zones and their reset flags, predators beside dens, ambush points), then the same here: our own levelled-actor
  lists per region and band (fixed levels, never scaled to the player), spawn-point refs placed by the planner
  (`tools/argonia_world/creatures/`), encounter zones per danger band, a no-spawn radius around places, dens for
  territorial creatures (`npc_den`), water creatures in water cells, insects and small life dense and harmless,
  the big predators rare and where the lore puts them.
- **Acceptance:** L1: every region class has a list of at least 12 creature kinds with a why from the ecology or a
  dossier; every spawn ref stands on ground or water its creature's habitat allows; 0 refs inside a place's
  no-spawn radius; levels fixed per band; the planner runs on the sample in under a minute. L4 (the round's
  batch): a journey walks three stretches (marsh, river, upland) at day and night and counts creatures seen and
  what attacked, read against the band; a still of each big predator at its den. **Lightened (owner, 2026-11-01):** no walk;
  `creature-spots` teleports to six spawn points of different habitats (water, marsh, upland; day and night), reads
  the loaded actors within 40 m and their kinds, and keeps a still each; the modders' behaviour is not retested.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
Fast and cheap: nothing goes to the game that L1 or L3 can prove, and one in-game batch per round.
- **L1 data (seconds, every run):** the water solve's checks; the ground's overflow, seam, fit and entrance checks;
  the material layer and blend checks; the weather derivation check; the placer's checks. All in `make world`.
- **L2 logic:** unit tests of the step rule (bed, bank, slope, chain) and the material gradient on synthetic
  cells; the season global from the month.
- **L3 off-game stills (a minute):** `tools/argonia_world/terrain/render.py` draws a window's hillshade, water,
  material weights and placements as one image; the four sample windows rendered every iteration and read by a
  `reader` with the checklist. Blender through its MCP for form checks only, if the hillshade is not enough.
- **L4 in game, one batch per round:** `world-panel` (24 stills) plus `claywater-walk`, `claywater-look` and a
  frame-time probe on the densest cell, on one deploy of main on `--gpu=pick`; a reader judges the panel against the
  look board; a failing class is fixed once at L1 and the panel re-run. Three rounds are the budget before a
  diagnosis of the method.
- **L5:** none (no transition this chunk owns; the near-to-LOD clip stays with 2.W6).
- **Joins owned:** water ↔ ground at every step and shore (L1 fit); ground ↔ places (the re-seating, L4 walk);
  materials ↔ grass (L4 panel); weather ↔ mist (L4 dawn stills).
- **Journeys:** `world-panel`; `claywater-walk`; `claywater-look`; `rock-booth`; `creature-spots` (§g).

## Owner check
The panel's 24 stills and the look board side by side, posted as one issue: "does each region read as its
dossier and your picks; what jars". Replies by issue comment; a ruling per region class goes to the owner brief.
Cost: one game-machine hour per round.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **BM&V's landscape pack lacks a texture a gradient needs:** Tropical Skyrim's vanilla-named file for that slot.
- **A reach too steep for the step rule:** a dry gully with bed pieces (vanilla's own answer); the graph is
  re-routed only where a reach would need several steps inside one cell.
- **The panel stays red after three rounds on one class:** stop, diagnose the rule against vanilla's data (as §11
  did for steps), never tune by eye.
- **LOD generation time:** skipped on the sample rounds; run once on the whole.

## Deliberately out of scope
Tides and floods (out; a quest flood as vanilla); the night sky (unchanged); region sound tables (2.16); insects
and disease (2.14); decay states (the built-places part); the dungeon interiors themselves (2.W5, 4.4).
