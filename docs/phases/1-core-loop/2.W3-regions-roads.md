## Deliverables

### a. Regions, climate, weather, sky, light (decision 0028: regions are the unit of authorship)
- Each REGN carries three lists the engine and the CK read: its **weather list** (Tropical Skyrim's weathers as the
  base, our Black Marsh WTHR variants for mist, tannin haze and storm, by chance and season), its **object list**
  (the vanilla tree, shrub, rock and flora forms Tropical Skyrim replaces, with density, slope and height bands,
  clustering and water avoidance from the imported flora rules; 2.W4 runs the CK's generator over it) and its
  **map name**. Mist FX references join the object list only for regions the design calls misty.
- REGN per catalogue region packet (8) and ecological class (tidal delta, coastal lagoon, deep river corridor,
  flooded forest, interior swamp, seasonal floodplain, rootland, northern transition, western frontier, deep
  sink basin, karst/root cavern belt); CLMT and WTHR from `climate/weather-states.json` (rain, storm, haze,
  mist, clear), sky and the star catalogue (13 Patrons, the Serpent) as far as Skyrim's sky allows; image spaces
  restyled to a humid green-gold marsh light (no Nordic blue-grey).
- **Volumetric light and interior sun** (decision 0026 §5): Community Shaders' volumetric lighting and fog
  outdoors, tuned per weather. For interiors, research first which current mod or Community Shaders feature
  gives sunlight through windows that follows the sun (record the findings in `docs/research/`). Use the best
  one that loads on the pinned runtime. If none does, place static light beams at windows, faded by time of day
  (accepted by the owner). Lighting templates per interior kind.
- Time scale 30 (one game hour per 2 real minutes) as the old design.
- **Acceptance:** every cell belongs to one region; weather cycles per region in a 7-day time-skip check (L4);
  a reader panel judges 12 weather screenshots against the old weather references.

### b. Roads, routes, travel infrastructure
- The road network (old 0069: six legs and two exits; 0068 graded by patches; 0115 no crossings): road
  surfaces as land textures plus kit road pieces where the old layouts had them; bridges from kit pieces;
  signposts with sign text; harbour stations, ferry and rootworm stations as placed docks and NPC stations
  (travel dialogue is 2.6).
- Write the **route graph** used by the directions generator (2.12): nodes (junctions, places, stations),
  edges (road legs with length and landmarks).
- **Acceptance:** each leg is checked end to end on the road surface by teleport hops (L4 probe heights and stills); the route graph's distances match the
  terrain within 5%.
