# 2.W4: Vegetation, navmesh and LOD

**Goal.** The marsh's plants on the ground, NPC-walkable navmesh for every exterior cell, and distant land and trees,
with the painted map image. Wide loads, each a scripted, re-runnable job.

**Status.** in progress; owed: the archive, its restore on argonia-play and a Claywater still; w4-veg-densest a draft. The row in [PROGRESS](../../PROGRESS.md) and this part's `checks.md` ledger are the record.

**Starting state.** 2.W2 and 2.W3 stand as PROGRESS says (2026-10-31).

*From 2.W3 (2026-10-29):*
- **Region object lists.** There are 48 REGN. 47 are packet × ecological class, and each is in Argonia.esm as an
  override of a shell in ArgoniaWorld.esm, whose cells' XCLR link the shells (decision 0031's shell pattern,
  `tools/argonia_world/regions/api.py`). The 48th is the border.
  - There are 866 RDOT entries over 34 vanilla TREE/STAT forms (`land_region_object`, `object_form`). Each carries
    density, clustering, slope band, height band (units), radius (units) and the ConformToSlope/Tree flags. The
    CK's generator needs the REGN areas: 2,181 cell rectangles in the override.
  - **Not yet measured:** the RDOT density unit against the CK generator. The rows hold stems per hectare, written
    as is. Calibrate them on one region first.
  - The water posture (`water_min_m`/`water_max_m`) is in the rows but not in RDOT. The CK cannot express it, so the
    generator job applies it.
  - Coastal lagoon and deep sink basin regions have no object list by design.
- **Route graph.** 46 nodes in one component, 55 edges (23 traced land legs, 9 joins, water and root legs). The
  tables are `route_node` (exits carry the city beyond), `route_edge` (3D length over the 2.W1 heights, worst 0.43%),
  `route_edge_landmark`, `signpost` and `signpost_arm` (arms name the first place beyond any junction).
  - Roads are painted into the land cover (`routes/paint.py`, BC_ROAD 30 and TRACK 29), so no grass grows on them.
    Navmesh should treat the painted road as the walk surface.
  - The generator's objects must keep off the road samples. That is the road exclusion the L4 road hops check.
- **Gates and landings** are planned by `routes/infra.py` (persistent refs in the WRLD override). They are pending
  until the Black Marsh pieces resolve.
- 8.6% of the BC_ROAD 10 m steps are steeper than the old 8° grade cap (stormhold-thorn has 1.17 km of them). That
  matters to navmesh slope and to NPC travel times.

*From 2.W2 (2026-10-29):*
- **Water in ArgoniaWorld.esm.** `make plugin` builds it from `python -m tools.argonia_world.water plan`
  (out/water/cells.jsonl and records.json; WaterRecords.cs). It holds 4 WATR (sea, brackish, fresh, black), 4
  water-plane ACTI (`Water\Water1024.nif`), the WRLD's default water (sea, at 0), 1,150 cells with a water
  height (XCLW and XCWT), and 33,745 REFRs: 32,214 water planes and 1,531 vanilla rapids, cascade, falls and mist
  pieces. Water FormIDs start at 0x100000, with 256 per province cell.
- **The plane budget.** At most 32 plane references per cell (`budget.BUDGET`, final after the L4 frame-time
  measurement `tests/journeys/water-budget.yaml`). 259 cells stand at one water height with their border
  regraded. The regrade's 11,243 vertex floors are in the `water_regrade` table, and the terrain applies them in
  `grid.Sources.heights` (terrain/water_regrade.py), so navmesh and LOD see the regraded ground. Every loss is a
  `water_loss` row and every reduced cell a `water_budget_cell` row.
- **For navmesh:** water is wherever `out/water/cells.jsonl` puts a plane or a height. The bed is the LAND
  under it, and the per-sample water level is `field.build` and `plan.shown_levels` (or plan.json's checks). NPC
  swim paths are owed at L4 (`tests/journeys/water-npc-swim.yaml`).
- **For vegetation:** do not place dry-land flora on samples under water. `realised()` in water/plan.py gives
  the level over each sample; the fallback cells flood their low ground (the `fallback-flooded` loss rows).
- **For LOD:** water LOD is the WRLD default at 0 plus the cell heights. Placed planes have no LOD of their own;
  that is an xLODGen question (the planes are activators).

## Deliverables

### a. Vegetation, by the region generator (decision 0028)
- **Grass is not placed.** It follows the LTEX paint of 2.W1: Tropical Skyrim's grass records are attached to the
  vanilla land textures it replaces, and the engine scatters them at runtime. The only knobs are the texture
  choice per land-cover class and the grass game settings in the graphics baseline (0.3 §d).
- **Trees, shrubs, rocks and scatter come from the CK's region generator**, run once as a scripted job on the
  Windows game machine over 2.W3's region records: each REGN's object list names vanilla tree, shrub, rock and
  flora forms (Tropical Skyrim's replacements make them tropical), with density, slope and height bands, clustering
  and water avoidance drawn from the old flora and groundcover rules (old 0036, 0048, 0070, 0082), read as data.
  The generated REFRs are reviewed as a diff like any generated record (0009); a hand-placed plant exists only
  inside a place. BM&V and Project Rainforest supply nothing to the land; a marsh-specific plant a place wants by
  hand comes from them as a prop.
- Density by region class with a performance budget per cell measured on the chosen GPU instance (draw calls
  and frame time), not guessed; thinning is a region density change, never hand deletion.
- **Acceptance:** frame time in the densest 10 cells stays within the 0.2 §d bar; reader panel on 20 vantage
  screenshots: no Nordic flora (pines, snow, heather) anywhere.

### b. Navmesh
- The production version of 0.4's route for every exterior cell and interior: **tes4skyrim's navmesh method**
  (decision 0026 §3), generating walkable triangles from the world's collision, with door triangles linked to
  load doors, water triangles flagged, cross-cell edge links, NAVI master data, and cover data where cheap.
- The Creation Kit's generator (decision 0018), scripted on the Windows game machine, is the check on a sample
  and covers any cell the generator cannot, with its output captured to Spriggit.
- **Acceptance:** 200 random NPC path requests between reachable points succeed (bridge path query); 20 NPCs
  with sandbox packages do not stand still for more than 60 s in a 10-minute sim.

### c. LOD and the world map image
- xLODGen (terrain LOD) and DynDOLOD (object and tree LOD) natively on the Windows game machine; 32–64 GB RAM
  advised, so a larger instance type for the run if the game machine's is short (budget in decision 0008),
  stopped after.
- **Who generates LOD (decision 0014 §4):** LOD output is derived from mod and vanilla textures, so it is never
  shipped. Measure the player-side cost (time, RAM) of the same steps; the installer runs terrain LOD always and
  object LOD only if a typical player PC (16 GB) can do it in under an hour; otherwise ship our LOD settings only
  and record the visual loss for players (the owner's GPU box gets full LOD).
- **The parchment chart** (owner, 2026-10-29): a painted-look world map image rendered from our own heightfield,
  hydrology and land cover (the UI exception), in two layers: the **sketch** that is always visible (the province
  outline, the coast, the major rivers, the major roads, and the seven major cities Gideon, Soulrest, Lilmoth,
  Blackrose, Archon, Stormhold and Thorn roughly drawn, as a rough chart), and the **revealed detail** that fills in
  as the player explores (discovered places, the local relief); consumed by the paper map (1.2 stage one, 2.12) and
  the owner's map in the stream (0.3). It fits the paper theme.
- **Acceptance:** distant views show terrain and tree LOD with no missing tiles; the LOD build is a scripted job.

## Risks and fallbacks
- **Navmesh scale-up** breaks on cases the sample did not have (cliffs, docks, water): survey all failures first,
  fix in one batch; the CK on the Windows game machine for residual cells.
- **Too many objects** for Skyrim's cell budgets in dense jungle: thin by region class; move density into grass and
  LOD.

Place-level dressing (2.W5); the final look (4.6).

## Outcome

### a. Vegetation (lane 2.W4-veg, 2026-10-30; the fallback, owner issue #15)
- **Our own placement from the region rules** (`tools/argonia_world/vegetation`). It is seeded per cell and
  form, works at the RDOT density, clustering, slope and height bands and water posture, and keeps off water, road
  and built places. Each region is thinned into vanilla Tamriel's forested range (p95 83, max 162 refs a cell,
  measured on Skyrim.esm) with a hard cap of 400 refs a cell (`vegetation_region_budget`). Method, numbers and
  findings: [the journal](../../research/journals/vegetation.md).
- **The province:** 470,508 REFRs in 9,538 cells; the province's cells mean 29.6, p95 82, max 107. The L1 checks
  are all green: ground within 0.5 m (decoded LAND), slope, height, water, road, places, budget, ids. Two builds
  give the same sha256. The placement takes 94-119 s at a 0.43-0.51 GB peak.
- **In ArgoniaWorld.esm** by a streaming splice (`ArgoniaGen vegetation`; Mutagen's model for these refs did not
  fit in 2.25 GB of heap). The Mutagen read-back count equals the placement's. It is opt-in for now:
  `tools/ArgoniaGen/pipeline.py vegetation`, not in `make plugin` or the gate's sample hash.
- **Navmesh:** trees do not cut it (NPC avoidance); recorded in the journal.
- **Owed:** L4 stop 8 (`tests/journeys/w4-veg-densest.yaml`: frame time, so the cap can be set; the three
  vanilla rocks; Nordic flora); the CK comparison; the wiring into `make plugin` with a deliberate re-pin of the
  sample manifest.
