## Deliverables (the rule, as built)

### a. Repo skeleton and rules
The PLAN §3 directories with a README each; `.gitignore`; `CLAUDE.md` under its 1,100-word budget (enforced by
`tooling/tests/test_word_budget.py`); `docs/PROGRESS.md` rows for every chunk. `LICENSE` and `LICENSE-content`
per decision 0015 once the owner answers O-14.

### b. Ways of working, lean (decisions 0011, 0027 §9)
The agents and skills above; `tooling/brief_template.py`, `shell_guard.py`, `job_guard.sh` (0.0),
`session_tokens.py`, `owner_inbox.py` (0.0); the rtk hook. The writing skills' adaptations: chunk ids for P-phase
ids; the branch's 0017/0018 → 0024/0025; old-repo records written "old 0145"; the corpus under
`$ARGONIA_PRIVATE/dialogue/morrowind/` only once O-21 is answered; no model names outside frontmatter. Nothing
else is copied until an incident earns it; universal-modder's knowledge base is read in place and its skills are
vendored one at a time by the brief that uses one (0027 §7; the audit names `game-recon`, `reverse-engineering`
with the Bethesda playbook, and `game-automation` as the first three).

### c. The no-assets guard and the gate
As built above. A new check earns its place by catching a real defect and is made to fail on purpose once.

### d. The build VM toolchain, just in time
`infra/build-vm/setup.sh` and `toolchain.lock`; a tool is added by the chunk that first needs it, with its
pinned version and hash: Node and Playwright for the UI harness (G11 or 1.2), Blender headless (2.W4 or the
first mesh question), soulstruct and DSAnimStudio (2.7, on the game machine), Ghidra (the first named question
under 0010 §4), JPEXS (the first SWF question, 0014 §7), OpenMW's source (G11's window study, cloned to
`private/`). It lives in `infra/build-vm/` beside 0.0's idle-stop and resume scripts.

### e. Secrets and game files
Secrets in `~/.argonia/secrets.env` (mode 600; the shell guard refuses reading it). Skyrim SE and DSR are on the
build VM under `~/games`; the Creation Kit lives on the game machine (0018). The R2 vault (`tools/fetch_vault.py`,
the frozen heightfield and apron rasters into `$ARGONIA_DATA`, checked against `world/sources/terrain/freeze.json`)
needs the old project's R2 credentials (`ES_SNAPSHOT_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `ES_SNAPSHOT_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`,
`ES_SNAPSHOT_ENDPOINT`): the owner copies those three lines from the password manager or from
`/workspaces/.es-secrets.env` on the stopped old machine's disk (start it, Session Manager, copy them into the new
secrets file through the editor, stop it) before answering O-1. `fetch_vault.py` is written and proved on its
no-credentials and `--check` paths; it reads the five frozen files' names and hashes from `freeze.json`, finds
their snapshot parts from the bucket's `.files` sidecars, streams only those parts and refuses a hash mismatch.

### f. The import
`tools/import_old.py` as built; the integrity check joins the gate once `jsonschema` is in the venv (it is in
`toolchain.lock`); `docs/design/` is edited only when a later decision changes the design.

### g. Infra record and cost baseline
`tools/cost_report.py`; the infra facts are 0.0's. The $100 cap binds from November 2026 (0008 note).

### h. First owner check-in
Posted at the close of this chunk: what 0.1 and the plan overhaul delivered, the costs, the O-1, O-6, O-14 and
O-21 asks, and what 0.2 needs.

### i. Fit the writing skills to the import (decisions 0024, 0025)
A side lane on the build VM, closing before 1.3 and 1.4. It reads the imported quest foundation
(`docs/design/quests/` in full; `world/sources/quests/` and its schema; the journal design; the dialogue README,
standards and culture sheets; the lore dossiers and extrapolation; the text style guide) and makes one format
each: the quest record is the imported quest schema extended with the record's sections; conditions and actions
use the condition vocabulary; `skill-uses.yaml` takes the old verb tags and every system the player can use;
each `profiles.yaml` entry comes from 40-factions and its culture sheet; `action_plan.py` is tuned until its
proposals read as plausible quests; cast comes from 36-cast-roster. An engine-mapping table in `quest-record.md`
gives every machine field its consumer (stages → the 1.4 and 2.12 generator; topics and lines → the topic store,
1.3 and 2.5; conditions → the evaluator; persona → 2.4; `new` things → the world-builder; journal text → the
journal). A field the engine cannot express becomes a decision. **Acceptance:** LH38 and one faction quest each go
record → `quest-review` → briefs → lines → linter on the imported data with no hand fixes; the mapping table has a
consumer for every field; `lint.py` exits 1 on a hit; the owner reads LH38's lines in a check-in.

### j. The build-versus-reuse audit (owner ruling, 2026-10-28)
Done: `docs/research/2026-10-29/reuse-audit.md`. Every later brief names its prior art and re-checks the rows it
uses; the UNVERIFIED rows (Infinity UI, Sailboats, Rowboats, Fuz Ro D-oh, Community Shaders on 1.7.104, the
regions and vegetation converters) are re-checked by the chunk that reaches them.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
None: tooling chunks run their acceptance commands and the gate, and prove each hook on a fail-on-purpose case.

## Exit gate
- `make check` green in CI and on the VM; every hook's fail-on-purpose test passes. **Met.**
- Toolchain smoke green; game files present; world imported and valid. **Met** (the R2 arrays wait for the owner).
- §i and §j done. **Met.**
- PROGRESS row for 0.1 done; 0.2's Starting state written. **Met.**

## Risks and fallbacks
- **R2 credentials lost:** the heightfield can be re-derived from Tamriel Worldspaces – Argonia (118678) with the
  old chain (expensive; owner call).
- **The side branch's skills prove to need the corpus to be useful:** O-21 is asked in the first check-in.

## Deliberately out of scope
The GPU box and anything game-facing (0.2 onwards); CI on a GPU (never); the port of `packages/ui` (1.2, 2.2);
every tool not named in §d (installed at first use).
