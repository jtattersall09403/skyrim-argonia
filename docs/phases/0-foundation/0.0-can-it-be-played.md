# 0.0: Can it be played? Vanilla Skyrim streamed to the phone and the Mac, with touch and a GameSir pad

**Goal.** Before anything else is built, answer the question everything else depends on. Can the owner play
Skyrim SE, running on an AWS GPU instance in London, in a browser on the Honor Magic V5 and the MacBook Air M2,
with controls that work: touch, and a GameSir gamepad? Is it responsive enough for Dark Souls-style combat?
Owner: "If that doesn't work or is too laggy then there's no point doing anything else."

This is gate **G0**. Nothing in 0.1–0.5 that costs more than a day starts before G0 passes, except the minimum
this chunk needs.

**Starting state.** The owner's setup steps 1–4 are done: the budget alert, the build VM, the VS Code tunnel,
Claude Code and `gh`, and the repo cloned. Nothing else exists.

**Re-check before starting:**
- the Selkies version and its gamepad and touch features (its 2.1 release lists browser gamepad polling, rumble
  and a touch gamepad);
- g6, g4dn and g5 spot prices in eu-west-2;
- the GameSir model the owner has, and its connection modes (Bluetooth XInput or Android HID, a 2.4 GHz dongle,
  or a USB-C phone clip).

**Read first.** Decisions 0007 and 0008; [hosting-streaming](../../research/2026-10-27/hosting-streaming.md) in
full; [infra/README.md](../../../infra/README.md).

## Deliverables

### a. The minimum infrastructure, and nothing more
- **Owner steps, in one check-in:**
  1. Attach an IAM policy to the build VM's role: EC2 start, stop, describe and run-instances on
     `project=argonia` tags, plus the launch template. The exact JSON is in the check-in.
  2. Check, and if needed raise, the EC2 vCPU quota for G and VT spot instances in eu-west-2. New accounts often
     have 0, and an increase takes a day or two.
  3. Add the Creation Kit to the Steam library (needed from 0.1 on; asked now so it is ready).
- **The GPU box** `argonia-play`, created by `infra/gpu-box/create.sh`:
  - a g6.xlarge spot (fallbacks g4dn.xlarge, g5.xlarge), persistent and stopped on interruption;
  - Ubuntu 24.04, the NVIDIA driver, 150 GiB gp3, in the same VPC and AZ as the build VM;
  - a security group allowing SSH from the build VM only, and the Selkies port only from the owner's IPs (or
    behind auth on 443);
  - tagged `project=argonia`.
- **`infra/gpu-box/setup.sh`:**
  - the Steam client (Linux), Proton (pinned in the lock file) and protontricks;
  - a virtual display (Xorg with the NVIDIA driver, or gamescope headless) and PipeWire for audio;
  - Selkies (the linuxserver.io build) with authentication;
  - an idle-stop timer (15 minutes with no stream client).
- **Owner step (O-7):** log in to Steam through the stream once and approve Steam Guard. Install Skyrim SE, then
  set Steam offline and disable updates for it.
- **Steam refresh:** offline mode needs an online login every couple of weeks. `infra/gpu-box/steam_check.sh`
  runs at each box start; if Steam refuses an offline launch, the agent stops GPU work and asks for a re-login.
- Record the Skyrim depot manifest ids in `infra/README.md` (decision 0002), and the per-hour cost.

### b. The stream and the controls (vanilla Skyrim, no mods)
- **The scene:** vanilla Skyrim at 1080p with an outdoor scene with trees and water near Riverwood, a fight with
  a bandit or two, and the inventory and a dialogue with text to read.
- **Devices and networks:** the Magic V5 folded and unfolded, on home Wi-Fi and then 5G, in Chrome; and the
  MacBook Air in Chrome or Safari, on its usual network (WebSocket/TCP-443 mode if UDP is blocked). Pairing a
  Bluetooth pad with the Mac is a setting, not an install.
- **Controls, each tried and recorded as works, works with a fix, or fails:**
  - **The GameSir pad on the phone**, through the browser's Gamepad API and Selkies' gamepad passthrough: every
    button, both sticks and the triggers map to Skyrim's XInput layout; no stuck inputs; rumble if Selkies
    passes it.
  - **The GameSir pad on the Mac** (Bluetooth).
  - **Touch on the phone:** Selkies' touch gamepad, if it gives Skyrim usable sticks and buttons; else a small
    touch overlay page of our own that sends gamepad events through the stream client, or Moonlight-Web as the
    alternative client. Touch does not have to be how the owner plays combat. It has to let the owner walk,
    look, talk, use menus and fight a little.
  - **Keyboard and mouse on the Mac.**
- **Latency, measured, not guessed:**
  - the stream's round trip, from Selkies' stats;
  - the game's own input-to-present time (with no bridge yet, a scripted key press against frame timestamps
    from a capture on the box);
  - the client's decode and display estimate.

  Sum them per device and network. A 60 fps capture of the phone screen, filmed by the owner with a second
  phone while pressing a button, gives the button-to-photon number directly if the owner is willing (optional).
- **Readability:** can the owner read the dialogue text, the HUD and a menu list on the phone in both screen
  shapes? Record the UI scale needed.
- **Frame time on the instance:** median and 1% lows in the scene on the chosen instance type; CPU or GPU bound.

### c. The feel probe (after §b passes; recommended, about two days)
- The question: can this engine, through this stream, feel like Dark Souls at all, before we build anything of
  our own?
- Install a stock souls-like stack by hand on the box: SKSE, Address Library, Precision, TK Dodge RE, TDM, and a
  permitted souls-style moveset. The research agent picks the current set the day before. It is for this test
  only and is not committed, shipped or kept.
- The owner plays 20 minutes with the pad: roll through a swing, block, try to parry, lock on, fight two or three
  enemies. The verdict compares against memory of Dark Souls Remastered, with the measured latency beside it.

## Acceptance (G0)
- **The owner's verdict on the phone and the Mac:** "playable" or not, with the pad, and with touch for the
  non-combat basics.
- **A recorded latency sum** per device and network.
- **Frame time:** ≥ 60 fps median and ≥ 45 fps 1% lows in the vanilla scene on one instance type, within the
  budget.
- **The feel probe verdict** (if run): "could feel like Dark Souls", "too laggy for parries", or "Skyrim's combat
  is the problem, not the stream".
- Everything is written into decision 0007 and `infra/README.md`.

## If it fails
In this order, each tried and measured before the next:
1. WebRTC transport (UDP) instead of WebSockets; a lower resolution or 900p; H.264 or AV1 on the L4 GPU; a higher
   bitrate on Wi-Fi.
2. Moonlight-Web with Sunshine as the alternative stack.
3. Another instance type, if frame time is the problem.

If the owner still calls it unplayable, **stop and re-plan with the owner** before any other work. The options
then are a local PC or Steam Deck for play (streaming only for the phone), a native engine, or a different
game shape (for example, less parry-dependent combat). This is a decision only the owner can make.

## Loops (docs/loops.md)
None. This is an owner-played test (L6) with measured numbers beside it.

## Owner check
One session of about 45 minutes (an hour with the feel probe):
- the phone, folded and unfolded, on Wi-Fi then 5G;
- the Mac;
- the GameSir pad, then touch.

Reply with "playable / not playable" for each device and network, which controls worked, and anything that
jarred.
