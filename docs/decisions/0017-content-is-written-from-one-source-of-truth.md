# 0017: Quests, dialogue and text are written from one source of truth, through generated briefs

**Status:** decided 2026-10-27 (owner, in the dialogue practice session: lines must read "as if they were
Morrowind lines"; the old written dialogue "is terrible" and must not be used as inspiration; skills start from
scratch; quests, places, dialogue, characters and facts must be consistent "first time, as opposed to doing it
one way, failing a test, tweaking"; banned constructions must be easy to add and followed; British English;
any of the ten races as speaker or player; quest types need their own principles, from research).

## Decision

1. **Sources of truth.** The world records (places, people, culture sheets, facts, routes, landmarks) and one
   **quest record** per quest (`.claude/skills/quest-write/quest-record.md`) plus one **line record** per
   questline. No downstream text restates a fact by hand.
2. **Order of work, every time:** line record → quest record (truth, then cast knowledge, things, clues,
   stages, endings, topics) → `quest-review` by a fresh agent → dialogue briefs generated by
   `quest-write/brief.py` (and the place-brief builder for non-quest talk) → `dialogue-write` → its linter.
3. **Writers add no facts.** A dialogue writer who needs a fact asks; the quest writer adds it to the record and
   the briefs are rebuilt. Every fact a line states cites its id.
4. **The world is built from the records.** A thing a quest needs that the place does not hold is a `new` row
   in the quest record; the world builder places it from that row (P3, P9). Dialogue names only things the
   record holds.
5. **Type principles.** `quest-write` routes each quest to the principles for its primary shape (the sixteen
   shapes of the old quest index: THEFT, MURDER, MISSING, DISPUTE, FRAUD, PREDATOR, CONTAMINATION, SMUGGLING,
   HAUNTING, RITE, SUCCESSION, BONDAGE, RECKONING, EXPEDITION, NEGOTIATION, WONDER) and for its line type
   (local, faction, main, Daedric), each distilled from a research pack in `docs/research/2026-10-27/`.
6. **Dialogue is its own skill.** `dialogue-write` serves quests and every other kind of talk; it takes only
   generated briefs. Morrowind's lines are models by id from the private corpus (0003); they never enter the repo.
7. **Old written lines are not used.** The imported dialogue's facts, topics, culture sheets and glossary are
   kept; its written lines (about 1,471 place lines and 569 pool lines) are neither shipped nor used as models.
   Every line is written afresh with `dialogue-write` (P4-WP4.7 for the slice's people, P9 for the rest).
8. **The old quest design is the foundation.** The old main quest, faction lines, cast roster, side quests,
   quest index, condition vocabulary and journal design are kept and improved, not replaced: P4-WP4.0 fits the
   skills to them, and each quest (the 38 staged ones and the skeletons) passes `quest-review` and is rebuilt
   through `quest-write` from its old premise and cast before its dialogue is written (P4-WP4.8, P9).
9. **Quests have action.** Nearly every stage makes the player use their skills (fight, persuade, sneak,
   steal, spy, dive, climb, sail, cast, craft, clear a place), varied and combined; a faction's quests lean to
   its own profile; a chain of conversations alone is not a quest (`quest-write/principles/actions.md`).

## Consequences

- Writing cost rises: every imported person's lines are re-authored. Dialogue writing stays on Sonnet (old
  owner ruling); quest review runs on Opus because a missed design flaw costs a whole quest's dialogue.
- Checks (the linter, the brief builder's refusal to write from an incomplete record, L4 scenarios) remain, but
  as confirmation: the build order is what makes the content consistent.
- Skills: `.claude/skills/quest-write`, `quest-review`, `dialogue-write` (with `bans.yaml`, `voices.md`).
