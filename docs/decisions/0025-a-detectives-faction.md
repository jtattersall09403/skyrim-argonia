# 0025: A detectives' faction, rival to the Shadowscales and the Eelskins

**Status:** decided 2026-10-27 (owner: "let's have a detectives faction! As rivals to those two. Solving murders
and thefts, with quests escalating up from petty crimes to some big grand murder mystery finale. It will need to
be grounded in lore and our lore extrapolation"). Planned so that it happens at the right point without the owner
having to remember.

## Decision

1. **A new joinable faction** whose members solve crimes in Black Marsh, set against the Shadowscales (the
   assassins) and the Eelskins (the thieves). Its name, founding, patrons and headquarters come from the lore pass
   below, not from this record.
2. **Its line escalates by case** from petty crimes to a **grand finale**, through escalating series of thefts,
   larcenies and murder mysteries. The final murder mystery involves highly important NPCs in the world of Black
   Marsh. The principles in `quest-write/principles/questlines.md` and `investigation.md` apply.
3. **Grounded in lore.** Before design, the lore pass reads the lore dossiers and the extrapolation
   (`story/lore/`, `story/lore/extrapolation/`) and the Shadowscales' and Eelskins' records, and writes the
   faction's place in 4E 201 Black Marsh as an extrapolation entry with sources and confidence. Who would pay for
   detectives here, and why they are trusted, must have an answer the lore supports. It is also grounded in
   classic detective and mystery structures that are known to work: we apply simple proven shapes in a way that is
   lore consistent, fun to play and within the quest rules, and do not invent a new structure.
4. **Built with the writing skills** ([0024](0024-content-is-written-from-one-source-of-truth.md)): a line record,
   then quest records through `quest-write` (MURDER, THEFT, FRAUD, MISSING and RECKONING principles),
   `quest-review`, then dialogue.
5. **The systems it needs** (evidence-gated topics, presenting evidence, accusation and the rival factions'
   reactions) are built whole when the first investigation quest needs them, since every MURDER, THEFT and FRAUD
   quest uses them too.
