# 0006: Black Marsh immersion: nothing we add is Nordic, and we make no art but music

**Status:** decided 2026-10-27; Nordic pieces in places judged case by case since 2026-11-20 (owner, #80). Owner:
"It will be very immersion breaking for there to be Nordic design elements sneaking into our Black Marsh game."

## Decision

1. Nothing we add to the game carries a Nordic element: no Nord names, places, gods, idioms or music in our
   records, lines, journal entries or items. Skyrim's own voiced barks stay (owner, 2026-10-31, #22), filtered of
   lines naming Skyrim places, people or events where our plugin can condition them out.
2. The vanilla systems that would carry Skyrim's story into Black Marsh are shut down
   ([0012](0012-vanilla-skyrim-is-quarantined.md)).
3. Nordic pieces in places have no blanket ban. A Nordic piece stays where it is nearly fine as it is; the
   Tropical Skyrim version is used where there is one; an easy swap is taken where there is one; each case is
   judged on how it reads to the player, and nothing is laboriously replaced. Plainly Nordic signs (a Doomstone
   circle, a Nord burial, a Talos shrine) are the cases where a swap is worth making.
4. The soulslike collection xyf1u3 comes in whole, Nordic-themed parts included (owner, 2026-11-06: "I want the
   full mod stack from that collection in, even if there are some nordic themed elements"); the rule binds what we
   write, not the collection's mods. The same holds for what a source mod such as BM&V ships.
5. We make no art. The one exception is music, which we make with Fal (owner, 2026-11-17). The font is Pelagiad.

## Consequences

- Our text goes through the `text-review` skill, whose linter carries the Nordic-tell list.
- The Nordic reading of a place is judged by the owner, not by a reader's taste.
