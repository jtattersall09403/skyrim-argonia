# 0024: Quests, dialogue and text are written from one source of truth, through generated briefs

**Status:** decided 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-28 (owner, in the dialogue practice sessions on a side branch:
lines must read "as if they were Morrowind lines"; the old written dialogue "is terrible" and must not be used
as inspiration; the skills start from scratch; quests, places, dialogue, characters and facts must be
consistent "first time, as opposed to doing it one way, failing a test, tweaking"; banned constructions must be
easy to add and followed; British English; any of the ten races as speaker or player; quest types need their
own principles, from research; nearly every quest has skill-based action, led by faction profile; the old
quest design is the foundation). Numbered 0017 on that branch; brought into this plan's numbering here.

## Decision

1. **Sources of truth.** The world records (places, people, culture sheets, facts, routes, landmarks), one
   **quest record** per quest (`.claude/skills/quest-write/quest-record.md`) and one **line record** per
   questline. No downstream text restates a fact by hand.
2. **Order of work, every time:** line record → quest record (action plan, truth, cast knowledge, things,
   clues, stages, endings, topics) → `quest-review` by a fresh agent → dialogue briefs generated by
   `quest-write/brief.py` (and `dialogue-write/place_brief.py` for non-quest talk) → `dialogue-write` → its
   linter.
3. **Writers add no facts.** A dialogue writer who needs a fact asks; the quest writer adds it to the record and
   the briefs are rebuilt. Every fact a line states cites its id.
4. **The world is built from the records.** A thing a quest needs that the place does not hold is a `new` row
   in the quest record; the world-builder places it from that row (2.W5, 4.2, 4.4). Dialogue names only things
   the record holds.
5. **Type principles.** `quest-write` routes each quest to the principles for its primary shape (the sixteen
   shapes of the old quest index: THEFT, MURDER, MISSING, DISPUTE, FRAUD, PREDATOR, CONTAMINATION, SMUGGLING,
   HAUNTING, RITE, SUCCESSION, BONDAGE, RECKONING, EXPEDITION, NEGOTIATION, WONDER) and for its line type
   (local, faction, main, Daedric), each distilled from a research pack in `docs/research/2026-10-27/`.
6. **Dialogue is its own skill.** `dialogue-write` serves quests and every other kind of talk and takes only
   generated briefs. Morrowind's lines are models, cited by id, from the private corpus in `$ARGONIA_PRIVATE`
   (decision 0003): they never enter the repo, a brief, a report, an issue or a line.
7. **Old written lines are not used.** The imported dialogue's facts, topics, culture sheets and glossary are
   kept; its written lines (about 1,471 place lines and 569 pool lines) are imported for reference only, never
   shipped and never used as models. Every line is written afresh with `dialogue-write`, once 0.1 §i has fitted
   the skills to the import: 1.3 for Claywater Station's three people, 2.5 for the slice's people, 3.3 for the
   opening, 4.2 for the rest.
8. **The old quest design is the foundation.** The old main quest, faction lines, cast roster, side quests,
   quest index, condition vocabulary and journal design are kept and improved, not replaced: 0.1 §i fits the
   skills to them, and each imported quest (the 38 staged ones and the skeletons) is rebuilt through
   `quest-write` from its old premise and cast, and passes `quest-review`, before its dialogue is written
   (2.12 for the slice, 3.3 for the opening, 4.2 and 4.3 for the rest).
9. **Quests are built round skill use.** An action is a use of one of the player's skills. Each quest starts
   from an action plan drawn from the skill-use catalogue (`quest-write/skill-uses.yaml`), weighted by its
   line's or faction's profile (`profiles.yaml`) and away from what its line and region already use; its truth
   and stages are written so those actions follow. A chain of conversations alone is not a quest
   (`quest-write/principles/actions.md`).
10. **Cities and the opening stay hands-on** (phases/README): on them the skills run with the owner reading
    the quest records and a sample of lines before anything is built; no skill runs unattended there.

## Consequences

- Writing cost rises: every imported person's lines are re-authored (Claude usage, decision 0013).
- The writing agents' models live only in their frontmatter: `dialogue-writer` on Sonnet (old owner ruling
  0079), `quest-reviewer` on Opus because a missed design flaw costs a whole quest's dialogue, `corpus-find`
  on Haiku.
- Checks (the linter, the brief builder's refusal to write from an incomplete record, quest journeys) remain,
  but as confirmation: the build order is what makes the content consistent.
- Skills: `.claude/skills/quest-write`, `quest-review`, `dialogue-write` (with `bans.yaml`, `voices.md`),
  brought in and fitted in 0.1 §b and §i.
