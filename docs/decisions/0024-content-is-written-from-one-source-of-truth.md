# 0024: Quests, dialogue and text are written from one source of truth, through generated briefs

**Status:** decided 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-28 (owner, in the dialogue practice sessions: lines must read "as if
they were Morrowind lines"; quests, places, dialogue, characters and facts must be consistent "first time, as
opposed to doing it one way, failing a test, tweaking"; banned constructions must be easy to add and followed;
British English; any of the ten races as speaker or player; quest types need their own principles; nearly every
quest has skill-based action, led by faction profile). Applies to the new world ([0042](0042-the-new-world-is-the-project.md)).

## Decision

1. **Sources of truth.** The place and people records of the new world, one **quest record** per quest in the story
   database (`.claude/skills/quest-write/quest-record.md`) and one **line record** per story line. No downstream
   text restates a fact by hand. The records change with the world: when we add to or edit a place, the records
   are updated in the same commit ([consistency](../design/consistency.md)).
2. **Order of work, every time:** line record, quest record (action plan, truth, cast knowledge, things, clues,
   stages, endings, topics), `quest-review` by a fresh agent, dialogue briefs generated by
   `quest-write/brief.py` (and `dialogue-write/place_brief.py` and `talk_brief.py` for non-quest talk),
   `dialogue-write`, its linter. The skills are entered at `quest-system`.
3. **Writers add no facts.** A dialogue writer who needs a fact asks; the quest writer adds it to the record and
   the briefs are rebuilt. Every fact a line states cites its id.
4. **The world is built from the records.** A thing a quest needs that the place does not hold is a `new` row in
   the quest record, placed from that row. Dialogue names only things the record holds.
5. **Type principles.** `quest-write` routes each quest to the principles for its primary shape (the sixteen
   shapes of `quest-write/shapes.yaml`) and for its line type (local, faction, main, Daedric), in
   `quest-write/principles/`.
6. **Dialogue is its own skill.** `dialogue-write` serves quests and every other kind of talk and takes only
   generated briefs. Morrowind's lines are models, cited by id, from the private corpus in `$ARGONIA_PRIVATE`
   ([0003](0003-nothing-shipped-that-players-do-not-own.md)): they never enter the repo, a brief, a report, an
   issue or a line.
7. **Quests are built round skill use.** An action is a use of one of the player's skills. Each quest starts from
   an action plan drawn from the skill-use catalogue (`quest-write/skill-uses.yaml`), weighted by its line's or
   faction's profile (`profiles.yaml`) and away from what its line and region already use. A chain of
   conversations alone is not a quest (`quest-write/principles/actions.md`).
8. **Fable writes the overall story** (the main quest and each major faction's line), inside the quest ceiling and
   to the owner's bar ([owner-bar](../../story/design/owner-bar.md)).

## Consequences

- The writing agents' models live only in their frontmatter in `.claude/agents/`.
- Checks (the linter, the brief builder's refusal to write from an incomplete record, the paper playtest) remain,
  but as confirmation: the build order is what makes the content consistent.
